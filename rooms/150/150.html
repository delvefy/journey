Words

You have walked the whole world to reach this corner, where the wrought meets the unseen, and you find the room is made of nothing you can hold. The hoard was never stone or coin; it was this — the named thing, the said thing, the breath that becomes a door. Every wall you ever passed, you passed by speaking. Here, at the far edge, the world admits what it always was: built of words.

South

I hold sixty, and am held by
four-and-twenty in a day;
the bell that counts me counts your life
in equal coins it gives away.

West

Older than the page, I am a word
that is also a wound:
cut into bone, or oak, or standing stone,
a letter the north once crowned
with power to bind, or to ward.
