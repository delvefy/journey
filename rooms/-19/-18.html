Reef

You are a reef - a long hidden ridge lying just beneath the waves, built of coral and rock over thousands of years. Out here the big rollers trip and break in a line of white foam, while behind you the water lies calm. You teem with life: clownfish, turtles, darting shoals, all sheltering in your nooks and ledges. Beautiful and busy and a danger to ships, you are an undersea wall and an undersea city both at once, alive with colour beneath the bright shallow sea.
