Tin

You are tin - the cheap, soft, pale-grey metal of bean cans and battered old toys. You bend if a strong hand twists you, and you keep no shine the way silver does. Bang on a sheet of you and you rattle thin. People wrap food in you and stamp you into soldiers and stars. Of all the metals dug up from the ground you are the plainest and the lightest, the humble one nobody guards behind glass.
