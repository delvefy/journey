Neck

You are a neck - the bendy column that holds the head up above the shoulders and lets it turn this way and that. No hand made you; you grew, and a giraffe's grew longest of all. You can be hugged, kissed, tickled, wrapped warm in a scarf. You swallow the dinner down, you carry the voice up from the chest, you crane to see over the crowd. Slim and strong and always working, you are the busy bridge between the body and the head.
