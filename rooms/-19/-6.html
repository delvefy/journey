Nest

You are a nest - a little cup of twigs and grass and moss and mud, woven together by a bird with nothing but its beak, to cradle its eggs. No hand made you; a bird did, gathering and weaving and pressing it round. You can be found in a hedge, a tree, a gutter, peeped into - but never disturbed. Inside you the eggs lie warm, then the chicks, all open mouths. You are a home built of odds and ends and held together by patience, the safest spot in the whole hedge.
