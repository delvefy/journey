Frost

You are frost - the white feathery coat of tiny ice that settles over everything on a cold clear night: the grass, the car, the window made into a garden of ferns. No hand makes you; the cold does, freezing the dew where it lies. You cannot really be held, only breathed on and melted with a fingertip. You crunch underfoot, you sparkle in the low sun, and by midmorning you are gone to dew again. You are winter's first soft warning, drawn in white.
