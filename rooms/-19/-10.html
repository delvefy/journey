Gem

You are a gem - a hard bright stone the earth keeps hidden in its dark for a thousand years, then yields. Miners dig you out, cutters polish you, and you drink the light and throw it back in colours. You are small enough to hide in a fist and dear enough to buy a house. Ruby, emerald, sapphire - each one is a kind of you. You are the earth's slow secret, made of plain stone yet worth more than gold.
