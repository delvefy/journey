Eyebrow

You are an eyebrow - the little arch of hair that sits above each eye, two small fences guarding them from sweat and rain. You speak without a word: raised, you ask a question or show surprise; lowered, you scowl. A single raised one can mean 'oh, really?'. People pluck you, draw you, and worry over you. You frame the face and give it half its feeling. You are a thin curved hedge of hair, the eye's small awning and the face's busiest little flag.
