Gush

You are a gush - water bursting out all at once, fast and strong and unstoppable. A burst pipe gushes across the kitchen; a broken hydrant gushes up the street; a tap turned full on gushes into the sink. There is no trickle or drip in you, just a great rushing flood pouring out faster than anyone can catch. Strong and sudden and soaking, you are water let loose in a hurry, gushing out in a torrent until somebody finds the valve and turns you off.
