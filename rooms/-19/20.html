Spurt

You are a spurt - a sudden short jet of liquid shooting out under pressure, then stopping just as quick. Squeeze the ketchup bottle and out it spurts; cut a tomato and the juice spurts; a kinked hose spurts when you let go. There and gone in a quick little burst. Sharp and sudden and a bit surprising, you are liquid forced out all at once in a thin fast jet, squirting where it's least expected and catching everyone off guard.
