Flicker

You are a flicker - the quick unsteady wavering of a small light. The candle does it in a draught, the dying fire does it among the coals, the old bulb does it before it fails. You are light that cannot keep still, brightening and dimming faster than the eye can follow, throwing shadows that jump on the wall. Blink and you have changed. You are the heartbeat of a flame, a glow that trembles between being there and being gone.
