Puddle

You are a puddle - a shallow little pool of rainwater caught in a dip in the path, going nowhere, gone by afternoon. No hand makes you; a downpour and a hollow do, and the sky lies mirrored in your skin until a boot comes down. You are the splash every child cannot resist, the brown bath of sparrows, the puddle the worms come up to. A small borrowed scrap of the rain, waiting for sun or a welly to end you.
