Shine

You are shine - the bright bounce of light off anything clean and smooth. The sun does you on the sea; a new coin does you in the palm; a fresh-shod shoe does you on the morning step. You are light given back, and you make people squint and grin at once. You cannot be held, only seen, and you fade the moment a cloud or a smear comes between. You are the world saying look at me, polished and gleaming and proud.

South

it haunts the old dark house and groans -
a ___, then drifts away.

West

stretched long when the sun is low;
