Grin

You are a grin - the widest, cheekiest smile of all, stretching right across the face from ear to ear and showing every tooth. No hand makes you; sudden delight does, or mischief, or pride. You cannot be touched, only worn and caught - one grin and the whole room grins back. The Cheshire Cat left only its grin behind. A grin says I am up to something, or I am simply, hugely happy. You are joy that the face cannot keep in.
