Drop

You are a drop - one small round bead of water, swelling on the lip of a tap or the leaf's edge until you are too heavy and let go. No hand makes you; weight and water do, and down you fall to burst into nothing or join a hundred more. You are a single tear, a single splat of rain, the smallest piece water comes in. Round and clear and brief, you hold the whole window bent and tiny inside your shining skin.
