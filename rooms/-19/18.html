Splosh

You are a splosh - the fat wet sound a thing makes when it lands in water and throws it everywhere. A welly stamped in a puddle, a stone dropped in a pond, a body bombing into the pool: all of them you. You are bigger and wetter than a splash, slower and sloppier, all flying drops and rocking ripples. Loud and soaking and a little bit naughty, you are the sound of fun by the water, and whoever is standing too close is going to get drenched.
