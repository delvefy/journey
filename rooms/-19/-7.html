Hive

You are a hive - the busy buzzing home of the honeybees, a hanging dome of wax built cell by perfect six-sided cell, where a whole humming city lives. No hand made you, though a beekeeper builds you a box; the bees did, with wax from their own bodies. You can be approached only with great care, in a veil and gloves. Inside, the queen lays, the workers store the golden honey, and the air thrums. You are a town of thousands, all working as one, sweet at its heart.
