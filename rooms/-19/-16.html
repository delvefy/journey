River

You are a river - a long wide ribbon of moving water, born in the hills as a trickle and grown fat and slow as it winds its way down to the sea. No hand digs you; the rain and the land do, over ages. Boats float on your back, fish hang in your green deeps, and willows trail their fingers in you. You never stop, never turn back, always going one way. Patient and powerful, you are the road that water takes home to the ocean.
