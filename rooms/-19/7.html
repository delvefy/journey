Ghost

You are a ghost - the pale see-through spirit of someone gone, said to drift through old houses at night, moaning and rattling its chains. No hand makes you; a story does, or a draughty corridor and a frightened imagination. You cannot be touched - your hand passes right through. You float, you wail, you go bump in the night, you hide under a white sheet on Halloween. Scary and sad and never quite there, you are the shape fear gives to the dark when nobody is sure what made that noise.
