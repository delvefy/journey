Creek

You are a creek - a small narrow waterway winding between muddy banks down to the sea. At low tide your bed lies bare and glistening; at high tide you brim and little boats float up you. Crabs scuttle in your mud, herons stand still in your shallows, and children wade in to fish with nets. Twisty and tidal and full of small life, you are a quiet sheltered finger of water, neither quite river nor quite sea, where the land and the ocean meet and mingle.
