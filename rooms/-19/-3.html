Conker

You are a conker - the smooth shiny brown seed of the horse-chestnut tree, that falls in autumn inside a spiky green case. No hand made you; the tree grew you, gleaming like polished wood the day you split your shell. You can be held, threaded on a string, swung to smash another in the old playground game. Children hunt for the biggest and hardest of you under the trees each September. You are autumn itself in the palm of a hand, brief and bright and brown.
