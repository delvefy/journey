Dribble

You are a dribble - the thin wandering run of liquid down the side of a too-full cup or a melting ice cream or a chin that missed the spoon. No hand makes you; a brimming edge and a tilt do. You escape in a slow crooked line and leave a sticky trail behind. Smaller than a pour and slower than a squirt, you are the little leak of a thing too full, the runaway drip that always finds the one clean sleeve.
