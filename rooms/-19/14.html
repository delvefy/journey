Winter

You are winter - the cold dark season at the bottom of the year. You come with short grey days and long black nights, with frost on the glass and snow on the hills and breath that smokes in the air. No hand makes you; the tilting earth does, leaning away from the sun. Folk pull on their coats and stay close to the fire while you grip the world still and quiet. You are the long sleep of the cold, waiting for spring.
