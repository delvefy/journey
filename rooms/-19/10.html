Squelch

You are a squelch - the wet sucking sound the mud makes when a boot pulls free. You are not a thing to hold; you are a noise the soft ground gives up, born of rain and clay and weight. Children stamp in puddles just to wake you. You live in bogs and marshes and the morning garden, half a slurp and half a sigh. You are damp made audible, the voice of every soggy step.
