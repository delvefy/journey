Mud

You are mud - earth and water mixed into a soft brown squelch, the stuff a puddle leaves behind and a pig is happiest in. No hand made you; rain made you, soaking the dry dirt into a glorious mess. You can be squeezed, splatted, tracked across a clean floor, shaped into pies that bake hard in the sun. Boots love you and mothers do not. You are the ground turned playful, the place where the path forgets to be a path.
