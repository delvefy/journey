Berry

You are a berry - a small round juicy fruit, red or blue or black, that grows in clusters on a bush or a bramble. No hand made you; the plant did, to wrap its seeds in something sweet so the birds will eat you and carry the seeds away. You can be picked, popped in the mouth, baked in a pie, squashed to a purple stain. Some of your cousins are sweet and some are poison, so a child learns which. You are summer made tiny and sweet.
