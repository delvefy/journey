Bush

You are a bush - a low woody plant, too small to be a tree and too big to be a flower, all tangled stems and leaves close to the ground. No hand made you; you grew, thick and round, in the hedge and the garden and the wild. You can be trimmed, hidden behind, pushed through, picked of your berries. Birds nest in your tangle and cats lie in wait beneath you. Common and green and full of secret rustlings, you are the cosy thicket of the everyday garden.
