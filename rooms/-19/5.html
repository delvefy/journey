Whine

You are a whine - a long high complaining cry that nobody likes to hear: a tired child who wants its way, a dog at the door to come in, a mosquito by your ear. No hand makes you; an unhappy creature does, a thin drawn-out sound that goes up at the end and asks and asks. You cannot be touched, only heard, and it wears a grown-up down faster than a shout. You are wanting-something turned into a sound.
