Starlight

You are starlight - the faint cold shine of a thousand distant suns, sprinkled across the black of a clear night. You travel for years, even ages, before you reach a watching eye, so the star you show may be long gone. You guide the sailor, the shepherd, and the wise men of old. Far fainter than the moon, you need a dark sky away from the town's glare to be truly seen. You are the oldest light there is, ancient and patient and pin-sharp.
