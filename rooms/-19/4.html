Storm

You are a storm - the sky in a fury: black clouds piling up, wind howling, rain lashing sideways, thunder rolling and lightning cracking the dark. No hand makes you; the hot and cold air do, crashing together until the whole sky boils over. You cannot be touched, only sheltered from. You send the boats to harbour and the children to the window, noses pressed to the glass. You knock and roar and pour, then pass, and leave the world washed clean and dripping.
