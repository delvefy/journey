Reed

You are a reed - a tall thin stalk of grass that grows where the land turns to water. You stand in the shallows in your hundreds, swaying when the wind walks through, whispering a dry papery song. The ducks hide among you and the wind plays you like a pipe. No hand plants you; the marsh does. You bend low in the gale and spring straight again, hollow and green, rooted in the soft wet ground.
