Scab

You are a scab - the crusty brown shield the body builds over a cut or a graze, sealing it shut so it can mend underneath in the dark. No hand makes you; the blood dries hard and you grow. You itch like mad and beg to be picked, but pick you too soon and the hurt comes back. When the new skin is ready, you drop off all on your own. Rough and brown and temporary, you are the body's own sticking-plaster, grown from the inside out.
