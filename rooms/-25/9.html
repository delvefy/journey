Glare

You are a glare - a hard fierce stare, all narrowed eyes and lowered brows, fixed on someone who has crossed you. You say without a single word: I am very cross indeed. A teacher gives you across the classroom; a big sister gives you over the last biscuit. There is no warmth in you, only a steady burning look. Sharp and silent and a little bit scary, you are anger sent straight out through the eyes, a long unblinking stare that can stop a naughty deed dead in its tracks.
