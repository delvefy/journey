Daytime

You are daytime - all the bright lit hours when the sun is up in the sky, from the first light of morning to the last glow of evening. This is when the world is awake and busy: children at school, birds singing, shops open, games in the park. Light and lively and full of doing, you are the half of the day made for getting up and getting on, when the curtains are open and everything can be clearly seen, and there is plenty of time still left to play.
