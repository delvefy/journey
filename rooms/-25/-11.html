Vein

You are a vein - one of the thin blue tubes that thread all through the body just under the skin, carrying the blood back to the heart. You show faint and blue on the back of a hand or the inside of a wrist. Branching like the twigs of a tree, you and your thousands of fellows make a great river-map of the body. Quiet and hidden and always flowing, you carry the blood home through its long looping journey, beat after beat, all the days of a life.
