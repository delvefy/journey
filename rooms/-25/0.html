Mountain

You are a mountain - a giant heap of rock heaved up so high that snow caps your head all year and the clouds catch on your shoulders. No hand piles you; the slow grinding earth does, over uncountable years. Climbers spend days crawling up your steep flanks to stand a moment at your peak. The wind howls cold around you. Huge and old and patient, you are the tallest thing the land can make, a stone giant that watches the whole country spread out small below.
