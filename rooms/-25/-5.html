Mango

You are a mango - a fat juicy fruit from the hot lands, golden-orange and sweet, with a big flat stone in the middle that the flesh clings to stubbornly. No hand makes you; the tall mango tree does. You drip down to the elbow when bitten and stain everything sunset-orange. The sweetest, messiest fruit to eat with bare hands, you are a taste of somewhere hot and far, best eaten over a sink with the juice running everywhere.
