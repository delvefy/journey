Talon

You are a talon - the sharp curved claw of a hunting bird, an eagle or an owl or a hawk, hooked and cruel and strong enough to snatch a mouse clean off the field. No hand makes you; the bird grows you keen. You clamp shut and do not let go. You grip the branch when the bird sleeps and the prey when it hunts. Hooked and deadly and never blunt, you are the eagle's grabbing fist, the part of a bird that no small creature wants to feel.
