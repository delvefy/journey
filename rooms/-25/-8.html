Manure

You are manure - the good brown muck the farmer spreads across the fields, straight from the cow shed and the stable. You smell strong and you look unlovely, but you are full of all the goodness the soil is hungry for. Forked out and dug in, you rot down and feed the roots, and up come fat cabbages and tall green corn. Earthy and humble and wonderfully useful, you are nature's way of giving back, turning yesterday's hay into next year's harvest.
