Avalanche

You are an avalanche - a whole mountainside of snow that breaks loose all at once and comes thundering down, faster than a train, burying everything in its path. No hand sends you; too much fresh snow on too steep a slope does, and one loud noise can set you off. Skiers fear you; the mountains rumble you. You move like a white flood and stop like a wall. Sudden and enormous and unstoppable, you are winter's heaviest landslide, the snow of a whole slope let go at once.
