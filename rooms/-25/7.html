Gargle

You are a gargle - the bubbling glugging growl you make tipping your head back with a mouthful of water and letting it churn at the back of your throat, the way grown-ups say to do for a sore one. No one finds you graceful; salt water and a tilted head do. You sound like a little blocked drain and make everyone laugh. Bubbly and gurgly and faintly silly, you are the noise a throat makes when it is rinsed, gargle-gargle, then spat away.
