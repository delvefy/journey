Wheeze

You are a wheeze - the thin whistling sound a chest makes when the breath cannot come easily. A bad cold brings you, a dusty room brings you, and a runner who has sprinted too far makes you at the finish. Each breath drags in and out with a faint high squeak, as if a tiny set of bagpipes lived inside the ribs. Tired and rattly and a little alarming, you are breathing made hard work, the sound of lungs labouring through a narrow space, longing for clear cool air.
