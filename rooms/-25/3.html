Breath

You are a breath - the air pulled down deep into the lungs and pushed slowly out again, over and over, all day and all night, without your ever having to think. No hand makes you; the body does, from the very first cry to the very last sigh. You fog the cold glass, you fill the balloon, you carry the words out. You catch in your throat when you are scared. Quiet and constant and easy to forget, you are the body's softest engine, the in-and-out that keeps everything alive.
