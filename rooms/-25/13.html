lilac

Lilac is a soft pale purple, gentle and a little bit grey, the colour of the spring blossom that shares its name. It is the shade of faded violets, of dusk just before dark, of a favourite cardigan washed a hundred times. Not loud like purple nor cold like blue, it is a quiet, kind, old-fashioned colour. Walk past a lilac bush in May and the smell is as soft and sweet as the colour itself.

North

White and crisp on a winter morning,
the grass all crunchy, your breath a cloud;
cold enough to nip your nose -
an icy, sparkly, chilly shroud.

East

The bright blue-green of a tropic sea,
a gemstone too, that colour cool;
between the blue and green it sits -
the shade of a warm lagoon pool.

South

A pale silver ray of light at night,
it slips through the curtains while you sleep;
not the sun's gold, but the moon's soft glow -
a thread of light, still and deep.

West

The last colour of the rainbow's arch,
a deep blue-purple, rich and dark;
a little flower of the same name too -
spring's small shy purple spark.
