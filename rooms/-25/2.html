Sleet

You are sleet - the miserable middle of winter weather, half rain and half snow, falling in cold wet lumps that sting the face and refuse to be either one thing or the other. No hand sends you; a sky too warm for snow and too cold for rain does. You melt the moment you land and turn the path to grey slush. Nobody likes you. The least loved weather there is, you are winter at its most half-hearted, too wet to play in and too cold to bear.
