moonbeam

A moonbeam is the moon's cool light reaching down in a single pale ray. It slips between the curtains and lays a silver stripe across your bed; it lies on the lake in a long shining road that no one can ever walk. Softer than sunlight, it casts the gentlest shadows and turns the night world to silver. You cannot catch it in your hands - close your fingers and they hold only the dark. Natural, weightless, and quietly cold.

North

A pale soft purple, almost pink,
the colour of spring flowers;
a bush of tiny scented blooms
that sweeten April hours.

East

A bright straight shaft of golden light
that pokes through cloud or trees;
it lands in a warm stripe on the floor
where the sleepy cat agrees.

South

From when the sun comes up at dawn
till evening shadows creep;
the bright awake half of the day,
before we go to sleep.

West

Look in the mirror, look in the pond,
and someone looks back too;
it copies every move you make -
a watery, glassy you.
