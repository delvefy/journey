Log

You are a log - a thick heavy chunk of tree trunk, sawn and split and stacked by the woodshed. Tossed on the fire you crackle and blaze and warm the whole room on a winter night. Left in the wood you grow soft and mossy, a home for beetles and a perch for a tired walker. Solid and weighty and smelling of sap, you are a piece of a fallen tree given a second life, either as a roaring fire's fuel or a mossy seat in a quiet green glade.
