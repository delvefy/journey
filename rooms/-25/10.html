Thud

You are a thud - the heavy dull thump a solid thing makes when it lands. No ring, no clatter, no bounce: just one flat heavy sound and then silence. A big book dropped on the floor, a sack of spuds set down, a body flopping onto a bed: all of them you. Low and dull and final, you are the sound of weight meeting ground, the soft heavy thump that says something solid has just landed, and isn't going to move again in a hurry.
