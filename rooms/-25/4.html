Downpour

You are a downpour - the heavy pelting rain that comes all at once and soaks a person to the skin before they can reach the door. No hand sends you; a fat dark cloud lets go all together. You drum on the umbrella, gush in the gutters, turn the street into a stream and the playground into a lake. Children watch you from the window, half longing to be out in you. Sudden and drenching and over almost as fast, you are the sky emptying its bucket in one go.
