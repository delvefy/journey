Buzz

You are a buzz - the busy droning hum a bee makes round a flower, a fly against the windowpane, a wasp you wish would leave. No tool makes you; tiny wings beating too fast to see do. You go on and on, a flat fuzzy note that sets the teeth on edge. A phone on a table does you too, juddering and rattling. Small and electric and never quite still, you are the sound of something tiny and winged and far too busy to land.
