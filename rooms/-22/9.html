Squint

You are a squint - the way the eyes screw half-shut to see when the light is too bright or the writing too small. You wrinkle the nose and pull the brows down and peer through the narrowest gap. No hand makes you; the dazzled eye does, all on its own, trying to sharpen the blur. You happen at noon on the beach and at the back of the class. You are the face the bright day pulls, a small struggle to see what the light has hidden.
