Well

You are a well - a deep round shaft dug straight down through the earth until it reaches cold clear water far below. A bucket on a rope goes down empty and comes up brimming, wound by a creaking handle. Long ago every village had one, and children threw in pennies and made a wish. Deep and dark and echoing, you are the way people reached the water hidden underground, a stone-lined tunnel down into the cool, where a dropped coin falls a long, long way before its tiny splash.
