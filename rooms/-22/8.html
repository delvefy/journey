Cough

You are a cough - the rough sudden bark that jumps out of a tickly chest or throat. A crumb down the wrong way brings you; a winter cold brings you again and again. You hold a hand to the mouth, give two sharp barks, and feel the tickle ease for a moment before it builds once more. At night you keep the whole house awake. You are the body trying to clear its own throat, a hard little gust thrown up from deep in the chest.
