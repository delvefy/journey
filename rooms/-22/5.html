Lightning

You are lightning - the jagged white flash that splits the storm sky in a blink, brighter than day for an instant. You leap from cloud to cloud or stab down to the ground, hot enough to split a tree. The thunder is only your noise, trailing a few seconds behind. You strike the tallest thing around, so the wise crouch low in a storm. You are the sky's sudden blinding spark, gone almost before the eye can catch it.
