Soggy

You are soggy - wet right through and gone soft and floppy with it. Cereal left too long in the milk, a cardboard box out in the rain, socks after a stamp in a puddle: all of them you. You squelch and sag and drip and will not hold their shape. Nobody likes you much - a soggy sandwich, a soggy field. Heavy and limp and dripping, you are what happens when a thing soaks up far more water than it was ever meant to hold.
