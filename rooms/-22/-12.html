Stomach

You are a stomach - the stretchy bag deep inside that takes each swallowed mouthful and churns it down to mush, where the food you eat begins its long journey through you. No hand makes you; the body grows you. You growl and gurgle when you are empty and ache when you are too full. Butterflies flutter in you when you are nervous. Soft and busy and never quite silent, you are the body's first kitchen, working away in the warm dark long after the plate is cleared.
