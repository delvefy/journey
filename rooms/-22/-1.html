Armpit

You are an armpit - the warm hollow under the arm where it joins the shoulder, ticklish and a little shy. You hold the thermometer when a child is poorly; you make a rude parping noise when a hand is cupped and the arm pumped down. You grow hair when a body grows up, and you sweat in the summer heat. You are the body's snug little cave, the most ticklish corner there is, hidden away until an arm goes up.
