Sunshine

You are sunshine - the bright warm light the sun spills over the world on a clear day. You wake the flowers, dry the puddles, and warm the cat asleep on the sill. You send children out to play with their sleeves rolled up, and you light the whole sky gold. You cannot be caught or saved for the rainy days. You are the kindest weather of all, the free and golden gladness the sun pours down on everyone alike.
