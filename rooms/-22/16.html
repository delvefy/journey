Tear

You are a tear - a single salty drop of water that wells up and rolls down a cheek when crying comes. Sadness brings you, but so does a hard laugh, a yawn, a sharp wind, or a sliced onion. You brim along the lashes, spill over, and drip off the chin. Wet and salty and full of feeling, you are the eye's own little flood, the drop that says without a word that a heart is hurting, or aching, or simply, sometimes, too full and overflowing with joy.
