Bud

You are a bud - a small green fist on the end of a twig, holding a folded flower no one has seen yet. No hand makes you; the warming spring does, swelling you fatter day by day until you split. You are all promise and no petal, a secret wrapped tight against the cold. Wait, and the sun will coax you open; until then you are the shut door of summer, packed and patient on the branch.
