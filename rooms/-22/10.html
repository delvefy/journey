Bang

You are a bang - the single sharp slap of sound that splits the quiet and stops every heart for a beat. No hand needs to mean you; a slammed door, a burst balloon, a far-off firework all make you alike. You arrive whole and gone in an instant, leaving only ringing ears and a jump in the chest. You are loudness with no warning, the world clapping its hands once, hard.
