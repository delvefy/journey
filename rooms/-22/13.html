Amber

You are amber - the warm golden-orange of honey and autumn and the middle traffic light that means get ready. You are also a real thing you can hold: old tree-sap that oozed out long ago and hardened over millions of years into a clear golden stone, sometimes with a tiny ancient insect trapped inside. Warm and glowing and old as the dinosaurs, you are a colour and a jewel at once, the golden in-between of red and yellow.
