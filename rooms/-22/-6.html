Hen

You are a hen - the round, clucking bird who rules the farmyard. You scratch the dirt for grubs, fuss over your fluffy chicks, and lay a warm brown egg in the straw each morning. You cannot fly far, but you flap and squawk a great deal about it. When a fox is near you raise the whole yard with your din. Bustling, busy, brown and feathered, you are the farmer's noisy alarm clock.
