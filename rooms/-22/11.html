Evening

You are evening - the soft slow end of the day, when the sun sits low and gold, the streetlights flicker on, and the world begins to settle. No hand brings you; the turning earth does, easing the bright day down toward the dark. You smell of cooking tea and feel like slippers and the telly warming up. The hour of winding down and drawing curtains, you are the long gentle goodbye the day makes before it hands the sky to night.
