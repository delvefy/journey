Shade

You are shade - the cool dark patch where something blocks the sun, a pool of welcome dimness on a blazing day. The big tree casts you on the grass; the wall throws you across the yard; the parasol spreads you over the deckchair. You are not as dark as night, just the sun's bright glare softened and turned cool. Cattle huddle in you at noon; readers seek you out. You are the gift of any shadow that keeps off the burning sun.
