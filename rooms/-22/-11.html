Kneecap

You are a kneecap - the small round shield of bone that floats at the front of the knee, guarding the joint where the leg folds in two. No hand made you; the body grew you to take the knocks, and take them you do, every trip and tumble onto the path. You slide a little under the skin when the leg straightens. A neat little bony lid over a busy hinge, you are the first thing to find the ground when a running child comes down.
