Hope

You are hope - the small bright thing that keeps you walking on through the dark. You are the candle that will not blow out, the held breath before good news, the certainty that tomorrow can be kinder than today. You cost nothing and weigh nothing, yet a whole life can lean on you. When everything else is gone, you are the last to leave. Quiet, stubborn, shining: you are the reason to go on.
