Grape

You are a grape - the little round fruit that grows in heavy bunches on the climbing vine, green or deep purple, smooth and bursting with juice. Eaten fresh you are a sweet cool snack; dried in the sun you wrinkle into a raisin; squashed and aged you turn into wine. You hang in clusters, pop in the mouth, and roll across the floor when one escapes the bowl. You are the vine's sweet jewel, the fruit that comes by the handful.
