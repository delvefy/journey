Dust

You are dust - the fine soft powder that settles silently on everything left still too long. You are bits of everything: old skin, dry soil, crumbs of the world ground small. No hand makes you; time does, sifting you down through the quiet light by the window. You rise in a puff when a book is opened, and dance in a sunbeam, and settle again. You are the patient grey blanket the world lays over whatever it has forgotten.
