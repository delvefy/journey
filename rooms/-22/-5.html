Cherry

You are a cherry - the little round red fruit that dangles in pairs from a long thin stalk, sweet and juicy with a hard stone hidden inside. You crown the top of a cake or an ice-cream sundae, the prize everyone wants. In blossom-time your tree foams pink and white. You stain the fingers and the lips a deep red. You are the small bright jewel of the orchard, the cheerful little fruit that says summer has come.
