Paw

You are a paw - the soft padded foot of a cat, a dog, a bear. Underneath you are leathery beans, warm and rough; on top, soft fur. You press silent prints in the snow, knead the lap of someone kind, and hide your claws until they're needed. A dog offers you for a shake; a cat taps you, curious, at a beetle. You are how an animal meets the ground - quiet, padded, and ready in a blink to show a hidden claw.
