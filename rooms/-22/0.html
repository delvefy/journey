Stone

You are a stone - the plain hard lump of rock that lies in the field and the riverbed, shaped by nothing but weather and time. You skip across the pond if thrown flat; you build the wall and pave the road; you sit cool and heavy in a child's pocket. You have no shine like a gem, no use like a tool - you simply are, solid and patient. You are the oldest plainest thing on earth, the bone of the ground itself.
