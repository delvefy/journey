Branch

You are a branch - the woody arm that grows out from the trunk of a tree. You hold up the leaves to the light, carry the birds' nests, and offer a low limb for a child to climb. In summer you are heavy with green; in autumn you let it all go; in winter you stand bare against the sky. Bend in a gale and you creak; snap and you fall with a crack. You are the tree reaching outward, an arm of living wood spread wide.
