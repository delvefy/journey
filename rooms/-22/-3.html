Plum

You are a plum - the soft round fruit with smooth dark-purple skin and sweet juicy flesh hiding a hard stone in the middle. You hang heavy on the tree in late summer, ready to drop. Dried in the sun you become a wrinkly prune; baked in a pie you turn rich and dark. Little Jack Horner pulled one from his Christmas pie. You are the plump sweet jewel of the orchard, the fruit that bruises if it is held too hard.
