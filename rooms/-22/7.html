Sweat

You are sweat - the salty water that beads on the skin when the body grows too warm. You break out on the runner's brow, the player's back, the worried palm before a test. As you dry in the breeze you cool the skin, which is the whole clever point of you. You taste of salt and smell of effort. You are the body's own way of fanning itself, a sheen of cool water it pours out to keep from boiling over.
