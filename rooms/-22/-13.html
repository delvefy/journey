Oyster

You are an oyster - a rough grey knobbly shell clamped tight shut on the seabed, ugly on the outside but hiding, just sometimes, a shining pearl within. No hand makes you; the cold sea does, and a grain of grit grows your pearl over years and years. You filter the water, you cling to the rocks, you snap shut at a touch. Lumpy and secretive and slow, you are the plainest shell with the loveliest secret, the one that turns a speck of grit into treasure.
