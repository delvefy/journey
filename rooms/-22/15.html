Raindrop

You are a raindrop - one small clear bead of water falling from a cloud. You begin high up, gather and grow, then let go and tumble down through the air, splattering on the windowpane or plopping into a puddle. Millions of you together make a shower; alone you are just one little splash. Round and clear and quick to fall, you slide down the glass in a wobbling race with your neighbours, and you soak into the thirsty earth to feed the rivers and the roots far below.
