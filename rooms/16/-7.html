Frisbee

You are a frisbee - a flat round disc that loves the open air. Flick your wrist and off you sail, spinning flat and steady across the whole park, hanging a moment before a hand or a leaping dog snatches you down. You skim, you curve, you wobble in the wind and land in the long grass. Moulded by a hand from bright plastic, light and smooth, you turn a sunny afternoon into a long game of throw and chase.
