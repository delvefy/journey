Perch

You are a perch - to settle and balance on a high narrow spot, the way a bird grips a branch or a telephone wire. You perch on the edge of a stool, on a wall, on the arm of a chair, just resting, ready to hop off again in a moment. Light and poised and never quite settled, you are the way birds and restless people alike come to rest without really sitting down, balanced up high with one eye on the next place to go.
