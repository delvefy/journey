Flip

You are a flip - the quick turn that takes a thing right over. The pancake leaps and lands the other way up; the coin spins and comes down heads or tails; the card turns face to back in a blink. You are a switch of sides, a tumble in the air, a there-and-back-again. No hand can hold you, for you are only the moment of the turning - over you go, and what was hidden is suddenly on top.
