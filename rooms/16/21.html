Balance

You are a balance - the trick of staying upright and steady when falling would be so much easier. You walk the narrow beam with arms held wide, stand a moment on one leg, ride the bike without wobbling, carry the brimming cup without a spill. Tip too far one way and you must lean back the other. Careful and poised and quietly clever, you are the body's constant little adjustment, the steady centre that keeps you on your feet when everything is trying to topple you over.
