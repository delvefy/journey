Fan

You are a fan - a set of spinning blades inside a cage, made to push the air about and cool a hot still room. Switch me on and I whirr to life, sending a steady breeze across your sweaty face. Some of me you hold and flap by hand, a folding paper fan; some of me hangs from the ceiling, turning slow. Whether by hand or motor, you stir the lazy air and chase the heat away.

North

A loop of springy wires on a handle,
the baker's busy tool;
beat the eggs up light and frothy -
round and round, keep cool.

East

A small round disc of shining metal,
silver, gold, or brown;
heads or tails, you flip me up -
I jingle in the town.

South

A little bed on four small wheels
you push along the street;
baby sleeps inside, all snug,
rocked gently on the seat.

West

A shiny box with two long slots,
you drop the bread inside;
press it down and wait - then POP!
hot toast on every side.
