Atlas

You are an atlas - a big heavy book packed with maps of the whole world. Turn your pages and you travel: here are the blue oceans, there the brown mountains, the green forests, the dotted lines of borders and the tiny names of faraway towns. Children pore over you, planning impossible journeys and hunting for the smallest island. Broad and colourful and full of everywhere, you hold the entire round Earth flattened out between two covers, ready to be explored from the comfort of a rug on the floor.
