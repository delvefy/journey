Month

You are a month - a chunk of time about four weeks long, one of the twelve that make a year. You have a name - January, June, December - and a number of days, and a whole feeling: the cold dark of one, the warm long evenings of another. A birthday lives in one of you; the school holidays in others. No hand can hold you - you are time bundled up into a page of the calendar, turned over, one by one, as the year rolls by.

North

The little high sound small birds make,
cheep-cheep up in the tree;
at dawn they fill the air with it -
a ___, so merrily.

East

From when the sun comes up till bed,
with breakfast, lunch, and tea;
Monday, Tuesday, and the rest -
a ___ for you and me.

South

The tiniest tick of time there is,
just one beat of the clock;
sixty of them make a minute -
a ___, tick-tock, tick-tock.

West

Seven days all in a row,
Monday through to Sunday too;
it comes and goes and comes again -
a ___ for me and you.
