Stumble

You are a stumble - the trip that almost becomes a fall. Your toe catches the kerb, your foot lands wrong, and suddenly you are pitching forward, arms windmilling, trying to get your feet back under you. Half the time you save it with a clumsy hop; half the time you go down. You happen in the dark, on the stairs, over the cat. You are the body caught off balance, that lurching half-second between walking and the floor.
