Rub

You are a rub - the back-and-forth press of your hand on a thing. You rub your sleepy eyes in the morning, you rub your hands to warm them, you rub the lamp and hope a genie comes. You rub a sore knee better, rub the dog's tummy, rub a smudge until it fades. To rub is to press and slide at once, over and over, until something is warmer, cleaner, or simply soothed.
