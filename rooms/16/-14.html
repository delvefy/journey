Kazoo

You are a kazoo - the silly little horn that makes no sound at all unless you hum into it, and then it buzzes your humming into a comic droning tune. There are no keys to learn and no notes to read; you just go doo-doo-doo down the tube and out comes the wobbly noise. You turn up at parties and parades, the cheapest toy in the band. You are music made easy, a buzz in a tube, the merriest racket a hum can make.
