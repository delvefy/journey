Screw

You are a screw - a little spike of steel with a spiral thread winding all the way down, and a slotted head for the driver to grip. Turn me round and round and I bite my own way into the wood, pulling tight, gripping far harder than a nail. Made by clever hands to hold things fast and yet come out again when needed. Small, sharp, and stubborn, you fasten the world together.

North

Three brass pins upon a block,
at the end of a long flex;
push me in the wall to wake
the lamp, the screen, what's next.

East

My long flat blade has many teeth,
but I do not bite or chew;
push and pull me through a plank -
I cut the wood in two.

South

A bundle of looping wires
with a handle you can hold;
beat the eggs up light and foamy,
so the cook has often told.

West

A fat metal pin with a threaded side,
I fix two beams up tight;
twist a nut upon my end -
I hold with all my might.
