Wiggle

You are a wiggle - the quick little side-to-side shake of something that won't hold still. You are the worm on the path, the loose tooth, the toes inside the socks, the puppy's whole back end when it's happy. You are jelly on a plate and a dance with no real steps. No hand can hold you, for you ARE the not-holding-still - a small wobbly squirm of pure fidget, here and there and never quite where it was.
