Tilt

You are a tilt - the lean of a thing tipped over to one side, no longer flat or upright but slanted, the picture frame knocked crooked, the boat heeling in the wind, the head cocked to listen. A nudge or a slope makes you. You are halfway to falling but not falling yet, the world set at an angle. A small steady lopsidedness, you are what happens when one side dips and the other lifts, and level gives way to a lean.
