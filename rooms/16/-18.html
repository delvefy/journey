Backpack

You are a backpack - a bag with two straps that rides on the shoulders so the hands stay free. You carry the schoolbooks, the packed lunch, the tent and the torch for the hike. Made by hands - cut, sewn, zipped, and buckled - you have pockets within pockets and never seem quite full. Heavier at the end of the day, you sag with everything that got stuffed in. You are the shell a traveller wears, all their world strapped snugly to their back.
