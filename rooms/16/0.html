Crane

You are the tall machine on the building site, a long steel arm against the clouds. Made by clever hands from girders and cable and hook, you lift what no one could carry - whole beams of steel, swung slowly up and set in place. All day you reach into the sky, raising the tower floor by floor. Down below the diggers bite the earth; you build the heights.

North

Count all your fingers,
then count them once more;
two tens added together -
and it's also called a score.

East

I have one heavy iron head
and a long straight wooden neck;
I bang the nails all the way in
with a bash and a knock and a peck.

South

Push my pins into the wall
and the power starts to flow;
pull me out, the lamp goes dark -
out of the socket I go.

West

The great yellow machine
with a long arm and a toothed steel jaw;
it scoops out holes for pipes and homes -
the deepest pit you ever saw.
