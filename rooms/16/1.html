20

You are twenty - two tens counted up, a score. Ten fingers, then ten fingers more. You are how old the big cousins seem, the number on a folded note, the steps from the door to the gate. Not a thing to hold but a count, a place along the endless line of numbers, climbing by ones toward a hundred. Say me, and a small heap becomes a known and certain amount.

North

Ten more than twenty,
three tens in a stack;
the days in June, or April -
how many? Count them back.

East

I start with a question
and end with a laugh;
'Why did the chicken...?' -
you know the second half.

South

A tall machine that lifts the steel
on every building site;
its long arm swings against the clouds
and sets the beams at height.

West

Half of a dozen,
the dots on a die's high face;
one less than seven, one more than five -
how many in this place?
