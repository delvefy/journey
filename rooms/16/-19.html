Map

You are a map - a flat drawn picture of the land seen from high above, with roads and rivers and towns marked in tiny lines and dots. Hands draw you and fold you into a pocket, and you unfold huge across the kitchen table. A finger traces a way across you from here to there. An X may mark a treasure. You shrink the whole wide world down small enough to hold, and show the lost which way to turn.
