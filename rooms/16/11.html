Chirp

You are a chirp - the bright little sound a small bird or a cricket makes, over and over, a tiny cheep that fills the morning hedge. No hand makes you; a beak or a pair of rubbing legs does, a sound so small and so cheerful you barely notice the air fill up with it. You cannot be touched, only heard - one chirp, then a hundred, the whole dawn chorus made of you. You are the smallest, happiest scrap of sound there is.
