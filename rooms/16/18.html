Topple

You are a topple - the slow tip past the point of no return, when a tall thing leans too far and goes crashing over. The tower of bricks, the stacked tins, the wobbly stool: they sway, they hang for a heartbeat, and then down they come. No hand need push you; gravity does, once the balance is lost. You are the moment the standing thing gives up standing. You are the last little lean before everything ends up on the floor.
