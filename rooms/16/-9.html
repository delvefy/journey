Boot

You are a boot - a big strong shoe that climbs right up the leg. You stomp through puddles and mud without a care, you keep the foot warm and dry in the snow, you lace up tight for a long walk. There are wellies for rain, walking boots for hills, football boots with studs. Stitched and soled by a hand, sturdy and waterproof, you are the shoe a foot wears when the weather turns rough and the ground turns wild.
