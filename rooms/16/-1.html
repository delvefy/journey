Plug

You are a plug - a small block on the end of a wire, three brass pins set in a row. Push me into the wall and the hidden river of power runs up through my cable to wake the lamp, the kettle, the screen. Pull me out and it all goes dark and quiet. Made by careful hands to be safe, you are the little gate between the wall and everything that hums and glows.

North

I am the tallest thing on site,
a steel arm reaching high;
I hoist the heavy loads by hook
and swing them through the sky.

East

A little spike of shining steel,
flat head and a point so fine;
hammer me deep into the wood
to hold the boards in line.

South

Like a nail but with a twist -
a spiral round my shaft;
turn me with a screwdriver
to grip the wood held fast.

West

On the wall I sit and wait,
a little plastic lever;
press me up to wake the light,
press me down - it's over.
