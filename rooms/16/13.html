Dip

You are a dip - to lower a thing just a little way in, and lift it straight back out. A toe goes into the cold pool to test it; a biscuit goes into the tea; a brush goes into the paint. The same word names a little hollow in the road, or a quick swim before tea. You are a small made motion, quick and shallow, never the whole plunge. In and out, hardly wet - that is the whole of you.
