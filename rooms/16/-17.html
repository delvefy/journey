Tape

You are tape - the long thin strip that comes wound on a roll. One kind of you is sticky, and holds the torn page, the wrapped gift, the box bound shut. Another kind is printed with little numbers, and you stretch out along a shelf or round a waist to measure it. Either way you live coiled up small and pull out long. You are length you can keep in a pocket, ready to bind a thing or to tell exactly how big it is.
