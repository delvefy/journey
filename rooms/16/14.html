Wish

You are a wish - the secret hope you whisper to a star, a candle, a coin dropped down a well. You shut your eyes, you cross your fingers, you say nothing out loud lest it not come true. You cannot make a thing happen, only long for it with all your heart. The first star of the evening hears a thousand of you. You are the gentlest kind of wanting, the dream you dare to ask the world to grant - half a prayer, half a secret, all longing.
