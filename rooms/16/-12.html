Tambourine

You are a tambourine - a little round drum with no top, hung all about its wooden rim with pairs of tiny jingling discs. A hand made you; a hand shakes you or smacks you against a knee, and you ring and rattle and thump all at once. You can be held high, spun on a finger, passed round the circle. The simplest, jolliest member of the band, you need no skill at all - just a happy arm and a sense of the beat. You are pure rhythm you can rattle in one hand.
