Thumb

You are a thumb - the short strong finger that stands apart from the rest. You press, you grip, you hold the spoon and the crayon tight; you point up to say 'good!' or hide in a tiny fist. A baby sucks you; a hitch-hiker holds you out; you squash down the lid and steady the ruler. Small and stubby and made of you, the hand would drop near everything it tries to hold.
