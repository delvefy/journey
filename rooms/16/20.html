Teeter

You are a teeter - the wobbly rocking of something balanced right on the brink, tipping this way, then that, not quite falling but never quite still, the stack of bricks one too high, the toe on the kerb's edge. No one means you; a wobble and a too-far lean do. You hold a breathless moment between safe and over. Wobbly and tippy and heart-in-mouth, you are the teeter of a thing deciding, second by second, whether or not to fall.
