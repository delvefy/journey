Hourglass

You are an hourglass - two rounded glass bulbs joined by a slender waist, with fine sand trickling slowly from the top into the bottom. Turn me over and the sand begins to run; when the last grain falls, the time is up. Cooks use a little one to boil an egg; pirates in stories keep great ones. Patient and silent and slowly emptying, you measure time you can actually watch, grain by grain, sliding away through your narrow glass neck.
