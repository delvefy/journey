Pole

You are a pole - a long tall round bar, stood up straight and tall. The flag flies from the top of you, the tent leans on you, the firefighter slides down you, the bean plant climbs you. You hold up the washing line and the telephone wires. Cut and smoothed by a hand, straight from end to end, you are the simple upright that props and holds and lifts things high - just by standing, stubbornly, straight up.
