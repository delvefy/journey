Slice

You are a slice - one thin flat piece, cut clean from a larger whole. A slice of bread, a slice of cake, a slice of pizza, a slice of apple. The knife comes down and there you are, a single even portion, ready to be eaten or shared. You are the act of cutting and the piece it makes, both at once. To slice is to share, to turn one big thing into many fair pieces.
