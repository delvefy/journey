Globe

You are a globe - a round ball model of the whole Earth, painted with all its blue seas and patchwork countries, mounted on a little stand so it can spin. A finger gives you a push and the world whirls; a finger stops you, and you land on somewhere far. You sit on the classroom shelf gathering dust between geography lessons. Round and tilted and slowly turning, you are the whole planet shrunk small enough to spin with one fingertip and dream of faraway places.
