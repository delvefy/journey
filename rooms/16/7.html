Wave

You are a wave - the friendly swing of a hand through the air. Lift it, swish it side to side, and across a whole field a friend knows you have seen them. You say hello, you say goodbye, you call a taxi, you shoo a fly. You are also the long roll of water that humps up and curls and crashes on the sand. No hand can hold you, for you are only motion - a greeting and a farewell, written for a moment on the air.
