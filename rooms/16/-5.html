Pram

You are a pram - the little bed on wheels that carries a baby out into the world. Four wheels, a deep cosy hood, a handle for a grown-up to push. The baby lies inside, tucked under blankets, watching the sky go by, lulled to sleep by the bumping of the path. You roll to the shops, round the park, along the street. A first carriage, a rolling cradle, a small one's first set of wheels.
