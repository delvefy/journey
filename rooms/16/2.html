30

You are thirty - three tens counted up, ten more than twenty. The days in June and September, the number of years that feels grown-up and not yet old. Halfway from twenty to forty, a tidy round amount. Not a thing to hold but a place far along the counting line, three full handfuls of ten. Say me, and a large heap is named and known.

North

Two more than ten,
a dozen eggs in a box;
the months in a whole year,
the hours round the clock.

East

Words set to a tune
that you sing out loud and clear;
'Twinkle, twinkle, little star' -
a ___ for all to hear.

South

Two tens make me, a score,
count your fingers, then once more;
ten more than ten I stand -
how many? Hold up every paw.

West

Twice four, or two fours,
the legs a spider wears;
one more than seven, one less than nine -
how many climb the stairs?
