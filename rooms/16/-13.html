Cello

You are a cello - the big brother of the violin, too large to tuck under a chin, so you stand on a spike between the player's knees. A bow drawn slow across your strings brings out a voice low and warm, the deepest and most singing of the strings. You are wrought of curved wood and four taut strings, built by patient hands. Sit you down, lean you close, and you hum like a great wooden throat.
