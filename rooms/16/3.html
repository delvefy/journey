12

You are twelve - a dozen, ten and two more. The eggs in a box, the months in a year, the hours on the clock face, the buns in a baker's batch. A friendly, useful number that splits into halves and thirds and quarters with no leftovers. Not a thing to hold but a tidy little count, the age when childhood starts to turn. Say me, and a dozen things are gathered into one neat word.

the ___ of a happy life.

East

A little run of musical notes,
that you hum or whistle or play;
'da-da-dum' stuck in your head -

South

Three tens counted in a heap,
ten more than twenty too;
the days in June, the days in April -
how many? Up to you.

West

The legs upon a table,
the wheels upon a car;
one more than three, one less than five -
how many there are?
