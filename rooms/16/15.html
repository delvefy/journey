Drum

You are a drum - not the barrel of skin but the beating itself, the tap-tap-tap of fingers on a desk, the thud of a heart in a frightened chest, the patter of rain drumming on the roof. You are rhythm laid down by knocking, a steady pulse you can feel as much as hear. The soldier marches to you; the bored child drums on the table; the rabbit drums the ground to warn of danger. You are sound made of beats, the plain heartbeat of every tune.
