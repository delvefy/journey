Second

You are a second - the smallest tick of time you can count, one beat of the clock. You are how long it takes to blink, to clap, to say a word; sixty of you make a minute. A whole rocket countdown is built of you - three, two, one! You are gone the instant you arrive. No one can hold you, only count you and watch you slip past, tick by tick by tick - the quick small heartbeat the whole of time is built from.
