Plop

You are a plop - the fat round noise a pebble makes when it drops into the pond and is gone. No one builds you; gravity and water make you together, once, and then you are only rings spreading out. You are the frog off the bank, the stone down the well, the last drip from the tap into a full sink. A small wet word for a small wet fall, here and then swallowed.

East

The deep loud roll that the thunder makes,
or a drum the big hand hits;
it shakes the floor and fills the room -

South

Water tipping side to side
in a bucket as you walk;
it climbs the wall and slaps back down -
a heavy, sleepy ___.

West

Stir the water round and round
and watch it start to spin;
it turns in circles down the drain -
a ___ that pulls you in.
