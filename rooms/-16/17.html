Slosh

You are a slosh - the heavy swishing sound water makes when it rolls about in a container. The half-full bucket sloshes as you carry it; the puddle sloshes under a stamping welly; the drink sloshes in a tummy after gulping too fast. You are wetter and weightier than a swish, a great lazy slap of water against the sides. You spill if no one is careful. You are the noise of water that cannot keep still, rocking and slapping from side to side.
