Grasshopper

You are a grasshopper - a green bug of the long summer grass, with folded springy legs built for leaping. Step near and you ping away, twice your height and gone, landing somewhere new with a tick. On warm still evenings you rub your legs and sing that dry buzzing chirp that fills the meadow. Grown by no hand, light in the palm for the half-second you can catch one, you are summer turned into a small green spring.
