Navy

You are navy - a deep dark blue, almost black, the colour of the sea at dusk and the night sky just before the stars. Sailors wear you on their coats and caps, which is how you got your name. Calm and serious and dependable, you are the blue of school uniforms and smart suits, darker and quieter than any bright sky-blue. You are the colour of deep water and deep evening, steady and grave.
