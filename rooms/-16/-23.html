ledge

A ledge is a narrow flat shelf where a cliff or a wall steps out for a moment before carrying on. A bird builds its nest there, safe from the fox below; a climber rests there, heels on stone and back to the rock, not daring to look down. Sometimes it is no wider than your two feet placed side by side. It is the one flat place on a steep face - a small held breath of level ground.

North

The very tip-top of a mountain high,
the sharpest, highest, pointiest place;
above the clouds where the eagles fly -
the climber's prize, the summit's face.

East

A precious stone of deepest red,
it sparkles on a royal ring;
red as blood and hard as glass -
a treasured, glinting, costly thing.

South

Golden orange, hard and clear,
I'm tree-sap turned to stone with time;
sometimes a fly is trapped inside -
a jewel made of ancient slime.

West

A baby dog with floppy ears,
I chew your shoes and wag my tail;
I tumble, yip, and lick your face -
soft and clumsy, I never fail.
