Sparkle

You are a sparkle - the dancing scatter of tiny bright flashes that play on a thing. You leap from the tinsel on the tree, the frost on the morning grass, the ripples of a sunny sea, the glitter on a card. You live in happy eyes too, when someone is full of joy. No hand can hold you, for you are only light caught and tossed back, here and gone and here again - the world winking and twinkling and showing off.
