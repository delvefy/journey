Puddle

You are a puddle - the little pool of rainwater that gathers in a dip of the pavement after a shower. You mirror the grey sky and the passing legs, until a child in wellies leaps in and shatters you to splashes. You are shallow and brief; the sun drinks you up by afternoon and you are gone, leaving only a dark stain that dries to nothing. You are rain caught for an hour in a hollow, the smallest, most jumpable-in water there is.
