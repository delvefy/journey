Slosh

You are a slosh - the wet, sloppy sound of water swung from side to side. Carry a brimming bucket and you slop over the rim; tip the bath and the wave runs slapping back. Not a splash, not a drip, but the heavy slap-and-roll of water that cannot keep still. You are made of motion and noise, never of anything you can hold - the song water sings when it is jostled and spilled.

North

The white and bubbly froth that rides
the top of the breaking wave;
or the soap suds in your bath -
a thousand bubbles brave.

East

Press two fingers to your wrist
and feel the steady beat;
it taps in time with your heart -
thud, thud, soft and neat.

South

Where the sea lies down to rest
against the patient land;
a strip of shells and seaweed,
and miles of golden sand.

West

When a wave smacks on the rocks
it flies up, fine and white;
a mist of flung sea-water -
it wets you in its flight.
