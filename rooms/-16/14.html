Thaw

You are a thaw - the slow gentle warming that loosens the grip of winter. The icicles begin to drip, the snowman slumps and shrinks, the frozen pond goes soft and dark at the edges. The world that was hard and white turns wet and brown and runs away in little streams. You cannot be touched, only felt as the air goes mild. You are the first kindness of spring, the great unfreezing, the day the cold lets go and everything begins to drip and melt and trickle off.
