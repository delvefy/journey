Island

You are an island - a piece of land with water all the way around. No road reaches you; only a boat or a long swim will do. You might be a tiny dot of sand with one palm tree, or a green country big enough for towns, but always the sea draws your edge. Castaways dream of you, pirates bury treasure on you, and the tide hems you in. You are land that stands alone, a little world cut off and ringed by blue.
