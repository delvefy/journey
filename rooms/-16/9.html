Sleep

You are sleep - the deep warm rest that folds over you at the end of the day. Eyes grow heavy, the book slips from the hand, and down you sink into the dark and quiet, breathing slow, while dreams drift past. The whole tired world does it: the dog, the bird, the baby in its cot. No one can hold you off forever, and no one can keep you when you go - you are the gentle tide that carries everyone, every night, safely away till morning.
