Canyon

You are a canyon - a deep narrow crack cut into the rock over millions of years by a patient river, with sheer red walls rising on either side and the thin glint of water far, far below. No hand digs you; the slow river does. Your echo bounces back twice. Eagles ride the warm air up your cliffs. Deep and steep and ancient, you are the river's great carving, a chasm so vast a shout takes a moment to come back across it.
