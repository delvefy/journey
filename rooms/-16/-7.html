Kiwi

You are a kiwi - a round brown bird the size of a chicken, with no proper wings at all. You snuffle through the dark forest floor on stout legs, poking your long thin beak deep in the leaf mould for worms, sniffing them out with nostrils right at the tip. You hide all day and bustle all night, and lay an egg enormous for your size. Grown by no hand, shy and odd, you are a bird that gave up the sky for the safe dark ground.
