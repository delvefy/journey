Heart

You are a heart - the fist of muscle behind the ribs that drums all your life without ever stopping to rest. You speed up when you run or when you are scared, and slow to a soft thud when you sleep. Press an ear to a chest and you can hear it. You are natural, grown not made, the tireless engine that sends the warm blood round and round. Of all the body's busy parts, you are the one that may never once take a break.
