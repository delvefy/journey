Throat

You are a throat - the narrow passage at the front of the neck, where the food goes down and the voice comes up. No hand made you; you grew, a pink tube doing two jobs at once. You can be sore, tickly, gargled, cleared with an ahem. You swallow the dinner, you carry the song, you go dry when you are nervous and lumpy when you are sad. A shout tears out of you; a hard word sticks in you. You are the busy gateway between the mouth and the rest.
