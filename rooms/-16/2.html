Foam

You are foam - a froth of tiny bubbles, white and light as a cloud. You ride the top of a breaking wave, you crown a mug of fizzing pop, you pile up in the bath from a squeeze of soap. Made of nothing but air caught in water, you cannot really be held - scoop me up and I melt to a damp white nothing in your palm. You are the sea's lace and the bath's quiet snow.

East

Just a thin little thread of water,
running slow and small;
drip by drip down the windowpane,
the gentlest flow of all.

South

The sloppy sound of water swung
from side to side in a pail;
it slaps and rolls and spills right out
over the bucket's rail.

the water leaps up high!
a great wet smack that soaks your socks -
