Back

You are a back - the broad flat rear of the body, from the shoulders down to the seat, with the long knobbly spine running down its middle. No hand made you; you grew, and you are the one part you can never quite see or scratch yourself. You can be patted, rubbed when it aches, ridden piggyback, turned on someone in a huff. You carry the heavy bag and bend over the work. Strong and patient and out of sight, you are the body's broad faithful behind.
