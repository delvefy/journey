Peace

You are peace - the deep calm that falls when the fighting stops and no one is angry any more. You settle on a house after a quarrel is mended, on a land when the war is done, on a heart that has stopped its worrying. You cannot be touched, only felt, a wide soft quiet that lets everyone breathe. You are the opposite of every shout and blow, the gentle settling of the world back into its rest.
