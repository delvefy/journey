Wish

You are a wish - the secret hopeful want you make and tell no one, or it won't come true. You ride out on the breath that blows the candles, on the eyelash on a fingertip, on the coin dropped in the well, on the first star of the night. You are pure wanting, shaped into a single silent hope. No hand can hold you, and you may never come true - but the world feels kinder for the making of you.
