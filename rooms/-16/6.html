Temper

You are a temper - the quick hot rush that rises when things will not go right. You are the slammed door, the stamped foot, the face gone red, the words you wish you hadn't said. You flare up in a flash and burn yourself out just as fast, leaving a sorry hush behind. No one can hold you or hand you over; you are a weather of the heart, all thunder, here and gone. Count to ten and let me cool.
