Lake

You are a lake - the great wide sheet of still fresh water cupped in a hollow of the land, ringed with reeds and trees. Boats drift across your calm; swans glide on your glassy face; the mountains lean down to mirror themselves in you. You are bigger than a pond and stiller than a river, fed by streams that trickle in and rest. In winter you freeze hard enough to skate on. You are the land's broad blue eye, holding the sky.
