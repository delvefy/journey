Trick

You are a trick - a clever bit of mischief or skill meant to fool or to delight: a magician's sleight of hand, a prank on a friend, a dog that rolls over on command. No hand can hold you; a quick mind makes you, and the fun is all in being fooled. You cannot be touched, only played or seen through. Trick or treat at the door, a card up the sleeve, a coin behind the ear - you are cleverness having a little harmless fun.
