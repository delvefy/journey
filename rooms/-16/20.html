Squish

You are a squish - the soft wet squashing of something giving way under a press, the mud sucking at a boot, the ripe tomato bursting in a fist, the sponge wrung out. No one makes you alone; soft wet stuff and a squeeze do. You ooze out the sides and feel deliciously horrible between the fingers. Children cannot stop doing you to anything squashy. Soft and wet and squelchy, you are the gleeful gross feeling of pressing something soft until it gives.
