Ear

You are an ear - one on each side of the head, a curl of soft skin and cartilage that gathers in the sounds. Through you come music and voices, birdsong and barking, the whispered secret and the shouted name. You pop on a high hill and ache in the cold wind; you wear the earring and hold up the glasses. Quiet and patient, you are the body's open door for every sound the world can make.

North

On the end of your foot I sit,
ten in a wriggly row;
the big one leads, the rest all follow -
in socks and shoes we go.

East

A sea creature in two hard shells
that snap tight shut for a home;
buried deep in the sand it hides -
it never has to roam.

South

A little green bug in the long grass
that leaps up high and far;
it rubs its legs to make a chirp -
'zree-zree', both near and far.

West

A tiny little branch of a tree,
thin and dry and brown;
snap me underfoot - I crack! -
I litter the forest ground.
