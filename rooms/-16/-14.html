Flipper

You are a flipper - the broad flat paddle a seal or a penguin or a turtle swims with, a limb gone wide and webby for the water. On land you are clumsy and you flop, but the moment the swimmer slips into the sea you become a wing, sweeping it along smooth and fast. The diver straps rubber ones on too, to kick the harder. You are the paddle a sea creature wears instead of a hand, made for pushing the whole wide ocean behind it.
