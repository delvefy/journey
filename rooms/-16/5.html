Hush

You are the hush - the soft fall of quiet when a noisy room goes still. Not quite silence: a held breath, a finger to the lips, the calm just before something begins. Grown-ups make you with a long shhh when the baby sleeps. You settle over a class, a wood, a church; sound tiptoes away and leaves you behind. A gentle quiet, asking only to be kept a little while.

North

Lose me and you stamp and shout,
I flare up hot and red;
count to ten and let me cool -
a flash of rage instead.

East

I always want a little more,
I never feel quite full;
I grab the biggest slice of cake -
a hunger, mean and cruel.

South

A deep dark blue, like sea at night,
the colour sailors wear;
between the bright sky-blue and black,
a shade beyond compare.

West

When you don't get your own way
you cross your arms and pout;
you will not talk and will not play -
a grumpy, silent bout.
