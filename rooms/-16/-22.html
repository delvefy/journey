Peak

You are a peak - the sharp pointed top of a mountain, the highest point of all, where the snow lies white the whole year round and the air is thin and bitterly cold. Climbers toil up for days to stand on you and plant a flag, and from up here the whole world spreads out tiny below, with clouds drifting past beneath your tip. Lofty and proud and hard to reach, you are the mountain's crown, drawing every climber's eye to the very top of the sky.
