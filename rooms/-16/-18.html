Valley

You are a valley - the low sheltered land that cradles between two ranges of hills, where the river runs and the farms lie green. Carved out slowly by water and ice over ages, you gather the streams and the morning mist down in your hollow. No hand made you; the patient water did. Sheep dot your slopes and a village nestles at your foot. You are the gentle bottom of the land, the calm green between the heights, where everything rolls down to rest.
