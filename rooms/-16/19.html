Splat

You are a splat - the flat wet smack of something soft thrown hard and bursting where it lands, the dropped tomato, the snowball on a coat, the paint flung at the page. No hand makes you alone; a soft wet thing and a sudden stop do. You spread out in a star with a single sticky slap and then you are still. Messy and sudden and oddly satisfying, you are the noise and the shape of something squashy meeting something flat at speed.
