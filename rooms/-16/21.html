Mush

You are mush - soft squashed pulp, mashed right down till it holds no shape at all. Overcooked peas, a dropped banana, paper left out in the rain: all of them go to mush. You squelch between fingers and slide off a fork and cannot stand up for yourself in the slightest. Babies eat you, puddles make you of fallen leaves. Soft and soggy and shapeless, you are what becomes of anything mashed and softened past all firmness, a gloopy heap that was once something solid.
