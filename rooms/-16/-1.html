Hand

You are a hand - five fingers and a thumb, a warm flat palm, the body's clever tool. You grip and point, you wave and clap, you make a fist or open wide. You hold the spoon, the pencil, the other hand you love. No machine yet matches all you do. Made by no maker, grown from the bone, you are the part of us that reaches out and takes hold of the world.

North

The long wet edge where land meets sea,
all pebbles, sand, and shell;
the waves run up and slide away -
I know this rim so well.

East

The thin flat blade upon a fish
that steers it through the deep;
a shark shows one above the wave -
it cuts the water steep.

South

In the middle of your face I sit,
with two round holes below;
I sniff the dinner, smell the rose,
and drip when cold winds blow.

West

The part of your face that opens and shuts
to chew and chomp and chat;
your teeth are set along its edge -
a ___, imagine that.
