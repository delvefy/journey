Slime

You are slime - cold, wet, slippery goo that stretches and drips and oozes between the fingers. A snail leaves a shining trail of you; children mix up bowls of it to play with, all wobbly and stringy and gloriously gross. You slip and slither and cling to everything. Squelchy and stretchy and wonderfully horrid, you are the stuff of slug-trails and home-made gunk, the gooiest, drippiest, most fun-to-squish substance there is, oozing slowly off the edge of the table while everyone squeals.
