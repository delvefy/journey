Story

You are a story - a string of happenings, made up or true, with a beginning, a middle, and an end: a hero, a problem, a way out. No hand can hold you; a voice or a book carries you, and a good one pulls you in and won't let go till the last page. You cannot be touched, only told and listened to. Once upon a time, and they all lived happily ever after - you are how we make sense of the world, one tale at a time.
