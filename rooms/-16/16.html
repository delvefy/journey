Freeze

You are freeze - what the cold does to water, turning it hard and still as glass. When you grip the pond it grows a lid of ice; when you grip the puddle it cracks underfoot; when you grip the washing on the line it goes stiff as a board. Your breath turns white and your fingers go numb. The deeper the cold, the harder you bite. You are the work of the frost, the great stilling-and-hardening that winter brings.
