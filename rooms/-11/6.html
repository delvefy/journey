Dread

You are the slow cold fear of something bad you know is coming - the dentist tomorrow, the test on Friday, the dark walk home. No hand makes you; the worried mind builds you out of waiting. You sit in the stomach like a stone and sour every hour until the dreaded thing arrives - which is often not so bad after all.

North

The dim grey half-dark of a cloudy day,
or the low sad heaviness of mind;
no sun, no cheer, just shadowy grey -
a dull and dreary kind.

East

A sudden scare that makes you jump,
when something leaps out: 'BOO!';
your heart bangs, your breath catches in -
it shoots right through you.

South

What you have at the fair or game,
all laughter, play, and run;
the opposite of dull hard work -
a good time in the sun.

West

Something you never saw coming at all -
a party, a gift, a 'BOO!';
'Oh!' you gasp, your eyes go wide -
a shock that's nice, for you.
