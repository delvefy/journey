Blaze

You are a great fierce fire, all roaring gold and leaping high - the bonfire, the burning house, the forest gone up in flame. No hand need start you; a spark, a dry wind, and you spread and climb and devour. You light the night for miles and throw a heat that drives folk back. Bigger and wilder than any single flame, you are fire grown huge and hungry.

North

The huge deep sound a lion makes
to warn you from its den;
the crowd, the engine, the waterfall -
a thunder loud as ten.

East

A bright hot tongue that dances up
from candle, match, or coal;
yellow, orange, blue at its root -
it flickers, warm and whole.

South

A tickle on the skin that nags
until you scratch the spot;
a midge's bite, a woolly sleeve -
it bothers quite a lot.

West

Not the burn of fire, just gentle heat -
the sun upon your face,
the glow beside the hearth at night,
a soft and cosy grace.
