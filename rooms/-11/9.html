Green

You are the colour of growing things - grass and leaf and moss, the first blade of spring, the deep shade of the summer wood. No hand made you; the living world wears you. Cool and calm and everywhere, the colour of go, of envy, of life itself. Look across a field in June: all that green, breathing.

North

The colour of clouds before the storm,
of stone and a winter sky;
not black, not white, but in between -
a dull and misty dye.

East

A quick small flash on a coin or blade,
a sharp bright wink of light;
there, then gone, on metal and eye -
it catches, sudden, bright.

South

The bright glad leap of joy inside
when something lovely comes;
a treat, a friend, a sunny day -
it fizzes up and hums.

West

The colour of mud and bark and bears,
of chocolate and of toast;
of autumn leaves and a conker's shine -
the earthy one we like the most.
