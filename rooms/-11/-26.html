swordfish

A swordfish is built like a thrown spear - a long sleek silver body and a great flat blade of a nose sticking straight out in front, sharp as the sword it is named for. It is one of the fastest fish in the sea, slicing through the water after smaller fish and slashing with that bill to stun them. Sailors tell of swordfish driving their swords clean into the hull of a boat. Quick, fierce, and pointed, it hunts the wide blue deep.

North

Five arms spread out in a star,
I creep the seabed slow as can be;
lose an arm and grow it back -
a living star beneath the sea.

East

Eight long arms with suckers all over,
soft and squishy, no bones at all;
I squirt black ink and change my colour -
the cleverest thing by the seabed wall.

South

A hard round shell upon my back,
I paddle slow through the warm blue sea;
I lay my eggs upon the sand -
old and wise and slow, that's me.

West

Long soft ears and a fluffy tail,
I hop through the field and nibble the grass;
I live in a burrow down a hole -
I twitch my nose as you walk past.
