Gloom

You are the dim grey half-dark of a sunless day, and the low heavy sadness of a sunless mood. No hand makes you; thick cloud, or a heavy heart, lets you settle. The room goes dim; the spirits sink. Everything seems duller, greyer, harder under you. A candle or a kind word lifts you.

North

The bright warm gladness of something lovely -
a treat, a sweet surprise;
'oh, how lovely!' your whole face says,
pure pleasure in your eyes.

East

Half rain, half snow, cold and wet,
it stings your cheeks and won't lie still;
it falls in winter, grey and mean -
slush sliding down the hill.

South

The slow cold fear of something bad
you know is on its way -
the dentist, the test, the dark ahead -
it sours the whole long day.

West

A sudden jolt that stops you dead -
bad news, a fright, a fall;
your mouth drops open, your face goes white -
'I can't believe it' is all.
