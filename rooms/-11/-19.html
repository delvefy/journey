Owl

You are an owl - the round-eyed bird of the night, who sleeps all day in a hollow tree and hunts when the world goes dark. Your wings are so soft you fly without a sound, swooping down on a mouse before it hears a thing. Your head turns almost all the way round, and your great eyes drink the moonlight. 'Twit-twoo,' you call across the wood. Wise, they call you, sitting silent and watchful. You are the silent hunter of the dark, all eyes and feathers and patience.
