Sunshine

You are sunshine - the warm bright pour of light from the summer sun, that gilds the grass and warms the back of the neck. You coax the flowers open and dry the morning dew; you send children running outside with no coat. You slip through windows and lay warm yellow squares on the floor where the cat curls up. You cannot be bottled or kept for the grey days. You are the gladdest weather of all, the sky's plain warm gift, free to everyone at once.
