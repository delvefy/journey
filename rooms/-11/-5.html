Flea

You are a tiny dark insect, smaller than a crumb, that lives in the warm fur of cats and dogs and bites for its dinner. No hand makes you; you breed unseen and leap astonishing heights, a hundred times your own size, from one warm body to the next. The dog scratches; the vet frowns. Tiny, itchy, and famous for jumping.

North

A warty lumpy hopping thing,
like a frog but dry and brown;
it hides in the cool damp shade all day -
squat and round, head down.

East

A soft slow shell-less crawling thing
that oozes after rain;
it leaves a silver slimy trail
and eats the leaves again.

South

A small plain brown-and-grey little bird
that chirps in every street;
it hops for crumbs, it nests in eaves -
the commonest you'll meet.

West

A long soft crawler with many legs
that munches every leaf;
it spins itself a little case -
a butterfly's first life, in brief.
