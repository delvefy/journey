Flash

You are a flash - a sudden burst of light, blinding-bright and gone in the same instant, the camera's pop or the lightning's white split of the night. No hand needs to make you; a storm or a spark will do. You print the room on the eyes for a moment after, then leave the dark twice as dark. Too quick to hold, too bright to ignore, you are light at its most sudden, a single dazzling blink the world gives and takes straight back.
