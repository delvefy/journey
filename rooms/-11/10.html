Grey

You are the colour in between - not black, not white, but the soft dull middle: storm clouds, old stone, a battleship, a winter morning before the light. No hand made you; the overcast sky wears you. Quiet and plain and everywhere on a rainy day. Mix black and white, and out of the muddle comes me.

North

The colour of carrots and of its own fruit,
of pumpkins and a fox;
mix red with yellow, here it comes -
bright as a sunset's locks.

East

The colour of the sky and the deep sea,
of summer noon, far away;
cool and calm, the shade of distance -
it cannot be held, they say.

South

The colour of grass and leaf and moss,
of spring's first growing blade;
cool and alive and everywhere -
the colour 'go' is made.

West

A soft pale yellowish-white,
the colour of thick milk's top;
of fresh-churned butter, soft and rich -
poured on the pudding, plop.
