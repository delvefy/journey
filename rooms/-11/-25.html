Starfish

You are a starfish - a flat five-armed star that lives on the bed of the sea, creeping so slowly along on hundreds of tiny tube feet that you never seem to move at all. You cling to the rocks in pools left by the tide, and if you lose an arm you can simply grow a new one. Orange or pink or purple, you spread your points like a child's drawing of a star. Slow and patient and perfectly star-shaped, you are the sea's own little starlight, washed up gleaming on the sand.
