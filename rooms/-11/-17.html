Armadillo

You are an armadillo - a strange little animal of the warm lands, wrapped head to tail in plates of bony armour like a knight in a suit of mail. No hand made you; the earth did, and when danger comes you curl into a tight ball that nothing can bite into. You can be seen snuffling for ants and grubs with your long claws, trundling along on stubby legs. Slow and shy and quite unbothered, you are a little walking fortress, safe inside your own hard shell.
