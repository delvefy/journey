Pool

You are a body of still water held in a hollow - a rock pool by the sea, a quiet pool in the wood, or the bright blue pool you swim in. Rain and spring can fill you, though a hand can dig you too. You hold the sky upside-down on your calm face. Children paddle you, fish hang still in you. You are the small kept water.

North

The long proud hair on a horse's neck
that streams out when it runs;
the lion wears a great gold one -
it frames him like the sun's.

East

A little run of hurrying water,
narrow, quick, and clear;
it trips over stones and chatters along -
a baby river here.

South

A great grey fish with rows of teeth
that hunts beneath the wave;
its fin cuts the water like a knife -
the sea's most feared and brave.

West

A five-armed creature on the sand,
shaped like a fallen star;
it clings to rocks beneath the tide -
a sea-thing, strange and far.
