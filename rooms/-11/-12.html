Koala

You are a koala - a small, round, grey, furry creature, like a living teddy bear, clinging high in a gum tree. You sleep most of the day curled in a fork of the branches, and when you wake you nibble the tough green leaves no one else will eat. With your big black nose and fluffy ears, you look forever sleepy and forever sweet, the cuddliest climber in the whole wide Australian wood.
