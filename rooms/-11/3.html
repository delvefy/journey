Roar

You are the huge deep sound that makes the ground shake - the lion's warning, the crowd at the goal, the waterfall, the jet, the storm. No hand makes you; the throat, the engine, the falling water flings you out, vast and rolling. You drown out every other sound. Loud as thunder and twice as fierce, you fill the world.

North

The open-mouthed amazement that you feel
at the stars, the sea, the snow;
'how can it be so big, so strange?' -
a marvel head to toe.

East

The busy hum a bee makes
as it darts from flower to flower;
'bzzzz' it goes round your ear -
a small wings' droning power.

South

A great fierce roaring fire of gold
that leaps up wild and high;
far bigger than a single flame -
the bonfire in the sky.

West

A low deep rolling growl of sound -
far thunder, an empty tum,
a lorry on the cobbled road -
it goes on, rrr, like a drum.
