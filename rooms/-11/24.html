Glint

You are a glint - a quick bright wink of light bouncing off something shiny. Sun on a wet window, light on a coin, a flash off a knife or a pair of glasses: a glint, there and gone. You are smaller than a gleam and quicker than a shine, just one sharp little spark caught for a split second. Sly and sudden, you are the way a hidden thing gives itself away, the tell-tale flash from a buckle in the grass or a tear in the corner of an eye.
