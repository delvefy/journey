Mutter

You are a mutter - words spoken so low and grumbly they barely leave the lips. You are what a cross person says under their breath when they daren't say it out loud, the half-swallowed grumble walking away, the words mumbled into a collar. Nobody quite catches you, and perhaps that is the point. No hand can hold you - you are speech kept just below hearing, a small dark cloud of words muttered to no one but yourself.
