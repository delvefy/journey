Swan

You are a great white bird with a long curving neck, gliding silent and serene across the still lake. No hand makes you; you build your nest at the water's edge and lead your grey cygnets in a line behind you. Calm as you look, you hiss and beat your great wings to guard your young. Graceful, proud, and snow-white.

North

A small plain brown-and-grey little bird
that chirps in every street;
it hops for crumbs, it nests in eaves -
the commonest you'll meet.

East

A little bird with a red-orange breast
that hops about the snow;
it sings all winter, clear and bold -
on Christmas cards you know.

South

A sleek brown swimmer of the river,
with whiskers and webbed feet;
it slides down muddy banks for fun
and cracks a shell to eat.

West

A great fierce bird with hooked sharp beak
that soars on the mountain wind;
it spies a mouse from miles up high
and dives - the king of its kind.
