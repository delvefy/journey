Delight

Pure pleasure, sudden and complete - delight is joy that catches you by surprise. The first bite of something delicious, a friend at the door you did not expect, snow falling on a school morning. It lights the whole face at once and cannot be faked or forced. You do not make a delight; it is given to you, and for a moment the world is exactly, wonderfully right. Lighter than happiness and quicker, it comes like a small clean burst of sun.

North

The colour of leaves
and the grass underfoot,
of a caterpillar's coat
and a tree's new shoot.

East

A bubbling bright joy
that won't stay still;
you clap and you crow
with a happy shrill.

South

A heavy grey sadness,
a dark and dismal mood;
the light seems to dim
and nothing feels good.

West

The colour of a rose,
of blood and of flame,
of a stop sign's glow -
hot and bright, the same.
