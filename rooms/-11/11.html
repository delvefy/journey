Orange

You are the warm bright colour between red and yellow - the carrot, the pumpkin, the fox's coat, the fire's glow, the sky at sunset. No hand made you; mix a hot red with a glad yellow, and out I come. The colour of autumn leaves and traffic cones - you cannot help but be noticed.

North

A really bad and powerful smell,
of rotten eggs and drains;
it fills the room, you hold your nose -
till not a sniff remains.

East

The colour of plums and violets too,
of kings' robes long ago;
mix red with blue and here it comes -
a royal, deep-dyed glow.

South

The colour of clouds before the rain,
of stone and a battleship;
not black, not white, but in between -
a dull and misty grip.

West

To talk so soft, with barely a breath,
a secret in the ear;
so quiet that only one close friend
is meant to ever hear.
