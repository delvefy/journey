Chatter

You are chatter - the quick light run of talk that spills out and never quite stops, voices overlapping, words tumbling over words. You are friends at play, birds at dawn, a whole room talking at once about nothing much. And when the cold bites hard you are teeth knocking together, clicking fast. Made by no hand, too quick to hold, you are sound pouring out for the joy of the noise itself.
