Shark

You are a great grey fish, swift and silent, with rows upon rows of pointed teeth. No hand makes you; you patrol the deep, your tall fin slicing the surface, smelling a single drop of blood from far away. You never stop swimming, not even to sleep. Feared above all the fish of the sea, you are all teeth and fin and shadow.

North

A little patch of water, still and clear,
held in a hollow place;
you swim in one, you paddle one -
the sky shines on its face.

East

A sleek grey swimmer of the sea
with whiskers and big dark eyes;
it barks and flops on rocks to bask -
it claps, it dives, it cries.

South

A warty, lumpy, hopping thing,
like a frog but dry and brown;
it sits in the cool damp shade all day -
the warty cousin, squat and round.

West

A soft sea-beast with eight long arms
that creeps along the deep;
it squirts black ink to hide and flee -
in rocky holes it'll keep.
