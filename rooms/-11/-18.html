Platypus

You are a platypus - the oddest animal there is, looking like the leftover bits of three creatures glued together. You have the soft brown fur of an otter, the flat bill and webbed feet of a duck, and yet you lay eggs as a bird does. You paddle the rivers of Australia, hunting with your eyes shut, feeling for food with your strange rubbery snout. You are natural, grown not made, a joke the world seems to have played on itself. You are proof that nature loves to surprise.
