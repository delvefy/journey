Otter

Sleek and brown and never still, it rolls and dives in the cold river as if play were the whole point of being alive - an otter is joy with whiskers. It cracks shells on its chest, slides down muddy banks for fun, and holds its baby's paw so the current cannot steal it away. Warm-blooded and quick, it belongs half to the water and half to the bank. Made by no hand, it is the river's own glad and busy child.

North

A long white neck
glides over the lake;
graceful and proud,
few sounds do I make.

East

A baby horse
all legs and no grace;
it wobbles by its mother
then learns to race.

South

A big striped cat,
orange and black;
it hunts in the jungle
and never looks back.

West

I build a dam
of sticks and mud;
my flat tail slaps
to warn of a flood.
