Grumble

You are a grumble - the low grumpy noise of someone who isn't happy and wants you to know. You rumble out at the bottom of the voice when the chores aren't fair, the weather's wrong, the dinner's late. A tummy grumbles when it's hungry; thunder grumbles far away. No one can hold you in for long - you are discontent let out low and muttery, a small dark cloud of complaint that rolls about under the breath.
