Whale

You are a whale - the biggest beast that has ever lived, a mountain of gentle muscle gliding through the deep cold sea. No hand made you; the ocean did, and you must rise to breathe and blow a spout of misty water high in the air. You sing long low songs that carry for miles through the dark water to others of your kind. Vast and slow and warm-blooded, you are the great soft giant of the waves, too huge to be anything but kind.
