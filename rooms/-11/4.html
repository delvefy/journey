Wonder

You are the wide-eyed amazement at something too big or too strange to take in - the night sky packed with stars, the sea that has no end, the snow falling silent. No hand makes you; the marvel raises you, opening the mouth and stilling the tongue. 'How can it be?' you ask, and do not need an answer.

North

What you have at the fair or game,
all laughter, play, and run;
the opposite of dull hard work -
a good time in the sun.

East

The bright glad leaping in your chest
when something lovely's true;
you want to sing and twirl about -
sheer gladness rushing through.

South

The huge deep blast a lion makes,
or a crowd when the goal goes in;
the waterfall, the jet, the storm -
a thunder, loud as sin.

West

The long sharp 'ssss' a snake lets out,
or the kettle's steamy breath;
the cat that arches up its back -
a warning sound, like death.
