Sunbeam

You are a sunbeam - one bright slanting shaft of light, poured through a gap in the clouds or a chink in the curtains. You land in a stripe across the carpet, and there the cat goes to lie. You light up the floating dust so it dances like gold. You are sunshine made into a single arrow, aimed down from the sky to one warm patch on the floor. Step into me and feel the day touch your face.
