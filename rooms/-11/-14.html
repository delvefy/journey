Skunk

You are a skunk - a small black beast with a bold white stripe and a big bushy tail. You waddle through the dusk looking for grubs, and you would rather not fight at all - so you carry the worst weapon in the wood: if something corners you, you lift that tail and spray a stink so foul it clings for days. Grown by no hand, striped like a warning sign, you are a gentle creature everyone has learned to leave well alone.
