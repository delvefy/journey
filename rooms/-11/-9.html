Tiger

You are the great striped cat of the jungle, orange and black, silent on huge soft paws. No hand made you; the wild did. You hunt alone, you swim, you spring from the long grass before the deer can blink. Fierce and beautiful and rare. Hear the deep cough of your call in the dark, and the whole forest goes still.

North

A sleek brown swimmer of the river,
it floats on its back to dine;
it cracks a shell upon its chest -
at play in the water, fine.

East

A baby bear or fox or lion,
furry, small, at play;
it tumbles with its litter-mates -
and sleeps the day away.

South

A black-and-white chattering bird
that loves a shiny thing;
it grabs the ring, it hides the spoon -
one for sorrow, on the wing.

West

A big brown beast with heavy paws,
that growls and stands up tall;
it loves the honey, sleeps all winter -
the biggest beast of all.
