Raccoon

You are a raccoon - a furry grey creature of the night, with a black mask round your eyes like a tiny burglar and a fat ringed tail. You creep out after dark on clever little hands, tipping over bins and washing your food in the stream, sly and curious and quick. Grown by no hand, masked and mischievous, you are the bandit of the back garden, raiding the dustbins by moonlight and gone again before anyone switches on the light.
