Grunt

You are a grunt - the short low noise a body shoves out when it heaves or strains. A pig roots in the mud and makes you; a man lifting a heavy box lets you slip; a tired boy answers his mother with nothing but you. You are barely a word at all, just a lump of sound pushed past the teeth. You live in the air for a heartbeat and are gone. You are the plainest noise of hard work, half-grumble and half-breath.
