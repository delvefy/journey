Stink

You are a stink - a really bad smell, strong enough to make your eyes water and your nose pinch shut. Rotten eggs, a blocked drain, old cabbage left too long, a skunk's defence. You hang thick in the air and follow people about, so that everyone cries 'what is that stink?' Worse than a pong, sharper and fouler, you cannot be seen, only smelled - the most unwelcome smell of all.
