Leopard

You are a leopard - the spotted big cat, gold coat dappled with black rosettes so you melt into the dappled shade. You are the quietest hunter, padding through long grass without a sound, then up a tree in one pour of muscle to drape along a branch and watch. Strong enough to carry your dinner skyward, patient enough to wait all day. Beautiful, silent, dangerous: that is you, the cat who wears the leaves.
