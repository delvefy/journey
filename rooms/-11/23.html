Twinkle

You are a twinkle - the soft winking sparkle of a star, brightening and dimming as its light comes wobbling down through the moving air, the spark in a kind eye, the glitter on the frost. No hand makes you; far light and the trembling sky do. Children sing about you before bed. You are smaller and softer than a flash, a light that breathes. Gentle and winking and far away, you are the night sky's quiet shimmer, the way the stars say hello.
