Pufferfish

You are a pufferfish - a funny little fish with a secret trick. Most of the time you potter along the reef looking quite ordinary, but the moment something frightens you, you gulp down water and swell up into a round prickly balloon, far too big and spiky to swallow. Once the danger passes you let the water out and shrink back to normal. Comical and clever and a little bit prickly, you are the fish that turns itself into a spiky beach ball whenever trouble comes too close.
