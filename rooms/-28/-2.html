sunburn

Sunburn is what the sun gives you when you stay out too long without a hat or a smear of cream - skin gone hot and red and tight and sore, so it hurts even to wear a shirt. It creeps up on you while the day feels lovely, and only bites that evening. A week later it peels in papery flakes off your shoulders and your nose. A summer's worst souvenir, it teaches the same lesson every year: cover up, and come inside.
