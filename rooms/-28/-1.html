wart

A wart is a small hard knobbly bump that grows on the skin, most often on a hand or a foot. Brown or grey, rough as a little pebble, it does not hurt - it just sits there, stubborn, until one day it gives up and goes. Storybook witches have one on the nose, and toads are blamed for them, quite unfairly. Harmless and ugly and oddly tough, it is the body's silly little lump.

North

A long high line where two slopes meet,
you walk the top, the land falls each way;
a roof has one, a mountain too -
the narrow crest where you can't stray.

East

Skin your knee on the rough hard ground,
a red raw scrape, it stings and weeps;
not a deep cut, just the top rubbed off -
a playground wound that barely seeps.

South

Stay too long in the summer sun
and your skin turns red and hot and sore;
it peels in a week and itches like mad -
so wear your hat and burn no more.

West

A little bubble under the skin,
from a shoe that rubs or a pan too hot;
a dome of water, tender and sore -
don't you pop it, no you'll not.
