gasp

A gasp is the quick sharp breath you suck in all at once when something takes you by surprise - a shock, a fright, a wonder. Your mouth flies open, the air rushes in, and for a second no words will come. The whole crowd gasps when the magician vanishes; you gasp when you break the surface after a deep dive. It is surprise turned into a single fast breath, the body catching up with the moment.

North

A roaring fire that races wild
across the dry hot summer hills;
it leaps from tree to tree so fast -
it eats the forest, smokes and kills.

East

A long slow breath let out, 'haaah,'
when you're tired, or sad, or glad it's done;
your shoulders drop, the air flows out -
a weary breath when the day is won.

South

A quick light flutter of falling snow,
a swirl of flakes, then gone again;
not a storm, just a scatter of white -
the briefest taste of snow, and then...

West

After you run, you breathe so fast,
huff, huff, your chest goes up and down;
a hot tired dog will do it too,
tongue right out, all round the town.
