Salt

You are salt - the little white grains that wake up the taste of everything they touch. You come dried from the wide sea or dug in blocks from deep in the ground. A pinch of you saves a bland soup; too much and the dish is ruined. You sting in a cut and dry the winter roads. You are natural, never made by any hand, the oldest seasoning there is. You are the sharp small crystal that turns plain food into something a tongue is glad to meet.
