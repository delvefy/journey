Breeze

You are a breeze - the softest kind of wind, just enough to lift a kite and turn the leaves over and cool a hot face. You wander in through the open window and ruffle the curtains; you carry the smell of cut grass across the garden. You break no branch and slam no door, only stir and stroke and pass on. You cannot be caught or kept, only felt for a moment on the skin. You are the gentle breath of a warm day, kind and light and gone.
