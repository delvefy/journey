Feather

You are a feather - the light dry plume that clothes a bird and lets it fly. You are soft to the cheek yet strong on the wing, a quill below and a fan above. You drift down slow when one is dropped, twirling on the gentlest breath of air. Long ago you were dipped in ink to write whole books; today you stuff the warmest pillows. You are the bird's clothing and the bird's flight both, weightless and warm and impossibly fine.
