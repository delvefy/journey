Stare

You are a stare - the long, fixed look that lands on a thing and does not let go. You can be rude, the way you fasten on a stranger; you can be dreamy, the way you drift out the window and forget the lesson. Your eyes go wide and still, and the world narrows to one held point. Hold me too long and someone says it is rude to do so. Eyes open, mind far away - that is you.
