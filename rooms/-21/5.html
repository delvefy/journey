Rainbow

You are a rainbow - the great arch of colours that bends across the sky when the sun shines through the falling rain. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and the rest, you hang there bright and silent, then fade as the cloud moves on. No one can walk to your end or touch your light; you are made of nothing but bent sunbeams and water. Some say a pot of gold waits beneath you. You are the sky's brief, painted smile.
