Crash

You are a crash - the huge sudden noise of things smashing or slamming together. The stack of pans goes over with you; the cymbals meet with you; the wave breaks on the rocks with you; the storm wave of thunder rolls with you. You are louder than a bang and longer too, a great messy tumble of sound. People jump, then run to see what broke. You are the noise of the world falling over all at once, loud enough to set the heart racing.
