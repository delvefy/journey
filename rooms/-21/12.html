Beam

You are a beam - a straight bright spear of light, the shaft a torch throws down a tunnel or the sun pours through a gap in the clouds. No hand shapes you; a bright thing and a dark room make you, and the dust dances in your edge. You point like a finger from the lighthouse and the lamp. A clean line of brightness laid across the dim, you show the way and pick one thing out of the shadow.
