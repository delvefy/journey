Bubble

You are a bubble - a round, shining, see-through ball of nothing but soapy air. You rise from a blown wand or a fizzy drink, wobbling and gleaming with rainbow skins, and then with the lightest touch you go pop and vanish into nothing. You cannot be held, only chased and watched. You live for only a second or two, fragile as a breath. You are the most delicate plaything there is, a tiny floating marvel that bursts the moment a finger comes too near.
