Skull

You are a skull - the hard white case of bone that cups the brain and gives the face its shape, made by no hand but grown around the soft and secret parts. Two dark holes for eyes, a row of teeth, you are the frame every face is hung upon. Pirates fly you, pumpkins grin like you at Halloween. Beneath every smile you wait, patient and bony, the helmet a body builds for itself.
