Dew

You are dew - the tiny beads of water that gather overnight on the grass and the spider's web and the car window, not fallen from the sky but breathed out of the cooling air itself. No hand makes you; warm air meeting cold ground does. You soak the early walker's shoes and sparkle like a field of diamonds when the first sun catches you. Gone by mid-morning, brief and bright, you are the secret water the night leaves behind for the dawn to set alight.
