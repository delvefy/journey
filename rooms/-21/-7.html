Claw

You are a claw - the sharp curved nail at the end of an animal's toe, made for gripping, digging, and tearing. The cat sheathes you in velvet, then shoots you out to climb or scratch. The eagle locks you round a fish; the mole uses you to shovel through the earth; the crab waves a great pincer of you. You can be drawn back or thrust out at will. You are the animal's built-in hook and dagger, the business end of every grasping foot.
