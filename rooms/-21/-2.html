Arm

You are an arm - the long limb that swings from the shoulder down to the hand, bending at the elbow halfway along. You lift and carry, hug and throw, reach high for the top shelf and wave hello across a street. A chair has two of you to rest a tired pair on. You are natural, grown not made, a clever rope of bone and muscle. You are the body's busy helper, never still for long, doing a thousand jobs a day from dawn to dark.

North

The bendy joint where hand meets arm,
you wear a watch on this;
it turns the hand all round about -
a ___, don't bend amiss.

East

The joint where your arm meets the trunk,
you carry a bag on this;

South

A sweet juicy fruit, fat at the bottom,
and narrow up at the top;
it ripens green to gold and soft -
a ___, eat every drop.

West

The soft flat inside of your hand,
where the lines run this way and that;
a fortune-teller reads it close -
a ___, hold it out flat.
