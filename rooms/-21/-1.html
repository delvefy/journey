Wrist

You are a wrist - the slim bendy joint that joins the hand to the arm and lets it turn every which way. You swivel so a wave can flap, a key can twist, a ball can be thrown. People wear a watch on you and a bracelet too, and feel for the soft thud of the pulse beneath your skin. You are natural, grown not made, a clever little hinge of bone and tendon. You are the turning-point of the whole arm, the joint that gives the hand its freedom.
