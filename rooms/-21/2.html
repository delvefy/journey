Swirl

You are a swirl - the round twisting motion a thing makes when it spins as it goes. Stir a spoon in the cocoa and you curl through the milk; a gust of wind catches the autumn leaves and sends them in one; water going down the drain makes you too. You cannot be touched, only watched as you turn and turn. You are the loveliest shape that moving things fall into, a spinning curl of leaf or cream or water, round and round and gone.
