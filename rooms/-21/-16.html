Pool

You are a pool - a small still patch of water, calm and clear and quiet. A rock pool left by the tide, a deep pool in the bend of a river, a paddling pool on the lawn: all of them you. You hold the sky upside down on your surface and go barely a ripple until a toe breaks in. Cool and inviting on a hot day, you are water at rest, gathered and waiting, just deep enough to splash in, to fish in, or to find a frog and a flash of darting minnows.
