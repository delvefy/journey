Eyelid

You are an eyelid - the thin soft fold of skin that drops like a curtain over the eye. You blink a thousand times a day without a thought, wiping the eye clean and keeping the light out when sleep comes. No hand made you; the body did, hanging you light as a petal above the eye. You close for dreams and snap open for the morning. Small and quiet, you are the little shutter that lets the eye rest.
