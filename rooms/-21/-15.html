Marsh

You are a marsh - a stretch of soft wet boggy ground, neither quite land nor quite water, where reeds grow tall and the mud sucks at every boot. No hand makes you; flat ground and slow water do. Herons stalk you, frogs sing in you, and a careless walker loses a welly to your squelching grip. Misty at dawn and loud with birds, you are the in-between place, the soggy edge of the world where the land gives up and the water has not quite begun.
