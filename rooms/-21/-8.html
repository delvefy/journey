Vine

You are a vine - the long green climber that twists and curls its way up walls, trees, and trellises, gripping with little tendrils as it goes. The grapes hang heavy from your arms; the ivy and the bean and the morning glory are all kinds of you. You cannot stand on your own, so you cling and climb on whatever you find. Left alone, you swallow a whole fence in a summer. You are the plant that travels, forever reaching upward and along.
