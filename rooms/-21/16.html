Spit

You are spit - the wet you make in your own mouth, and the rude little shot of it fired out between the lips. You help to chew and swallow, you wet a hanky to wipe a smudge, and llamas and footballers and cross cats let you fly. Grown-ups frown on you; children find you fascinating. A camel can spit a great green glob; a cherry stone spits a fair way too. Wet and quick and a bit disgusting, you are the mouth's own water, sometimes useful and sometimes just plain bad manners.
