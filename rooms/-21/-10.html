Stem

You are a stem - the slender green stalk that holds a flower up to the light. Water climbs you from the roots below; leaves branch off your sides; and at your tip the bloom rides high. You bend in the wind but spring back, and snap with a clean green smell if a child picks the flower too low. Without you the petals would lie in the mud. You are the plant's straight back, lifting the colour up where the bees can find it.
