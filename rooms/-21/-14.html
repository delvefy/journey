Pinecone

You are a pinecone - the woody egg of scales that hangs on the pine and drops to the forest floor, brown and prickly and full of seeds tucked behind your overlapping flaps. No hand makes you; the tall dark pine does. You open your scales wide on a dry day and shut them tight in the wet. Children gather you for bird-feeders and Christmas crafts. Knobbly and woody and faintly resin-scented, you are the pine tree's neat little parcel, packed with next year's forest.
