Banana

You are a banana - the long curved fruit in its bright yellow jacket, sweet and soft inside. You strip down in three quick pulls and there is the pale fruit, ready to eat without a knife or a wash. The monkey loves you best, and the clumsy clown slips on your tossed-off skin. You grow in great hands of green and ripen to gold, then to brown spots that mean the very sweetest. You are the easiest fruit of all, no stone, no pips, just peel and bite.
