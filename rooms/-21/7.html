Heat

You are heat - the fierce warmth that pours from fire and sun and oven, far stronger than gentle warmth. You shimmer above the summer road, make the desert dance, and bring the sweat to a brow. You cook the dinner, soften the wax, and melt the ice to water. Too much of you, and a body wilts and gasps for shade. You cannot be seen, only felt, blazing off anything that burns. You are the wild fierce edge of warmth, the breath of the fire itself.
