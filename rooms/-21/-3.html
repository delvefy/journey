Pear

You are a pear - the sweet green fruit shaped fat at the bottom and narrow at the top, as if a apple had melted and run down. You hang from the branch heavy and slow, and when at last you ripen you turn so soft and juicy that the juice runs down a chin. You sit in the fruit bowl beside the banana, bruising easily if knocked. You are the gentle drooping fruit, all sweetness and shape, best eaten the very day you soften.
