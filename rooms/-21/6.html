Blush

You are a blush - the sudden warm red that floods the cheeks when a person is shy, caught out, or quietly pleased. No one chooses you; you rise on your own, betraying the heart before a word is said. A clumsy compliment, a stumble in front of the class, a secret half-guessed - any can light you up. You cannot be hidden or held back, only felt. You are the face telling the truth the mouth would rather keep, feeling made suddenly visible.
