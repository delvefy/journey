Stampede

You are a stampede - a wild thundering rush of animals all bolting together in a panic. One frightened beast sets off and the whole herd follows, hooves pounding, dust rising, the ground shaking under the drumming feet. Cattle do it, wildebeest do it, and woe to anything caught in the path. Loud and unstoppable and a little bit terrifying, you are a crowd of creatures turned into one great rolling wave of muscle and dust, charging blindly on until the fright wears off and they slow.
