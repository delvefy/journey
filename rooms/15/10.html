Week

You are a week - seven days strung together in a row, Monday to Sunday and round again. You are the shape of school and weekend, the wait from one swimming lesson to the next, the time a felt-tip lasts before it dries. Long when you are looking forward to something, gone in a blink when you're not. No hand can hold you - you are time bundled into sevens, the steady drumbeat the whole year marches to.
