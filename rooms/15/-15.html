Thimble

You are a thimble - the tiny dimpled cap of metal that fits over a fingertip when somebody sews. The needle would prick and bite, so you take the push for the finger and let it shove the point through the tough cloth. You are small enough to lose down the back of the chair, and so you often are. Grandma keeps you in the sewing box with the pins and thread. You are the finger's little helmet, dented all over, guarding against the sting of the needle.
