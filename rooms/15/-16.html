Flag

You are a flag - the bright cloth on a pole that snaps and ripples in the wind, the badge of a country or a club. You climb the mast at dawn and come down at dusk; you wave at the parade and droop at half-mast for sorrow. Sailors signal with you; mountaineers plant you at the summit. Your stripes and stars and crosses each tell a story. You are loyalty made of cloth, a patch of colour flying high for all to gather under.
