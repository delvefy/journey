Rush

You are a rush - the great fast surge of going too quick, the morning scramble for the door, the crowd that pours from the gates at once. People make you out of lateness and want, a wave of hurry with no patience in it. You knock the cup, you skip the step, you arrive breathless and forget your coat. Half a feeling and half a stampede, you are speed with the brakes off, the world all trying to be somewhere at the same moment.
