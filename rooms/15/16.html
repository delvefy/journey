Brush

You are a brush - not the bristled tool but the touch itself, the light quick grazing of one thing past another. A wing brushes the water; a sleeve brushes the wet paint; a hand brushes a cheek and is gone. You are barely a touch at all, gentler than a tap, softer than a stroke. You leave almost nothing behind - a tingle, a smudge, a shiver. You are contact at its lightest, the faint passing kiss of thing on thing.
