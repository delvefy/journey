Bat

You are a bat - a long flat club of springy wood, made for swinging at a ball. Grip the handle, watch the bowler, and whack, off the ball flies for a six. You crack against the rounders ball, you tap the ground while you wait. Carved and oiled and taped by a hand, smooth and heavy and just the right balance, you are the friend of every summer game played on a field, sending the ball sailing into the sky.
