Crow

You are a crow - the loud ringing call a rooster flings up at the break of day. He puffs out his chest, lifts his red comb to the dawn, and cock-a-doodle-doos so the whole farm wakes whether it wants to or not. You are the proudest, loudest morning sound there is. No hand can hold you - you are a bird's triumphant shout, the bugle of the barnyard, telling the sleepy world that the sun is up and the day has begun.
