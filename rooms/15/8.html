Yawn

You are the yawn - the great wide stretch of the mouth that comes when you are tired or bored, sucking in a long slow breath. You cannot help me, and once I start I spread: one yawn at the table sets the whole room off. Eyes water, jaw cracks wide. I am the body asking for sleep, the slow signal that the day is done. Catch me - everyone always does.

North

Sixty seconds, ticking by,
a small slice of an hour;
'wait a ___!' the grown-ups cry -
time's little measured shower.

East

Shake and squirm from side to side,
like a worm or a wagging tail;
a jelly does it, so does a tooth
that's loose and about to fail.

South

Heave it up right off the ground,
raise it with all your might;
or step inside the box that climbs
from floor to floor in flight.

West

Bend your knees and hunch down low,
all folded up and small;
a cat does it before it pounces -
ready to spring and fall.
