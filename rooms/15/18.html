Whizz

You are a whizz - to shoot past so fast you leave a humming, rushing sound behind. The racing car does you down the straight; the arrow does you off the bow; the child on the bike does you down the hill, whooping. You are speed you can hear, a blur and a noise together. Blink and whatever made you is already gone by. You are quickness turned into a sound, the rushing hum of something flying past far too fast to catch.
