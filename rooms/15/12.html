Flutter

You are a flutter - the soft quick trembling of something light caught in the air: a butterfly's wings, a flag's loose edge, a heart that skips when you are nervous. A breeze makes you, or a wing, or a sudden thrill; you are dainty busy motion, all a-tremble and going nowhere far. You cannot be held, only watched - a moth round the lamp, a ribbon, an eyelash blinking fast. You are the lightest way a thing can move, too small almost to call a motion at all.
