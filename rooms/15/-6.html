Cup

You are a cup - a small round vessel with a handle, made to hold a hot or cold drink. Tea, cocoa, juice, or just plain water; you carry it warm to the hand and steady to the lips. Made of china, plastic, or tin, you sit on the table waiting to be filled and drained and filled again. Small and plain and dear, you are the first thing a child learns to hold and sip from.

North

A little bed that rocks the baby,
gently to and fro;
'rock-a-bye' upon the treetop -
soft and sweet and slow.

East

A little bed on four small wheels
that you push along the street;
the baby sleeps inside, all snug,
while you walk on your feet.

South

A soft thick pad the baby wears,
wrapped snugly round behind;
when it's wet you change it quick -
a fresh one you will find.

West

A little square of cloth sewn on
to cover up a hole;
on the knee of your worn-out jeans -
it makes the trousers whole.
