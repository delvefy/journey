Toaster

You are the little chrome box that browns the bread - two slots, a lever, glowing red wires within. Made by clever hands. Drop the slices in, push down, and wait; up they spring, hot and crisp and golden, when the time is done. Butter quick, before I cool. Just don't leave me too long, or it's smoke and black.

North

A hot metal box in the kitchen wall
where the cake and the roast go in;
you turn it up and shut the door -
and dinner can begin.

East

Spinning blades that blow cool air,
to fix a too-hot day;
it whirs and turns its head about -
and blows the heat away.

South

A baby's bed that rocks and sways,
on curved feet, to and fro;
'rock-a-bye' you sing to it -
soft, and swinging low.

West

A cold white cupboard, humming low,
that keeps the milk from spoiling;
you open it for cheese and juice -
inside, it's never boiling.
