Hurry

You are a hurry - the quick flustered going of someone who has left it too late, shoes half on, toast in hand, 'quick, quick, we'll miss it!' No one wants you; the clock and a slow start make you. You skip the careful steps and bump the door frame and forget the very thing you needed. Half a worry and half a sprint, you are time turned against a person, the breathless dash to be somewhere that began a minute too late.
