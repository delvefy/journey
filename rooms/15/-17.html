Tent

You are a tent - the little house of cloth you pitch out in the wild, held up by poles and pegged tight to the ground. You flap in the wind and drum under the rain, while inside the campers lie snug in their sleeping bags by torchlight. Children make one of a sheet draped over chairs. You fold away into a bag no bigger than a cushion. You are a roof you can carry on your back, a home for a night under the open sky.
