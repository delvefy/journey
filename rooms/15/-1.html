Switch

You are the little lever on the wall that gives or takes the light - up for on, down for off, click and the room is changed. Made by clever hands to tame the lightning. Flick me by the door without even looking. One small movement of a finger, and night becomes day, or day, the dark.

North

The big yellow machine that scoops
great bites of earth and clay;
its long arm digs the giant holes -
it works the site all day.

East

Three metal pins you push in the wall
to give the lamp its power;
you yank the cord, it pops right out -
it feeds the screen for hours.

South

A thick metal pin you slide across
to lock the door up tight;
shoot it home into its slot -
it holds against the night.

West

The little glass pear in the lamp
that glows when switched on bright;
it lights the room at a flick of the thumb -
a little globe of light.
