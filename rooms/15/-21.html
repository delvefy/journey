Microscope

You are a microscope - a tube of strong lenses pointed not up at the stars but down at a slide, to swell the tiniest speck into a whole strange world. You shut one eye and squint down through, and a fleck of pond water turns out to be teeming with wriggling life, a hair becomes a rope. Hands twist your focus until the blur snaps sharp. Patient and precise, you are the eye that shrinks itself to a pinhead, and finds, in the very small, a hidden universe.
