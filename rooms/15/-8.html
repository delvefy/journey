Anchor

You are the anchor - a great hooked weight of iron, dropped from a ship on a long chain to grip the seabed and hold her fast. When the storm blows and the waves heave, you bite into the sand and will not let the boat drift away. Hauled up, you swing dripping at the bow; let down, you are the one still thing in all that moving water. Heavy, sure, and steadfast.
