Sweep

You are a sweep - one long wide stroke that clears a space from end to end. The broom does you across the kitchen floor; an arm does you to clear the crumbs off the table; a searchlight does you slow across the dark. You are not a poke or a dab but a whole grand passing-over, gathering all before you in one go. You cannot be held, only made. You are the big clean motion that leaves nothing behind it but a tidy floor and a little pile of dust.

North

You drag the bristles through your hair,
or comb the dog's soft coat;
you do it to your teeth at night -
a ___, twice round each note.

East

You beat it loud with stick or hand,
boom-boom, a steady sound;
you tap your fingers on the desk -
a ___, a rolling pound.

South

A leaf goes one upon the stream,
with no one at the oar;
the snow heaps up against the wall -
a ___, and steers no more.

West

A quick small snap of finger or wrist,
you do it to a switch;
you send a crumb off the table so -
a ___, a tiny twitch.
