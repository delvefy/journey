Nappy

You are a nappy - the soft thick cloth a baby wears, folded snug where trousers would go. You catch what the baby cannot help, and keep the cot dry through the night. Pinned or taped, then bundled off to wash; a fresh one on, and the baby gurgles, comfy once more. You are humble, and you are everywhere a small one is - the very first clothing that anybody wears.
