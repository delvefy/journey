Minute

You are a minute - a small handful of time, sixty ticking seconds wide. You are how long it takes to brush your teeth, to boil an egg, to wait when you are told to wait. You feel like nothing when you are playing and like forever when you are bored. No one can hold you, only count you out and watch you go. Sixty of you make an hour; you are time chopped into a piece small enough to feel slip past.
