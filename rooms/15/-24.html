magnifier

A magnifier is a round lens of curved glass set in a handle, and its one trick is to make small things look big. Hold it over a ladybird and there it is, huge and gleaming; hold it over the tiny print and the words swell up plain to read. Tip it toward the sun and it can gather the light to a hot bright dot. Every detective and every curious child needs one. It is a window that makes the little world enormous.

North

A long bent tube with mirrors inside,
a submarine peeps through me to see;
poke my top above the waves -
look over the wall, look out through me.

East

Click to start, I count the seconds,
click to stop and freeze the time;
the coach holds me at the running track -
who is fastest? watch me climb.

South

Tight round eye-cups with a strap,
pull them on to swim or dive;
keep the water out of your eyes -
see clearly where the fish survive.

West

A can with a handle and a long thin spout,
fill me up at the garden tap;
tip me over the thirsty flowers -
down comes rain from my sprinkly cap.
