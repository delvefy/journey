Harp

You are a harp - a tall triangle of polished wood strung with many strings, played by plucking them with both hands. A skilled hand made you and a skilled hand plays you, fingers running up and down to spill out a shower of soft sweet notes. You can be held - leaned against the shoulder - tuned, dusted, carried with care. Angels play you on clouds in the old paintings; the Irish put you on their coins. You are music you reach into with your fingers, gentle as falling water.
