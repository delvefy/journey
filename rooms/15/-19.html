Binoculars

You are binoculars - two little tubes joined side by side, made of glass and metal, that you press to both eyes to haul a distant thing up close. A hand lifts you on a strap and twists the wheel until the blur snaps sharp. Suddenly the far hill, the shy bird, the boat on the horizon are right there. You shrink the miles between, a pair of borrowed eagle-eyes that a careful watcher hangs about the neck.
