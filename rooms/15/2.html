You are a number, two fours, twice four - the legs of a spider, the arms of the octopus, the points of a snowflake's star. A made idea, neat and even. Tip me on my side, and I become the sign for endless, the loop with no end. Count to me, and you are nearly at ten.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

One more than three, one less than five,
the legs upon a chair;
the wheels on a car, the sides of a square -
count them, fair and square.

East

Three tens together, count them up,
the days in most a month;
ten more than twenty, ten less than forty -
how many, all at once?

South

One more than five, one less than seven,
the dots on a die's high face;
a half of twelve, two threes in a row -
how many in this place?

West

Three rows of three all counted up,
one less than ten you'll find;
the cat is said to have these lives -
the last lone digit signed.
