Swarm

You are a swarm - a great humming cloud of insects all on the move together, thousands of them whirling as one. Bees swarm when they leave the hive to find a new home, hanging in a buzzing brown clump on a branch; locusts swarm and darken the whole sky. There is safety and strength in such numbers. Buzzing and seething and impossible to count, you are a crowd of tiny things turned into one great drifting cloud, alive and moving all at once.
