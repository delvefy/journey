Compass

You are a compass - the little round dial with a trembling needle that always swings to point north, no matter which way you turn. The hiker holds you flat in a palm and waits for the needle to settle, then knows which way to go. Lost in fog or deep wood, you are the one thing that never loses its sense of direction. You fit in a pocket and weigh almost nothing. You are a tiny faithful magnet on a pin, forever pointing the wanderer home.
