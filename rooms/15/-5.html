Cradle

You are the baby's first bed, the little boat on curved feet that rocks the small one to sleep. Made by gentle hands. Rock-a-bye, and you sway, soft and slow, while the lullaby winds down. To cradle a thing is to hold it as carefully as this - in the bend of two arms, gentle as you.

North

A little box that browns your bread,
two slots to drop it in;
you push it down, it pops back up -
hot toast, all crisp and thin.

East

A baby's bed on big tall wheels,
that mum or dad can push;
the little one rides out for air -
along the path, no rush.

South

A little round pot with a handle,
to drink your tea or juice;
you hold it by the ear and sip -
then wash it after use.

West

A little bed with high barred sides
where the baby sleeps;
it rocks, it has a soft warm sheet -
inside, the small one keeps.
