Kaleidoscope

You are a kaleidoscope - a cardboard tube you hold to one eye and slowly twist. Inside, little chips of coloured glass tumble and the mirrors catch them, so every turn makes a new bright pattern, perfectly symmetrical, like a flower of stained glass. Twist again and it is gone, replaced by another, and another, never the same twice. Magical and dazzling and endlessly changing, you turn a handful of broken glass into a fountain of beautiful patterns, a whole garden of colours blooming and vanishing at the flick of a wrist.
