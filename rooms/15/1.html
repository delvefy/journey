6

You are a number, two threes, a half of twelve, the high face of the die. A made idea, small and friendly - six eggs in a box, six sides to a cube, six o'clock for tea. Hold up one hand and one finger more, and there I am. Count to me, and you are nicely on your way.

North

One more than seven, one less than ten,
two fours stacked up high;
turn it sideways - there's the sign
for endless, by and by.

East

Two tens together, count them up,
all your fingers and your toes;
one more than nineteen you will find -
how many, do you suppose?

South

The big yellow machine that scoops
great bites of earth and stone;
its long arm digs the giant holes -
on building sites it's known.

West

The fingers of one open hand,
the points upon a star;
one more than four, one less than six -
count them, near and far.
