Lift

You are a lift - the heave that takes a thing up off the ground. You bend your knees, take hold, and raise: the heavy box, the little one who wants carrying, the bag onto your back. Up is the only way you go. A crane lifts a beam, a wave lifts a boat, your own chest lifts with every breath. To lift is to win, for one short moment, against the steady pull of the earth.
