flock

A flock is a great gathering of birds or sheep, all moving together as if they shared one mind. Watch a flock of starlings at dusk swirl and fold across the sky in one dark shifting cloud, turning all at once. A flock of sheep flows through a gate behind the dog, packed woolly shoulder to shoulder. There is safety in the crowd, and a strange beauty in the way they keep together. Alone is one; a flock is a hundred as one.

North

A great big group of grazing beasts,
cows or elephants, all together;
they roam the plains and munch the grass -
a moving crowd in any weather.

East

Go down on your knees upon the floor,
bend your legs and rest there low;
to pray, to weed, to tie a lace -
down on your knees you go.

South

A great loud cloud of buzzing bees,
or ants that pour across the ground;
thousands moving all as one -
a busy humming, crawling crowd.

West

Over and under, in and out,
thread the strands to make some cloth;
a spider does it for its web -
a basket too, or a tartan swathe.
