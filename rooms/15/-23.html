Periscope

You are a periscope - a tall tube with a slanted mirror at the top and another at the bottom, so that you can peep over a wall or up out of the water without showing your head. A submarine raises one to spy on the ships above; a soldier uses one to peek over the trench. Clever and sneaky and a bit like magic, you let an eye see round corners and over the top, bouncing the view down the tube from one mirror to the other and into the watcher's eye.
