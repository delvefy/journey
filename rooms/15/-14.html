Banjo

You are a banjo - round-bellied, with a face stretched tight like a little drum and five strings strung across it. A thumb rolls down you and out comes a bright, twangy ring, quick and cheerful, made for foot-tapping songs on a porch. You are wrought of wood and skin and wire, a thing a hand has built and a hand must play. Pluck or strum, you cannot stay quiet for long.
