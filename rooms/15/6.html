Sip

You are a sip - the smallest, neatest drink, just a little taste taken slow. You sip a too-hot cocoa, you sip from a brimming cup so it will not spill, you sip and sip to make a treat last longer. Not a gulp, not a glug, but a careful, polite little pull of drink across the lips. You are gone in a moment, leaving only a warmth on the tongue and the cup a touch less full.
