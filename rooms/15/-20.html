Telescope

You are a telescope - a long polished tube of curved glass, made to gather faint far light and pull the distant near. You shut one eye and squint through the small end, and the moon swells huge and pocked, the rings of Saturn hang clear, a far hill leaps to your eye. Hands point you up at the night and the whole sky comes closer. Patient and precise, you are the eye that reaches across the dark to touch the stars and bring a little of them back.
