Recorder

You are a recorder - the plain little flute they hand out at school, a tube of plastic or wood with a row of holes down the front. You cover them with your fingers, blow soft and steady, and a thin whistly tune comes out. Squeak the high notes wrong and the whole class winces. You are wrought, the easiest instrument to learn and the first most people meet. Hot Cross Buns, played a hundred times, is mostly your fault.
