Shrug

You are a shrug - the quick lift of both shoulders that says "I don't know" or "who cares" without a single word. Someone asks you a question you can't answer, and up your shoulders go, palms turned out, mouth pulled down. You are a whole sentence made of muscle, a little gesture that shrugs off the world. You cost no breath and leave no sound, yet everyone who sees you understands at once. You are an answer with no words in it.
