Clap

You are a clap - the bright sharp slap of two palms struck together, the sound of being pleased. You ring out at the end of the show, you keep the time of the song, you call the dog and start the game. No hand holds you; two hands make you, again and again, until the whole crowd is one great roar. You are praise you can hear, joy with a beat to it. You are how delight escapes the body, through the palms, out loud.
