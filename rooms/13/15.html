Sway

You are a sway - the slow gentle leaning, side to side, of a thing not quite still: a tall tree in the breeze, a hanging lamp, a dancer with the music, a crowd singing along. No hand need make you; a breeze does, or a beat, or your own happy body. You cannot be held, only felt and watched, a smooth lean one way and back. You are motion at its calmest, the world keeping time with itself, rocking softly to and fro.
