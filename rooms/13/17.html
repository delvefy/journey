Shuffle

You are a shuffle - the slow dragging walk of feet that are too tired or too lazy to lift right off the ground. Slippers do you across the kitchen floor on a sleepy morning; a long queue does you forward inch by inch; old card-players do you to a deck before the deal. You are halfway between a walk and a stand-still, all scuff and scrape. You cannot be held, only made. You are the laziest way of getting anywhere, feet barely rising, going forward bit by dragging bit.

South

to soothe the baby's cry;

West
