Lantern

You are a light kept safe inside a glass case, with a handle to carry or a hook to hang you by. A hand builds you to hold a candle or a flame out of the wind and rain. The night-watchman swung you down the dark street; the miner carried you below. Your warm glow throws long shadows.

North

A great big boat that crosses the sea,
with decks and a tall mast or two;
it carries folk and cargo far -
a steamer with a funnel's plume.

East

A little glass pear that hangs from the ceiling,
it lights the room up bright;
you flick the switch, it glows and hums -
it floods the dark with light.

South

The little metal joint that lets
a door or a gate swing wide;
oil it well, or it will creak -
two flaps and a pin inside.

West

A tube you hold inside your hand
that shines a beam of light;
click it on beneath the sheets
to read a book at night.
