Kneel

You are the going-down onto the knees - to pray, to do up a small shoe, to look a child in the eye, to be knighted by the queen's light sword. No standing tall in you; you lower yourself on purpose. Down on one knee or both, you make yourself humble, and small, and close to the ground.

North

A little push with the elbow's tip,
to say 'look there' or 'hey';
a quiet poke to a friend nearby -
a hint, without a say.

East

Turn one half over onto the next,
the paper, the shirt, the sheet;
you fold it small to pack away -
corner to corner, neat.

South

Stretch your arm out, far as it goes,
up to the topmost shelf;
on tiptoe, fingers spread out wide -
to get it for yourself.

West

Stick out one finger, straight and stiff,
to show the way to go;
'Look over there!' your finger says -
though rude to do it, so.
