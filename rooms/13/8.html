Reach

You are the arm stretched out to its full length - for the high shelf, the last apple on the branch, the hand held out to be pulled up. No giving up in you; you strain that extra inch on tiptoe. Just out of reach, they say, of the thing you want most - so stretch, and stretch again.

North

Bend down low on both your knees,
to pray or do up a shoe;
down on the floor you settle there -
knees taking weight from you.

East

Bend your knees and sink down low,
all folded, close to the ground;
the cat does it before it pounces -
small, and tight, and round.

South

You do it when you touch your toes,
the road does round the hill;
a wire once straight is now a curve -
it gives, and holds it still.

West

Up the tree or up the stairs,
hand over hand you go;
the mountain, ladder, garden wall -
you reach the top, not low.
