Van

You are a van - a boxy vehicle with a roomy back, bigger than a car and smaller than a lorry, made for carrying things about. The plumber packs you with pipes, the postman with parcels, the family with the camping gear. Made by hands - welded, painted, and wheeled - you trundle the streets all day on errands. No windows in the back, just space and space. You are the workhorse of the road, the one that turns up when something needs moving.
