Truck

You are a truck - the big strong vehicle of the road, with a cab in front and a long bed behind for hauling heavy loads. You carry bricks and logs and crates from town to town, rumbling on great fat tyres. Tip your back up and the gravel slides out in a roar. Children point at you on the motorway and count your many wheels. You are muscle on wheels, the workhorse that brings the world its bricks and bread.
