Cupboard

You are the tall shut box against the wall, doors that swing to hide the plates, the cups, the tins of beans. Made by the carpenter's hand, you keep the kitchen's clutter out of sight. Shelves within, a handle without. Open your doors and the day's meals wait inside; close them, and all is tidy again.

North

The long soft seat where the family flops
to watch the screen at night;
cushions deep, with room for three -
you sink in, snug and tight.

East

A little bed with high barred sides
where the baby sleeps;
it rocks, it has a soft warm sheet -
inside, the small one keeps.

South

A single loop of thread sewn through
to join two edges tight;
the needle dips and rises up -
small even rows, drawn right.

West

Pull the handle, out it slides,
a box inside the chest;
socks and spoons and odds and ends -
push it shut to rest.
