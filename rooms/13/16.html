Rock

You are a rock - not the stone, but the slow gentle sway back and forth that soothes. A cradle does you to hush a baby; a chair does you on the porch; a boat does you on the swell. You are a made motion, set going by a hand or a foot or the water. You cannot be held, only done, on and on, the same way and back again. To rock is to comfort - the oldest sleepy beat there is.

the whole world seems to swing;

when the wind comes through the wood;

a ___, slow as you should.

and down you nearly go;
