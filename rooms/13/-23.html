You climb above the clouds and carry hundreds of people to the far side of the world in a single day. Fighter jets loop and roll; airliners cruise smooth and high. Swift and powerful and impossibly loud on take-off, you are flight at its fastest, a silver dart against the blue, here in a thunder of engines and gone over the horizon in a moment.
