Triangle

You are a triangle - a bar of bright metal bent into three sides, with one corner left open. A hand made you, and a hand taps you with a little steel beater so you ring out a single clear ting that hangs in the air. You can be held by a string, struck, hushed with a touch. The smallest, simplest voice in the whole band, you wait through the loud parts for your one perfect note. You are a shape that learned to sing.
