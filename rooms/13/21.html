Dance

You are a dance - the happy moving of a body in time to music, the spin and step and sway when the song takes hold and the feet refuse to keep still. No tool makes you; rhythm and joy do. You are the wedding twirl, the wild jump about the bedroom, the slow shuffle, the silly wiggle. Some learn you by careful steps and some just let it happen. Made of music turned to movement, you are gladness with somewhere to put itself.
