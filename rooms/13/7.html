Bend

You are the curve put into a straight thing - the road round the hill, the wire pressed into an arc, the back stooped to touch the toes. No snapping in you; you give and hold the shape. The river bends, the knee bends, the bow bends to send the arrow. Curve me, but not too far, or I break.

North

Stretch your arm out, far as it goes,
up to the topmost shelf;
on tiptoe, fingers spread out wide -
to get it for yourself.

East

Tip your body to one side,
rest against the wall;
the tower of Pisa does it too -
it tilts, but does not fall.

South

A short quick blow of breath or air,
the candle's flame put out;
the steam train chuffs, the smoker's pipe -
that breath comes puffing out.

West

Press it tight in both your hands
till the juice or air comes out;
a hug too hard, a sponge wrung dry,
the toothpaste tube, no doubt.
