Nod

You are a nod - the little dip of the head down and up that says 'yes' without a word, or 'hello' to a passing friend. You bob when you agree, and bob again when sleep creeps up on a boring afternoon and the head keeps falling forward. A nod is a promise, a greeting, a small bow of the chin. One quick dip and everyone knows your answer. You are the head's plainest little word, a yes made out of one small movement.
