Curl

You are a curl - the coiling of a thing round into a cosy ring. Hair springs into you in little corkscrews; a cat tucks nose to tail and becomes a furry circle; a hedgehog rolls into a prickly ball; smoke winds up in lazy loops. You are a curve that keeps on curving until it meets itself. No hand can hold you - you are the snug round shape a thing makes when it wraps up small and safe.
