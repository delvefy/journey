Sofa

The softest seat in the house, long enough to lie down on - a sofa is comfort with arms. It catches you after a long day, swallows lost coins in its cushions, holds the whole family on film night in a row of warm shoulders. Built of wood and stuffing and cloth, it asks nothing and gives only ease. You sink into it and the day lets go. Of all the made things in a home, it is the kindest to a tired body.

North

A long hard seat
of wood or stone;
in the park you rest
on me alone.

East

A tall cold box
that hums all day;
it keeps the food fresh
and the milk, they say.

South

A box on the wall
with a door and a shelf;
I hold the plates and cups -
open me yourself.

West

A soft thick cloth
that covers the floor;
warm on bare feet
from wall to door.
