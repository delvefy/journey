Piano

You are a piano - a great wooden box of music with a long row of black and white keys. Press a key and a little hammer strikes a string inside; press many and a tune pours out, low notes growling at one end and tiny tinkling ones at the other. A child plinks out chopsticks; a player makes the whole room hush. Built and tuned by a hand, grand and heavy, you are a whole orchestra a single pair of hands can play.
