Hundred

You are the number one hundred - a one and two round noughts, ten tens stacked up, a big round milestone of a number. No hand makes you; the counting mind builds you. A hundred years is a century; a hundred runs is a famous score; counting to you takes a while. You are the first really big number a child learns to climb to.

North

The tiny high sound a mouse lets out,
or a new shoe on the floor;
'eek!' goes the hinge that wants some oil -
a small thin sound, no more.

East

One less than ten, one more than eight,
the highest single number;
a cat is said to have this many lives -
count them up, no blunder.

South

How many blind mice in the rhyme,
how many sides has a triangle got,
how many bears found Goldilocks?
Count them - it's the lot.

West

Count your fingers, all of them,
held up on two spread hands;
count your toes, the same again -
how many? where it lands.
