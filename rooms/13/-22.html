Helicopter

You are a helicopter - a flying machine with a great spinning rotor of blades on top, that beats the air so hard it lifts straight up, no runway needed, and can hover stock-still in the sky. Hands build you and a pilot tilts you this way and that. You clatter loud overhead, you rescue folk off mountains and roofs, you land in a field in a storm of wind. Whirring and hovering and noisy, you are the one flying thing that can stop and stand still in the air.
