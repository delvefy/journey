Tractor

You are a tractor - the chugging workhorse of the farm, with little wheels in front and great fat ones behind, built to drag the plough and the trailer through the heavy mud. A farmer climbs up high in your cab and you grumble across the field all day, turning the soil in long brown stripes. The gulls follow you for the worms. Slow and strong and splattered with earth, you are the most useful, least hurried machine there is, doing the hard work of the land.
