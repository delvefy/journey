Xylophone

You are a xylophone - a row of little wooden bars, each a different length, laid out like a ladder and struck with a pair of beaters. A hand made you, tuning each bar to its own note; a hand plays you, bouncing the beaters from bar to bar to make a bright tinkling tune. You can be carried, the bars rearranged, the beaters lost down the back of the sofa. From a toy one to a big concert one, you are music you can hammer out, plink by colourful plink.
