Kidney

You are a kidney - a bean-shaped organ tucked away in the small of the back, and there are two of you, one each side. All day you sieve the blood as it flows through, taking out the waste and the spare water and sending it off as wee. Without you the body would soon be poisoned by its own rubbish. Quiet and tireless and shaped just like a butter bean, you are the body's washing machine, scrubbing the blood clean over and over, day and night, and never asking for thanks.
