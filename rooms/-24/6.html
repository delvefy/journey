Love

You are love - the warm strong feeling that ties a heart to the people and things it holds most dear, the wish to be near them and keep them safe. No hand makes you; it grows on its own between a parent and a child, between friends, between a person and their old dog. You make folk hug and share and forgive and stay up worrying. The biggest and quietest of all the feelings, you are what is left when everything else is taken away.
