Slam

You are a slam - the hard bang of a door swung shut with all the force a temper can give it, rattling the windows and shaking the wall. No gentle hand makes you; a gust or a flounce or a fury does. You make everyone jump and someone shout 'don't slam the door!' from another room. A full stop made of noise, you are the loudest way to leave a room, the bang that says, with no words at all, that someone is cross.
