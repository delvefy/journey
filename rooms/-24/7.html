Burp

You are a burp - the gassy bubble of air that comes back up the throat after a fizzy drink or a fast gulped dinner, and pops out loud just when it shouldn't. No one quite means you; a swallowed bubble does. You are rude and sudden and a little bit funny, and the bigger you are the harder it is not to laugh. 'Pardon!' they say after you. A small escaped burst of swallowed air, you are the body's cheekiest, least polite little noise.
