Hoof

You are a hoof - the hard horny foot of a horse or a cow or a deer, a single great toenail grown tough enough to carry the whole heavy beast. No hand makes you; the animal grows you. You clip-clop on the cobbles and thunder on the turf and need a metal shoe nailed on to keep from wearing through. You kick when the beast is cross. Hard and round and tireless, you are the boot a four-legged thing is born already wearing.
