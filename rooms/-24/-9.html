Ember

You are an ember - the last glowing lump in a dying fire, no longer a flame but still red-hot, pulsing orange when you are blown on and grey when you are left. No hand makes you; a fire burning low does. You hide under the ash, keeping the heat alive, ready to catch a new log come morning. Campers bury you carefully to wake the fire at dawn. Warm and glowing and patient, you are the fire's sleeping heart, still alight long after the flames have gone.
