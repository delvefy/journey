Rustle

You are a rustle - the soft dry whisper of many small things stirring at once: leaves in the hedge, paper in a bag, a mouse in the straw. You are barely a sound at all, a papery shiver that comes and goes with the wind. No hand makes you; the breeze does, brushing through the dry and the crinkly. You make the autumn wood sound alive. You are the quietest noise the world keeps, the soft secret stir at the edge of hearing.
