Wrinkle

You are a wrinkle - a small soft crease folded into skin or cloth. You gather at the corners of a grandmother's smiling eyes, you crinkle a shirt left too long in the wash, you ruck up the bedsheet under a restless sleeper. No hand makes you on purpose; time and creasing do. You can be smoothed with an iron or a careful palm, or earned slowly over a long happy life. You are the little fold the years and the days press in, the soft record of a life well lived.
