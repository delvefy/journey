Sniffle

You are a sniffle - the wet snuffly sound of a runny nose sniffed back in rather than blown, the sound of a cold settling in or a small cry not quite finished. No one chooses you; a head full of cold or a wobble of tears does. You go on all day, snnff, snnff, and someone hands you a tissue at last. Soggy and stuffy and a little bit sad, you are the sound of being not quite well and not quite over it.
