Bone

You are a bone - one hard white piece of the frame that holds a body up. Stacked and joined together, you and the rest make the skeleton, the scaffolding under the skin: long ones in the legs, little ones in the fingers, a great cage of them round the heart. Dogs love to gnaw you, doctors x-ray you when you crack. Strong and pale and built to last, you give the soft squishy body its shape and let it stand and walk and run, hidden away inside all your life.
