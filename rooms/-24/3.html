Draft

You are a draft - the thin cold thread of moving air that finds the gap under the door and slides in where it is not wanted. No one makes you on purpose; a cracked window and a warm room do, and you creep along the floor to chill an ankle. You make the candle gutter and the curtain stir. Too small to be a wind, too sly to be ignored, you are the cold breath of outside, leaking quietly into the warm.
