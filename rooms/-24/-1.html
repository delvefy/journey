Bruise

You are a bruise - the tender blue-black mark left under the skin where it was knocked. No cut, no blood, just a spreading patch that aches when pressed and slowly changes colour: purple, then green, then a sickly yellow as it fades. No hand made you on purpose; a door, a fall, a flying ball did. You are the body remembering the bump for a week or two. You are a hurt that shows without breaking the skin, a little map of where it all went wrong.
