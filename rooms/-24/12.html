Sunbeam

You are a sunbeam - one bright shaft of sunlight, slanting down through a gap in the clouds or a chink in the curtains. Where you fall, the dust dances golden in your light and the cat curls up to bask. You stripe the forest floor between the trees and lay a warm bar across the carpet. Slim and golden and warm to sit in, you are sunlight given a shape you can almost touch, a single glowing ladder of light reaching down from the sky to the ground.
