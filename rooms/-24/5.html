Snowstorm

You are a snowstorm - the wild winter weather when the snow comes not in flakes but in armfuls, driven sideways on a screaming wind until the world goes white and lost. No hand sends you; the cold sky and the gale do, piling the snow in great soft drifts against the doors. You blot out the road and the sky and the next house along. A blinding, muffling fury of white, you shut the whole world indoors and bury it deep until you have blown yourself out.
