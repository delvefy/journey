Nightfall

You are nightfall - the quiet turning of the day, when the last light drains from the sky and the dark comes creeping in. The birds fall silent, the lamps blink on in the windows, and shadows pool under the trees. Bats come out, the first star pricks the sky, and the world settles down to sleep. Soft and certain and a little bit hushed, you are the gentle closing of the day's long door, the hour when light gives way to dark and the night takes over.
