Compost

You are compost - the dark crumbly stuff a garden heap becomes when peelings and leaves and grass clippings are left to rot down together over the months. No hand makes you all at once; worms and time and warmth do. You steam on a cold morning and smell of rich brown earth. Dug into the beds, you feed the carrots and the roses. The gardener's quiet magic, you turn the kitchen's rubbish into the best food a plant could want.
