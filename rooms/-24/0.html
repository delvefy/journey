Volcano

You are a volcano - a mountain with fire in its belly, a chimney straight down to the molten heart of the world. For years you sleep, green and quiet, and then you wake and roar, hurling ash to the sky and pouring red rivers down your sides. No hand made you; the deep earth did, pushing up through a crack. Whole islands are your old work, cooled and grown over. You are the mountain that is never finished, building and burning by turns.
