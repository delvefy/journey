Coconut

You are a coconut - a great brown hairy ball that grows high in the palm and drops like a cannonball to the sand. No hand makes you; the island sun does, packing sweet white milk inside a shell so hard you need a hammer to get in. You bob across whole oceans to wash up and sprout on a new shore. Three little eyes, a coat of bristles, you are the toughest fruit of all the tropics.
