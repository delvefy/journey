Pineapple

You are a pineapple - a fat golden barrel of fruit in a coat of diamond scales, with a spiky green crown of leaves perched on your top like a king. No hand makes you; the hot far countries do, growing you low and slow in the sun. Inside you are bright yellow rings, sweet and sharp enough to tingle the tongue. Tough and prickly outside, gold and juicy within, you are the most tropical treat to ever roll into a fruit bowl.
