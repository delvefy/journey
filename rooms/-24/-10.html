Pollen

You are pollen - the fine golden dust a flower makes deep in its petals, brushed onto the legs of every visiting bee and carried off to the next bloom so new seeds can grow. No hand makes you; the flower does, by the dustful. You drift on the summer breeze and settle on the car and set hay-fever noses sneezing. Tiny and golden and everywhere in spring, you are the flower's powdery message, sent out on the wind and the bee to make more flowers.
