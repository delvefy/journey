Hammock

You are a hammock - a sling of net or canvas tied between two trees or two posts, that you climb into and lie in as it rocks gently side to side. Hands knot you up at each end and a lazy afternoon does the rest. You are wonderfully hard to get into and even harder to get out of without tipping flat onto the grass. Swinging and saggy and sun-dappled, you are the laziest bed there is, made for dozing in the shade with a book sliding off your chest.
