Stride

You are a stride - the long swinging step of someone walking with purpose. Legs reach out, arms swing, the ground falls away beneath you, and you eat up the distance without seeming to hurry. A tall person strides; a person in a rush strides; a person who knows exactly where they are going strides. Easy and rangy and sure, you are walking at its most confident, each big step covering twice the ground of a little one, head up and shoulders back, going somewhere that matters.
