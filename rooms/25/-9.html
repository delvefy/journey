Mitten

You are a mitten - a cosy hand-warmer with no separate fingers, just one snug pouch for all four to huddle in together, and a little stall for the thumb. On a frosty day you keep small hands far warmer than a glove, because the fingers share their warmth. You dangle from a string up the coat sleeves so you are never lost. Woolly and warm and round at the end, you are winter's friendliest glove, knitted by grans and pulled on for snowball fights and frosty walks.
