Highfive

You are a highfive - the happy slap of one raised flat palm against another, hands up high, smack, to share a triumph: a goal scored, a test passed, a joke landed. No tool makes you; two pleased people and two quick hands do. Sometimes you miss and slap the air, which is even funnier. Sharp and loud and grinning, you are joy turned into a slap, the quickest way two friends can say well done without a single word.
