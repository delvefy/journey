Paintbrush

You are a paintbrush - a slim wooden handle with a tuft of soft bristles at the end, made to load up with colour and sweep across the page. Dip in red, dip in blue, and away you go: bold splodges, fine little lines, a whole bright picture. Fat ones for the sky, thin ones for the whiskers. Swishy and colourful and a bit messy, you are the artist's magic wand, turning a blank white sheet into anything at all, one wet stroke of colour at a time.
