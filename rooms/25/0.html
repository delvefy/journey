Chain

You are a chain - a long line of metal loops hooked one through the next, made by hands to pull and hold and bind. Each single link is weak, but joined together you can haul a ship or lock a gate or hang a swing. You clink and rattle and never stretch. You are only as strong as your weakest loop, they say. Cold and heavy and endlessly long, you are strength made out of small things linked, the proof that many little rings can hold what no one ring could.
