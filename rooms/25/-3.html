Pen

You are a pen - a slim hard stick that bleeds a thin line of ink wherever its tip is dragged, and what you write stays for good, no rubbing out. A hand clicks you open and signs a name, fills in a form, doodles in a margin. You run dry at the worst moment and leak in a shirt pocket. You sign the most important papers in the world. Permanent and businesslike, you are the grown-up's writing tool, the one that means a thing is really, truly done.
