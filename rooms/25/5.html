Blink

You are a blink - the quick shut-and-open of the eyes, so fast it is over before you notice, done a thousand times a day without a thought, to keep the eyes wet and clean. No one decides to do you; the body does it on its own. You happen quicker than anything, which is why we say a thing was gone in one. Hold you back too long and the eyes begin to sting. Tiny and constant and unnoticed, you are the eyelids' steady, faithful little wink at the world.
