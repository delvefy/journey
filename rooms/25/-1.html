Needle

You are a needle - a thin bright sliver of steel, sharp at one end and pierced with a tiny eye at the other, made for carrying thread through cloth. A hand licks the thread and squints and pokes it through your eye, then darts you in and out, in and out, drawing two pieces into one. You hide cruelly in the carpet for a bare foot to find. Small and sharp and quick, you are the smallest tool that mends and makes, stitching the whole wardrobe together one prick at a time.
