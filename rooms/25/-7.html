Curtain

You are a curtain - the cloth hung in folds at a window, drawn across with a swish at bedtime to shut out the dark, and flung open in the morning to let the day pour in. Hands hem you and hang you on rings along a rail. You billow when the window is open and hide a small one playing peep behind your folds. The theatre's great one rises on the play. Soft and folding and full of light, you are the window's gentle eyelid, opening and closing on the day.
