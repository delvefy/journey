Coop

You are a coop - the snug wooden house where the hens live, with a little ramp up to the door and a row of cosy boxes inside for laying eggs. At dusk the chickens climb up and settle on their perch, and you shut tight to keep the fox out in the dark. By day they scratch about in the run beside you. Warm and safe and smelling of straw, you are the henhouse at the bottom of the garden, where the morning's eggs are gathered up still warm.
