Marker

You are a marker - a fat felt-tipped pen with a juicy nib, made to draw a bold thick line that you can read from across the room. A hand pops your cap and squeaks you across a poster or a whiteboard. You bleed through thin paper and stain a careless finger. Left without your lid, you dry up and die. Bold and bright and a little bit smelly, you are the loudest pen in the tin, the one for big letters and bigger pictures.
