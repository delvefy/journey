corner

A corner is where two lines meet and the world turns sharp. In a room it is where the walls shake hands; on the pitch it is the flag at the edge, where a corner-kick is swung in high toward the goal. You stand a ball there, on the very point, and curl it into the crowd of players. Made by ruled lines and right angles, yet you cannot pick one up - it is only the meeting of two edges, the place a turn begins.

North

Strike the ball while still in air,
before it hits the ground;
a sudden swinging boot or bat -
no bounce, just thwack, full sound.

East

Swing your foot and meet the ball,
send it flying down the pitch;
no hands allowed in all this game -
your boot does every hit.

South

Hee-haw, hee-haw, the donkey calls,
so loud across the farm;
the rudest, silliest, loudest sound -
it means the beast no harm.

West

No feet, no hands, just leap up high
and meet the ball with your brow;
nod it into the net to score -
a goal, the crowd goes wow.
