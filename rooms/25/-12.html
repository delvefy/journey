clarinet

A clarinet is a long black tube of wood, all silver keys and little holes, with a single reed that buzzes when you blow. You cover the holes with your fingers, press the shining keys, and out floats a smooth woody note - low and round, or high and clear. Made by careful hands in a workshop, it is wholly a thing you can hold, cold and heavy and gleaming. Of all the band, it sounds the most like a calm, talking voice.

with three small buttons on top;

a stubby purple stick;

with pipes up to the roof;
press my keys and music booms -
