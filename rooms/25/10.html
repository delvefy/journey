Snooze

You are a snooze - a short light sleep, the lovely extra few minutes after the alarm goes off and you snuggle back down. Not a deep night's sleep, just a cosy drifting half-doze, eyes shut, warm under the covers, putting off getting up. Grandads have one in the armchair after lunch; cats have them all day long. Gentle and drowsy and just a little bit naughty, you are sleep stolen back in small delicious helpings, the snug five-more-minutes that somehow always turns into twenty.
