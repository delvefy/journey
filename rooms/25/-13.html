gluestick

A gluestick is a fat little tube of glue you twist up from the bottom like a chunky lipstick, then rub over your paper to leave a tacky, slightly shiny smear. No mess, no spills, no waiting - press your picture down and it holds. The lid pops on with a satisfying click, and if you forget it the glue dries hard as a rock. The hero of every craft table and scrapbook, it sticks the whole afternoon together.
