Open

North and west, where nothing was made and nothing can be grasped, you reach the lightest corner of the world. A strange word for the farthest room — for a place walled on two sides by the edge of creation itself. But you came here through doors, every one shut until you spoke. This was never the absence of a wall; it was the word that passed you through. The world ends here, and it ends unlocked.

East

I am not silence, but its hand:
the sudden quiet laid on a hall,
the held breath before the storm,
the word you lay upon a child.
Say me aloud, and you undo me.

South

I am the thin ghost of the rain,
lighter than fog, the breath of dawn
that lies along the river-field
and lifts the moment day comes on.
