Brag

You are a brag - the loud crowing of someone pleased with themselves, telling everyone how fast, how strong, how clever they are. No tool makes you; pride with the volume up does. You puff out the chest and trumpet every win, twice as big as it really was. Nobody loves a bragger for long. Half a boast and half a show-off, you are the noise pride makes when it cannot keep its triumphs to itself.
