Guess

You are a guess - the answer you give when you do not really know, a shot in the dark, a hopeful stab. 'How many sweets in the jar?' - you take one; 'which hand is it in?' - you point. You can be wild or clever, lucky or wrong. A good one feels like magic; a bad one just makes everyone laugh. You are knowledge's cheerful stand-in, the brave little leap a mind makes when it has not been told the answer.
