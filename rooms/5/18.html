Word

You are a word - the smallest piece of language that means a thing on its own. 'Cat', 'jump', 'hello', 'why': each one is a tiny parcel of meaning, made of letters in a row. String enough of us together and you have a story, a song, a whole shouted argument. You live on the tongue, on the page, in the head. No hand can hold you - you are the brick that every sentence, every book, every kind or cruel thing ever said, is built from.

East

A secret way of writing things,
in dots and dashes, numbers, signs;

South

The softest way of speaking,
breathed so none but one can hear;
a secret told behind a hand -
soft words for a single ear.

West

A happy shout you give your team,
'hooray!' and 'go!' and 'yay!';
the whole crowd roars when they score a goal -
a ___ to win the day.
