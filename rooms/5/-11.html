Slide

You are a long smooth slope of shining metal in the playground, with a ladder up the back and a swoosh down the front. A hand bolts you together and sets you on the grass. Children queue to climb you, then whoosh down your slick face and tumble off the end, laughing, to run round and climb again.

North

A long thin thread of bendy metal,
it carries the electric light;
it fences fields, it twists to shapes -
bend it, it holds tight.

East

A mesh of knotted string with holes,
to catch the fish or ball;
the goal, the trawl, the butterfly's doom -
the water slips through all.

South

A board with two small wheels and a bar,
you stand and push with a foot;
you zoom along the pavement fast -
the brake's where your heel is put.

West

A little spinning toy you flick
or wind up with a string;
it dances on its pointed tip
till it tires of the thing.
