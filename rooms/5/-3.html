Saw

A long flat blade lined with sharp teeth, pushed and pulled until it bites clean through the board and the sawdust falls in drifts. The saw is a made thing, all edge and rhythm, the cutter of the carpenter. It keeps the worked south-east, among tools and timbers. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A thick steel pin with a threaded end,
a nut winds on to clamp me tight;
I fasten beam to beam as one —
name the fastening pin. Hold right.

East

A rough flat bar of hardened steel,
I rasp and smooth the metal's edge;
rubbed back and forth, I wear it down —
name the grinding tool. Now pledge.

South

Two jaws that screw down hard and tight,
I grip the work and hold it still;
I press the glued boards while they dry —
name the holding tool. I will.

West

A blade set sideways on a shaft,
I hack the weeds and break the crust;
I draw the soil up round the rows —
name the garden tool. I must.
