Fan

You are a fan - a set of blades on a spinning hub that whips the still air into a breeze. On the hottest day you whirr away in the corner, ruffling the papers and cooling the sweat. Some of you fold up in the hand, paper pleats that flutter with a flick of the wrist. You are wrought, built by a hand to make a wind where there is none. Switch you off and the room goes hot and quiet again.
