Mumble

You are a mumble - the low unclear talking of someone who will not quite open their mouth, the words all sliding into a soft mush nobody can make out. No one means you; shyness or sulking or a mouthful of food does. 'Speak up!' the grown-ups say, leaning in. You hide the answer inside a hum. Quiet and blurry and hard to catch, you are speech with the volume and the edges turned right down, talk that would rather not quite be heard.
