Speak

You are to speak - to open your mouth and turn thought into words out loud. You speak your name, you speak the truth, you speak up at the back of the class. Not a song, not a shout, just plain words said so others hear them. Babies learn to speak one word at a time; the brave speak their mind. To speak is to send what is inside your head out into the world as sound.
