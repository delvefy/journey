Boat

Step in carefully, and the whole thing rocks. A boat is a clever hollow - wood or steel bent into a shape that holds the water out and you in, so that a heavy thing made by hands can ride on the soft back of a river or the sea. Push off, and the bank slides away. There is nothing under you now but a plank and the deep, and the slow tug of the oars carrying you across.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

North

I am a puzzle made of words,
I speak of frogs and keys and birds;
I hide one answer, sly and small -
guess it right, and pass the wall.

East

I run on iron rails all day,
with many carriages in a way;
I chuff and whistle, long and slow,
from town to town the people go.

South

I have a handle and a spout,
I hold the milk and pour it out;
fat and round upon the table,
I fill your cup whenever able.

West

I am a box on wheels you pull,
with hay or apples loaded full;
a horse or hand can drag me round,
I rattle slow across the ground.
