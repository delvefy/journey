Axle

The straight rod a wheel spins upon, holding the two wheels of a cart in line. The axle is a made thing of wood or iron, plain and strong, the still heart of all that rolls. It keeps the wrought east, among gears and machines. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

North

The steady pulse beneath a song,
you tap your foot and clap along;
drum it, count it, one-two-three-four —
the time that keeps the music's law.

East

A wheel with teeth all round its rim,
I turn the next and pass the spin;
inside the clock, inside the mill,
tooth meets tooth and I drive it in.

South

A heavy head upon a handle stout,
I drive the nail with a ringing blow;
the carpenter swings me, the smith as well —
down on the iron, hard I go.

West

Round I roll and round I run,
on cart and bike and motorcar;
a hub, a rim, and spokes between —
without me you'd not travel far.
