Grin

You are the widest of smiles, all teeth and stretched-back cheeks, the look of mischief or of pure delight. No object, only a shape the face throws on purpose - the Cheshire cat's that lingers when the rest has gone, the cat-that-got-the-cream, the toothy beam of someone up to something. You can be sheepish, sly, or huge with joy. A wrought arrangement of the mouth, you are happiness or cunning written plain across a face, ear to delighted ear.

North

A sulky push of the lower lip
when you don't get your way;
the bottom lip stuck out and down -
a grumpy face display.

East

A quick sharp draw of air up the nose,
to smell, or stem a drip;
the dog does it along the ground,
a short and snuffly sip.

South

Eyes locked still upon one thing,
unblinking, fixed, and long;
rude at strangers, lost in dreams -
a gaze that lingers on.

West

A short sharp crack as something breaks,
a twig, a stick, a bone;
or fingers clicked to keep the beat -
a sound that stands alone.
