Wire

You are a long thin thread of metal, bendy but strong - drawn out from iron or copper by a hand and a machine. You carry the spark of electric light through the walls; you fence the field; you twist into shapes that hold. Bend you and you stay bent. Bare and bright or wrapped in rubber skin, you are the metal string that runs unseen through everything.

North

A curl of metal, sharp of point,
to hang a coat or catch a fish;
shaped like a letter J or U -
it grabs the thing you wish.

East

Thin and soft and good for tying,
to fly a kite up high;
a ball of it sits in the drawer -
you snip a length to tie.

South

You climb the ladder to the top,
then whoosh - down you go!
A long smooth slope of shining steel -
the playground's best, you know.

West

A seat that hangs on two long ropes,
you push your feet and fly;
back and forth, then up and down,
you almost touch the sky.
