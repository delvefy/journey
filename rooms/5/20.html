List

You are a list - things written down one under the next so that none gets forgotten. The shopping, the chores, the names for a party, all lined up tidy with a box to tick. You are made of words and order, a thing a hand writes and a hand crosses off. You cannot be touched as a thing, only kept and read and added to. Short or long, you turn a muddle of jobs into a plain straight line.
