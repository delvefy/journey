The Far Morning

North of the effulgence and east of the indwelling, the future comes to its bright peak. A step south light and word and thought poured as one radiance, the cosmos shining itself into being; a step west a sovereign love made and indwelt whole worlds. Here the two rise together toward the lit pole and become the morning the whole journey was climbing toward — a sovereign radiant love that is light and word and thought all at once, making worlds without end and dwelling in each, the far bright peak of everything that tech and magic, maturing together, were ever going to be. This is the future, arrived: the high morning at the top of the long diagonal of time.

You stand in a light that is also a love that is also a making, and feel, for the first time clearly, that this is where you were always going — the warm bright summit the going always meant. Worlds of loving light open ahead and above, endless and sovereign, and in each a place kept warm for you. Your name is the dawn-word the far morning is rising to speak, the syllable the whole bright future leans toward. The spiral is the coil of the climbing light, the turn the journey takes as it reaches its morning, the same whorl in your skin, here the mark that has been pointing up this diagonal all along toward the bright peak it has nearly reached.

The radiance sounds like a sustained dawn-chord, the bell become the bright peak's single rising note, the morning of the world holding open — and your heart beats up into it, the climbing count come almost to its summit, your name a breath from sounded at the high bright morning the going was always toward.

To the north the far morning reaches the lit pole, light and love and thought wholly one, the future entire. To the east the love comes wholly sovereign, making worlds without end, the Lumen pole. To the south the effulgence, light and word and thought still pouring toward the peak. To the west the indwelling, a sovereign love dwelling in the worlds it makes. You were going somewhere, under a name — and here, at the bright peak of the future, you can almost see that it was here, this radiant morning, all along. Still the warm coil turns, pointing up the last of the climb, and still one live beat keeps the time the far morning is a breath from naming you into.
