Shrug

You are the body's plainest answer to a question it cannot solve: two shoulders lifted to the ears, palms turned up, and let fall again. 'Who knows?' you say, 'not me,' 'what can you do?' - and never spend a word. You can mean don't care, or can't help it, or have it your way; the meaning rides on the face above you. Quick and silent, a little tilt of the frame, you close a matter as neatly as any speech, and ask for nothing back.

North

My brows draw down, my mouth turns low,
when I am cross or sad;
the very opposite, you know,
of the smile that means I'm glad.

East

A sudden breath sucked sharp and quick,
when something gives a fright;
'oh!' you cry, your breath goes thick,
at a wonder or a sight.

South

You do not know, so take a try,
and name the hidden thing;
you might be wrong, you might be sly,
a stab in the dark you fling.

West

Round your lips and blow the air,
a sharp clear note flies free;
you call the dog, you start the fair,
no words, just tune from me.
