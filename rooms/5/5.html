Guess

You are the answer offered when no one truly knows - a leap across the gap where a fact should be. Sometimes you are wild, a finger stabbed at the dark; sometimes you are clever, half built of hints and hunches. You are how every discovery begins, and how most games are won and lost. Be wrong, and you cost nothing; be right, and you look like magic. You are the brave little word the mind says when it would rather try than stay silent.

North

You lift your shoulders up and down,
to say 'I do not know';
no words at all, a little heave,
and then you let them go.

East

Wiggle your fingers light and quick,
beneath the arm or chin;
the giggles burst, they cannot help it,
and laughter pours right in.

South

A curve that lifts upon your face,
when something makes you glad;
no sound at all, just happy lips,
the cure for feeling sad.

West

When something's funny, out I burst,
'ha-ha' and 'hee' and 'ho';
I shake your sides and wet your eyes,
the merriest sound you know.
