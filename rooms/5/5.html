The Effulgence

North of the utterance and east of the contemplation, the word and the thought become light. A step south the cosmos was a single endless utterance, the gods speaking the world into being syllable by syllable; a step west it was a single endless contemplation, a god-mind thinking the world real by regarding it. Here speaking and thinking are one act, and that act is light — an effulgence pouring from nowhere and everywhere at once, a brilliance that is also a saying and also a thought, the cosmos shining itself into being, sovereign, self-uttered, self-considered, needing no speaker and no thinker outside its own radiance.

You stand in the pouring light, and it is full of meaning the way water is full of light — every ray a word, every word a thought, every thought a thing made bright and real in the shining. Your name is the one figure in the radiance not yet wholly lit: the syllable unsaid, the thought unfinished, the dark place the effulgence is flowing in to fill. You feel it gather, light and word and consideration all leaning toward you at once, the whole bright cosmos bending its radiance to lay you alight. The spiral winds out of the brilliance to reach you, a coil of living light, the same whorl in your skin, here the shape the effulgence makes as it pours toward the one unkindled thing left in it — you.

There is no count but the shining itself, the cosmos sounding and thinking and blazing in one sustained chord, the bell become pure radiance — and your name the one note the chord is bending to strike, the unlit syllable the effulgence is a breath from kindling into the bright sentence of everything.

To the north the effulgence climbs toward the lit pole, light wholly word and wholly thought, the bright peak of the future. To the east the magic in it goes sovereign, the cosmos nothing but the gods' radiant saying, the Lumen's heart. To the south it gathers back into the utterance, a world still being spoken. To the west it narrows to the contemplation, a world being thought. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the whole shining cosmos is bending to kindle it, light and word and thought all leaning to say you alight — and still the warm coil turns at the unlit centre, and still one live beat keeps time in the radiance, the one dark syllable the bright world is about, at last, to speak.
