Camera

You are a camera - the little box you point, frame, and click to catch a moment and keep it forever. "Say cheese!" and everyone grins; the shutter blinks and the day is saved. You freeze the birthday, the holiday, the silly face, and hold them long after they have gone. Some of you flash, some of you click, some of you hide inside a phone. You are a memory machine, a small box that steals a slice of time and keeps it safe to look at later.
