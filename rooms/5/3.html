The Unfinished City

North of the spoken hall and east of the guest, the naming turns upon itself, and the place builds itself without end. The hall a step south reshaped to the words spoken in it; here the city speaks its own self into being, endlessly, a reality of light that names itself street by street and god by god and never finishes — towers rising as their names are said, ways opening as they are called, the whole bright world in perpetual self-creation, gods of light building it forever as they walk it.

It is the Lumen near its pole, where to be is to be spoken and the speaking never stops. The spiral is the engine of it: an endless coil of light winding outward, name calling name calling name, a self-naming that never closes its loop. And in all that ceaseless making, one name goes unsaid — yours. The city speaks everything into being but you; it builds and builds around the space where you stand and leaves you the one unbuilt thing, the gap in the bright design. The gods turn, and you feel the offer in their turning: that they would name you too, build you a self into the endless making, set your name in the coil and let it raise you with the rest. The whorl in your skin is the place the city would begin. It leans to speak you. The making waits on the one word it has not yet said.

The perpetual naming is a continuous chord, the bell ringing new being without pause, an hour that does not pass but accumulates, time laid up as more and more world — and your name the one note held back, the silence at the centre of all that sound.

To the north the city refines toward pure light naming itself, the lit pole's endless self-creation. To the east the world wholly self-dreams, the magic pole, reality naming itself without end. To the south the building narrows to a single hall reshaped by what is spoken in it. To the west it gathers to one guest, giving you a name palm to palm. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a whole world builds itself forever and leaves your name unsaid, the one gap in the bright design — and the gods lean to speak it and raise you into the endless city, the making held, forever, on the verge of you.
