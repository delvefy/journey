Refrain

The line that comes around again at the end of every verse — the bit you remember and hum, returning and returning. A refrain is made wholly of words and tune, the song's own echo of itself. It keeps the made north-east, among songs and verses. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

The big bright part where all join in,
the crowd, the choir, the whole room's voice;
sung out together, loud and strong —
name the shared part. Make your choice.

East

A poem of just fourteen lines,
of love most often, neat and tight;
old Shakespeare penned a hundred-odd —
name the short fixed verse, just right.

South

Eight notes from one tone to its twin,
from 'do' right up to 'do' again;
the span a singer's voice can climb —
name the eight-step reach. Sing then.

West

A solemn song a nation sings,
that stirs the blood and lifts the head;
we rise and sing it, proud and loud —
name the great song. Once it's said.
