Score

It is the running count, kept aloud or chalked on a board: how many goals, how many points, who is ahead and by how much. A score is made by people keeping track - it has no weight and no colour, only a number that climbs as the game goes on. It can be level, it can be a landslide; at the final whistle it is fixed forever. Win or lose, it is how we know who won.

North

Turn the corners of your mouth right up,
and wear me on your face;
I cost you nothing, give for free,
and brighten any place.

East

I am a dip of the head for 'yes',
a silent way to agree;
no word at all, I tip my chin,
and that's enough from me.

South

I am made of letters in a row,
I carry one small thought;
you speak me, write me, read me out,
on every page and note.

West

When your side has won the day,
you raise a happy roar;
'Hooray!' the glad crowd shouts as one,
and calls for me once more.
