Phone

You are a phone - the little handheld box that lets you talk to someone miles away, as if they stood beside you. Once you hung on the wall with a curly cord and a dial; now you slip in a pocket, glowing with games and photos and a thousand chattering messages. You ring, you buzz, you light up with a name. You bring far-off voices near and put the whole world in a hand. You are the pocket-sized doorway to everyone you know.
