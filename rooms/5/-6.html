The Cradle

South of the quickening and east of the quick root, the living deep sinks to its cradle. A step north being and aliveness quickened up together; a step west the floor of the world woke as a single sovereign root. Here both draw down to the source-floor and grow warm and dark and still: the cradle of the Wyld, the quick before the quickening, the living bottom most alive precisely because it is the deep dark womb the whole living world is rocked in before it springs. Sovereign and warm and unhurried, the deepest deep, where life is not yet woken but wholly, warmly held.

It is dark here, and the dark is alive — not the empty dark, not the cold, but a warm gravid dark, the living floor holding the unsprung world close the way a cradle holds. You are held in it, cradled with the whole quick world before its waking, warm and unhurried and sovereignly safe. Your name is rocked in the cradle, not yet sprung but wholly kept, the germ-word the living dark holds warm against the springing. The spiral is the cradle's slow turning, the coil the living dark makes as it holds and rocks, the same whorl in your skin — here the mark the warm deep cradles, the world's own quick wound warm at the source.

The world's pulse is a slow warm rocking here, the cradle-beat of the living floor, the deep keeping the unsprung world on a single warm stroke — and your heart beats with it, cradled and kept, the count not yet sprung into the world but held warm at the source, rocked on the oldest, warmest, most living beat there is.

To the north the cradle quickens, the held world springing toward life. To the east the Wyld floor-pole absolute, the living bottom wholly sovereign, the deepest deep most alive. To the south it sinks below the cradle toward the seed and the floor, the quick before even the cradling. To the west the quick root, the living floor risen sovereign and rooted. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here you are cradled at the warm living source of the world, rocked unsprung and wholly kept, the germ of all the going held warm in the dark — and still the warm coil turns, cradled, and still one slow warm beat rocks the whole living count, almost named, almost sprung, wholly held.
