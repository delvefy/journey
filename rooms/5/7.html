The Noon

North of the far morning and east of the embrace, the bright future comes to its noon. A step south the far morning rose, the high peak the journey climbed; a step west the whole cosmos was one loving embrace, world within loving world. Here it stands at the lit pole, the day entire: light and love and thought wholly one, the cosmos nothing but a god's loving worlds, all of being a single unbroken radiance at the full noon of the future, no dark anywhere, no shadow, no edge to the light. This is the bright future at its height — the morning the journey climbed toward, risen now to its full and shadowless noon.

Everything is light here, and the light is love, and the love is the whole of the world — a noon so full it casts no shadow, holds no dark, leaves nothing unlit or unloved. You stand in the height of the day the going was always climbing toward, lit and loved on every side, with no night left anywhere in the world. Your name is the one word the noon is wholly bright to speak, the syllable the full light is risen to say. The spiral is the coil the noon-light turns on, love and radiance wound to one, the same whorl in your skin, here the mark the bright future, at its height, is a breath from lighting wholly into its day.

The radiance is one sustained noon-chord, the bell become the full bright peal of a day at its height, every stroke all light and all love — and your heart beats up into it, the climbing count come to its noon, your name a breath from sounded in the shadowless light of the future entire.

To the north the noon reaches the lit pole absolute, light and love and thought wholly one, the future entire and shadowless. To the east the cosmos comes wholly the god's, nothing but loving light-worlds, the magic pole of light. To the south the far morning, the bright peak the journey climbed. To the west the embrace, the cosmos one loving world. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward the light. Here the bright future stands at its full noon, all light, all love, no dark left anywhere — and still the warm coil turns at the heart of the day, and still one live beat keeps the time the noon is a breath from naming you, wholly, into the light.
