Squeal

You are the high thin shriek of delight or fright - the piglet's cry, the child whizzing down the slide, the brakes that scream on the track. No deep sound in you; just a shrill, sudden, piercing note. Out it comes before you can stop it, when the surprise is too big to hold inside.

North

The busy sound the brown hen makes,
'cluck-cluck' around the yard;
she clucks while scratching in the dirt -
then prouder when she's laid.

East

A soft sad little crying sound,
the puppy left alone;
a quiet whine of hurt or fear -
a feeble, weepy moan.

South

Fast and endless talking on,
or teeth that click with cold;
the monkeys do it, friends do too -
quick noise, both young and old.

West

The deep slow call the cow gives out
across the evening field;
a long low 'mooo,' she calls for hay -
her hunger thus revealed.
