Smile

You are the smallest kindness a face can make - a quiet curve of the mouth that costs nothing and changes everything. You need no sound, unlike your louder cousin the laugh; you can cross a crowded room in silence and still be understood in every language there is. A stranger wears you and the day softens. You are the first thing a baby learns to give back, and the last thing a face forgets how to do.

North

You shut your eyes and pick a number,
not really knowing which is right;
a stab, a hunch, a lucky shot -
the answer you make up on sight.

East

A little hint to help you see
the answer to a mystery;
detectives follow where I lead,
the trail of signs that solvers need.

South

At the end of every game,
we add up who has won;
the count of goals, the tally kept -
the number, when it's done.

West

I make you look the wrong way round,
while my real move slips by;
a sly deceit, a fooling ruse,
a cheat upon the eye.
