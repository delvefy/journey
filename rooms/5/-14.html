Sledge

You are the runner-footed seat for the snow, no wheels at all, just smooth steel rails to hiss down the white hill. Made of wood and rope. Sit, push off, and the world rushes up - faster, faster, into the drift at the bottom. Then the long cold trudge back up, dragging me behind, to do it all again.

North

Two wheels, a frame, a saddle, bell,
you pedal hard to ride;
you lean to turn, you brake to stop -
the wind blows at your side.

East

The grand high chair where a king sits,
all gold and red and tall;
he rules the land from up on it -
the proudest seat of all.

South

A short flat board on four small wheels,
you ride it standing tall;
you push and roll and do your tricks -
and sometimes take a fall.

West

A soft stuffed bear you hug in bed,
with button eyes and fur;
you take it everywhere you go -
your oldest friend, for sure.
