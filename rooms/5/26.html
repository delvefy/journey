Babble

You are a babble - a fast tumbling stream of words pouring out, half of them making no sense at all. A baby babbles happy nonsense in its cot; an excited child babbles too fast to follow; a crowd all talking at once is a great babble of noise. The words trip over each other and spill out in a rush. Bubbly and breathless and not at all clear, you are talk run away with itself, more sound than sense, like a little brook chattering merrily over the stones.
