Message

You are a message - a parcel of words sent from one mind to another, across a room or across the sea. You can be scribbled on a note, tapped on a phone, or whispered in an ear. You carry news, a warning, a happy birthday, an 'I miss you'. You are not the paper or the screen; you are the meaning riding inside them. Speak me, send me, and a thought leaps the gap between two heads.
