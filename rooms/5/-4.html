Drum

You are a hollow made to be struck - a barrel of wood with a skin pulled tight across its mouth, and your whole purpose is the boom. Tap you softly and you mutter; strike you hard and you speak to the whole valley. You keep no tune, only time: the heartbeat under every march and dance, the pulse the feet obey. Hands or sticks, it makes no difference - you answer every blow with the same round, rolling thunder.

North

With bristles stiff and handle tall,
I chase the dust along the hall;
I gather crumbs into a heap,
the floor I'm always made to sweep.

East

With little bristles in a row,
through tangled hair I gently go;
or dipped in paint I stroke the wall,
a handy thing, but small, not tall.

South

I am thin and sharp and bright,
with an eye but cannot see;
I pull a thread through cloth all day,
to mend a tear for thee.

West

I stand upon your table-top,
and hold a little sun;
flick my switch and shadows stop,
I light the room when day is done.
