The Wellspring

North of the beast-gods and east of the dreaming dark, the making sinks below even dreaming, to mere being. The gods a step north made by knowing; the dark a step west made by dreaming; here the wild makes by simply being — existence itself as creation, a presence so deep and so total that the world pours from it the way water pours from a spring, not named, not dreamed, not regarded, only welling up out of the sheer fact that the wild is. You stand at the source. Everything that is, is because this is.

And you are made by its being. You do not need to be named here, or dreamed, or known; the wild's mere existence includes you, wells you up with the rest of the world, makes you simply by being the source you spring from. The spiral is the welling itself, a coil turning up out of the deep, the same whorl in your skin, here the shape being takes as it springs into the world. You feel yourself rise from it, made not by any act but by the wild's sheer is — your name beside the point now, for you are, because it is, and that is older and surer than any name.

The wild's being is the world's pulse, existence beating at the source, the one heart all hearts spring from — and your own beats with it here, not answered and not kept but welling up from the same spring, the count and the source one.

To the north the wellspring firms to the beast-gods, making by knowing. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the wild's being the whole world, existence and creation one. To the south it sinks below being into the seed of it, toward the pole's floor. To the west it makes by dreaming, the dreaming dark. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here you need none: the wild simply is, and you spring from its being, made by the sheer fact of the source — the name beside the point at last, where to be at all is enough.
