Note

You are a note - a small written message, or a single sound of music, depending on who needs you. A hand makes you either way: scribbled on paper and left on the fridge, or played on a string and let ring in the air. You cannot be held for long - the paper one is read and binned, the music one fades in a second. A note of thanks, a note of warning, a high note, a low note. You are a little piece of meaning, sent and gone.

a ___ the page can bring.

West

or steam from a kettle's spout;
a cat will do it, cross and scared -
a ___, watch out, watch out.
