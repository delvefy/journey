News

You are news - the reports of what has just happened, gathered up and passed along. You arrive on the telly at six, splashed across the front of the paper, buzzing on a phone. Some of you is grand and far away - storms, elections, ships lost at sea - and some of you is small and close - a wedding, a fete, a lost cat found. You are always about what is new, today's happenings told before they go stale. You are the world telling itself what it just did.
