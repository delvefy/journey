Pout

You are the sulky push of the lower lip, the face a child makes when things don't go their way. No hand makes you; the mouth makes you, all on its own, when 'no' is the answer. You say, without a word, that you are cross and hurt and will not smile. Stick out far enough, and a grown-up laughs - which only makes you worse.

North

A high thin grizzly sort of noise
when a child does not get its way;
'I waaant it!' on and on it drones -
the dog does it, the gate hinge may.

East

Salt water spills from both your eyes,
your mouth turns down, you sob;
a baby does it day and night
for milk - a wailing job.

South

A wide bright smile from ear to ear
that shows off every tooth;
cheeky, pleased, and full of mischief -
it tells the happy truth.

West

The loud rude blare a car horn makes,
or a goose that's flying past;
'HONK!' it blasts to clear the road -
a sound both harsh and vast.
