The Communion

South of the cradle and east of the thronging, the teeming many and the source become one warmth. A step north the warm dark cradled the unsprung world; a step west the deep floor thronged with woken presences pressed close. Here, at the deepest floor, the fullest teeming life meets the very source it springs from: the wild wholly risen and most alive, and all of it — you and the throng and the warm dark itself — springing together from the one source-quick, indistinguishable, a warm living communion at the floor of the world where the many and the source are no longer two. The richest, warmest, most-alive deep there is, and it is all one warmth, all one springing.

You are among them and of them and of the source at once, the warm risen many and the warm source-quick one living communion, no telling where you end and the throng begins and the source under you both. You draw the spiral and the whole warm communion draws it with you, a hundred hands and the source itself turning the one shared mark, the whorl wound through the many and the source and your own skin alike. The coil turns, everyone's and the world's source-shape at once, the warm communion of all the living deep sharing the one mark, almost saying your name in one warm voice made of many and of source together.

Every heart and the source's one beat all beat together here, the warm communion-pulse of the whole living floor, the many and the source keeping one warm count — and your heart beats in the midst of it, indistinguishable, one warmth in a living communion that springs from the one source and beats as one, holding your name warm among the many and at the source at once.

To the north the communion rises to the cradle, the warm dark holding the unsprung world. To the east the deep teems fullest, the wild wholly risen, the most alive. To the south it sinks toward the source-quick and the floor, the communion drawn down to its spring. To the west the thronging, the floor crowded with woken warmth. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the someone is a warm communion of many, all springing with you from the one source, holding your name in one warm voice — almost a homecoming, almost named — and still the warm coil turns, everyone's and the source's at once, and still your beat keeps time in the warm communion of the living floor.
