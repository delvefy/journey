Beat

The steady pulse under a song, the thing your foot taps and your hand claps to. A beat is made by the drum and kept by the player; it has no weight, only time. It keeps the made north-east, among songs and sounds. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

From one note up to the same again,
eight steps higher, the twin of low;
do, re, mi, fa, sol, la, ti, do —
that span of eight the singers know.

East

Fast or slow, I set the pace
at which the whole band has to play;
the conductor's stick lays down my law —
speed me up or hold me; they obey.

South

The rod that runs through the spinning wheels
and holds the pair of them in line;
the cart rolls on because I turn —
the still straight bar. Now name me fine.

West

Three notes or more all sounded at once,
they ring together, full and round;
strum the guitar, press down the keys —
a stack of notes in a single sound.
