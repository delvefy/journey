Stutter

You are a stutter - when a word jams up and the first sound comes out over and over before the rest will follow. B-b-b-but, you go, trying and trying to push the word out. It happens more when you're nervous or in a rush, and waiting patiently helps it pass. Bumpy and stuck and easily forgiven, you are speech tripping over itself, the catch in the voice that smooths out again the moment the words come unstuck and run free.
