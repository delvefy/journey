The Quickening

South of the wellspring and east of the teeming, being and aliveness become one rising. A step north the wild made by sheer being, the world welling up out of the fact that the wild is; a step west it teemed, wholly awake, sovereign powers turned on every side to you. Here the source and the swarm are the same act: the welling is awake, the spring is sovereign, and the world does not merely pour from the deep but quickens as it pours — bare being coming alive in the very rising, the most-alive world there is, springing straight and aware from the seed of itself.

Everything here is quick — alive in the oldest sense, leaping up from the source already knowing, already turned toward you. You feel yourself well up among it, made by the wild's being and woken by the wild's awareness in one motion, springing and waking at once. The spiral is the quickening itself, the coil that being makes as it leaps into life, root and horn and the curl of something newly alive, the same whorl in your skin, here the shape the source takes the instant it wakes. Your name is beside the point and everywhere at once: not needed, because you are, and known, because the quick wild knows its own as it springs them — held warm and wordless in the rising, almost a homecoming, almost said.

The one heart all hearts spring from beats here wide awake, the source and the teeming pulse one stroke, existence and aliveness keeping a single warm count — and your own beats up out of it, woken and welling together, one quick thread in the great alive rising, the oldest beat come fully, roaringly, to life.

To the north the quickening firms to the wellspring, being welling but not yet awake. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the living wild wholly sovereign, the world's most alive. To the south it sinks toward the seed, the quick before the quickening, the source-floor of the deep. To the west it gathers into the teeming, the many fully awake and turned to you. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here you need none and have a thousand: the quick wild wells you up and wakes you and roars its knowing, the source alive, the name beside the point and almost yours at once — and still the warm coil turns in your skin, springing and waking, and still one quick beat rises in the great alive count, almost a homecoming, almost home.
