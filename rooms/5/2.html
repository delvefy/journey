Word

Say it out loud and it is gone the instant it leaves you, yet it has done its work: a small package of sound, agreed by everyone, that carries a whole idea from your head into another's. A word is the smallest brick of speech - 'cat', 'no', 'tomorrow' - and from a handful of them you can build a promise, a poem, an entire quarrel. It costs nothing and weighs nothing. Choose the right one and it can open a door; choose the wrong one and it can start a war.

North

Count up the goals on either side,
I tell you who has won with pride;
two numbers chalked up on the board,
the tally that the game has stored.

East

Twenty-six of us in all,
from A to Z we stand up tall;
put us together, two or three,
and words and books are what you'll see.

South

I dress a thing in sly disguise,
and ask you to name it - here's your prize;
a question with one hidden word,
the trickiest puzzle you've ever heard.

West

Open a book and I begin,
with a hero brave and a wicked twin;
a beginning, middle, end have I,
and 'happily ever after' as goodbye.
