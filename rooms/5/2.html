Octave

Eight notes from one tone up to its echo above — sing 'do' to the next 'do' and you have crossed an octave. It is a made thing of music, a measured span you hear but cannot hold. It keeps the made north-east, among notes and measures. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

The same few lines that close each verse,
returning round again, again;
the song's set part you know by heart —
name the recurring line. Say when.

East

A short catchy tune on the screen,
that sells you soap or beans or cars;
it sticks all day inside your head —
name the little advert's bars.

South

The steady pulse that taps your foot,
the drum's tick under every song;
one-two, one-two, it keeps the time —
name the throb that drives along.

West

A single sound the singer holds,
a dot upon the staff's five lines;
high or low, I'm struck or sung —
name the one tone music signs.
