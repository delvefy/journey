Whisper

You are a whisper - speech turned down as low as it will go, words shaped on breath alone. You are the secret told behind a cupped hand, the bedtime story murmured in the dark, the hush of a library where even talk goes soft. You carry meaning with almost no sound at all, slipping from lips to a single waiting ear. No hand can hold you - you are the quietest a voice can be and still be heard.
