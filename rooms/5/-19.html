Fridge

You are a fridge - the big white humming cupboard in the kitchen that keeps everything cold and fresh. Many hands built you; you run on electricity, and when you open your door a light comes on and a breath of cold air spills out. You can be opened, leaned into, decorated with magnets and drawings. Inside you the milk stays sweet, the butter stays hard, the leftovers wait. Always humming, never resting, you are the cool keeper of the kitchen, guarding the food from going bad.
