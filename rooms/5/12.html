Moan

You are a low drawn-out sound of complaint or pain - 'ohhh', let out long and weary when the news is bad or the tummy aches. No hand makes you; the throat lets you slide out, half a groan, half a grumble. The wind in the chimney does you; the ghost in the story does you. Low, long, and gloomy.

North

To talk so low no one can hear,
half to yourself, head down;
'mumble-mumble,' cross and quiet -
you mutter, with a frown.

East

A low cross muttering under the breath,
'it's not fair, why must I?';
you do your chores but moan all through -
a grouse, a gripe, a sigh.

South

A high thin moaning, on and on,
when a child won't get its way;
'I waaant it!' droned right through your head -
the dog does it all day.

West

The big deep blast of a far-off gun,
or thunder rolling near;
the firework bursting in the night -
'BOOM!' it shakes your ear.
