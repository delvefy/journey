Nod

You are a nod - the small dip of the head that means 'yes' without a word, or a quick hello to a friend across the street. You bob once to agree, twice to greet, and you droop again and again when sleep sneaks up in a dull lesson. A nod can seal a deal or wave a stranger through. You speak the body's plainest yes, a whole agreement folded into one small dip of the chin.
