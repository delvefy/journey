Limp

You are a limp - the uneven, lopsided walk that comes when one leg is sore. You lean away from the bad side, take a short careful step, then a long one, dragging a little. A splinter, a twisted ankle, a stone in the shoe can all bring you on. Everyone can see something is wrong just by the way the body rocks along. You fade once the hurt heals. You are walking gone crooked, the slow tilting gait of someone nursing a sore leg.
