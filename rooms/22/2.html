Boo

You are boo - the short sharp shout that leaps out from behind a door to make a person jump. You are the ghost's word, the only thing a sheet-and-eyeholes spook need say. But you have another face: the long jeering 'boooo' a crowd lets fly at a villain on the stage or a referee's bad call. Frightening or scornful, you are always sudden and always loud. You are the word made for a fright, two little letters with a great big bang.

North

A sudden sharp breath sucked right in,
when something gives a fright;
the crowd does it at the fireworks -
a ___ at the sight.

East

A loud sad call when tears come down,
or a shout to raise the alarm;
the baby does it for its milk -
a ___ of grief or harm.

South

A board on a post beside the road,
with letters big and clear;
'STOP' or 'SCHOOL' it warns the cars -
a ___ to make them steer.

West

A loud and sudden shout you give,
to call or warn or cheer;
you open wide and let it out -
a ___ that all can hear.
