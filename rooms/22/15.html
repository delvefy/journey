Hike

You are a hike - a good long walk out in the open country, boots laced and a pack on your back. Up the hill and along the ridge and down through the woods you go, mile after mile, stopping only for a sandwich and a swig from the flask. Your legs ache and your cheeks glow and the view from the top makes it all worthwhile. Healthy and happy and a little bit tiring, you are a whole day spent walking just for the joy of the fresh air and the far places it takes you.
