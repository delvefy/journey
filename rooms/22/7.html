Jog

You are a jog - the slow steady run people do to keep fit, not to win. You bounce along the park path in soft shoes, breath puffing, arms loose, in no real hurry. Faster than a walk, gentler than a sprint, you could keep on for miles. The morning runner nods at you; the dog trots happily alongside. You are exercise turned into a rhythm, the easy patient pace of someone going nowhere fast on purpose.
