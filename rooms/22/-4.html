Purse

You are a purse - the little bag where coins and notes are kept, snapping or zipping shut to hold them safe. Grandma fishes in you for the exact change; a child empties you out to count the saved-up pennies. You can be leather or beaded or plastic, big enough for a phone or small as a fist. Lose me and the money's gone with me. You are the small soft safe a pocket carries, the keeper of all the spending money.
