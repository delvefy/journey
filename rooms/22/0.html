Bucket

You are a bucket - the deep round pail with a swinging handle, for carrying water from the well or sand from the beach. Children fill you to build castles; cleaners fill you with soapy suds; the well lowers you down on a rope and hauls you up brimming. You have a flat bottom to stand on and a lip to pour from. Kick one over and you spill your whole load at once. You are the plainest carrier of water there is, made useful with a handle.
