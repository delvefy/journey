Rope

You are a rope - the thick strong cord of twisted strands, made to pull and bind and climb. Sailors haul the sails with you; cowboys lasso with you; children skip and tug-of-war with you. Knotted, you hold fast; frayed, you give way with a snap. You can moor a great ship or hang a swing from a tree. You are strength braided into a line, the long muscular cousin of string, made for any job that needs holding, lifting, or tying down.
