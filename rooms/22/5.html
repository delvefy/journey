Kiss

You are a kiss - the soft press of the lips that says 'I love you' without a word. On a cheek for hello, on a grazed knee to make it better, on the brow of a sleeping child. A frog turns into a prince beneath one, the stories say. Blown across a room, you fly on the fingertips; sealed in a letter as an X, you travel miles. You are love made into a touch, the warmest little word the lips can speak.
