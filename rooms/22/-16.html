Velcro

You are velcro - two scratchy strips, one rough with tiny hooks and one soft with tiny loops, that grab and hold each other the moment they are pressed together. Rrrip, you go, when they are pulled apart. You fasten shoes with no laces, jackets with no buttons, and a hundred straps besides. Clingy and scratchy and wonderfully easy, you are the fastener little fingers love best, the one that needs no fiddly tying, just a press and a satisfying rip.
