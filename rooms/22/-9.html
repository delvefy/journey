Jeans

You are jeans - tough blue trousers cut from hard-wearing denim, made for climbing trees and skidding on knees. You have pockets for treasures and rivets at the corners and you only get better the more you are worn and washed. Made by hands - cut, stitched, and dyed that deep indigo - you fade pale at the knees and fray at the hems. You are the trousers that never wear out, the favourite pair pulled on for any kind of day.
