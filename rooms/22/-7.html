Couch

You are a couch - the long soft padded seat in the living room, wide enough for the whole family to flop down at once. You hold the cushions and lose the coins down your cracks; you take the cat, the crisps, the Sunday nap. Children build forts from you and bounce until told to stop. You sag a little where everyone sits the most. You are the comfiest landing in the house, a long lazy bed that pretends to be a seat.
