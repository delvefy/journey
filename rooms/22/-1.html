Button

You are a button - the little round disc that slips through a slit to hold your clothes together. You sit in a row down the front of a shirt, do up a coat against the cold, fasten the cuff at the wrist. Lose one and the gap gapes open; sew one back on with a needle and thread. You also live on machines, waiting to be pressed to ring a bell or start a lift. You are the smallest, quietest fastener there is.
