Lullaby

You are a lullaby - the slow soft song hummed low over a cot, made on purpose to grow heavy and warm and pull the eyelids down. A voice shapes you out of almost nothing, the same few notes round and round, until the breathing slows and the small fists open. You are the last sound of the day, sung not to be remembered but to be sunk beneath. A made thing whose whole work is to be no longer heard.
