Bed

You are a bed - the soft warm place where every day ends. A frame, a mattress, a heap of covers pulled up to the chin. You hold the tired and the sleepy, the sick and the dreaming. In the morning you are hard to leave; at night you are the kindest word in the house. Jump on me, hide under me, read by torch in me - but mostly, just sleep, and let the dark go by.
