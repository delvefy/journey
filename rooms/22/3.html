Gasp

You are a gasp - the sudden sharp breath sucked in when something amazes or frightens you. The crowd gasps at the fireworks; the diver gasps coming up for air; you gasp when you hear shocking news. You are half a word and half pure shock, a quick loud pull of breath that the body does on its own. A magic trick, a scary turn, a great surprise - any can pull one out of you. You are wonder and alarm made of a single indrawn breath.
