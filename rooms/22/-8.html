Kennel

You are a kennel - the little wooden house in the garden where the dog sleeps. You have a round doorway but no door, a sloping roof to shed the rain, and just room enough for a curled-up dog and an old blanket. The name is painted over your arch. On hot days the dog dozes in your shade; on cold nights it burrows deep inside you. You are a dog's own front door and bedroom in one, a snug small house built just its size.
