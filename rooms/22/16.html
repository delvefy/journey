Swivel

You are a swivel - to turn smoothly round on one fixed spot, like a chair that spins or a head that twists to look behind. Office chairs swivel, lighthouse lamps swivel, a curious owl swivels its whole head almost the whole way round. You stay put but face a new way. Smooth and turning and a little bit dizzying, you are spinning that keeps its feet in one place, swinging round to point wherever you please without taking a single step.
