screw

A screw is a little metal spike wound round and round with a ridge, made to bite its own way into wood. You set the screwdriver in its slotted head and turn, and turn, and slowly it pulls itself deep, gripping far tighter than any nail. Cheap, sharp, and clever, a whole shelf may hang on one. Cold and heavy in the palm, wholly a made thing, it is a nail that learned to spin - and so it never lets go.

North

Two strips that grip with a ripping sound,
one furry and one rough;
press them together, they hold fast -
no laces, quick enough.

East

A little round disc on your shirt or coat,
with two or four small holes;
push me through the slot to fasten up -
I hold your clothes in folds.

South

A little pointed hand-held spade
for digging up the weeds;
you scoop the soil and plant the bulbs -
the gardener's tool for seeds.

West

A short sharp pin with a flat round head,
push me in with your thumb;
I pin the poster to the wall -
small, but I get things done.
