Ukulele

You are a ukulele - a tiny guitar with only four strings and a round cheerful voice, small enough to tuck under one arm. Hands strum you on a knee and out comes a plinky, sunny, holiday sort of tune that makes everyone want to sway. You are easy to learn and impossible to be gloomy with. Little and light and bright as a beach, you are the friendliest instrument there is, the one that turns a quiet afternoon into a sing-song.
