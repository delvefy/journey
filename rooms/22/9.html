Alarm

You are an alarm - a loud insistent signal made to grab the ear and not let go. You are the clock that drags the sleeper up at dawn, the bell that empties the school, the wail that says fire. No hand holds you, yet a hand sets you, winding or switching you to go off at the dreaded hour. You jangle the nerves on purpose. You are the noise nobody wants but everybody needs, the made sound that means: now, wake, move.
