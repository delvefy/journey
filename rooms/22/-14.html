Blender

You are a blender - a tall jug on a buzzing base, with sharp blades spinning at the bottom. Drop in fruit and milk and ice, snap the lid on tight, and press the button: you roar and whirl and turn it all into a smooth thick swirl. Smoothies, soups and milkshakes are your work. Loud and quick and a little fierce, you mash and mix in seconds what hands could never blend, and then you fall silent and wait, sticky, for a wash.
