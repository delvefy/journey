Woof

You are a woof - the deep friendly bark of a dog, thrown out at the postman, the doorbell, the squirrel, or just at the joy of being a dog. No tool makes you; a wagging tail and an open jaw do. You mean hello and danger and play and dinnertime, all in one round brown sound. Loud in the morning, gruff at the gate, you are the one word the dog knows and uses for everything, again and again, all day long.
