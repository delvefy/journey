Mitten

You are a mitten - the cosy winter glove that keeps the four fingers together in one warm pouch, with just the thumb in a room of its own. You are warmer than a glove, for the fingers huddle and share their heat. You dangle on a string through both coat sleeves so you cannot be lost, and still you are lost by spring. Pulled on with the teeth, peeled off red and damp. You are a small wool burrow for a cold little hand.
