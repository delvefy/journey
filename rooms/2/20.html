Click

You are a click - the small crisp sound of two hard things tapping together: a switch flicked, a pen pressed, a seatbelt fastened, a door latched. No hand can hold you, though a finger makes you; you are sound at its smallest and tidiest, here and gone in an instant. A click means done, locked, on, connected. A mouse clicks, a tongue clicks, the camera clicks the photo. You are the tiny snap of one thing slotting neatly into another.
