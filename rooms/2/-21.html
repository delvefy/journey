Button

You are a button - the little round disc, sewn on with thread, that pushes through a slit to hold a shirt or coat done up. You come in pearl and plastic and shiny brass, in every colour, lost down the back of the sofa by the dozen. You are also the thing you press to ring a bell or start a machine. You are made by hand, small and humble and easy to lose. You are the tiniest fastener that does the most, holding warm coats shut against the cold all winter long.
