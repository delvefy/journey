Hop

You are a little jump on one foot or two - the way the sparrow goes, the way the hopscotch is played, the bunny's quick spring. No hand makes you; the springy legs do it, up and down, light and quick. You hop on one foot when the other is sore; you hop the puddles. Small and bouncy.

North

To twist and squirm and writhe about,
the way a worm will go;
you wriggle free of a too-tight grip,
or in your seat when bored, you know.

East

The loud smash sound of something breaking -
a tower of bricks falls down,
the plates slip off and hit the floor -
'CRASH!' rings round the town.

South

A box of jigsaw bits to fit
into a finished scene;
or any tricky problem set
to tease the brain, I mean.

West

Turn over and over along the ground,
the way a ball will go;
down the grassy hill you tumble,
round and round, just so.
