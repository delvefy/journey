Clack

You are a clack - the short, hard, brittle sound of two stiff things knocked smartly together. Wooden blocks, a beak snapping shut, heels on a stone floor, knitting needles in busy hands. Sharper than a clatter, drier than a clang, you are a single dry knock of a noise, made and gone in an instant. You cannot be touched or kept; you live only in the moment two hard things meet.
