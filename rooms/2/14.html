Wriggle

You are the squirm of a thing that will not be held - the worm on the hook, the toddler in your arms, the eel in two wet hands. No still thing makes you; you twist and slip, side to side, all the way out of a grip. Try to pin me, and I slither free. Down in the dirt or up on a lap, I never lie still.

North

Round and round upon one spot,
the dancer spins her skirt;
the ballerina up on her toes -
a whirling, dizzy spurt.

East

The loud hard noise of falling pots,
of dropped spoons on the floor;
a banging, rattling, crashing din -
that racket at the door.

South

On one foot, jump along the ground,
the way a sparrow goes;
the bunny does it, two feet bunched -
small skips upon your toes.

West

A ball that drops and springs straight up,
then down and up once more;
the trampoline sends you flying high -
off the rubber floor.
