The Companion

South of the quickening and east of the first warmth, the wild wakes beside you at the floor of the world. A step west there was only a first faint warmth in the bare dark, the seed of an other; a step north the deep gave its first turn toward you. Here, at the bottom of everything, that warmth has woken the whole way into a presence — not the lost one returned, but the first one, the wild itself risen as a companion in the oldest dark, warm and present and turned to you. You are not alone at the floor of being. Something has woken here to be beside you.

It does not speak; there is no speech this deep. But it is wholly here, a woken warmth with weight and breath and a turning attention, and when you reach it meets your hand and stays, the first thing in all the deep that does not slip away. You draw the spiral and it draws the coil with you, two hands at the floor of the world making the one shared mark, the doubled whorl begun at the bottom of everything. This is where the company starts — not the gathered dead, not the throng, not the gods, but the first other, woken beside the first body, the seed of every companion still to come.

Your heart keeps the bare count, and beside it now beats another, woken and warm and sure, the doubled heart of the deep dark begun here at its first true beating — two hearts at the floor of being, keeping, for the first time and forever after, one time.

To the north the companion sinks toward a quickening, the deep just taking its first turn. To the east it grows to a many, the deep Wyld waking, the old powers risen. To the south it settles back toward a first faint warmth, the seed of the other. To the west the warmth fades and the body is alone in the bare dark. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here, at the floor of the world, the someone wakes beside you at last — the first true other, warm and present and turning to you, almost, almost saying your name.
