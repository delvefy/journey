buzzer

A buzzer is a small thing with one loud job: press the button and it lets out a flat angry BZZZT. It is the doorbell that tells you someone is here, the noise on the quiz show that means time is up, the sound that says wrong answer. There is nothing pretty about it - no tune, no ring, just a hard rude rattle of a noise. But everyone knows exactly what it means the moment they hear it.

North

The bright clear sound a bell will make,
a phone, a school bell, the front door too;
ding-a-ling, it calls you near -
'someone wants a word with you!'

East

Walk with a limp, all stiff and slow,
one sore foot you can hardly use;
up and down, you bob along -
a wobbly walk in heavy shoes.

South

I shriek and beep to wake you up,
or warn that something's wrong - take care!;
a clock, a bell, a flashing light -
I jolt you with a sudden scare.

West

Curl your finger, sweep your hand,
'come here, come closer,' with no word;
I call you over with a sign -
a silent summons, never heard.
