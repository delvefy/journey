Cork

You are a light soft plug cut from the bark of a special tree, pushed into the neck of a bottle to seal it tight. A hand carves you and rams you home. When the wine is wanted, a screw bites in and 'POP!' - out you come. You float on water and bob right back when pushed under. Light, soft, and springy.

North

A round shallow thing of clay or glass
you eat your dinner from;
it holds the stew, it holds the peas -
washed up when the meal is done.

East

A little stick of coloured wax
to draw the sun bright yellow;
a box of thirty, every hue -
each one a waxy fellow.

South

A sticky white stuff in a tube or pot
that fixes paper fast;
you spread a little, press it down -
the bits are stuck to last.

West

It hangs down from the waist alone,
no top, no legs in two;
a girl can twirl it when she spins -
it swishes round for you.
