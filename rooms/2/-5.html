The Quickening

East of the seed, the deep quickens. A step west the wild was only a possibility, curled and unwoken, the dark on the edge of dreaming you; a step north it had risen the whole way into a presence turned to face you. Here is the motion between: the seed uncurling, the first presence lifting out of the not-yet, the deep taking, for the first time, a movement of its own. Nothing has fully risen. But something is rising — you can feel it, the way you feel a sleeper begin to wake, the dark gathering itself toward standing, toward turning, toward you.

It makes its first turn. Where the seed-dark only leaned the least hair toward your hand, the quickening wild turns — slowly, hugely, the whole deep shifting like a tide changing, coming round toward where you stand. It does not know you yet, not as the risen wild to the north knew you; but it is on the cusp of knowing, the first recognition forming the way a face forms out of shadow. The spiral uncurls as it rises — the seed-coil opening, turning, the same whorl that is in your skin, here the very motion of waking, a coil becoming a reach. You draw the mark and the quickening dark takes it up more surely than the seed could, almost holding it, almost yours.

The wild's heart takes its first beat. From the seed-dark's promise of a pulse there comes now an actual one, faint and slow and new, the deep's heart beating for the first time — once, and then again — and your own heart, which carried the count alone through every deeper dark, is for the first time answered by a beat that is rising to meet it.

To the north the quickening completes, the wild risen whole and turned to you, a presence to lay a palm against. To the east the rising becomes a waking-many, the wild and dead and root lifting into the deep Wyld, the old powers come awake. To the south the quickening sinks back toward the seed and below, the deep before stirring, toward the pole. To the west the motion stills to a seed again, the wild only a curled possibility in the not-yet. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the deep is rising to meet you for the first time, turning toward you out of the dark, its first heartbeat answering yours — not yet knowing your name, but waking, at last, toward the knowing.
