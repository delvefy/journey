Letter

You are a letter - a message written out by hand, folded into an envelope, sealed and stamped and sent. You carry news and love and birthday wishes across the whole country, dropped through a far-off door days later for someone to open with a happy gasp. In a world of quick bright screens you are slow and special. Made by a hand for a hand, you are words that travel - a little piece of one person carried all the way to another.
