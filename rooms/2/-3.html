The Answering Dark

Below the herd the hides thin away. The warm press of many bodies grows scarce and then single, the beasts not yet dreamed this deep, and what is left is older than any beast and warmer than the cold dark to the west — the body before it was ever drawn or stood up living, just the close black and the heat of being, and one other in it. Reach, and the dark gives back not hide and horn but skin: a shoulder, the turn of a jaw, warm over the hardness of the bone beneath, the living and the long-dead held in one shape.

This is the breath you brushed a step to the west, come nearer and found its voice. It is a presence now, turned to you, and when you make the smallest sound — not a word, you have none this deep, only the wish to be answered — it answers. A warmth shaped at the end of a breath, close against your ear, almost speech, almost a name, and this time it does not let go before the saying: it reaches you. You cannot tell if it says your name or gives you its own. Either way you are, for the first time since you woke, not alone in the dark and known to be there.

The two hearts that drifted in and out of time to the west have fallen, here, into one step. Yours and the other's, beat for beat, the oldest count doubled and made true — no bell, no hoof, only two bloods keeping the same time, the way two who walked a long road together once kept pace without thinking.

To the north the one warmth multiplies into the standing herd, bodies risen and breathing, the wild quick and many. To the east the single answer becomes a company, the dark full of the warm dead turned toward you, the Wyld where the lost are here and speak your name plainly. To the south the warmth thins and the other slips away, older dark before any company, the deep that was never lain in two. To the west the voice falls back to a breath, near but not answering. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Someone is here in the dark with you now, keeping your time and answering when you call, and still you cannot say the name — only that it is the one you have been missing the whole way.
