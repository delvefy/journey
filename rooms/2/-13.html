Glue

You are sticky white stuff in a tube or a pot, made to fix one thing to another - paper to card, wood to wood, the broken handle back on. A hand squeezes you out and presses the pieces. You dry hard and clear and hold fast. You stick to fingers, you go stringy, you make a happy mess at craft time.

North

A soft light plug for a bottle's neck,
pushed in to seal it tight;
'POP!' it leaves the wine at last -
it bobs and floats, all right.

East

Coloured wet stuff you brush on a page,
or roll on a wall in a coat;
it drips, it dries, it brightens all -
in pots and tubes it floats.

South

The dark wet stuff that flows from a pen
to leave its words on white;
spill it, and it stains for good -
black rivers of writing might.

West

An open shoe for a summer's day,
just straps across the toes;
your bare feet breathe, no socks at all -
to the beach in them one goes.
