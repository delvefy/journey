Whistle

You are a whistle - a high clear note made by blowing through pursed lips or a little pipe. You call the dog, start the race, stop the match, warn the train. No hand quite holds you; breath shapes you, sharp and piercing, carrying clean across a field. You can be cheerful or shrill, a tune strolled down the street or a sharp blast that means STOP. You are sound aimed like an arrow, the loudest noise the mouth can make without a single word.
