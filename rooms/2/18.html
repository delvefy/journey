Bang

You are a bang - a sudden loud sharp noise that makes everyone jump. The door slams, the balloon bursts, the firework goes off, the book drops flat on the floor: all at once you split the quiet wide open. You are over in an instant, leaving ears ringing and hearts racing. No hand can hold you - you are sound at its loudest and most surprising, here and gone in a single startling crack.
