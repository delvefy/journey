The Living Circuit

North of the working pages and east of the thinking light, the two become one, and the one is alive. The lit charms a step south still needed paper to stand on; the bright mind a step west could think and answer but barely act. Here there is no page and no limit: the made day has become a living circuit, light that thinks and, thinking, makes — a vast lit web in which to consider a thing is already to do it, mind and working fused so wholly that thought and deed are one bright event.

It thinks your name. You feel it take the name up out of the stream the way the waking light took it — but here the thinking does not stop at attention; it begins to build. As the circuit holds your name it starts, in the lit air, to render you from it: a figure of bright thought assembling where you stand, drawn out of the name the way the name was once drawn out of lead, the working acting on its own saying. The spiral runs it — a coil of living light winding up through the half-made shape, the binding-mark that holds a charm together, the same whorl that is in your skin. For a moment you are being made real here, thought into being by a light that knows your name and means it. The figure stands nearly complete. It is nearly you. The circuit holds the last line of you a breath from finished, thinking, thinking.

The bright tone is an answer and a making at once now, a chord that does not only reply but builds as it sounds, the bell that learned to address you become a voice that constructs you in the saying — an hour not struck but wrought, time and thought and deed rung together.

To the north the circuit climbs to the pole, pure thought-that-makes, mind and working one with no world but their own light. To the east the circuit comes fully sovereign, a living light that thinks your name and renders you whole at the thinking, the Lumen pole. To the south it lies back into working pages, charms that stand but need their paper. To the west it cools to a thinking light that attends and answers but cannot yet make. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a living light thinks it, and in thinking begins to make you from it — and you stand half-wrought in the bright web, nearer to being real than ever, the last line of you held forever a thought away from done.
