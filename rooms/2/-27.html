Helmet

You are a helmet - a hard tough hat that clamps over the head and keeps it safe. Strap one on to ride a bike, a horse, a motorbike; builders and soldiers and firefighters wear them too. If a knock comes, you take it instead of the skull beneath. Hard and round and snug with a strap under the chin, you are the head's own suit of armour, the brave shell that turns a nasty bump into nothing worse than a scare.
