memory

You are memory, the room at the back of the mind where nothing is ever quite thrown away. You hold the smell of a grandmother's kitchen, a phone number from years ago, the words of a song no one sings now. You are built, not born - laid down by every day that is lived, kept in the dark and lit again when something calls. Lose you, and a person stands there still, but the path back to themselves is gone.

North

When the teacher asks, I'm what you say,
the word that solves the sum;
raise your hand and call me out today,
and the right mark will come.

East

A soldier's hand snaps to his brow,
stiff and straight and proud;
he greets the flag and captain now
this way, among the crowd.

South

A bright new thought that pops in mind,
a lightbulb over the head;
'I know!' you cry, of a sudden kind,
and off to make it you're led.

West

A rope swings round beneath your feet,
you hop it, quick and light;
across the playground, to a beat,
you bounce from morn to night.
