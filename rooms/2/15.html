Twirl

You are the spin upon one spot - the dancer's skirt flaring out, the ballerina up on her toes, the child going round and round till the world tips. No straight thing makes you; delight does, turning and turning. Spin me fast enough, and even standing still feels like falling.

North

The hard sharp sound of two things knocked,
of heels on a wooden floor;
the train on the track, the typing keys -
'clack-clack' and then some more.

East

The dull soft knock when two things meet,
the car's small kiss, the knee on the shelf;
'bump!' in the night, you sit straight up -
a thump you feel yourself.

South

The squirm and twist of a slippery thing,
the worm, the wormy child;
it slips your grip from side to side -
all loose and slithery-wild.

West

Turn it right over, top to bottom -
quick, the pancake's tossed;
the gymnast turns heels over head -
over it goes, not lost.
