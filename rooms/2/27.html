Alarm

You are an alarm - a loud insistent ring or shriek that demands attention right now. The clock alarm drags you out of sleep; the fire alarm sends everyone hurrying out; the burglar alarm screams into the night. You will not be ignored: you ring and ring until somebody comes and switches you off. Urgent and shrill and impossible to sleep through, you are noise with a job to do, the sound that says wake up, or get out, or pay attention this very minute.
