The Quickening Dark

South of the answering dark and east of the gathering, the warmth comes true. A step up, the deep was almost never shared — the other thinned and slipped, the cold older dark that was never lain in two; a step west, there was only a leaning of the black, a someone barely begun. Here the leaning has drawn itself up and warmed. Out of the no-one a someone has gathered far enough to breathe: a shade with the first warmth in it, present where a moment ago was only the deep's slow turning, the company the cold below could never hold begun at last on this thin edge of it.

You reach the way you have learned to reach, and this time something warm meets the reaching — faint, so faint it could still be the wanting, an omen you only half believe. But it holds. It draws nearer, gathering as it comes, and a breath starts in it, drawn and let go, drawn again, the first breath of a someone learning to be one. When you make your small sound it does not answer in words — it has no name yet, no more than you — but a warmth shapes at the end of its breath, turned toward you, an answer beginning where for so long there was none. You almost have it: not the name, but the nearness of the one who keeps it.

The slow turning of the deep has wound in close and found its centre at last — the spiral with no middle coiling now around this one gathered warmth, you and it together at the still heart of it. And a second pulse has started up beside your own, faint as a moth, the count that was learning itself below now beating soft in another chest, your two times not yet matched but no longer alone. North the warmth fills out and the other firms into a presence that answers and keeps your step. East the one shade becomes a company, the dark full of the warm dead turned toward you, your name said plainly. South the warmth thins away to nothing, the deep before any leaning, no one yet on the way. West it falls back to a faint gathering, a someone barely begun. You were going to meet someone, once. Here, on the cold edge of everything, they have just begun to be.
