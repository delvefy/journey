Answer

You are the reply that fills a question, the word that fits the empty space. No object - you are made of thought, handed back when something is asked: the sum's solution, the riddle's single word, the yes or no to a knock at the door. You can be right or wrong, quick or long in coming. Teachers wait for you; the puzzle is not done until you arrive. A wrought thing of the mind, you are the door a question opens onto, the click of a thing understood.

North

A thing you ask when puzzled,
it ends in a curling mark;
who and what and where and why,
it seeks an answer's spark.

East

A loud shrill cry that warns of harm,
or drags you out of bed;
fire, thieves, or morning sets it off -
a ringing sense of dread.

South

The keeping of the things gone by,
inside your busy head;
a birthday, a face, an old song -
all that's done and said.

West

A great big jump with a running start,
both feet up off the ground;
the frog, the dancer, and the deer
all clear the gap with a bound.
