The Doubling

South of the rousing and east of the germ, the one begins to double. A step north an other roused toward presence in the floor-dark; a step west the undivided harboured only the germ of two-ness, a hint that it might someday be more than one. Here, at the primal pole, that germ wakes a little and the doubling begins: the seed of an other starting, faintly, to be — not the woken companion yet, not the mere possibility, but the first true seed of a someone-else, the one quickening toward two, the undivided learning, gently, to be a you and an other both.

It is the tenderest beginning there is: the one dark, which was wholly undivided, now holding the first real seed of a second — a warmth that is starting to be not-yours, an other-in-the-making at the floor of the world. You draw the spiral and a second motion begins, faintly, to shape itself alongside, the single whorl beginning to double, the seed of the doubled mark that the first warmth will one day make whole. The coil in your skin turns, and beside it the seed of another coil quickens toward being, the company doubling from one toward two in the primal deep.

Your heart keeps the slow primal count, and beside it the seed of a second beat begins to be — not yet a beat, but a beat-in-the-making, the one heart doubling toward two, the first true seed of a shared rhythm quickening at the floor of the world, the doubling begun in the dark before warmth was whole.

To the north the doubling wakes toward the rousing, the other rousing fuller toward presence. To the east the seed of the other quickens further, the wild waking it toward a self. To the south it sinks back toward the germ and the undivided, the doubling sinking to its first hint again. To the west the germ, the bare possibility of two in the one. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the self, before the other. Here the one first doubles toward that someone, the seed of them beginning to be — and still the warm coil turns, beginning to double, and still one beat keeps the time a second is, gently, quickening to keep.
