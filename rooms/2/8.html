Idea

You are the bright spark that leaps unbidden into the dark of the mind - the lightbulb above the head, the sudden 'what if', the answer that arrives before it is even sought. You cost nothing and weigh nothing, yet from you come bridges and songs and cures and whole new ways to live. Most of you flicker and die forgotten; a few catch and change the world. You cannot be held or bought, only had, and the more freely you are shared, the more of you there always seem to be.

North

What stays behind when the day is done,
of things you saw and knew;
a picture kept of times long gone,
your grandma keeps a few.

East

A gentle tap upon the head,
or soft upon the back;
'good dog,' or 'well done,' it has said,
a kindness, not a smack.

South

The careful order, mapped with care,
of what to do, step one;
you think it through before you dare,
then start and get it done.

West

Left, right, left, the soldiers tread,
in step and straight and tall;
they stamp along where they are led,
to the beat of the drum's call.
