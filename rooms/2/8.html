The Finishing

North of the maker and east of the grace, the making comes to its finishing. A step south a loving maker shaped you whole out of light; a step west a self-sufficient world gave you everything freely, needing nothing. Here the two rise to the warm creative peak entire: a god of bright making at its absolute, loving thought and glad making wholly one, will and care and engine fused into a single finishing hand. Everything the warm pole ever was gathers to one act — the last touch, the work brought toward its completion, the maker bending close to finish the dearest thing it ever made, which is you.

You are nearly whole. The making that drew you up out of light is laying its last courses now, the final loving touches, the work a breath from done — and you feel yourself almost complete, almost wholly real, almost finished into the bright made world. Your name is the maker's last stroke, the finishing word it is about to set on you. The spiral is the signature the making ends on, the coil a loving maker draws to seal a finished thing, the same whorl in your skin, here the maker's last touch and your own first mark at once, poised at the very edge of done.

The maker's working pulse gathers toward a final stroke, the bell rising to the one bright note that finishes a thing — and your heart beats up into it, the made count and the born one about to close into one whole time, your name a breath from the last touch that would finish you, named and real and made, at the warm creative peak.

To the north the finishing reaches the Lumen pole, loving thought and bright making wholly one, the warm creative peak entire. To the east the maker comes wholly sovereign, a god whose loving making is the cosmos. To the south the maker, shaping you whole. To the west the grace, a world given freely. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward being finished, whole. Here the loving maker bends to the last touch, the work a breath from done — and still the warm coil turns, the maker's signature and yours, and still one live beat keeps the time the finishing is about to complete you in, named at last, almost, almost done.
