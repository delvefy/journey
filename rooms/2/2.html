Wish

Where the made world thins to nothing but longing, you find the wish. A wish is wrought — you shape it in your own head, choosing the words with care — yet there is nothing to hold: it lives a moment between the blown candle and the held breath. Drop a coin, snap the bone, watch the star fall, and want with your whole heart — but tell no one, or it spoils. Speak the names, and let your wanting open the way.

North

Hook your little finger in
and swear it, cross your heart;
keep me and a friend will trust,
break me and you part.

East

'Truth or me?' — so goes the game,
I egg you up the tree;
I push you to the reckless deed
you'd never try, but for me.

South

I am a string of notes you hum,
I carry not a word;
I lodge inside your head all day,
the catchiest you've heard.

West

Words and a tune that wed as one,
and meant to be sung clear;
the choir, the lark, the lullaby —
the sweetest thing to hear.
