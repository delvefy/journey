Squeak

You are a squeak - the thin high little sound, the smallest noise a thing can make. The mouse squeaks in the wall; the door squeaks on its hinge; your shoe squeaks on the polished hall floor. A rusty wheel, a balloon rubbed, a toy pressed - all let one out. You are barely a sound at all, a sharp short pip too small to be a shout. You are the tiny voice of small and rusty things, the noise that begs for a drop of oil.
