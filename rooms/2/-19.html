Parcel

You are a parcel - a thing wrapped up in brown paper and string and sent across the country to land on someone's mat. A hand made you, folding the corners, tying the knot, writing the address; another hand will tear you open with a gasp. You can be held, weighed, shaken to guess what's inside. You are a surprise in a box, a gift that has travelled, all the waiting and the wondering bundled up until the moment you are torn apart.
