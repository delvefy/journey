Folder

You are a folder - a stiff card wallet bent in half, made to keep loose papers tidy. Slide your drawings, your letters, your school sheets inside, and they stay flat and clean and all in one place. Made by a hand from a single fold of card, you are the plainest sort of keeper - no lock, no lid, just two flaps and a crease. Open me, and the muddle becomes a neat little stack.

North

A flat white paper pocket,
I keep a letter from the rain;
lick my edge and seal me shut,
then post me down the lane.

East

A shiny little sticky picture,
peel me off the sheet;
press me on your book or hand -
a star, a smile, a treat.

South

A tiny square of sticky paper,
with a bird or a queen or a rose;
lick my back and press me down -
on the envelope I go.

West

A little slip stuck on a jar,
that tells you what's inside;
jam or beans or honey -
read me as your guide.
