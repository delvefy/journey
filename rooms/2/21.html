Clink

You are a clink - the bright little ringing knock that glass or metal gives when two of them touch. Coins do you dropped into a jar; two raised cups do you in a toast; keys do you on a ring. You are a small clear sound, sharper than a thud and softer than a clang, here and gone in a blink. You cannot be touched, only heard. You are the cheerful chime of hard small things meeting, the sound of money counted and good cheer shared.
