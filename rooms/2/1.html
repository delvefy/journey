Harp

A harp leans in the corner, a wooden frame strung tight, made by a careful hand. Touch it and it answers — not with weight, but with sound, a thing you cannot hold. It is the meeting of the made and the unmade: cut wood and twisted wire, yet its whole purpose is to set the air singing. Pluck the right word at each of the four walls to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

Pair me up: a star and far,
a hand and band, a king and ring;
when two line-endings sound alike,
I am that matched and chiming thing.

East

A hollow pipe with a row of holes,
blown across, not struck nor strung;
cover the holes with dancing hands
and a high sweet note is sung.

South

A round face on the wall I keep,
two hands that point but cannot hold;
I tick the seconds, strike the hours,
and tell the time to young and old.

West

I am the word that means just you,
written first atop the page;
lose your house, your gold, your crown —
I stay with you to your last age.
