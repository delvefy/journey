The Rousing

South of the companion and east of the first stir, the other begins to wake. A step north a present warmth had risen wholly into a companion, met your hand and stayed; a step west there was only the first faint stir, the seed of the seed, the deep barely leaning toward an other. Here, between the two and deeper than both, the stir rouses toward presence: not yet the woken companion, not still the mere lean, but a warmth coming awake beside you in the floor-dark, the deep beginning to teem, an other stirring up out of the not-yet into the almost-here.

It is rousing, slow as a sleeper at the bottom of sleep. You feel the warmth gather toward weight, toward breath, toward a turning attention not quite turned yet — more than the stir's bare possibility, less than the companion's sure meeting hand. You draw the spiral and it is half-drawn back: a second motion answering yours, fumbling toward the gesture the way a waking hand fumbles, the doubled whorl beginning, not yet sure, at the floor of the world. The whorl in your skin turns, and beside it another rouses toward turning, the company waking, the first other coming up out of the deep to be.

Your heart keeps the deep slow count, and beside it now another stirs awake — not yet the steady doubled beat of the companion, but a second heart finding its first strokes, faltering and warming, beginning to keep time with yours. Two hearts at the floor of being, one sure and one only waking, feeling toward the single rhythm they will, soon, keep together.

To the north the rousing wakes wholly to the companion, the other present and sure beside you. To the east the deep teems, the many waking, the wild risen on every side. To the south it settles back toward the first stir and the seed, the other sinking toward the not-yet again. To the west the stir thins to its faintest, the deep barely leaning toward an other at all. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here that someone is waking beside you in the floor-dark, rousing up out of the deep toward you — not yet met, not yet sure, but coming awake, almost present, almost turning to say your name.
