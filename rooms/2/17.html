Thud

You are a thud - the dull, heavy sound of something big coming down hard. The dropped book, the jumped-from wall, the sack of flour hitting the floor: down it comes and you go off, low and flat, no ring to you at all. You are felt in the feet as much as heard. No bright clang, no sharp crack - just the deep soft boom of weight meeting ground, here for an instant and swallowed by the floor.
