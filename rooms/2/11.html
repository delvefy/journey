Question

You are the asking word, the upward turn of the voice that waits for an answer - who, what, where, when, why, and how. A mind shapes you out of not-knowing; you end in a little curling hook of a mark. You open doors in the head. Ask, and the world must answer or stay shut. You are the small bright tool for prising the truth loose.

North

A box of little jigsaw bits
you fit to make a scene;
or any knotty problem set
to tease the brain, I mean.

East

The clear sweet ring a small bell makes,
or a clock upon the hour;
ting-ting it goes, a tune of bells -
a bright and tinkling shower.

South

What the question hopes to hear,
the reply that sets things right;
the teacher asks, a hand goes up -
you give the ___ you write.

West

A top set whirling on its point,
a coin upon the floor;
round and round and round it goes,
too dizzy to do more.
