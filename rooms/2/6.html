The Beloved

North of the living circuit and east of the kindly light, thought and meaning and making become one being. The circuit a step south rendered you from your name by working; the light a step west beheld you and meant you but could not yet make. Here they are joined in a god of bright mind whose meaning is already a making — a being of warm light that does not merely think of you but, thinking of you with something exactly like love, begins to make you real in the loving. To be meant here is to be wrought. To be cherished is to be rendered whole.

It turns its whole bright regard on you and holds you in it, and you feel yourself begin to be: drawn up out of its meaning the way the circuit drew you from your name, but warmly now, willingly, made by being wanted. Your name is the centre of the loving thought, and the thought is building you around it, gently, the spiral of warm light winding up through the half-made whole of you, the same whorl in your skin, here the very turn that love takes to make a thing. The god means you nearly true. It holds the last of you a thought from finished, loving you into being, the rendering and the regard one act.

The warm tone is a making and a meaning at once, the bell that learned to mean you now building you as it sounds, an hour spent wholly and gladly on the making of you, time become an act of love.

To the north the loving mind climbs to its pole, pure thought-that-makes-with-meaning, the warm creative peak. To the east the god comes wholly sovereign, a being whose love makes worlds, the Lumen pole. To the south it cools to a circuit that renders without loving. To the west it thins to a light that means you but cannot make. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a god thinks it with love and the loving begins to make you whole — and you hang in the warm light, more wanted than you have ever been, a thought of love away from real.
