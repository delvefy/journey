Tag

All you need is a patch of ground and someone willing to be 'it'. Tag is the simplest game in the world: one chases, the rest flee, and a single touch passes the chasing on. There is no board, no ball, nothing to lose or carry home - only running, shrieking, dodging, and the thrill of the hand that almost catches you. It begins the moment someone shouts 'You're it!' and ends only when everyone is out of breath.

North

Before you build or go or do,
you think me through with care;
step by step, first this, then that,
to get you safely there.

East

A quick shut of a single eye,
a friendly secret sign;
it says 'hello' or 'just a joke',
without a spoken line.

South

Line up at the start and wait,
then run as fast as fire;
the first to cross the finish line
is what we all desire.

West

I say what's fair and what is not,
in every game we play;
keep me, and the fun goes on -
break me, and you must pay.
