Promise

It is only words, and yet it binds. A promise is a thing you build out of breath - no weight, no edge, nothing the hand can hold - and still it can hold you for years. You give it freely and it ties you tight; you break it and something real is broken, though no one heard a snap. Of all the made things, this is the lightest and among the strongest: a wall raised out of nothing but your word.

North

I have my rules, I have my play,
I keep you busy half the day;
with cards or balls or board you start -
to win me is the player's art.

East

I pour from radios and strings,
the sound a band or choir brings;
you tap your foot, you sway, you hum -
made all of notes and beat and drum.

South

I am the hope you breathe out low
on candle flame or falling star;
blow once, say nothing, let me go -
and maybe I will travel far.

West

I am a tale told for a laugh,
a tickle hidden in my half;
I trap you with a sly surprise,
the punch line lights up your eyes.
