Scarf

You are a scarf - a long warm strip of knitted wool wound round and round the neck when the frost bites. You tuck into the collar, trail down the back, or wrap right up over the nose on the coldest days. Made by hands - knitted stitch by stitch, often by a grandmother - you come striped and tasselled and far too long. You keep the wind from creeping down inside the coat. You are the cosiest part of winter, a woolly hug worn round the throat.
