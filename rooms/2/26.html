Siren

You are a siren - the loud wailing call of a fire engine or an ambulance, rising and falling, swooping up and sliding down again. You split the traffic and turn every head; cars pull over, people stop and look. You mean hurry, you mean danger, you mean someone needs help right now. Urgent and unmistakable, you race through the streets ahead of the flashing lights, clearing the way so the help can get there in time.
