Game

Make up some rules, agree to follow them, and suddenly there is a game. It needs no real stakes - a ball, a board, a chalk square on the pavement will do - yet your whole heart pours into winning it. A game is pure invention, a little world with edges everyone pretends are real for an afternoon. Made by people for the joy of it, it turns idle time into something with a beginning, a struggle, and a winner. Then you shake hands and play again.

North

On your marks, get set, then go -
the fastest wins, the slowest slow;
we sprint or swim or pedal hard,
first past the line gets the reward.

East

Put on the music, find the beat,
and let it travel to your feet;
I'm waltz and jig and disco spin,
I move your body, out and in.

South

Cross your heart and say it true,
'I will, I swear' - that's me from you;
a word you must not break or bend,
the trust you give a faithful friend.

West

Lean in close, I'm just for you,
don't tell a soul, whatever you do;
hushed and hidden, mine to hold -
the thing that's never to be told.
