Honk

You are a honk - the loud rude blare of a car horn pressed in a hurry, or a goose stretching its neck down by the pond. No gentle thing makes you; a startled driver or a cross bird does. You blast out flat and sudden and make everyone jump and look. Nobody honks to be polite. Brash and brassy and impossible to ignore, you are the noise of impatience and of geese, a single loud parp flung out across the road or the water.
