Shush

You are a shush - one finger pressed to the lips and a long soft "shhh" of breath, asking everyone to be quiet. The librarian gives you, the grown-up at the back of the concert gives you, anyone trying to keep a sleeping baby asleep gives you. No words needed, just the finger and the hush. Gentle and firm and just a touch bossy, you are quiet made into a sign, the polite hushing that settles a noisy room into stillness.
