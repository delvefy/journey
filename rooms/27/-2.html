Paperclip

You are a paperclip - a little length of springy wire bent into a clever double loop. Slide your jaws over a sheaf of papers and they pinch tight, holding the whole lot together without a single hole. When you are done you slip off and curl up in the drawer with a hundred others, forever getting tangled into chains. Small and bendy and endlessly handy, you are the tidiest way to keep a stack of pages as one, and the most fun to unbend into a long wiggly wire when nobody is looking.
