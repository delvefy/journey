Tumble

You are a tumble - to fall and roll right over, head over heels and round again. You tumble down the grassy hill on purpose, laughing; you tumble off the climbing frame by accident, with a yelp. Acrobats tumble for show, all rolls and flips and handsprings. Over and over you go, the world spinning round. Quick and rolling and a bit dizzy-making, you are a fall turned into a roll, the head-over-heels gone-over-and-up-again that ends in a giggle or a grazed knee.
