Groan

You are a groan - a low long moan that rolls out when something hurts or wearies you. A tummy ache brings you, a too-heavy load brings you, and so does the news of extra homework. You rumble out deep in the chest, half a moan and half a grumble, saying without words: oh no, not that. Low and drawn-out and full of complaint, you are the sound of a body or a spirit that has simply had enough, sagging out in one long weary groan.
