Thumbsup

You are a thumbs-up - one fist closed, the thumb stuck straight up, a cheery silent way of saying "good!" or "yes!" or "all's well." Pilots give it before take-off, friends give it across a noisy room, a diver gives it to say they're fine underwater. One little hand says it all. Bright and friendly and quick, you are approval boiled down to a single thumb, a tiny cheer anyone can flash without a word to say everything is just fine.
