Calculator

You are a calculator - a flat little machine covered in number buttons, with a small grey screen along the top. Tap in the sums, press the plus or the times, and the answer flashes up faster than any brain. Schools let you out for the hard maths; shopkeepers tot up the bill on you. Quick and clever and never wrong, you do in a flash what would take a pencil and paper an age, and you light up with the answer before the finger has even left the button.
