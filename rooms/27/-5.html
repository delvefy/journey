Bubblewrap

You are bubble wrap - a sheet of clear plastic studded all over with little air-filled bubbles, wrapped round anything breakable to keep it safe in the post. But everyone knows the real fun of you: pop, pop, pop, squeezing each bubble between finger and thumb until the whole sheet is flat. Crackly and squashy and impossible to resist, you protect the parcel on its journey and then give hours of delicious popping to whoever unwraps it.
