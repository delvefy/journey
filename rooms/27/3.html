Smirk

You are a smirk - a small sideways smile that says "I know something you don't." One corner of the mouth curls up, the eyes go knowing, and there it is: smug, sly, just a little bit annoying. You smirk when you win, when you have a secret, when you have got away with something. Not warm like a real smile, not wide like a grin, you are the cheekiest of all the faces, the little knowing curl that makes everyone else want to know what on earth you are smirking about.
