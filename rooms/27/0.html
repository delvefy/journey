Loop

You are a loop - a circle made where a string or a line curves round and crosses itself. You make a loop to start a knot, to hang a label, to skip a rope; a rollercoaster loops right over in a great upside-down ring. Round and round you go and back to the start. Curvy and clever and endlessly useful, you are the simplest shape a length of string can make, the doubled-back circle that catches, hangs, ties and loops-the-loop.
