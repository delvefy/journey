Sleepingbag

You are a sleeping bag - a long padded bag with a zip up the side, that you wriggle right down into to sleep warm on a camping trip or a sleepover. Cosy as a cocoon, you keep out the night chill, and in the morning you roll up small and strap to the bottom of a rucksack. Snug and zip-up and just the right size, you are a bed you can carry anywhere, turning a hard tent floor or a friend's living room into a warm little nest for the night.
