sty

A sty is the pen where pigs are kept - a little walled yard with a low shelter in the corner, and mud, always mud, churned up and wallowed in and snouted about. The pigs love it; they roll in the cool muck to keep off the sun and the flies. To us it means the messiest place imaginable: tidy your room, it's like a pigsty! Grunting, snuffling, and gloriously filthy, the sty is the happiest dirty home on the farm.

North

A flap of wood beside the window,
fold me across to keep out the storm;
bang me open to let in day -
the window's wooden eyelid, warm.

East

A sturdy box of wooden slats,
pack me full of bottles or fruit;
stack me high in the back of a truck -
a strong rough box, and not so cute.

South

A woolly hat with a fluffy ball
bouncing right upon the top;
pull me down on the coldest days -
warm your ears, the bobble won't stop.

West

A box of bars to hold a bird,
a hamster, lion, or little mouse;
they can't get out, you peer right in -
a barred and locked-up little house.
