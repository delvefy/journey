vanish

To vanish is to be here, and then - nothing. The magician throws a cloth, lifts it, and the rabbit is gone. The puddle vanishes in the sun; the ship vanishes into fog; the last biscuit vanishes when no one is looking. It is more than just leaving - it is going so completely that there is no trace at all, as if you were never there. One blink, and the place where a thing was is simply empty air.

North

On soft slow paws a tiger creeps,
low and quiet, hunting in the night;
round and round it stalks its meal -
it sneaks about, just out of sight.

East

The wizard mutters magic words
and waves his wand to work a charm;
abracadabra, hey-presto! -
and something changes, for good or harm.

South

Fist closed tight, one thumb sticks high,
it means 'well done!' or 'all is good';
no words at all, just a happy sign -
you'd give it if you could.

West

Break out of the cage and run away,
slip the trap and flee the cell;
get free of the thing that holds you tight -
out the door, and all is well.
