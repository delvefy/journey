Pencilcase

You are a pencilcase - a soft zip-up pouch that holds all the bits and bobs for school: pens, pencils, a rubber, a sharpener, a half-chewed ruler. You go in the bag every morning and tip out across the desk with a clatter. Some of you have a favourite cartoon on the front; all of you somehow lose pens down the bottom. Handy and bulging and always slightly too full, you are the little kit-bag of school days, keeping a child's writing tools together and (mostly) in one place.
