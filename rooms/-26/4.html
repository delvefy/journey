Thunderstorm

You are a thunderstorm - the sky's great show of temper. Black clouds pile up and the air goes still and heavy; then the first flash splits the dark and the boom rolls across the hills, and down comes the rain in sheets. Lightning forks, thunder cracks and grumbles, and everyone counts the seconds between to see how far away you are. Wild and loud and a little bit thrilling, you clear the muggy air and pass on grumbling into the distance, leaving the world washed clean and fresh.
