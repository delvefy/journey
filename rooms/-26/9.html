Glance

You are a glance - a quick little flick of the eyes, there and gone before anyone notices. You glance at the clock to check the time, glance over your shoulder to see who's coming, glance sideways at a friend to share a secret thought. No staring, no lingering, just a swift flicker of looking. Brief and darting and easily missed, you are the smallest, fastest kind of looking, the quick peek that takes in what it needs and is gone in less than a blink.
