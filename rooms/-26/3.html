Puff

You are a puff - a small quick burst, gone almost before it comes. A puff of breath blows out the birthday candles; a puff of wind lifts a hat; a puff of smoke rings up from a chimney pot. The dragon makes you, the steam train makes you, the runner out of breath makes you at the top of the hill. Light and brief and round, you are air given a sudden little shove, here in a moment and scattered the next, soft as a dandelion clock blown away.
