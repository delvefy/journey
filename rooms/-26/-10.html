sap

Sap is the blood of a tree - sticky, slow, and sweet, it climbs up inside the trunk to feed every branch and leaf. Snap a twig in spring and a clear bead wells up; leave it on the bark and it hardens to a golden lump that catches the light. It glues your fingers together and smells of the woods. From it comes syrup for your pancakes and amber old as the hills. It is the tree, quietly running.

North

A tiny sharp needle of wood,
I slip in your skin from a rough old plank;
I sting till you dig me out with a pin -
so small, yet hardly anyone's thank.

East

Deep in a flower I hide, sweet and thin,
the bee dips down and drinks me up;
I'm the sugary juice of every bloom,
sipped from each soft petal's cup.

South

A thin pale thread inside of you
that carries the news of touch and pain;
stub your toe and I send a shout -
a tiny wire straight to your brain.

West

Flat and green, I grow on a twig,
I drink the sun and dance in the breeze;
in autumn I turn to red and gold
and flutter down from the trees.
