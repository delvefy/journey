Hay

You are hay - grass cut in summer and dried golden in the sun, then gathered into great bales and stacked high in the barn. All through the cold months you feed the cows and horses and sheep, smelling sweetly of the long warm days you came from. Children love to leap and tumble in your soft loose heaps. Golden and sweet and prickly, you are summer saved up for winter, the meadow's grass kept dry and good to feed the hungry animals until the green grows back.
