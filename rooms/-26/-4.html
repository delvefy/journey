Lime

You are a lime - a small round fruit, bright green and glossy, packed with sharp sour juice. Cut you open and you smell zingy and fresh; squeeze you and you make eyes water and lips pucker. You go in fizzy drinks and squashed over fish and into a wedge on the rim of a glass. Tart and tangy and tropical, you are the zestiest of the citrus family, a little burst of sunshine and sourness all at once, too sharp to eat alone but perfect to liven up everything else.
