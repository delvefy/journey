Chirp

You are a chirp - the short bright note of a little bird on a branch, or a cricket hidden in the long grass. You come quick and high and cheerful, one small sound after another, stitched into the hush of early morning before anyone is awake. Sparrows make you, robins make you, and on warm evenings the crickets keep you up. Tiny and tuneful and easy to miss, you are the smallest happy voice of the garden, repeated and repeated all day long.
