smack

A smack is a quick flat crack - the sound of a hand meeting a hand, a ball hitting a bat, or a big loud kiss on the cheek. It is sudden and sharp, here and gone before you can blink, and it leaves a sting and a ring in the air. You hear it more than you see it. Whether it is fun or it hurts, it is always loud and always quick, the sound of two things meeting hard.

North

When both the clock's hands point straight up
and the old day rolls into the new;
the darkest deep middle of night -
twelve o'clock, when owls call too.

East

The dull deep sound a dropped book makes,
no ring, no clang, just heavy and low;
a sack of spuds falls off the shelf -
one fat soft thump on the floor below.

South

A quick short look, then eyes away,
you peek and then you turn;
you didn't stare, you didn't gaze -
just a flick, with nothing to learn.

West

Your nose's job, both sweet and bad,
of flowers, or of rotten cheese;
a baking cake, a stinky sock -
you breathe me in upon the breeze.
