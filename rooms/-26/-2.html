Pimple

You are a pimple - a small red bump that swells up on the skin overnight, usually right where it shows. You sit on a nose or a chin, sore to the touch, and you arrive without asking just before a photo or a party. Everyone gets you; nobody wants you. In a day or two you fade and go. Tiny and tender and badly timed, you are the skin's little grumble, here today, gone by the weekend, and best of all left well alone.
