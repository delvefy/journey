Scale

You are a scale - one of the hundreds of tiny hard plates that cover a fish or a snake, overlapping like the tiles on a roof. Together you make a skin that is both flexible and tough, flashing silver as the fish twists in the water or the snake slides through the grass. Smooth one way, rough the other. Small and shiny and neatly fitted, you are armour grown right out of the skin, a coat of glittering little shields that lets a creature bend and slither and swim with ease.
