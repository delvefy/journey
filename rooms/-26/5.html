Earthquake

You are an earthquake - the terrible moment when the solid ground itself begins to shake. Deep below, the great plates of the Earth grind and slip, and up here the floor heaves, the walls crack, the dishes leap from the shelves. People run for doorways and hold on tight as the whole world rocks and rumbles. Sudden and frightening and over in moments, you are the Earth shrugging in its sleep, a reminder that even the ground beneath our feet is not always as steady as it seems.
