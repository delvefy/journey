Melon

You are a melon - a big heavy round fruit with a thick rind outside and cool sweet flesh within. Thump you and you sound hollow when you're ripe. Cut you open and out comes the juice, pink or green or orange, studded with seeds. On a hot day a wedge of you, cold from the fridge, runs down chins and fingers. Round and ripe and refreshing, you are summer's juiciest treat, sliced into grins of dripping sweetness and devoured down to the rind.
