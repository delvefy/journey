Splinter

You are a splinter - a tiny needle-thin sliver of wood that breaks off a rough old plank or fence and slides into a finger, where it stings and throbs until it's tweezed out. So small you can hardly see it, you cause a great deal of fuss for such a little thing. Sharp and sneaky and best handled with care, you are wood at its most spiteful, lurking on a splintery surface ready to catch the next unwary hand that brushes too carelessly past.
