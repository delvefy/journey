Splutter

You are a splutter - the spraying cough-and-gasp that bursts out when a drink goes down the wrong way, or a shock makes you choke on your words. Out it comes, half a cough and half a spit, in a fine wet spray, while you wave a hand and try to get your breath. A spluttering candle does it too, fizzing and popping before it gives up. Sudden and wet and a bit embarrassing, you are speech and breath gone wrong all at once, sprayed out in a flustered fit.
