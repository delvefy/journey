nerve

A nerve is one of the thin pale threads that run all through your body, carrying messages at lightning speed. Touch something hot and a nerve shrieks the news to your brain so fast your hand jumps back before you even decide. Stub your toe and a nerve sends up the ouch. They are the body's wiring, thinner than thread, reaching from your fingertips to the top of your spine - the reason you can feel anything at all.

North

The sticky sweet juice inside a tree,
it climbs the trunk to feed each leaf;
snap a twig and a bead wells up -
it glues your fingers, to your grief.

East

A blue line underneath your skin,
it carries your blood back to your heart;
a thin soft tube that runs throughout -
the body's roads, each one a part.

South

A little brown bird with a bright red chest,
it hops on the lawn for a worm to eat;
on Christmas cards it sits in the snow -
a friendly garden bird, so sweet.

West

A small brown bird, plain and quick,
it chirps and hops in the hedge all day;
it pecks up crumbs and dust-bathes too -
the commonest bird in the way.
