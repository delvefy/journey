Gulp

You are a gulp - a big sudden swallow, the throat bobbing up and down. You gulp your drink too fast when you are thirsty, and gulp from fright when something startles you in the dark. There is a sound to you, a little glug, and a look: wide eyes and a bobbing throat. Quick and nervous and easy to spot, you are the swallow that gives away a fright or a thirst, the big down-it-goes that comes before a brave deed or a too-hasty drink.
