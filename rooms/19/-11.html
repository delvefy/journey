Wall

You are a wall - the upright slab of brick or stone that holds the roof up and shuts the weather out. You make a room a room, dividing inside from outside, mine from yours. Children draw on you, ivy climbs you, pictures hang from you. Some of you keep cattle in a field; one of you was so long it could be seen from space. To talk to one is to get no answer; to be up against one is to be stuck. You are the solid maker of every shelter.
