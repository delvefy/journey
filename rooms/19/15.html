Scoot

You are a scoot - the quick little dash you make to get out of the way or get gone fast, sliding along on a seat or shooting off on two wheels. People do you in a hurry: scoot over, scoot off, scoot down the path before you're caught. You are nimble and low and faster than a walk, a sudden small getaway. Half a slide and half a sprint, you are the cheeky quick escape of someone who would rather not stay.
