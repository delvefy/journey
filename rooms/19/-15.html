Wool

You are wool - the thick warm fleece a sheep grows all winter, sheared off in spring and washed and combed by busy hands into something to wear. You are itchy and cosy at once, the stuff of jumpers and mittens and blankets that smell faintly of the field. You trap the warm air close and keep the cold out. Grown on a back in the meadow, then turned by people into the snuggest cloth there is.
