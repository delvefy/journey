Today

You are today - the day happening right now, the one you are awake inside of this very minute. You sit between the day that has gone and the day not yet arrived, the only day a person can ever truly act in. When the sun sets you slip quietly into yesterday and a new today begins. You cannot be touched or kept, only lived. You are the present itself, the single day where everything real is done, used up, and never given back.
