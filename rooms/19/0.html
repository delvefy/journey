Rake

You are a rake - a long wooden handle ending in a row of stiff teeth, made for gathering. A hand carved and fitted you; a hand drags you across the lawn to comb the fallen leaves into a heap, or smooths the soil flat for the seed. You can be held, leaned in the shed, stepped on - and then your handle leaps up and bonks the nose. You are patient work made easy, the gentle comb of the whole garden.
