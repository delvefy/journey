Dance

You are a dance - the happy moving of your body in time to the music: a twirl, a hop, a stamp, a sway, arms up and round you go. No hand can hold you; the music makes you, and your own two feet, and you cannot keep still once you start. You are made of motion, gone the moment it stops. At a party, a wedding, or alone in your room, you are joy the body simply must let out, music turned into the whole of you.
