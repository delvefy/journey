News

You are the news - word of what has just happened, gathered up and carried to everyone at once: a flood, a winner, a king's new baby. No hand can hold you, though hands print and broadcast you; you are made of fresh facts, and you go stale by tomorrow. You arrive on the doorstep, the screen, the radio, the lips of a neighbour over the fence. You are how a person far away learns that the wide world has moved while they slept.
