Surf

You are surf - to ride the breaking wave on a board, standing tall as the sea rushes you in. You paddle out past the swell, wait, then spring up and race the curling water to the shore. The salt spray stings, the board hums beneath your feet, and for a few wild seconds you fly on water. Wipe out and the sea tumbles you over and over. You are the bold game of riding the ocean, balanced on its very edge.
