Wait

You are a wait - the standing-still while time goes slowly by, holding on for the bus, the turn, the day that has not come. You are all patience and tapping feet and watching the clock crawl. The dog does you by the door for its walk; the child does you for a birthday that takes forever. You cannot be hurried, only borne. You teach the hard lesson that some things will not come faster for all your wishing. You are the quiet, restless in-between, the long pause before the thing arrives.
