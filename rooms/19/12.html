Sink

You are sink - to go down under the water when you are too heavy to float. The stone you drop plops and sinks to the riverbed; the holed boat fills and sinks; the sun seems to sink into the sea at dusk. Down, down you go, slow or fast, until you rest on the bottom. The opposite of float, you are what the heavy and the waterlogged do. You are the slow going-under, the journey down to the deep.
