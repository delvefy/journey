Clap

You are a clap - the sharp bright sound of two hands smacked together, again and again, to show that you are pleased. A pair of hands makes you, and a crowd of them makes a roar of you when the show is done. You cannot be held, only heard - a single clap to call attention, a storm of them for applause. Thunder claps; a teacher claps for quiet. You are joy and praise turned into noise, the sound of many hands agreeing well done.
