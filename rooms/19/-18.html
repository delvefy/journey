Axe

You are an axe - a heavy wedge of sharp steel fixed to a long wooden handle, made for chopping. Swung high and brought down hard, you bite deep into a log and split it clean in two. Woodcutters carry you, firewood needs you, and a fairy-tale woodsman is never without one. Strong and simple and a little dangerous, you turn a fallen tree into a winter's worth of logs, one mighty stroke at a time, and you must always be handled with the greatest of care.
