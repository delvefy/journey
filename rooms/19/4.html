Mash

You are mash - the soft smooth heap you get by squashing boiled potatoes with a masher and a little butter, until every lump is gone. A hand makes you, pressing down and stirring, turning hard lumps into a fluffy white cloud on the plate. You cannot be held, only spooned, and a hollow pressed in the top is best for the gravy. Comfort itself, you sit beside the sausages and soak up everything good. You are dinner at its softest and kindest.
