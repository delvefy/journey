Fidget

You are a fidget - the restless wriggling of someone who cannot sit still, fingers drumming, feet jiggling, bottom shuffling on the hard assembly floor. No one chooses you; too much energy and too little to do make you. 'Sit still!' they say, and you try, and last about four seconds. You twiddle and squirm and tap and twist. Wriggly and restless and bursting with held-in energy, you are a body's quiet rebellion against being made to keep still.
