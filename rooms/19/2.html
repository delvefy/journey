Cheer

You are a cheer - the great glad shout a crowd lets up when something wonderful happens: a goal scored, a race won, a hero come home. No hand can hold you; many voices make you all at once, hip-hip-hooray, and you fill the whole stadium like a wave. You cannot be touched, only felt in the chest and heard for miles. You are joy made loud, the sound of a hundred people agreeing, for one bright second, to be happy together.
