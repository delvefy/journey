Nail

You are a nail - a slim straight spike of steel with a flat little head, made to be hammered through one plank into another and hold them locked for good. A hand sets you, a hammer drives you, and you bite deep into the wood and grip with all your length. Bend, and you are useless; straight, and you hold a whole house up. Plain and cheap and countless, you are the smallest, stubbornest fastener there is, doing your one job in the dark inside the wood forever.
