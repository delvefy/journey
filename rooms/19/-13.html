Scissors

You are scissors - two sharp blades crossed and pinned at the waist, with two round holes for finger and thumb. Open wide and snap shut, and you bite cleanly through paper, ribbon, hair, or string. You walk along a folded line with a steady snip-snip-snip. Held wrong you are blunt and clumsy; held right you cut a straight road through anything flat. You are a pair that works as one, two knives that only cut when they cross.
