Pickaxe

You are a pickaxe - a heavy iron head, pointed at one end, fixed across a stout wooden handle. Swing it high and bring it down, and the sharp point cracks into rock or frozen earth that no spade could ever dig. Miners hew at the rock face with you; road-menders break up the old surface. Heavy and pointed and swung with a grunt, you are the tool for the hardest digging of all, biting deep where everything else just bounces off.
