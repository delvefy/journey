Billow

You are a billow - to swell and roll outward in great soft folds. A ship's sail billows full in the wind; smoke billows up in rolling clouds from a fire; a long dress billows out as the dancer spins. You puff and bulge and roll, never quite still. Soft and rolling and grand, you are cloth or smoke or cloud caught by the wind and heaved out into great curving waves, swelling and sinking and swelling again.
