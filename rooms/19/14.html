Trick

You are a trick - a clever sly move that fools the eye or beats the odds. The skateboarder flips the board and lands it; the magician makes the coin vanish; the fox doubles back to lose the hounds. You take practice and cunning and a little showing-off. Half of you is skill and half is surprise. People gasp, then grin, then ask how it was done. You are cleverness with a flourish, the quick move that leaves everyone wondering.
