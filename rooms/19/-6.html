Doll

You are a doll - a little pretend person made of cloth or plastic, with painted eyes and stitched-on hair, made to be loved and looked after. A hand made you and a child's hands carry you everywhere: dressed and undressed, fed pretend dinners, tucked into a shoebox bed. You can be held, cuddled, dropped, mended when a seam comes loose. You never grow up and never answer back. You are a small still friend who waits to be picked up, the keeper of a child's pretend.
