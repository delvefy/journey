Skirt

You are a skirt - the piece of clothing that hangs from the waist and swishes loose about the legs, with no trouser-legs at all. You twirl out wide when the wearer spins, and flutter in the wind on a summer day. You come long or short, pleated or plain, in every colour there is. You are made by hand from cloth and thread, cut and hemmed and gathered. You are the easy, breezy thing to wear when the day is warm, the garment that loves to dance.
