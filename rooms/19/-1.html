Hook

You are a hook - a clever bend of metal or wire, shaped to hold or to catch. A hand forged you; a hand screws you to the wall to take a coat, or ties you to a line to take a fish. You can be held, hung from, snagged on a sleeve. Your whole secret is the curve: straight, you would let everything slide off, but bent just so, you grip and will not let go. You are the simplest way to keep a thing from falling.
