Leap

You are a leap - a great bold jump, up and over and far, more than a hop and braver than a step: a frog off a log, a dancer in the air, a salmon up the falls. No hand makes you; two strong legs and a bit of courage do. You cannot be held, only taken, that thrilling moment of hanging in the air with nothing underneath. Leap-frog, a leap year, a leap of faith - you are the jump you take when walking will not do.
