Kite

You are a kite - a light frame of sticks and paper or cloth, with a long tail and a string, made to fly. A hand built you and a hand holds your string, running into the wind until you lift and climb, dipping and diving high above the field. You can be held, launched, reeled in, tangled in a tree. You need the wind and you need a runner, and between them you dance in the sky like a fish on a line. You are flight held by a thread.
