Jar

You are a jar - a little tower of clear glass with a screw-on lid, made to keep things and to show them off at once. A hand blew or moulded you; a hand fills you with jam, with pickles, with buttons, with a captured beetle to watch a while. You can be held, shaken, screwed shut tight - sometimes too tight to open. You sit in a row in the cupboard, see-through and proud of your contents. You are how a person saves a little of something good for later.
