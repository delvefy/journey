Fork

You are a fork - a handle ending in sharp little prongs, made for stabbing and lifting. A hand forged you; a hand holds you at dinner to spear a sausage, twirl the spaghetti, mash the peas. There is a bigger cousin of you out in the garden, for turning the soil, but most know you from the table, beside the knife and spoon. You can be held, washed, dropped with a clatter. You are how a person picks up food without using fingers.
