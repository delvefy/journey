Stroke

You are a stroke - the smooth gentle movement of a hand passed slowly over something soft: a cat's fur, a child's hair, a worried brow. A hand makes you, slow and kind, again and again, and the cat purrs and leans in for more. You cannot be held, only given and felt. A stroke of a brush lays the paint; a stroke of luck just happens; a stroke of the bell marks the hour. You are the kindest thing a hand can do, soothing and slow.
