Waft

You are a waft - to drift softly through the air, slow and gentle and going wherever the breeze takes you. The smell of baking bread wafts from the kitchen; a feather wafts down from a pillow; smoke wafts up lazy from a chimney. You never rush and never fall straight; you float and curl and sink so slowly you seem to hang. Light and dreamy and soft as a sigh, you are the way scents and feathers and the lightest things move, riding the air without a care in the world.
