Hose

You are a hose - the long green rubber snake that carries water from the tap out across the garden. Turn the handle and you swell and stiffen and spit a bright cold jet from your nozzle, and the flowers get their drink and the children get a soaking on a hot day. You coil up on the wall when you are done, dripping. You kink and tangle and need a good unknotting. You are the garden's long thin river on a tap, all squirt and spray and summer fun.
