Book

You are a book - a stack of pages bound along one edge, waiting to be opened. Lift the cover and the words begin, row after tidy row, and you carry a whole world inside you that cannot be seen until someone reads. You are made by hands - cut, printed, sewn, and glued - yet you hold quiet voices and far places. Turn the leaves one by one, and you are a door that opens flat in the hand.

North

Two crossed blades pinned at the waist,
with rings for finger and thumb;
I snip along a folded line -
name me, and the cut is done.

East

I sit on the stove and start to sing
when my belly is filling with heat;
a spout, a handle, a rattling lid -
a ___ boils water to drink.

South

Soft and warm, I grow on a sheep,
they shear me off in the spring;
they spin me to yarn and knit me up -
a jumper's the ___ they bring.

West

A long brass horn you push and pull,
its shiny slide slides out;
it blares a deep and swooping note -
a ___, without a doubt.
