Slouch

You are a slouch - the droopy way of sitting or standing when all the bones go soft. Shoulders round, back curved, chin sunk, you ooze down into the sofa like you might never get up. "Sit up straight!" they say, but you only slump lower. You are the shape of a lazy afternoon, of a long boring lesson, of a teenager at breakfast. Loose and lolling and comfortable, you let gravity win and fold yourself into the saggiest, easiest shape a body can make.
