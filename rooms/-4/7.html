The Blank

West of the rust and north of the nullity, the grey wears past grey into a blank. A step east the machine future rusted wholly down, a ruin dissolving into the Waste; a step south the killed mind and the never-mind cancelled to a pure nullity. Here the cold corner is exceeded entirely: the grey worn so far past grey that it blanches, bleaches, blows out into a featureless blank — not dark now and not even grey, but a colourless over-exposure, the Chrome past the Chrome, the made dissolved past trace and the nullity bleached past zero into a blank that is no longer the dead future's and not yet anything else, only blank.

It is the over-pole's last condition, past dead and past grey: a blank glare with nothing in it to be dead or grey or cancelled, the cold worn so absolute it has bleached even darkness white. You draw the spiral and the blank neither rusts it nor cancels it nor shrouds it; there is no surface, no grey, no anything for the mark to be against, only the featureless blank. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its warm dark turn, the one thing with colour and edge in a blank that has bleached past all of it, the single un-blanked mark at the over-pole of the cold.

There is no count, only the blank, where the clatter rusted to silence and the silence bleached past even itself. Your heart beats into the over-exposure, the one warm dark stroke in a blank glare that has worn past grey and past nothing alike, keeping a coloured time in a place bleached colourless to the pole.

To the north the blank reaches the over-pole absolute, grey worn past all grey, the Chrome exceeded entirely. To the east the rust, the machine future ruined but not yet blanched. To the south the nullity, the cancelled zero. To the west the blank dissolves wholly into the Waste, the made forgotten past trace, the pole. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold age recorded it once. Here even the record is bleached past grey into a blank that holds nothing — your name blanched colourless — and still the warm dark coil turns, the one un-blanked mark, and still one coloured beat keeps on into the blank at the over-pole of the cold.
