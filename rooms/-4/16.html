Tear

You are a tear - a single salty drop that wells up in the eye and rolls down the cheek. You come with sadness, with pain, with laughing too hard, and sometimes for no reason at all. You blur the world for a moment, then fall and are gone, leaving a wet shining trail. Wiped away by a thumb or a sleeve, you are the body's quiet way of letting the feeling out.
