The Paling

West of the never-was and south of the long quiet, the nothing pales past nothing. A step east there was at least a never-was — a floor of raw cold ground, a pallor that knew itself the oldest thing in the room; a step north the cold at least held still, gathered toward a wind it had not yet breathed. Here even those thin claims bleach out. The pallor that was past all colour pales further, the way a thing left too long in the light goes from grey to less, until there is not even the never-was: no nothing to be old, no absence to be kept, only a paling-on where the last faint fact — that nothing was here — has itself gone pale and let go.

You do not reach for the coil, and this time it is not the hand or the ground that is missing but the memory of why. The spiral followed you as chalk, as horn, as light, as a small cold fear of its own loss; here even the loss has paled, the fear bleached too faint to feel, so that you cannot say whether you have forgotten the mark or forgotten that there was ever a mark to forget. And yet something in you balks at the not-knowing — a last grain that minds the paling, that would rather it not. That minding is the whole of the spiral left: not the turn of it, only the faint refusal to let it go all the way white.

There is no count, and no held breath waiting to start one. The long quiet to the north at least gathered toward a wind; here nothing gathers, because gathering would be a thing that happened, and nothing happens in the paling. No wind. No bell. No grey. North the cold firms back to the held quiet, the origin the wind is not yet born from. East the paling gathers back into the never-was, the Waste proper, ash without a fire and old at last. South the void sinks before even the paling, before there was anything pale enough to fade. West the Waste opens on toward the forgotten pole, where names died so long ago that nothing recalls they were names, and nothing answers, and nothing ever did. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the name has not been forgotten — that would be too much to have happened; here it simply never paled into being, and you carry, into the white, only the grain in you that minds.
