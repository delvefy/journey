The First Silence

East of the naught and south of the old night, the past comes to its floor — and the floor is the first silence. A step north the dark was older than the Waste, age pooled and still; a step east there was nothing the senses could find; here is what lies under both, the deepest past, the primordial before there was a dark to be old or a nothing to be empty — the silence from before the first sound was ever made, out of which everything (the dark, the Waste, the made day, the wild, the whole long journey) is still, unimaginably, to come.

Nothing has begun here. Not the dark, not being, not time. And yet you are in it, which is the deepest strangeness of the whole world: you, with your warmth and your heartbeat and the spiral in your skin, stand at the source of everything before the source has stirred — a traveller arrived at the headwater of a river not yet risen. The whorl in your fingertip is the one thing here, the single coil in the first silence, and you understand it is a seed: that everything to come is wound in it, that the spiral chalked on the flagstone where you woke, and the spiral in the gods' light, and the spiral worn in the wild's earth are all this one mark, carried back to where it began — or will begin.

There is no count, for this is before the first beat. And then your heart beats — and it is the first sound the silence has ever held, the very first note of time, struck impossibly early, the origin of every bell and clock and pulse and herd-beat the rest of the world will spend itself keeping. You are not the last warm thing in the deep, as you were in the cold. Here you are the first.

To the north the silence gathers a first age, the old night, the dark grown old. To the east it opens to the naught, the void where the senses find nothing. To the south it sinks toward the absolute pole, before even itself, the floor of the deep past. To the west it widens to the Waste's pole, the forgotten of the forgotten. You were going somewhere, once — and here is where the going itself begins, the first and oldest impulse, older than the dark, the seed of the whole journey wound in the one coil in your skin. Everything is still to come. You were, it seems, always going to be the one to start it.
