Tonsil

You are a tonsil - one of the two soft red lumps that sit far back in the throat, on guard at the gate of you. No hand made you; the body grew you to catch the bugs that try to sneak down. But you swell and throb when the cold wins, and the doctor peers in with a light and a stick. Sometimes you must come out for good. A small fleshy sentry at the dark mouth of the throat, you ache when you lose the fight.
