Pea

Small and green and perfectly round, hidden in a long velvet pod - a pea is a tidy little secret of the garden. Split the pod with your thumb and there they sit in a neat row, a handful of bright beads ready to roll away. Sweet raw, sweeter cooked, too small to be a meal and too cheerful to be missed. Made by no hand but the sun and the rain, it is one of the plainest, gladdest green things that grows.

North

A sour yellow fruit,
I make your lips pucker tight;
squeezed in sugared water
I'm a summer delight.

East

A long yellow fruit
with a peel you slip on;
monkeys love me best -
bend me and I'm gone.

South

Red and round and juicy,
I grow upon a vine;
in a salad or a sauce
my squashy self is fine.

West

Long and orange,
I grow underground;
rabbits love to munch me
with a crisp loud sound.
