Heat

You are heat - the fierce pouring warmth of fire and sun, far stronger than a gentle glow. You shimmer above the summer road and make the desert dance. You cook the dinner, melt the ice, and bring the sweat to a brow. Stand too near a blaze and you drive me back, gasping, to the shade. You cannot be seen, only felt, blazing off everything that burns. You are the wild fierce face of warmth, the breath of every fire.
