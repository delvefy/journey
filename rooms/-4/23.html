Fire

You are fire - the dancing flames of heat and light. You leap orange, red, and gold, crackle through the logs, and throw warm light on every face turned toward you. You cook the food, warm the cold, and chew wood down to ash. Get too close and you bite; left untended you run wild. People have gathered around you and told stories since the very beginning. You are tamed lightning in the hearth, beautiful and hungry, the oldest friend and danger both.
