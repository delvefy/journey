Pour

You are a pour - the steady stream of liquid tipped out and falling: milk into a glass, rain coming down hard, tea from the spout. No hand need make you, only tip something up and let gravity do the rest. You cannot be held, only watched and caught in a cup. It is pouring with rain; pour me a drink; pour your heart out. You are flowing-out at full tilt, a whole stream poured from one place to another in a smooth bright fall.
