Toe

You are a toe - one of the ten little wigglers at the end of the foot. You grip the ground so the body can balance and spring; you curl in the sand, you peek from a sandal, you get stubbed hard on the table leg and make the whole house hear about it. The littlest of you went to market; the big one leads the way. Grown of you, bunched in a sock, you are how a foot feels its way and holds the world.
