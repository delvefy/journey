Second

You are the tick of the clock, the smallest beat that time is cut into - sixty of you to a minute, and gone in the saying of one. No hand can hold you; you are the pulse under everything, the count of a heartbeat, the one elephant a child says to measure you out. Quick as you are, you matter: a race is lost by you, a rocket launches on you. You are time at its finest grain, here and instantly past.

North

The warm strong feeling in the heart
for family and friend;
it sends a kiss, it gives a hug,
and never wants to end.

East

What a little star will do,
a wink of far-off light;
on and off it seems to blink
across the velvet night.

South

Three hundred and sixty-five whole days,
four seasons in their turn;
from New Year's Day right round again -
a long time, you will learn.

West

The day that's done and slipped behind,
the one before today;
it cannot ever come again,
forever tucked away.
