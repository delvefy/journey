Mouth

You are a mouth - the opening in the face where food goes in and words come out. You hold the teeth and the tongue, you stretch into a smile and gape into a yawn. You taste the sweet and the sour, you whistle and you sing, you blow out the birthday candles in one big puff. You kiss goodnight and bite the apple. You are the busiest doorway in the body, where eating and talking and laughing all crowd in together, never quite still for long.
