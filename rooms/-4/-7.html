The Last Dark

South of the unfounded and west of the dark before, even the oldest dark dissolves. A step north there was no foundation, the deep resting on nothing; a step east the body was given back to the dark before the first making, the oldest dark of all. Here that last dark goes too. The undoing reaches past even the primordial dark and dissolves it — no foundation, no under, no oldest dark to be returned to, not even an under to be the underside of. This is the corner where the deep past and the Waste converge past their last thing: the last dark, dissolving, after which there is no dark, no floor, no under, nothing the nothing can even be the absence of.

It is the end of the descent and past it: the oldest dark, which every undoing fell back into, here undone itself. You draw the spiral and there is no dark to draw it in, no under to set it on, no oldest origin to keep it faint — the gesture dissolves with the last dark. And still — impossibly, past the last dark, at the corner of everything — the whorl in your skin turns, the one coil that needs no dark to hold it, the single mark where even the oldest dark has dissolved, refusing the corner past the corner.

There is no count, and no dark for one to keep time in. Your heart beats into the dissolved last dark, and there is no oldest dark left to take the warmth, no under for the beat to fall through, only the bare stroke itself past the end of everything — the one warmth where even the dark is undone, refusing, against all the corner has dissolved, to dissolve.

To the north the last dark rises to the unfounded, a deep at least resting on nothing. To the east the dark before, the oldest dark still there to be returned to. To the south the corner-pole absolute, past even the last dark, where there is not even a dissolving left. To the west the Waste corner entire, the oldest dark dissolved wholly, the pole. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the name, before the dark, before the first making. Here even the last dark dissolves beneath the going — and still the warm coil turns where no dark holds it, and still one beat keeps on past the last dark, at the corner of everything that ever was undone.
