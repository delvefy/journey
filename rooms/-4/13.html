Comfort

A blanket, a warm drink, a familiar voice when things have gone wrong - comfort is the soft easing of a hurt. It does not fix what happened; it only sits beside you and makes the weight bearable. A hug after a fall, a known song, the lap of someone who loves you. You cannot buy it or hold it, only give it and receive it. Of all the quiet gifts one person can hand another, it asks the least and means the most.

North

The colour of candyfloss
and a piglet's nose,
of bubblegum and lips
and a soft summer rose.

East

The way a candle dances
when a draught slips by;
the light wavers and shakes
and threatens to die.

South

The deep heavy sorrow
when someone you love is gone;
it sits like a stone
as the days drag on.

West

The brave strong feeling
that helps you face your fear;
you do the hard thing
though your knees shake near.
