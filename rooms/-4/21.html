Steam

You are steam - the hot white cloud that rises curling off the boiling pot and the kettle's spout. You fog up the bathroom mirror and the cold window; you whistle the kettle to a scream; you drive the great old trains down the track. You are water turned to air by heat, too hot to touch, vanishing the moment you cool. You twist and coil and rise and are gone. You are the breath of boiling things, white and warm and fleeting, here one moment and melted into nothing the next.
