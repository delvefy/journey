Love

You are the deepest, warmest feeling there is, the pull of the heart toward another - the parent for the child, the friend for the friend, the old pair hand in hand. No hand makes you; you grow on your own, quiet or fierce, and you give without counting the cost. You forgive, you wait, you stay. Songs and stories cannot stop telling of you, and still they never finish. Greatest of all the feelings, you are the warmth that binds one life to another, the heart's true home.

North

The heavy, teary, downcast feeling
when something good is lost;
it brings the tears, it slows the step,
a grey and gloomy frost.

East

The quiet faith that good will come
when all looks dark and grim;
it keeps you trying one more time
and lights the way back in.

South

The smallest tick the clock counts off,
one beat, and then it's spent;
sixty make a minute up -
a tiny measurement.

West

The warm glad swell of being pleased
with something done just right;
it lifts the chin and puffs the chest -
a prize won fair, a height.
