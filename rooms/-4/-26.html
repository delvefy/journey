Molar

You are a molar - one of the big flat teeth right at the back of the mouth, broad and strong and ridged on top. While the sharp front teeth bite and tear, you do the heavy work: grinding and crushing every mouthful to a soft pulp, side to side, ready to swallow. You are the last to grow and the one the dentist watches for holes. Solid and patient and out of sight, you are the millstone of the mouth, chewing tirelessly away at breakfast, lunch and tea.
