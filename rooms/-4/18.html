Gush

You are a gush - a sudden strong rush of water all at once. The burst pipe gushes across the floor; the unblocked drain gushes away; turn the tap on full and out you blast. You are water in a hurry, far too much far too fast, pouring and flooding and splashing. No hand can hold you back once you start - you are the great whooshing rush of water set free, gone in a roaring torrent before anyone can find the off-switch.
