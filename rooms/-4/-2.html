The Long Quiet

South of the never-built and west of the scoured flat, the day is wholly gone and the wind is not yet born. A step north there was still bare cold ground and a grey forgetting of a day; a step east the wind already went combing, steady and eastward, having somewhere to go. Here is the place it goes from. This is older than any country, the deep before there was a ground to forget upon — and it is stiller than the flat, stiller than the going wind, because the long quiet is not crossing here but beginning: a held nothing, gathered to no purpose, that has not yet breathed out and may not, for nothing made it and nothing waits on the far side to be reached.

You stand where a body should make the cold break around it, and the cold does not notice the breaking. There is less here than on the flat to the east — not even the worked grey ground, only the bare cold origin the scouring comes out of, a place that was never anything and so keeps nothing and has, in all the long quiet, nothing to forget. You think to draw the spiral and your hand will not lift to it; there is no surface, no ash, no need. The only coil is the slow first turn the quiet makes as it leans, far off, toward becoming the wind — a curve with no hand in it, winding once at the mouth of the going and letting go.

No count has started. The wind that keeps its slow in-and-out a step east has not, here, drawn the first breath to keep; before the exhalation there is only the held cold, too whole to tick. North the ground firms back to the never-built flat, the day half-remembered, the draught lifting to go. East the quiet stirs awake into the wind proper, combing the grey and carrying nothing. South the deep sinks below even this, before there was a place for quiet to be born. West the Waste opens past the wind's own mouth, into the forgotten where names died so long ago that nothing recalls they were names. You were going somewhere, under a name. The quiet here was never going anywhere, and has nothing to carry, and would not know your name to let it go.
