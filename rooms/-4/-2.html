The Standstill

The wind comes here to stop. East of you, where the long quiet was born, a draught at least crossed the open ground and carried the dust; north of you the day at least still failed, a last grey light leaving a sky. Here the day has gone wholly out and the wind has thinned to nothing, and what is left is a standstill so complete it has a weight to it — the open Waste at the bottom of its night, lightless, windless, the cold risen all the way up out of the ground to stand in the still air like water in a flooded room.

You draw the spiral in the dust, and for once nothing takes it away. There is no draught to smooth it, no silt to slump it shut, no slow weight to close it; the line simply stays, exact, where your finger left it. And that is worse, somehow, than the blowing-away — for the mark holds and means nothing, kept perfectly by a stillness that will never send anyone to read it, a coil drawn for no eye in a dark that will hold it untouched until the end of a world already ended here. The whorl in your skin and the whorl in the dust, the only two marks, and no one, ever, to tell them apart.

There is no count. With the wind gone there is not even the low voice the Waste once had; the silence is total, and so deep it presses on the ears like depth, a quiet you could drown in. Your heart beats, and the standstill neither keeps it nor spends it nor hears it; it falls into the stillness and is gone, and the stillness does not so much as ripple.

To the north the dark lifts toward a failing grey day, the open Waste with a last light leaving and a wind not yet wholly stopped. To the east the still air stirs back into a draught, the long quiet where the wind is at least born. To the south the standstill sinks into the deep before any ground, the dark older than the Waste, older than the wind it stopped. To the west the Waste opens wider into the void, past the last grain of ash, where there was never even a wind to come to a stop. You were going somewhere, once, with the wind at your back. The wind has stopped here, and the dark with it, and your one mark holds in the dust behind you, perfect and meaningless, for no one to follow.
