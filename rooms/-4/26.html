Cinder

You are a cinder - a small hard lump of coal that has burned right through, all its fire spent. In the morning you lie cold and black and crumbly in the bottom of the grate, where last night's blaze roared. Rake the ashes and you rattle out, light and brittle, ready to be shovelled away. Burnt and brittle and done, you are what is left when the warmth has gone, the grey-black remains of a fire that has had its night and given up all its heat.
