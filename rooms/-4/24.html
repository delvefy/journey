Ash

You are ash - the soft pale dust a fire leaves behind when all the wood is spent and the flames have gone to sleep. No hand makes you; burning does, turning the solid log to a grey ghost of itself, light as a feather and quick to scatter. You drift up the chimney, you smudge a finger, you settle warm in the grate. The quiet end of every fire, you are what is left when the heat and the light have finished and gone out.
