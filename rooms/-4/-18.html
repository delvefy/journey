Ear

You are an ear - one of the two soft curled flaps on the sides of the head, and the cleverest of listeners. You catch every sound that floats by: a whisper, a song, a far-off bark, a secret cupped behind a hand. You go red in the cold, you wear an earring, you get washed behind. Grown of the living head, curled like a little shell, you are how the whole noisy world finds its way, sound by sound, into the listening mind.
