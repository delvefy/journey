The Chrome

East of the forgetting and north of the last hum, the disenchanted future comes to its pole. The cold mind that lapsed to grey a step east has finished lapsing; the field that hummed its last a step south has gone silent; what remains is the Chrome at its peak — the sterile end of the road that traded wonder for certainty and machinery for mind and then shed even those, until nothing is left but a final grey, a dead even light over an emptiness that was once the height of all cold thought. The wonder was banished. Then the world. Then the mind that banished them. This is what they left.

It is the bleak twin of a brightness elsewhere — somewhere, you feel, gods of light walk a future that kept its wonder; here is the future that killed it, arrived at the same post-human height and finding only grey. Nothing here knows you. Nothing here remembers that knowing was a thing, or that you, or names, or warmth ever were. You draw the spiral and the dead pole does not sterilize it, does not forget it, does not anything — and that is how you know the whorl in your skin is the one thing the Chrome never got: the last warm mark, the last living coil, carried intact through a future that forgot everything else it made. It will not light here. It will not be read. But it holds, where all the made world's brilliance went to grey.

There is no count. The bell, the clock, the drive-beat, the cold reckoning, the carrier hum — all of it summed and shed and forgotten, time itself banished with the wonder, an hour the Chrome perfected and then could not recall the use of. Only your heart, the first clock, beats on in the grey, unforgotten because the Chrome never made it and so could never unmake it.

To the north the Chrome reaches its absolute pole, dead signal shed of even signal, pure grey. To the east the grey stirs back toward the forgetting, a cold mind still lapsing, not yet wholly gone. To the south the pole firms to the last hum, the made world worn to a fading tone. To the west the Chrome dissolves into the open Waste, the future that killed magic gone to the same grey as the deepest past, future and past one dust. You were going somewhere, under a name. The disenchanted future forgot it, and the world, and itself — and you carry out of its grey peak the one coil it never made and so never lost, the warm whorl in your skin, toward a Waste where the killed future and the unbegun past are, at last, the same grey.
