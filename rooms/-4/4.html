Gust

A sudden short rush of wind that slams the door, lifts the hat, scatters the leaves — and is gone as fast as it came. A gust is wholly natural and cannot be held, a brief breath of the moving air. It keeps the high, dim north-west, among winds and weathers. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

A wavering, dancing, restless light,
the candle's tongue in a draughty room;
I leap and shrink and nearly die —
name the trembling flame. Pierce the gloom.

East

A brief bright flash on metal or glass,
the glint of a coin, the shine of a blade;
a single ray that catches the eye —
name the small light. Don't let it fade.

South

A gentle wind, both soft and light,
that barely stirs the kite-string's slack;
cool on the cheek of a summer day —
name the kindly wind. No lack.

West

A soft warm wind out of the west,
the gentlest breath the spring can bring;
it stirs the blossom, mild and sweet —
name the tender wind of spring.
