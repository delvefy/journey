Pink

You are the soft pale red of roses and rosebuds, of bubblegum and piglets and a baby's cheek. No hand made you; a blush of red in white makes you. Gentle, sweet, and warm - the colour of candyfloss and the first light of dawn. Mix a drop of red in a pool of white, and there I am, blushing.

North

A soft pale purple, a flower's name,
that grows down low in spring;
shy in the hedge, sweet-smelling too -
a small and modest thing.

East

The trembling shine on a moving thing,
heat-haze on the road;
the sun scattered on rippling water -
a wavering glow bestowed.

South

The warm soft ease that calms you down
when you have cried your fill;
a hug, a blanket, gentle words -
they soothe you, and you're still.

West

The quiet art of waiting on,
without a whine or fuss;
you wait your turn, you wait the bread to rise -
it asks the most of us.
