lip

A lip is one of the two soft pink edges that meet around your mouth - top lip and bottom lip, pressing shut and parting again. They shape your words, purse round a straw, pout when you sulk, and stretch wide when you grin. Bite your lip when you are nervous; lick them when they're dry; press them to a cheek for a kiss. The soft gateway of the mouth, they are the first thing a smile reaches.

North

The soft round side of your face,
it goes bright pink when you blush;
Granny pinches it, dimples hide here -
a rosy curve, all warm and lush.

East

A teeny tiny biting fly,
a cloud of us dance in the evening air;
so small you barely see me come -
one itchy bite, then I'm not there.

South

The flat wide front above your eyes,
below your hair, above your brow;
it wrinkles up when you're puzzled hard -
and burns when fever finds you now.

West

A low round bush in the garden bed,
smaller than a tree, all leafy and dense;
it grows by the path, all twiggy and green -
trim it neat by the garden fence.
