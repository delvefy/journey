The Nullity

West of the never-thought and north of the pall, the grey passes its own pole. A step east the killed mind and the mind that never was had become one indistinct grey; a step south a featureless pall lay shrouding a nothing. Here both are exceeded: this is the Chrome's over-pole, the grey gone past the dead-bright peak into a pure nullity, where there is no longer a dead mind to be grey nor a shroud to lie over anything, only the absolute — a grey that neither shines nor darkens nor thinks nor forgets, the cancelled sum of the killed future and the never-made past, nothing left even to be the absence of.

It is the most nothing of all the grey nothings, past cold, past bright, past dead, a neutral that has exceeded even neutrality. You draw the spiral and the nullity does not take it or shroud it or fail to think it; it simply does not register, being past the having of anything to register with. There is no event here and no place and no light, only the even zero in every direction. And yet the whorl in your skin turns, warm, the one quantity the nullity cannot cancel, the single thing that is in a place that has subtracted itself past nothing — the last unzeroed mark at the over-pole of the grey.

There is no count, and nothing for a count to differ from; the nullity is past the dead pole's last tick and past the Waste's old silence both, a zero that does not even hold still, because holding is something. Only your heart beats into it, the one nonzero stroke, the lone remainder where everything else has cancelled to grey, keeping a time the nullity has no value for.

To the north the nullity reaches the over-pole absolute, grey past all grey, the Chrome exceeded entirely. To the east the never-thought returns, the killed mind and the never-mind one dust. To the south the pall, a shroud still laid over the nothing. To the west it cancels wholly into the Waste, the never-anything pole, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the name is cancelled past even erasure, zeroed, valueless — and still the warm coil turns, the one unzeroed thing, and still one nonzero beat keeps on into the grey that has exceeded even nothing.
