Splash

You are a splash - the loud wet burst when something meets water all at once. Both feet in the puddle, a stone in the pond, a belly-flop in the pool, and up you leap in a scattered crown of drops with a happy smack of sound, soaking whoever stands too near. Made by no hand and gone in a blink, you are water startled into the air - here for a glittering instant, then gone, leaving only wet shoes and a giggle.
