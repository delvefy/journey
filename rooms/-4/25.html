Soot

You are soot - the fine black greasy dirt that smoke leaves behind, coating the inside of the chimney and the face of the sweep who climbs it. No hand makes you; smoke and fire do, the smudgy ghost of what has burned. You smear a finger black and never quite wash out. Father Christmas comes down through you and gets himself dirty. Black and powdery and clinging, you are the dark stain a fire leaves on everything above it, the shadow of the flame.
