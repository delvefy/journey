The Unfounded

South of the bedrock and west of the erasure, the deep goes below its own foundation. A step north was the bedrock, the absolute floor on which a world might one day be built; a step east the erasure wiped the slate so clean that even the absence was gone. Here the two undercut each other: below the bedrock there is no deeper rock, only the place the foundation rests on nothing, and the Waste so far forgotten that magic was never even a question anyone framed. This is the unfounded — the underside of the floor of the deep past, where there is neither ground to stand on nor the memory that ground was ever wanted, a nothing no one ever thought to ask of.

Nothing holds here, and nothing minds that nothing holds. The bedrock above you bears its impossible weight on this, which is not even emptiness, only the never-founded; and you hang below the foundation of everything with no rock under you and no question of rock, in a forgetting that forgot to wonder. You draw the spiral and there is no floor to set it on and no Waste to refuse it, only the unfounded taking the gesture nowhere; and still the whorl in your skin turns, the one coil that needs no ground, the single founded thing in a place that was never founded at all.

There is no count, no floor for a beat to sound against, no question of an hour ever asked. Your heart beats into the unfounded and the beat rests on nothing and asks for nothing and is not lost — one warm stroke under the foundation of the world, the only founded thing where nothing was ever laid, keeping a time the deep beneath the deep never thought to want.

To the north the unfounded firms to the bedrock, a floor at last to be beneath. To the east the erasure, the slate wiped clean of even absence. To the south it sinks toward the corner-pole, below the unfounded, where there is not even an under to be the underside of. To the west the Waste deepens toward its pole, magic forgotten past all asking, the corner. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here there is no ground for the going and no name anyone ever thought to ask — and still the warm coil turns under the floor of everything, and still one beat keeps on, founded on nothing, in the unfounded dark.
