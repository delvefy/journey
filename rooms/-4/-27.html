Cheek

You are a cheek - the soft round side of the face, below the eye and beside the nose. You go pink with cold and red with running about; you puff right out when you hold your breath or blow up a balloon. Grannies pinch you, dimples hide in you, and a goodnight kiss lands on you. There are two of you, one each side, framing a smile. Round and soft and rosy, you are the warm padded curve of a face, the comfiest place for a cuddle to land.
