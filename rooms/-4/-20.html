Jaw

You are a jaw - the strong hinged bone at the bottom of the face that works your mouth, opening and closing to bite and chew and talk. No hand made you; you grew, with your row of teeth set in it, and it is the only part of your skull that moves. You can be clenched in anger, dropped open in surprise, rubbed when it aches. A crocodile's snaps shut with a fearsome force. You are the busy hinge of eating and speaking, working away all day long.
