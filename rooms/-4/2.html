Storm

The sky turned black and furious — wind, thunder, lightning, lashing rain all at once. A storm is wholly natural and wholly beyond holding; it comes, it rages, it passes, and you shelter and wait. It keeps the wild north-west, among winds and weather. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

My gentler cousin, soft and light,
I barely lift the kite-string's slack;
no fury in me, just cool air —
a pleasant wind, and that's a fact.

East

I float up high in white or grey,
I gather thick before the rain;
a sheep, a castle, then a whale —
the wind reshapes me once again.

South

The grey half-dark of a heavy day,
the dimness deep in a shaded wood;
no lamp is lit against me yet —
a sunless mood where bright once stood.

West

A wind far stronger than a breeze,
I bend the trees and tear the sail;
not quite a storm — no thunder, rain —
just roaring air. They call me?
