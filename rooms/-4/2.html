Dew

You did not see it arrive. Some time in the cool of the night, the air gave up its water onto every blade and web, and the morning finds the whole field beaded silver. Dew belongs to no cloud and no rain - it simply gathers out of the dark air itself, hangs a moment in the early light, and is gone by noon as if it never was. Touch the grass and your hand comes away wet with it.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

I bite your nose and nip your toes,
I come with winter and with snows;
you shiver, shake, and pull on more,
and still I creep beneath the door.

East

I fall in flakes both soft and white,
and turn the whole world pale and bright;
you roll me up to make a man,
and catch me on your tongue - you can!

South

I am a wind, but soft and slight,
I stir the leaves and kites in flight;
I cool your face on summer days,
a gentle, playful, breathing haze.

West

I follow you all through the day,
but in the dark I slip away;
I have your shape but not your face,
a flat black twin in every place.
