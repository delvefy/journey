Tongue

You are a tongue - the wet pink muscle in the mouth that tastes, talks, and turns the food about for chewing. You curl to make the words, lick the ice cream, and dart out to catch a falling crumb. You taste the sweet, the sour, the salt, the bitter. Stick me out and it's rude; tie me in knots and the tongue-twister beats you. A lizard flicks one to smell the air. You are the busiest muscle in the head, never still while a mouth is awake.
