Dusk

You are dusk - the soft grey hour after the sun has slipped away but before the dark has fully come. The sky turns peach and then deep blue; the lamps blink on along the street; the birds go quiet and the bats come out. You are the day breathing out its last light, neither bright nor black but something gentle in between. You cannot be held, only watched as it deepens. You are the hush of the failing light, the world settling down to sleep.
