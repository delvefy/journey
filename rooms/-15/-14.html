Skin

You are skin - the soft outer coat that wraps the whole body from crown to toe, holding everything in and keeping the weather out. You stretch and fold, you tan and freckle, you prickle into goose-bumps when the cold creeps near. A scrape tears you and you knit yourself back. You are natural, grown not made, the largest part of a person and the one the world touches first. Every creature wears its own.
