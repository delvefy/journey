Cave

You are a cave - a hollow worn into the hillside, dark and cool the deeper you go. Water drips somewhere you cannot see, and your breath comes back to you as echo. Bats hang from your roof; a bear might sleep the winter in your mouth. The daylight reaches only a little way past your door, then gives up, and the rest of you is older than any candle.
