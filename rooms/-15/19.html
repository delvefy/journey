Crack

You are a crack - the thin jagged line that runs through the ice or the windscreen or the old plaster wall, and the sharp loud report that came when it gave. No hand needs to mean you; cold and strain and a careless knock all make you. You are the first sign a thing is breaking, the lightning-shape in the glass, the whip's sudden snap on the air. A flaw and a noise at once, you split the smooth and shatter the quiet.
