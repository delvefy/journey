Ostrich

You are an ostrich - the biggest bird in all the world, and far too heavy ever to fly. You run instead, on two long powerful legs, faster than a horse across the dusty plain. You lay the largest egg of any bird. When danger comes they say you bury your head in the sand - though truly you only lie low. A bird, but earthbound; a giant, but swift.

North

I drum upon the tree-trunk hard
with my beak, rat-a-tat;
I drill for grubs beneath the bark -
no other bird does that.

East

A tall pink bird that wades the lake,
I stand on just one leg;
I dip my curved beak upside-down -
pink from beak to peg.

South

A long-necked woolly mountain beast,
it carries packs uphill;
annoy it and it spits at you -
a ___ with a will.

West

A small brown bird that cannot fly,
with a long and poking beak;
I snuffle in the leaves at night
for worms I love to seek.
