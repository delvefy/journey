Hump

You are a hump - a rounded lump that rises up where the ground or a back should be flat. The camel carries two of you, soft with stored-up fat for the long dry road. The little bridge arches you over the stream so the boats can slip beneath. The road wears one to make the cars slow down. You are a bump grown big, a swelling in a smooth thing. You are the gentle rounded rise the body or the land lifts itself into, then settles down again.
