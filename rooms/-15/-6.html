Woodpecker

You are a woodpecker - a bright bird with a hard sharp beak, clinging to the side of a tree. Rat-a-tat-tat! you hammer at the bark all day, drilling for grubs and tapping out your drumming song. Your skull is built to take the blows; your stiff tail props you up as you climb. Red-capped and busy, you fill the quiet wood with a knocking that carries far between the trees.

North

A darting jewel above the pond,
with four see-through shining wings;
long and thin and quick as light,
round the reeds it zips and zings.

East

A plump grey bird that struts the streets
and coos upon the sill;
it pecks at crumbs in the busy square -
it eats and eats its fill.

South

The biggest bird in all the world,
too heavy ever to fly;
it runs so fast on two long legs,
its head held to the sky.

West

A little green bug in the long grass
that springs up high and far;
it rubs its legs to make a chirp -
'zree-zree' both near and far.
