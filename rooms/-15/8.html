Glimmer

You are the glimmer - a faint, weak, far-off light, the smallest shine there is. The last red ember in the grate, the first dim star before the dark goes full, the thin line under a closed door. You are barely there, almost gone, and yet you are not nothing: a glimmer of light, a glimmer of hope. In the deepest dark, even the weakest of you is enough to follow.
