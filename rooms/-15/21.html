Click

You are a click - the small sharp dry sound of two little things snapping neatly together, a light switch flicked, a pen pressed, a seatbelt buckled, a camera taking its shot. No big noise about you; one crisp tap and it is done. You mean a thing has caught, locked, started. Computers are full of you now. Small and neat and final, you are the tidy little snap of something going exactly where it should, the sound of click into place.

West
