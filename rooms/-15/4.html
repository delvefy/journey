White

You are the colour of snow and milk and clouds, of the wedding dress and the dove and the page before a word is written. No hand made you; you are all the colours at once, and none. Clean, blank, and bright. Pour every colour of light together, and out of the mix comes white.

North

The piggish want for more and more,
too much cake, too many toys;
never enough, you grab it all -
it spoils your simple joys.

East

The colour of the sun and lemons,
of chicks and buttercups;
the brightest, gladdest, sunniest shade -
that lights the daytime up.

South

When water bursts out fast and strong,
from the broken pipe or hose;
a sudden flood, a rushing spout -
out and over it goes.

West

A deep dark blue, near black at night,
the colour of the sea in storm;
the sailor's coat, the school's smart kit -
a dark blue uniform.
