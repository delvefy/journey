Snow

You are snow - the soft white flakes that drift down from a heavy winter sky and pile up deep and quiet. You hush the whole world, hiding the grass, rounding the roofs, blanketing the hills in cold clean white. A child runs out to catch you on a tongue, to roll you into a man, to sledge and slide and fall in you laughing. Made by no hand, gone with the first warm day, you turn the grey old world brand new.
