Squid

You are the ten-armed jet of the deep sea, soft-bodied and quick, with great round eyes and a beak hidden among your arms. No hand made you; the dark ocean did. Squirt your cloud of ink and vanish; pulse away backwards faster than the fish can follow. Strange, and clever, and almost all the way to invisible.

North

The flat thin blade on the fish's back
that cuts the water clean;
the shark shows one above the wave -
the scariest ever seen.

East

A long slick fish, almost a snake,
that slips through mud and weed;
slimy and quick, hard to hold -
in dark deep water freed.

South

A tiny pink curl of the sea,
with feelers, fine and thin;
smaller than your littlest finger -
a mouthful, tail and fin.

West

A rough grey shell shut tight in the sea,
that clamps its two halves close;
a pearl may grow inside its dark -
a treasure no one knows.
