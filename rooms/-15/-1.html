Fin

You are the thin stiff blade on the back of the fish, slicing the water as it swims. No hand made you; the sea shaped you for steering and balance. The shark's fin cutting the surface is the whole beach's fear. Spread wide or folded flat - without me, the fish would only roll and sink.

North

A curve the sea cuts into the land,
where boats lie safe from storm;
a half-round bite of sheltered water -
calm, and round, and warm.

East

A long stiff hair on the cat's nose,
it twitches left and right;
it feels the gaps too small to see -
a feeler in the night.

South

A sea-beast with ten waving arms,
that squirts out clouds of ink;
it jets away through the deep dark sea -
quicker than you think.

West

A stony pink reef that grows in the sea,
built by tiny living things;
bright fish dart through its branches -
a garden the warm sea brings.
