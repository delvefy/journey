Dragonfly

You are the glittering needle of the pond, four shining wings whirring as you hover and dart, all blue and green and gold. No hand made you; the still water did, where once you were a grub below. You hang in the air, then flick away too fast to follow. The whole of summer is in your wings.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A sea-thing shut in two hard shells
that clamp together tight;
it buries down in the wet sand -
shut up, and out of sight.

East

A tiny round red beetle bug
with little black dot spots;
it lands upon your finger-tip -
then flies to garden plots.

South

A bird that drums upon the tree,
'rat-a-tat-tat' all day;
it bores for bugs beneath the bark -
then knocks the hours away.

West

A green long-legged bug that leaps,
and chirps in the summer grass;
it springs away with a sudden hop -
as through the field you pass.
