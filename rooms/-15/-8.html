Llama

You are the llama - the woolly, long-necked beast of the high Andes mountains, cousin to the camel but smaller and softer. You carry packs up steep stony paths without complaint, your two-toed feet sure on the rock. Cross you, though, and you will spit. With your gentle face and shaggy coat and ears like two little bananas, you are the calm, humming worker of the thin mountain air.

North

The biggest bird of all the earth,
too heavy to take wing;
it sprints on two great legs instead -
a swift and giant thing.

East

A giant deer of the northern woods,
with antlers wide and flat;
it wades the cold lakes down to feed -
no deer is bigger than that.

South

Soft white flakes that drift from clouds
and cover all the ground;
build a man, then throw a ball -
it falls without a sound.

West

A branching bony crown of horn
upon the deer's proud head;
it sheds them every single year
and grows new ones instead.
