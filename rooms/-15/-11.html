Tongue

You are a tongue - the busy pink muscle that lives in the mouth. You taste the sweet and the sour, you shape every word the voice says, you lick the ice cream and the stamp, you poke out cheeky at a friend. You feel the gap where a tooth has gone. Soft and quick and never still, grown of the living mouth, you are the clever helper that does the tasting and the talking and the licking, all day long.
