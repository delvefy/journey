Shrimp

You are the tiny pink curl of the sea, finer than your littlest finger, all feelers and legs and a flick of a tail. No hand made you; the shallows did. Netted by the bucketful, you turn from grey to pink in the boiling pot. So small, your name means anything little - but a whole shoal of you feeds a town.

North

A sea-beast with ten waving arms,
that squirts out clouds of ink;
it jets away through the deep dark sea -
quicker than you think.

East

A sea bug, red when cooked, dark when not,
with two great snapping claws;
it scuttles on the ocean floor -
and grabs with its strong jaws.

South

A sea-thing shut in two hard shells
that clamp together tight;
it buries down in the wet sand -
shut up, and out of sight.

West

A blue-black shell shut tight in twos,
that clings to the harbour wall;
you steam them open, eat them up -
small shellfish, one and all.
