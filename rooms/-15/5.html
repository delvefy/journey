Greed

You are the never-enough want for more - more cake, more toys, more gold, the grabbing hand that cannot stop. No hand makes you; the hungry heart does, the eye bigger than the belly. You spoil the having by always wanting the next. The dragon on his hoard, the miser at his chest - both eaten alive by me.

North

When something might go wrong ahead,
it nags inside your head;
a heavy fear of what's to come -
you lie awake in bed.

East

The heavy bad feeling deep inside
when you've done a thing that's wrong;
you broke the vase and hid the bits -
it weighs on you all day long.

South

The colour of snow and milk and clouds,
of a wedding dress and a dove;
no colour at all, and all at once -
the purest, up above.

West

A quick hot flash of crossness,
you 'lose' it in a flash;
you stamp and shout and slam the door -
then calm comes after the clash.
