Fuss

You are a fuss - all the bother and noise made over something small. A toddler kicks up a fuss at bedtime; the cat makes a fuss to be fed; grown-ups fuss with the cushions before the guests arrive. You are flapping and flutter and far too much ado, a storm in a teacup that blows itself out. Nobody can hold you in a hand, yet a whole room can fill with you in a second.
