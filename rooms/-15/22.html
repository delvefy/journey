Ding

You are a ding - one bright clear ring of a little bell, here and gone. The lift announces its floor with you; the shop counter bell goes off with you when a hand smacks the top; the timer says the cakes are done with you. You are smaller than a clang and shorter than a chime, just a single silvery note that pings out and fades. Cheerful and tidy and quick, you are the friendliest little sound there is, a polite tap on the ear that says: attention please, something is ready.
