Boom

You are a boom - the deep loud rolling sound you feel in your chest as much as hear. The big drum makes you; the firework makes you high in the night; thunder makes you rolling across the hills. You are lower and rounder than a bang, a great hollow blast that shakes the windows and rumbles away slowly. Dogs hide from you; small children clap their hands over their ears. You are the biggest, deepest noise there is, a sound with weight behind it.
