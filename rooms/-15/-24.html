sapphire

A sapphire is a precious stone of the deepest blue - the blue of a midnight sky, of the sea in a story, glinting with cold bright fire when the light catches it. It is one of the hardest stones there is, second only to the diamond, and kings and queens have prized it for their crowns and rings for thousands of years. Hold one up to the light and you seem to look right down into a tiny, endless, blue evening sky.

A precious stone of deepest red,
it sparkles on a royal ring;
red as blood and hard as glass -
a treasured, glinting, costly thing.

deep as a forest, bright and clear;
set in a crown or a golden ring -

cut to flash with a thousand lights;
set in a crown or a treasure chest -
a glinting prize of pirate nights.

I'm tree-sap turned to stone with time;
