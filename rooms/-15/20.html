Snap

You are a snap - the quick sharp sound of something brittle giving way all at once, the dry twig in two, the biscuit broken, the fingers clicked. No long groan, no slow bend - you happen in a single instant and it is done. You are the noise of a clean break, crisp and final. A turtle's jaw, a mousetrap, an elastic band stretched too far all make you. Sudden and sharp and over before it's begun, you are breaking turned into one small loud word.
