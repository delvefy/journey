Dream

You are a dream - the strange bright story the mind tells itself once the eyes are shut. You let a child fly over rooftops, talk to a dog, swim down a staircase, meet someone long gone. You can be sweet as honey or scary enough to wake them with a gasp, and by breakfast you are mostly gone, leaving just a shred. No hand can hold you, no one else can see you - you are a whole secret world that lasts only as long as the sleeping does.
