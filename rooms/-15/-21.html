Crystal

You are a crystal - a clear hard stone grown slowly in the dark, with flat shiny faces and sharp points, that catches the light and splits it into rainbows. No hand makes you; minerals and time and patient water do, deep in a cave. You hang in windows to throw colours on the wall and stand in clusters on a shelf. Clear and glittering and many-sided, you are stone turned to glass, the earth's own jewel grown one shining face at a time.
