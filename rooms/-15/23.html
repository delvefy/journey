Chime

You are a chime - the sweet tuneful ringing of bells sounded one after another, a little tune all of their own. The grandfather clock chimes the hour, the doorbell chimes a few bright notes, and wind-chimes hung on the porch chime softly whenever the breeze stirs them. Clear and musical and gently announcing, you are the prettiest sound a bell can make, not one note but a whole bright phrase of them, ringing out and slowly fading on the air.
