Fossil

You are a fossil - the stony shape of a creature that lived millions of years ago, pressed deep into the rock and turned to stone itself. A curled ammonite, a fern leaf, a great dinosaur's bone: all of them you. Buried for ages, you wait until a cliff crumbles or a hammer taps, and out you come into the light at last. Ancient and hard and full of secrets, you are a message from the deep past, a window onto creatures no living eye has ever seen.
