Ruby

You are a ruby - a precious gemstone of the deepest glowing red, like a frozen drop of fire. Cut and polished, you flash and sparkle in a ring or a crown, and you are so rare and hard that you are worth a fortune. Kings and queens prize you above almost any jewel. Red and brilliant and dazzlingly precious, you are a tiny stone with the colour of a heart and the worth of a treasure, set glittering in gold for all to gasp at.
