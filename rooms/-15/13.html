Sense

You are a sense - one of the five ways the body learns about the world: sight, hearing, smell, taste, and touch. No hand made you; you grew with the eyes and ears and skin, and you are how all of the outside gets in. You cannot be touched, only used. Sense is also when a thing is clear and right - it makes sense, you say, or use your common sense. You are the body's open windows, and the cleverness that knows what to make of all that comes through.
