Boulder

You are a boulder - a great grey lump of rock, far too big and heavy for any push to shift, that has sat in the same field or stream since long before the farm. No hand set you here; an old flood or a slow glacier rolled you and left you. Moss climbs your shady side and children scramble your sunny one. You are stubbornness made of stone, a giant marble dropped by the world and never picked back up.
