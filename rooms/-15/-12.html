Belly

You are a belly - the soft round front of you, below the chest, where the dinner goes and the laugh comes from. No hand made you; you grew, and you swell when you are full and rumble when you are empty. You can be patted, tickled, flopped upon, filled with cake. A baby grows in one, a happy man wears a round one, and a good belly-laugh shakes the whole of it. You are the cosy middle of the body, warm and round and honest.
