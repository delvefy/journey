Puff

You are a puff - a small soft burst of air or smoke, here and gone in a breath. The dragon lets out a puff of smoke; the runner climbs the hill in puffs; you blow out the birthday candles in one big puff. You are gentler than a gust and smaller than a gale, a single quick breath of moving air. A powder-puff, a puff of wind, a puff of the train's white steam - you are the world breathing out in tiny, soft amounts.
