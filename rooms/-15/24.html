jingle

A jingle is the bright little ringing sound that small things make knocking together - sleigh bells on a frosty night, coins shaken in a pocket, the keys on a janitor's ring. It is lighter than a clang and merrier than a chime, a quick silvery shiver of sound. Adverts have catchy ones you cannot get out of your head. Wherever something small and metal dances, there it is: jingle, jingle, jingle, all the way.

North

The soft quick tap of rain on the roof,
or little feet running down the hall;
pitter-patter, light and fast -
a gentle drumming, not a fall.

East

Not happy at all, all grumpy and mad,
arms folded tight and a deep dark frown;
told off, or your block tower fell -
a stroppy mood that gets you down.

South

A clear sweet ring, a tinkling note,
the doorbell sings, the clock marks time;
wind-bells dance and ding in the breeze -
the bell-sound's name, it rhymes with time.

West

Wet brown earth all soft and squishy,
after the rain the garden's full;
pigs love to roll, your boots get stuck -
squelch through me, it's wonderful.
