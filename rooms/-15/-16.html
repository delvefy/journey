Hill

You are a hill - the gentle swelling of the land, a green rise lower than a mountain and rounder of shoulder. You take the puff out of the legs that climb you and give the whole valley back as a view from the top. Sheep graze your slopes; children roll down them in summer and sledge down them in snow. The grand old Duke of York marched his men up one and down again. You are the earth lifting itself, just a little, to see further.
