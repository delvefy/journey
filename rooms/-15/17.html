Crackle

You are crackle - the dry, snapping song a fire sings as the twigs catch. You are the small sharp pops of sparks, the noise of autumn leaves crushed underfoot, the sound a sweet wrapper makes in a quiet room. You are not loud, but you are everywhere at once, tiny and busy. Listen close to a bonfire and that quick stitching of sound, that is you, talking to yourself in the flames.
