scowl

A scowl is the face of someone properly cross - eyebrows dragged down hard, mouth set in a grim line, the whole face dark as a thundercloud. It says go away and leave me alone without a single word. You scowl when you are sulking, when you have been told off, when the rain spoils the trip. Hold it too long, the grown-ups warn, and your face will stick that way. It is anger, worn right out in the open.

North

A quick scrunch of your face in pain,
when the needle stings or you stub your toe;
your eyes screw up, your mouth pulls tight -
a flinch of the face that says 'oh, ow, no!'

East

A hard cross stare with narrowed eyes,
you fix it on the one you're mad at;
no words, just a long fierce look -
it says 'how dare you do that!'

South

A small weak cry, both soft and sad,
a puppy shut behind a door;
not a wail, just a tiny whine -
too sad to cry out more.

West

A sly small smile, a bit too pleased,
one corner of your mouth turned up;
you know a secret, you got your way -
smug as the cat at the cream cup.
