wince

A wince is the quick little scrunch your face makes when something hurts or you dread it coming - the jab of a splinter, the screech of nails on a board, the sight of someone else's nasty fall. Your eyes screw up, your mouth pulls tight, your shoulders hitch for half a second, and then it passes. You did not choose to do it; the face just does it for you. It is pain and dread, written for a moment plain across your face.

North

Hold out your skirt and dip down low,
bend your knees in a polite hello;
a girl's way to greet the queen -
a graceful little bob, just so.

East

Mouth wide open, eyes gone round,
you stare and stare at the wondrous sight;
too astonished to say a word -
you gawp, jaw dropped in pure delight.

South

Eyebrows down and mouth gone grim,
a dark cross face that says 'keep away';
the look you give when you're really mad -
a thundercloud face all day.

West

A great big happy grin so wide
it lights your whole face up with glee;
when you're proud and pleased as punch -
a sunny smile for all to see.
