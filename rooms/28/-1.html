felt

Felt is cloth made the simplest way of all - no weaving, no thread, just wool pressed and rubbed and squashed until it mats into one thick soft sheet. It does not fray when you cut it, so it is the friend of every craft table: stick it, snip it, glue it into shapes. It comes in every colour, soft as a cat's ear, and the tip of your bright pen is made of it too. Warm, fuzzy, and easy, it asks for no needle at all.

North

Loop and pull a rope so tight
it grips itself and won't let go;
it ties your shoes, it holds the boat -
a tangle you can't undo, you know.

East

A long thin strip of shiny cloth,
tie me in your hair in a bow;
I wrap the present, deck the prize -
snip me, and the fair can go.

South

A fat bright pen of yellow or pink,
I swipe across the words you read;
I leave a glowing see-through line -
the bits to remember, marked indeed.

West

Tough blue cloth that jeans are made of,
strong and stiff, it never tears;
cowboys wear me, so do you -
the hard-wearing cloth that everyone wears.
