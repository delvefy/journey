whimper

A whimper is the tiniest cry there is - softer than a sob, quieter than a wail, the small weak sound a puppy makes shut behind a door, or a child too tired and sad to cry out loud. It barely breaks the quiet. You make it when you are hurt but trying to be brave, when the tears want to come but stay just behind your eyes. It is sorrow turned right down low, almost too small to hear.

North

Pull your eyebrows down and glare,
a cross hard face that says 'go away';
a deep dark frown of pure bad mood -
the look you give on a grumpy day.

East

A long high complaining cry,
'I'm boooored, it's not faaaair,' you moan;
a dog does it at the kitchen door -
a thin sad nag in a needy tone.

South

Take a deep breath and leap right in,
down through the cold deep water you go;
no toe dipped first, just all at once -
a steep fast drop to the dark below.

West

A low deep sound of 'ohhh, not again,'
when your tummy aches or the homework's hard;
the floorboards do it, the old gate too -
a long sad rumble, weary and jarred.
