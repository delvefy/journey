holepunch

A holepunch is the heavy little tool on the desk that bites two neat round holes in a sheet of paper. Line your pages up, press down hard with a satisfying clunk, and out drop two perfect paper circles into the little tray below - and now your sheets will slot onto the rings of a binder. Empty the tray and a shower of tiny paper dots comes out, brilliant for confetti. Half tool, half stamp, it makes the holes that hold a folder together.

North

A flat clear half-circle, marked with lines
from zero up to a hundred and eighty;
lay me on a corner you've drawn -
I'll tell you the angle, neat and weighty.

East

A stiff flat board with a clip on top,
snap your papers under, held tight;
a doctor, a coach, a score-keeper holds me -
write while standing, grip it right.

South

A thin clear sheet that clings and sticks,
wrap it tight round the leftover food;
it stretches and crinkles and sticks to itself -
keeps the sandwich fresh and good.

West

Press the little numbered buttons,
it does your sums in a flash;
add and times and take away -
the answer pops up, quick as a dash.
