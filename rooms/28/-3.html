protractor

A protractor is a flat half-circle of clear plastic, its curved edge marked with little lines from zero round to a hundred and eighty. It lives in the maths set and comes out for angles: lay its straight edge along one line, read off where the other crosses, and you know exactly how wide the corner is. Right angle, sharp angle, wide angle - it measures them all. A small clear ruler bent into a rainbow, made for the geometry of corners.

North

A fat bright pen of yellow or pink,
I swipe a glowing line on the page;
the words to remember, lit up bright -
the helper of the studying sage.

East

A little pad of paper sheets,
tear one off to scribble a note;
'buy milk, call Gran' upon the top -
a stack of pages, neatly wrote.

South

Press me down with a heavy clunk -
two round holes in your sheet appear;
then they slot in a binder's rings -
I bite the paper, never fear.

West

A zip-up bag for all your pens,
pencils, rubber, ruler too;
unzip me on your school desk -
your bits and bobs all come in view.
