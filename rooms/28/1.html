plunge

To plunge is to go down all at once, steep and headlong - off the high board into the cold of the pool, down the first drop of the rollercoaster, deep into the dark water with a great white splash. There is no easing in, no toe dipped first; you commit and you fall, and your stomach leaps up while the rest of you goes down. It is the brave, breathless drop, all in one go.

North

A small sad sound a puppy makes
when it is hurt or left alone;
not a wail, just a tiny cry -
a soft weak whine, a quiet moan.

East

Let go of a thing and down it falls,
out of your hand and onto the ground;
a clumsy oops, the cup goes smash -
one slip, and it tumbles down.

South

Loop the rope and pull it tight,
now it will not come undone;
it ties your shoe and holds the boat -
a tangle drawn to one.

West

Head over heels you roll and fall,
down the grassy hill you go;
a clown does it on purpose - flop! -
over and over, head to toe.
