highlighter

A highlighter is a fat felt-tipped pen filled with see-through colour - yellow, pink, green - made for one job: to swipe a glowing stripe across the words that matter. Run it over a line in your book and the words shine out, easy to find again. It does not hide what is underneath; it lifts it up, marks it, says remember this. Pop the lid back on or it dries out by morning. The favourite friend of anyone studying hard.

North

A thick soft cloth with no weave at all,
made of wool all squashed and pressed;
snip me into shapes for crafts -
no fraying edge, the glue likes me best.

East

Two legs, one sharp, one holds a pen,
spin me round on my pointy toe;
I draw a perfect ring on the page -
a circle, round I go.

South

A flat half-circle, marked with lines
from zero up to a hundred and eighty;
I measure the angles and corners you draw -
in your maths set, neat and weighty.

West

A little loop of bent shiny wire,
I pinch your pages so they stay;
slide me on a stack of sheets -
I hold them tight, then slip away.
