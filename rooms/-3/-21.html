Clover

You are clover - the small soft plant that creeps across the lawn in a carpet of three round leaves. The bees love your little pink-white flowers and bumble all over you on a warm afternoon. Children kneel and hunt you over, leaf by leaf, hoping to spot the rare fourth that means good luck. You are humble and everywhere, trodden and mown and growing right back. You are the lucky green carpet of the field, three leaves to a stem and sometimes four.
