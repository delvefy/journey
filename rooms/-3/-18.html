Thorn

You are a thorn - a sharp little spike that guards a stem. You grow in rows along the rose and the bramble, daring careless fingers to grab; prick one and a bright bead of blood wells up. You hold the climbing rose fast to the wall and keep the hungry rabbit off. Grown by no hand, small and fierce, you are how a soft green plant says, very quietly and very clearly, please do not pick me without care.
