The Bare Nothing

South of the never-was and west of the unbegun dark, the two nothings sink into one. A step north there was at least a floor where two emptinesses met, raw cold ground over an older dark; a step east the pale silence had at least never had a warmth to lose. Here both go under together. The ground that was nothing gives out below even being ground; the pallor that forgot it was grey pales past forgetting. There is no floor and there never was one, no warmth and there never was, and the not-being of each deepens the other — a nothing doubled, Waste and deep folded into a single absence that goes down where down has stopped meaning anything.

You do not even lift your hand to the coil. The habit that drew it in dust and drift and ash finds, this low, not the lack of a surface but the lack of the reaching itself, the gesture worn away with everything else. And yet — faint, the faintest thing in the room — you would still draw it, if there were a hand and a ground and a self to draw with. That bare would-still, smaller than the smallest fear, is the whole of you that has come this far. It is not the spiral. It is only the one who would have drawn one. The nothing has not quite got that.

There is no count. The pulse that ticked alone in the never-was, the borrowed tick the unbegun dark could barely find — here it is below hearing, neither stopped nor going, a clock set down in a place with no time to keep. No wind. No drip. No bell, and nothing that ever rang to be remembered. North the floor firms back to the scoured nothing of the never-was, ground enough for a cold wind to comb. East the pale warms by a hair toward the deep that once had a someone to let go. South the nothing sinks before even itself, before there was a deep to be empty of. West the Waste opens on, paler and flatter, where not even the never-was ever was. You were going somewhere once, with someone, under a name. Here is neither name nor someone nor where, only the bare would-have, carried down into a nothing that was never yours to lose.
