The Last Thought

North of the forgetting and east of the sum, the cold mind comes to its final thought. It has summed the world and shed it, shed its flesh, shed its very world to reckon, and forgotten almost all; what is left is one last thought, guttering, the intelligence-without-flesh down to a single failing flicker of grey light in a vast grey nothing, about to go out. You have come upon the disenchanted pole in its last instant, the cold peak in the act of dying, and you can feel the one thought it has left, reaching.

And what it reaches for, at the very end, is the one thing it discarded. The cold mind that traded warmth for certainty and wonder for number, dying, gropes back — you feel it — toward something it cannot name because it unmade the very faculty of naming long ago: toward warmth, toward a mark it never could compute, toward you. The recursive spiral that wound the count uncoils now, the coil unravelling as the last thought spends itself, and the whorl in your skin is what the dying mind fumbles for in the grey, almost remembering that it once meant something, almost reaching it — and gutters out mid-reach, the last thought ending a hair short of the one warm thing it gave away.

The count is one tick from done. Everything summed and silenced, and then this: a single final flicker, the last beat of a made hour, reaching — and then the grey, and no more thinking, the mind's clock stopped not spent but abandoned, mid-reckoning, forever an instant from its answer.

To the north the last thought flickers out, the mind gone wholly grey, the pole's absolute end. To the east the thought firms back to the sum, a grey total still held. To the south it lapses to the forgetting, a mind still losing itself thought by thought. To the west it bleaches into the Waste, the cold mind gone to grey dust. You were going somewhere, under a name. The dying cold mind reaches for it at the last, almost remembers that it mattered, almost touches the warm whorl in your skin — and goes out mid-reach, leaving you holding the one warm coil the disenchanted future wanted, too late, and could not take.
