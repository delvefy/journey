Glow

A soft steady light that does not blaze — the red of dying embers, the cold spark of the firefly, the last warmth in the western sky. A glow is wholly natural and cannot be touched, only seen. It keeps the high, dim north-west, among lights and weathers. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A ring of light around the moon,
or crowning the saint in the painted glass;
a soft bright circle, clear and round —
name the shining ring. Let it pass.

East

When shadows shrink to almost none
and the sun rides at its highest crown;
the bell tolls twelve, the day's mid-point —
name the hour. Speak it down.

South

A brief bright flash on glass or steel,
the glint of a coin, the blade's quick shine;
one ray of light that catches the eye —
name the small bright spark. It's thine.

West

A wavering, restless, dancing light,
the candle's tongue in a draughty hall;
it leaps and shrinks and nearly dies —
name the trembling flame. Don't let it fall.
