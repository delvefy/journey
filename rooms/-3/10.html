Hope

You are the quiet light that says things will get better, the lean toward a good that has not come. No hand makes you; you rise on your own in the dark, faint but stubborn, when there is little reason for you. You keep the sick getting up, the lost still walking, the waiting still watching the road. The smallest of feelings, and the last to die, you are the candle the heart keeps burning against the night - not certainty, only the warm belief that morning will come.

North

The chance of fortune, good or bad,
a four-leaf clover brings;
cross your fingers, touch the wood -
it's what a horseshoe sings.

East

A low soft shine on a polished thing,
a glint of caught light;
not a flash and not a blaze,
but a quiet shining, slight.

South

The flicker of a far-off star,
its small light winking on
and off across the night-time sky -
now there, now nearly gone.

West

The deepest warmest feeling there is,
for family, friend, or pet;
it gives, it cares, it never stops -
the strongest feeling yet.
