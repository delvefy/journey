The Unbegun

This is where it has all not yet happened. North of here a wind at least crossed the open ground; east of here the cold at least had a grey to settle into; here there is neither wind nor grey, only raw cold earth and, beneath it, the older dark the quiet sank toward — the deep before the first wind, before the first warmth, before there was a thing to be made or lost or even forgotten. Nothing answers, the way nothing answers all through the Waste. But here it is not that the answering has stopped. It is that it never began.

You stand on ground that has never been stood on, in a dark that has never been entered, and your weight and your warmth are the first event the place has ever had — too small to count, refused nothing, because nothing here knows how to refuse. You go to draw the spiral and your finger finds no surface that will keep it: the raw cold takes the line and closes it the way deep water closes, not erasing, never having held. The only coil left is the one whorled into your own skin, carried down from a warmth this deep has no notion of, and even that feels far off, as though your hand belonged to a later world that had reached too far back.

There is no count. Your heart still beats, faintly, and the unbegun does not take it up or echo it or spend it; it simply does not hear, never having learned that there is such a thing as time to keep. You listen for the bell, the drip, the herd, the breath — for anything that has ever marked an hour. There has never once been an hour here to mark.

To the north the raw earth firms to open ground and a wind crosses it, the long quiet where the draught is at least born. To the east the cold finds a grey to settle as, the silt and, past it, a body that once cooled — leavings that were at least once something. To the south the deep sinks past even raw cold, before the first dark had a before, the floor beneath the floor. To the west the Waste widens past the last of the ash, the void where not even nothing has settled. You were going somewhere, once — you carry that the way you carry the spiral, in the skin, from elsewhere. Here, where nothing ever began, it is the one thing that has.
