Bumblebee

You are a bumblebee - a fat round ball of black-and-yellow fuzz that bumbles drowsily from flower to flower, far too plump, it seems, to fly at all, yet fly you do. No hand makes you; the warm meadow does. You gather pollen on your furry legs and hum a low contented hum. You bump softly against the window and have to be helped back out. Gentle and clumsy and beloved, you are the friendliest bee there is, the bumbling, bustling heart of summer.
