Wasp

You are a wasp - a narrow nip of yellow and black that no hand made, droning angry circles round the jam. You are all temper and a sting in the tail, and unlike the busy bee you can sting again and again. Picnics dread you; you blunder in at the open window and will not be shooed. A small armoured spite on wings, you mean business, and everyone leaves the table when you arrive.
