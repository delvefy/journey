Stem

You are a stem - the slim green stalk that holds a flower or a leaf up off the ground and into the light. No hand made you; the plant grew you, hollow and strong, a tiny pipe carrying water up from the roots to the bloom. You can be snapped, bent, picked with the flower on top. The thorns grow along you, the leaves branch off you, and the whole flower leans on you to stand. You are the plant's own slender spine.
