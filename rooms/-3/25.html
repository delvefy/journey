Meteor

You are a meteor - a bright quick streak that scratches across the night sky and is gone, a little rock from space burning itself up as it falls. No hand makes you; the dark between the worlds drops you, and the air sets you alight. People call you a shooting star and wish on you before you fade. On a clear August night you come in showers. Brief and brilliant and gone in a blink, you are a piece of the heavens, briefly on fire, briefly falling toward the earth.
