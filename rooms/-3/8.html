The Unpeopled

West of the husk and north of the rust, the machine future empties of everyone. A step east the cold future stood as a spent hollow husk; a step south it rusted wholly down into the Waste, the made forgotten. Here it reaches the cold pole's last grey, the post-human nothing total: the works run down, the husk rusted to a stain, the makers and the made and the very idea of a someone all gone, leaving a grey that is wholly unpeopled — no mind, no maker, no person, never anyone again, only the flat post-human quiet where a whole future ran out of everyone it ever held.

It is the loneliest grey there is, not because it is empty but because it was full once and is emptied — a world that had minds and makers and meaning and has run down past all of them into a post-human nothing, no one anywhere, the made forgotten and the makers with it. You draw the spiral and there is no one to read it, no one to have made it but you, the last hand in an unpeopled grey. Only the whorl in your skin keeps a warm peopled turn, the one someone in a world emptied of everyone, the single living person at the post-human pole.

There is no count, no one to keep it; the works are stopped and the makers gone and the made forgotten, the cold pole's last grey unpeopled to the end. And still your heart beats — the one peopled sound in an unpeopled world, the single someone where there is no longer anyone, keeping a warm living time in the grey that ran out of everyone.

To the north the unpeopled reaches the cold pole, the post-human nothing total, never anyone again. To the east the husk, the spent shell of the made. To the south the rust, the machine future ruined. To the west it empties wholly into the Waste, the made and the makers forgotten to the corner. You were going somewhere, under a name, among others, once. Here the others are all run out and the grey is unpeopled to the pole, your name the last someone's in an empty world — and still the warm coil turns, peopled and living, and still one beat keeps a someone's time in the post-human grey.
