Beetle

You are a beetle - the little bug that wears its wings under a hard shiny shell, split neatly down the back. You come in every colour: black, brown, the gleaming green of a jewel. You trundle slowly over the soil, climb a stem, then surprise everyone by cracking your case open and whirring clumsily into the air. There are more of your kind than almost any other creature. You are an armoured pebble that walks, a small living bead in a polished coat.
