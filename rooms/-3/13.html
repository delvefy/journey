Flicker

You are a wavering, unsteady light, never quite still - the candle's dance in a draught, the dying fire, the old lamp on its last legs, the firefly's blink. No hand makes you; light caught in a stir of air, or fading away, gives you, now bright, now nearly out. You jump and shudder and almost go.

North

The soft trembling shine of sun on the sea,
of silk, of a heat-hazed road;
the light seems to ripple and dance and move -
a wavering glow bestowed.

East

The rushing sound of something fast -
a rocket, a slide, a gust;
'WHOOSH!' it goes as it flies right by -
a sweep of air and dust.

South

A loud rude jumble of sound,
all clatter, bang, and din;
the building site, the crashing pots -
shut the door, keep it in.

West

A hug when you are hurt or sad,
a soft warm blanket, a cup of tea;
the gentle easing of your tears -
'there, there,' it says to thee.
