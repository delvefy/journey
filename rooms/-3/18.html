Glow

You are a glow - a soft warm steady light that does not flash or blaze. You are the orange heart of the dying fire, the lamp left low at bedtime, the moon behind a thin cloud, the green shine of a glow-worm in the hedge. A happy face has you too. No hand can hold you - you are gentle light at rest, the warm low shine that says all is calm, the day is done, and it is safe to fall asleep.
