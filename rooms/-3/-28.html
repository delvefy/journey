gnat

A gnat is one of those teeny-tiny flies that gather in little dancing clouds on a warm evening, right where you want to walk. You barely see one on its own - just a speck - but a whole swarm of them hums about your head and gets in your eyes and your tea. Some kinds bite, leaving a small hot itch. Small and annoying and everywhere at dusk, the gnat is summer's littlest pest.

North

A tiny bug that hops and bites,
it lives in the fur of a cat or dog;
scratch, scratch, it makes them itch -
a leaping speck, quick as a frog.

East

First through the cold late-winter ground,
a little cup of purple or gold;
I poke up brave through melting snow -
spring is coming, or so I'm told.

South

A long soft crawler with many legs,
munching leaves all day, so greedy;
one day I'll wrap up tight and sleep -
a butterfly comes out, all ready.

West

Two soft pink edges round your mouth,
I press them shut, I let them part;
I kiss, I pout, I hold a straw -
the rim of the mouth, where smiles start.
