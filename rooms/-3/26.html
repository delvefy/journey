Asteroid

You are an asteroid - a great tumbling lump of rock that drifts through space, going round and round the sun the way the planets do, but far smaller and oddly shaped. Thousands of you circle together in a wide belt between Mars and Jupiter, bumping and spinning in the cold dark. No air, no water, no life: just rock and dust and the long silent journey. Lonely and ancient, you are a leftover piece of the early days of the sky, still wandering its endless loop.
