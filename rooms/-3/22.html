Moonlight

You are moonlight - the cool silver shine that falls when the moon is up and the sun long gone. You silver the rooftops and lay a glittering road across the dark water. You are gentle and pale, never warm, the borrowed light of the sleeping half of the day. By you the cat prowls and the nightbird calls. Switch off every lamp and you alone keep the night from being wholly black. You are the soft glow the moon hands down to the dark.
