Sky

You are the sky - the great open dome above every head, with no roof and no walls. By day you are blue and full of drifting clouds and crossing birds; at evening you bruise into pink and gold; by night you pull back to show the deep dark and its scatter of stars. Rain and snow fall out of you; the sun and moon ride across you. You cannot be touched or reached, only looked up into. You are the whole vast lid of the world.
