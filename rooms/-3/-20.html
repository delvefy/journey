Berry

You are a berry - a small soft juicy fruit, no bigger than the tip of a finger, growing in bunches in the hedgerow and the garden: strawberry, blueberry, blackberry, raspberry. No hand made you; the plant did, wrapping its seeds in sweetness so the birds will spread them far. You can be picked, popped, baked, jammed, or squashed to stain your fingers purple. Some are sweet, some are sour, and a few are not to be eaten at all. You are a tiny mouthful of summer.
