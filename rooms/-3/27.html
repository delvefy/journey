Eclipse

You are an eclipse - the rare and eerie moment when the moon slides right across the face of the sun, and in the middle of the day the world goes dim and cool. Birds fall silent, the sky darkens, and a black disc rimmed with a ring of fire hangs where the sun should be. People travel across the world to stand in your shadow. Strange and awesome and over in minutes, you are the sky's greatest trick, the day briefly turned to dusk while everyone gasps and stares.
