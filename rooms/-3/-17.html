Vine

You are a vine - a long green climber that will not stand alone. You twist and curl and grip with little tendrils, hauling yourself up the wall, the pole, the tree, reaching always for the light. Broad leaves hang from you, and heavy bunches of grapes, and you sprawl wherever you are let. Grown by no hand, restless and reaching, you are a plant that travels, roping the whole garden together as you climb.
