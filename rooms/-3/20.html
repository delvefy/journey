Shadow

You are a shadow - the dark shape a thing throws on the ground when it stands between the light and the floor, a flat copy of you that the sun draws in black. No hand makes you; light does, by being blocked. You cannot be touched or caught - step on it and it slides away under your foot. You shrink at noon, stretch long at evening, and vanish in the dark. You are the cool dark twin that follows everyone everywhere the sun shines.
