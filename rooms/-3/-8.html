The Older Dark

South of the dark before and west of the last floor, there is always an older dark. A step north the body was given back to the dark before the first making, the oldest dark of all; a step east the cold descent reached its last floor, the warmth gone to never-was. Here the oldest dark proves not to be oldest: beneath the dark before lies the dark before that, and beneath it another, each older than the last — an endless regress of before, the primal origin receding forever, never once reaching a first, sinking toward the corner where the always-older dark and the never-was become one. There is no first dark. There is only ever an older one.

It is the bottomless before, where every floor of the primal dark has a deeper floor beneath it and every oldest has an older — the origin forever one step further down, never arrived at, receding as you sink. You draw the spiral and the older dark neither keeps it nor takes it; there is no first dark for it to be the first mark in, only an ever-older one. And still the whorl in your skin turns, the one thing that does not recede, the single fixed mark in a dark that is always older than itself, refusing the bottomless before.

There is no count and no first beat to count from; behind every oldest beat is an older silence, behind every origin an older dark. And still your heart beats — the one thing that does not recede into the before, a fixed warm stroke in a dark forever older than itself, refusing the regress, insisting on a now where the older dark allows only ever-before.

To the north the older dark rises to the dark before, a dark that thinks itself the oldest. To the east the last floor, the cold-primal gone to never-was. To the south the corner-pole, the dark before the dark before the dark before, the regress sinking to the Waste's bottom. To the west the warmth leaches wholly to the Waste, the older dark gone to never-was, the corner. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the body, before the making, before the before. Here there is only ever an older before, and no first to reach — and still the warm coil turns, unreceding, and still one beat keeps a now in the dark that is always older than itself.
