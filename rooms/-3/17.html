Frost

You are frost - the white breath of a cold clear night, crept silent over everything while the world slept. You fur the grass in silver, you trace ferns on the window glass, you stiffen the puddle to a pane and crunch beneath the boot. No one sees you arrive; you are simply there at dawn, sparkling and cold. Made by no hand, gone with the first warm sun, you are winter's quiet handwriting on the morning.
