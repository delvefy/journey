The Rust

West of the running down and north of the never-thought, the machine future rusts wholly down. A step east the great works and the great mind were winding to a stop together, slowing toward the cold pole; a step south the killed mind and the never-mind had become one indistinct grey. Here the winding-down completes: the works has run all the way down and corroded blind, a vast rusted ruin of mindless machinery sinking into the Waste, and the cold mind that drove it rusted with it to a post-human nothing. The made future is a wreck now, mindless and rusting and forgotten, dissolving grain by grain into the grey.

Everything here is rust — the blind works seized and crumbling, the dead-bright lamps corroded dark, the whole machine-age oxidised down to a brown-grey ruin that the Waste is patiently eating. You draw the spiral and find it already cut into a rusted plate, a maker's seal flaking away, the made forgotten as it dissolves. Only the whorl in your skin keeps a whole un-rusted coil, the one mark the corrosion cannot reach, the single living turn in a ruin run wholly down to rust and post-human nothing.

There is no count, only the slow chemistry of rust, the works long since stopped, the bell seized and crumbling, the made hour oxidised to a stain. Your heart beats among the ruin, the one warm stroke the rust never reached and the cold mind never kept, keeping a time the dead machinery has forgotten it ever ran.

To the north the rust firms toward the cold pole's last grey, the machine future wholly run down, the post-human nothing total. To the east the running-down, the works and the mind still winding to their stop. To the south the never-thought, the killed mind and the never-mind one dust. To the west the ruin dissolves wholly into the Waste, the machinery rusted past trace, the made forgotten to the pole. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold works stamped it into rusting steel. Here the steel has rusted blind and the mind rusted to nothing and your name flakes away with the ruin — and still the warm coil turns, un-rusted, and still one beat keeps on among the wreck of the future.
