Horse

You are a big strong four-legged beast with a flowing mane and tail, made for running and for carrying. No hand makes you; you were born on the farm or the wide plain, and you gallop faster than a man can run. People ride on your back, harness you to carts, and feed you hay and oats. You neigh, you snort, you stamp your hoof.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A horned beast that climbs the rocks
and chews on almost all;
it gives us milk, it bleats and butts -
it nibbles tin cans small.

East

A cup of colour on a stem
that opens up in spring;
red and yellow, smooth and bright -
from a bulb it rises in.

South

A little horse, both small and stout,
with short strong legs and a shaggy mane;
just right for a child to learn to ride -
it trots along the lane.

West

A small round fruit, bright shiny red,
that grows in twos on a stalk;
a hard stone hides inside the sweet -
you spit it as you walk.
