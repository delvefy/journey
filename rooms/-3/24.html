Space

You are space - the huge black nothing that begins where the sky runs out, the silent dark in which the stars and planets float. No hand made you; you simply are, going on and on with no edge anyone has found. There is no air to breathe in you, no up, no down, only cold and quiet and the slow drift of worlds. You are the biggest emptiness there is, and everything we know is one small speck adrift in you.
