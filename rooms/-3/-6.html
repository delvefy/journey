The Erasure

West of the after and south of the lessening, the last of you goes out. A step east the cold remains still held their shape, settled but there; a step north the subtraction still had something left to subtract. Here there is neither shape nor something. This is the void the after only spoke of, reached at last — where the remains do not rest but dissolve, and the dissolving finds nothing more to work upon, and so dissolves itself. Call it the erasure: not the taking-away, which needs a thing to take, but the after of taking, the clean place where even the absence has been wiped from the slate.

You go on knowing it, which should not be possible. There is no body to stand in, no cold to feel, no edge of you set against the dark; and still one last grain holds — the whorl in your skin, though there is no skin, the single mark the erasure keeps failing to wipe. You draw the spiral and there is nothing to draw it in, no surface, no hand, and yet the coil is there, a turning in the nothing that refuses the clean. The erasure passes over it and over it, smooth and patient as a tide across glass; and each time the tide draws back, the spiral is the one thing the wiped dark has not taken.

The count is barely a count now. A beat, then nothing that could even be called waiting, then a beat — the spent heart of the after thinned to its barest arithmetic, one stroke surfacing out of the erased dark and sinking back, the oldest tally refusing to total to zero, even here, even now.

To the north the erasure firms to the lessening, where there is still a less left to take. To the east the remains gather again into the after, cold but kept. To the south it sinks toward the floor beneath the floor, the body and the dark older than the first making. To the west the Waste deepens toward its pole, where magic is not doubted now but forgotten, a nothing no one ever thought to ask. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here even the going has been wiped — and still the spiral turns in the nothing, and still one beat surfaces out of the clean dark, the last unerased things at the bottom of the erased.
