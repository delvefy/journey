Banana

You are a long curved fruit in a thick yellow skin, growing in a great hanging bunch on a tropical tree. No hand makes you; the hot sun ripens you from green to gold. You peel back in three or four neat strips to the soft sweet white inside. The monkey loves you; the clown slips on your skin.

North

A little horse, both small and stout,
with short strong legs and a shaggy mane;
just right for a child to learn to ride -
it trots along the lane.

East

A bright yellow flower of early spring
with a trumpet in its heart;
it bobs in the breeze on a tall green stem -
from a bulb it gets its start.

South

A lumpy brown root dug from the soil,
with little eyes all round;
mashed, or chipped, or baked in its coat -
the best spud to be found.

West

A little round green ball you grow
in a long green pod by the row;
you pop the pod and out they roll -
a princess felt one through the bedroll.
