Sunshine

You are the sun's gift poured down for free - bright, warm, and everywhere at once. You pour through the window and lie in a gold square on the floor where the cat will find you. You warm the back of the neck, open the flowers, dry the washing, and lift the whole world's mood by a notch. You ask nothing and fall on everyone alike. Clouds can hide you for a while, but the moment they part, here you are again, gladdening the day.

North

At night, when the great moon is high,
a pale and silver light I pour;
no warmth, no gold, just gentle shine,
across the silent moor.

East

In and out, you do me all day,
without a single thought;
on a cold morning you can see me -
a little cloud, then naught.

South

When the sun has just gone down,
and the sky still holds some light;
the grey and gentle in-between,
of daytime and the night.

West

From the rising to the setting sun,
the bright and busy light;
you wake, you work, you run, you play,
until it turns to night.
