The Never-Thought

West of the aftermind and north of the last thought, the cold mind's grey stops being the mind's at all. A step east it was the after of all that intelligence, shed and bleached but still its own grey; a step south it was one last thought, guttering, reaching. Here the last thought is wholly out and the after has drifted loose, and the grey it leaves can no longer be told from a grey that never thought anything — the killed mind and the never-mind arrived at the same dust. This is the never-thought: not the after of thinking now, but the place where the after and the never have become one grey, the cold future dissolving at last into the Waste that was never quick.

You stand where nothing was ever thought and nothing thinks, and the two feel exactly alike. The recursive coil that wound the cold mind is not merely shed here but forgotten as though it had never wound — and yet you draw the spiral, and it holds, the one mark that is neither a thing the mind made nor a thing the never-mind failed to make. The whorl in your skin keeps its warm turn in the grey, the single coil that belongs to a maker in a place that has unmade the very idea of one.

There is no count, and no after of counting either — past even the aftermind's silence, into a grey where counting was never invented and so cannot be missed. Your heart beats across it, the one tick neither thought nor unthought, keeping a time the never-thought has no name for and no way to lose.

To the north the never-thought firms toward the cold pole's last grey, the post-human nothing total. To the east the aftermind returns, the mind's own after-grey still its own. To the south the last thought, the mind a flicker from gone. To the west it dissolves wholly into the Waste, the never-thought and the never-anything one dust to the pole. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the killed mind and the mind that never was have lost it into the same grey, unthought and never-thought at once — and still the warm coil turns, the one made mark, and still one live beat crosses the grey that thinks nothing and never did.
