Cucumber

You are a cucumber - a long green vegetable, cool and crisp, with pale watery flesh and tiny soft seeds inside. You grow fat and dark on a trailing vine, then get sliced thin into the summer salad or cut into sticks for dipping. So full of water you are that to eat you is almost to drink. Smooth-skinned and refreshing, you are the calmest, coolest thing in the whole crunchy garden.

North

A great big orange ball that grows
upon a trailing vine;
scoop me out at Halloween -
a spooky face will shine.

East

A tiny green ball of a vegetable,
like a cabbage shrunk down small;
boiled on the Christmas dinner plate -
you might not like at all.

South

A long thin climbing twisty plant,
it winds up wall and tree;
grapes and cucumbers grow on me -
I curl and climb, you see.

West

The bendy hinge in the middle of your leg,
it lets you crouch and kneel;
you bang and scrape me when you fall -
ouch, the sting I feel!
