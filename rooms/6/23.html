Lie

You are a lie - a thing said that is not so, told on purpose to fool. You are made of words, like the truth, but bent the wrong way, and you grow when you are fed, needing more of yourself to stand. The teller's cheeks go warm; the listener is led astray. No hand holds you, yet you do real harm. You are the crooked twin of the truth, found out in the end and never quite forgiven.
