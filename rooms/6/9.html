Sniff

You are a short sharp pull of air up the nose - quick, nosy, and useful. You catch the wet drip of a cold; you draw in the smell of dinner, of rain, of a flower held close. Half-swallowed, you can hold a tear back and keep it secret. No hand makes you; the nose makes you, again and again, a small inward breath that listens by its own two holes.

too loud to lock inside a cell,

South

A tickle, then a gathering -
a speck of dust, a beam of sun;
your eyes squeeze tight, 'Ah-CHOO!' it bursts,
the storm that stops for no one.
