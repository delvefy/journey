Scoop

You are a scoop - the deep round spoon that bites a perfect ball from the ice cream tub, or lifts a heap of flour, or sand, or dog food, in one go. You are hungry-shaped, cupped to carry more than a spoon ever could. Children love you most at the seaside and the sweet shop, where one good dig of you means treasure. Dig, lift, plop - and the bowl fills up. That is the whole of your happy work.
