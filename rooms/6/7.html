blink

You are a blink, the shortest deed a body does, over before the mind can name it. Down come the lids and up again, washing the eye, and the world goes dark and back so fast you never miss it. You happen ten thousand times a day unasked; you give away surprise, or doubt, or a held-back tear. 'In the blink of an eye,' people say, when they mean no time at all. You are the small dark curtain that keeps the seeing clear.

North

A tickle climbs inside your nose,
your breath winds up to go;
'a-tish-oo!' out the great gust blows,
and bless you, all say so.

East

A tickle in the throat, and then
you bark it, harsh and rough;
through cold and flu it comes again,
this dry and hacking stuff.

South

A sudden catch of breath you make,
at a fright or great surprise;
you suck the air in for the sake
of the shock before your eyes.

West

Your brows draw down, your mouth turns low,
when you are cross or sad;
the opposite of a smile, you show
the world that you are mad.
