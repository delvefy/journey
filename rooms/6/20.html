Clue

You are a clue - the small dropped sign that points the way to a thing kept hidden. A muddy footprint, a torn note, a word that means more than it says. The detective gathers you up, one by one, until the whole answer stands plain. You cannot be touched, only noticed and followed. You live in mysteries and treasure hunts and the careful lines of a riddle. You are the breadcrumb trail that leads a sharp mind out of the dark and into knowing.
