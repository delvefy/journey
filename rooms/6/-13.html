Crown

You are the ring of gold upon the king's head, set with jewels, heavy with rule. Made by the finest hands, you mark the one who reigns. To wear me is to carry a kingdom; to lose me is to lose it all. Lift me high at the crowning - and bow, for here sits the ruler of the land.

North

Tie the laces, tie the string,
pull the loops up tight;
this tangled lump that holds it fast -
undo it if you might.

East

A little paper stub you buy
to ride or see the show;
you keep it safe inside your hand
and tear it as you go.

South

The grand high chair where a king sits,
all gold and red and tall;
he rules the land from up on it -
the proudest seat of all.

West

Two wheels, a frame, a saddle, bell,
you pedal hard to ride;
you lean to turn, you brake to stop -
the wind blows at your side.
