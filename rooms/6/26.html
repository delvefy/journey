Carol

You are a carol - a merry song sung at Christmas time, of shepherds and stars and a baby in a manger. Bundled up against the cold, singers go from door to door in the frosty dark, breath misting, lanterns glowing, voices rising together. Inside, warm hands clap along and mince pies wait. Old and joyful and full of the spirit of the season, you are the sound of Christmas itself, ringing out in the snowy night, gathering everyone who hears you into one warm circle of song.
