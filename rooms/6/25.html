Chant

You are a chant - the same few words shouted again and again in rhythm by a whole crowd at once, the football terrace, the playground game, the marching protest. No one person makes you; many voices beating as one do. You build and swell until everyone is joined in, stamping in time. You are a spell made of repetition, a slogan with a pulse. Loud and rhythmic and many-mouthed, you are the sound of a crowd that has found its single voice.
