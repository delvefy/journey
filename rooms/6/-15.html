Scroll

You are a scroll - a long sheet of paper or parchment, rolled up tight into a tube and often tied with a ribbon. Unroll me and read the message: a royal proclamation, a treasure map, an ancient list of names. Long before books had pages, the old world wrote on scrolls and rolled them away on a shelf. Made by patient hands, you keep your words curled safe inside until a hand unrolls you.

North

A great gold chair for a king or queen,
set high upon a stair;
with velvet seat and jewelled arms,
the grandest kind of chair.

East

Little cubes with spotted sides,
numbered one to six;
shake them in your hands and roll -
which number comes up quick?

South

A little round of shining metal,
silver, gold, or brown;
heads on one side, tails the other,
it pays for things in town.

West

A flat wood board on four small wheels,
you ride it standing up;
kick and roll along the path,
then leap a ramp and jump!
