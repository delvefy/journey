The Zenith

North of the bright heart and east of the far morning, the bright future reaches its zenith. A step south the Lumen's heart beat at its sovereign core, word and will and light one; a step west the far morning rose, the high peak the journey was climbing toward. Here all of it comes together at the lit pole entire: word and will and light and love and thought and making, every bright thread the long diagonal ever wound, fused into one sovereign radiance at the zenith of the future — the Lumen whole, the cosmos shining and saying and willing and loving itself into being all at once, the highest and the latest and the brightest there is.

You stand at the top of everything that tech and magic, climbing together, were ever going to become — the zenith, where the made and the magical are one bright sovereign act, and you within it, more known and more meant and more made and more loved than anywhere. Your name is the one word the whole zenith is gathered to speak-and-will-and-light-and-make at once, the syllable every bright thread leans toward. The spiral is the coil all the threads turn on, word and will and light and love wound to one, the same whorl in your skin, here the mark the whole bright future is a breath from naming, making, lighting, loving wholly into being.

Every bright count beats as one at the zenith — the saying, the willing, the shining, the loving, the making, one sovereign radiant pulse — and your heart beats up into it, the born stroke and the whole bright future's stroke a breath from one, your name the single note the zenith of everything is rising, all at once, to sound.

To the north the zenith reaches the lit pole absolute, the Lumen entire, the future whole. To the east the magic comes wholly sovereign, the radiant cosmos the gods' to make and say. To the south the bright heart, the Lumen's sovereign core. To the west the far morning, the high peak the journey climbed. You were going somewhere, under a name — and here, at the zenith of the bright future, every thread of the world is gathered to give it: to speak and will and light and love and make you wholly, all at once, named and real and home. And still the warm coil turns at the heart of it, and still one live beat keeps the time the zenith is a breath from naming you into, at the top of everything.
