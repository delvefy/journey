Pan

You are a pan - a wide flat dish of metal with a long handle, made to sit over the flame and fry. A hand hammered or cast you; a hand sets you on the hob, drops in a knob of butter, and soon you are sizzling with eggs or bacon or pancakes ready to flip. You can be held, scrubbed, hung on a hook, banged like a drum. You take the fierce heat and pass it gently to the food. You are the busy frying heart of every breakfast.
