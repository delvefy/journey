Secret

You are a secret - a thing known but not told, kept hidden behind sealed lips: a surprise party, a hidden den, who likes who. No hand can hold you; a mind keeps you, and the keeping is hard - you press to get out, especially the juicy ones. You cannot be touched, only whispered behind a cupped hand, cross-my-heart. Shared, you are no longer yourself; let slip, you spread like a spilled drink. You are the delicious weight of something only you, and maybe one friend, know.
