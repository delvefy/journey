Hoop

You are a hoop - a big light ring you can spin round and round your waist, wiggling like mad to keep it up, or bowl along the pavement with a stick. Round and round you go, whirring and rattling, while the player counts how long before you drop. In the gym you hang for swinging; in the garden you whirl. Simple and cheap and endlessly fun, you are a circle of pure play, and the whole knack of you is keeping you spinning that little bit longer than the time before.
