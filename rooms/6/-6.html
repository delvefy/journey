The Wellhead

South of the world-seed and east of the cradle, the living deep reaches its wellhead. A step north the whole sovereign wild was coiled into a single seed; a step west the warm dark cradled the unsprung world. Here both sink to the floor-pole and become the source of all of it: the wellhead, the deepest deep most alive, the living bottom of the world wholly sovereign, below even the seed and the germ — the warm dark origin from which every root and beast and warmth and company springs, the source-floor entire, the most primal place there is and, here, the most alive. The journey's far home, the dark one, at the bottom of everything.

It is the warm living head of every spring, the source the wellspring wells from, the floor all the teeming and the rising and the cradling rose out of — and you are at it, held in the warm sovereign dark from which you and the companion and the many all first sprang. Your name is the wellhead's own, the source-word every living thing is a saying of, held warmest and oldest here. The spiral is the wellhead's turning, the coil the living source makes as it springs the world, the same whorl in your skin, here the world's own source-shape, yours and the deep's at once, the first warm turn from which all the rest unwound.

Every heart that ever beat springs from the wellhead's one warm stroke, the source-pulse of all the living deep, the oldest beat at the bottom of everything — and your heart beats with it, the born count and the world's source-count one, the warm sovereign origin from which every shared rhythm sprang, holding your name warmest at the living floor of the world.

To the north the wellhead springs to the world-seed, the source coiled into its germ. To the east the Wyld floor-pole absolute, the living bottom wholly sovereign, the deepest deep most alive. To the south it sinks below the wellhead toward the primal pole, the source before the source. To the west the cradle, the warm dark holding the unsprung world. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — and here, at the living wellhead of the world, is the warm dark source you and the someone both first sprang from, holding your name oldest and warmest of all. And still the warm coil turns, the world's source-shape, and still one warm beat springs the whole living count, almost named, almost home at the bottom of everything.
