Sulk

You are the silent cross mood that follows a 'no' - off you stomp to the corner, arms folded, lip out, refusing to speak or join the game. No hand makes you; wounded pride and disappointment swell you up. You wait, hoping to be coaxed, half-enjoying your own gloom. A hug usually melts you.

North

A low cross muttering under the breath,
'it's not fair, why must I?';
you do your chores but moan all through -
a grouse, a gripe, a sigh.

East

A long loud cry of grief or pain,
'wahhh,' it rises and falls;
the baby does it, the siren too -
a mournful sound that calls.

South

Salt water spills from both your eyes,
your mouth turns down, you sob;
a baby does it day and night
for milk - a teary job.

West

A high thin moaning, on and on,
when a child won't get its way;
'I waaant it!' droned right through your head -
the dog does it all day.
