Spinningtop

You are a spinning top - a little toy you pinch and twist and let go, and away it whirls, balanced on its single point, humming round and round in a blur of colour. For a while it spins so smooth it seems to stand quite still; then it slows, starts to wobble, and at last topples over with a clatter. Old as old, simple and mesmerising, you are a scrap of wood or tin given life by a flick of the fingers, dancing on its toe until the spin runs out.
