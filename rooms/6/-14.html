Throne

You are the great high chair of the king, all gold and crimson velvet, raised on a step above the hall. Made by the finest hands to seat a crown. To sit on me is to rule; empty, I wait for the rightful one. Climb the steps, turn, and sit - and the whole court bows before you.

North

A ring of gold upon the head,
with jewels bright and tall;
the king and queen wear this to rule -
the grandest hat of all.

East

A stiff little rectangle of paper,
you send one on a birthday;
or fifty-two you deal for games -
a king, a queen, you play.

South

A long rolled-up sheet of old paper,
tied with a ribbon or seal;
the king's grand message, unrolled and read -
a proclamation real.

West

On snowy hills you ride me down,
no wheels, just runners thin;
you steer and tip into the drift -
then trudge back up to win.
