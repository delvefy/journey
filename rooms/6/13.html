Huff

You are the cross puffed-out breath of someone who has had enough - arms folded, cheeks blown wide, the door shut a shade too hard. No calm thing makes you; sulk does, all wounded pride. You are temper let out in one hot gust. Cross your arms and blow, and the whole room knows you are not pleased.

North

A short low 'ugh' down in the throat,
the pig's own snuffly word;
you make one heaving a heavy box -
the laziest sound heard.

East

The flat loud call of the pond's white bird,
'quack-quack' for bread it begs;
the duck says it, the toy one too -
it waddles on orange legs.

South

A low cross muttering of complaint,
when nothing's going right;
the empty tummy does it too -
a grouchy, rumbling spite.

West

To speak so low none hears the words,
half-swallowed and unclear;
the cross remark beneath the breath -
not meant for any ear.
