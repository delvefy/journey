The Elders

East of the kept hearth the dead come into their power. The homely household a step west, where the long-dead sat easy at the fire, is here a court — the elders sovereign, the old powers at the high board, the long-dead grown into masters not only of the house but of the world it stands in. This is the pure pole of magic: no engine, no wild, only the old certainty that a true name, rightly spoken, bends matter to obey it. The elders speak, and the world answers; they name a thing and it is so; reality here is not found but agreed, and it is the dead who do the agreeing.

They turn to you, the eldest among them, and you feel the weight of what they hold: your true name, kept at the high table since before you woke, the name that is not only who you are but what you may command, for here to know a name is to wield it. The spiral is graven everywhere as a ruling sigil — over the board, in the floor, on the ring of the eldest's hand — the house-mark grown into a seal of power, the same whorl in your skin, here the sign of a name that bends the world. The eldest rises to bestow it. To be given your name, in this hall, would be to be given mastery, a seat among the sovereign dead.

The house-bell has become a great tolling of names, each stroke a word that commands, the old names rung into the world like law — and your own waits to be tolled, the next stroke, the naming that would seat you among the elders and bend the world to your saying.

To the north the elders' names are fitted to craft and light, the old magic given engines, the magitech to come. To the east the magic pole absolute, the old names wholly the world, gods walking, reality pure consensus. To the south the elders give way to the wild, the old powers less ancestor than beast-god, the Wyld at the door. To the west the court loosens to a homely hearth, the dead easy and unsovereign at the fire. You were going somewhere, under a name. The elders have held it for you all this while, the name that is also a mastery — and the eldest rises to give it, the great bell drawing breath to toll you, the world leaning to obey whoever you turn out to command.
