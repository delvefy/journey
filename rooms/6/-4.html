The Sovereign Wild

South of the wild's eye and east of the wellspring, being and the wild become the same word. A step north the great beast-regard held the world in being by seeing it; a step west the wild made the world by welling up out of its own sheer existence. Here there is no seeing and no welling, no source and no regard, because there is no longer a gap between the wild and what it makes: the wild's being is the whole world entire, existence and creation one and indivisible, sovereign and total and without an edge. Nothing here is made by the wild. Everything here is the wild, simply, being.

You are not seen into being now, nor sprung from a source; you are the wild, one of its beings, indistinguishable from its sheer sovereign is. There is no telling where you end and the living all begins, and no need to tell — you are it, being itself, and that is older and surer than seeing or springing or saying. Your name is beside the point past even the wellspring's beside-the-point: not unneeded but unmeaning, for there is only the one being and you are it. The spiral is the wild's own suchness, the coil being makes simply by being, the same whorl in your skin, here not a mark the wild gives or sees but the wild's own shape, which is also yours, because here there is only one.

The wild's being is the world's whole pulse, existence beating as one sovereign stroke, and your heart is not a separate count under it but that very stroke, the one beat the one being keeps — the count and the wild and you all the single fact of is, beating itself, total and alive and needing nothing.

To the north the sovereign wild rises into the wild's eye, being held by seeing. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the wild's sheer being the whole of everything, existence and creation wholly one. To the south it sinks below being toward the seed and the floor, the wild before it wholly is. To the west it gathers to the wellspring, being welling from a source again. You were going somewhere, under a name, as someone apart. Here there is no apart and no name to need: you are the sovereign wild, being itself, home by being unable to be anything else — and still the warm coil turns, the wild's own suchness, and still one beat keeps the time that is the one being, simply, going on.
