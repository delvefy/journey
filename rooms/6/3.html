The Becoming

North of the wielding and east of the unfinished city, reality stops being made and starts making itself. The powers a step south wielded the world at will; the city a step west named itself street by street; here there are no powers and no city, only the becoming — a reality of pure will that wills itself into being moment by moment, autonomous and total, no maker behind it, the world its own ongoing act, lit and self-creating and without end. Everything here is happening because it wills itself to, and goes on willing, and never finishes becoming.

You stand in the one place the becoming has not yet willed: the unwilled seed, the thing not yet decided into being. All around you the world wills itself brighter, fuller, on and on; and you are the held possibility at the centre of it, the one self the becoming has left for you to will. For it would have you will yourself in — you feel the invitation in the lit air, that here, uniquely, you might speak your own name and become, will yourself into the self-creating world as one more thing it is glad to have willed. The spiral is the seed of that will, the coil in your skin the becoming's one unwilled mark, poised to wind itself into the world. The becoming leans toward you, holding a space, willing you to will.

There is no count, only the becoming's continuous self-making, a tone that is the world deciding to be — and your name the one decision left unmade, the will the becoming waits on.

To the north the becoming refines to pure lit will, the Lumen pole self-willing absolute. To the east the magic pole, reality wholly its own will, self-creating without end. To the south it loosens to the wielding, a world willed by powers. To the west it gathers to a city naming itself. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the world wills itself endlessly and leaves you the one unwilled thing — the becoming holding a space for you, the saying of your own name a breath from willing you into the world at last.
