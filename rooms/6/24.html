Shout

You are a shout - a voice flung out at full strength to carry across a playground or stop a friend at the kerb. No tool makes you; the chest and the wide-open mouth do, in a rush of air. You can be glad or cross, a warning or a welcome, but you are always loud and always meant to reach. You are the word that will not be quiet, hurled out where a whisper would be swallowed up and lost.
