Whisk

You are a whisk - the looping cage of wire the cook grips to beat eggs and cream into soft white peaks. Round and round you go, faster and faster, until the bowl is full of froth and air. You are made by hand, all bent and springy wire on a little handle, light to lift and quick to clean. Of all the tools in the busy kitchen you are the whirliest, the one that turns a runny mess into something fluffy and fine.
