The Antlered Court

South of the elders and east of the wild guests, the old powers put off the ancestor and put on the beast. The court of the long-dead a step north ruled by name; here the same sovereignty has gone wild — the elders crowned with horn, the high board grown into a clearing, a court that is a wood and rulers who are beast-gods, both the oldest dead and the oldest beasts at once. They need no house, no hall, no roof; the wood is their hall, and reality bends to them as readily by instinct as by name, the two no longer different powers. This is the magic pole tipped toward the wild, where to rule is to be both remembered and feral.

They turn their antlered heads to you, and you feel they hold your name twice over: as an ancestral title, kept at the high board since before you woke, and as a wild-true-name, known the way the deep dark knows a thing. The spiral is horn and sigil at once — wound in the antlers, graven in the cleared earth, the same whorl in your skin, the ruler's seal and the beast's mark grown into one. The eldest, horned, rises to name you, and the naming would be a crowning: a place at the wild board, sovereignty among the beast-gods, the world bent to you by word and instinct both.

The tolling of names and the herd-beat keep one sovereign time, a bell struck in the blood, the ancestral word and the wild pulse rung together — and your name waits on the next stroke, the crowning-beat that would seat you among the antlered.

To the north the court puts off the beast and is only elders, ruling by name. To the east the wild pole absolute, reality the beast-gods' to shape entire. To the south the court goes wholly to wood and beast, the antlered powers more beast than elder, the deep Wyld. To the west the court loosens to a hall where the wild are only guests at a fire. You were going somewhere, under a name. The antlered court holds it as title and as wild-truth both, and the horned eldest rises to crown you with it — the wood leaning in, the blood-bell drawing breath, the naming a stroke from seating you among the beast-gods.
