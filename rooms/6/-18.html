Pot

You are a pot - a deep round vessel of metal, made to sit over a flame and hold what cooks. A hand hammered or cast you; a hand fills you with water and beans and bones, and a long slow afternoon turns it all to soup. You can be held, scrubbed, dented, hung on a hook. Of all the things in the kitchen you are the patient one, bubbling away while the cook does something else, asking only to be stirred.
