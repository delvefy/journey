Net

You are a mesh of knotted string, all holes and crossing threads, made to catch what is bigger than your gaps and let the rest slip through. A hand knots you, row by row. You hang behind the goal, you sweep the sea for fish, you swing on a pole to catch a butterfly. Water and air pass straight through you.

North

Thin and soft and good for tying,
to fly a kite up high;
a ball of it sits in the drawer -
you snip a length to tie.

East

A carrier with handles up,
of paper, cloth, or string;
you fill it full at the shop with food
and lug it home, the whole thing.

South

Loop the string and pull it tight,
a lump that holds things fast;
you tie one in your shoelace bow -
hard to undo, steadfast.

West

You climb a ladder to the top,
then whoosh - you zoom right down!
A long smooth metal shining slope -
the best ride in the town.
