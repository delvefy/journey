Grumble

You are the low cross muttering of someone who must do what they don't want to - 'it's not fair, why me?', under the breath, all through the chore. No hand makes you; a put-upon mood lets you out, a steady grousing grumble. The tummy does it too, rumbling for its dinner. Half-heard and never quite stopped.

North

All cross and puffed, you fold your arms
and stamp off in a strop;
'hmph!' you snort and blow out air -
you're in a right old ___.

East

A short sharp cry of sudden pain -
the dog whose tail you tread;
'yip!' it goes, then runs away -
a quick hurt sound, soon fled.

South

You don't get your way, so off you go
to sit alone and frown;
you won't speak, you won't join the game -
a silent mood, head down.

West

A low and grumbly drawn-out sound,
half a groan and half a grouse;
'ohhh, not again,' you let it out -
the grumble round the house.
