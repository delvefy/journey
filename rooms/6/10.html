Cry

You are the salt water spilling from the eyes, the sound of sorrow let out loud - the baby for its milk, the child for a scraped knee, anyone for a grief too big to hold. No hand makes you; the heart wrings you out. You wail, you sob, you soak the hankie. Let out, you ease the ache a little.

North

You don't get your way, so off you go
to sit alone and frown;
you won't speak, you won't join in -
a silent, cross-armed mood, head down.

East

A loud high shriek that splits the ears
when you are scared half to death;
'AAAH!' you cry on the ghost-train's plunge -
it empties out your breath.

South

A short sharp pull of air up the nose,
to smell a rose or stop a drip;
a snuffle when you've had a cry -
a quick and nosy sip.

West

Your lower lip pushed right out far
because you can't have your way;
no tears, just a cross and sulky face -
it says what you won't say.
