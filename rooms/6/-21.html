Sieve

You are a sieve - the bowl of fine wire mesh that lets the small things fall and keeps the big things back. You sift the lumps from the flour, the stones from the rice, the peas from their water. Shake me and a soft white snow drifts down into the bowl. You are full of holes yet you do a careful job; nothing fine escapes my catching, nothing coarse gets through. You are the kitchen's patient sorter, a net that strains the world into fine and not-fine.
