Clue

You are the thread left lying in the dark, the small bright thing that does not fit until, all at once, it does. A muddy footprint, a torn note, a coin where no coin should be - you are how the detective begins and the riddle ends. You point without speaking, and you mean nothing alone; only laid beside your fellows do you make a road to the truth. Follow enough of us and the locked door simply opens.

North

A feather on the bare pink sole,
a finger in the side;
I make you squeal and lose control,
and laugh until you've cried.

East

Rap your knuckles on the wood,
tap-tap upon the door;
'who is there?' is understood,
then I am heard once more.

South

My head bobs gently up and down,
to say a silent yes;
no word at all, no smile, no frown,
just this, and nothing less.

West

The corners of my mouth turn up,
when I am glad inside;
no laughter spills, no sound, no cup,
just gladness, open wide.
