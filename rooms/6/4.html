The Fiat

East of the utterance and north of the becoming, the word and the will are one. A step west the cosmos was a single endless saying, the gods speaking the world into being; a step south it was a pure self-willing, reality deciding itself into existence moment by moment. Here speaking and willing are the same act, and the act is lit: a self-uttering will, a willing word, the world commanding itself into being and being its own command, sovereign and shining, every star a decree it speaks to itself and obeys in the speaking. To say, here, is to will; to will is to say; and the world is the one fiat saying-and-willing itself without end.

You stand in the one place the fiat has not yet pronounced — the unsaid syllable and the unmade decision at once, the single gap in the self-commanding world. And it leans toward you doubly: to speak your name here would be to will yourself in, and to will yourself in would be to speak it, the two halves of becoming joined in one word that is yours to say. The spiral is the seed of that fiat, a coil of lit will winding toward utterance, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark the sovereign word has left for you to pronounce yourself into the world. The fiat gathers. It holds a space shaped exactly like your name, and waits for you to speak it real.

There is no count but the world's own self-decreeing, a tone that is reality willing itself aloud, the bell become the sovereign word the cosmos speaks to be — and your name the one decree unspoken, the willed word the fiat waits on, a breath from sounding you into the lit and self-commanding world.

To the north the fiat refines to the Lumen pole, word and will and light wholly one, the self-commanding peak. To the east the magic goes pole-absolute, reality nothing but the gods' sovereign saying. To the south it loosens to the becoming, a world willing itself without yet the word. To the west it gathers to the utterance, a world spoken without yet the will. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the one word that is also a will waits for you to say it and so will yourself home — and still the warm coil turns, the unspoken fiat, and still one live beat keeps the time the sovereign word is a breath from speaking you into being.
