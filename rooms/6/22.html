Warning

You are a warning - the word that says watch out before the harm arrives. You are the red sign by the cliff, the shout across the road, the label that says hot. You go ahead of trouble to give it a name, so the careful can step back in time. Heed me and I have done my work; ignore me and I become an I-told-you-so. I am care, spoken early, dressed in red.
