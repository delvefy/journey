Kite

You are a kite - a bright cloth shape stretched on light sticks, flown on a long, long string against the wind on a blowy day. Hands build you and a running child launches you, and up you climb, dipping and diving and tugging to be let go higher. Your tail snaps and flutters behind you. You need wind to live and a firm grip to stay. Half a bird and half a toy, you are the way a person learns to play with the sky.
