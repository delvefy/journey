Tickle

You are the strange light touch that turns a child to helpless giggles, a feather's drag along the sole, fingers wriggling at the ribs. No one can do you to themselves - you need another hand, and a little trust. You are half delight and half torment, a laughter that begs to stop and start again. Cats know you, babies know you, and the ticklish dread and love the threat alike. Soft and quick, you prove how close laughter and surrender truly lie.

North

A quick sharp breath sucked in with fright,
'oh!' at a sudden thing;
a shock, a scare, a wondrous sight,
the catch of breath I bring.

East

A jab of one stiff finger-end,
into your ribs or back;
'wake up!' or 'look!' it means, my friend,
a small sharp little smack.

South

A footprint, a torn scrap, a key,
the hunter follows on;
I point the way for all to see,
till the mystery is gone.

West

When you don't know, you have a try,
and say what might be true;
you take a stab, you aim, you spy -
a hopeful shot from you.
