The Wild's Eye

North of the world-dream and east of the beast-gods, the wild's regard becomes the whole of reality. The gods a step north dreamed and named the world; the beasts a step west made by knowing; here the two sink below dreaming and naming alike into a single vast wild regard — the beast-gods' slow ancient eye open on everything, and everything existing because that eye is on it. This is the Wyld pole: not spoken, not dreamed, but seen; a world held in being by the wild's looking, all things made and kept by the great beast-regard that never blinks.

The eye is on you. You feel it the way prey feels it, but without the fear, for the regard is not hunting, only holding — the wild seeing you, and in the seeing making you real, keeping you in being by sheer attention, the oldest and surest making there is. To be seen by the wild's eye is to be. Your name is what the regard rests on, and where it rests you are most wholly here, drawn into being by being watched. The spiral is the eye's own shape, the slow coil of the wild's looking, the same whorl in your skin, the thing the great regard knows you by. The eye holds you, and you are, because it sees you.

The beasts' regarding is the world's pulse, a seeing that beats, each slow look a thing kept in being — and your heart beats under the wild's eye, held in its regard, real for as long as it is seen.

To the north the regard rises into dreaming and naming, the world-dream. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the wild's eye the whole world, all made by being seen. To the south it sinks below seeing into pure being, the wellspring. To the west the beasts' regard renders true, the beast-gods. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the wild's eye sees it and holds you in being by the seeing — real because watched, kept by the great unblinking regard, your name the place its ancient looking rests.
