Coin

You are a coin - a little round of metal money, cool and heavy in the palm. You have a head on one side and a tail on the other, an edge that glints, a number stamped clean. You drop with a chink into the jar, buy the sweets, roll under the sofa and hide. Made by a press in a mint, touchable and bright, you are wealth small enough to lose down the back of a chair.
