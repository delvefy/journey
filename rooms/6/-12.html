Knot

You tie the world together with a twist and a tug. A knot is the smallest machine - no engine, no spark, only rope crossed over rope and pulled until it grips. Sailors trust it, shoelaces wear it, parcels are bound by it. Undo it and a thing falls loose; pull it tight and it holds against the storm. You made it with your hands, you can touch it, and it will not let go.

North

I am full of holes
yet I hold what I catch;
cast me in the sea
and fish I will snatch.

East

Folded in a pocket,
flat and made of hide,
I keep your coins and cards
tucked safely down inside.

South

Gold upon a head,
with points that rise like rays;
no king is truly king
without me, the old books say.

West

Two wheels and a bar to hold,
one foot kicks the ground;
I carry you along the path
with a rolling, humming sound.
