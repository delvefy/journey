Lullaby

You are a lullaby - the soft slow song a grown-up hums or sings to settle a small child to sleep. Gentle and low, sung over and over, you rock the cradle and smooth away the day, of stars and moons and dreamland far away. Eyelids grow heavy, the breathing slows, and at last the little one drifts off. Tender and drowsy and full of love, you are the quietest, kindest song there is, sung in the dim of the nursery until sleep finally comes.
