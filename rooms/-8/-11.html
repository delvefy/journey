Turkey

A big bustling bird with a fan of a tail and a wobble of red at its throat - a turkey struts the farmyard like it owns the place. It gobbles and puffs and spreads its feathers wide, all show and noise. Heavy and warm and entirely earthbound, it is the centre of the winter feast in many lands. Made by no hand, it is the grandest of the farm birds, proud as anything, and quite unable to fly very far at all.

North

A great horned beast,
the father of the herd;
he paws the ground and snorts
without a gentle word.

East

A baby pig,
pink and small and round;
it squeals for its mother
and roots in the ground.

South

The king of the jungle
with a great furry mane;
he roars so loud
it sounds like thunder and rain.

West

I carry my shell
on land and in the sea;
I pull my head inside
when danger comes to me.
