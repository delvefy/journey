Slurp

You are a slurp - the loud wet sucking sound made when drinking noisily. The soup goes off the spoon with you; the last of the milkshake comes up the straw with you; the cat laps its saucer with a tiny one. Grown-ups frown and say it's rude, but a really good slurp is hard to resist. You mean a drink is too hot, or too tasty, or nearly gone. You are the cheerful messy noise of drinking with more enthusiasm than manners.
