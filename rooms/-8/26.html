Suck

You are a suck - to draw something in with your mouth and your breath, cheeks pulled in tight. You suck a boiled sweet till it's tiny, suck milkshake up a straw, suck your thumb when you're small and sleepy. A vacuum cleaner sucks too, and so does a plughole pulling the bathwater down. Steady and pulling and quietly busy, you are the mouth working the other way round from blowing, drawing things in rather than puffing them out.
