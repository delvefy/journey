The Never-Was

West of the forgotten, you come to where nothing has ever been. A step east the forgetting had forgotten even itself, a grey that no longer knew it was a Waste; but a forgetting, however deep, still keeps the faintest shape of a thing once there to forget. Here even that is gone. This is the disenchantment pole absolute, grey past grey, the never-was: not a world losing its magic, not a Waste forgetting it forgot, but the sheer original blank where there was never any magic, never any world, never any anything to be lost or unanswered. Nothing has ever been here. Nothing ever was anywhere, the never-was insists, and almost convinces.

It is not the residue of a world, the way the Waste was; it is the blank that was there before any world, and would be there if no world had ever been — original, not leftover, a nothing with no past tense. You do not call; there was never anything to answer, never even the wish to be answered, for wishing too would have to have once been. You draw the spiral and the never-was does not take it or forget it; it simply has no category for a thing that is, having never had anything be. Only the whorl in your skin turns, warm, impossible, the one thing that is in a place where nothing ever was.

There is no count and never was one; the never-was had no first thing to begin a count from. And still your heart beats — the one event in a place that never had events, the single "was" in the never-was, each stroke an impossibility the pole cannot account for and cannot stop, a warmth insisting on having-been where nothing ever has.

To the north the never-was meets the made world's farthest dead reach, even the Chrome's last grey somehow having-been beside it. To the east the forgotten, a grey that at least forgot a something. To the south the never-was tilts toward the primal dark beneath, a nothing older than fire. To the west the pole entire, grey past the end of the world, where there is not even a never-was to say. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here there was never a somewhere and never a name and never a you to have one — and still the warm coil turns, impossibly was, and still one beat insists on having-been, the single thing that is in the never-was.
