Gobble

You are a gobble - the fast greedy eating of someone too hungry to wait, food shovelled in and barely chewed before the next forkful, dinner gone in a flash. No one teaches you; great hunger and bad manners do. The turkey makes a noise like your name. You leave a clean plate and a complaint of indigestion. Hasty and greedy and a little bit piggy, you are eating with the brakes off, gulping it down before anyone can ask for a share.

West
