Zebra

You are the horse in stripes of black and white, no two of you marked quite the same, galloping the dusty African plain. No hand made you; the wild grassland did. You herd together so the lion's eye is dazzled by the dazzle of stripes. Wild and skittish - no one has ever quite tamed a zebra.

North

The great gold cat with shaggy mane,
the roarer, king of all;
it hunts the plains and naps in sun -
the biggest, proudest call.

East

A little furry pet you keep
that spins around its wheel;
it crams its cheeks right up with seeds -
how round its face can feel!

South

A huge grey beast with a horn on its nose,
thick-skinned and short of sight;
it charges at what makes it cross -
a heavyweight with might.

West

A beast that bounds on great hind legs,
with a pouch where its baby hides;
it boxes, hops, and thumps its tail -
in hot far lands it strides.
