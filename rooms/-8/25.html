Lick

You are a lick - one swipe of the tongue, wet and quick. You lick an ice cream before it drips, lick a stamp to stick it down, lick a spoon clean of cake mix. A cat licks its fur, a dog licks your face, a flame licks up the side of a log. Small and warm and a little bit cheeky, you are the tongue reaching out to taste or clean or tidy, gone in an instant and back again, and round a melting cornet you are never quite quick enough.

To chew on something hard and tough,
over and over, slow;

South

To eat up your food too fast and greedy,
great mouthfuls, gone in a flash;
you bolt your dinner before the rest -
a ___, a hungry dash.

West

To drink it down greedy in great big gulps,
the whole glass gone in one go;
you glug your squash too fast and burp -
a ___, glug-glug, you know.
