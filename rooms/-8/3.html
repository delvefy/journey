The Last Silence

West of the unmourned and north of the last reach, the made world goes wholly to silence. A step east even the machine future's ending was forgotten, unmourned; a step south its last extent reached the edge of the made, dissolving. Here the open Waste takes it wholly: the made world's final dead extent worn to a grey silence that stretches to the pole, the dead machine and the open Waste become one soundless grey, the made and its ending and its silence alike forgotten. This is the last silence — the very last quiet the made world will ever keep, extended flat and grey and forgotten to the end of the world.

It is a silence with no sound left to have fallen from, no hum to fade, no bell to still, no ending to mourn — only the flat grey quiet to the pole, the made world's last silence indistinguishable from the open Waste's old one. You draw the spiral and the last silence neither keeps it nor takes it; there is no sound, no record, no answer, only the grey quiet. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its warm turn, and your heart its small sound, the one unsilenced thing in the last silence of the made.

There is no count; the last silence is past the hum and past the hush and past the unmourned ending, a grey quiet with nothing left to tick. And still your heart beats — the one sound in the last silence, the single unsilenced count where the made world's every other sound has gone grey and quiet to the pole, keeping a small warm time in the silence at the edge of everything made.

To the north the last silence firms toward the Chrome's outermost grey, the made world's final dead extent. To the east the unmourned, the dead future's ending forgotten. To the south the last reach, the made dissolving at its edge. To the west the open Waste takes the silence wholly, grey and soundless to the pole, the made forgotten to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the made world sounded it once. Here that sounding is gone to the last silence, your name quiet and forgotten in the grey — and still the warm coil turns, and still one small beat keeps a sound in the last silence of the made.
