Whisper

You are speech with the voice taken out of it, words shaped on nothing but breath, brought close to the ear so no one else can hear. Secrets travel by you in the dark dormitory; lovers trade you; the conspirators lean their heads together over you. You can carry only a little way, and that is your whole purpose - to be heard by one and missed by all the rest. Soft as the wind in dry grass, gone the instant it is spoken, you are how the quietest things are said.

North

The leaves turn gold and red and fall,
the days grow short and cool;
the harvest's gathered, one and all,
and children back to school.

East

What the ear drinks from the air,
a bark, a bell, a word;
no weight, no shape, yet everywhere,
the moment it is heard.

South

A thin straight beam of golden light,
that slants down from the sun;
through cloud and leaf it pierces bright,
a single shining one.

West

The sound that rises from your throat,
in speech, or song, or shout;
each one is different, every note,
it's how the words come out.
