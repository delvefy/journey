Ripple

You are the small ring spreading on still water where the stone went in - the gentlest wave, the wrinkle on the pond, the shiver across the lake when the wind breathes. No hand makes you; a touch on the water does. Outward and outward, softer and softer, until the pond is glass again.

North

The fine wet mist the waves throw up,
that flies off the crashing sea;
the hose, the fountain, the splashing oar -
small drops blown wild and free.

East

The in-between light, not day, not night,
when the sky goes violet-grey;
the bats come out, the first star pricks -
the soft end of the day.

South

The slow fall of one drop at a time
from the tap not turned off right;
'plip... plip...' it goes in the dark -
it keeps you up all night.

West

The colour of the sun and rings,
of treasure, crowns, and corn;
the shiniest, most precious shade -
a yellow richly born.
