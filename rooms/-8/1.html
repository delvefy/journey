The Vestige

West of the traceless and north of the never-was, the made world makes its farthest dead reach. A step east the last trace of the made dissolved into the forgetting, traceless; a step south the never-was insisted that nothing ever has been at all. Here, between, persists the faintest vestige — the absolute outermost point the made world ever reached, a single grey having-been at the very brink of the never-was. It is almost nothing: a ghost of a notion that something, once, somewhere, was made and was. But beside the never-was even that faint “was” stands out, the last claim of the made against a nothing where nothing ever was.

It is the thinnest distinction there is — the difference between having-been-and-dissolved and never-having-been-at-all — and here, at the made world's farthest dead reach, that difference flickers like the last ember of an idea. You draw the spiral and it is the vestige's proof: a mark, therefore a maker, therefore a having-been, set against a never-was that would deny it ever was made. Only the whorl in your skin keeps a whole and certain having-been, the one undissolved vestige in a grey that the never-was, a step on, would swear had never been at all.

There is no count, only the vestige's faint insistence that an hour once was kept, somewhere, by something made — the ghost of a tick at the edge of a nothing that never counted. And your heart beats on, the one sure having-been at the made world's farthest reach, the last “was” refusing the never-was, keeping a warm certain time at the brink of all that never was.

To the north the vestige firms toward the Chrome's last dead reach, the made world's outermost grey, faint having-been. To the east the traceless, the made dissolved past its last grain. To the south the never-was, where nothing ever has been. To the west the pure Waste pole, no made thing ever again, no trace and no memory of trace, the never-was entire. You were going somewhere, under a name, in a world that was. Here the faintest vestige of that having-been flickers at the edge of the never-was — your name a ghost of a “was” against a nothing — and still the warm coil turns, wholly having-been, and still one beat keeps the last sure time at the made world's farthest dead reach.
