Touch

You are the sense that needs no special part, for you live in the whole skin at once - the warm and the cold, the silk and the splinter, the kiss and the sting all reported home. Shut every other sense and you remain; the blind read whole books by you, fingertip by fingertip. You are the first to wake in the newborn and the last to leave the old. A hand laid on a shoulder says more by you than any word. To be held, to be stroked, to be warmed - this is how the body knows it is not alone.

North

A thin straight shaft of golden light,
that slants down from the sun;
through cloud and leaf it pierces bright,
a single beam of one.

East

On the tongue I make my home,
and sort the sweet from sour;
in honey, salt, and lemon's foam,
I am the eating's power.

South

Where the green land suddenly ends,
a wall of rock and air;
straight down to the sea it bends,
the gull alone climbs there.

West

The sense that lives behind the eye,
that drinks the colours in;
the green, the red, the wide blue sky -
without me, all is dim.
