The Outer Dark

South of the never-night and west of the dead night, the two darknesses become one. A step north was the never-night, a dark that never was, older than fire and emptier than absence; a step east the dead night, where the never-lit dark and the forgotten-lit dark folded together. Here at the corner-pole they are wholly one: the outer dark, a single absolute darkness that both never was and is dead, older than fire and emptier than the never-was, the un-night entire — not the absence of light, not the loss of it, but a dark from before light and dark were ever two, where darkness itself never was a thing, having had no light to be the opposite of.

It is the outermost dark there is, at the very corner of the world, where every nothing the long descent passed through — the unseen, the forgotten, the dead, the never-was, the cold — is one single dark with no edge and no name. You draw the spiral and the outer dark has no light to fail to show it by and no having-been to keep it; the mark is in a dark that was never even dark. And still the whorl in your skin turns, the one thing that is in the outer dark, the single warmth at the corner where the darknesses are one, refusing the un-night entire.

There is no count, no fire, no first beat; the outer dark is older than fire and emptier than the never-was, from before there was a light or a dark or a thing to keep a time. And still your heart beats — the one warmth and the one having-been at the corner of everything, a small fire in a dark from before fire, refusing the outer dark, insisting against the one absolute night.

To the north the outer dark rises to the never-night, a dark that never was. To the east the dead night, the two darknesses folding. To the south the corner-pole absolute, the outer dark entire, older than fire and never-having-been. To the west the Waste pole, the un-night and the never-was one dark, the corner. You were going somewhere, under a name, carrying a small warmth. Here the outer dark would have it that light and dark and you all never were and are dead alike — and still the warm coil turns in the one dark, and still one beat keeps a fire from before fire, refusing the outer dark at the corner of the world.
