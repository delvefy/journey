Earwig

You are an earwig - the slim brown bug with a curved pair of pincers at the very end of your tail. You hide all day under stones, in flower petals, and down the cracks of the shed, then come out at night. The old story says you creep into sleeping ears, though you almost never do. Your pincers look fierce but rarely pinch hard. You scuttle for cover the moment a stone is lifted. You are a quick brown garden creature, armed at the tail and shy of the light.
