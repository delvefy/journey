mantis

A mantis is a long, slow, green insect that holds its two front legs folded up under its chin, as if it were saying its prayers - which is why people call it the praying mantis. But it is no saint. It sits perfectly still on a leaf, swaying just a little, until a fly comes near, and then those folded arms snap out and snatch it in a blink. Patient, strange, and a fearsome little hunter, it waits all day to pounce.

North

A long round bug with a thousand legs,
they ripple like a wave as I crawl;
I curl up tight into a coil -
so many feet, I'll never fall.

East

The giant of the nut bowl, big and curved,
in a shell so hard it beats the cracker;
the last one left at Christmas time -
a creamy nut, a tough old whacker.

South

A long green bug with springy legs,
I leap so far across the grass;
I rub my legs to chirp a song -
hop! and gone before you pass.

West

A grasshopper that swarms in clouds,
millions of us darken the sky;
we land and eat the whole field bare -
the farmer weeps as we fly by.
