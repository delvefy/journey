Bubble

You are a bubble - a thin round skin of soapy water, holding a breath of air, shining with every colour as it floats. Blow me from a little plastic wand and I drift up, wobbling, weightless, lighter than anything. You cannot keep me; touch me, and with a tiny silent pop I am gone, leaving only a cool damp speck on your finger. You are the most fragile, most beautiful nothing in the world.

North

When every noise has gone away
and not a sound is heard;
the deep still hush of an empty room,
unbroken by a word.

East

Tiny shining specks of sparkle
you stick on a card with glue;
they twinkle and they catch the light,
and stick to me and you!

South

When a wave hits the rocks it flies
up fine and white and wet;
a misty cloud of flung sea-water -
the wettest you can get.

West

A shiny reddish-brown metal,
it makes the pennies bright;
it bends to wire and carries power,
and gleams with a warm light.
