Grin

You are a grin - a big wide smile stretched right across the face, ear to ear, with all the teeth on show. You burst out when something is too good to keep in: a surprise, a winning goal, a joke that got you good. A cheeky one means trouble's been had; a sheepish one means you've been caught. No hand can hold you - you are gladness gone all the way to the edges of the face, the very biggest a smile can grow.
