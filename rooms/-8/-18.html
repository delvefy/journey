Whisker

You are a whisker - one of the long stiff hairs that sprout from the cheeks of a cat, a seal, a mouse, far longer and tougher than ordinary fur. No hand made you; the animal grew you, and you are not just for show: you twitch and feel the way through the dark, telling the cat if a gap is wide enough to squeeze through. You can be seen, gently touched, never pulled. You are the animal's own set of feelers, reading the world by the lightest touch.
