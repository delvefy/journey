Snore

You are a snore - the deep rumbling growl that escapes a sleeping nose and throat, loud enough to rattle the windows and wake the whole house. The deeper the sleep, the louder you grow, until a nudge in the ribs cuts you short. Grandpa is famous for you; the dog does a small one in its basket. The sleeper never hears you; only the poor soul awake beside them does. You are the helpless rough music of the deepest sleep.
