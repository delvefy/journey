The Last Reach

West of the dead march and north of the vestige, the made world makes its last reach. A step east the empire's last frontier dissolved into the limitless forgetting; a step south the faintest vestige of having-been flickered at the brink of the never-was. Here the two open out to the made world's absolute outermost grey: a flat grey reach stretching level and forgotten to the very edge of the made, the Chrome's last dead extent, the empire's frontier gone wholly to forgetting, grey to the end of the world. Beyond it there is no made thing and no having-been at all, only the pure Waste pole; this is the last reach, the farthest the made ever got, dissolving as it reaches its edge.

It is the widest, emptiest grey there is, a level reach to a horizon that is the edge of all that was ever made — and the horizon is dissolving even as you face it, the last reach worn to forgetting at its rim. You draw the spiral and it is a faint cast mark on the flat grey, dissolving toward the edge with everything else; only the whorl in your skin keeps a whole warm coil, the one mark that does not dissolve at the made world's last reach, carried to the very edge of having-been.

There is no count, only the flat grey reaching to its edge, the made world's farthest tick long since dissolved, the bell that marked its last frontier gone to forgetting. Your heart beats at the edge of the made, the one warm count the last reach could not dissolve, keeping time at the very rim of all that was ever built.

To the north the last reach firms toward the Chrome's outermost grey, the made world's final dead extent. To the east the dead march, the frontier dissolving. To the south the vestige, the faint having-been at the brink of the never-was. To the west the pure Waste pole, beyond the edge of the made, no having-been at all, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the made world reached its farthest once toward it. Here that last reach dissolves at the edge, your name worn to forgetting at the rim — and still the warm coil turns, whole, and still one beat keeps the time at the outermost edge of all that was ever made.
