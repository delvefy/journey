Ray

You are a single straight line of light, a thin gold spear thrown down from the sun through a gap in the cloud or a chink in the curtain. Dust dances in you, made suddenly visible; the cat finds you on the carpet and lies down in your warmth. You pick out one thing and leave the rest in shadow, like a finger pointing. Bend you through water and you break into colour; gather your kind and you become the whole bright day. Quick, clean and weightless, you are how the far-off fire reaches down and touches the floor.

North

A voice so soft, as light as breath,
held close beside the ear;
a secret hushed, that none but you
will ever come to hear.

East

Swift and bright, I fill the room,
the moment the lamp is lit;
I chase away the deepest gloom,
and the eye can see by it.

South

With the skin all over you,
I feel the rough and smooth;
the cold, the warm, the sharp pin too -
no eye, yet still I prove.

West

A tiny fleck of fire that leaps,
from flint, or wire, or coal;
bright for an instant, then it sleeps,
yet it lights the fire whole.
