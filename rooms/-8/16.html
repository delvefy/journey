Breeze

You are a breeze - the softest, friendliest sort of wind. You come cool across a hot cheek, you lift the corner of the picnic blanket, you set the leaves whispering and the washing swaying on the line. You carry the smell of cut grass and the sound of far-off bells. No hand can catch you, and no one minds you the way they mind a gale - you are just enough wind to be welcome, a gentle breath the warm day gives.
