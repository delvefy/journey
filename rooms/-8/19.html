Squawk

You are a squawk - the rough, ugly shout a bird lets fly when something gives it a fright. A parrot does you, a hen does you, a gull on the pier does you loudest of all. You are sound, not a thing to hold, harsh and short and rude. People borrow you too, for a complaint shrieked all of a sudden. You are the least musical noise in the whole farmyard, a ragged squall of feathers and fuss.
