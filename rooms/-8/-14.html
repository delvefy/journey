Rhino

You are a rhino - a huge grey beast, thick as a tank, with skin like folded armour and one or two great horns upon your nose. You graze the dusty plains, short-sighted and short-tempered, and when you charge, the ground shakes. For all your bulk you are gentle enough until troubled. Heavy, ancient, and slow, you are one of the last of the giants, plodding the wide hot grass.

North

A horse in black-and-white striped coat,
galloping over the plain;
no two of us are marked the same -
you'd count my lines in vain.

East

I swing from branch to branch by my tail,
and chatter, screech, and play;
I love a banana best of all,
in the jungle trees all day.

South

The slowest creature in the wood,
I hang upside-down all day;
I move so slow that green moss grows
on my shaggy fur, they say.

West

A little desert digger,
I stand up tall and thin;
I keep a sharp lookout for hawks
while my family digs within.
