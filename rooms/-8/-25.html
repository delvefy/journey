Woodlouse

You are a woodlouse - a tiny grey bug in a suit of overlapping plates, no bigger than a pea. You live in the dark and the damp, under stones and rotting logs, scuttling on more legs than anyone can count. Touch you and some of your kind curl up tight into a little armoured ball. Harmless and shy and oddly endearing, you trundle about the leaf-mould all your life, a tiny knight in grey armour, and children turn over every log just to watch you scuttle for cover.
