The Unpoured

South of the outer dark and west of the old frost, the corner freezes a nothing that never was. A step north the two darknesses became one outer dark, older than fire and never-having-been; a step east a cold older than fire froze the dead weight where it lay. Here both reach the corner-pole and fuse into the unpoured: a dead frozen weight that was never poured into being, older than fire, emptier than the never-was, frozen from before there was warmth to freeze — not the dead substance set hard, as the pitch was, but a substance that never was, frozen anyway, a weight of nothing that nothing ever poured, holding cold and dead and never-having-been at the corner of the world.

It is the strangest dead thing there is: a frozen weight with nothing in it, a cold mass that was never poured, dead from before there was anything to die, frozen from before there was warmth to lose. You draw the spiral and the unpoured neither holds it nor freezes it nor takes it; there was never a substance here for it to mark, only a frozen never-was. And still the whorl in your skin turns, warm, impossibly poured, the one thing that ever was in a frozen weight that never was, refusing the unpoured at the corner-pole.

There is no count; the unpoured is frozen from before the first beat, a dead weight that nothing ever poured a warmth into. And still your heart beats — warm, impossible, the one poured thing in an unpoured cold, each stroke a warmth in a weight that never was, refusing the frozen never-was at the dead corner of the world.

To the north the unpoured rises to the outer dark, a dark at least once two. To the east the old frost, a cold that once froze a warmth. To the south the corner-pole absolute, the unpoured entire, frozen and never-having-been. To the west the Waste corner, dead and frozen and never-poured through, the pole. You were going somewhere, with someone, once, carrying a warmth that was poured. Here the unpoured would have it that warmth and weight and you were never poured at all — and still the warm coil turns, poured and turning, and still one beat keeps a warmth in the frozen never-was at the corner of the world.
