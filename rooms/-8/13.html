Spray

You are the fine wet mist flung off the moving water - the crash of the wave on the rocks, the garden hose held to the thumb, the fountain on a windy day. No hand makes you; water broken into a thousand drops does. Stand near the falls, and I will hang in the air and settle on your skin like cool breath.

North

A round see-through ball of air in a film,
you blow them from soapy hoops;
they float and shimmer and drift away -
then pop in little loops.

East

The hard bright light that hurts your eyes -
the sun on snow at noon,
the headlamp shining in your face -
you squint and look down soon.

South

When a stone drops in the pond,
small rings spread out and grow;
the gentle wrinkle on the water -
outward, soft and slow.

West

A brownish-gold, the third-place shine,
of an old coin and a bell;
of autumn beech and a suntanned arm -
a metal's mellow spell.
