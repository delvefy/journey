The Never-Night

South of the never-was and west of the unseen, the nothing goes primal and dark. A step north was the never-was, the disenchantment pole where nothing has ever been; a step east the unseen, a dark that had at least once a dusk to forget. Here the two tilt together into a dark that never was — not a night fallen, not a light forgotten, but a primal old dark from before fire that was nonetheless never anything, the never-was gone to a never-night, unseen and forgotten and cold all in one, a darkness older than the first flame and emptier than the first absence, the un-night at the pole.

It is a dark with no light behind it and no light ahead, and, stranger, no past in which a light might have been — a night that never was lit because there was never anything to light, older than fire and emptier than dark. You draw the spiral and cannot see it and the never-night never could, having never had eyes or light or a thing to see; the mark is unseen in a dark that was never seen-able. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its turn, felt and warm, the one thing that is in a night that never was, refusing the un-night at the pole.

There is no count and never a clock and never a fire to mark an hour by; the never-night is older than the first flame and emptier than the first dark. And still your heart beats — warm, impossible, the one thing that ever was in a night that never was, each stroke a small fire in a dark from before fire, refusing the never-night, insisting, against everything, on having-been.

To the north the never-night rises to the never-was, a nothing with no dark to it, only grey. To the east the unseen, a dark that once had a dusk. To the south it deepens toward the primal pole, the un-night absolute, older than fire and never-having-been. To the west the Waste pole entire, the never-night and the never-was one dark, the corner. You were going somewhere, under a name, carrying a small warmth. Here the never-night would have it that night and you and the warmth all never were — and still the warm coil turns, felt in the dark that never was, and still one beat keeps a fire from before fire, refusing the un-night at the corner of the world.
