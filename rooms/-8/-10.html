Bull

You are the great male of the cattle, heavy and muscled, with a ring in your nose and horns on your head. No hand makes you; you snort and paw the ground, and woe to the one in red who steps in your field. You lead the herd; the cows and calves keep clear of your temper. Massive, fierce, and short-fused.

North

A woolly beast that says 'baa-baa'
and grazes on the hill;
the farmer shears its coat for wool -
it follows the flock at will.

East

A baby cat, all fluff and mischief,
that chases its own tail;
it mews, it pounces, climbs the curtains -
small, and soft, and frail.

South

A big fat bird with a wobbly red throat
that gobbles round the farm;
at Christmas time it's the dinner roast -
'gobble-gobble,' it sounds alarm.

West

A fluffy yellow baby bird
that paddles after its mum;
it cheeps and waddles down to the pond -
an ugly one turned handsome.
