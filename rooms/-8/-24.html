Slug

You are a slug - a soft slimy snail that lost its shell, oozing slow across the cool wet garden at night and leaving a silver trail that catches the morning light. No hand makes you; the damp dark earth does. You munch the lettuces and the gardener despairs. You shrink up small at a touch and slide on a foot of slime. Squidgy and shiny and gloriously gross, you are the garden's slowest traveller, in no hurry at all to be anywhere.
