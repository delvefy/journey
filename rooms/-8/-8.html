Ram

You are the male sheep, heavy and strong, with great curling horns on your head. No hand makes you; you grow wild and woolly, and you lower your head and charge - BUTT - to settle who is boss. You lead the flock; the ewes and lambs follow. Thick-fleeced, hard-headed, and proud, you are the horned ruler of the hillside field.

North

A big white bird with a long curved neck
that honks beside the pond;
it hisses, flaps, and chases you -
of nipping it is fond.

East

A faithful friend that wags its tail
and barks at every sound;
it fetches sticks, it licks your face -
the best pet to be found.

South

A woolly beast that says 'baa-baa'
and grazes on the hill;
the farmer shears its coat for wool -
it follows the flock at will.

West

A baby dog, all paws and ears,
that tumbles, chews, and plays;
it yaps and wags and wets the floor -
and grows up in a few short days.
