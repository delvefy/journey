Nest

You are a nest - the little cup of twigs and grass a bird builds high in the fork of a tree. You hold the speckled eggs through wind and rain, then the bald blind chicks that cheep for worms all day. You were woven beak by beak, with feathers and mud and stolen string. In autumn you sit empty, a brown tangle in the bare branches. You are home made by no hand, a basket the wild birds weave.
