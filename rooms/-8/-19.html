Wing

You are a wing - the feathered arm a bird beats to climb the air, spread wide to glide and tucked away to rest. A sparrow flickers you fast; an eagle holds you still and rides the wind. A moth and a bee and a dragonfly all wear a pair of you too. You are natural, grown not made, the thing that lifts a body off the ground. Fold and you are nothing; open and the whole sky is yours.
