Burp

You are a burp - the small rude bubble of air that climbs back up your throat after a fizzy drink and pops out with a sound. You are not a thing to keep; you are wind in a hurry to be free. Babies need a pat to bring you up; the bold let you boom and laugh. In some lands you are a thank-you to the cook. You are a tiny gust from the belly, here, loud, and gone.

A rumbling noise you make asleep,
that rattles in your nose;
it shakes the bed and wakes the dog -
a ___ as deep sleep grows.

East

A sharp bark from your tired throat,
when a tickle makes you choke;
you cover your mouth and let it out -
a ___, a rough little croak.

South

The harsh loud cry a parrot makes,
or a hen that's caught a fright;
it scrapes the air, all rough and shrill -
a ___ from morning to night.

West

You draw the air up through your nose,
to catch a smell or stop a drip;
a dog will do it on the ground -
a ___, a little sip.
