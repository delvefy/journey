Scuttle

You are a scuttle - to run with quick little legs, low and fast, hurrying for cover. The crab does you sideways across the sand; the beetle does you when its stone is lifted; the mouse does you along the skirting board the moment the light comes on. There is a scared, secret look to you, all hurry and no grace. You stop dead the instant you reach a hiding place. You are the fast low scramble of a small thing rushing out of sight.
