Pot

You are a pot - a deep round pan with two handles and a heavy lid. You sit on the stove and bubble away all afternoon, brewing the soup, the stew, the spaghetti; steam lifts your lid and fogs the windows and fills the house with a good smell. You also hold a plant on the sill, soil and roots and all. Cast and lidded by a hand, deep and sturdy, you are where slow good cooking quietly happens.
