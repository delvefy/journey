Toolbox

You are a toolbox - a metal case with a carry-handle and a lid that lifts to show trays of hammers and screwdrivers and odds and ends, all jumbled and oily and useful. Hands haul you out when something needs fixing and rummage through you for just the right tool. You rattle when you are carried and weigh a ton. Battered and heavy and full of clever things, you are the whole workshop shrunk into a box, ready to mend whatever has gone wrong.
