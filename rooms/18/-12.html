Drum

You are a drum - the round hollow barrel with a skin stretched tight across the top, that booms when you beat it. Two sticks, two hands, or one steady foot keep the beat the whole band marches to. You are the heart of the music, the thump you feel in your chest at the parade. Soldiers once kept step to you; children bang you with a wooden spoon. You make no tune, only time - the loud, plain pulse that everything else dances on.
