Screwdriver

You are a screwdriver - a handle with a steel shaft and a flat or crossed tip, made to turn a screw home or back it out. Righty-tighty, lefty-loosey: you grip the slot in the screw's head and twist, fixing the shelf, opening the toy to find why it stopped. Made by hands, simple and strong, you live in every toolbox and odd kitchen drawer. You are patience turned into a tool, the slow steady twist that fastens the made world together one screw at a time.
