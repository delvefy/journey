Bounce

You are a bounce - the happy springing-back of a ball that hits the ground and leaps up again, boing, boing, a little lower each time. No hand makes you, though a thrown ball will; you are what happens when something springy meets something hard and will not stay down. You cannot be held, only watched and counted. A trampoline gives you, a kangaroo lives by you, a dropped egg does not. You are the world refusing to let a thing simply stop.
