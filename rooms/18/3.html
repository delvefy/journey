Music

You are music - the lovely braided sound of drums and strings and voices, woven to please the ear. You set feet tapping and heads nodding; you turn a grey afternoon golden; you can make a person dance, or cry, or fall fast asleep. You live in pianos and whistled tunes and the hum of a happy worker. No hand can hold you - you are sound shaped into beauty, the most magical thing a person can make out of plain thin air.
