Deflate

You are a deflate - to let the air rush out and go from plump and firm to floppy and flat. The balloon you forgot deflates overnight into a sad little wrinkled scrap; the bouncy castle deflates with a long slow hiss when the pump switches off; a punctured tyre deflates with a sad whoosh. Saggy and shrinking and a bit of a let-down, you are the air going out of things, the slow sigh as something proud and round goes soft and limp and small.
