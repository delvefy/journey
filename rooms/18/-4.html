Box

You are a box - a square hollow thing with four flat sides and a lid that lifts. You hold the toys, the shoes, the cornflakes, the present wrapped in paper. Empty, you are even better: a fort, a boat, a rocket, a cave, a car. A cat will choose you over any cushion. Folded and glued by a hand, plain and useful, you are the simple square that keeps things tidy - and, turned upside down, becomes whatever a child can imagine.
