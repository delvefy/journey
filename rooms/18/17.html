Slump

You are a slump - the heavy sinking down of a body that has given up holding itself straight, shoulders rounding, head dropping, spine going soft against the chair. No one decides to do you; tiredness or gloom or a long boring lesson does. You are how a person folds when the day has been too much. Half a posture and half a mood, you are sitting up's surrender, the slow collapse of someone who would much rather be lying down.
