Moment

You are a moment - the smallest scrap of time a person really feels, gone almost before it comes: a blink, a heartbeat, a held breath. No hand made you; you are just now, the thin sliver of time that is happening this very instant. You cannot be touched, only noticed - wait a moment, just a moment, in a moment. A special moment is one you wish would last. You are now, slicing thinner and thinner, forever turning into was.
