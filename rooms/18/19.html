Hover

You are a hover - to hang in the air in one fixed place, wings or blades beating fast to hold you steady. A hummingbird does it at a flower, a kestrel does it over a field, a helicopter does it above a roof. You go neither up nor down nor along; you simply stay, balanced on busy wings, watching and waiting. Poised and patient and humming with effort, you are stillness in mid-air, the trick of staying exactly where you are while the whole world keeps moving below.
