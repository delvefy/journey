Knife

You are a knife - the blade with a handle, the sharpest helper in the house. You slice the bread, carve the roast, spread the butter, and peel the apple in one long curl. Beside the fork you wait at every dinner. Blunt, you are safe and useless; sharp, you are quick and a little dangerous, so a child is taught to hold you with care. You are the plain edge that cuts the world to pieces small enough to eat.
