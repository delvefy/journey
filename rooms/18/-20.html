Clamp

You are a clamp - a strong screw-tool with two jaws that you wind down tight to grip something and hold it dead still. Glue two bits of wood together and a clamp holds them squeezed until they set; saw a plank and a clamp pins it to the bench. You have no give once you are tightened home. Firm and patient and gripping hard, you are an extra strong hand that never tires, holding fast for as long as it takes while the worker's own two hands get on with the job.
