Swim

You are swim - to move through the water with arms and legs, head up, going where you please. The fish was born to do it; the dog does a clumsy paddle; the child learns it with armbands in the shallow end. You can do a splashy doggy-paddle, a smooth front crawl, or float lazily on your back. Without it, water is danger; with it, water is play. You are how a body crosses the deep, the trick that turns drowning into a game.
