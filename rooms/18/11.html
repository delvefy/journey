Coo

You are a coo - the soft, round, low sound a pigeon or a dove makes deep down in its throat. You are gentle and content, a sleepy bubbling note that says all is calm. A baby does you too, snug and warm in someone's arms, and a fond grown-up does you back. You cannot be touched, only heard and answered. You are the most peaceful little noise there is, the murmur of comfort that needs no words at all.
