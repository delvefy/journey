Dangle

You are a dangle - the loose swinging hang of a thing held only at the top, legs off a high wall, a spider on its thread, a conker on a string. No one props you up; gravity and one fixed point do. You sway and twirl and bob with every breeze and bump. A carrot dangled keeps a donkey walking. Loose and swinging and never quite still, you are the happy in-between of hanging on and letting go.
