Tile

You are a tile - a flat hard square of baked clay or stone, made to be laid down side by side until a whole surface is covered. A hand fired you and a hand sets you, grouting the lines between, on the bathroom wall, the kitchen floor, the sloping roof. You can be held, stacked, walked on, cracked if dropped. Cool and smooth and easy to wipe, you join with your fellows into a neat checkered skin. You are how a surface is made wipe-clean and whole.
