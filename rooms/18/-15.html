Yarn

You are yarn - the long thick thread of spun wool, wound up into a fat soft ball. The knitter pulls you out loop by loop and clicks you into a jumper, a scarf, a pair of socks. The kitten loves to bat you across the floor and tangle you into a hopeless knot. You come in every colour and smell faintly of sheep. You wait in the basket between projects. You are warmth not yet made, a whole woolly winter wound up and waiting on two needles.
