Spade

You are a spade - a flat sharp blade on a sturdy handle, made for digging. Press me down with a boot, lever back, and up comes a wedge of dark earth; on you go, hole by hole, planting the bulbs or burying the time capsule. A child digs to the centre of the world with a smaller one on the beach. Forged and fitted by a hand, you are how the ground is opened up - one good footful of soil at a time.
