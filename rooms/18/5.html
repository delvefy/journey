Pat

You are a pat - a soft light tap with the flat of the hand. Pat-pat, gentle and kind, you pat the dog hello, you pat a friend on the back to say well done, you pat the baby off to sleep, you pat the sand into a smooth firm castle. There is nothing rough in you at all. No hand can hold you, for you are only the touch itself - the kindest, lightest little tap there is, given to comfort and to praise.
