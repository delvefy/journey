Spanner

You are a spanner - a bar of hard steel with a notched jaw that grips a nut just so. Slip me on, give a good firm pull, and the bolt turns and tightens, or loosens and comes free. You build the bike, you fix the leaky pipe, you live in the toolbox with your many-sized brothers. Forged and shaped by a hand, cold and heavy in the grip, you are how things made of metal are screwed up tight and held.
