Rhyme

You are a rhyme - the chime of two words that finish on the same sound. Cat and hat, star and far, wall and fall: you ring at the ends of lines like a pair of little bells, and you make a verse stick fast in the head. You live in nursery songs, in skipping chants, in the rhymes told in the playground. No hand can hold you - you are a match of sounds, the trick that makes words dance in pairs and never quite be forgotten.

you dance and sing along to it -
the ___ a band can make.

East

A happy shout you give your team,
'hooray!' and 'go!' and 'yay!';
the whole crowd roars when they score a goal -
a ___ to win the day.

South

A story told of long ago,
of heroes, kings, and knights;
you read it by the fire at night -
a ___ of grand old sights.

West

Words set to music, sung out loud,
with a chorus all can share;
you belt it out, you tap your feet -
a ___ fills the air.
