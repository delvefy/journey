Tale

You are a tale - a story told, the older and grander the better. You begin 'long, long ago' and carry the listener off to castles and dragons and dark deep woods, to clever youngest sons and wishes that come true with a sting. You are passed from grandparent to child by the fire, changing a little with every telling. No hand can hold you - you live only in the telling, a whole bright world conjured out of nothing but words.
