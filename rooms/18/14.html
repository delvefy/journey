Magic

You are magic - the impossible made to happen, the rules of the world bent for one bright moment. You are the rabbit pulled from an empty hat, the coin found behind an ear, the wish that somehow comes true. You live in spells and fairy tales, in birthday candles and crossed fingers. No one can quite explain you, and that is the whole point: you are wonder with its sleeves rolled up, daring the world to be more than it is.
