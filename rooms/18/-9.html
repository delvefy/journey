Shirt

You are a shirt - the cloth garment you pull on over your top half, with sleeves for your arms, a collar at the neck, and buttons down the front. A hand cut and stitched you; a hand does up your buttons in the morning, often one out of line. You can be worn, washed, ironed, tucked in or left hanging out. Striped or plain, smart or scruffy, you cover the chest and back and keep a person decent. You are the everyday clothing of the upper half.
