Wrench

You are a wrench - a heavy metal tool with jaws shaped to grip a nut and turn it. Slot it on, heave the handle round, and a stubborn bolt squeaks loose or pulls up tight. Mechanics keep a whole row of you in different sizes, hung on the garage wall. Hard and cold and strong in the hand, you give a person the extra muscle to undo what fingers never could, and you keep the wheels and engines and bicycles of the world bolted firmly together.
