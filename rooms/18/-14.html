Trombone

You are a trombone - the big brass horn with the long sliding arm. You blow into one end and push the slide out and in to hunt the note, low and golden and a little cheeky. In the band you sit beside the trumpets and blare out the deep parts, sliding from a groan up to a honk. You are the funniest brass of all, all elbow and breath, the sound of a marching parade going by.
