Ball

You are a ball - a round bouncy thing, the heart of half the games there are. You get kicked into the goal, thrown and caught, bounced sky-high, rolled along the floor for a baby. Footballs, tennis balls, beach balls, ping-pong balls: round all over so you'll roll any way at all. Stitched or moulded by a hand, springy and true, you are the simple sphere that turns an empty field and a free afternoon into a game.
