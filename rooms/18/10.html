Tomorrow

You are tomorrow - the day that has not come yet, always just one sleep ahead, forever arriving and never quite here. People put off their chores for you and pin their hopes on you. When you finally come you have quietly become today, and a new tomorrow waits beyond. You cannot be touched or saved, only promised and planned for. You are a thing people made out of counting days - a name for the part of time no one has reached.
