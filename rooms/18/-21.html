Anvil

You are an anvil - a great heavy block of iron, flat on top with a pointed horn at one end, that a blacksmith hammers hot metal against. Glowing orange iron is laid across you and beaten into shape, sparks flying with every ringing blow. You do not budge an inch. Solid and immovable and ringing under the hammer, you are the blacksmith's workbench and partner both, taking blow after blow without complaint while the red-hot metal is shaped against your sturdy face.
