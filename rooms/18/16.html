Squirm

You are a squirm - the twisting wriggling fuss of a body that cannot keep still. The toddler does you to escape a hug, the worm does you on the hook, you do it when the assembly drags on and on. No hand holds you; the restless body makes you, writhing and turning and sliding free. Half discomfort, half escape, you are the wiggle of someone who wants to be somewhere else. You are stillness losing the fight, the body's small rebellion against sitting still.
