Sag

You are a sag - the slow droop of something giving way in the middle under a load. The clothes line sags with the weight of the wet washing; the old shelf sags under its row of heavy books; the worn-out sofa sags where everyone has sat. You do not snap or break; you just dip and bow and give a little. Tired and heavy and slowly bending, you are what happens when something has held a weight too long, sinking gently in the middle until somebody props it up again.
