Shiver

You are a shiver - the quick run of shaking that ripples through a body that is cold or afraid. The wind cuts through a wet coat and you go skittering down the spine; a scary story sets the small hairs up and brings you on. You cannot be held or seen, only felt, gone almost before it comes. Teeth chatter and shoulders hunch when you arrive. You are the body's own little earthquake, a shudder the cold or the fright shakes loose.
