Brain

You are the brain - the soft grey wrinkled organ folded up inside the skull, busy every second of your life. You do the thinking, the remembering, the dreaming; you work the fingers and read these very words. Asleep or awake you never switch off, humming away in the dark behind the eyes. Pink-grey and crinkled like a walnut, you weigh hardly anything, yet you hold a whole person's worth of thoughts. You are the quiet boss of the body, hidden in its bony helmet.
