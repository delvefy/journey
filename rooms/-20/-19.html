Harbour

You are a harbour - a sheltered pocket of calm water, ringed by strong stone walls that hold back the wild open sea. Fishing boats and yachts bob at anchor in your stillness, ropes creaking, gulls wheeling overhead. When a storm blows up, every boat hurries home to you. Safe and busy and full of bobbing masts, you are the place where the sea is tamed, where boats rest from their voyages and tie up snug behind your sturdy walls.
