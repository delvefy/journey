Stream

You are a stream - a little narrow run of water, thin enough to leap in a single hop, that chuckles down over shiny stones on its way to join a bigger river. No hand digs you; the hill and the rain do. Children dam you with pebbles and float sticks down you and dip their toes in your cold. Minnows hang in your clear shallows. Small and quick and forever talking to itself, you are the river when it is young, hurrying happily downhill.
