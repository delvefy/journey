Rapids

You are the rapids - the wild stretch of a river where the water speeds up and crashes white over hidden rocks, bucking and foaming and roaring. No hand makes you; a steep drop and a rocky bed do. Canoeists shoot you with whoops and screams, paddles flying, hearts in mouths. You soak everything and spit it out downstream. Loud and frothing and fast, you are the river at its rowdiest, the part nobody floats through quietly.
