Nut

You are a nut - a hard little shell with a tasty kernel hidden inside, grown on a tree and dropped in autumn: an acorn, a walnut, a hazelnut, a conker. No hand made you; the tree did, wrapping its seed in armour so tough you need a cracker or a strong squirrel's tooth to get in. You can be gathered, hoarded, roasted, cracked. The squirrel buries you and forgets, and from the forgotten ones whole new trees grow. You are a seed in a strongbox.
