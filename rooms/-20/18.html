Slop

You are a slop - liquid sloshing about and spilling messily over the rim. Carry a brimming bucket and it slops down your legs; tip the soup and it slops over the bowl; the dog's water bowl slops across the kitchen floor. You are clumsy and wet and always leave a puddle behind. Sloshy and sloppy and a bit of a nuisance, you are what happens when a too-full container is jostled, the careless spill that has somebody reaching straight away for a cloth.
