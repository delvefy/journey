Surf

You are surf - the white foamy water of the waves as they break and rush up the beach, then drag hissing back over the sand. No hand makes you; the sea does, heaving its waves up until they topple and froth. You cannot be held, only run through, jumped over, ridden on a board. You roar and crash all day and all night, the loud edge where the ocean meets the land. You are the sea in its frothy, tumbling, never-still excitement.
