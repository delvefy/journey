Web

You are a web - the fine silk net a spider spins out of its own body and hangs in a corner or across the grass. At dawn the dew strings little beads along you and you glitter. A fly blunders in and sticks, and the spider feels you tremble and comes running. You are no bigger than a hand yet stronger, thread for thread, than steel. You are a trap made of patience and silk, the spider's whole larder hung on the air.
