Sob

You are a sob - the deep, broken cry that shakes the whole chest when the sadness is too big to hold. You come in gulps and hiccups, half a breath swallowed, half let go. A child does you into a parent's shoulder; a grown-up hides you behind a door. You cannot be touched, only heard and felt. You are the sound grief makes when it spills over, the cry that comes from somewhere deeper than tears.
