Sprinkle

You are a sprinkle - a light quick scatter of little drops or grains, the first few spots of rain, the dusting of sugar over a cake, the seeds shaken out across the soil. No heavy hand makes you; a gentle shake does. You fall not in a pour but in a soft handful, here and there, dotting the path or the icing. Light and even and pleasant, you are giving-out done kindly, just enough and never too much, scattered thin across whatever waits below.
