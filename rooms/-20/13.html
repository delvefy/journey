Ray

You are a ray - a single straight line of light, shooting out from the sun or a lamp or a torch in the dark. You break through the cloud in a golden bar, you slip under the door, you stripe the dusty air of an attic. No hand makes you; the light does, travelling dead straight until something stops it. You carry the warmth and the brightness from there to here in an instant. You are light drawn out into a line, the arrow the sun shoots down.
