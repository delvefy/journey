Shoulder

You are a shoulder - the broad strong joint where the arm meets the body, one on each side, just below the neck. No hand made you; you grew, and you can lift, carry, shrug, and bear. You can be tapped to get attention, cried on in sorrow, ridden upon by a small child to see over the crowd. You shrug to say I don't know, and you put your shoulder to a heavy door. Steady and strong, you are where the arm hangs and the burden rests.
