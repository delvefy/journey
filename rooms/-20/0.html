Chalk

You are chalk - the soft white rock you can crumble between your fingers and draw with, dug from the great white cliffs by the sea. No hand made you; tiny sea creatures did, their shells pressed down over millions of years into soft white stone. You can be held, snapped, scraped on the pavement to draw a hopscotch grid, squeaked maddeningly down a board. You wash away in the first rain. You are the earth turned into a crayon, soft enough to write the world in white.
