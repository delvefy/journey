Cloud

You are a cloud - a great soft heap of floating water-mist, drifting slow across the blue, white and fluffy or dark and heavy with rain. No hand makes you; the sun lifts water from the sea, and high and cold it gathers into you. You cannot be touched - fly through one in a plane and it is only damp fog. You change shape as you go: a dragon, a sheep, a face. You bring the rain and hide the sun, and you sail the sky like a slow white ship.
