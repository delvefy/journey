Petal

You are a petal - one of the soft coloured leaves that ring the heart of a flower, made to dazzle the bee and the eye. Red, pink, yellow, white, you spread wide in the sun and curl shut at night. 'Loves me, loves me not,' a daisy's are plucked one by one. When the bloom grows old you loosen and drift to the ground, a fallen scrap of colour. You are the flower's bright soft dress, the prettiest part of the plant.
