Shadow

You are a shadow - the dark twin the sun throws down behind whatever stands in its light. You have no weight and no colour, only a shape, stretched long at evening and stamped short at noon. You copy every move yet you cannot be caught or held. Cloud over the sun and you vanish; step into the dark and you are everywhere at once. You are the cool grey ghost that the bright day cannot help but cast.
