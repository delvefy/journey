Blink

You are a blink - the quick wet shut and open of the eyelids, faster than the eye can catch. You do it a dozen times a minute without a thought, wiping the eyes clean and keeping them moist. 'In the blink of an eye,' they say, to mean no time at all. Surprise can make you skip one; tears can make you flutter many. You are the body's tiniest curtain-drop, the eye's own little wink at the world, over before it begins.
