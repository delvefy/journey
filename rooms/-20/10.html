Thunder

You are thunder - the great rolling boom that follows the lightning's flash, the loud voice of the storm. You growl far off, then crack right overhead, rattling the windows and sending dogs under the bed. You are only the sound of the air slammed apart by the lightning, but you sound like a giant rolling barrels across the sky. Children count the seconds between flash and you to tell how far the storm is. You are the sky's mighty, frightening drumroll.
