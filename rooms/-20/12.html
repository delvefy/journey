Gleam

You are a gleam - a soft bright dab of light that bounces off something smooth. The coin gleams in the gutter; the wet road gleams under the streetlamp; the clean window gleams when the sun finds it. You are smaller than a blaze and steadier than a sparkle, just one clean catch of light that draws the eye. A polished thing wears you proudly. You are light reflected and gathered to a point, the bright wink a surface gives back to the sun.
