Noon

You are noon - the very middle of the day, when the sun climbs to the top of the sky and stands there a moment, and shadows pull in tight beneath your feet. The clock points straight up; the church bell counts to twelve. You are dinner time and the brightest hour, the hinge the whole day turns on, when morning tips over at last into afternoon. Bright, high, and exactly halfway - that is you.
