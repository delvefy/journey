Splatter

You are a splatter - liquid flung out in a spray of scattered drops, spotting everything around. Flick a loaded paintbrush and the colour splatters the wall; drop a ripe tomato and it splatters the floor; a passing car splatters muddy puddle-water up your legs. The drops land everywhere, in a random spreading mess. Sudden and messy and impossible to aim, you are liquid thrown wide in a burst of flying spots, leaving its scattered mark all over the place.
