Eyelash

You are an eyelash - one of the tiny curved hairs that fringe the rim of the eye. You stand in a row to keep the dust and the bright out, fluttering down each time the eye shuts. When one of you falls loose onto a cheek, a child lifts you on a fingertip, makes a wish, and blows you away. Small, soft, and curling, you are the gentlest guard in the whole face.
