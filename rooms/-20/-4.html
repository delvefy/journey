Apple

You are an apple - a round firm fruit, red or green or gold, that grows on a tree and crunches when you bite it. No hand made you; a blossom did, swelling all summer from flower to fruit. You can be picked, polished on a sleeve, bobbed for in a tub, baked in a pie, pressed into juice. An apple a day keeps the doctor away, they say, and one fell on Newton's head. You are the plainest, cheeriest fruit of all, sweet and sharp and crisp.
