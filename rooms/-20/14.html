Cold

You are cold - the chill with no warmth in it, the bite of the air that creeps under the coat and into the bones and sets the teeth chattering. No hand makes you; the loss of heat does, the night and the winter and the open freezer door. You numb the fingers and fog the breath and send everyone running for blankets. The plain opposite of warm, you are what is left when the heat has gone, and the body's first complaint against the dark months.
