Brook

You are a brook - a small bright stream no wider than a stride, hurrying clear and cold over its bed of pebbles. You babble and chatter to yourself all day, winking in the sun, with little fish darting in your shallows and ferns leaning over your banks. Children dam you with stones and float twig boats down you. Cheerful and busy and always moving, you trickle down from the hills to join a bigger river at last, singing your small song the whole way down.
