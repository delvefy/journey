Bin

You are a bin - the open-mouthed pail that swallows everything no one wants. Apple cores, torn paper, the peel of an orange, all tossed in and forgotten. You wear a lid to keep the smell down and a bag inside to be lifted out. Each week you are dragged to the kerb and emptied with a clatter. You are not pretty, but a house would drown in its own mess without you, the patient keeper of all our throwing-away.
