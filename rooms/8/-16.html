Rattle

You are a rattle - the baby's first toy, a little ball of noise on a handle. Tucked in a fist and shaken, you clatter and brr-brr and tinkle, and a baby stares at you wide-eyed, delighted to learn that a shake makes a sound. You are beads in a drum, a bell in a ring, a happy racket made to be made. Moulded bright and chunky by a hand, you are the very first instrument anyone ever plays.
