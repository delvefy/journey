Bang

You are the loudest, most sudden sound there is, the slammed door, the burst balloon, the firework's flat hard crack against the night. You come without warning and you are over in an instant, leaving the ears ringing and the heart jumping. The gun makes you, the drum, the dropped tray, the thunder right overhead. Children love you and dread you in the same breath. You are noise at its sharpest and shortest - no music, no meaning, just the pure loud shock of something happening, all at once and now.

North

Two fingers squeeze a fold of skin,
a quick and stinging nip;
of salt a little, held within,
or held in finger's grip.

East

A small high note a bell will ring,
one bright clear sound, then gone;
the lift, the till, the bicycle's spring -
that little chime moves on.

South

A long slow breath let softly out,
when tired or sad or bored;
'oh well,' it says without a shout,
a weariness outpoured.

West

Rap your knuckles on the door,
tap-tap, 'is someone in?';
the sound is heard, then heard once more,
to ask to be let in.
