Test

You are a test - the row of questions set to find out what you know. The room goes quiet, the pencils move, and you wait to be passed or failed. A teacher sets you; a driver sits you; a doctor runs one to be sure. You are made of questions and a marking scheme, a thing built to measure. You cannot be touched, only taken and scored. Pass and the door opens; fail and you may sit again.
