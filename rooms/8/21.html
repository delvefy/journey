Sum

You are a sum - the answer you get when you add the numbers up. Two and two make a sum of four; the whole shopping bill is a sum. You are done with a pencil, on fingers, or in the head, and the teacher marks you right or wrong with a tick or a cross. Add, and you grow; the total at the bottom is you. You are arithmetic's plain question and plain answer, the adding-up that says how many in all.
