Broom

You are a broom - a bundle of stiff bristles or twigs bound to a long wooden handle, made for sweeping the floor clean. A hand made you, a hand pushes you back and forth to chase the dust and crumbs into a heap. You can be held, leaned in the corner, ridden by a witch in a tale. Older than the vacuum by centuries, you ask for no electricity, only a willing arm. You are the simple swish that keeps the whole house tidy, one push at a time.
