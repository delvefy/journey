Double

You are double - twice as much, the same amount counted out again. Double four is eight; a double scoop is two scoops; a double bed sleeps two. You take whatever there is and you make it twice the size. Children love you at the ice-cream van and dread you in their sums. Big and generous and easy to understand, you are the simplest sort of growing: just take what you have and have it all over again, and there you are with twice as much as before.
