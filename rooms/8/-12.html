Robot

You are the metal person built to work - blinking lights, whirring gears, a flat voice that does as it is told. Made by clever hands, you weld the cars, sweep the floors, walk on the moon. You never tire, never grumble. Press my button, and I beep to life and set about the task, tireless and strange.

North

A little roof upon a stick,
with ribs and a stretchy skin;
you put it up against the rain -
to keep the wet from getting in.

East

You wear it on your face to hide,
at parties or on stage;
a monster, a hero, a cat, a clown -
pull it on, change your age.

South

A shiny brass horn you blow and toot,
with three small keys to press;
it blares out loud across the band -
a golden noisy mess.

West

A small folding case for notes and coins,
kept safe inside a pocket;
cards in its slots, it snaps shut tight -
your money's little locket.
