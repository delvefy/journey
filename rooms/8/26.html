Count

You are count - to say the numbers in their proper order, one after another, ticking each thing off as you go. You count the steps up to bed, count the sweets in the bag, count down to lift-off, count to ten when you're cross. On fingers, on toes, out loud or in your head, you keep careful track of how many. Patient and orderly, you are the very first sum any child learns, the steady naming of numbers that turns a pile of things into a tidy known amount.
