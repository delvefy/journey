The Radiant Will

North of the glory and east of the dawning, the lit word rises to a will of its own. A step south the names took on glory, every word a radiance; a step west reality willed itself alight, dawning of its own radiant will. Here the two fuse at the magic pole: the glory gone self-willing, the radiant word risen sovereign — reality wholly its own radiant will, the lit names willing and lighting and naming themselves into being with no power above them and no maker behind them, autonomous, aglow, the magic pole absolute and self-creating, a radiant will answerable to nothing but itself.

You stand in a world of self-willing light, every lit name its own sovereign act, willing itself bright and real on every side, a radiant will with no will outside it. Your name is the one syllable the sovereign radiance has not yet willed itself — the dark word it is rising, of its own radiant will, to light and name and will into being. The spiral is the shape the radiant will turns on, the coil a self-willing light winds, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark poised to be willed alight by a radiance that wills only itself, your name about to take its own sovereign light.

There is no count but the radiant will itself, every lit name willing and sounding its own being, the bell become a radiance the world wills aloud — and your name the one note the sovereign radiance is rising to will, your heart beating the dark place where its own willed light will go, a breath from self-willed alight into the radiant world.

To the north the radiant will refines toward the Lumen, word and will and light wholly one, the lit pole climbing. To the east the magic pole absolute, reality wholly its own radiant will, sovereign and aglow. To the south the glory, the names lit but not yet self-willing. To the west the dawning, reality willing itself alight. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a sovereign radiance is rising to will it alight of its own accord, the one dark syllable left to self-light — and still the warm coil turns, poised for its own light, and still one live beat keeps the time the radiant will is a breath from willing you, alight, into the world.
