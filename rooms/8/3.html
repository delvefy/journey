Sigh

You are the long breath let slowly out, the body's quiet comment when words will not do. Weariness breathes you, relief breathes you, the patient lover waiting breathes you; so does the child made to tidy the room. 'Oh well,' you seem to say, or 'at last', or 'if only'. You empty the chest of more than air - a little of the day's weight goes out with you each time. Half sorrow and half release, you ask for nothing and fix nothing, yet somehow the heart is the lighter for having let you go.

North

A sudden loud and crashing noise,
the slam, the drum, the gun;
it startles all the girls and boys,
the loudest sound, bar none.

East

A small sharp snap the switch will make,
the latch, the pen, the key;
a tiny tap, a quick light break,
the lightest sound to be.

South

One, two, three, and four, and five,
I count out everything;
the days, the cost, the sheep alive,
how much, is what I bring.

West

A soft warm press of lip to cheek,
goodnight, goodbye, hello;
no word at all, yet love I speak,
the sweetest sign I know.
