The Named

East of the spoken world, reality is nothing but its names. A step west the world was held in being by the old names spoken on every side, a saying that kept the hills and gods and dead alike from slipping out of existence. Here even the keeping falls away, because there is no longer anything underneath to keep: this is the magic pole entire, reality wholly consensus, a world made of nothing but the names that hold it, weightless, substanceless, pure word. There is no matter beneath the saying. There never was. To be, here, is simply to be named — and everything is named, and so everything is.

You move through a world with no floor but its own naming, and it holds, because here naming is enough, is all, is the only kind of being there is. The gods are names that walk; the dead are names that stay; you are a name among the names, and the all-saying says you with the rest. Your name is not held back to be bestowed now, as the elders held it — it is simply the one syllable of the all-saying not yet quite sounded, the last unspoken word in a world that is nothing but words. The spiral is the shape every name turns on, the coil the saying makes to hold a thing in being, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark that is your name's own and waits only to be sounded.

There is no count but the all-saying itself, every name sounded and re-sounded to keep its thing in being, the world a single unbroken word holding everything aloft — and your name the one note not yet sounded in it, your heart beating the place where that note will go, a breath from said, a breath from wholly, namedly, real.

To the north the all-saying takes on craft and light, the names given engines, the magitech. To the east the magic pole absolute beyond even this, names without anything left to name, the saying its own whole world. To the south the saying goes wild, root and horn and blood, the names grown feral. To the west the spoken world, reality held by saying over a world that still half-remembers having weight. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here you are a name among names in a world that is nothing else, the all-saying gathered to sound the one syllable left — and still the warm coil turns, your name's own shape, and still one beat keeps the place the word is a breath from speaking you wholly into.
