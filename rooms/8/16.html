Rhyme

You are a rhyme - the happy chime of two words that end the same. Cat and hat, moon and spoon, wall and fall: you make the ends of lines ring like little bells and stick a poem fast in the memory. You live in nursery songs and skipping games and the jokes told in the playground. No hand can hold you - you are a matching of sounds, the trick that makes words dance two by two and never be forgotten.
