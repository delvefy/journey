The Glory

North of the stilled engine and east of the shaping light, the names take on glory. A step south a wrought engine stood still in a world of pure word, craft needless; a step west the powers wielded a world of light, reality bending bright at their will. Here the all-saying itself catches fire with radiance: the names given glory, every word a light, reality wholly the powers' to wield and wholly aglow, the magic pole climbing into the Lumen. The named world, which was weightless word, now shines — word and radiance one, a glory where to say a thing is to light it and to light it is to say.

You stand in a world made of luminous names, every saying a radiance, the gods' word blazing softly in the air on every side, holding all things in a glory of lit word. Your name is among the lit names, the one syllable not yet kindled to its glory, the dark word the radiance is rising to speak alight. The spiral is the shape the lit names turn on, the coil a glorious word winds, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark waiting to be said and lit at once, kindled into the shining all-saying, your name about to take its glory.

There is no count but the glory itself, every lit name sounding and shining to keep its thing aglow, the bell become a radiance the named world rings — and your name the one note not yet kindled, your heart beating the dark place where its glory will go, a breath from said and lit into the shining names of everything.

To the north the glory refines toward the Lumen, word and light wholly one, the lit pole climbing. To the east the magic pole absolute, reality wholly the powers' to wield, the names sovereign. To the south the stilled engine, a wrought craft at rest in the word. To the west the shaping light, the powers wielding a world bright. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the lit names are gathered to kindle it, the one dark syllable the glory is rising to speak alight — and still the warm coil turns, waiting its glory, and still one live beat keeps the time the shining names are a breath from saying you alight.
