Pyjamas

You are pyjamas - the soft warm two-piece suit, a loose top and matching bottoms, pulled on at the end of the day for sleeping and lounging and not much else. Hands print you all over with stars or stripes or dinosaurs. You go on warm from the radiator on a winter night. You are worn all day on a poorly day off school. Cosy and slouchy and only for indoors, you are the comfiest clothes anyone owns, the uniform of bedtime and the morning lie-in.
