Slipper

You are a slipper - a soft squashy shoe with no laces, made just for padding about the house. You wait by the bed for cold morning feet and slide on warm and snug. Some of you are shaped like rabbits or have a fluffy lining; all of you are too soft to wear outside. Cosy and worn and a little bit floppy, you are the comfiest thing on a foot, the gentle shuffle of a lazy Sunday, kicked off at the door and lost forever under the sofa.
