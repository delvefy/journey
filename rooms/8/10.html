Punch

You are a hard hit with the closed fist, swung straight from the shoulder - the boxer's blow, the angry lash-out, the thump that lands with a THWACK. No hand-but-a-fist makes you; the arm drives you out fast and heavy. You knock things flat. There's a sweet fruit-punch too, but this is the fierce kind.

North

Pull back your arm and let it fly -
the ball goes sailing high;
you do it to a stick for the dog,
or a stone to skim and shy.

East

The low deep rumble a dog gives off
when it bares its teeth to warn;
a bear, a tum, a cross old man -
'grrr', from deep down drawn.

South

Bring your foot down hard and flat,
BANG, upon the floor;
you do it when you're cross and loud -
it shakes the room and more.

West

A loud high shriek that splits the ears
when you are scared half to death;
'AAAH!' you cry on the ghost-train's plunge -
it empties out your breath.
