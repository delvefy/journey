The Dreamt

South of the feral names and east of the green dream, you are wholly dreamt. A step north the all-saying went feral, names of root and horn and blood; a step west the world dreamed itself sourceless, no dreamer apart from the dreaming. Here the dream goes absolute: the Wyld pole entire, reality wholly a dream with no edge and no waking, older than the gods and older than the word — a dreaming so total that there is no telling dreamer from dream from dreamt, and you are not in the dream but of it, dreamt through and through, one of the things the oldest wild dreams and is and cannot wake from.

There is no outside to it, no part of you the dream does not reach, no edge where you end and the dreaming begins. You are dreamt the way the root and the rain and the slow dark are dreamt, wholly and from within, named not by any word but by the wild's dreaming of you. Your name is the dream's own dreaming of you, older than any name a god could speak, a wild-truth dreamt and not said. The spiral is the turn the deep dream takes, the coil of a sleeping wild that dreams the world, the same whorl in your skin, here the seal the oldest dream knows its dreamt things by — and you are one, dreamt true, almost wholly dreamed into the world.

There is no count, only the deep dream's slow sourceless breath, the oldest wild dreaming and being on one rhythm with no waking to break it — and your heart beats with it, dreamt onto the breath of a dream that has no dreamer and no edge, your name a thing the wild is about to dream wholly true, without ever a word, without ever waking.

To the north the deep dream firms to the feral names, the dreaming roared into wild naming. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, reality wholly the dream, dreamt to the end. To the south it sinks toward the oldest green, the dream before the gods, the world before the word, the deep Wyld. To the west the green dream, the world dreaming itself with a faint edge yet. You were going somewhere, under a name, as someone who might wake. Here you are wholly dreamt, no edge and no waking, the oldest wild dreaming you true — and still the warm coil turns, the dreamt seal, and still one beat keeps the breath the deep dream is about to dream you wholly into, without a word.
