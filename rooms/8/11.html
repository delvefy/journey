Throw

Arm back, then forward, and the thing leaves your hand for the air - to throw is to give something to distance. A ball to a friend, a stone across a pond, a stick for a dog that will bring it racing back. For a moment it is no longer yours; it belongs to the arc and the air and wherever it lands. You cannot hold a throw; it is the very act of letting go, aimed.

North

When a ball comes flying,
open your two hands wide;
snap them shut around it
so it stays inside.

East

The soft warm rumble
a cat makes on your knee;
it means it's glad and cosy
and as calm as can be.

South

A blow with a closed fist,
struck quick and hard and tight;
a boxer's heavy swing
in the middle of a fight.

West

A long sad cry,
loud and full of woe;
a baby in the night,
or a siren's rising flow.
