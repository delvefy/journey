Kick

You are a sudden strike of the foot - the boot to the ball, the swimmer's churn, the mule's quick lash of the hind legs. No hand makes you; the leg makes you, swift and strong. You send the football flying, you thrash in temper, you swim. Quick and sharp and over in an instant, you are the foot let fly.

North

Bring your foot down hard and flat,
BANG, upon the floor;
you do it when you're cross and loud -
it shakes the room and more.

East

A smack with the flat of an open hand,
SMACK upon a cheek or back;
it stings and leaves a red mark there -
a quick and open whack.

South

A long deep moan of weary woe -
'ohhh,' you let it out;
too tired, too sore, too sick of it -
a grumble, not a shout.

West

A bubble of air from a fizzy drink
comes climbing up your throat;
'BURRP!' it bursts out at the table -
rude as a billy goat.
