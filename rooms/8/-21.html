Blanket

You are a blanket - the thick warm cloth that lies on top of the bed and keeps the sleeper cosy through the cold night. Pulled up to the chin, you shut out the chill; thrown over two chairs, you make a den; spread on the grass, you hold the picnic. A baby clings to a favourite one for comfort. Wool or fleece or patchwork, you are the soft warm weight that turns a cold bed into a snug one. You are warmth you can wrap around yourself.
