Stamp

You are the hard bang of a foot brought down flat upon the floor - in temper, in time to a marching band, to knock the snow off a boot. No hand makes you; the foot does it, BANG, shaking the floorboards. A cross child does you; a soldier does you, all together, BOOM. You are the thump of the angry or marching foot.

North

A hard hit with a tight closed fist,
swung straight from the shoulder out;
the boxer throws it in the ring -
THWACK, and a knockout, no doubt.

East

The long loud wail a wolf sends up
at the round and rising moon;
the wind down the chimney does it too -
'ahooooo,' a mournful tune.

South

A sudden strike with the swinging foot -
the football flies away;
the mule lets fly with its back legs -
swift and sharp in play.

West

Too cross for words, too loud to quell,
you puff, you stamp, your face goes red;
you open wide your mouth and - well,
the angry noise you make is ___.
