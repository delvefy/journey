Guitar

You are a guitar - a wooden body with a round dark hole, a long neck strung with six tight strings. Cradle me in your arms and strum, and the air fills with music; press the strings and the notes climb and fall. Made by skilled hands from curved wood and wire, you are shaped like a smooth, fat number eight. Pluck me, and a song comes spilling out into the room.

North

A shining brass horn that you blow,
with three small buttons on top;
press them down and blow out loud -
a blast that will not stop.

East

A big black iron cooking pot
that hangs above the flame;
the witch stirs bubbling potions here -
'double, double' is her game.

South

A little tube of metal or plastic,
you blow it hard and shrill;
the referee gives a sharp loud 'peep!'
and the players all stand still.

West

A stiff flat rectangle of paper,
sent to say 'get well' or 'hooray';
or one of a deck of fifty-two
the players deal and play.
