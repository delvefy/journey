Catch

You are the snatch of a thing from the air - the ball into cupped hands, the falling glass saved at the last, the dog leaping for the stick. No still thing makes you; quick eyes and quick hands do. Throw's twin and answer. Hold your palms ready, and what flies toward you stops, safe, in your grip.

North

Jump aside to miss the ball,
quick, before it hits;
you duck and weave and slip away -
the game of quickest wits.

East

The sound the cat makes at the door,
to ask for milk or in;
a soft high call, 'mee-ow, mee-ow' -
it rubs against your shin.

South

Draw back your arm and let it go,
the ball flies through the air;
you hurl it far across the park -
for the dog to chase with care.

West

The short sharp cry a dog lets out
when its tail is trodden on;
a sudden 'yipe!' of hurt or fright -
then just as quick, it's gone.
