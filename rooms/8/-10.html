Slipper

You are a soft warm shoe for indoors only - no laces, no hard sole, just a snug cosy thing to shuffle round the house in. A hand sews you of felt or sheepskin, perhaps shaped like a rabbit with floppy ears. You wait by the bed for cold morning feet. Cinderella lost a glass one.

North

A soft small hat with a sticking-out brim
to shade your eyes from sun;
you wear it backwards, wear it round -
on a sporty head it's fun.

East

A little metal clasp with a pin
that fastens a belt or a shoe;
you slot the strap and snap it shut -
it holds the two ends true.

South

A round cloth roof on a folding frame,
you raise it against the rain;
its handle's curved, its spokes spring out -
it shuts back small again.

West

A little bag for holding coins,
snapped shut with a click;
Granny keeps her pennies there -
fish them out, be quick.
