Half

You are a half - what you get when a whole thing is split fair and square down the middle, one of two equal pieces. People make you with a knife through a cake or a word at sharing time: half for you, half for me. You are the kindest number, the one that means we both get the same. Half past, half done, half asleep - you are the middle of everything, the line that cuts it even and the share that no one can argue with.
