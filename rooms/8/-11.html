Umbrella

A little roof you carry by hand, sprung open against the sky - an umbrella is shelter you can fold. Black or bright, it blooms above you at the first drop of rain, a dome of stretched cloth on thin steel ribs, and the storm drums harmlessly overhead. Turn it inside out in a gust and it becomes a useless flapping bird. But hold it right and you walk dry through the wet world, a small calm circle moving under the downpour.

North

A soft warm shoe
for indoors only;
it keeps your feet cosy
when the nights are lonely.

East

A hard round shell
you wear on your head;
it guards your skull
when you bike, it's said.

that beeps and clicks;
it walks and talks
with mechanical tricks.

West

and a wide-open mouth;
