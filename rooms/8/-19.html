Mop

You are a mop - a long stick topped with a shaggy head of strings or sponge. Dunk you in the bucket, slap you on the floor, and you swab the dirt away in wet grey swirls. Then you wring out and start again. You are wrought, made by a hand to do the jobs a hand would hate to do bare. The kitchen, the hall, the spilled milk - you wash them all, then stand in the corner dripping, waiting for the next mess.
