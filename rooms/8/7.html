Groan

You are the long low sound of weariness or pain, half a moan and half a complaint, dragged up from the chest. No hand makes you; the body lets you out when the alarm goes off, when the homework comes, when the back gives a twinge or the bad joke lands. A whole class can groan as one. You say, without quite words, oh no, or not again, or it hurts. A wrought sound of dismay, you are the body grumbling aloud at what it would rather not.

North

A hard sharp strike of the foot held out,
to boot the ball away;
the donkey does it, cross and quick,
the striker, hard at play.

East

What you do when you wake and yawn,
arms flung wide and long;
the cat does it, all curved and slow,
to limber and feel strong.

South

A jumping catch inside the chest
that hops and won't be stilled;
you hold your breath, you drink a sip,
until at last it's killed.

West

A sharp rough bark to clear the throat,
a tickle's loud reply;
it spreads the winter cold around
and makes the sick chest cry.
