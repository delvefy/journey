hotwaterbottle

A hot water bottle is a flat rubber bag you fill with water from the kettle, screw shut, and slip down into a cold bed before you climb in. Hug it to your tummy or warm your feet on it, and the chill melts away while you drift off. By morning it has gone cool and a little sloshy. Often it wears a knitted jumper of its own. Simple and old-fashioned and kind, it is a pocket of warmth on the coldest night.

North

A long warm robe you wear round the house,
over pyjamas, tied with a cord;
fluffy and soft, for slumping about -
cosy mornings, never bored.

East

Two long poles with canvas between,
lifted by four, one at each end;
I carry the hurt away from harm -
off to hospital round the bend.

South

Soft fluffy shoes for round the house,
no laces, just slip your feet inside;
warm on cold floors, comfy as toast -
kick them off and step aside.

West

A folding chair of wood and stripes,
open me up on the lawn or sand;
lean right back in the summer sun -
mind your fingers where I'm spanned.
