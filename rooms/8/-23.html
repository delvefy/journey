Duvet

You are a duvet - a great fat fluffy quilt stuffed with feathers or downy filling, that lies on top of the bed and weighs just enough to feel like a hug. Hands sew you and slip you in a cover, and on a cold night you are the warmest thing in the house. You trap a cosy bubble of heat and refuse to let it go. The thing children burrow under and cannot be coaxed from in the morning, you make the bed a snug white cloud.
