Riddle

You are a riddle - a puzzle folded up in words, with one secret answer hidden inside. 'What has teeth but cannot bite?' You tease and tickle the brain, dropping clues just clever enough to be fair, until - aha! - the answer pops into the head. You live in playgrounds and on lolly sticks and in old, old books. No hand can hold you, for you are a question wearing a disguise, waiting patiently for someone sharp enough to see right through it.
