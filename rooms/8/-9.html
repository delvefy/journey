Cap

You are a soft small hat with a stiff peak at the front, worn to shade the eyes or to crown a team. A hand stitches you; a head pulls you on, forwards or cheekily backwards. The cricketer wears you, the schoolboy, the sailor. Off, it is a sign of respect; on, of belonging.

North

The soft cloth cap sewn to a coat,
pulled up against the rain;
it shadows the face when it's up -
down, it hangs again.

East

A cloth you tie around the front
to keep your clothes from mess;
the baker, cook, and painter wear one -
no stain upon the dress.

South

A soft warm shoe for indoors only,
no laces and no heel;
you shuffle round the house in them -
how snug and warm they feel.

West

A little cloth pouch sewn in your coat
where hands and treasures hide;
a coin, a sweet, a bit of string
all kept warm inside.
