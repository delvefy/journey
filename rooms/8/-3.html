The Wordless

South of the dreamt and east of the green eye, the world is wholly without the word. A step north you were dreamt through and through, of the dream and not in it; a step west a sourceless old green held the world in being by seeing it. Here, at the Wyld pole, the dreaming and the seeing and the being sink below even the gods into the oldest green of all — the world before the word, reality grown and seen and dreamed and lived but never once said. There is no naming here and no saying and no need of either; the wordless green is the whole world, wild and mute and absolute, holding everything in being without a single word.

It is the deep mirror of every spoken pole: where the word made worlds elsewhere, here there was never a word, and the world is no poorer for it — the green grows you, sees you, dreams you, is you, all wordless, all wild. Your name is the one thing here that was ever a word, and the wordless green holds you without it, knowing you the way root knows rain, by being and not by saying. The spiral is the green's wordless shape, the coil of a fern and a sleeping beast and the old sight all at once, the same whorl in your skin, here the wild's own mark that needs no name, the mute seal of the world before the word.

There is no count and no toll and no said hour; the wordless green keeps its time in growth and breath and the slow turn of the old sight, a wild wordless rhythm older than any word for rhythm — and your heart beats with it, the one beat that was ever counted in words now beating wordless and wild, kept by the green that needs no name to keep you.

To the north the wordless green rises to the dreamt, the world dreamed and nearly worded. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the old green's wordless world entire, all grown and seen and dreamed. To the south it sinks toward the seed and the floor, the green before even the green. To the west the green eye, the world held by sourceless sight. You were going somewhere, under a name — a word. Here the wordless green holds you without one, knowing you by being and not by saying — and still the warm coil turns, the mark that needs no name, and still one beat keeps a wild wordless time in the world before the word.
