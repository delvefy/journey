Fizz

You are the fizz - the tickly hiss of a thousand tiny bubbles racing up through the lemonade. Pop the cap and out you rush, all sparkle and prickle on the tongue. You are the sound a fresh drink makes, the sting of bubbles up the nose, the sherbet crackle on the tongue. Open me quick before I go flat - for fizz, once it is gone, never comes back.

North

Cat and hat, and bee and tree,
words that end the same;
poems line them up just so -
a chiming little game.

East

The sound a bell or phone lets out,
a bright and chiming peal;
it calls you to the door or desk -
answer it, it's real.

South

A quick short run, a sudden sprint,
you make one for the door;
also a little line - like this -
a hurry, nothing more.

West

I'm what you do when a song's so good
you can't keep it inside;
open your throat, let the notes fly out -
on a tune the words will ride.
