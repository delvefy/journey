Pinch

You are a small sharp nip of the skin between finger and thumb, done in fun or spite or to be sure you are awake. No tool makes you - only two fingers and a quick squeeze - and you leave a little red mark and a yelp behind. A pinch of salt is the smallest measure in the kitchen. You are mischief in miniature, a tiny wrought hurt, gone as fast as it comes but felt all the same.

North

A little jump inside your chest
that hops and won't be still;
hold your breath or drink a sip
to cure me if you will.

East

A small and bubbly kind of laugh
that will not stay inside;
children do me behind their hands
and find me hard to hide.

South

A sudden, slamming, crashing sound
when a door is shut too hard;
the firework's burst, the balloon's last word,
that startles half the yard.

West

A quick sharp jab with one stiff finger
right into your side;
I make you turn, I make you jump,
there's nowhere you can hide.
