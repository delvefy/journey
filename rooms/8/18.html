Quiz

You are a quiz - a string of questions asked to see how much you know: capital cities, times tables, the names of dinosaurs. A sharp mind makes you, one tricky question after another, and you keep a score at the end. You cannot be held, only sat and answered. A pub quiz, a telly quiz, a Friday spelling quiz - you turn knowing things into a game, and the fun is in shouting out the answer before anyone else. You are a friendly little test in disguise.
