The Stilled Engine

North of the named and east of the spoken engine, the world's first craft falls still. A step south reality was nothing but its names, a weightless world of pure word; a step west an engine turned easily beside the saying, word and work one. Here, at the magic pole, the two meet and the engine stops: the all-saying takes on a first wrought craft and a first faint light — and finds nothing for it to do. The names are the whole of reality; the word holds everything; and so the engine stands perfectly, contentedly still, a stilled wheel in a world of pure word, craft needless at the pole where the saying is all.

It is a strange and peaceful thing, this stilled engine: graven, ready, lit faintly from within, and never turning, because there is nothing the word has left for it to bend. It is not broken and not idle; it is at rest, a made thing in a world that needs no making, kept only as a kind of grace-note to the all-saying. The spiral is its motionless hub and the word's own seal at once, the same whorl in your skin, here the still center of a wheel that will never turn, in a world held wholly by name. The engine waits, unturning, beside your name, which the saying alone will speak.

There is no count of turning, for nothing turns; only the all-saying sounding every name to keep it in being, and the stilled engine keeping a silent time beside it, the bell rung by word alone while the wheel holds still. Your heart beats in the quiet, the one moving thing where even the engine has stopped, a warm stroke in a world that needs no motion to be.

To the north the stilled engine takes on more light, the names given radiance, the magitech climbing toward the Lumen. To the east the magic pole absolute, the names the whole of reality, no engine at all. To the south the named, reality nothing but its names. To the west the spoken engine, a wheel that still, barely, turns. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the world holds it in word alone and the engine stands still beside it, needing never to turn — and still the warm coil turns at the stilled hub, the one motion left, and still one beat keeps the time the all-saying is a breath from speaking you into.
