Mirror

You are a sheet of silvered glass that gives back everything and keeps nothing. Stand before you and there you are again, exact and backwards, every freckle and frown returned in an instant. You cannot lie, though you cannot quite tell the truth either, for left is right in your cool flat world. The vain adore you, the shy avoid you, the toddler is amazed to meet themselves. You hang there showing the room its own face, and the moment anyone steps away, you forget them utterly.

North

A long tall tube that spits out fire,
and leaps up off the ground;
it climbs past cloud, it rises higher,
with a great and roaring sound.

East

A square of glass set in the wall,
to let the daylight through;
I show the street, the rain, and all,
yet keep the cold from you.

South

A flat plank fixed up off the ground,
in a tidy level row;
I hold your books and jars around,
all stacked up in a show.

West

A long thick cord of twisted strand,
to haul and tie and bind;
you climb me, swing me, hand on hand,
the strongest line you'll find.
