The Feral Names

South of the named and east of the wild word, the names grow feral. A step north reality was nothing but its names, a weightless world of pure word; a step west the saying went wild, the world grown and roared instead of spoken. Here, at the magic pole tipped toward the beast, the two become one: the all-saying gone wholly feral, every name a thing of root and horn and blood, reality wholly the beast-gods' to shape — not by word now but by wild knowing, the names lived in tooth and growth and the hot dark of the blood. The wild pole absolute, where to name is to hunt and grow and bleed a thing into being.

There is no speaking here, only the feral naming — the beast-gods knowing each thing by its wild-true-name, the way a wolf knows its own, and the world shaping itself to that knowing on every side. Your name is among the feral names, not a word to be said but a scent, a blood-beat, a wild-truth the deep dark knows you by. The spiral is the shape the feral naming turns on, horn and root and the whorl of a curled and sleeping beast, the same whorl in your skin, here the wild-true-mark the beast-gods know you by — and the wild leans in, knowing you by blood, a breath from naming you feral and true into the world.

There is no tolling now, only the blood-bell, the herd-beat, the wild pulse that is the feral names' whole speech — and your heart beats with it, indistinguishable, one wild-true-name among the many, the count gone wholly feral, your name a thing the blood is about to know without a word at all.

To the north the feral names firm to the named, the wild-truth lifting back into pure word. To the east the wild pole absolute, reality wholly the beast-gods' to shape, the deep Wyld entire. To the south the magic sinks toward the older wild, the names before the gods, the world before the word. To the west the wild word, the saying grown wild but not yet wholly feral. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the wild knows it by blood and is about to name it feral and true into you — and still the warm coil turns, the wild-true-mark, and still one wild beat keeps the time the feral names are about to know you in, without a word.
