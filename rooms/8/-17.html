Bucket

You are a bucket - a round deep pail with a curved handle to swing you by. You carry water for the garden, soapy suds for the car, sand for the castle, conkers home from the park. Tip you up and out it all pours. A child wears you upside-down for a hat. Pressed from metal or plastic by a hand, deep and sturdy, you are the trusty carrier that hauls all the wet and heavy and loose things from one place to another.
