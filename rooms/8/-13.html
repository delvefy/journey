Trumpet

You are the bright brass horn that blares the loudest in the band - three small valves, a flared bell, a sound that cuts through everything. Made of shining metal. Press the keys and blow a buzz through your lips, and out comes a fanfare. When the trumpet sounds, the whole parade stands up and marches.

North

A metal man that walks and beeps,
with buttons, lights, and gears;
it does just what it's told to do -
a made man, it appears.

East

A thin straight stick the wizard waves,
with a star upon the end;
a flick, a 'swish,' and magic comes -
to enemy or friend.

South

A wooden box with a long thin neck
and six tight strings to strum;
you pluck or twang to make a tune -
in a band it loves to thrum.

West

A little paper stub you buy
to ride or see the show;
you keep it safe inside your hand
and tear it as you go.
