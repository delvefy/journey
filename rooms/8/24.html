Quarter

You are a quarter - one of four equal pieces when a whole thing is cut fair in four, the slice of cake, the segment of orange, the fifteen minutes that make a quarter of an hour. People make you with a knife or a clock. Quarter past, quarter to, quarter of the way there. You are smaller than a half and bigger than nothing much. Neat and fair and exactly measured, you are the world divided into four, and the gentlest way to wait: just a quarter of an hour more.
