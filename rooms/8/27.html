add

To add is to put numbers together and find how many you have in all - two apples and three apples make five, and the plus sign tells you to do it. It is the very first sum you ever learn, counting on your fingers, piling things into one big heap. Add and add and the total grows; it is the friendly arithmetic, the one that only ever makes things bigger. Everything else in maths is built on top of it.

North

The little dash that means take away,
the opposite of the plus sign too;
five take two leaves three behind -
I make your number smaller for you.

East

Slide your legs out flat and wide
till you sit low, one leg each way;
gymnasts do me, straight as a line -
right down to the floor, hooray!

South

One, two, three, and on you go,
say the numbers in their row;
count the sheep to fall asleep -
how many? say them, fast or slow.

West

Every letter, A to Z,
lined up in their proper row;
sing me through to find a word -
twenty-six in all, you know.
