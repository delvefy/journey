Sheet

You are a sheet - the flat smooth cloth spread over the mattress, tucked in tight at the corners. One of you goes under, one over, and you sleep snug between the pair. Fresh from the wash you smell clean and feel cool; on a winter morning you cling warm. Throw one over your head with two eye-holes cut and you become a ghost. You crease, you rumple, you billow when shaken out. You are the smooth clean skin of the bed, the cloth a whole night is spent between.
