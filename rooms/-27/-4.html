Grapefruit

You are a grapefruit - a big round citrus fruit, far bigger than an orange, with a thick rind and segments that are sour and sweet and a little bit bitter all at once. Cut you in half and scoop you out with a spoon for breakfast, maybe with a sprinkle of sugar to take the sharpness off. Pink or pale yellow inside, you make the mouth pucker. Big and tangy and refreshing, you are the wake-up fruit of the morning, a great juicy jolt of sour-sweet sunshine.
