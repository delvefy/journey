Tangerine

You are a tangerine - a small bright-orange citrus fruit, smaller and sweeter than an orange, with a loose skin that slips off in one easy round piece. Underneath, you fall apart into neat little segments, each one juicy and sweet, perfect for sharing one by one. You turn up in stockings at Christmas and lunchboxes all winter. Cheerful and easy and sweet, you are the friendliest fruit of all, no knife needed, just a thumb to start the peel and away you come.
