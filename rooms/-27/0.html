Peak

You are a peak - the sharp pointed top of a mountain, the very highest point, where the snow lies white all year and the air is thin and cold. Climbers toil up for days to stand on you and plant a flag, and from up here the whole world spreads out small below. Clouds drift past beneath your tip. Lofty and proud and hard to reach, you are the mountain's crowning point, the summit that draws every climber's eye and every climber's aching, determined feet.
