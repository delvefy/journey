Hoot

You are a hoot - the round, low, hollow call of an owl floating out of the dark trees at night. Twit-twoo, you go, soft and mournful and wise, carrying far across the still night air. Owls call to one another with you, hunting in the dark. People shiver a little to hear you in a lonely wood. Deep and breathy and full of the night, you are the voice of the owl, the spookiest and most magical sound the moonlit countryside makes.
