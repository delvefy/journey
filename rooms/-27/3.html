Sigh

You are a sigh - a long slow breath let out soft and low. You come at the end of a tiring day, at the sight of something lovely, at the relief when a worry lifts. Shoulders drop, breath whooshes out gently, and a whole feeling spills into one quiet whoosh of air. Soft and weary and full of meaning, you are the body letting go of a held breath, saying without a single word that something is finished, or wished for, or simply, at last, all right.
