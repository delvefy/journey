Mole

You are a mole - a small dark spot on the skin, flat and round and brown, that has always been there and never hurts at all. Everyone has a few, dotted here and there: on an arm, a cheek, the back of a hand. Some people's join up into freckly little maps. Quiet and permanent and harmless, you are a tiny dark fleck the skin was simply born with, a little dot of brown that stays put your whole life long.
