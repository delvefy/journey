Tsunami

You are a tsunami - a giant wall of sea water, born far out where an earthquake shakes the ocean floor. You race across the deep faster than a jet, low and unseen, then rear up huge as you reach the shore, towering over the beach and crashing inland with terrible force. The sea draws back first, as if taking a breath. Vast and sudden and unstoppable, you are the ocean's most fearsome wave, swallowing the coast in one great thundering surge.
