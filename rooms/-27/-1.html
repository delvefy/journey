Graze

You are a graze - the raw red scrape left when skin is dragged across something rough. A tumble off the bike, a slide on the path, a knee skidded along the playground: out you come, stinging and pink and gritty. You are not deep like a cut, just sore and weeping a little. A wash, a dab and a plaster, and in a few days you scab over and mend. Common and quick to heal, you are the everyday badge of a brave and adventurous afternoon.
