wonder

Wonder is the wide-open feeling that stops you still. You tip your head back at the stars, or watch a snail's slow silver road, and something in you goes quiet and amazed. No one builds it; it rises by itself, the way breath does. You cannot hold it or hand it over - only feel it lift, light and astonished, in the chest. Natural and weightless, it is how the world first looks when you truly look.

North

Not a gulp and not a guzzle,
just a tiny taste of tea;
I take my drink so small and careful,
quietly, daintily.

East

A bursting, giggling kind of joy,
too big to keep inside;
you clap your hands and jump about -
delight you cannot hide.

South

An earthquake shakes the ocean floor
and I rise up, a wall of sea;
I race to shore and swallow towns -
a giant wave; you flee from me.

West

I am the cold that grips your chest
when something goes bump in the night;
your heart beats fast, you want to run -
I am the feeling of fright.
