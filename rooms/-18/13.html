Moonlight

You are moonlight - the soft pale silver glow the moon spills down on the sleeping world. You are not warm like sunshine, but cool and dim and a little magical, laying a shining path across the sea and edging the clouds with white. Owls hunt by you; lovers walk by you; werewolves are said to wake by you. You are only the sun's light borrowed and softened, yet you turn the plainest night into something out of a dream.
