Glug

You are a glug - the round knocking sound a bottle makes as it empties, glug-glug-glug, when the liquid and the air take turns to pass the narrow neck. You are the noise of a big jug of squash poured fast, of a bath draining, of a thirsty gulp from a flask. Deep and gloopy and oddly satisfying, you come in a steady rhythm until the very last drop, and then you give one last hollow knock and the bottle stands empty and silent.
