Waterfall

You are a waterfall - the place where a river runs out of ground and leaps off the edge of a cliff, falling in a roaring white curtain to the pool below. No hand makes you; the river and the drop do. You throw up spray and rainbows and a noise that drowns out talking. Salmon hurl themselves up you; daredevils gawp at you. Loud and tireless and dazzling, you are the river at its most thrilling, hurling itself headlong off the world and falling, falling, falling.
