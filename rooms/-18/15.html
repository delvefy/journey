Leak

You are a leak - the slow sneaky escape of water where it should not go. A crack in the pipe, a hole in the boot, a worn old roof: out you seep, drip by patient drip, until a dark stain spreads or a bucket fills. You work in secret, behind walls and under floors, and nobody notices until the ceiling sags. A drip at a time, you can empty a whole tank. You are water finding the one weak spot and quietly slipping through.
