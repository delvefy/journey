Fern

You are a fern - a soft green plant of the shady wood, all feathery fronds that arch and nod. You uncurl from a tight little fist in spring, each leaf splitting into a hundred smaller leaves, and you spread cool and lacy beneath the trees where the light comes green and dim. No flower, no fruit, just leaf upon leaf. Grown by no hand, soft to the touch, you are the gentlest green thing on the whole forest floor.
