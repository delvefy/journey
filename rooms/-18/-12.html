Forehead

You are a forehead - the broad smooth stretch of face between the eyebrows and the hair, the first place a kiss lands when a child is sick. You wrinkle when you frown and shine when you sweat; a hand goes to you to feel for a fever. You hold no eye or mouth, only skin and the brow's small furrows. You are the flat front of the skull, the part you slap when you forget, the calm broad page above the busy face.
