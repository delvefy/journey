Ant

You are an ant - a tiny dark insect with six busy legs, no bigger than a crumb, yet able to lift a crumb many times your own weight. No hand made you; the earth did, and you live in a great hidden city under the path, a thousand thousand of you marching in a line. You can be watched, lifted on a leaf, followed to your door. You never rest, never hurry, never give up. You are the small patient strength of the world, carrying on.
