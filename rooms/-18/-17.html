Forest

You are a forest - a great cool crowd of trees standing close together for miles, their branches knitting a green roof over a hush of moss and fallen leaves. No hand plants you; the seasons do, seedling by seedling over hundreds of years. Deer step through your shade, owls hoot in your dark, and a path winds in and is soon lost. Deep and old and full of quiet, you are the biggest gathering of trees there is, a whole world made of wood and shadow.
