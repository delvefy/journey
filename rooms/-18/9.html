Yawn

You are a yawn - the slow wide opening of the mouth and the long deep breath that comes when you are sleepy or bored. No hand makes you; tiredness does, all on its own, and you cannot really hold one back once it starts. You cannot be touched, only caught - one yawn round a room and soon everyone is yawning. Big and slow and watery-eyed, you are the body's gentle hint that it is time, very soon, for bed.
