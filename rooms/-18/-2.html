Chin

You are a chin - the firm rounded part of the face below the mouth. You jut out when someone is being brave or stubborn, you rest in a cupped hand when someone is deep in thought, you wear the milk moustache and the chocolate smear. A grown-up tilts you up gently to look at a face that's sad. Grown of the living face, you are its lowest point - the chin a person holds up high to say, 'I can do this.'
