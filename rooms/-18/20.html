Babble

You are a babble - the busy chattering sound a brook makes as it hurries over its stones. All day you talk to yourself, bright and quick and never quite making words, a running murmur that fills the quiet of the wood. People say a stream babbles; so does a happy baby, and so does anyone who talks too fast and too long. Cheerful and tumbling and endless, you are water given a voice, gossiping and burbling its way downhill without ever once stopping for breath.
