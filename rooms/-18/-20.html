Bay

You are a bay - a wide curving scoop bitten out of the coast, where the land sweeps round in two long arms and the sea comes gently in between. Sheltered from the worst of the waves, you make a calm safe place: boats anchor in you, swimmers splash in you, and a crescent of golden sand often rims your edge. Broad and gentle and welcoming, you are the friendliest shape a coast can make, a great calm curve of water cradled in the land's embracing arms.
