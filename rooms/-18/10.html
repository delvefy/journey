Squeal

You are a squeal - a high sharp cry that bursts out before you can stop it. A piglet makes you; a child makes you on the fastest ride; brakes make you on a tight corner. You are pure pitch and surprise, half delight and half fright, too loud and too sudden to hide. You cannot be held, only let out. You are a natural sound, no instrument needed, the noise that means a thing has gone way past quiet.
