Ocean

You are the ocean - the vast deep salt water that wraps most of the way round the world, too big to see across, too deep to plumb. Whales and sharks roam your dark below; storms whip your surface to mountains of grey. No hand made you; you were here before the land had names. The tide is your slow breath, in and out, twice a day. You are the biggest thing there is, the blue that the rivers all run home to in the end.
