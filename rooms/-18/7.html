Spell

You are a spell - a string of magic words that, spoken just right, bends the world to your will: turns a prince to a frog, opens a sealed door, sends someone fast asleep. No hand can hold you; a witch or wizard makes you, and the power is all in the saying. You cannot be touched, only cast, and broken only by another. In tales you are everywhere - and in this very hoard, the right word spoken opens every wall. You are a wish with the words to make it come true.
