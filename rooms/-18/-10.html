Diamond

You are a diamond - the hardest stone there is, clear as glass and cold to the touch, throwing back the light in little fires. No knife can scratch you; you cut everything and nothing cuts you. Deep in the dark earth, under years of squeezing, you were made of plain black soot turned to ice that lasts forever. You are natural, grown not built. People dig you, polish you, and wear you on a ring, sure that you will outlast them all.
