Icicle

You are an icicle - the long glassy spike that hangs from the gutter when the cold sets in. You grow downward, drip by frozen drip, sharpening to a point that catches the low sun and shines like a dagger of glass. Tap me and I chime; warm me and I weep, then drop. Children dare each other to snap me off and lick me. Cold, clear, and pointing always at the ground - that is you.
