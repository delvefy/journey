Glow

You are a glow - the soft steady light a thing gives off without a flame, warm and low and gentle on the eyes. The embers glow when the fire dies down; the screen glows in a dark room; a firefly glows in the summer hedge. You are light that does not blaze or flash but simply shines, quiet and round. You are the last warmth of a dying coal, the lamp behind a curtain, the gentle shining of things that are not quite asleep.
