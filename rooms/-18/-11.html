Beard

You are a beard - the bushy hair that grows on a grown man's chin and cheeks when he leaves the razor alone. You can be neat and trimmed or long and wild, grey on a wizard, white on Santa, red on a pirate. Children love to tug you, and crumbs love to hide in you. You are natural, grown not made, springing up all on your own. You are the woolly badge of a chin, the soft proof that the boy has become a man and the years have rolled on.
