Squirt

You are a squirt - the thin fast jet of liquid that shoots out when something full is squeezed hard, the water pistol, the ketchup bottle, the orange peeled too eagerly. No hand makes you alone; a squeeze and a narrow hole do. You dart across the room and catch someone square in the eye. Quick and cheeky and a little bit naughty, you are water turned into a dart, gone in a flash and leaving only a wet surprise and a yelp behind.
