Gurgle

You are a gurgle - the soft bubbly throaty sound water makes slipping down a plughole or babbling over the stones of a little brook. No hand makes you; water and a narrow gap do. A baby makes you too, happy in its cot. A tummy does it when it's hungry. You are half a laugh and half a drain, a wet and chuckling noise that sounds, somehow, contented. Bubbly and soft and friendly, you are the gentle music of water finding its way through.
