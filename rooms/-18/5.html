Pout

You are a pout - the pushing-out of the bottom lip when things don't go your way. The face goes long, the lip sticks out, the arms fold, and the whole sulky look says 'it isn't fair' without a single word. A puppy can pout; a film star pouts for the camera. No hand can hold you - you are a small storm cloud of disappointment, sitting right there on the lip, daring anyone to try and cheer you up.
