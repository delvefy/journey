Copper

You are copper - the warm reddish-brown metal, dug from the rock and beaten into pots and pipes and pennies. No hand made you; the earth did, and you were one of the first metals people ever learned to use. You can be held, polished till you glow like a sunset, left out to turn a dull green over years. Wires of you carry power into every house; coins of you fill every pocket. You are the friendly working metal, neither precious nor plain, but useful everywhere.
