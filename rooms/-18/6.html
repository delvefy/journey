Frown

You are a frown - the downward pull of the brow and the mouth when something is wrong: a hard sum, a grey sky, a friend being unfair. No hand makes you; the face does, all on its own, folding the forehead into little hills. You cannot be held, only worn and read. It takes more muscles than a smile, they say, and yet out you come, the honest weather of the face, telling everyone exactly how a person feels.
