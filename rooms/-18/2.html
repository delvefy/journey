Wave

You are a wave - the long roll of the sea heaving itself up and toppling over. Far out you are just a swell; near the beach you rise and hump and curl into a green wall, then crash down white and roaring and rush hissing up the sand. A child jumps you, dives under you, runs shrieking from you. No hand can hold you - you travel a thousand miles to arrive, break in one glorious second, and slide away to gather your strength and come again.
