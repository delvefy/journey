Fist

You are a fist - a hand drawn up tight into a ball, fingers folded down over the thumb. You knock on the door, you bump hello with a friend, you hold a found treasure where no one can see it, you punch the air when the goal goes in. A baby grips one little finger and will not let go. Made of the hand itself, clenched hard, you are a hand turned strong and secret - ready to knock, to hold, or to say without a word, 'I mean this'.
