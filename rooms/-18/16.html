Spill

You are a spill - the moment a cup leans too far and the milk goes everywhere at once. No hand meant to make you; a careless elbow did, and now you spread across the table and run for the edge. You are juice on the floor, water off the lap, the puddle nobody wanted. Quick to happen, slow to mop, you find every crack and creep into all of them.
