Ash

You are ash - the soft pale-grey powder left in the grate when the fire has burned itself out and gone cold. No hand makes you; burning does, turning the heavy log into a heap so light a breath can scatter it. You smudge a finger grey and drift up the chimney on a draught. Gardeners dig you in to feed the soil. The quiet leftover of every flame, you are what remains when all the heat and light have finished and the warmth is only a memory.
