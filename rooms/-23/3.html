Gust

You are a gust - a sudden short burst of wind out of a quiet day. You come from nowhere, snatch the hat off a head, flap the washing flat, slam the open door, and scatter the pile of leaves a child just raked. Then, as quickly as you came, you are gone, and the air is still again. You are wind in a hurry, a single hard push of moving air. You do not last - you arrive, you shove, you vanish.
