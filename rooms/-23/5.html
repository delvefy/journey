Snow

You are snow - the soft white flakes that drift down from the winter sky and settle silently over everything. You hush the world, round off its edges, and turn the dull grey street into something new and bright. Children roll you into snowmen, pack you into snowballs, and sledge down hills on you. You squeak underfoot when cold, melt to slush when warm. Each flake of you is different. You are winter's quiet gift, a clean white blanket fallen overnight.
