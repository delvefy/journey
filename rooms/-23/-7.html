Fur

You are fur - the soft thick coat of hair that keeps an animal warm and dry. You cover the cat, the fox, the bear, the rabbit: sleek or shaggy, striped or spotted, growing thick for winter and shed in the spring. No hand made you; the creature grew you, layer on layer, for warmth and for hiding. You are lovely to stroke and warm to curl against. You are the living blanket every wild thing wears, the coat that is never taken off.
