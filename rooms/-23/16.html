blob

A blob is a small round splodge of something soft with no proper shape at all - a blob of jam, a blob of paint, a blob of glue squeezed out too fast. It wobbles when you poke it and spreads if you wait. It is the shape of a thing that has no shape, soft and gloopy and slightly silly. Wipe it up before it drips. Nobody plans a blob; it just lands where it lands.

North

Not proper rain, just tiny drops,
a fine and misty wet;
too light for brollies, soft and grey -
the smallest rain you'll get.

East

A salty drop that fills your eye
and slides on down your cheek;
it falls when you are sad or hurt -
no word you have to speak.

South

A little ball of ice that falls,
it bounces off the roof - rat-tat!;
hard and round and cold as winter,
down from a storm cloud, just like that.

West

Jump in the puddle, in goes your boot -
up flies the water, all around;
a wet loud burst in the bath or pool,
SPLOSH! with a watery sound.
