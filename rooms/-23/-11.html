Muscle

You are a muscle - the stretchy ropey stuff packed under the skin that tightens and pulls to move the bones, the bulge that pops up when an arm is flexed for show. No hand makes you; the body grows you and exercise builds you bigger. You ache the day after a long run. You work the legs, the jaw, even the heart. Strong and stretchy and tireless, you are the engine of every movement, pulling the body about from the inside all day long.
