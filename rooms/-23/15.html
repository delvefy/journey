Hailstone

You are a hailstone - a hard little ball of ice that tumbles out of a storm cloud, bouncing and rattling off the roofs and the road. Some of you are tiny as peas; the biggest can be as large as a marble and sting where they land. You drum on car roofs and bounce in the gutters, then melt away to nothing on the warm ground. Cold and hard and clattering, you are rain frozen solid, pelting down in a rattling shower and gone again the moment the sun comes out.
