Peek

You are a peek - the quick secret look sneaked through spread fingers or round the edge of a door, when you are not quite meant to see but cannot help it. No one teaches you; curiosity does. You take you at the wrapped present, the scary bit of the film, the answer at the back of the book. Half a glance and half a dare, you are the smallest, cheekiest kind of looking, gone again before anyone can catch your eye.
