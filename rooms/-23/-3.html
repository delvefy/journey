Orange

You are an orange - the round fruit with the dimpled rind, so bright you lent your name to the colour itself. Dig a thumb into your skin and the spray of zest stings the air; peel you open and you fall into neat juicy segments, each a little crescent of sweetness. You roll well, stack badly, and brighten the dullest winter fruit bowl. You hide your pips in your middle. You are sunshine you can hold in one hand, sweet and sharp and round.
