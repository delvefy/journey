Whoosh

You are a whoosh - the rushing sweeping sound of something hurtling past, fast and full of air. The rocket leaves the ground with you; the slide drops the child with you; the big door swings and you pour through the gap. You are wind and speed turned into noise, a single long breath of motion. Nobody can hold you - the moment you are heard you are already gone by. You are the sound of the world rushing somewhere, quickly, all at once.
