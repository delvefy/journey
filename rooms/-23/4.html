Hail

You are hail - the little hard balls of ice that come clattering out of a dark storm cloud. You bounce off the roof and the road, rattle the windows, and sting any bare head caught out in you. You can be small as peas or, in a fierce storm, big as marbles that dent the cars. You fall fast, pile up white, then melt to nothing once the sun comes back. You are rain frozen into stones, drumming down hard and cold and quick.
