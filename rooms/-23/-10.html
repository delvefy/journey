Blossom

You are blossom - the sudden crowd of pink and white flowers that breaks out all over a fruit tree in spring, so thick the branches seem to foam. No hand makes you; the warming days do, opening every bud at once. The bees come drunk on you, and a single breeze sends you drifting down like fragrant snow. You last only a week or two. Brief and frothy and impossibly pretty, you are the tree throwing a party, and the promise of the apples to come.
