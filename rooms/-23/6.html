Flash

You are a flash - a sudden burst of light that comes and goes before you can blink. You are the lightning's white crack, the camera's pop, the glint off a window across the street. No hand need make you; the storm does, or the sun on water. You arrive all at once and vanish just as fast, leaving a ghost of yourself swimming in the eye. You cannot be held or kept, only seen and startled by. You are brightness with no warning and no goodbye.
