Smash

You are a smash - the hard blow that breaks a thing to flying bits, the window struck, the plate dropped, the tennis ball walloped. No gentle hand makes you; force and a fragile thing do. You burst out in shards and splinters and a great loud crash, and someone reaches for the dustpan. Tennis stars do you on purpose; clumsy elbows do you by mistake. Loud and final and a little bit thrilling, you are breaking done all at once, with all the noise turned up.
