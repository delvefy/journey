Scarlet

You are scarlet - a bright, blazing red, the loudest and most dashing of all the reds. You are the colour of a pillar box, a guardsman's coat, a poppy in a field, a king's velvet cloak. You shout for attention; nobody wears you to hide. Bold and warm and just a touch fierce, you are red turned all the way up, the colour of festivals and danger and finest dress, splashed across anything that wants to be seen and admired from right across the room.
