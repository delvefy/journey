Lemon

You are a lemon - the bright yellow fruit so sharp and sour that one bite screws the whole face up tight. You are too sour to eat plain, yet a squeeze of you wakes up everything: lemonade, pancakes, a glass of water, a fish supper. Your peel smells clean and zingy when scratched. People say a thing that goes wrong is "a lemon," but cooks could not do without you. You are pure pucker in a sunny yellow skin, sour and useful at once.
