Lava

You are lava - the red-hot melted rock that pours from the mountain's fiery top. You glow orange and white, too bright to look at, and you ooze in slow rivers down the slopes, burning all you touch. Where you cool you turn to black stone, hard and crackled. Inside the earth you wait as fire; when the mountain blows you rise and overflow. You are the ground itself turned molten - the planet's hot blood, set loose.
