Sunlight

You are sunlight - the bright warm light that floods down from the sun and fills the whole day, streaming through windows, warming a cat on the sill, lighting up the dust that drifts in the air. No hand makes you; the great burning sun does, and it takes eight minutes to reach the earth. You make the plants grow and the shadows sharp and the washing dry. Golden and warm and everywhere at once, you are the day's free gift, the light that makes everything visible and glad.
