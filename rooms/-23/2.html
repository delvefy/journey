Current

You are a current - the steady unseen pull within moving water. You sweep the river toward the sea, drag the leaf downstream, and tug sideways at the swimmer who strays too far. On the calm surface nothing shows, yet underneath you never rest, always going one way with a quiet strength. Boats ride you to go faster; the careless are carried off. You are water with a mind made up, a river's hidden muscle pulling everything along.
