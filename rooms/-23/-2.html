Freckle

You are a freckle - a tiny brown speck the summer sun sprinkles across a nose and cheeks. Out in the bright days you multiply, scattering like pepper, until a face is dusted all over. You fade a little in winter and come back with the warm weather. Nobody draws you on purpose; the sun does the painting. You are a little brown kiss left by the light, one of a hundred small spots that no one ever quite manages to count.
