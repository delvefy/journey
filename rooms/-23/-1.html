Fingernail

You are a fingernail - the hard smooth shield at the tip of each finger, pale pink with a little white moon at its base. You grow so slowly nobody sees you move, yet there you are again next week, needing a clip. You scratch the itch, peel the sticker, tap on the glass. Bitten down you sting; left long you click on the keys. You are armour for the tender fingertip, a small hard guard worn at the very end of the hand.
