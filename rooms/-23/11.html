Afternoon

You are an afternoon - the long slow stretch of day after lunch and before tea, the drowsiest, dreamiest part of the clock, when the sun leans west and the lessons drag. No hand brings you; the turning day does. The cat finds the warm square on the carpet, the bee bumbles slow, time itself seems to yawn. Wide and golden and a little bit sleepy, you are the day's deep breath, the lazy gap between the morning's bustle and the coming dark.
