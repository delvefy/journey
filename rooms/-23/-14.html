Woodlouse

You are a woodlouse - a tiny grey bug in a suit of overlapping armoured plates, no bigger than a pea. You live in the cool dark damp, under stones and rotting logs, scuttling on a great many little legs. Touch you and you tuck up tight into a perfect armoured ball. Harmless and shy and a favourite of every log-turning child, you trundle about the leaf-mould all your days, a knight in grey armour the size of a fingernail, rolling away from the light.
