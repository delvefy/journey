Soil

You are soil - the dark crumbly earth that lies under the grass, where the roots dig down and the worms wind through. No hand makes you; old leaves and stone and rain mix you slowly over years into the stuff that feeds the world. You smell of rain and growing things. A seed sleeps in you, a carrot fattens in you, a worm tills you. Plain brown and patient, you are the floor of the garden and the cradle of everything green.
