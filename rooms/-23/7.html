Hiccup

You are a hiccup - a funny little jump in the chest that comes with a squeaky catch of the breath, and then again, and again, just when you thought you'd stopped. No hand makes you; the body does, all by itself, and nobody is quite sure why. You arrive uninvited and stay too long. Folk hold their breath, drink upside-down, and jump out to scare you away. You are the silliest hitch the body has, the small loud surprise that won't be told to leave.
