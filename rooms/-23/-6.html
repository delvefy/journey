Beak

You are a beak - the hard horny mouth of a bird, with no lips and no teeth, just two smooth points that meet. You peck the seed, crack the nut, preen the feather, and feed the gaping chicks. No hand made you; the bird grew you, sharp for a hawk, flat for a duck, fat for a parrot. You can be a chisel, a spear, or a nutcracker. You are the one tool a bird is born holding, and never has to put down.
