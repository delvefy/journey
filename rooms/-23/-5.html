Peach

You are a peach - the soft round fruit with skin like warm fuzzy velvet, gold blushed with pink. Bite in and the sweet juice runs down a chin in summer; deep in your heart sits a hard wrinkled stone. You bruise if you are dropped and must be eaten ripe. People call a lovely thing "a peach" because of you. You smell of sunshine and orchards. You are summer made soft and sweet, a fruit so gentle you must be handled with care.
