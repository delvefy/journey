Liver

You are a liver - a big dark wedge of an organ tucked under the ribs, working away without rest to clean the blood and store up goodness from the food. No hand makes you; the body grows you, and you can even patch yourself up when hurt. You turn up fried with onions on a plate, to groans from half the table. Dark and busy and quietly vital, you are the body's hardest-working filter, scrubbing away in the warm where no one ever sees.
