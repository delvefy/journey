Pole

You are a long straight rod stood upright - the flagpole, the tent pole, the bean-pole in the patch. No single thing you are; you hold up, prop, and mark. Wood or metal, smooth and tall. The climber shins up you, the flag snaps at your top. Plant me deep, and I will not lean.

North

A thin dry branch fallen to the ground,
you fetch it for the dog;
good for poking, good for fires -
snapped off a fallen log.

East

A long sticky strip you tear and press
to mend or wrap a gift;
off the roll it comes, and binds -
the torn page gets a lift.

South

A long round tube that carries water
under the road, through the wall;
the rain runs down it off the roof -
hollow, and standing tall.

West

A big round fruit, green-skinned outside,
pink-red and sweet within;
full of black pips and cool fresh juice -
you spit the seeds with a grin.
