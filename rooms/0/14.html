Snooze

You are a sleep you steal in daylight, the cat's long stretch in the sun, the grandad gone under behind his newspaper. No hand makes you; tiredness does, soft and warm. Not the deep night sleep - just a borrowed half-hour, dropped into and climbed out of. Hit the button once more, and you steal five minutes again.

North

How many winks make a little nap,
the saying that we keep?
Two times twenty, the old folks say -
a short and cosy sleep.

East

A ball that drops and springs right back,
up and down it goes;
the trampoline, the kangaroo -
high and light it throws.

South

A light short sleep in the afternoon,
you nod off in the chair;
not deep, just drifting half-asleep -
the telly droning there.

West

A faint and trembling little light,
the candle nearly gone;
the far-off star, the dying coal -
a weak and wavering one.
