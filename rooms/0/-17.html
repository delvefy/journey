Chin

You are the chin - the round front point of the jaw, below the mouth. You jut out when a person is proud and tuck in when they are shy. Soup dribbles down you; a grown-up tilts you up with one finger to make a child look them in the eye. Some chins wear a little dimple, some a beard. You are the bottom of the face, the part that leads the way when it is stuck out.
