The Bare Dark

South of the groundless, being finds its floor — and the floor is bare. Where a step north you could not be sure there was a you at all, here you are, undeniably, but stripped to the very least of it: a warm body in an old dark, before fire, before the made thing, before the tool, before the name. No flint, no clay, no mark on any wall, because nothing has yet been made and nothing yet thought to make it. There is only this, the oldest condition there is: a living warmth, and the dark around it, and nothing else asked or offered. The bedrock of being. The least that it takes to be at all.

And it is enough. After the cold's subtractions and the deep's uncertainties, there is a strange plain comfort here in being merely a body in the dark, warm, breathing, indisputably present. You find the spiral whorled in your fingertip — the one mark in a world before marking, carried not on any wall but in the skin, the oldest made thing because it was never made, only born — and it is enough to hold. You have no name here; names are not yet. You have no memory; memory is not yet. You have a body, and the dark, and the certainty, bare and warm and wordless, that you are.

The heart is the whole of the count now, the first clock and the only one, beating plain and strong in the bare dark — no bell, no drip, no herd, no other, only the body keeping the oldest time there is, the proof and the measure of being, unhurried, sure.

To the north the bare dark lifts toward the groundless, being grown unsure of itself again. To the east the dark stirs with the first wild, the body no longer alone in it. To the south it sinks below the floor of being, the edge of none, toward the pole. To the west the warmth begins to leach toward the cold and the Waste, the body's long undoing. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before fire, before the name, before any of it. Here is what was left when all the rest was stripped away: a warm body in the old dark, its heart keeping time, and the bare wordless certainty that it was going.
