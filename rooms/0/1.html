Echo

The room answers you. Speak, and a heartbeat later the walls give your words back — thinned, changed, as though something just behind the stone is learning to talk. You came in on a single word, and it still hangs in the air here, repeating, refusing to fade. Four more riddles wait. Each one you solve will be flung back to you as the name of the room beyond.

North

I am broken the very moment I am named.
Crowded halls have none of me;
I fill the room when no one speaks,
and the deepest night is thick with me.

East

I belong to you, yet others use me more.
I am not your face, your coat, your hand,
yet called across a crowded floor
I'll turn your head before you understand.

South

Teeth I have, and yet no jaw;
I turn, and yet I am no wheel;
the bolted door, the locked-up drawer,
my small cold body will unseal.

West

I am not seen, yet I bend the tree;
I have no body, yet I roar;
I fill the sail, I turn the mill,
and slam the unlatched door.
