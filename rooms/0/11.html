Peace

You are the deep calm when all the fighting stops - no quarrel, no noise, no war. No hand makes you; you settle like still water over a troubled pond. You are the hush after the storm, the handshake after the argument, the quiet of a sleeping house. People long for you and sign their treaties for you, the soft unbroken stillness in which everything can rest.
