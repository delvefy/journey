eyelash

An eyelash is one of the little curved hairs that grow in a row along your eyelid, fencing your eye against dust and grit. They flutter when you blink and cast tiny shadows on your cheek. Catch one as it falls, balance it on your fingertip, blow it away and make a wish - that is the old game. So small you forget you have hundreds, they guard your eyes all day without your ever asking.

North

A little arch of hair up high,
above each eye, a furry line;
I lift in surprise, I dip in a frown -
two of me, a hairy sign.

East

One of a pair of slender sticks,
you hold us both in just one hand;
pick up your rice and noodles too -
from China, Japan, across the land.

South

A little flap of skin that drops
to cover up your eye for sleep;
blink it fast or shut it tight -
it keeps the light out, dark and deep.

West

A yellow flower of early spring,
a trumpet on a long green stalk;
first to nod when winter ends -
I line the path along your walk.
