Pipe

You are the long hollow tube that carries things along - water under the road, smoke up the chimney, the drainpipe down the wall. Made and laid by working hands. Round and empty, you let the flow pass through. Tap me, and I ring hollow; block me, and the whole house knows. The water always finds its way down a pipe.

North

A long straight rod stood tall and proud,
the flag snaps at its top;
the tent, the bean, the climbing kind -
planted, it won't drop.

East

A little bent wire that holds your sheets,
a pinch to keep them tight;
you slide it on the stack of pages -
they stay, and don't take flight.

South

A long thin stick for catching fish,
with a line and hook and reel;
you cast it out into the lake -
and wait for a bite to feel.

West

A sweet soft fruit with golden flesh,
from hot lands far away;
orange-yellow, big as your fist -
sticky juice on a summer day.
