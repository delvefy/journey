Hush

It is the sound of no sound, the finger laid against the lips. A hush falls when the baby is sleeping, when the curtain is about to rise, when something is about to happen and everyone holds their breath. It is not quite silence - more the held, waiting quiet that comes just before. Speak too loud and you break it. It settles softly over a room and asks, without a single word, for everyone to listen.

North

When you are tired or it is late,
your mouth swings open wide;
out comes a great big sleepy breath,
you cannot keep me inside.

East

I tell you what you may and mayn't,
in games and class and home;
'No running by the pool,' I say,
'and don't you dare to roam.'

South

I am the dark half of the day,
when stars come out to peep;
the moon climbs up, the world grows still,
and most folk go to sleep.

West

I am a giant ball in space,
that circles round the sun;
the Earth is one, and Mars, and more -
count them, there's more than one.
