Night

The dark half of the turning day, from the last red of sunset to the first grey of dawn — when the stars come out and the world goes quiet and folk lie down to sleep. Night is wholly intangible, a darkness you pass through but cannot grasp. It keeps the high north, due up the intangible axis. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

I am the want of any light,
I fill the cellar and the cave;
strike a match and I am gone —
name the black that shadows crave.

East

An old tale of a hero bold,
of Arthur's sword or Robin's bow;
half true, and grown in the telling —
name the famous story. Now you know.

South

When darkness falls your eyelids drop,
your breathing slows, you drift away;
the deep still rest till morning light —
name the night's repose, I pray.

West

A waking daze, a fixed blank stare,
the mind gone far, the body still;
spellbound, lost, you do not move —
name the deep daze that holds your will.
