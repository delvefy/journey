The Older Dark

Below the warm dark the warmth was never yours to begin with. A step up, the blood-warm black at least had your body in it, standing, certain, the one made-of-nothing thing that was still a thing. Here even that is too new. This is the deep the warm dark named beneath itself — the dark from before the body, before there was a body to be warm or to stand or to be afraid, the dark that never once needed anyone to lie down in it and does not know it is being stood in now.

You put a thumb to a fingertip to find the whorl you carried the whole way, the one mark that needed no chalk — and the skin is harder to find than it was, the edges of you gone vague, as though the dark were older than the very idea of a surface. But the spiral has not gone. It is only no longer in your skin: it is in the dark, a slow vast turning you feel rather than touch, winding in the black around no centre at all — and you understand, the way you understand things here without being told, that your fingertip only ever copied this, that the whorl was turning down here long before there was a hand to carry it.

You listen for the heartbeat that was all there was above, the first clock, and it is faint now, far up and small, as though you had left it behind with the body it belonged to. Under it, slower, is the thing the heart was only copying in its turn: a long deep revolution with no edges and no haste, the dark turning over once in an age, keeping the count that was kept before there was a pulse to keep it. No drip. No bell. No breath. Only that, and you, unmade enough to hear it.

To the north the dark firms back toward a body and its blood, the warm close hand, the first clock loud again and the made thing about to be dreamed. To the east the deep stirs and warms toward presence, a someone gathering out of the dark before there was anyone, the shade that breathes. To the west the dark empties instead of deepens, the same nothing but cold with it, ash and the body already given up. To the south there is older dark still, before even this, before the turning, the deep before there was a deep. You were going somewhere once, to meet someone there. You have come to where the someone is not yet, and neither, quite, are you. The dark turns over once, slowly, keeps the count, and is in no hurry to begin.
