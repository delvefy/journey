70

You are seventy - seven whole tens stacked up in a tall round pile. You are a good age for a grandpa, the speed of a car on the motorway, a long way to count when you are small. You sit on the great counting road between sixty and eighty, exactly ten steps from each. No hand can hold you, for you are only a number - but a big, round, friendly one, the sort a child says proudly when they have counted very far indeed.
