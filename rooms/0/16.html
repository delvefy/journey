You are half of a hundred, five tens, two-and-a-half score - the speed sign on the road, the years of a golden wedding, the big round number on the birthday card. A made idea, plump and friendly. Halfway to a hundred, you feel like a milestone. Count to me, and you have counted a good long way.

North

Six tens together, count them up,
the seconds in a minute;
the minutes in an hour too -
how many are there in it?

East

Head over heels you roll downhill,
the acrobat's wild fall;
over and over, round you go -
a clumsy, jumbled sprawl.

South

Two score, four tens, the thieves in the tale,
the winks of a good long nap;
the days the rain came down on the ark -
how many, on the map?

West

The smell a thing gives off on the air,
the rose's, the bread's, the pine's;
the dog can follow it for miles -
a trail of tell-tale signs.
