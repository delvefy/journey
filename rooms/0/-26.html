Knuckle

You are a knuckle - the bony lump in the middle of a finger where it bends, the row of them that bulge when a fist is made. No hand makes you; the body builds you over the joint. You crack with a satisfying pop that makes grown-ups wince. You go white when the grip is tight and skin a graze when a fist meets a wall. Bony and bendy and faintly knobbly, you are the hinge of every finger, the knuckle that lets the hand curl into a fist.
