Eyebrow

You are an eyebrow - the little arched line of hair sitting above each eye. You keep the rain and the sweat from running down into the eye, and you do a great deal of talking without a word: raised high in surprise, drawn down low in a frown, waggled up and down to be funny. There are two of you, one over each eye, always working as a pair. Expressive and busy and easy to forget, you are the eyes' own pair of little flags, signalling every feeling that crosses a face.
