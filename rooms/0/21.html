100

You are a hundred - ten tens stacked up, the big round number children race to count to. You are a full mark on a test, the years in a century, the pennies in a pound. People say it when they mean a great many - a hundred times I told you. You cannot be touched, only counted and reached. You are the first really big number a small hand learns to write, three proud digits long, a milestone on the long climb of counting.
