Sleep

Each night it comes for you, soft and certain, and takes the day away. Sleep is not a place but a leaving - your eyes grow heavy, the room blurs, and you slip under into hours you will never remember. No one can hand it to you or hold it back for long; it arrives on its own, the body's quiet tide. You go down into the dark, and somewhere in there the dreams begin, and you rise in the morning made new.

North

I am the dark half of the day,
when sun is gone and stars hold sway;
the owls come out, the world grows still,
I bring the moon above the hill.

East

Whisper me low so none can hear,
I'm meant for just one trusted ear;
locked in your heart, I stay unknown -
a thing you keep, and keep alone.

South

At night I visit while you rest,
the strangest tales, the silliest quest;
you fly, you fall, you meet the dead,
all painted in your sleeping head.

West

I am a spark in night's wide dome,
a million miles from any home;
you join my dots to make a bear,
I twinkle, cold and far, up there.
