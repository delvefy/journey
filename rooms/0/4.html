Dream

Asleep, you walk through rooms that never were and speak with the long-gone, and none of it can be touched or kept. A dream is woven of nothing — no maker, no matter — and dissolves the moment you wake and reach for it. Wholly intangible, it sits due north, on neither the wild nor the wrought side of the world. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

When darkness comes and the eyes grow heavy
I steal across the bed;
the whole wide world goes far away
while you rest your weary head.

East

A little story of beasts that talk —
the fox, the crow, the hare —
it ends by teaching right from wrong,
a lesson tucked in there.

South

The softest way that words are sent,
no voice behind the sound;
lean in close to catch the breath
of secrets passed around.

West

When sleep is near I stretch your jaw
so wide you start to cry;
catch me from a neighbour's face —
you cannot pass me by.
