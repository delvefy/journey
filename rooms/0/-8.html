The Undivided

South of the unparting, you reach the dark before the parting. A step north the body sank, warmth and dark beginning to un-part, loosening back toward the one thing they were drawn from; here is that one thing — the primal pole, the absolute old dark before the body, before the first making, before warmth and night were ever told into two. There is no body to sink, for the body has not yet been drawn out; there is no dark to be in, for dark and warmth and you are all still the one undivided thing, the origin from which every separation would, much later, be parted. The deepest place, and the first: the undivided.

It is not lonely and not empty; it is before either, before there was a you to be lonely or an emptiness to be in. You are not in the undivided dark; you are the undivided dark, not yet parted into someone who could stand apart from it. And yet — the oldest refusal, the first distinction of all — the whorl in your skin turns, the single place where something is not quite the undivided, the first parting, the seed of every separateness to come. You draw the spiral and it is the first edge in the edgeless, the one mark by which the undivided begins, barely, to become a someone who could make a mark.

There is no count, for counting needs a two, and here all is one. And still your heart beats — the very first parting, the one stroke by which the undivided distinguishes a self from itself, the seed of all time in the timeless old dark. Each beat is the first beat, the first "this, not that," the warm refusal that begins to part a you from the one undivided dark.

To the north the undivided parts toward the unparting, a body beginning to be drawn from the dark. To the east the first wild stirs in the undivided, the seed of an other before there is even a self. To the south the primal pole absolute, before even the undivided, if before can be said. To the west the warmth tips toward cold and the Waste, the undivided souring toward un-being. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the body, before the parting, before there was a you to go or a someone to go with. Here is the one dark you were all parted from — and still the warm coil turns, the first edge, and still one beat keeps the first time, parting you, barely, from the undivided.
