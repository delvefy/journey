The Unparting

South of the bare dark, the body sinks below its own floor. A step north was the bedrock of being — a warm body in the old dark, present, certain, the least it takes to be. Here even that floor gives downward, and the warmth and the dark begin to un-part: not the body undone, as it would be undone in the cold west, but the body sinking back toward the one undivided thing it was drawn from, before warmth and dark were ever told apart. This is the edge of none approached from beneath — the going-down under being, where the bare you loosens toward the not-yet it rose out of.

It is not frightening. After the bare dark's plain certainty there is only a deepening of it, a settling lower, the warmth spreading out into the dark until you could not say where the body ends and the old night begins. You find the spiral whorled in the skin and hold to it — the one assertion that there is still a finger to hold it with, a you not yet sunk wholly into the undivided. You draw it and it stays, faint, the oldest mark carried down past the floor of being, the single thing that has not un-parted with the rest.

The heart, the first clock, beats slower here — not cold, not failing, only sinking, each stroke spaced wider as the body settles toward the dark it came from, the count loosening back toward the long unbroken stillness before there was a beat to break it. You hold to it as to the spiral. While it beats, you are still parted from the dark. You feel how near the not-beating is.

To the north the floor firms again to the bare dark, the body present and warm and sure. To the east the sinking dark stirs with the first wild, a warmth not yet alone, something beginning in the deep. To the south it sinks toward the primal pole, before the body, before the parting, the absolute old dark where warmth and night were never two. To the west the warmth leaches toward the cold and the Waste, the body not sinking now but undone. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before fire, before the name, before you were even parted from the dark. Here you sink back toward it, beat by widening beat — and still the warm coil turns in the skin, and still one slow stroke keeps you, for now, a body, and apart.
