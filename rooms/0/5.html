Sleep

Each night it takes you and you do not feel it come. Sleep closes the eyes, slows the breath, and hands you to your dreams; you cannot watch yourself begin it. It is wholly untouchable, the deep rest the body must have. It keeps the high, intangible north. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

When the sun goes down and the stars come out,
the dark, still half of every day;
the owl awakes, the town grows quiet —
I am the time you sleep away.

East

An old, old tale of gods and heroes,
told to explain the sea, the sky;
no one believes it happened now —
a grand made story. What am I?

South

While you lie sleeping, deep and still,
I play you pictures in your head;
strange and sweet, or dark with fear —
I vanish when you leave the bed.

West

A short light sleep in a comfy chair,
chin to your chest in the afternoon;
not deep, not long — you wake with a start —
that little nap, that gentle swoon.
