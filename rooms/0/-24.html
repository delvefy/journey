Kneecap

You are a kneecap - the little round bone that floats on the front of the knee, a smooth shield over the joint. You slide up and down as the leg bends and straightens, guarding the hinge beneath. No hand made you; the body grew you, a hard pebble of bone under the skin. You take the worst of every trip and tumble, scraped raw on the playground tarmac. You are the small brave armour of the knee, first to meet the ground when a child falls.
