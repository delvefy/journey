Heel

You are a heel - the hard round back of the foot, the part that lands first with every step. You take the thud of walking and running and jumping down; you rub sore in a stiff new shoe; you lift up high when a dancer goes on tiptoe. A puppy chews you off an old slipper. Grown of bone and tough skin, you are the sturdy back of the foot, knocking out the steady beat of every walk.
