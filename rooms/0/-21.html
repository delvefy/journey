Calf

You are a calf - the round soft muscle on the back of the lower leg, between the knee and the heel. You bunch up hard when a runner pushes off, and ache the morning after a long uphill walk. Stand on tiptoe and feel yourself tighten and rise. You are natural, grown not made, a part of the body's own machine. You are the engine of every step and jump and dash, the quiet muscle that does its work behind the leg, mostly unnoticed until it cramps.
