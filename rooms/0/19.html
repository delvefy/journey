80

You are eighty - the number that is eight tens, written 80, a good round sturdy number. No hand made the idea of you; you were here in the counting before anyone counted. You are how old the oldest granny may be, how many years a long life runs, how many pence in some small pile. You cannot be touched, only counted to. Climbing the ladder of tens, you are the rung between seventy and ninety, high up but not yet at the very top.
