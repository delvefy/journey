The Unturning Dark

Below the older dark, even the turning stops. A step up, the deep at least turned — a slow vast revolution with no centre, keeping the count that was kept before there was a pulse to keep it. Here there is not even that. This is the dark the older dark named beneath itself: before the turning, before there was a deep to turn, the still place from which the slow revolution had not yet begun to move. Nothing here has started. Nothing here is late. There is only the dark, and it is not going round, because going has not happened yet.

You look for yourself and find less than you brought. Above, the edges of you had gone vague; here there are no edges to lose, no surface, no thumb to lay against a fingertip, nothing of you firm enough to be found. And the spiral — the one mark, in the skin and then in the turning of the deep — is here too, but unturned: not a coil winding, only the bare possibility of a coil, the still point a spiral would start from if anything had begun to spin. You feel it the way you feel a held breath that is never let go. It is not turning. It is waiting to have ever turned.

There is no count to hear. The heartbeat was faint in the older dark, the slow revolution slower still beneath it; under both is this, where neither beats nor turns — no bell, no drip, no breath, and now not even the long deep wheeling that kept time before time, only the stillness the count would later copy. North the dark begins to turn, the slow revolution starting up, the first faint keeping of time. East the stillness stirs toward a someone, the first warmth leaning in out of the dark before there was anyone. South there is stiller dark yet, before even the waiting, the deep with not so much as a not-yet in it. West the dark goes cold instead of still, emptied, ash where there was never even a body to give up. You were going somewhere once, to meet someone there. Here is before the going, before the someone, before the where. The dark does not turn, and keeps no count, and has not yet begun to wait for you.
