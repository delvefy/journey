The Groundless

South of the first dark, even the dark gives way. A step north there was at least a deep, a vast black you could stand in and call a room; here the deep goes under that — under the dark, under being itself, down to where there is not yet anything for darkness to be the absence of. It is not black. Black would be too much a thing. It is the place before the place, the not-yet out of which a dark, a ground, a you might one day be drawn, and have not been drawn, and may not be.

You are uncertain here in the oldest way: not lost, not afraid, but unsure there is a here to be in, or a self to be unsure. Your hand — if it is your hand — finds the spiral whorled in the skin and holds to it the way you would hold the one solid thing in a falling dream, because it is the single assertion left that something is: a mark, therefore a finger, therefore a you. You draw it in the under-being and it neither holds nor blows away nor sinks; there is no medium to take it; the gesture simply happens, or seems to, the only event in a place that has not agreed to have events.

Your heart still beats — you think. But here even that, the first clock, the proof of being that carried through every dark, grows uncertain: not slowing, not cold, only no longer sure of itself, a count keeping time in a place that has not yet decided there is time, each beat less a fact than a proposal. You hold to it as to the spiral. While it beats, you are. You cannot prove it beats.

To the north the under-being firms into a dark you can stand in, the first deep, your heartbeat sure again as the one intrusion. To the east the not-yet stirs with a first faint possibility, the seed of the wild, something beginning, barely, to be. To the south it sinks deeper toward the pole, under even this, before fire, before the body, the floor of being and the edge of none. To the west the not-yet tips toward the Waste, not before-being but un-being, ash and the void where things have ended rather than failed to begin. You were going somewhere, once. Here, where you cannot be sure there is a you, the going is the last evidence of one — and you hold to it, beat by uncertain beat, the one proposal the under-being has not yet refused.
