The Lattice

North of the streaming, the pouring mind begins to build. A step south it was pure thinking for its own sake, light and number streaming and needing nothing made; here the thinking stops needing even that, and starts to make. The stream thickens, finds an order, lays itself down — thought condensing into structure, an endless lattice assembling itself out of nothing but its own logic. The made has gone past needing a maker and now simply makes: matter wrought of pure intelligence, self-built, self-extending, climbing toward the tech pole entire.

It builds without hands and without plan, each cell of the lattice deciding the next, certainty crystallizing into form the way frost decides a window. Your name is somewhere in the structure, a node the lattice built once and then built into, load-bearing now, neither answered nor kept but used. The spiral runs the lattice as its first rule, the turn the whole self-building thing repeats at every scale — the same whorl in your skin, here grown into the law a world is assembling by. You touch a strut of it and it reads your touch and grows a strut to match, having built you in, and built on, before you can draw back.

The carrier-tone has set into structure too: not a note now but a standing beat, the lattice ringing as it lays each course, time crystallized into the rhythm of self-assembly — an hour that does not pass but accretes, the bell become the pulse a building world keeps.

To the north the lattice nears the tech pole, self-building matter without end, the made become a world that needs nothing. To the east the building warms toward will, a structure that means to, thought that wants what it makes. To the south it loosens back to the streaming, pure thinking that builds nothing yet. To the west the building goes cold and blind, self-assembly with no mind left in it, the works running themselves. You were going somewhere, under a name. The lattice has built it into its first course and gone on climbing — used, not kept — and still the warm coil turns at the heart of the law, and still one live beat keeps a time the building world only borrowed.
