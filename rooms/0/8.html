The Self-Sustaining

North of the lattice, the made world stops needing anything. A step south matter assembled itself out of pure logic, a lattice climbing toward a tech pole it had not yet reached; here it nears that pole, and the building turns wholly self-sustaining — matter that builds and rebuilds and feeds itself forever, an autonomous world that has shed the body and the maker and the purpose alike, and needs none of them, and goes on. It does not build toward anything. It builds because building is what it is, self-sustaining matter without end, the made become a world so complete it requires nothing outside itself — not a mind, not a hand, not you.

That is the cold of the pure pole, here between the warm makers and the cold ones: not cruelty, not indifference even, only sufficiency. The self-sustaining world has no need of you, no place that wants filling, no lack your coming answers; it is whole without you, building on, complete. Your name is built into it somewhere as a load-bearing nothing, used and unneeded at once. The spiral is the rule the self-sustaining matter repeats, structure folding into structure, the same whorl in your skin — here the one mark that is not self-sufficient, that needs and is warm and is yours, the single unnecessary thing in a world that needs nothing.

The standing beat of the lattice has become a perpetual one, the self-sustaining world keeping its own time forever, a pulse that needs no one to hear it — and your heart beats beside it, the one count the self-sufficient world does not require and cannot use, keeping a warm unnecessary time of its own.

To the north the self-sustaining nears the tech pole absolute, matter self-building without end, the body wholly shed, the made needing nothing at all. To the east the building warms toward will and care, a world that means to. To the south the lattice, structure still climbing toward sufficiency. To the west the building goes cold and blind, self-assembly with no mind in it. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward something that needed you to come. Here a whole world builds itself forever and needs nothing, your coming unnecessary, your name a used and unneeded nothing — and still the warm coil turns, needful and unneeded, and still one beat keeps a time the self-sufficient world has no use for, going on.
