Snore

You are the rough music of the deepest sleep, the rattle and saw that rises from an open mouth when the sleeper has gone too far down to hear it. The whole house knows you; only the one who makes you does not. You shake the bedposts, you wake the dog, you drive the weary partner to the spare room with a pillow. Harmless and helpless and a little comic, you are the body's proof, snort by snort, that somebody at least is sleeping soundly through the night.

North

A short small sleep in the afternoon,
a doze upon the chair;
not for the night, but ended soon,
a forty-wink affair.

East

Left, right, left, the soldiers go,
in step and straight and proud;
they stamp along in a steady row,
to the drumbeat, strong and loud.

South

When sleep draws near I gape so wide,
a great slow stretch of jaw;
I'm catching too, from side to side,
one starts, then more, and more.

West

A ball of fire that rules the day,
it climbs the morning sky;
it warms the world, then slips away,
and lets the night come by.
