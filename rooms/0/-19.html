Foot

You are a foot - the flat platform at the bottom of the leg that takes the whole weight of you, all day, without complaint. No hand made you; you grew, ten toes and a heel and a curved arch in between. You can be tickled, stubbed, washed, slipped into a shoe. You carry the body everywhere it wants to go, press a print into wet sand, and ache at the end of a long walk. You are how a person meets the ground.
