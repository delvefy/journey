Slip

You are a slip - the quick skid of a foot that finds no grip: on wet tiles, on ice, on a banana peel in a cartoon. No hand makes you; a slick surface does, stealing the foot out from under you before you can catch it. You cannot be held, only suffered, all in half a second of windmilling arms. You slip on the path, slip in the bath, slip a word out you meant to keep in. You are the body's small betrayal, the ground saying gotcha.
