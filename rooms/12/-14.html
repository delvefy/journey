Bow

You are a bow - a knot of ribbon tied so it springs into two pretty loops with two trailing ends. You sit on top of a birthday present, you hold a plait in place, you tie up a shoelace so it won't come undone. Pull one end and you slip undone in a blink. Looped and pulled tight by a hand, neat and cheerful, you are the little flourish that turns a plain wrapped thing into something that looks like a gift.
