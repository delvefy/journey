Carpet

You are a great soft covering for the floor, woven thick and warm and laid from wall to wall. A hand weaves you and tacks you down. Bare feet sink into your pile; the cat kneads you; the crumbs hide in you. Red, blue, or patterned, you hush the footsteps and warm the room. In tales you even fly.

North

A little seat with no back at all,
on three or four short legs;
you perch on it to reach up high,
or sit to scrub the steps.

East

A long soft seat with cushioned back
where a family can flop;
you watch the telly sunk in it -
two or three can plop.

South

A box that slides in and out of a chest,
you pull it by its knob;
your socks and pants are folded there -
to shut it is your job.

West

A soft fat pad you sit upon
to soften a hard chair;
stuffed full of feathers, plumped up round -
the comfiest thing there.
