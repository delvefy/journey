Seven

You are the number seven, lucky and a little odd - the days in a week, the colours in the rainbow, the seas, the dwarfs, the wonders of the world. No hand makes you; the counting mind holds you, one more than six, one less than eight. Children count you on their fingers and one thumb. Famous in tales and rhymes.

North

Count your fingers, all of them,
held up on two spread hands;
count your toes, the same again -
how many? where it lands.

East

How many blind mice in the rhyme,
how many sides has a triangle got,
how many bears found Goldilocks?
Count them - it's the lot.

South

Bigger than a van, with mighty wheels,
it growls along the road;
it hauls the timber, stone, and steel -
a giant with its load.

West

A sheet of questions, hard or fair,
to show how much you know;
no talking, no peeking at your friend -
just answers in a row.
