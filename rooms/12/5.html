Munch

You are the loud happy chewing of crunchy food - 'munch-munch-munch' go the jaws on a carrot, an apple, a fistful of crisps. No hand makes you; the busy teeth and the open mouth do it, loud enough to hear across the room. The rabbit munches the lettuce; the horse munches the hay.

North

After a run your breath comes fast,
in and out, in and out;
the dog does it with its tongue hung low -
you puff and gasp about.

East

Grind it round and round with your teeth,
mash the food up small;
before you swallow, do it well -
don't gulp it down at all.

South

The sound and spray when something drops
into a pool or puddle;
'SPLOOSH!' the water flies up high -
you jump in for a muddle.

West

The noisy way to drink your soup
or suck a noodle up;
'SLLLRP!' you go against the spoon -
ill-mannered with a cup.
