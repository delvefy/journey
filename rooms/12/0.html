Truck

You are a great roaring road-beast, bigger than a van, with mighty wheels and a long flat back for heavy loads. A hand builds you to haul - timber, stone, steel, a whole house of furniture. You growl up the motorway, gears grinding, headlamps blazing. The little cars pull aside to let you pass.

North

Count the colours in the rainbow's bow,
count the days that make a week;
count them up, you'll always find
the same fine number you seek.

East

A great big boat that crosses the sea,
with decks and a tall mast or two;
it carries folk and cargo far -
a steamer with a funnel's plume.

South

A tube you hold inside your hand
that shines a beam of light;
click it on beneath the sheets
to read your book at night.

West

A boxy car with no back seats,
just room for boxes to ride;
it brings the parcels to your door -
the postman's friend outside.
