Plane

You are a plane - a great metal bird with two wide wings and roaring engines, built to carry people through the sky. Many hands built you; you thunder down the runway, faster and faster, then tilt your nose up and climb above the clouds. You can be boarded, buckled into, watched as a tiny silver cross far overhead. You shrink the whole world, turning a journey of months into a single afternoon. You are how a person flies, sitting still in a chair while the land slides by below.
