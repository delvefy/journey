Zigzag

You are a zigzag - a line that will not go straight, jagging hard left then hard right, a row of sharp little mountains laid on their side. A pencil or a frightened hare can draw you, cutting one way then snapping back the other to throw a chaser off. You are lightning's shape and the crack in old glass and the stitch along a hem. Never the short way, always the busy one, you get there by darting everywhere.
