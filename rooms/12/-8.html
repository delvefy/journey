Wig

You are false hair worn upon the head - the judge's grey curls, the clown's orange mop, the actor's wild disguise, the cover for a bald patch. Made strand by strand by patient hands. Pop me on, and you are someone else entirely. Take me off, and the secret hangs there, limp, on its stand.

North

A soft warm cloth around the shoulders,
knitted, with no sleeves;
Granny wears one by the fire -
it wraps and warms and weaves.

East

A thin soft cloth across the face,
the bride wears one of white;
you see right through its misty net -
it hides her, soft and light.

South

The arm of a coat or a shirt,
you push your hand right through;
short for summer, long for cold -
two of them on you.

West

A long loose cape with a hood behind,
that wraps you head to heel;
the wizard, traveller, and spy
pull it close to conceal.
