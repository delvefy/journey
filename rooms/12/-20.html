Bus

You are a bus - the big long road vehicle with rows of seats, made to carry a whole crowd at once. You pull in at the stop with a hiss of doors, swallow the queue, and rumble off to the next. People ring the bell to be let down; children scramble for the front seat upstairs. You run your route over and over, rain or shine, packed at rush hour and empty late at night. You are a little moving room full of strangers, all going the same way.
