Locket

You are a locket - a tiny hinged case worn on a chain around the neck. Press the little catch and you open like a clam, and inside lives a small secret: a curl of hair, a photo of someone loved, a face kept close to the heart. You shut again with a soft click and hide it all away. Made small and precious by a hand, warm from the skin you rest on, you are a secret you can wear.
