Raft

You are a raft - a flat float of logs or barrels lashed together with rope, the simplest boat there is, pushed out across a lake or down a slow river with a long pole. No hull, no sides, just a flat deck riding low in the water with your feet getting wet. Castaways build you; children build you for the pond. Flat and bobbing and a little bit risky, you are the boat anyone can make from what floats, riding the water by sitting right on top of it.
