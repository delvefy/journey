Splash

You are the sound and the spray when something hits the water - the stone dropped in the pond, the diver off the board, the welly stamped in the puddle. Weight meets water and 'SPLOOSH!', up the drops fly and out the rings spread. You soak the watcher, you delight the child.

North

The loud crunchy chewing of something hard -
a carrot, an apple, a crisp;
'munch-munch-munch' go your busy jaws -
a sound both loud and brisk.

East

The hard sharp sound of snow or gravel
that breaks beneath your tread;
or a crisp apple bitten through -
'CRUNCH!' the hard thing said.

South

The long slow squeak an old door makes
on a stiff and rusty hinge;
the floorboard groans beneath your foot -
it sets your teeth on edge.

West

The dull deep sound a heavy thing
makes falling on the floor;
no ring, no clang, just 'THUD' it lands -
a flat sound, nothing more.
