Vault

You are a vault - to leap clean over something by planting your hands on top and swinging your legs across. Over the gate, over the gym horse, over the wall: down go the hands, up go the legs, and you are on the other side in one smooth bound. Gymnasts make it beautiful; a hurrying child just gets over the fence. Bold and springy and quick, you are jumping with a helping push, the fastest way over any barrier that is too high to simply step across.
