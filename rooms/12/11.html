Pour

You are a pour - the steady stream of liquid tipped from one place into another. You pour the milk on the cereal, the tea from the pot; the rain pours from the sky in a great grey rush. Tip the jug, and out you come in a smooth bright rope of water that splashes and fills. You are all motion and direction, the act of one thing flowing out to fill another up.
