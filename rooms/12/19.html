Creep

You are creep - to move so slowly, softly, and low that not a soul can hear you coming. The cat creeps up on the bird, belly to the ground; the burglar creeps through the dark house; the ivy creeps up the wall an inch a week. You are stealth made into motion, every step careful and quiet. A scary feeling can creep over you too, slow and cold. You are the slow secret advance of anything that does not want to be noticed.
