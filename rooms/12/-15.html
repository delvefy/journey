Rocket

You are a rocket - a tall pointed tower of metal that stands on the launch pad, packed with fire, and roars straight up into the sky. A great many hands built you; at the countdown you light your engines and ride a pillar of flame faster and faster, up past the clouds, up past the air, all the way to the stars. You can be touched only on the ground; once you go, you are gone. You are how a person reaches for space.
