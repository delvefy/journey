Crawl

You are a crawl - the slow low travel on hands and knees, the first way a baby ever moves across the floor. You go where legs won't fit: under the bed, through the tunnel, into the den. Soldiers do you in the mud; swimmers do you through the water, arm over arm. You are not proud or fast, but you get there. You are motion brought down to the ground, patient and close to the earth.
