Train

You are a train - the long line of carriages that runs on two steel rails, hauled by a roaring engine. You whistle into the station, doors hiss open, and crowds pour on and off. You thunder through tunnels and over bridges, the fields blurring past the windows. Steam ones puffed great clouds; the new ones glide near-silent and fast. You carry hundreds at once from town to town. You are the great iron snake of the railway, keeping always to your track.
