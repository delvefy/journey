Climb

You are the going-up by hand and foot - the tree, the stairs two at a time, the rope in the gym, the mountain's long haul. No easy thing makes you; effort does, pulling your own weight skyward. Up and up, hold by hold. Look down from the top, breathless, and there is the whole world, smaller.

North

Stick out one finger, straight and stiff,
to show the way to go;
'Look over there!' your finger says -
though rude to do it, so.

East

Stretch your arm out, far as it goes,
up to the topmost shelf;
on tiptoe, fingers spread out wide -
to get it for yourself.

South

Press it tight in both your hands
till the juice or air comes out;
a hug too hard, a sponge wrung dry,
the toothpaste tube, no doubt.

West

Reach out fast and snatch it tight,
your fingers close around;
you grab the rail before you fall -
quick hands that hold what's found.
