Medal

You are the round disc of metal on a striped ribbon, hung round the neck of the one who won - gold for first, silver for second, bronze for third. Made to be earned and shown. You shine on the chest of the soldier, the swimmer, the spelling champ. Bite me, they say, to check that I am real.

North

The arm of a coat or a shirt,
you push your hand right through;
short for summer, long for cold -
two of them on you.

East

A big gold cup you lift up high
when your team has won;
they pour the drinks in it sometimes -
a prize for what you've done.

South

A little gold loop or a dangling gem,
you hang it from your ear;
a pair you wear, one on each side -
a sparkle, far and near.

West

A little pin you wear with pride,
on your coat or on your chest;
it shows your club, your name, your win -
the gold star for the best.
