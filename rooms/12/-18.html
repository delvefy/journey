Car

You are a car - the four-wheeled box of metal and glass that carries a family down the road. You drink petrol and breathe out smoke, you growl when you start and purr when you cruise. A key or a button wakes you; a wheel and two pedals steer and stop you. You sit in the drive all night and take everyone to school by day. You are the most everyday machine there is, a room on wheels that goes wherever the road leads.
