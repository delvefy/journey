Fall

You are a fall - the quick helpless drop when balance lets go and the ground rushes up to meet you. You happen when a foot slips on ice, when a branch gives under a climber, when a leaf finally lets go of its tree. You are a motion, not a thing to hold, the going-down that nobody chooses. Sometimes you mean the whole season, when all the leaves come loose at once. You are gravity having its sudden, certain way.
