Leap

You are a leap - the great big jump that flings the whole body up or across, both feet leaving the ground at once, the frog off the bank, the dancer through the air, the brave hop over the puddle. No tool makes you; strong legs and a moment of daring do. For one breath you are flying, and then you land. Bold and bounding and free, you are the jump at its biggest, the body briefly throwing off the ground to sail through empty air.
