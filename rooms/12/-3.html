Stool

You are a small simple seat with no back and no arms, just a flat top on three or four sturdy legs. A hand saws and nails you together. You stand by the breakfast bar, by the piano, in the milking shed. You wobble if one leg is short. A child stands on you to reach the high shelf. Plain and low and steady.

North

A little round ball stuck on a door,
you grip it, give a turn;
it works the latch and lets you in -
a small round thing to learn.

East

A long hard seat for several folk,
of wood or iron slats;
you find one in the park or church -
sit in a row, like that.

South

A big soft cover for the floor
that runs from wall to wall;
soft underfoot, in red or blue -
it warms and hushes all.

West

A tall smooth jar of glass or clay
that holds a bunch of flowers;
fill it with water, set it down -
the blooms last days and hours.
