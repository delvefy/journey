Drawer

You are a sliding box built into a chest or a desk, pulled out by a knob and pushed back in. A hand dovetails you and fits you on your runners. You hold the folded socks, the spare string, the odds and ends nobody knows where else to put. You stick on a damp day. The slide-out box where things are kept and lost.

North

A big soft cover for the floor
that runs from wall to wall;
soft underfoot, in red or blue -
it warms and hushes all.

East

A tall box with a door and shelves
where the plates and cups are kept;
Old Mother Hubbard's was quite bare -
hers held nothing, except...

South

Two rows of little metal teeth
that lock together tight;
you pull the tab and 'zzzip!' it goes -
to do your coat up right.

West

A little flat thing by the door
to wipe your dirty feet;
it lies before the step all day -
'wipe!' it says, 'be neat.'
