Point

You are the straight stiff finger that shows the way - 'over there,' 'that one,' 'look!' No word needed; just the arm flung out and the world's attention dragged along it. Rude, they say, to do it at people. But how else to send a friend's eyes exactly where you mean? Aim me, and all heads turn.

North

A sharp loud blast of air through the nose,
the laugh that won't stay in;
the pig does it, the horse does too -
a snuffle and a din.

East

Bend down low on both your knees,
to pray or do up a shoe;
down on the floor you settle there -
knees taking weight from you.

South

Up the tree or up the stairs,
hand over hand you go;
the mountain, ladder, garden wall -
you reach the top, not low.

West

The silent walk on the balls of the feet,
heels held high off the ground;
you creep past the sleeping baby -
not making one small sound.
