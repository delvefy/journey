Sneak

You are a sneak - to move so quietly and slyly that nobody notices you are there. You ease the door open an inch, step over the creaky stair, and pad down to the kitchen in the dark for one last biscuit. You hold your breath when someone stirs. Half the fun of you is not being caught; the other half is the secret. You are tiptoe and cunning rolled together, the careful art of getting somewhere unseen.
