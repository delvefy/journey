Turn

You are a turn - the going-round to face a new way. You spin the key in the lock, you flip the page, you swing the steering wheel, you wheel right round when someone calls your name. In a game it is yours and then the next person's: 'my turn!' No hand can hold you - you are the quarter-circle, the half-circle, the whole-circle of facing somewhere new, the small swivel that points a thing in a brand-new direction.
