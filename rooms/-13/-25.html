narwhal

A narwhal is the strangest whale of all - a grey speckled creature of the freezing northern seas, with one long spiral tusk that juts straight out from its head like the horn of a unicorn. That tusk is really a giant tooth, and the narwhal swims under the arctic ice with it leading the way. For hundreds of years sailors brought the tusks home and sold them as proof that unicorns were real. The unicorn of the sea, hiding at the top of the world.

North

A great white bear on the arctic ice,
I swim the freezing sea for seal;
my fur is thick, my paws are huge -
the biggest bear, both fierce and real.

East

A dog with curly fluffy fur,
clipped in puffs and pompom style;
fancy and posh at the dog show -
I prance along with a haughty smile.

South

A black-and-white bird that cannot fly,
I waddle on ice and dive in the sea;
I huddle for warmth in a big cold crowd -
in a smart little suit, that's me.

West

A baby cat, all soft and small,
I chase a ball of wool for fun;
I mew and pounce and curl to sleep -
a tiny purring fluff, well done.
