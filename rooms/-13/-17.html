Shoulder

You are a shoulder - the broad strong joint where the arm meets the body, the place that carries and shrugs and gives a small child a ride above the crowd. You hold up the heavy bag and the long day; you droop when you are tired and square up when you are brave. A friend cries on you and a hand rests on you to say well done. You shrug when you do not know. You are the body's sturdy yoke, the carrier of burdens and the giver of rides.
