Gorilla

You are a gorilla - the biggest of all the apes, black and broad and powerfully strong, knuckling along the floor of the green jungle. You beat your great chest with both fists when you want to be heard, and grunt and hoot to your family. For all your size you are gentle, eating leaves and shoots, cradling your babies, dozing in the warm shade. The mighty, soft-hearted giant of the deep wild forest.
