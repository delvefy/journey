Echo

You are an echo - a sound thrown back. Shout in a cave or a long stone hall and the hall answers in your own voice, the same words coming home a heartbeat late, fainter each time. You are made by no hand, yet you are no thing a hand can hold - only a returning, a copy of a cry. Call out, and wait, and the world says it back to you.
