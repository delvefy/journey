Wolf

You are the wild grey dog of the forest, lean and quick, that hunts in a pack and sings to the moon. No hand made you; the wilderness did. You are the big bad one of a hundred tales, huffing at the door, prowling the dark wood. But it is your long lonely howl, not your teeth, that the night remembers.

North

A bird that spreads a great wide fan
of feathers, blue and green,
with shining eyes upon its tail -
the proudest ever seen.

East

A flat-tailed swimmer of the stream
that gnaws the trees right down;
it builds a dam of sticks and mud -
a builder, wet and brown.

South

A desert beast with a humpy back,
that goes for days with no drink;
it carries folk across the sand -
the ship of dunes, you'd think.

West

A great brown beast with flat broad antlers,
that wades in northern lakes;
the biggest deer there is, they say -
a heavy step it takes.
