glum

Glum is the quiet grey kind of sad - not crying, just down in the dumps, chin in your hands, staring at nothing. The rain has stopped play, your friend has gone home, there is nothing on the telly; and so you sit there feeling glum. It is sulky and droopy and a bit sorry for itself. A biscuit and a kind word usually help. It is the mood of a wet Sunday afternoon when nothing at all is going right.

North

The warm glad feeling when you've done well,
chest puffed out, a great big smile;
Mum and Dad feel it when you win -
pleased as punch, it lasts a while.

East

When a tickle on your skin won't stop
and you simply have to scratch and scratch;
a midge's bite, a woolly jumper -
that scritchy feeling, hard to catch.

South

Spun round and round till the room won't stop,
all dizzy and silly and light in the head;
wibbly-wobbly, can't walk straight -
too much excitement, it's said.

West

Too timid to say a word out loud,
you hide behind your mum's warm leg;
cheeks gone pink, you watch your shoes -
too bashful even to beg.
