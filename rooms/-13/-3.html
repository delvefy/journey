Dolphin

You are a sleek grey swimmer of the warm seas, leaping and spinning in the bow-wave, clicking and whistling to your friends. No hand makes you; you are a clever, breathing mammal of the deep, not a fish at all. You herd the shoals, you save the drowning in old tales, you grin your bottle-nosed grin. Playful and bright.

North

A wobbly blob of see-through jelly
that drifts upon the tide;
it trails long stinging threads below -
no bones to hold inside.

East

A soft sea-beast with eight long arms
that creeps along the deep;
it squirts black ink to hide and flee -
in rocky holes it'll keep.

South

A little water-lizard, smooth and dark,
that swims in the springtime pond;
it walks on land, it breathes the air,
of damp it's very fond.

West

A sea-beast clad in a hard dark shell,
with two great snapping claws;
it scuttles backward over the rocks -
and turns bright red when caught.
