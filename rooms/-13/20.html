Throb

You are a throb - a steady painful beating that comes and goes in time with the heart. You live in a bumped thumb, a bad tooth, a hot bruise, swelling and easing, swelling and easing, the body's own slow drum of hurt. No hand makes you; the pulse does, knocking against the sore place again and again. You cannot be seen, only felt, and counted, almost. You are pain with a rhythm, the dull insistent beat that says here, here, here.
