Tooth

You are a tooth - one of the hard white pegs set in a row in your jaw, made for biting and chewing. No hand made you; you pushed up through the gum all by yourself, and the first set falls out for the fairy and a coin. You can be brushed, lost, knocked out, filled. You crunch the apple and the toast, you flash in a grin, and you ache like nothing else when something goes wrong. You are the body's own little millstone.
