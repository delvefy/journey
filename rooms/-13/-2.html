Jellyfish

You are a wobbly see-through blob that drifts on the tide, no bones, no eyes, no brain - just a soft bell of jelly trailing long stinging threads. No hand makes you; you pulse and float wherever the sea takes you. Touch you washed up on the sand and you sting still. Ghostly and shapeless.

North

A long curved tooth that juts right out,
the elephant has a pair;
the walrus too, the wild boar -
they dig and fight with their share.

East

A five-armed creature on the sand,
shaped like a fallen star;
it clings to rocks beneath the tide -
a sea-thing, strange and far.

South

A smooth grey swimmer of the sea
that leaps and clicks and plays;
clever and friendly, it rides the bow-wave -
through the bright blue bays.

West

A long thin fish like a swimming snake,
all slippery, smooth, and grey;
it hides in the mud of the river-bed
and slithers right away.
