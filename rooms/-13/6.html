Nightmare

You are the bad dream that grips you in the dark - the chase you cannot run from, the fall with no floor, the monster at the foot of the bed. No hand makes you; the sleeping mind does, out of its own deep fears. You feel so real until you wake. Switch on the light, and I dissolve to nothing at all.

North

When you spin fast or lose your specs,
the world goes soft and smeared;
the speeding car is just a streak,
its sharp edges disappeared.

East

What jumps out when you least expect,
the gift you didn't know;
the friends who hid and shouted loud -
your mouth makes a round O.

South

The green and gnawing want inside
for what is not your own;
their bike, their slice, their bigger prize -
it gnaws you to the bone.

West

The nagging 'maybe-not' inside,
the feeling you're not sure;
you half-believe and half do not -
unsteady, insecure.
