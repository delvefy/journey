Finger

You are a finger - one of the five clever bendy digits on your hand, with a knuckle to fold it and a nail on the tip. No hand made you; you grew, and you point, poke, count, tickle, grip, and pick things up. You can be pricked, crossed for luck, held up to say one, dipped in the cake mix. You wear a ring and you wag a warning. Of all the body's tools you are the busiest and most useful, one of the hand's ten little helpers.
