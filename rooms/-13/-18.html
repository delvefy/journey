Neck

You are a neck - the bendy stalk that holds the head up above the shoulders and lets it turn this way and that. You swallow the food down, carry the voice up, and wear the scarf in winter. A giraffe's is enormously long; a swan's curves like a question mark. Crane me to see over a wall; risk me on a daring climb. You are the slender bridge between head and body, the busy little tower that lets you look around the world.
