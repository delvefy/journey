Collarbone

You are the collarbone - the long slim bone that runs straight across the top of the chest, from shoulder to throat. Press the soft hollow above your chest and you will feel me, a smooth hard ridge just under the skin. I brace the shoulders apart so the arms can swing wide. Of all the buried bones I am one of the easiest to find with a finger. I am the strut across the top of you, the bone you can trace without looking.
