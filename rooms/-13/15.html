Mood

You are a mood - the weather inside a person, the way you feel right now: sunny and bright, or grey and grumpy, or stormy and cross. No hand makes you; it just settles, sometimes for no reason at all, and colours everything you do. You cannot be touched, only caught, like a cold passed round a room. A good mood lifts the whole house; a bad one sours it. Always changing, never asked for, you are the private weather a person carries about inside.
