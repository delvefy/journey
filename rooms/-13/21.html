Dizzy

You are dizzy - the swimming, tilting feeling when the world will not hold still, after a spin on the roundabout or a hop up too fast from bed. No one chooses you; a spinning head and a confused inside ear make you. The floor seems to lean, the walls go soft, and you grab for something solid and giggle or groan. A giddy, reeling, sea-sick feeling, you are the price of spinning too long, and the proof that the body likes the ground to stay put.
