Envy

You are the green and gnawing want for what is not yours - your friend's new bike, your sister's bigger slice, the prize that went to someone else. No hand makes you; the wanting heart does. You sour the good you have by staring at the better next door. Look away, they say, and you fade. Look on, and you grow.

North

The bad dream in the dead of night
that wakes you with a cry;
monsters chase, you cannot run -
till morning lights the sky.

East

The flat grey nothing of a rainy day
with not one thing to do;
you sigh and slump and stare at the wall -
the slow hours crawling through.

South

The spitting hiss of frying food,
the bacon in the pan;
rain on hot coals will make it too -
that sound since fire began.

West

The heavy bad feeling deep inside
when you've done a thing that's wrong;
you broke the vase and hid the bits -
it weighs on you all day long.
