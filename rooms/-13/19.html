Snowball

You are a snowball - the round packed lump of snow a child scoops up in cold red hands and squeezes hard until it holds. You fly through the frosty air and burst soft against a coat, or pack down icy and sting. Roll one along the ground and it gathers and grows until it tops a snowman. You last only as long as the cold does, then sag into a wet grey nothing. You are winter made into a thing to throw.
