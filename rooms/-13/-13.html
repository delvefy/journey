Shin

You are a shin - the long hard bone down the front of the lower leg, with hardly any padding over it. That is why you hurt so much when you crack into the coffee table in the dark, or a football thumps you. You wear the shin-pad in football for exactly this reason. Grown of bone, close beneath the skin, you are the brave front edge of the leg that meets every bump the world leaves lying in the way.
