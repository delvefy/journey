Blister

You are a blister - the sore little bubble of fluid that swells up under the skin where a new shoe rubbed or a hot pan touched, a tender dome you mustn't pop. No hand makes you; friction or heat does, and the skin lifts to make a tiny cushion over the hurt. You sting at every step. Left alone, you flatten and heal; burst too soon, and it stings worse. Tender and bubbly and sore to the touch, you are the skin's small shield over a hot or rubbed-raw spot.
