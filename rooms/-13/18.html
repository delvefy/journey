Snowflake

You are a snowflake - a tiny six-armed star of ice that drifts down from a winter sky, no two of you ever quite alike. You are born high in the cold clouds, spun into lace as you fall, and you melt the moment you land on a warm hand. Millions of you together blanket the world in white. You settle on eyelashes and gather on the windowsill. You are winter's most delicate gift, a fragile crystal star too light to make a sound.
