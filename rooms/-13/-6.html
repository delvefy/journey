Parrot

You are the bright bird that talks like a person, red and green and gold, perched on the pirate's shoulder. No hand made you; the hot jungle did. You crack nuts in your hooked beak and shout the words you have learned, over and over. 'Pieces of eight!' Teach me a phrase, and I will say it back for years.

North

Wings of colour on the summer air,
once a crawling thing;
it sips the flowers with a curling tongue -
on the breeze, light as a wing.

East

A soft white bird that stands for peace,
it coos up in the tree;
set free at weddings, fluttering high -
as gentle as can be.

South

A bird that spreads a great wide fan
of feathers, blue and green,
with shining eyes upon its tail -
the proudest ever seen.

West

A grey town bird that pecks for crumbs
on the pavement and the square;
it coos and struts and bobs its head -
it nests up anywhere.
