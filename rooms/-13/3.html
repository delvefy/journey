Patter

You are the soft quick tapping of light things falling or running - the rain on the roof and the window, the little feet hurrying down the hall, pit-pat, pit-pat. No hand makes you; rain and small swift feet make you, light and fast and many.

North

The spitting hissing sound of frying -
sausages in the pan,
bacon curling, oil that pops -
'ssss', as hot as it can.

East

A low deep rolling growl of sound -
far thunder, a hungry tum,
a lorry on the cobbled road -
it goes on, rrr, like a drum.

South

The snap and pop of a burning log,
or dry leaves underfoot;
'crackle-snap' the bonfire spits -
a sound of burning wood.

West

The bubbling glugging sound of water
down a drain or a stream;
a baby makes it, a tummy too -
'glug-glug,' a watery theme.
