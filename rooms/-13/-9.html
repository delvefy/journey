Camel

You are the great humped beast of the desert, swaying across the dunes for days without a drink, your hump a larder of fat. No hand made you; the dry sand did. The ship of the desert, they call you - slow, tall, and grumpy, with long lashes against the blowing sand. Kneel, and let the traveller climb aboard.

North

A wild grey dog that hunts in packs,
it howls up at the moon;
it huffed and puffed at three pigs' doors -
a fierce and hungry tune.

East

A big brown beast with heavy paws,
that growls and stands up tall;
it loves the honey, sleeps all winter -
the biggest beast of all.

South

A great black ape that beats its chest,
the biggest of them all;
it knuckles through the jungle deep -
huge, and strong, and tall.

West

A patient beast, half horse, half donkey,
that carries heavy packs;
it plods up the mountain path -
sure-footed on the tracks.
