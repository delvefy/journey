Sleepy

You are sleepy - the warm heavy feeling that creeps over the eyes at the end of the day, the lids growing too heavy to hold, the head nodding toward the chest, the yawns coming one after another. No one chooses you; a long day and a soft chair do. You make the bravest child go quiet and the busiest grown-up doze on the sofa. Warm and drowsy and slow, you are the gentle pull toward bed, the body asking, please, to be allowed to sleep.
