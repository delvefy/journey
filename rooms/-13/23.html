Cosy

You are cosy - warm and snug and wrapped up safe, with nothing in the world to trouble you. A blanket over your knees, the fire crackling, rain on the window and you tucked up dry inside: that is cosy. A kitten curled in a basket is cosy; a bed on a winter night is cosy. Soft and warm and gently happy, you are comfort you can feel all over, the lovely settled feeling of being exactly warm enough, in exactly the right snug little spot.
