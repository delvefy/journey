Tan

You are tan - a soft warm light brown, the colour of sand, of dry grass, of biscuits and milky tea. You are the colour skin goes after days in the summer sun, and the shade of a well-worn leather shoe. Not as dark as brown, not as pale as cream, you are a gentle, earthy, in-between colour. Quiet and cosy, you are the hue of warm and sun-baked things.
