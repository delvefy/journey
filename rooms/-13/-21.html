Bruise

You are a bruise - the tender purple-blue mark that blooms under the skin where a knee met a table or a ball met a shin. No one means to make you; a knock does, and the skin stays whole but goes sore and dark beneath. Day by day you ripen through blue and green to a sickly yellow, then fade away. You ache when pressed and tell the tale of every tumble. A quiet badge of clumsiness, you spread and dim and finally forgive.
