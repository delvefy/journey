Cheek

You are a cheek - the soft round side of the face. You go pink in the cold and red with a blush, you puff out fat when a breath is held, you crease up when a grin spreads wide. A granny pinches you, a kiss lands on you, a tear rolls down you. Warm and smooth and rosy, grown of the living face, you are the soft place the whole world reaches for when it wants to show you love.
