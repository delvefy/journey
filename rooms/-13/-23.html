Graze

You are a graze - the raw red scrape left when skin is dragged across something rough. A tumble off the bike, a slide on the playground, a knee skidded along the path: out you come, stinging and weeping little beads. You are not deep like a cut, just sore and pink and gritty. A wash, a dab and a plaster, and in a few days you scab over and heal. Common as can be and quick to mend, you are the everyday badge of an adventurous afternoon spent falling over and getting up again.
