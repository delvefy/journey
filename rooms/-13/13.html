Memory

You are a memory - a piece of the past kept safe inside the head. You are the smell of a summer holiday, the face of a grandparent, the day the puppy came home, all stored away to be taken out and looked at again. Some of you are bright as new; some go soft and faded at the edges. No hand can hold you - you live only in the mind, the warm bright keepsake a person carries of everything that has already happened.
