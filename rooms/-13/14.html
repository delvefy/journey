Guess

You are a guess - an answer you reach for when you do not really know, a leap of the mind across a gap. No hand makes you; the brain does, in a hurry, filling in what it cannot quite see for certain. You cannot be held or proved, only made and then checked. You guess how many sweets are in the jar, what the wrapped present is, the answer to the riddle. Sometimes you are right and feel clever; mostly you are just a hopeful try.
