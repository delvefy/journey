Giddy

You are giddy - light in the head and reeling, the world spinning round even after you've stopped. You go giddy from too much twirling, from a fast ride, from a fit of the giggles that just won't stop. The floor seems to tilt and you wobble and grin and grab for something to hold. Dizzy and silly and a little bit wobbly, you are the lovely whirling muddle of too much fun all at once, when laughing and spinning leave your head turning long after your feet have stopped.
