Thigh

You are a thigh - the thick strong upper leg between the hip and the knee, holding the body's biggest muscle. You do the heavy work: pushing up out of a chair, climbing the hill, kicking the ball hard. Slap one when you laugh; rub one when it aches. You are natural, grown not made, a pillar of meat and bone. Two of you carry a whole person all day long, and never once think to complain.
