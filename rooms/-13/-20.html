Scab

You are a scab - the hard brown crust that forms over a cut while the skin mends beneath. You are the body's own plaster, dried blood turned to a little shield, holding the edges shut while new skin grows. No hand made you; the wound did, sealing itself. You itch, and you must not be picked, though everybody picks. When the healing is done you flake away and leave clean pink skin. You are the rough small armour the body builds in a hurry.
