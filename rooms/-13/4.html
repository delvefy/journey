Sizzle

Drop something cold into hot fat and the pan answers at once - a sizzle is the sound of heat hard at work. Bacon in the morning, rain on a bonfire, a sparkler hissing down to its stick. It is busy and bright and makes your mouth water before you even see the food. You cannot hold it; it lives only while the heat does, then fades to nothing. Few sounds promise so much, so quickly, as that first eager hiss of the pan.

North

The green-eyed feeling
when your friend has more;
you want what they have
and it makes your heart sore.

East

The sound of a snake,
or a kettle's steam,
or air from a tyre -
a long thin scream.

South

The light quick sound
of rain on the roof,
or small running feet -
tip-tap, soft proof.

West

The colour of the sun
and a buttercup's glow,
of a lemon and a chick
and the corn in a row.
