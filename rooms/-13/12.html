Promise

You are a promise - a word given and meant to be kept. You are the 'I will' sealed with a crossed heart, the vow not to tell, the pledge to come back. You weigh nothing and you weigh everything: light as a breath when made, heavy as stone if broken. No hand can hold you, yet a whole friendship can rest on you. Keep me, and trust grows; break me, and it is very hard to mend.
