Polarbear

You are a polar bear - the great white bear of the frozen north, bigger than any other bear, padding across the ice on huge furry paws. You swim through the icy sea after seals, your thick white coat keeping out the bitter cold. A mother digs a snow den for her tiny cubs. Powerful and patient and beautifully white against the snow, you are the king of the arctic, roaming the endless ice at the very top of the world.
