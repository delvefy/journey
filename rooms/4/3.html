Anthem

The great song of a people, sung standing with the flag raised high. An anthem is made of words and music and pride; it belongs to a whole nation at once and yet weighs nothing in the hand. It keeps the made north-east. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

A story set to a simple tune,
sung verse by verse till the tale is done;
of doomed lovers or outlaws bold —
the minstrel's story-song. Which one?

East

The line that comes back every verse,
the part the whole crowd sings along;
'fa-la-la' or 'sing we now' —
the chorus that repeats the song.

South

A single sound with a name of its own,
struck on the key or sung up high;
string a row of us together —
a tune is born. Each one am I.

West

A merry song for the midwinter feast,
at the door, in the snow, by candle-flame;
'God rest ye' and 'the holly green' —
the Christmas song. Now speak its name.
