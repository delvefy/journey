Cheer

The home team scores, and the whole stand stands up as one and roars. A cheer is a noise you make on purpose - the opposite of a boo - flung up from a hundred throats at once to say well done, keep going, we are with you. You cannot keep it; it lasts a few seconds and is gone. But it lifts the tired runner, fills the small singer with courage, and turns a crowd of strangers into one glad voice.

North

A coin made to vanish, a card from a sleeve,
clever surprises that fool and deceive;
a magician's whole act, or a prank on a friend,
sneaky and quick from beginning to end.

East

At the end of the match they read me out,
how many goals is what I'm about;
two numbers with a dash set in between,
the count of points in a game you've seen.

South

I have a start, a middle, an end,
with heroes and dragons and many a friend;
read me in bed by the light of the lamp,
'once upon a time' is my favourite stamp.

West

Notes set in rows, both soft and loud,
the sound a whole brass band is proud;
not words, not noise, but something between,
the loveliest thing your ears have seen.
