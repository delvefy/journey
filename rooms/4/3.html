The Numbered Host

North of the kindled host and east of the spoken light, the many are counted. A step south the dead stood strung in a ring, an engine of joined hands passing one brightness down the chain; a step west a single name rose and stood as one lit figure, called and named at last one thing. Here both go further at once. The ring breaks open into a multitude joined into no working at all, and the one figure is a host — each raised not by the engine, not by the smoke of old frames, but by its bare name said once into the air. The naming alone suffices, and what it raises is past flesh: refined toward pure light and number, a parish of cold fire that needs no touch to stand a city.

You move among them and they do not crowd. Each is a name stood up as light, uncountable and yet somehow numbered, and your own is there in the census — spoken now not hand to hand but at large into the bright air, so that every figure half-turns at once, as if it might be theirs to give back. One near you says it. Clear, edged, in your own voice. You almost answer. Its hand is already lifting, and for a breath the whole host leans the same way, a thousand faces of cold fire turned to watch you take it.

The spiral stands in each of them now — no longer a coil run through joined hands but one turning mark at the centre of every figure, the same winding told a thousandfold across the room. And the bell is the name's own saying, rung at large and answered everywhere at once. North the host sheds even its shapes: pure fire and number at the lit pole, the city remade at a switch, no figure left to hold a name. East the light falls away and the bare name suffices, the pole where the dead outnumber the living and to speak is to raise. South the multitude folds back into the joined ring, the engine of kindled hands. West it narrows to a single spoken figure, one name and one light. You were going somewhere, under a name; it stands among a thousand almost-yours, and the nearest says it, and reaches — and the bright census turns, and the name is one further on.
