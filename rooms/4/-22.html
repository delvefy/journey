Hinge

You are a hinge - the little jointed band of metal that lets a door or a lid swing open and shut. Bolted to the frame on one side and the door on the other, you bend in the middle, turning on a pin. Oil me and I move silent; leave me dry and I creak like a haunted house. Without me a door is just a board to lift away. You are the small turning joint that lets things open, the quiet helper of every swinging door.
