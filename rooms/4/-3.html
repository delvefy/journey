Glass

Sand, melted in a furnace and cooled, becomes the strangest of made things: a stone you can see straight through. Glass is hard and smooth and cold to the lip, and it holds your water as faithfully as any cup - until the day it slips, and shatters into a hundred bright and dangerous pieces. It lets the light in at every window and keeps the weather out. Born of the beach, clear as still water, and one careless knock from ruin.

North

I'm a fat cup with a sturdy ear,
I hold your cocoa, hot and dear;
on cold dark mornings, gripped in hand,
I'm the cosiest cup in the land.

East

A bundle of bristles, a handle tall,
I sweep the dust from floor and hall;
a witch will ride me through the night,
I brush the cobwebs out of sight.

South

Click my switch and night turns day,
I push the gloomy dark away;
a bulb, a shade, a steady glow,
on desk or table, soft and low.

West

I have one edge so sharp and thin,
I slice the bread and cut the skin;
beside the fork at supper laid,
be careful of my shining blade.
