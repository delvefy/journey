Cheer

You are a cheer - a big loud shout of joy flung up all at once. The crowd leaps to its feet when the goal goes in; the playground roars when the bell rings for summer; everyone yells 'hip-hip-hooray!' together. You are gladness too big to keep inside, let out as one great glad noise. No hand can hold you - you are the happiest sound a crowd can make, a wave of joy that lifts the whole room up at once.
