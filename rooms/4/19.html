Hiss

You are a hiss - the long thin sss sound made by escaping air or a warning creature: a snake, a punctured tyre, a kettle, an angry cat. No hand need make you, though a hand can let the air out; you are a sound all on one breath, drawn out and sharp. You cannot be touched, only heard, and it always means keep away or watch out. The crowd hisses the villain, the goose hisses the dog. You are the sound of warning itself, sliding out between the teeth.

when you push it open wide;
the stairs do it under your foot at night -
a ___ - what's inside?

East

A single sound a song is made of,
high or low, you sing it out;
the piano has one for each white key -
a ___, clear and stout.

South

A great glad shout the whole crowd makes
when the team has scored a goal;
hip hip hooray, three times loud -
a ___ from every soul.

West

the lift, the toast, the till;
struck just once, then gone again -
a ___, clear and shrill.
