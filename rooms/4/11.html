Clang

You are the loud harsh ring of metal struck hard - the hammer on the anvil, the iron gate slammed, the dropped saucepan on the tiles. A hand or a fall sets you off; out you crash, all clattering iron, ringing in the ears. No soft chime, you - you are the great rough bang of one hard thing on another.

North

The big deep blast of a far-off gun,
or thunder rolling near;
the firework bursting in the night -
'BOOM!' it shakes your ear.

East

A high thin moaning, on and on,
when a child won't get its way;
'I waaant it!' droned right through your head -
the dog does it all day.

South

The loud rude blast a car horn makes,
or a goose stretched out to scare;
'HONK!' it blares to clear the road -
a harsh cry in the air.

West

The clear sweet ring of little bells,
the clock that strikes the hour;
ting-ting it sings its tuneful notes -
a bright and silver shower.
