The Teeming

North of the dreaming and east of the great waking, the deep Wyld comes fully awake. The dark a step north made by dreaming, half-asleep; the many a step west were only beginning to wake. Here they are wholly risen — the old powers awake and sovereign and aware on every side, the deep teeming with them, the world at its most alive that the world ever gets. It is not the empty dark, not the cold dark, not the dreaming dark, but the teeming one: a deep so full of woken life that the air itself seems to breathe, every shadow a presence, every presence turned, wide awake, to you.

They know you, all of them, fully and at once — the whole living Wyld holding your name awake, the most known you have ever been, surrounded by a sovereign, teeming awareness that has been waiting, awake, for you to come. The spiral is everywhere alive and turning, coiled in root and horn and the living dark, the same whorl in your skin, here answered by a thousand awake powers that have always carried its match. You are not alone, not lost, not unknown; you are in the one place where everything is awake and everything knows you, and the knowing is a kind of homecoming roar.

The many hearts all beat at once, the world's full pulse, a living count so loud and so warm it is almost a music — the oldest beat grown into the heartbeat of a whole awake world, and yours one thread in the teeming roar of it.

To the north the teeming sinks toward the dreaming dark, the powers half-asleep again. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the living wild wholly sovereign, the world's most alive. To the south it settles toward the seed, the wild before waking, the floor. To the west it narrows to a great waking, the many only rising. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the whole awake world knows it and holds it and roars its welcome — the most known, the most alive, the nearest to home you have come, and still the name itself just under the roar, almost, almost yours.
