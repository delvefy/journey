News

You are word of what has just happened, carried fast to all who will listen - the birth and the battle, the score and the storm, the price of bread and the fall of kings. Once you travelled by runner and rumour; now you flash round the whole world in a heartbeat, on screen and page and crackling air. Good or bad, you cannot wait; you must be told. The household leans in to hear you; the town square once gathered for you. You are how the far-off becomes, suddenly, everybody's own concern.

North

A quick sharp crack of thumb and finger,
or a dry twig broke in two;
the brittle sound that does not linger,
short and sharp and through.

East

A long hard look that will not move,
fixed straight upon your face;
it makes you fidget, shift, and prove
you'd rather leave the place.

South

A great loud word flung from the chest,
to carry over all;
'come quick!' it cries, or 'do your best!',
the loudest kind of call.

West

A gentle tap of an open hand,
upon the back or head;
'well done,' it says, or 'that is grand,'
a kindness done instead.
