Snap

You are a short sharp breaking sound - the dry twig, the biscuit, the bone, the clean break of a thing giving way at once. No hand can hold you; you are the quick crack of the moment it parts. Fingers do you for a beat; a turtle does you with its jaws; a temper does you when it finally goes. You are decision made audible, the sudden enough of a thing that will bend no more - over in an instant, and unmistakable.

North

The loud rude blast a car horn makes
in a traffic jam,
or the noise a big white goose
will aim at where you stand.

East

A wide and toothy stretch of mouth,
a smile turned up to ten;
sly or merry, ear to ear -
the Cheshire cat's again.

South

Fresh word of all that's lately happened,
read out or in the press;
the headlines and the weather too,
today's events, no less.

West

A soldier's stiff and snapping greeting,
flat hand up to the brow;
given to the flag and officer -
the body's formal bow.
