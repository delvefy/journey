Bolt

You are a bolt - a stout pin of metal, made to fasten things shut and hold them fast. A hand forged you; one kind of you screws into a nut to clamp two beams together, and another kind slides across to lock a door or a gate. You can be held, greased, hammered, slid home with a satisfying clunk. You are strength you can hold in a hand, the small heavy thing that keeps the big thing from coming apart.
