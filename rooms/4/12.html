Boom

You are the great deep blast of sound that shakes the chest - the cannon, the firework, the thunderclap, the big bass drum. Something bursts or strikes with all its might, and out you roll, 'BOOM', felt as much as heard. You echo off the hills. Deep, huge, and sudden.

North

The little sharp sound a bubble makes,
or a balloon on a pin;
a cork from a bottle, a cap from a gun -
'POP!' and the air rushes in.

East

A low and grumbly drawn-out sound,
half a groan and half a grouse;
'ohhh, not again,' you let it out -
the grumble round the house.

South

The loud harsh ring of iron struck -
a gong, a slammed iron gate;
no soft sweet chime, just a great rough bang -
of metal, loud as fate.

West

A short bright blast of a horn or whistle,
'toot-toot!' the little train;
the trumpet's peep, the tugboat's call -
a cheerful blowing strain.
