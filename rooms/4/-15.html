Jigsaw

You are the picture cut into a hundred wandering pieces, all knobs and holes and curving edges, tipped out in a heap on the table. Made to be broken and made whole again. Corners first, then the edges, then the long slow search for the bit of sky. Snap the last piece in, and the picture is complete.

North

A soft stuffed bear you hug in bed,
with button eyes and fur;
you take it everywhere you go -
your oldest friend, for sure.

East

A short flat board on four small wheels,
you ride it standing tall;
you push and roll and do your tricks -
and sometimes take a fall.

South

A flat round disc you throw and skim,
it floats out flat and far;
the dog leaps high to snatch it back -
in the park, a flying star.

West

A fat felt pen with a juicy tip,
that draws in bright thick lines;
it squeaks across the whiteboard wide -
and stains your hands sometimes.
