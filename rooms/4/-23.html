Knob

You are a knob - the round little handle made to be gripped and turned. On a door you twist and the latch springs back; on a radio you spin and the music grows loud or soft; on a drawer you pull and out it slides. You sit just where the hand expects you, smooth and round and waiting. Some of you are brass and shiny, some plain and white. You are a small round invitation to turn, the part of a thing that says: take hold and twist.
