Pop

A tiny burst, and then nothing - a pop is the smallest bang the world allows. A balloon meets a pin, a cork leaves a bottle, a bubble of gum bursts on a lip. It is quick and bright and a little bit funny, gone before its echo. You make it without meaning to, and it makes everyone turn and grin. The smallest explosion there is, holding no danger at all: just a sudden, cheerful little sound.

North

The sharp quick sound
a dog makes at the gate;
'someone's here!' it says,
again, again - don't wait.

East

To speak so low
that no one can hear,
cross words half-said
not meant for the ear.

South

The deep loud blast
of a cannon or a drum;
it shakes the very ground
and makes you feel it come.

West

The loud rough smash
when things collide and break -
a stack of plates
falls down with a quake.
