Talk

You are talk - words traded back and forth between people, the everyday music of voices. You chat at the bus stop, you natter over tea, you talk and talk on the phone for hours. Sometimes talk is just noise; sometimes it changes everything. You cannot be held or seen, only heard - sound shaped into meaning and passed from mouth to ear. Of all the things people do together, talk is the one they do the most.
