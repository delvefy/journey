Creak

You are a creak - the slow eerie sound old wood makes when it bends: a stair underfoot at night, a rusty hinge, a tall tree in the wind, the timbers of an old ship. No hand quite means to make you; weight and age and stiffness do. You cannot be touched, only heard - and in a quiet dark house, one creak can stand the hair up on your neck. You are the long-drawn complaint of anything old that is asked to move.
