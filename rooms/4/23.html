Sizzle

You are a sizzle - the hot spitting hiss of food meeting a frying pan. The bacon does you, the sausages do you, the onions hop and dance in the bubbling fat. You are the sound of breakfast, of dinner getting close, the noise that pulls a hungry nose toward the kitchen. Half a hiss and half a crackle, you mean heat and good things cooking. You are the happy spitting song of a hot pan, promising that food is nearly ready.
