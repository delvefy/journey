Story

Once upon a time - and already you are leaning in. A story is a made path through made events, a shape we lay over the muddle of things so they mean something. It can be true or invented, told round a fire or printed in a book, and either way it carries you somewhere and brings you back changed. Of all the things people build out of words, this is the oldest and the most loved: a beginning, a trouble, and an end that satisfies.

North

Hip hip - hooray! the people call,
a happy shout that fills the hall;
when good news comes you give a roar
of joy, and then you give one more.

East

I am one piece of what you say,
a row of letters every day;
you read me, write me, speak me clear -
strung together, sentences appear.

South

In lines and verses I am penned,
where sound and meaning gently blend;
I may not rhyme, I may be short -
a crafted thing of words, well-wrought.

West

'Go on, I bet you cannot do it!'
I egg you on until you'll prove it;
to leap, to climb, to take a chance,
I push you on with one bold glance.
