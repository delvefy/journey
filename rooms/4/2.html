The Bright Company

East of the engine the glass is gone, and the dead remain. What the rank of panes a step west still needed a surface to call through, and what the apparatus below still turned a wheel to clear, here needs neither: the summoning has become a thing of light alone, a low engine humming somewhere in the walls, and by its light the dead stand free in the open hall — not behind glass, not stepping through, simply here, sustained and present, a bright company kept by no candle and no caller but the machine's cold patience.

They are like the company you met far to the south — the standing shapes that knew you, the neighbours of the quiet — but lit now, each one threaded with a coil of cold fire, a spiral of light running spine and limb, the same mark that warded the circle and bound the ranks, here the very current that keeps them standing. They turn toward you together. Your name runs through the hall in light, pane to absent pane, throat to throat, and each of them shapes it as it passes — bright, true, almost plain — the engine saying it down the line and the free dead saying it after, and still it goes by faster than you can take it up.

The choir of the conjuring engine has settled to a single steady hum, low and bright, the bell that learned to ring and reply now become the very tone that holds the dead in the room. Cut the hum, you feel, and the company would go: it is the count that keeps them, an hour rung without pause so the dead need never leave it.

To the north the engine and the dead alike refine to pure light and number, a city of the called at the bright pole, the living mind and the kept dead woven as one. To the east the engine falls away as needless and the dead stand sovereign, here of themselves, the high Lumen where they speak first and a name reshapes the room. To the south the hall narrows back into the apparatus, the dead called pane by pane at the turn of a wheel. To the west the free company steps back behind the ranks of glass, summoned and certain through the lit panes. You were going somewhere, under a name. It runs the bright hall throat to throat, lit and near and never still, and the company keeps it for you in the humming light, and turns, all together, to watch you fail again to say it.
