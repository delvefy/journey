Nail

You are a nail - a thin straight spike of metal with a flat little head. Hold me steady, swing the hammer, and bang, bang, bang, in I bite, deep into the wood, and now the shelf will hold, the fence will stand, the picture will hang. Forged sharp and hard by a hand, small and plain and countless, you are how loose things are made to stay - one good knock at a time, until they are one.
