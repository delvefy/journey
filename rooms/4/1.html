Chord

Press three keys together and the air fills with a single braided sound. A chord is wrought — built of separate notes a hand chooses and sounds at once — and yet nothing you can hold; it is gone the instant the strings fall still. Made and untouchable, it keeps the north-east. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

On five thin lines I take my seat
to tell the player's hand
just which sound to strike, how long —
one bead in the tune's long band.

East

Tap your foot and clap your hands —
I'm the pulse that drives the song;
steady, steady, on I go
and the whole band marches along.

South

A turning ring upon a rod
that rolls the cart and mill;
spoked and round, I go nowhere
but round and round I will.

West

A slim bright pipe you hold across
and blow on its near end;
quick fingers dance the silver keys
and high sweet birdsong send.
