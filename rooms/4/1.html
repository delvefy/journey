The Spirit Engine

East of the frame the summoning is given a mechanism. The single warded pane to the west has multiplied: a tall engine of brass and dark glass stands here, pane behind pane behind pane, each cut along its edge with the salt-line and the spiral, a whole apparatus built to do at the turn of a handle what the graven circle did with salt and patience. Where the séance needed a caller, this needs none. A weight descends, a wheel turns, the black candle is fixed now in a made lamp, and pane by pane the dark glass clouds and clears.

The shape of smoke and held breath that pressed at the one pane has learned the trick the engine teaches. It steps from glass to glass down the rank, nearer at each, the way a figure crosses a row of mirrors. As it passes, the name blooms on every pane — no longer reversed and backward but set true at last, running as a thread of pale light through the works, your name conducted pane to pane like a current along a wire. The spiral is graven on each edge, and where the light runs the coils glow faintly, all turning the one way.

The bell's answering tone, once warm from within a single glass, rings struck and mechanical now: a small clear chime as each pane clears, the dead's reply fitted to a movement, an hour of presence told off by clockwork — and under it, still, the low warm second tone, not yet wholly given over to the machine.

To the north the engine sheds glass and caller and candle alike, the summoning gone over to light and number, the dead called up by machinery alone, the bright pole. To the east the engine falls away as needless, the dead simply here and speaking first, names given before you think to ask. To the south the apparatus loosens into the standing company, shapes free in the room with no frame to hold them. To the west the rank narrows to a single warded pane with a hand pressed to its inside, the shape not yet across. You were going somewhere, under a name. It runs the rank pane to pane in pale light, set true and never still, carried past you on the turning of a wheel.
