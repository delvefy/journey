Keyhole

You are a keyhole - the little slot cut into the lock, shaped exactly to one key's crooked tooth and no other. Hands made you to be choosy, to let in only the one who belongs. You are also the eye of the shut door, the place a curious child kneels to spy on the room beyond. A tiny dark keyhole of light, you are both the gate's small mouth and the secret's narrowest window.
