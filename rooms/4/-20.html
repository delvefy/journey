Lock

You are a lock - the clever metal catch on a door or a box that holds it shut until the right key turns. A hand forged you, with little pins and springs hidden inside that only one key can lift just so. You can be locked, unlocked, picked by a clever thief, jammed with rust. A padlock guards the shed; a diary lock keeps secrets safe. You are how a thing is kept private and protected - a small steel puzzle with one true answer.
