Carpet

You are a carpet - the soft thick covering that runs from wall to wall across a floor, warm under bare feet on a cold morning. You hush the footsteps, cushion the tumbles, and gather up every dropped crumb and lost button in your deep cosy pile. Children sprawl on you to play and the cat kneads you in the sun. Snug and woolly and patient, you are the gentlest floor there is, and a story read by the fire is twice as nice when it's read sitting on you.
