Shout

You are the voice flung open all the way, a word hurled across the field or up the stairwell so it cannot be missed. Anger throws you, fear throws you, joy at the winning goal throws you; the drowning man spends his last strength on you. You carry where a whisper dies, over the traffic, through the storm, into the next room - 'dinner!', 'look out!', 'I'm here!'. Brief and loud and impossible to ignore, you are how the body makes itself heard when softness will not do.

North

What's told of all that's lately done,
on the page and on the screen;
of storms, of games, of races won,
the freshest things you've seen.

East

My brows pull down, my lips turn low,
to show I'm cross or sad;
the opposite of smile, you know,
when something's turned out bad.

South

Pucker your lips and push the air,
a sharp clear note rings out;
you call the dog, you hail the fair,
a tune without a shout.

West

Two arms thrown round and pulled in tight,
to hold a friend with care;
no word at all, just warmth and might,
a comfort given there.
