Moo

You are the deep slow call of the cow, rolling across the evening fields - 'mooo,' long and low, at milking time or when the calf has wandered off. No small sound you make; a great soft bellow from a great soft beast. Lean on the gate at dusk, and hear the whole herd answer one another.

North

To say your thoughts out loud in words,
to chat and tell and ask;
all day long with your busy mouth -
a never-ending task.

East

A long high happy cry of delight,
the piglet's, the child's on a ride;
a shrill thin shriek of fun or fright -
you can't keep it inside.

South

The sharp loud sound a dog lets out,
'woof-woof!' at the gate;
it warns, it greets, it wants its walk -
a noise it can't abate.

West

The dull soft knock when two things meet,
the car's small kiss, the knee on the shelf;
'bump!' in the night, you sit straight up -
a thump you feel yourself.
