Frisbee

You are a frisbee - a flat round disc of brightly coloured plastic, made to fly. Flick your wrist and let me go, and I sail spinning across the park, gliding flat and far on the summer air. The dog leaps to catch me; your friend runs to grab me before I land. Light and smooth and curved just so, I float far longer than a ball could ever fly. Throw me, and I soar.

North

A picture cut to a hundred bits,
all funny knobbly shapes;
fit them edge to edge again -
the puzzle that escapes.

East

A bat with a net of criss-cross strings,
you swing it at a ball;
in tennis, squash, or badminton -
you whack it past the wall.

South

A little spike of shining steel,
a flat head up on top;
hammer me deep into the wood -
bang, bang, and then I stop.

West

A shiny sticky little picture,
peeled off a printed sheet;
press me on your book or hand -
a star, a smile, a treat.
