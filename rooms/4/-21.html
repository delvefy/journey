Latch

You are a latch - the little catch of iron on a gate or door that drops into its notch and holds the thing closed. Lift you with a thumb and the gate swings free; let you fall and it clicks down snug again. You are simpler than a lock, with no key to lose, just a bar that drops and a thumb that lifts it. The garden gate and the cottage door both wear you. You are the humble little fastener, all click and clack, that keeps a door from swinging in the wind.
