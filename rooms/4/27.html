Rustle

You are a rustle - the soft dry papery whisper of things stirring: leaves in the breeze, a turning newspaper, a mouse moving in the dry grass. You are gentle and busy and never quite still, a hush of little crackles all run together. In the autumn woods you are everywhere underfoot. Quiet and crinkly and soft, you are the smallest sound dry things make when they brush against each other, a secret little shuffle that makes you turn your head to look.
