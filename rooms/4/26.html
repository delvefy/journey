Scrunch

You are a scrunch - to crush a thing up small and tight in your fist, with a loud crackly crunch. A crisp packet, a sheet of wrapping paper, a fistful of dry autumn leaves: grab them and squeeze and they scrunch down to nothing. You are a noisy sort of crushing, all crackle and crinkle and pop. Quick and satisfying and just a little bit loud, you turn something smooth and stiff into a wadded-up ball, and the bigger the crackle, the better the scrunch.
