Laugh

You are joy with the lid off - the sudden bark of it, the helpless shaking, the tears that come from being too happy to stand. No one can plan you; you arrive when something tips just past the edge of sense. You spread faster than any cold: one of you in a room, and soon the whole room is gone. You cost nothing, you mean everything, and you leave the ribs aching and the day lighter than it was.

North

Purse your lips and blow out air,
a thin sweet note flies everywhere;
to call your dog or carry a tune -
the cheery sound you make at noon.

East

When you don't know and cannot tell,
you take a shot and hope it's well;
not sure, not proved, you simply say
the answer that seems best today.

South

A sly surprise, a clever cheat,
that fools your eyes and makes deceit;
the magician's pull, the fox's play,
a sneaky thing to trip your way.

West

Two open hands that meet and smack,
to praise a show that's grand;
a sharp glad sound, again, again -
applause made by your hand.
