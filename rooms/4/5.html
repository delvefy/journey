The Contemplation

North of the lumen and east of the bright mind, the gods stop walking and become thought. Where they walked a step south, making the world by name, here there is no walking and no naming, only considering — a mind so vast that its contemplation is reality, gods of pure thought whose regarding of a thing is the same as its being. You do not stand in a room here; you stand in a thought, inside the considering of a mind that thinks the world by thinking it, with no gap left between the thinking and the made.

And you are in the thought, being considered. You feel the great contemplation rest on you, weighing you, thinking you over, and where it thinks you, you grow nearer to real — not rendered, not spoken, not dreamed, but considered into being, held in a regard so complete it is almost existence. Your name is the question the mind is contemplating, turning it, deepening it, and the spiral is the shape the thought makes around it, a coil of pure consideration winding tighter, the same whorl in your skin, the seed of the thought that thinks you. To be fully thought, here, would be to be fully real. The contemplation deepens. It holds you, considered, a thought's breadth from become.

There is no count, only the held tone of the considering, thought sustained as a single note — the bell become contemplation, an hour spent wholly in the thinking of you.

To the north the contemplation reaches its absolute, pure thought-world, the lit pole's peak. To the east it comes wholly sovereign, the god-mind's thought the entire cosmos. To the south the thought walks again as gods who make by name. To the west it narrows to a single mind holding you whole. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a god-mind contemplates it, and the contemplating thinks you toward being — and you hang in the great regard, considered, a thought from real.
