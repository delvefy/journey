The Company

East of the circle the ring is no longer needed. The double ring graven in the floor a step west, the salt, the wards set at their quarters — here the floor is bare, the candle gutters somewhere out of sight, and still the shapes stand. Not one now but several, dim as breath on cold glass, ranged about the room as though it were a hall they had always kept and you the one who wandered in. No one summoned them. No one drew a line to hold them. They are simply here, the way neighbours are here, each turned a little toward you, patient, and not unglad.

They know you. Whatever the smoke-shape in the ring waited to be told, it seems to have been answered and to have passed the knowing on, for when you find the place where each one's eyes would be, your name forms in the thick cool air between you — gently, surely, the way you speak the name of someone long expected. You cannot keep it; it is theirs to give and your ear's to lose. One lifts what would be a hand and draws, slowly, on the cold air, a spiral — a greeting, perhaps, or the last sign the living and the dead still hold in common.

The single answering tone of the circle has grown into a low chord, many soft voices keeping one time beneath the stroke in your skull — the bell taken up across a whole valley of the quiet, a murmur of company that does not want anything of you but your being here.

To the north the standing shapes are fitted to craft and light, the called name set running in glass and engine, summoning and made thing fused. To the east the company thickens to solid neighbours, the dead no longer dim but here and speaking first, needing no asking at all. To the south the wards loosen wholly into the wild, the shapes less called than grown from root and dark, always here and never summoned. To the west the company narrows back to a single presence in a graven ring, waiting to be told the name. You were going somewhere, under that name. They part to let you through, all of them holding it, and not one says it aloud — only incline, as to someone going on ahead.
