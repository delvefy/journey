Crinkle

You are a crinkle - the crisp little sound of foil or cellophane bending, and the maze of tiny folds it leaves behind, the wrapper round a sweet, the silver round a chocolate. No hand makes you on purpose; a careless squeeze does. You are too small and dry to be a crunch, a whisper made of paper. Children cannot resist you in the back row of the cinema. Crisp and small and ticklish to the ear, you are the fidgety music of unwrapping.
