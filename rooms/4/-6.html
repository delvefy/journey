Doll

You are a small person made of cloth or wood or shining plastic, sewn a face that never changes. A child takes you up and you become whatever she needs - a baby to be fed, a friend to be scolded, a brave knight off to war. You sit at the tea that has no tea; you are tucked in tighter than the child herself. Years pass, your dress goes thin, one eye hangs loose - and still you are loved beyond all sense.

North

On a windy day I climb the sky,
a string runs to your hand;
of paper bright, with a long tail's fly,
I dance above the land.

East

Two sharp blades that cross and meet,
with handles for your thumb;
I snip the paper, clean and neat,
and bite where I am come.

South

A little ball of coloured glass,
you flick me with your thumb;
across the ring the others pass,
and click as on I come.

West

Round and bouncy, thrown and caught,
I roll across the floor;
in every game I'm sought and sought,
then kicked right out the door.
