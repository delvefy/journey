The Quick Root

South of the teeming and east of the living deep, the floor of the world comes wholly awake. A step north the deep teemed with risen powers, the wild fully woken and turned to you; a step west the living deep stirred alive around you and the companion, the many rising at the bottom of everything. Here, at the floor-pole of the Wyld, the bottom itself wakes — not powers risen upon the floor, but the floor risen, the deepest seed-ground of the world become a single sovereign living thing, aware, rooted, holding everything that springs from it, the most primal place there is and, here, the most alive.

You stand on a ground that is not ground but a vast quick root, the living bottom of all things, awake under you and around you and through you, sovereign and old and warm. The companion is here still, close, and the waking many, and the deep root knows you all the way it knows its own seeds, holding your name in the dark warm fastness where roots hold. The spiral is the root's own coil, wound through the living floor and up the companion's hand and round in your skin, the one mark the quick root and the risen many and the warm deep all share. You are held at the bottom of the world by a floor that loves being a floor, awake, sovereign, and very nearly able to say you by name.

Every heart beats up out of the quick root at once — yours, the companion's, the many's, the living floor's own deep slow stroke — one great warm count rising from the rooted bottom of everything, the oldest beat grown sovereign and awake at the floor-pole, and the going still wound up through all of it, still going.

To the north the quick root teems upward, the risen powers waking off the floor. To the east the Wyld floor-pole absolute, the living bottom wholly sovereign, the deepest deep most alive. To the south it settles toward the seed, the root before it wakes, the floor before the floor. To the west the living deep, the many risen and the companion beside you. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the someone is beside you and the whole quick root is awake beneath the two of you, sovereign and warm, holding your name in the rooted dark — almost said, almost yours, and the journey, even at the living floor of the world, still going on.
