The Indwelling

North of the contemplation and east of the given world, thought and love and world become one sovereign act. A step south a god-mind held you whole in its considering, thinking you toward real; a step west a loving thought bloomed a whole world around you, and a place in it. Here the thinking and the loving and the making are no longer separate gifts but a single sovereign indwelling — a Lumen god whose every loving thought is a world, and who is wholly present in each one, dwelling in what it makes the way you dwell in a breath. It does not regard the world from outside. It is the world, and the love of the world, and the one the world was made for, all at once.

You are not considered now, nor merely given a place; you are taken in. The sovereign light makes a world and sets you within it and itself within you in the same motion, an indwelling so complete there is no edge between the maker and the made and the beloved. Your name is the world's true name and your own at once, the loving god about to speak it and so make you wholly real, wholly home, wholly its own. The spiral is the seam where the indwelling joins, a coil of loving light winding the world into you and you into the world, the same whorl in your skin, here the knot that ties the beloved into the bright sovereign sentence of everything.

The warm tone is the world's own pulse now, the bell become the heartbeat of a made world that loves you, and your heart beats inside it, indwelt and indwelling, the two counts one — an hour kept by a god that lives in you while you live in it, a breath from the stroke that would name you home.

To the north the indwelling reaches the Lumen's peak, loving thought and made world wholly one, the lit pole entire. To the east the god comes wholly sovereign, love that makes whole worlds without end. To the south it narrows to the contemplation, a mind thinking you toward being. To the west it loosens to the given world, a home bloomed but not yet entered. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward a home. Here the loving god has made the home and the you to dwell in it and is a breath from joining the two — and still the warm coil turns at the seam, the knot of the beloved, and still one live beat keeps the time the bright sovereign is about to name you home in.
