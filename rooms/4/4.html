Trick

You never see it coming - that is the whole point. A trick is made on purpose: a sleight, a prank, a clever turn meant to fool your eye or spring a surprise. There is nothing to hold; it lives in the doing and is gone the moment it is done. Played with a wink it delights you; played in spite it stings. Either way, the secret is never quite where you are looking.

North

When something tickles you inside,
out I burst, ha-ha, hee-hee;
I shake your shoulders, crease your eyes,
the merriest sound to be.

East

I cost you nothing, give for free,
I curve your lips up at each end;
I light your face and warm the room,
and welcome every friend.

South

When your team has won the day,
you give a happy shout;
'Hip hip hooray!' the whole crowd calls,
and lets the gladness out.

West

Put on the music, tap your feet,
and twirl around the floor;
you spin and sway and skip in time,
then bow and ask for more.
