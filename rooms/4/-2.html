The Horned Word

East of the herd the wild has every word. Where the horned one a step west could shape the old lowing only almost to sense, here the speaking has deepened into the whole Wyld, and there is no edge left between the living and the long-dead, the beast and the person, the root and the voice — all of it one wild that speaks, and speaks plainly, in a tongue that was never taught because it is the one that all teaching was cut from. You have come down out of the throng of standing shapes into something older and surer: less a company than a wood, less persons than presences, and at the heart of it the horned watcher, fully here at last.

It turns to you, the horn wound in its tight spiral — the coil you have met as shell and shade and living bone, the same mark whorled in your own skin — and it says your name. Plainly. Whole. The wild says it after, every root and throat and the dark between them, your name carried on the one breath the world breathes. For an instant you have it; it sits in your mouth, certain. And then the wild says the next word, and the next — it has every word, and yours is only one of them — and your name is swept on into the great speaking before your throat can close on it: kept, but not kept by you.

The herd-beat, the one heart beneath all the hearts, carries the whole of it now — the oldest count that learned in the herd to say a thing, here saying everything, the bell of the blood become a voice that never stops for breath, your name rising under it and falling after, one beat in a speech as old as the dark.

To the north the wild orders back into a throng of standing shapes, persons again, the name passed hand to hand. To the east the speaking becomes a making, the Wyld at its pole where the wild not only says the name but renders it true, gods walking, the word the world. To the south the words sink back into beast and root and burrow, the wild before speech, the horned watcher gone wholly to instinct and dark. To the west the wild narrows to a herd whose horned one shapes the name almost, in the old lowing. You were going somewhere, under a name. The wild has it, and says it, plainly and entire — and having every word, gives yours back to you only as it gives all the rest, swept past in the one long speaking you cannot hold a single word of.
