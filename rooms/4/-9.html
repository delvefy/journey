Balloon

You are a skin of coloured rubber, limp and small until a breath blows you round and full. A maker dipped and shaped you; a party fills you with air or with the gas that lifts you to the ceiling. You bob on a string, you squeak when rubbed, you are the bright sign that today is special. But you live a short life: a pin, a thorn, a careless squeeze, and with one loud bang you are a sad scrap on the floor.

North

A big round ring you spin and twirl
around your wiggling middle;
or bowl me down the road with a stick -
a round and rolling riddle.

East

A bent sharp bit of metal made
to catch and hold and hang;
it lands the wriggling fish, it grips
the coat upon its fang.

South

I hang from chains on the playground frame,
a seat that flies up high;
push me, pump your legs, and soar
to almost touch the sky.

West

A slim tube of ink with a rolling tip,
it glides across the page;
it signs, it scribbles, doodles too,
in blue or black all day.
