Tune

You are a tune - the rise and fall of notes that makes a melody. You are what a person hums when they have forgotten the words, the few bright notes a music box turns out, the jingle everyone in the country knows. You sit in whistles and hummed-along radios and the kettle's climbing whine. No hand can hold you - yet let me into a head and I will go round and round and round, and not be shaken out till bedtime.
