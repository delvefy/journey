The Thronging

South of the quick root and east of the rising, the deep floor throngs with the woken wild. A step north the floor of the world had risen as a single sovereign root; a step west the many began to wake, a first few presences stirring up out of the seed-dark. Here, at the deepest floor, the rising comes full: not a few now but a throng, the wild risen on every side, the deep teeming warm and crowded with woken presences pressed close around you. The living bottom of the world is full of company, a warm multitude waking at the very seed, the Wyld thronging at its floor.

They are everywhere around you, warm and wordless and turning, a press of woken presences at the floor of the world — not the cold crowd of any throng above, but the deep's own thronging, kin and beast and root and the warm dark itself risen close. You draw the spiral and a hundred faint hands draw it with you, the shared mark made manifold, the whorl wound through the whole warm throng. The coil in your skin turns among a multitude of matching coils, the company come full at the floor, the wild thronging warm and close and almost, almost saying your name in a hundred wordless voices at once.

A hundred hearts beat around you at the floor, a warm thronging drum, the deep's full pulse risen close on every side — and your heart beats in the midst of them, one stroke in a teeming warm multitude, the count grown from a single beat to a throng's, the whole living floor beating you in among its own.

To the north the thronging rises to the quick root, the floor risen sovereign and rooted. To the east the deep teems fullest, the wild wholly risen, the most alive. To the south it settles toward the seed and the cradle, the throng before it wakes, the floor before the floor. To the west the rising, the many just beginning to wake. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the someone has become a warm thronging multitude, risen close on every side at the floor of the world — almost a homecoming, almost named — and still the warm coil turns among the throng's coils, and still your beat keeps time in the teeming warm drum of the living floor.
