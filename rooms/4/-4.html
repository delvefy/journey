The Dreaming Dark

South of the knowing and east of the elder dark, the deep begins to dream. A step north the wild knew you wholly, kin-deep and without a word; a step west the old powers kept your name in root and slow blood. Here the knowing sinks below even instinct, down to the dark before recognition — and finds, at that depth, not emptiness but the oldest power of all: the dark that makes. This is the Wyld near its pole, the deep past at the age of wonders, where the dark does not know things or keep things but dreams them, and the dreaming is the world.

It regards you — not knows you, the knowing is below it now, but regards, the way a dreamer regards the figures of a dream — and where its regard falls, things begin to be. You feel it gather you into its dreaming: the dark composing you out of itself, drawing you up the way it draws up root and beast and star, ready to dream you whole into the world it is endlessly making. The spiral is the shape the dreaming takes, the old coil winding being out of the dark, the same whorl in your skin, here the seed the dark dreams from. Your name is what it reaches for — not to say, not to keep, but to dream: to make you true by imagining you, the deepest making there is, older than speech, older than the wild's every word.

The wild's heart is a dreaming-pulse here, the one beat under all beats no longer keeping time or making by speech but dreaming on its own slow rhythm, each beat a thing imagined into being — and your name waits to be dreamed real on it, the oldest count become the heartbeat of creation.

To the north the dreaming firms to a knowing, the wild that recognizes you wholly and wordlessly. To the east the dark becomes wholly a maker, the Wyld pole, reality its endless dream, your name dreamed into the world entire. To the south the dreaming sinks toward the seed of the wild, the dark before it could dream, toward the pole's floor. To the west the dark keeps rather than makes, the old powers holding your name in root and blood. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the oldest dark would dream you into the world, make you true by imagining you — and it gathers you into its dreaming, reaching for the name to dream you whole, the world about to be remade as the dark's slow dream of you.
