Drill

A tool that spins a sharp bit round and round to bore a clean hole through wood or wall or stone. The drill is a made thing, all turning and bite, the borer of the workshop. It keeps the worked south-east, among tools and timbers. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A flat blade fixed across a pole,
I scrape the soil and slay the weed;
down the garden rows I go —
name the chopping tool you need.

East

Two jaws I have, that screw down tight,
I grip the work and will not let go;
I hold the glued boards while they set —
name the gripping tool. Now show.

South

A block of iron thick to thin,
hammered into a crack to split;
I lift the load, I cleave the log —
name the tapered tool that's hit.

West

Three or four sharp prongs in a row,
I lift the hay, I turn the ground;
at table too, I spear your food —
name the pronged tool. Look around.
