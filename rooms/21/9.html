Morning

You are morning - the fresh early part of the day, when the alarm rings and the curtains open on a sky still rinsing itself pale. You are toast and cold milk, the rush for the bus, dew still on the grass. The world wakes into you, blinking and yawning, and everything still lies ahead. You are the day's clean first page, before a single thing has gone wrong. Up you get: here I am again.
