Window

You are a window - a square of glass set into the wall so the room can see out and the day can come in. Light pours through you, and the view of the garden, and sometimes the rain runs down your face in long beads. They open you for a breeze and shut you against the cold. Made by hands - framed, glazed, and hinged - you are the wall's clear eye, the place where inside meets the wide world.
