Wink

You are a wink - the quick shut of one eye that says a thousand things and not a word. 'It's our secret,' you mean; or 'I'm only teasing'; or 'well done, you.' Too fast to catch on camera, you are gone before the other eye can blink. You cost nothing and mean much, a flick of an eyelid that turns a stranger into a fellow-conspirator. You are a tiny joke shared between two faces, a smile that uses only half the face.
