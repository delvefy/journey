Trot

You are a trot - a horse's bouncy little run, quicker than a walk but gentler than a gallop. The pony trots round the ring, bobbing the rider up and down in the saddle; you 'rise to the trot' to soften the jolts. A small dog trots to keep up; a busy person trots off to work. You are a brisk, neat, steady pace, all clip-clop and bounce, the cheerful middle gear between strolling and racing.
