Tease

You are a tease - the playful poking of fun, the pulled face and the silly name, the joke held just out of reach to make a friend grab for it. No tool makes you; a grin and a glint do. You stop just short of unkind, and end in a laugh from both sides. Brothers and sisters are made of you. Half mischief and half affection, you are the gentle ribbing that lives between people who like each other enough to wind each other up.
