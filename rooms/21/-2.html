String

You are string - the thin twisted cord that ties up a hundred everyday jobs. You knot a parcel, fly a kite, hang a bird-feeder, and wind into a tidy little ball in the kitchen drawer. You are thinner than rope and stronger than thread, the in-between cord everyone reaches for. You are made by hand, spun and twisted from many slim fibres. You are the humble helper of the house, looped and tied and snipped, never quite used up and never quite thrown away.
