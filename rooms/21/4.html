Peel

You are a peel - both the thin skin a fruit wears and the act of stripping it away. You tug the orange open and the rind comes off in fragrant bits; you run the knife round an apple and the long red curl unwinds. The banana's yellow jacket comes off in one quick pull. Once you have done your work the sweet soft fruit lies bare and ready. You are the undressing of the fruit, the careful pulling-back that gets to the good part underneath.
