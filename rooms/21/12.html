Goal

You are a goal - two posts and a net at the end of the pitch, and the roar that goes up when the ball finds the back of you. People build you out of frames and string, then spend the whole game trying to beat you. You are the point of all the running, the target of every kick, the line a thing must cross to count. Half a wooden frame, half pure delight, you turn a scramble into a shout of joy.
