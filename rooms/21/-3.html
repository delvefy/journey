Tin

You are a tin - a small can of shiny silver metal, light in the hand and cold to the touch. Beans and beans of soup hide in you on the shelf; biscuits rattle in the bigger round one with the picture on the lid. When the food is gone a child keeps you for buttons and string and little secret treasures. You ping when tapped and dent when dropped. You are the humble metal box that keeps things safe and dry, then keeps small things again.
