Shovel

You are a shovel - the wide flat blade on a long stout handle that scoops and heaves whatever a strong arm asks. You bite into snow, into coal, into garden earth, and fling it up and over in a great brown arc. You are made by hand, all steel and wood, heavy and honest. You are bigger than a trowel and blunter than a spade, the worker's friend on every building site and frosty drive, the tool that moves a whole heap one scoop at a time.
