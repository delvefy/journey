Map

You are a map - the land drawn small and flat on a sheet so a traveller can find the way. Roads thread across you, rivers wiggle blue, towns sit as little dots, and a pirate inks an X where the gold is buried. You fold up into a pocket, then open wide to hold a whole country. You cannot be touched in the real, only read. You are the world shrunk down to paper, the picture that turns a lost wanderer into someone who knows just where to go.

South

you dig and scoop with this;
it heaves the snow up off the path -
a ___, no heap you'll miss.
