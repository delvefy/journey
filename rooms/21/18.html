Huddle

You are a huddle - to bunch up close and small, pressed in tight against each other for warmth or comfort. Penguins huddle in a great shivering crowd against the polar wind; players huddle in a ring to share a secret plan; children huddle under one umbrella in the rain. The closer you crowd, the warmer and safer you feel. Snug and squeezed and shoulder-to-shoulder, you are a group pulled tight into one warm bundle against the cold and the wide world outside.
