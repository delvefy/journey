Saxophone

You are a saxophone - a great curved pipe of golden metal, all keys and pads under busy fingers, made to wail and croon and growl. A player breathes through a wooden reed and you turn that breath to honey and smoke. You are the voice of the late-night band, bending notes that no straight horn could reach. Heavy and shining and full of soul, you hang from a strap and sing like a brass throat.
