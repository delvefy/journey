Sandpaper

You are sandpaper - a sheet of stiff paper coated all over with rough sandy grit, made for smoothing. Rub a rough plank up and down with you and the splinters and bumps wear away to a silky finish. You come coarse for the heavy work and fine for the last gentle polish, and you wear out and need a fresh sheet. Scratchy and rough and hard-working, you are the tool that smooths the wood, sanding away every snag until it's soft enough to stroke.
