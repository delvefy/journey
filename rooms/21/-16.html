Pin

You are a pin - a thin sliver of shiny steel, sharp at one end and topped with a tiny round head. Dressmakers push you through cloth to hold two pieces while they sew, then pull you out again. You hold a hem, a pattern, a note to a board. Small and sharp and easily lost, you hide in carpets to prick a bare foot, and you come by the boxful, glinting, ready to nip a fold of fabric neatly into place.
