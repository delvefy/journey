Sleep

You are sleep - the deep rest that takes you each night when your eyes grow heavy and the day lets go. You slow the breath, soften the body, and slip the mind off into dreams. Babies need you most; the tired beg for you; the sleepless miss you sorely. You cannot be forced, only welcomed, by a dark room and a still pillow. In the morning you leave a little fog behind the eyes. You are the gentle nothing that mends the worn-out day.
