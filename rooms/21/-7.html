Chair

You are a chair - the four-legged seat with a back to lean on, waiting at every table and desk. You hold a person up off the floor, take their weight all day, and never complain. Pulled out, you welcome; pushed in, you tidy. A king's one is a throne; a baby's one is high with a tray. Tip back on me and you might topple. You are the plainest place to sit, the patient servant of every tired pair of legs.
