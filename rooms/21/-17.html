Tack

You are a tack - a short stubby nail with a wide flat head, made to be pressed in by a thumb. Push one through the corner of a poster and onto the wall it goes; a row of you holds the carpet down round the edge of a room. Small and sharp and easily dropped, you lurk point-up on the floor to catch a bare foot. Quick and handy and just a little bit treacherous, you pin paper and cloth and notices wherever a hammer would be far too much.
