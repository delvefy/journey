Oink

You are an oink - the round snuffly grunt a pig makes, nose-down in the mud. You are not a thing to touch but a sound to hear, short and snorting and pleased with yourself. The pig rolls and roots and lets you out between mouthfuls, a happy muddy noise. Children make you for fun, scrunching their noses up. You are the one word the pig knows, repeated all day long in the warm straw of the sty.
