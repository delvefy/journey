Silk

You are silk - the smoothest, shiniest, most slippery thread there is, spun not by a machine but by a little worm wrapping its cocoon, then unwound by careful hands into shimmering cloth. You slide cool through the fingers and catch the light like water. Scarves and ties and the finest dresses are made of you. Smooth and lustrous and faintly cool to the touch, you are the most luxurious cloth of all, woven from the patient spinning of a worm.
