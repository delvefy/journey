Whistle

You are a whistle - the little metal or plastic tube that, with one hard puff, lets out a shriek that cuts across a noisy field. The referee blows you to stop the game; the guard blows you to start the train; the kettle whistles when the water boils. You hold a tiny ball inside that rattles and trills. You are small enough to wear on a string round the neck, yet loud enough to freeze a whole playground. You are a shout you can carry in your pocket.
