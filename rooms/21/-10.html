Wire

You are wire - the thin bendy thread of metal, sometimes bare and bright, sometimes wrapped in coloured plastic. You run hidden through the walls carrying the electricity that lights the lamp and wakes the kettle. Stripped and twisted, you mend a fence, hang a picture, or bind a parcel tight. Bend you too often and you snap. You are thin and quiet and everywhere, the secret thread that ties the powered world together and feeds it its spark.
