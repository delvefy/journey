Cuddle

You are a cuddle - the slow soft holding that asks for nothing but to stay close. Not the quick squeeze of a hug, but a long warm folding-together on the sofa, under a blanket, when the rain is at the window. A child cuddles a teddy; a cat cuddles into a lap. You cannot be bought or built, only given. You are comfort with arms around it, the body's plain way of saying you are safe.
