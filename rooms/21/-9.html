Sock

You are a sock - the soft knitted bag that hugs the foot, warm and snug inside the shoe. You come in pairs, but somewhere between the wash and the drawer you lose your twin forever, so a whole odd army of you piles up unmatched. Children skid across the floor in you, hang you up at Christmas, pull you on inside out by mistake. Humble, woolly, and always disappearing - that is you.
