Surprise

You are a surprise - the sudden jolt of a thing nobody saw coming. The lights snap on and the room shouts your name; a wrapped box rattles with something you cannot guess; a friend appears at the door who was meant to be far away. You make eyes go wide and hands fly up to mouths. You cannot be kept long, only sprung, and the keeping of you is half the fun. You are the gift of the unexpected, sprung at just the right moment.
