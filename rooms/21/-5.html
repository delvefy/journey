Marble

You are a marble - a little ball of swirled glass that fits between two fingers, cool and round and hard. Children flick you across the floor to knock another from the ring, and the winner keeps the lot. Inside you a ribbon of colour is frozen in the clear, a cat's-eye caught in glass. You roll and click and gather in a jar. You are a tiny planet of play, the smallest treasure a pocket can hold.
