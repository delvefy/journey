March

You are a march - the steady walk in step, left-right, left-right, that an army moves by. The band strikes up, the boots come down as one, and the whole parade rolls forward like a single beast. You can be proud and grand at a ceremony, or weary on a long, long road. Protesters march for what they believe; soldiers march to war. You are walking made into one great drumbeat, many feet falling together as if they shared one pair of legs.
