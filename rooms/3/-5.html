The Great Waking

South of the elder dark and east of the quickening, the deep wakes — not one presence now but all of it, at once. A step west a single seed stirred and made its first turn toward you; here that quickening has become a multitude, the whole Wyld coming alive in the dark together, presences rising on every side out of the one seed-deep, the old powers opening their eyes all at the same slow moment. It is not a dawn; there is no light. It is a waking, vast and dark and everywhere, the deep filling with itself as it comes round to life.

They rise toward you as they wake. Where the elder dark held its many ancient and already-risen, these are only now lifting out of the seed-dark, still half-curled, half-dreaming, turning toward where you stand the way a whole field turns to a wind. None of them knows you yet — but all of them are on the cusp of it, a thousand first recognitions forming at once, the deep about to know you manyfold. The spiral is everywhere uncurling: seed-coils opening on every side, the old sign waking in root and horn and dark together, the same whorl in your skin answered now not by one but by all of them, recognized at last by a whole world coming awake.

The first beats take up together — not one heart rising to meet yours, as in the quickening, but many, a hundred first pulses starting at once in the dark, the deep's whole blood beginning to move, a great rising count that answers your single one a hundredfold and gathers it in.

To the north the waking completes into the elder dark, the old powers risen and ancient, keeping your name. To the east the many wake wholly into the deep Wyld, the old powers sovereign and aware, the pole. To the south the waking sinks back toward the seed, the many subsiding to one possibility. To the west it narrows to a single quickening, one presence taking its first turn. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the whole deep wakes around you at once, a thousand presences turning toward you on the cusp of knowing — the world coming alive to find you already in it.
