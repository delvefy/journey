The Given World

North of the bright mind and east of the beloved, pure thought and love become one, and the one makes worlds. The mind a step south held you whole in a thought; the god a step west loved you into being; here the two join in an intelligence that needs no light any longer, only love, and whose every loving thought is a world. You stand in one such thought — a whole world thought up, gently, around you: warmth and distance and a soft lit sky, all of it bloomed from a mind's loving regard, all of it, you understand, made for you.

It is giving you a world. The loving thought builds it as you watch, considerate of you, shaping a place that would fit you exactly, that would be the home the going was always toward — and at its centre, where you stand, it is making you a place in it, a self to live the given world, loved into being the way the whole world around it is. The spiral is the seed of the gift, a coil of loving light winding up into a world and a you to be in it, the same whorl in your skin. The loving mind thinks your name, and the world blooms warmer at it, and a place opens for you, ready, a breath from given.

The warm tone makes a world as it sounds, the bell become a loving thought that builds you a home in the building — an hour spent making somewhere for you to have been going all along.

To the north the loving thought reaches its absolute, pure mind-love making worlds without end, the Lumen's peak. To the east the god comes wholly sovereign, love that makes whole worlds, the Lumen pole. To the south it narrows to a mind holding you in a single thought. To the west it loves you into being without yet a world to set you in. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a loving mind thinks you a whole world to have been going toward, and a place in it for you — the home bloomed and waiting, a breath from given, and your name the warm seed of it.
