Wink

You are a word spoken with one eye. A single lid drops and lifts, quick as a thought, and a secret passes between two people that the rest of the room never sees. You can mean 'I'm joking,' or 'I'm on your side,' or 'watch this' - and you say it without a sound, faster than any sentence. Babies cannot do you; it takes a little practice to close just one eye. A small, sly, friendly thing, gone before anyone else can catch it.

North

Two arms around you, warm and tight,
a gentle squeeze to hold you near;
no words at all, it says 'I care,'
and chases off a fear.

East

Push your lips into an O,
and blow a thin sweet note;
you call the dog, you cheer the team,
a tune without your throat.

South

Bring your two palms hard together,
to make a happy smack;
the more you like the song or show,
the louder goes the crack.

West

One of us is 'it' and chases,
round the yard we tear and run;
touch a friend and now they're 'it' -
a breathless, laughing, racing fun.
