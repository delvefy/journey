Rattle

You are a rattle - the dry busy noise of small hard things shaken fast. You are the baby's first toy, the box of buttons, the loose old window in a gale, the dice cupped in two hands. Brr-brr-brr, on and on, a hundred tiny knocks too quick to count. No hand can hold the sound itself, only the shaken thing that makes it. You are clatter set going and kept going - the music of anything not nailed down.
