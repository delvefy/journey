March

You are a march - feet moving all together, left, right, left, in one long straight line. Soldiers do it, bands do it, and a class crossing the playground does it when the teacher claps the beat. Backs straight, arms swinging, every footfall landing as one. There is a drum somewhere keeping time. Proud and orderly and loud, you turn a crowd of people into a single moving thing, and you carry them forward step by even step, nobody out of time.
