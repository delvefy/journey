Pat

You are the small kind touch of an open hand, light and quick - on the dog's head, the baby's back, the shoulder of a friend who has done well. You are not a blow and not a stroke, just a gentle tap or two that says, without a single word, 'there now', 'good', 'I'm here'. The nurse calms with you; the coach praises with you; the comforter soothes the crying child with you. Soft and brief and warm, you are one of the plainest ways the body has of being kind.

North

A hand raised stiff up to the brow,
the soldier's smart hello;
to flag and captain, here and now,
a sign of respect to show.

East

What's told of all that's lately done,
on the page and on the screen;
of storms and games and races won,
the newest things you've seen.

South

Two arms thrown round and folded tight,
to hold a friend you love;
no word at all, just warmth and might,
a comfort thought enough.

West

A bright new thought that lights the head,
a spark, a sudden gleam;
'I know!' is all that need be said,
the start of every scheme.
