The Rejoicing

North of the plenitude and east of the finishing, the loving making becomes pure joy. A step south a sovereign love made worlds without end; a step west a maker bent to the last loving touch, finishing the dearest work. Here, at the warm Lumen pole, the making and the love and the finishing all turn to gladness: a god whose loving making is the whole cosmos, and who makes not from need nor even only from love but from sheer joy, delighting in every world it raises, rejoicing in the making the way a song rejoices in being sung. Creation here is jubilation, endless and glad, the bright peak entire and overjoyed to be.

Everything blooms in delight around you, worlds raised for the joy of raising them, light glad to be light, and you the gladdest-made thing of all — not merely loved, not merely finished, but rejoiced over, made and remade in a creation that delights in you. Your name is the note the rejoicing keeps returning to, the dear refrain the glad making sings and sings. The spiral is the figure joy turns on, the coil delight winds as it makes, the same whorl in your skin, here the mark a rejoicing cosmos draws over and over for the sheer glad love of drawing it, your name and the world's joy one turning.

The bright pulse has become a music of rejoicing, the bell pealing for gladness, every stroke a world made and delighted in — and your heart beats up into the jubilation, the one warm count the rejoicing cosmos is overjoyed to keep, your name a breath from sung aloud in the glad making of everything.

To the north the rejoicing reaches the Lumen pole, loving making and pure joy wholly one, the bright peak entire. To the east the maker comes wholly sovereign, a god whose glad making is the cosmos. To the south the plenitude, worlds made without end. To the west the finishing, the last loving touch on the dearest work. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward joy. Here a rejoicing cosmos makes and remakes you for the sheer gladness of it, your name its dearest refrain — and still the warm coil turns, the figure of joy, and still one live beat keeps the time the rejoicing is a breath from singing you, gladly, home.
