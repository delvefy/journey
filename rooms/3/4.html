Chant

Words said over and over in steady time, half-speech and half-song. A chant is made by voices moving together — the monk at prayer, the crowd at the match — and though it fills the room you cannot hold it. It keeps the made, airy north-east. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A poem written all in praise,
of autumn, or a Grecian urn;
just three short letters name the form —
that lofty song. Now take your turn.

East

A song that tells a tale all through,
of love, or war, or a robber bold;
verse after verse the story runs —
an old folk-song with a story told.

South

Sung at the door in the cold of Yule,
by lantern-light while breath turns white;
a joyful song of the winter feast —
the wassailers' song on a frosty night.

West

Raised in the church to the organ's swell,
a song of praise the people sing;
solemn and slow from the wooden pews —
a holy tune. Now name the thing.
