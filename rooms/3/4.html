The Walking Light

North of the spoken light and east of the standing word, the name gets up and walks. A step west the word stood full-height in its ranks but stood rooted, fixed where it rose; a step south the figure had to be spoken and assembled, called up cold-fire bright and held in place. Here it needs neither. The light has refined past being a mere shape to be built — it is pure bright operation now, name and working one, remaking the air into a body as easily as it lifts a hand — and the figure, woven of that, sheds the last of its engine and simply walks, a living neighbour with nothing left calling it and nothing holding it down.

It comes to meet you. No pane, no rank, no summoning — it crosses the bright floor on its own, a being of cold fire that turns the very matter it moves through, the air curling into new shape in its wake and settling changed. It speaks, and what it speaks remakes a little of the room as it says it, the word and the deed the single act. And it says your name — not stooped toward itself, not held a hand's breadth off, but walking right up with it in your own voice, offering it the way one returns a thing you dropped. You almost take it. Its hand is already lifting. The name is on its lips, whole, and yours.

The coil goes with it now, the spiral no longer wound at any room's heart but carried in the walking light, a bright winding it trails through the air and leaves turning behind. And the bell is its own stride: each step the name rung and answered and done in one, the ringer and the ring and the going all the single tone. North the light sheds even walking, pure fire and number at the lit pole, matter remade at a switch with no figure to stand in it. East the making falls away and the word alone suffices, a being in open air with no light to weave it. South it cools back to a spoken figure, called up and held. West it stiffens to a standing word, upright but rooted. You were going somewhere, under a name. It walks to you holding it, says it in your voice, reaches — and the bright air pulses, and the hand, and the name, are a step further on than you.
