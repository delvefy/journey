Dance

Music starts, and your feet decide before you do. A dance is something you make out of nothing but movement - a shape drawn in the air with arms and steps, gone the instant it is made. No hand can hold it and no box can keep it, yet people have built whole evenings around it, in ballrooms and barns and kitchens. You learn the steps, then forget them, and let the music move you. Two left feet are welcome.

North

Bring your two hands smartly together,
I'm the sharp sound they make whenever;
at the end of a song or play,
a hundred of me ring 'hooray'.

East

A magician pulls me from a hat,
a fox can play me, and a cat;
part magic and part bit of fun,
a clever, sneaky, surprising one.

South

I'm made of notes both high and low,
from flute and drum and fiddle's bow;
the thing a band or choir makes,
that fills your ears till your feet it shakes.

West

I have my rules, I have my play,
two sides that want to win the day;
with ball or board or cards or dice,
I'm fun to play, not once but twice.
