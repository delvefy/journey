Pen

You are a slim tube that carries ink, a nib or a little rolling ball at your tip. A maker moulded you of plastic or metal; you hold a well of blue or black and let it down in a steady line. You sign the name, you write the letter, you cross out and underline and doodle in the margin. Mightier than the sword, they say. You are thought given a thin wet trail to follow, the hand's quick wrought tool for making words stay.

North

A wooden stick with a lead-grey heart,
I write in soft dark grey;
rub me out with the other end
and start again your way.

East

A bag of coloured stretchy skin
blown big with all your breath;
I float, I pop with a sudden bang,
and shrink to a wrinkled death.

South

A flat straight stick all marked in lines,
from naught to thirty, see;
I measure how long objects are
and draw lines straight for thee.

West

A little can of shiny metal
where soup and beans are sealed;
a sharp blade cuts around my lid
and supper is revealed.
