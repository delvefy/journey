Crash

You are the loud violent smash of things breaking and falling - the tower of bricks toppled, the tray of plates dropped, the two cars meeting, the cymbals struck. No hand means to make you; an accident does, with a great 'CRASH' that brings everyone running. You scatter pieces everywhere.

North

The loud rattly racket of hard things knocking -
pots and pans tipped on the floor,
hooves on the cobbles, a dropped tin tray -
clack-clack-clack and more.

East

The little sharp sound a bubble makes,
or a balloon on a pin;
a cork from a bottle, a cap from a gun -
'POP!' and the air rushes in.

South

A short bright blast of a horn or whistle,
'toot-toot!' the little train;
the trumpet's peep, the tugboat's call -
a cheerful blowing strain.

West

A little jump on one foot or two,
the way a sparrow goes;
you hop the hopscotch squares along -
up and down on your toes.
