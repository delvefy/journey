The Living Deep

South of the great waking and east of the companion, the floor of the world comes alive. A step west you found a first other woken beside you in the bare dark; a step north the whole deep began to wake at once. Here, at the bottom of everything, the two meet: the companion is with you still, close and warm and present, and around the both of you the waking many have risen — the old powers come awake at the floor of the world, root and beast and shade and the warm dark itself, all turned to where you and your other stand together. The deepest place there is, and it is the most alive.

They know you both. The companion's hand is in yours, and the waking many lean in around you, and your name passes among all of them — the first other holding it warm, the risen powers keeping it old, the living deep saying it under its breath on every side. The spiral is everywhere drawn at once: in your skin, in the companion's hand on yours, in root and horn and the dark's own turning, the one mark all the living deep shares. You are not alone, and not lost, and very nearly named, at the warm awake floor of the world.

Every heart beats together here — yours, the companion's, the many's, the deep's own — one great warm count at the bottom of everything, the oldest beat grown into the heartbeat of a whole living deep, and the going still wound through all of it, still going.

To the north the many rise into the great waking, the deep coming alive at once. To the east the living deep wakes wholly, the Wyld at its floor-pole, the powers sovereign and aware. To the south it settles toward the seed and the first warmth, the deep before the many. To the west the many subside to a single companion, the first other beside you. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the someone is beside you, and the whole living deep is awake around the two of you, holding your name warm among them — almost said, almost yours, and the journey, even at the floor of the world, still going on.
