Ball

You are the roundest thing a child owns - and round is your whole genius. You roll, you bounce, you fly, you come back; you turn a flat field into a game just by appearing on it. Kicked, thrown, headed, caught, you are the heart of a hundred sports and the simplest toy there is. No corners, no front or back, no right way up - only the perfect, patient circle that goes wherever it is sent, and waits to be sent again.

North

I am a slim white stick of wax,
a little flame upon my head;
I light the cake, I shrink and weep,
till all my length is shed.

East

A tiny person made of cloth,
with button eyes that cannot blink;
you feed me, dress me, sit me up,
though I can't move or think.

South

I have a spine but not a bone,
and pages thin as paper grown;
full of words and pictures bright,
you read me by the lamp at night.

West

With a row of teeth but not a mouth,
I gather leaves in autumn's chill;
across the lawn I scratch and pull,
to heap them in a hill.
