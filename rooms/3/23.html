Patter

You are a patter - the light quick tapping of many small soft sounds running together. Rain does you on the windowpane; little bare feet do you down the hall at bedtime; a hundred hurried taps that never quite become a drum. You are gentle and busy at once, a sound that makes a room feel cosy and full. Half a whisper, half a knock, you fill the quiet without ever being loud. You are the soft music of small things in a hurry.
