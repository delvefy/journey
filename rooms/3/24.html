Thump

You are a thump - the deep dull blow of something heavy landing, a fist on the table, a heart in a fright, a box dropped on the floor above. A hand or a weight makes you, and the sound comes thick and low, more felt in the chest than heard in the ear. You have no ring and no echo, just one solid beat and done. You are the noise of heaviness arriving, blunt and sudden and round.
