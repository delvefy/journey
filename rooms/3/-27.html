Postcard

You are a postcard - a stiff little rectangle of card, a sunny photo on one side and a space for a message and a stamp on the other. On holiday, everyone scribbles a hasty "Wish you were here!" on you and drops you in the box, and off you travel home to land on a doormat days later, salty with seaside air. Cheerful and breezy and just big enough for a few words, you are a little square of someone's holiday, posted all the way home to a friend.
