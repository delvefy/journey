Beep

You are a beep - the short flat electronic note a machine makes to get your attention: the microwave done, the till at the shop, the alarm clock at dawn. No hand makes you with a voice; a circuit does, a quick clean tone with no warmth in it at all. You cannot be touched, only heard - and a machine that beeps and beeps soon drives everyone mad. You are how the modern world says here, now, look at me.

A deep low rolling growly sound,
far thunder or an empty tum;
a lorry, a drum, the shaking ground -
a ___, here it'll come.

East

The slow eerie sound an old door makes
when you push it open wide;
the stairs do it under your foot at night -
a ___ - what's inside?

South

The single bright note a small bell gives,
the lift, the toast, the till;
struck just once, then gone again -
a ___, clear and shrill.

West

The small sharp sound a button makes,
or a switch upon the wall;
a pen, a latch, a seatbelt's catch -
a ___, the smallest call.
