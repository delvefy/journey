The Gathered Lost

East of the answering dark the one becomes many. A step west a single warmth turned to you and answered, almost a name; here the dark is full of them — a company of the warm dead, the lost gathered close, all turned toward you in the close black. And the herd's hides have thinned away: no hide, no horn, the beast not yet dreamed this deep. These are not beasts but presences, the dead from before they had bodies to wear, warmth and voice without shape, crowding warm and many in a dark older than the animal.

When you make your small sound — the wish to be answered, the only word you have this deep — they answer, and not one voice now but all of them, and not almost. Here in the Wyld the name is said plainly. It comes from every side at once, warm and sure, the name they have kept for you, spoken whole into the dark — and the strangeness is that you, who have chased it the whole way, still cannot close your own mouth on it. You hear it plainly in theirs. You cannot yet answer to it. The lost are here, saying you; you are the only one who cannot yet say back.

They have gathered in a slow coil about you, the old spiral made now of the warm dead themselves, winding inward to where you stand at its centre — the mark you have followed as chalk and horn and bone, here a ring of presences turning gently in toward you. And the doubled heart of the west has multiplied: many bloods fallen into your single step, the oldest count become a chorus, every heart keeping your time and carrying the name out on its beat.

To the north the company gathers into the standing herd, hide and horn risen, the beast dreamed and the horned one speaking. To the east it widens into a whole country of the present dead, the lost not gathered but lived among, names traded like breath and the living grown few. To the south the warmth thins toward older dark, before any presence, the deep from before there was anyone to lose or be lost. To the west the company narrows back to one answering warmth, a single companion, the name only almost. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. They are all here now, leaning in around you, saying your name plainly, and waiting to hear whether you will say theirs.
