Marker

You are the fat felt pen with the juicy tip, that draws in thick bright lines and squeaks across the whiteboard. Made to be bold and seen. Pop my cap, and the colour flows wet and strong - red, green, blue, black. Leave my cap off, and I dry up and die. Drawn on the wall, I will not come off.

North

A little pink block at the pencil's end
that rubs the wrong lines out;
it crumbs to bits the more you press -
mistakes, it puts to rout.

East

A picture cut in a hundred bits,
all knobs and curvy ends;
you fit them back to make it whole -
the corners first, my friends.

South

A little picture, sticky-backed,
you peel it off and press;
a gold star, a smiley face -
stuck on for cleverness.

West

A paper pocket for a letter,
you lick and seal it tight;
you write the name and stick a stamp -
then post it out of sight.
