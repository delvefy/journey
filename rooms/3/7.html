The Plenitude

North of the given world and east of the maker, the loving making goes sovereign and endless. A step south a bright mind bloomed a single world for you, a home and a place in it; a step west a maker shaped you whole out of light. Here the making knows no single world and no last work: a god of pure mind-love at the Lumen's peak, whose every loving thought is another whole world, made without end, worlds upon worlds blooming from a love that cannot exhaust itself. This is the plenitude — creation as sheer overflowing tenderness, a sovereign generosity that makes and makes and never empties, and means every made thing wholly while it makes the next.

Worlds open around you on every side, each one entire, each one loved into being and held and gone on from, an endless bright bounty of made places — and in world after world a you, beloved, given a home, named and kept. The making does not choose among them; it has love enough for all, and for you in all. Your name is the seed it plants in every world it blooms, the dear recurring word the plenitude says over and over into being. The spiral is the overflowing itself, the coil love makes as it pours into world after world, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark that is yours in every world the sovereign love makes.

There is no count but the plenitude's own pouring, the bell become the endless glad sounding of worlds bloomed and beloved, each stroke another world made and meant — and your heart beats in the overflowing, one warm thread in an inexhaustible making, your name a breath from said in every world at once.

To the north the plenitude reaches the Lumen pole, mind-love making worlds without end, the bright peak entire. To the east the making comes wholly sovereign, the cosmos nothing but a god's loving worlds. To the south it narrows to the given world, one home bloomed for you. To the west it gathers to the maker, shaping the single beloved whole. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward a home. Here a sovereign love makes you worlds without end and a home and a self in each — and still the warm coil turns, yours in every one, and still one live beat keeps the time the plenitude is a breath from naming you home in, in world after bright world.
