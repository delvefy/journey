Rip

You are a rip - the quick loud tear of cloth or paper pulled too hard. Riiip! goes the seam of your trousers as you climb the fence; riiip! goes the wrapping paper on a birthday morning. You are both the sound and the long ragged split it leaves behind. Made in a moment by two hands pulling apart, you cannot be unmade - once a thing is ripped, the hole is there to stay.
