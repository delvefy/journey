Comb

You are a comb - a flat little tool with a long row of close-set teeth. Pull me through tangled hair and out come the knots; draw me down to part the hair in a neat straight line. You slip in a pocket, you scrape across a comb-and-paper to make a buzzing tune. Moulded from plastic or carved from horn by a hand, slim and toothy, you are how a messy morning mop is turned, tooth by tooth, into tidy hair.
