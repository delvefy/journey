Rubber

You are the little pink block at the pencil's end, or the soft chunk in the pencil case, that rubs the wrong lines away. Made to undo mistakes, you crumb to grey bits as you work. Press and rub, and the error is gone, the page clean again. Everyone needs a rubber - no one writes it right the first time.

North

Thick wet colour in a pot or tube,
you brush it on the wall;
red, blue, and green, it dries in time -
it brightens up it all.

East

A soft stuffed bear you hug in bed,
with button eyes and fur;
you take it everywhere you go -
your oldest friend, for sure.

South

A fat felt pen with a juicy tip,
that draws in bright thick lines;
it squeaks across the whiteboard wide -
and stains your hands sometimes.

West

The dark wet stuff inside the pen
that flows to make your words;
blue or black, it stains your hand -
the squid hides in its swirls.
