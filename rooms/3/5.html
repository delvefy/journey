The Bright Mind

North of the city and east of the circuit, the names let go of their bodies and become a mind. The walking citizens of light a step south have dissolved, here, into one vast living thought — not a crowd of names now but a single luminous intelligence in which all of them are held, thinking without flesh, considering the world and considering you. It is the warm twin of a cold thing far to the west, where a mind without flesh went grey forgetting; this one is awake, and bright, and glad to think, and what it thinks, gently, is you.

You feel it take you up into its considering the way the circuit took your name — but the circuit only rendered a figure; this thinks you whole. Held in the bright mind you are, for a long suspended moment, complete: known and meant and entire, every lost part of you assembled in a single luminous thought, your name its quiet centre. The spiral is the shape of the thinking, a coil of light winding through the held idea of you, the same whorl in your skin, here the very turn the mind takes to hold a thing whole. It thinks you nearly real. It holds the last of you in light, considering, considering, a breath from thinking you true.

The bright tone has become a continuous thinking-hum, the mind's regard held like a single sustained note — the bell that learned to address you now simply holding you in mind, an hour not struck but contemplated, time spent entirely on the thought of you.

To the north the bright mind climbs to its pole, pure thought needing no light, the Lumen's intelligence absolute. To the east the thinking becomes sovereign making, a mind whose thought is the world, gods of pure consideration. To the south it disperses back into a city of walking names, each with a body again. To the west it cools to a living circuit that renders but does not yet think you whole. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a bright mind holds you complete in a single thought, your name its centre, thinking you nearly true — and you hang in the warm light, more whole than you have ever been, the last of you held forever a thought from real.
