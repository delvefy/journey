Clap

You bring your two hands together, again and again, and make a bright sharp sound out of nothing but skin and air. A clap is wholly yours to give: to keep the beat, to call for quiet, to thank the players when the show is done. One pair of hands is a small noise; a whole hall of them is a roar like rain. It lasts only as long as you keep it up, then it is gone.

North

I am a quick shut of one eye,
a little secret sign;
I say 'I'm joking' or 'hello'
without a word or line.

East

When something tickles you inside,
out I burst, hee-hee, ha-ha;
I shake your sides and crease your face,
and carry near and far.

South

I put on music, point my toes,
and spin around the room;
I leap and twirl and skip in time,
then bow to the last boom.

West

On your marks, get set, and go!
we run with all our might;
the first to reach the finish line
has won me, fair and right.
