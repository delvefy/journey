Book

You are a stack of leaves bound at the spine, and inside you whole worlds lie folded flat, waiting. Open your cover and a voice begins - a story, a fact, a far-off place - though no one in the room has spoken. You keep the words of the long dead as fresh as the day they were set down, and ask only a quiet hour and a little light. Dog-eared, loved, read past midnight under the blanket: you are the cheapest journey anyone can take.

North

Round I bounce and round I roll,
across the grassy ground;
kick me toward the open goal,
I'm caught and thrown around.

East

A tiny sphere of swirling glass,
I roll across the floor;
flick me hard and watch me pass,
and knock the others more.

South

A wooden stick with a soft grey core,
I draw and write all day;
sharpen me when I'm worn and sore,
and rub mistakes away.

West

Six flat sides and a fold-down lid,
I hold what's put inside;
your toys, your shoes, a present hid,
with flaps shut tight, I hide.
