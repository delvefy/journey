The Rising

South of the living deep and east of the rousing, the many begin to rise. A step north the whole deep had woken at once, the risen powers and the companion gathered close around you; a step west a single other roused alone toward presence in the floor-dark. Here, between, the deep starts to teem: not one waking warmth now but several, the wild rising on every side at the floor of the world, presences stirring up out of the seed-dark, the company multiplying from one toward many. The deepest place is filling, warmth by waking warmth, with others coming alive around you.

They rise the way the companion rose, but more of them, on every side — warm and wordless and turning toward you as they wake, the deep no longer holding one other but gathering a first many out of itself. You draw the spiral and more than one motion answers now, several faint hands at the floor of the world feeling toward the shared mark, the doubled whorl becoming a manifold one. The coil in your skin turns among them, the first of many it is matched by, the company rising warm and unspeaking in the primal dark, the wild waking plural around the place where you stand.

Your heart keeps the deep slow count, and around it now not one but several beats wake and warm and feel toward your rhythm — the doubled heart of the rousing become a first chorus, a low rising drum of waking presences at the floor of being, the count gathering toward the great warm beat the living deep will keep.

To the north the rising wakes wholly to the living deep, the many risen and the companion close, the whole floor alive. To the east the deep teems richer, the wild risen full on every side, the Wyld waking. To the south it settles back toward the seed and the first warmth, the deep before the many. To the west it narrows to the rousing, a single other waking alone. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the someone has become a first many, rising warm around you in the floor-dark — almost a homecoming, almost named — and still the warm coil turns among the waking coils, and still your beat rises in the gathering count, the wild coming alive on every side.
