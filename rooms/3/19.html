Ding

You are a ding - the single bright note a little bell gives when it is struck just once: the lift arriving, the toast popping up, the shopkeeper's counter bell tapped by a hand. No hand can hold you, though a hand makes you; you are one clear ring, here and then gone, hanging a half-second in the air. You are how a small machine says ready, how a bell says yes. The tidiest sound there is - just one note, and done.
