Stomp

You are a stomp - the heavy angry crash of a foot brought down flat and hard, the way a child marches off in a huff, shaking the floorboards with every step. No one teaches you; temper and big boots do. You rattle the cups and bring grown-ups running. Dinosaurs do you, giants do you, and so does anyone who has just been told no. Loud and cross and floor-shaking, you are a bad mood gone all the way down to the feet.
