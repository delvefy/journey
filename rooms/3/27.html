Stride

You are a stride - the long swinging step of someone walking with purpose. Legs reach out, arms swing, and the ground falls away beneath you as you eat up the distance without seeming to hurry. A tall person strides, a person in a hurry strides, a person who knows just where they're going strides. Easy and rangy and sure, you are walking at its most confident, each big step covering twice the ground of a little one, head up and going somewhere that matters.
