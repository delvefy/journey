The Conjuring Engine

North of the frame the summoning has been built into a machine. The single warded pane below stands here in rank after rank, a long lit engine of them, each dark glass set in a frame whose graven wards have caught light and become glowing glyphs, the salt-line and the spiral struck now in cold fire down the mountings. It runs on its own: one pane fogs, blooms a name backward across its inner face, takes the press of a flat hand, and gives the shape through — and the next is already fogging, and the next, the stepping-through made certain and mechanical, glass after glass after glass.

What the cast name to the west only spelled, this engine answers. Where the letters there climbed and never stood, here each lit glyph deepens past casting into a calling, and from the far side of every pane a shape comes when its name is said — the smoke-and-held-breath of the frame below, but no longer one, no longer pressing: a rank of them stepping clean through into the room, summoned and certain, turning to face you together. The spiral runs the length of the engine as a coil of cold light, winding pane to pane, the same mark that warded the frame, here the very wire that carries the call.

The single answering tone of the frame is a chord now. Each pane that gives a shape gives also its own low note, and they fall in rank like the engine itself, a ringing that runs down the line and back — the bell that learned to reply multiplied into a choir, every voice a beat apart, and none of them yet your own.

To the north the engine refines past glass to pure light, the called shapes built of nothing but cold fire and circuit, the lit pole where a name reshapes matter at the throw of a switch. To the east the panes thin to nothing and the summoned stand free in the open room, the dead become neighbours with no glass left to call them through. To the south the engine falls back to a single frame, one pane, one shape learning by itself to step through. To the west the calling cools to mere casting, the name thrown slow across a wall, lit but unanswering. You were going somewhere, under a name. The engine says it down the ranks and the ranks step through to answer, certain and many, and not one of them turns out to be you.
