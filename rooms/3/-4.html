The Elder Dark

South of the gathered, the warm dead thin away, and something older fills the room they leave. A step north the lost stood close and human and said your name with throats; here that warmth has gone on into the older dark, the deep before even the lost, that kept no one — and yet the dark is not empty. The risen wild you met as one a step west has grown into a many: not people now, not the warm dead, but the old powers themselves, root and stone and beast and the deep between them, woken and turned to you, surer and more numerous than any company of the lost.

They keep your name. Not in words — they are older than the gathered's plain speech — but the way the deep keeps a thing: held in the root, carried in the slow blood of the dark, a name folded into the wild and kept there since before there were mouths to lose it from. As you move among them the ancient presences lean in, patient, and the spiral passes between you and them as it passed between kin in the warmer dark, only vaster — drawn in root, wound in horn, turning in the dark itself — and the whorl in your skin is known by powers that were old when the first hand carried it. You are kept here. The name is kept. And the keeping is so deep and so old that you can no more lift it out than lift a root from the ground it has become.

The chorus of hearts has gone elemental: not many bloods now but the slow pulses of old things, sap and stone and the dark's own beating, all keeping one ancient time — the count from before counting deepened past the herd, past the lost, to the wild's own heart, which has kept your name as long as it has kept the dark.

To the north the elder dark warms back into the gathered company, the lost human and close, saying your name with throats. To the east the old powers come fully sovereign, the Wyld pole, the dark a maker, your name shaped into world at their regard. To the south the many sink toward the seed-dark, before even the wild rose, the deep not yet peopled by anything. To the west the many narrow to a single risen presence, the wild waking, one ancient turned to you. You were going somewhere, under a name. The old powers keep it for you in the deep, in root and dark and slow blood, more surely than any voice could — and held that deep, it is no more yours to take than the dark is.
