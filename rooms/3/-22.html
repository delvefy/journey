Mat

You are a mat - the flat thin pad that lies on the floor to be trodden on. 'WELCOME', you say at the front door, catching the mud from a hundred boots. You soften the bathroom tiles for bare wet feet, cushion the gym for tumbling, and give the cat a place to sit. You are humble and low and walked all over, yet you keep the whole house cleaner. You are the patient flat servant of the floor, asking only to be shaken out now and then.
