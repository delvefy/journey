Soap

You are soap - the slippery bar or squirt of liquid that, rubbed with water, foams up and lifts the dirt away. A hand made you from fat and ash, long ago, or mixes you in a factory now. You can be held, dropped in the bath and chased round it, worn down to a sliver. You smell of lavender or lemon, and you make slippery bubbles that drift and pop. You are the great enemy of grime, turning grubby hands clean with a swish and a scrub.
