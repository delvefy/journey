Toot

You are a short bright blast of a horn or whistle - 'toot-toot!' goes the little train, the tugboat, the trumpet's quick peep. A puff through a horn or whistle makes you. Cheerful and small, you announce, you greet, you clear the way. Not a great honk, just a friendly little parp.

North

The loud smash sound of something breaking -
a tower of bricks falls down,
the plates slip off and hit the floor -
'CRASH!' rings round the town.

East

The big deep blast of a far-off gun,
or thunder rolling near;
the firework bursting in the night -
'BOOM!' it shakes your ear.

South

The clear sweet ring of little bells,
the clock that strikes the hour;
ting-ting it sings its tuneful notes -
a bright and silver shower.

West

A box of jigsaw bits to fit
into a finished scene;
or any tricky problem set
to tease the brain, I mean.
