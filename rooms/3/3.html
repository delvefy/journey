Music

It pours out of the radio, the piano, the throats of a choir, and something in you answers before you decide to. Music is sound arranged on purpose - notes and beats and silences set in an order that means more than noise. You cannot hold it or keep it; it exists only while it plays, then lives on, somehow, in your humming head. Made by people in every land that ever was, it can make you dance, weep, or march, all without a single word.

North

When music plays I cannot stay,
my feet begin to skip and sway;
I twirl my partner, round we go,
across the floor, now fast, now slow.

East

At football games the whole crowd shouts,
a happy roar, a joyful bout;
when your team scores you give a great
big shout of joy to celebrate.

South

I am a challenge, bold and rash,
'I bet you won't' - now make a dash;
to climb, to jump, to ring and run -
I goad you on to risky fun.

West

I am a word you give and hold,
'I swear I will' - a vow of gold;
break me and the trust will end,
keep me and I bind a friend.
