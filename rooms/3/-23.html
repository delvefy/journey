Handle

You are a handle - the part of a thing made just for the hand. You arch off the side of the mug so the hot tea won't burn you; you stick out from the door to be pulled; you bridge the top of the bucket and the case so they can be lifted and carried. Wherever something must be held, turned, or hauled, there you are, shaped to fit a grip. You are the thing's offered hand, the place it says: hold me here.
