Jungle

You are a jungle - a hot green tangle of trees and vines so thick the sun barely reaches the floor. Parrots screech, monkeys swing, and somewhere a big cat is always watching. No hand made you; the heat and the rain did, growing you wild and dense and dripping. Everything here climbs over everything else, racing for the light. You are the loudest, greenest, most crowded place on earth, a riot of life with no straight paths through it.
