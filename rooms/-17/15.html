Melt

You are melt - the moment ice gives up and turns to water. The snowman droops, the icicle drips, the frost on the window runs down in little roads. You are spring arriving, the white world going soft and wet, the lolly sliding off its stick before you can eat it. You are the cold thing forgetting how to be hard, sinking quietly into a puddle and then to nothing at all.
