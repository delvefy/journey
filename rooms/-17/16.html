Soak

You are soak - to leave a thing in water until it is wet clean through. The dirty pan soaks in the sink overnight; the beans soak before the pot; the walker caught out in a downpour gets soaked to the skin. You are slow and thorough, not a splash but a steady drinking-in, until not a dry thread or crumb is left. You darken cloth and swell up paper. You are wetness that takes its time, water sinking deep into everything it touches.
