Sulk

You are a sulk - the quiet grumpy cloud that settles when things don't go your way. You sit apart, arms folded, lip out, saying nothing and meaning plenty. You won't join in, you won't be cheered, you nurse the grievance like a sore tooth. No one can hold you or hurry you; you simply have to be waited out, until at last the cloud lifts on its own and the games begin again as if you'd never gone.
