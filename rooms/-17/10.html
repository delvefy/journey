Squeak

You are a squeak - the tiniest, highest sound there is: a mouse, a new shoe on a clean floor, a door that wants oiling, a toy when it is squeezed. No hand makes you, exactly, though a rusty hinge will; you are sound stretched thin and pushed up high, sharp and small. You cannot be touched, only heard, and you make everyone look round. The smallest voice of all, you are how a little thing says here I am, in the very top of its range.
