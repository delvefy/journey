Huff

You are a huff - the short cross gust of temper that sends a person stomping off. Arms folded, chin up, out the door with a stamp: 'fine then!' You blow out hot through the nose, you slam things a little harder than you need to, you refuse to be cheered for a good five minutes. No one can hold you; you puff up big and grumpy, then quietly leak away, and soon enough the sulker is back, pretending the huff never happened.
