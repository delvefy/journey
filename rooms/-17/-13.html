Tummy

You are a tummy - the round soft belly where the food all goes after the chewing is done. You rumble and grumble when lunch is late; you fill up full and tight at a feast; you ache and groan when the sweets were one too many. A mother rubs you to soothe a poorly child. You rise and fall with every breath. You are the cosy round middle of a body, where the porridge and the cake all gather, the first part to complain when it is empty and the last to stop being glad when full.
