Whirlpool

You are a whirlpool - a spinning funnel of water that opens in the river or the sea where the currents fight, turning faster and faster and dragging down whatever drifts too near. No hand makes you; clashing waters do. Sailors have feared you for a thousand years. A smaller cousin of you spins above every emptying plughole. Round and hungry and dizzying, you are water wound into a spinning drain, a spiral with a pull you cannot fight.
