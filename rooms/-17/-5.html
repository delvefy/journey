Seed

You are a seed - a tiny hard speck with a whole plant folded up asleep inside. Drop me in the dark warm soil, give me rain and a little sun, and out I push a pale thread of root, then a green shoot reaching for the light. From something smaller than a freckle comes a flower, a bean, a towering tree. Grown by no hand, patient in the ground, you are a promise the earth keeps every single spring.
