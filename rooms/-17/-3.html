Thumb

You are a thumb - the short broad finger that faces all the others, and that is the whole trick of you. Because you can reach across, the hand can grip: the pencil, the spoon, the rope, the rung. You press, you pinch, you hitch a ride, you point straight up to say 'good!' A baby curls a fist round you and will not let go. Grown of bone and muscle, stubby and strong, you are the finger that makes a hand a hand.
