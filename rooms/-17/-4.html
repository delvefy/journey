Twig

You are a twig - a small thin stick, the littlest finger of the tree. You bud in spring, you carry a leaf or two, you snap off in the wind and lie on the path for a child to pick up. You make a tiny boat for the gutter stream, a leg for a stick-man, the smallest crack underfoot in the wood. Grown by no hand, dry and light, you are the tree shrunk down to a thing a small fist can hold.
