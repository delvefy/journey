Wind

You are the wind - the air on the move, felt everywhere and seen nowhere. You shove the trees till they creak and bow, you fill the sail, you snatch the hat and chase the leaves down the street in spinning crowds. You can be a soft cool breath on a hot cheek or a howling wall that bends the world. No hand can catch you or shut you in - you come from somewhere no one can point to, and go on past, restless, never still.

or a sailor's coat at sea;

South

all grey and cold and wet;
it hides the hills and dims the lamp -
a ___ you won't forget.

West

A wild fierce wind that roars and rips
and bends the trees right down;
it tears the tiles and slams the gates -
a ___ that whips the town.
