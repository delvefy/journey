Fog

You are fog - the cloud come down to walk among us. You hang thick and grey and damp across the road, and the world goes soft and lost: the lamp a smudge, the far hill gone, every sound muffled and close. You wet the cobwebs and bead the hair. No hand can grip you, only walk into you and feel you fold cold around. You drift, you thicken, you thin - and when the sun climbs high you simply lift, and the world comes back.
