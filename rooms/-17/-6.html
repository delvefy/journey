Snail

You are a snail - a soft slow creature carrying its own little house upon its back. You glide along on a ribbon of silver slime, two eye-stalks waving, in no hurry at all; touch you and you tuck right inside your spiral shell and wait. You come out after rain to munch the garden. Grown by no hand, gentle and unhurried, you are the patient traveller who is always at home, because home is always with you.
