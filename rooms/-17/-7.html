Spider

You are a spider - eight long legs around a round fat body, busy and clever. You spin a web of silk between the twigs, sticky and perfect, a wheel of fine thread strung with morning dew, and you sit at its heart and wait for a buzzing fly to blunder in. Some folk shriek at you and some scoop you gently out the door. Grown by no hand, patient and quick, you are the little weaver who builds a brand-new trap every single day.
