Gold

You are gold - the soft heavy yellow metal that has never lost its shine, dug from deep rock and washed from river sand. No hand made you; the earth did, long ago, and ever since, people have crossed oceans and dug deep mines for the gleam of you. You can be held, melted, hammered into a ring or a crown, bitten to test if you are real. Of all the metals you are the kingly one, the colour of the sun made solid. You are treasure itself.
