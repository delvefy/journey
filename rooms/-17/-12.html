Spine

You are a spine - the long knobbly chain of little bones that runs down the middle of the back and holds the whole body up. You bend when someone stoops and straighten when they stand tall and proud. Run a finger down a back and you feel each small bump of you, one after another. You are natural, grown not made, the strong soft-curved tower that carries the head and lets a person turn and twist. Without you, no one could ever stand up at all.
