Swell

You are a swell - the slow heaving rise and fall of the open sea, long smooth hills of water that lift a boat up and set it gently down again. There are no breaking crests in you, just the deep rolling breath of the ocean, the lazy lift that makes a small boat tip and a queasy sailor groan. Calm-looking but powerful, you are the sea's slow heartbeat felt far from any shore, rolling on and on under the wide grey sky.
