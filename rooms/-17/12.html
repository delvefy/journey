Rhyme

You are a rhyme - the little chime two words make when they end on the same sound. Cat and hat, moon and June, fall and tall - you knit them together so a verse rings like a bell. You live in songs and nursery lines and the jokes children chant in the yard. You cannot be touched, only heard, a soft echo at the end of a line. You are the trick that makes words easy to remember and a pleasure to say aloud.
