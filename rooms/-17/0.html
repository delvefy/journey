Sea

You are the sea - the vast grey-green water that wraps the whole world round, rising and falling without rest. You crash on the shore and slide back, crash and slide, day and night forever. Ships ride on you, fish swim in you, the moon pulls you up and down the sand. Salt and cold and deep and wide, full of waves and whales and secrets, you are the biggest thing there is.
