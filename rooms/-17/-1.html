Jaw

You are the jaw - the strong bony hinge that holds your lower teeth and lets your mouth open and shut. You chomp and chew, you yawn so wide it cracks, you drop in surprise. The teeth are set in a row along your edge; you clench tight when you are cross. The strongest muscle for its size in the whole body, you grind the dinner down and shape the words that come out. Open wide!
