Knee

You are a knee - the clever hinge halfway down the leg. You bend so you can crouch and kneel and climb the stairs; you fold to let you sit, you spring to let you jump. You are the first thing to meet the ground when a child trips, the first to wear a plaster and a scab. Grown of bone and bend, knobbly and bare beneath the shorts, you are the joint that turns a stiff straight leg into a leg that runs.
