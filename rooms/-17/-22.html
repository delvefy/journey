mound

A mound is a small round heap of earth, a little hill made by piling. A molehill in the lawn, a grassy bump where ancient kings were buried, the pile the digger leaves behind - all mounds. You can scramble up one in three steps and stand on top like the king of a tiny country. Solid, earthy, and wholly a thing you can climb and touch, it is the gentlest, smallest hill there is, just a swelling of the ground.

North

Tilt the ground, but not too steep,
a gentle downhill run;
roll a ball and it trickles down -
sledging here is fun.

East

The very tip-top of the mountain,
pointed, white with snow;
the highest place a climber stands -
the world spread out below.

South

A baby dog with floppy ears,
it wags and yaps and plays;
it chews your shoes and licks your face -
a bundle of waggy ways.

West

White and sharp, I sit in a pot
beside the pepper, see;
a tiny shake upon your food -
I come from the deep blue sea.
