Coal

You are coal - a hard black shiny lump, dug from deep tunnels under the ground, made of forests that grew long before there were people at all. Heap me on the fire and I catch and glow red-hot, throwing out heat for hours. Santa leaves me in a stocking for the naughty. Made by no hand over millions of years, black and heavy and ancient, you are buried summer sunlight, dug up at last to warm a winter night.
