Treat

You are a treat - a lovely little something extra, given just to make a person glad: a sweet, an ice cream, a day at the seaside, a story before lights-out. No hand can hold the idea of you, though the sweet you can; you are kindness made into a gift. You cannot be earned exactly, only given - though good behaviour helps. Trick or treat, a special treat, my treat - you are a small bright surprise of pure niceness, just because.
