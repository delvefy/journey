Snore

You are a snore - the rough rattling sound a deep sleeper makes, breathing in through an open mouth: a buzz-saw, a piglet, an old dog by the fire. No hand makes you; sleep does, all on its own, and the snorer never hears a single one. You cannot be touched, only heard through the wall, keeping the whole house awake. Loud and honest and helpless, you are the funny music of someone fast asleep, gone too deep to be embarrassed.
