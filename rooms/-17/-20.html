Dune

You are a dune - a great smooth hill of sand, heaped up grain by grain by the wind. Your back is gentle, your far side steep, and your crest is sharp as a blade where the wind spills over. In the desert you march slowly along, never quite the same shape twice; by the sea you hold the beach in place with your tough spiky grass. Soft and shifting and endlessly patient, you are sand given the shape of a wave, a slow hill that the wind is forever rebuilding.
