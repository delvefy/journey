Current

You are a current - the steady push of moving water, flowing always one way through river or sea. You carry the leaf downstream, sweep the swimmer along, and run cold and strong beneath the calm-looking surface. Out at sea you are a great invisible river, warm or cold, that ships and fish and even whales ride for thousands of miles. Powerful and unseen, you are water on the move, never resting, always travelling, tugging gently or fiercely at anything that drifts into your endless flow.
