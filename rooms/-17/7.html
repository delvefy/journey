Magic

You are magic - the wonderful, impossible power that turns frogs to princes and pumpkins to coaches. You are the rabbit pulled from an empty hat, the coin found behind an ear, the wand-wave that makes a wish come true. You live in fairy tales and conjuring tricks and the breathless moment a child still half-believes. No hand can truly hold you - you are the shiver of delight when something happens that simply, wonderfully, should not be able to.
