Desert

You are a desert - a vast dry land of sand and rock and burning sun, where the rain hardly ever comes and the days bake and the nights freeze. No hand makes you; the long thirsty years do. Your dunes shift in the wind like slow waves. A camel plods across you, a snake hides under you, and a traveller dreams of water. Empty and enormous and golden, you are the hottest, driest, loneliest stretch of the whole wide world.
