Beach

You are a beach - the strip of sand where the land runs out and the sea begins. The waves slide up you and slip back, leaving shells and ribbons of weed. Children dig you into castles, bury each other up to the neck, and race the foam. You are warm underfoot at midday and cool by evening, dotted with towels and ice-cream drips. The tide redraws you twice a day. You are the soft golden edge of the world, where everyone comes to play in summer.
