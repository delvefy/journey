Clay

You are clay - the smooth heavy earth that is soft and squashy when wet but bakes hard as stone in the heat. No hand made you; rivers and rocks ground you down over ages. You can be dug, squeezed, rolled, shaped into a pot or a wobbly model and left to dry. The potter spins you on a wheel; a child rolls you into a snake. Of all the earth you are the one that remembers a shape - you take the press of a thumb and keep it.
