Slope

You are a slope - a stretch of ground that leans, tilting up one way and down the other. Roll a ball and it runs down you; push a barrow and you make the climb hard work. Skiers love a snowy one; a gentle grassy one is perfect for rolling down. Tilted and tiring to climb but easy to come down, you are the world refusing to lie flat, the lean of a hillside that makes every journey either a puff uphill or a happy run down.
