Field

You are a field - a wide flat stretch of green under the open sky. A fence runs round you; a gate lets the farmer in. Cows graze across your back, and in summer you turn gold with wheat before the harvest. Children run and tumble on you, kick a ball the length of you. You are the plainest, broadest piece of the world, and you go on until the hedge says stop.

North

Green and damp, I sit and blink,
I leap before I look;
I catch the flies with one quick tongue
beside the lily'd brook.

South

where buckets build and spades dig deep;

it splatters when you play;
pigs roll in it, glad and round -
