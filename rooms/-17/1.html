Spray

You are the spray - the fine mist of flying droplets thrown up where water breaks. The wave smashes the rock and you burst into the air; the hose, the wave, the waterfall all fling you out in a cloud of tiny drops. You catch the sun and make a little rainbow, then settle wet and cool on every face nearby. Too fine to call rain, too wet to call air, you are the sea flung up in pieces.
