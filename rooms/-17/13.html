Drift

You are a drift - the slow, steered-by-nothing float of a thing the water or the wind has taken charge of. A leaf goes one upon the stream; a boat with no oars goes one out to sea; your idle thoughts go one when the lesson is dull. Snow becomes you too, blown into soft white heaps against a wall. You cannot be aimed, only let go. You are the going-along of whatever has stopped trying to steer, carried wherever the current chooses.
