Spoon

You are a spoon - a little shallow bowl on the end of a handle. You scoop the soup, the cereal, the ice cream; you stir the tea round and round; you measure the sugar, you feed the baby aeroplane-style. A child taps you on a pan for a drum, balances you on a nose, licks you clean. Shaped by a hand from metal or wood, smooth and curved, you are the gentlest tool at the table - made to carry food safely to the mouth.
