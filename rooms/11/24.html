Frolic

You are a frolic - playing for the pure joy of it, all skips and leaps and silly tumbles. Lambs frolic in a spring field, puppies frolic on the lawn, children frolic in the waves at the seaside. There is no point to you except fun: a bursting-out of happiness that has to move, has to jump, has to roll in the grass. Light and giddy and brimming with glee, you are what a happy heart does with a sunny day, kicking up your heels just because you can.
