Dozen

You are twelve, the baker's favourite number - twelve eggs in a box, twelve buns on a tray, twelve months in a year. No hand makes you; the counting mind makes you, a tidy round bundle just past ten. Buy a dozen and the baker slips in one more for luck. You are the cosy crate-sized number, neither few nor many, exactly twelve.

North

A baby's toy you shake and shake
that clatters, beads inside;
or the sound a loose old window makes
when the wild wind blows wide.

East

Count your fingers, every one,
on both your hands held high;
count your toes - the same again -
how many? You can't deny.

South

A sheet of questions, hard or fair,
to find out what you know;
no talking now, no peeking either -
write your answers in a row.

West

Sixty minutes ticking by,
the big hand round once more;
from school-bell to the next school-bell -
a slice of time, no more.
