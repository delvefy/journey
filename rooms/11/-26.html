clog

A clog is a shoe carved from a single block of wood, heavy and hard and clattering loudly on a stone floor - clop, clop, clop. The Dutch are famous for them, dancing in pairs with their toes turned up, and farmers once wore them to keep their feet dry in the mud. They are stiff and clumsy and you would never run a race in them, but they last for years and they never, ever let the wet get in.

A flat rubber sole and a strap for your toe,
slap-slap-slap as you walk down the beach;
summer shoes you slip right on -
cheap and comfy, within reach.

A long pole with a flat blade at the end,
dip it left and then dip it right;

strap it on and roll away;
glide and zoom along the path -
down the pavement, smooth all day.

for legs that need to take a rest;
roll along where ramps allow -
you move sitting down, it's for the best.
