Grab

Your hand shoots out and closes - to grab is to take, fast, before the chance is gone. The last biscuit, a falling glass, a friend's sleeve as the bus pulls away. There is nothing gentle in it; a grab is hunger made quick, all fingers and sudden grip. You cannot keep the act, only what it catches. Quick as a thought and twice as greedy, it is the body deciding it wants something now, and reaching to make it so.

North

To creep on your toes,
silent as a mouse,
so you do not wake
a soul in the house.

East

Hand over hand you go
up a ladder or a tree;
you use your arms and legs
to reach the top, you see.

South

The opposite of push;
I tug a rope to me,
drag a cart behind,
or yank a weed up free.

West

To shove with your hands,
to make a thing go away;
lean hard on a heavy door
to move it out the way.
