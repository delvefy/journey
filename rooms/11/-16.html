Teapot

You are a teapot - a round little pot with a curved spout, a lid, and a handle, made for brewing tea and pouring it out. A hand made you of china or metal; a hand fills you with leaves and hot water, lets you sit and steep, then tips you to fill the cups. You can be held, warmed, dressed in a cosy, painted with flowers. I'm a little teapot, short and stout - you are the cosy heart of teatime, dribbling a little from the spout.
