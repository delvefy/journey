Platter

You are a platter - the big flat dish that comes out only for a feast. Too wide for one person, you carry the whole roast, the heaped sandwiches, the pyramid of fruit, and you wear them proudly to the middle of the table. Everyone reaches across you at once. You are the dish of sharing, of birthdays and Sundays, the broad generous plate that says: there is plenty here for all.
