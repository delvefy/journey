Rattle

You are a baby's first toy - a hollow bauble of beads on a handle that clatters when it's shaken - and the loud loose sound of anything jiggling: a window in the gale, pebbles in a tin, a train over the tracks. A hand makes you for tiny hands to grip. Shake, shake, shake, and out comes your dry knocking song.

North

The dull deep sound a heavy thing
makes falling on the ground;
no ring, no clang, just 'THUD' - it lands,
a flat and weighty sound.

East

The long slow squeak an old door makes
when pushed on rusty hinge;
the floorboard's groan beneath your foot -
it sets your teeth on edge.

South

Twelve eggs sit snug inside their box,
twelve buns upon the tray;
the baker's tidy round-number word -
how many? you can say.

West

A short high sound a button makes,
'beep' - the microwave is done;
the car when locked, the watch alarm -
one little electric one.
