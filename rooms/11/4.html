Thud

You are the dull heavy sound of something big dropping - the book off the shelf, the sack on the floor, the boot kicked off at the door. No ring, no clang, just one flat 'THUD' and done. A hand or gravity makes you when weight meets ground. You land deep and dead, soaking up your own noise.

North

The noisy way to drink your soup
or suck a noodle up;
'SLLLRP!' you go against the spoon -
ill-mannered with a cup.

East

The sound and spray when something drops
into a pool or puddle;
'SPLOOSH!' the water flies up high -
you jump in for a muddle.

South

A baby's toy of beads that shake
and clatter when you swing it;
or a loose old window in the wind -
shake-shake, you cannot still it.

West

The little ting when two glasses touch
to toast and say 'cheers';
or coins that knock inside a jar -
a small bright sound one hears.
