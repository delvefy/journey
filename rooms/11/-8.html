Cloak

A great sweep of cloth that falls from the shoulders and hides all beneath - a cloak is a wall you can wear. No sleeves, no buttons, only a clasp at the throat and a long dark drape to the heels. Travellers wrap in it against the rain, wizards vanish inside it, and a child playing knight throws one on to become someone braver. Made of heavy wool or velvet, it keeps out the weather and keeps in the mystery.

North

A strip of silk
knotted at the throat;
worn with a shirt
under a smart coat.

East

Fake hair to wear
upon a bald head's dome;
a judge or a clown
puts me on, not at home.

South

A short warm coat
with a zip up the front;
you put it on outdoors
when the cold winds hunt.

West

Knitted from wool,
pulled over the head;
it hugs you warm
when the cold has spread.
