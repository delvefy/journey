Slurp

You are the noisy sucking sound of drinking - soup off the spoon, a long noodle whipped up, the last of the milkshake through a straw. No hand makes you; the lips and the breath make you, SLLLRP, loud and rude at the table. Grown-ups frown; children love you. A drink too good to sip quietly.

North

When you run hard or the day is hot,
small drops break out on your brow;
salty water on the skin -
you wipe it dry somehow.

East

The loud crunchy chewing of something hard -
a carrot, an apple, a crisp;
'munch-munch-munch' go your busy jaws -
a sound both loud and brisk.

South

The dull deep sound a heavy thing
makes dropping on the floor;
no ring, no clang, just 'THUD' it lands -
a flat sound, nothing more.

West

A big loud swallow down the throat,
a whole glass in one go;
or a scared dry swallow of nothing -
the throat bobs to and fro.
