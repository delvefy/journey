Stir

You are the round and round of the spoon in the bowl, the swirl that blends and keeps the pot from catching - the cake mix, the soup, the sugar in the tea. No still thing makes you; the wrist's slow circle does. Round and round, and the lumps melt out, and all becomes one smooth thing.

North

Rub it hard with a brush and soap,
to get the dirt right off;
the floor, the pan, the muddy knee -
you scrub till it comes off.

East

Tip the jug and let it flow,
the milk down into the cup;
a steady stream from up to down -
careful not to fill it up.

South

A quiet little laugh held in,
low in the throat, not loud;
a soft 'heh-heh' at a private joke -
amused, but not a crowd.

West

A short hard pull, a quick sharp yank,
on the rope or the lead;
the dog strains forward, you pull back -
one strong jerk indeed.
