Glide

You are a glide - the smooth effortless way a thing moves when nothing stops it: a swan on the lake, a skater on the ice, a paper plane on the air. No hand need push you on; once started, you slide along all by yourself, level and silent and serene. You cannot be held, only watched - and it always looks easy, though it almost never is. You are motion with the bumps taken out, the loveliest way of all to travel.
