Tiptoe

You are the silent walk on the balls of the feet, heels held high, creeping past the sleeping baby or the guarding dog. No loud thing makes you; care does, soft and slow. Each step a held breath, the floorboards begged not to creak. Move like me, and no one hears you come or go.

North

A quiet little laugh held low,
down in the throat, not loud;
a soft 'heh-heh' at a private joke -
not sharing with the crowd.

East

Stick out one finger, straight and stiff,
to show the way to go;
'Look over there!' your finger says -
though rude to do it, so.

South

Reach out fast and snatch it tight,
your fingers close around;
you grab the rail before you fall -
quick hands that hold what's found.

West

A light quick knock, tip-tap, tip-tap,
a finger on the glass;
a gentle beat upon the desk -
soft sounds that lightly pass.
