Sock

You are a sock - a soft knitted tube that hugs the foot from toe to ankle. You come in pairs, though one of you is forever lost in the wash, and you keep the toes warm inside the shoe. Made by hands - spun, knitted, and stitched - you are pulled on each morning and peeled off each night. Stripy or plain, woolly or thin, you are the small warm wrapping that no cold floor can get past.
