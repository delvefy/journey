Saucer

You are a saucer - the little round dish that sits beneath a teacup, shallow with a dip in the middle to catch the drips. You keep the tablecloth clean and give the cup a tidy place to rest. People say a cat laps cream from one of you, and that a bright full moon is as round as you are. You are made by hand of china, smooth and glazed and easy to chip. You are the quiet partner of every cup of tea, small and round and always underneath.
