Swoop

You are a swoop - the fast curving dive a bird makes when it drops from the sky, skims low, and sweeps up again in one smooth rush. The hawk does you on a mouse; the swallow does you over the pond; a bat does you through the dusk. You are a motion, not a thing to hold, all speed and graceful falling and rising. You are the most thrilling move in the whole air, the long bold plunge and the swift climb out, done in a single breath.
