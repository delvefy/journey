Welly

You are a welly - a tall rubber boot, shiny and waterproof, made for stamping through every puddle and bog. Pull one on each foot and the rain and the mud have no chance: you keep toes warm and dry while the whole world turns to slosh. Farmers wear you, gardeners wear you, and no child can resist the deepest puddle while wearing you. Bright and bouncy and gloriously splashable, you turn a wet grey day into the very best kind of fun.
