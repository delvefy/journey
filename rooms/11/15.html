Slide

You are a slide - the smooth swift glide along or down. Climb the ladder, sit at the top, and whee, down the steep chute you go in a rush; or skate flat across the frozen pond from one end to the other. You are also that smooth helpless slither when a sock meets a polished floor. No hand can hold you - you are the lovely frictionless rush of going fast with nothing in the way, all the way to the bottom.
