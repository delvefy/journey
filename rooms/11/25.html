Bound

You are a bound - to spring forward in great long leaps, all four feet leaving the ground at once. A deer bounds over a gate, a big dog bounds across the field to greet you, a kangaroo bounds across the plain. Each leap covers the ground in one joyful spring. Springy and energetic and full of bounce, you are running turned into flying, eating up the distance in huge happy leaps, soaring a little higher and further with every bound.
