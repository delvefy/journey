Prance

You are a prance - the springy high-stepping strut of a show pony or a pleased-with-itself person, knees lifted, head held high, hooves or feet barely touching the ground. No one teaches you; pride and good spirits do. You bounce and toss and make a little show of every step. The horse does you round the ring; the winner does you to the changing room. Bouncy and proud and a touch ridiculous, you are walking turned into a boast.
