Chuckle

You are the small quiet laugh held low in the throat - not the loud guffaw, just a private 'heh-heh' at a joke only you have caught. No big sound you make; amusement kept gentle does. Shoulders shaking, lips pressed tight. You bubble up when something is funny and nobody else need know.

North

Round and round with a spoon in the bowl,
to mix the cake or tea;
you stir the soup so it won't catch -
a circling, blending spree.

East

A sharp loud blast of air through the nose,
the laugh that won't stay in;
the pig does it, the horse does too -
a snuffle and a din.

South

The silent walk on the balls of the feet,
heels held high off the ground;
you creep past the sleeping baby -
not making one small sound.

West

The heavy stamp of an angry foot,
brought down hard in a mood;
the giant's tread, the cross child's march -
it thunders, loud and rude.
