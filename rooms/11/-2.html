Handle

You are the part a hand was meant to hold - the curved grip of a mug, the bar on a door, the shaft of a spade. A hand shapes you to fit a hand. Without you the kettle scalds, the suitcase will not lift, the door will not open. You stick out, ready, waiting to be grasped. You are the made-for-gripping part of every useful thing.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A roof of cloth on bending poles,
pegged out upon the grass;
you crawl inside to sleep at night
and watch the rainstorms pass.

East

A little round ball stuck on a door,
you grip it, give a turn;
it works the latch and lets you through -
a small round thing to learn.

South

A tall glass jar where flowers stand,
filled up with water clear;
you set it on the table top -
the blooms look pretty here.

West

A little iron guard upon a door
that will not let you in;
you fit the key and give a turn -
'click,' and you begin.
