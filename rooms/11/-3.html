Vase

You are a tall smooth jar made to hold flowers - of glass or painted clay, filled with cool water to keep the blooms alive. A hand shapes you on the wheel; a hand sets you on the windowsill or the table's heart. Knock you and you topple, smash, and spill. You show off the roses and the tulips, the open mouth where a bunch of stems stands proud.

North

The bit you grab to lift or carry -
on a bucket, mug, or case;
a hand was made to fit just there,
to grip and hold in place.

East

A little seat with no back at all,
on three or four short legs;
you perch on it to reach up high,
or sit to scrub the steps.

South

A soft fat pad you sit upon
to make a hard chair kind;
stuffed with feathers, plumped up round -
the comfiest you'll find.

West

A length of cloth across the window
you draw to shut the night;
pull it back to greet the morning,
closed, it blocks the light.
