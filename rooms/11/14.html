Skid

You are a skid - the sudden slide of something that has lost its grip. The car hits ice and slews sideways with a screech; the bike locks its wheel and leaves a long black streak; you run too fast on the wet floor and your feet shoot out from under you. No hand can hold you back once you start; you are speed with nothing to hold onto, sliding wide-eyed and helpless until the friction runs out and you stop.
