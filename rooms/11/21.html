Pounce

You are a pounce - the sudden spring of a cat from stillness to strike. You crouch low, you waggle, you go utterly quiet, and then all at once you launch, paws spread, landing exactly where the mouse was. You are not a thing to hold but a thing to do, a coiled moment let loose. Half patience, half explosion, you are the leap that comes from nowhere and lands on its mark before the prey can blink.
