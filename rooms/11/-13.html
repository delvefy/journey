Clock

You are the clock - the round face on the wall with two hands that never stop. The little hand crawls, the big hand sweeps, and somewhere inside you tick-tock, tick-tock, counting out every second of the day. You tell us when to rise and when to sleep, when the lesson ends and the train departs. Wind me, plug me, hang me high: I am how the world keeps time, one tick at a time.
