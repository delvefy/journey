Scamper

You are a scamper - the quick light run of small feet going somewhere in a hurry. The mouse does it across the kitchen floor, the squirrel up the trunk, the children down the hall when the ice cream van is heard. You are all twinkling steps and no weight at all, a dash done by something little and quick. Patter patter patter and gone - that bright small busy rush is you.
