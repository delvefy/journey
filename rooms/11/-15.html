Jug

You are a jug - a round-bellied pot with a handle on one side and a pouring lip on the other. You sit full of milk on the breakfast table, water on a hot day, juice at a party; tip me up and out it streams in a smooth cool arc, neat into the glass. Thrown from clay or moulded by a hand, fat and friendly, you are the cheerful pourer that carries the drink from the fridge to the cup without a single spill.

A little bowl upon a stick,
you scoop your soup or peas;
you stir your tea and lick it clean -
a ___, if you please.

East

A tall thin ship that blasts to space
on a roaring tail of fire;
ten, nine, eight... blast off! it climbs -
a ___, higher and higher.

South

A round fat pot with a spout and lid
to pour the tea out neat;
'I'm a little ___, short and stout' -
you tip me, nice and sweet.

West

Push it in the socket in the wall
to make the lamp glow bright;
or pop one in the bathtub's hole -
a ___ holds water tight.
