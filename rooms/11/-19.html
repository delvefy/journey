Napkin

You are a napkin - the square of cloth or paper that sits beside the plate and saves the lap from spills. You wipe the gravy from a chin, dab the jam from sticky fingers, and tuck under the collar of a messy eater. At a grand dinner you are folded into swans and fans; at a picnic you are just a thin paper sheet. You are the meal's tidy helper, the little flag of cloth that stands between dinner and a stain.
