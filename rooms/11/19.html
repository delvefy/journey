Sprint

You are a sprint - the all-out dash, flat and fast and over in seconds, when you run as hard as your legs will go. The hundred-metre racer does you down the track; the child does you for the ice-cream van. You cannot keep one up for long - the breath burns, the legs ache, and you must slow. You are speed spent all at once, the body emptied into a few flying strides. You are running with nothing held back.
