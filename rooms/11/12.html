Scrub

You are a scrub - hard cleaning with a brush and water, rubbing back and forth to shift the dirt that will not budge. You scrub the muddy floor, the grubby pots, the ring around the bath, your own grimy knees on a Saturday night. Elbow grease and soapy suds, round and round and back and forth, until the grime gives up and the thing comes clean. You are honest work, the busy sound of getting clean.
