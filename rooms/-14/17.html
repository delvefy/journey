Thirst

You are thirst - the dry tight craving for a drink, when the mouth turns to dust and the tongue sticks. You come on after running in the heat, after salty crisps, after a night's sleep. A glass of cold water is your only cure, and the first gulp is the sweetest thing there is. Lost in a desert, you grow cruel; safe at home, you are just a nudge toward the tap. You are the body's plain call for water, the dryness that begs to be quenched.
