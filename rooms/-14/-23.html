Snowflake

You are a snowflake - a tiny six-armed star of ice, drifting down from a grey winter sky. No two of you are ever quite the same; each one is a perfect little pattern of frozen lace, far too small and far too quick to study before you land on a mitten and melt. Millions of you together turn the world white and soft and silent. Delicate and fleeting and impossibly intricate, you are winter's finest handiwork, a frozen flower of water that lives for a moment and then is gone.
