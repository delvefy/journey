Whisker

You are a long stiff hair upon the cat's face, finer than thread, twitching at the world. No hand made you; you grew to feel. You tell the cat if the gap is too small, if the mouse has moved, if the wind has changed. Brush against me and I quiver. Cut me off, and the night goes blind.

North

Wet black land of mud and reeds,
where frogs and herons hide;
it sucks your boots, it hangs with mist -
no firm ground to stride.

East

A long curved tooth that juts right out,
the elephant's great pair;
the walrus has them, white and sharp -
it digs and fights with care.

South

A long thin fish, like a swimming snake,
that slips through mud and weed;
slick and slimy, hard to hold -
in dark deep water freed.

West

The flat thin blade on the fish's back
that cuts the water clean;
the shark shows one above the wave -
the scariest ever seen.
