Slush

You are slush - the grey wet mush that snow becomes when it starts to melt, trodden and churned along the pavement. Not the crisp white snow you can play in, not clean water either, but something cold and sloppy in between. Your boots go squelch through you; the gutters run with you. You are winter going soft and tired, the slow grey end of every snowfall, soaking through the toes.
