Hyena

You are a hyena - a wild dog of the hot dry plains, with a sloping back and a powerful jaw. You roam in cackling packs through the dark, scavenging and hunting, and you make a wild yipping call that sounds for all the world like someone laughing far too hard. Grown by no hand, sly and tough and tireless, you are the creature whose laugh on the night wind makes everything else go quiet and listen.
