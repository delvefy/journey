Ice

You are ice - water gone hard and still with cold, clear or cloudy, smooth and slippery. You creep across the puddle overnight and skin the pond so it cracks and groans. You clink in a glass, you glaze the path so boots go skidding, you hang in spikes from the gutter. Cold to the touch and quick to melt in a warm hand, you are winter made solid, the season's grip on the world, here in the morning and gone by noon.
