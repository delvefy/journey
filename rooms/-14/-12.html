Lip

You are a lip - one of the two soft edges that frame the mouth, top and bottom. You press together to say 'mmm', you push out in a pout, you stretch wide in a grin, you smack when the food is good. You go cracked and chapped in the winter wind. You whistle, you blow a kiss, you hold a secret behind you. Grown of the living face, soft and pink, you are the gentle gate the words and kisses and whistles all come out through.
