Throb

You are the deep slow drum of pain - the hit thumb, the headache, the cut that beats in time with your heart. No hand makes you; the hurt body does, pulsing dull and red. Each throb a wave that rises and fades and rises again. Hold the sore place still, and feel me knock, and knock, and knock.

North

The tickling hiss of bubbles rising
in lemonade and pop;
the sherbet on your tongue goes wild -
that bubbly, prickling hop.

East

The hot bright hurt when fire bites skin,
or the sun on your back too long;
red and sore, it stings and smarts -
the kettle's touch is wrong.

South

Wet black ground of mud and reeds
where nothing stands up dry;
the heron wades, the bog-pools gleam -
mist creeping low and sly.

West

The steady beat beneath your wrist,
the thump of the heart inside;
press two fingers, feel it knock -
the drum on which you ride.
