Jolly

You are jolly - cheerful right through, the sort of mood that hums a tune and laughs out loud and slaps a friend on the back. Father Christmas is jolly; so is a good party, a fat round robin, a sing-song round the fire. You make a room warmer just by walking in. Bright and bouncy and full of good cheer, you turn grey days golden, find the funny side of everything, and pass your bubbling happiness round like a plate of warm mince pies.
