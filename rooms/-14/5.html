Guilt

You are the heavy weight that sits inside when you have done a wrong - the broken thing hidden, the lie told, the kindness withheld. No hand makes you; your own knowing does. It will not let you rest or look them in the eye. Own up, they say, and I lift; keep the secret, and I press, and press.

North

The nagging 'maybe-not' inside,
the feeling you're not sure;
you half-believe and half do not -
unsteady, insecure.

East

The green and gnawing want inside
for what is not your own;
their bike, their slice, their bigger prize -
it gnaws you to the bone.

South

The colour of the sun and lemons,
of chicks and buttercups;
the brightest, gladdest, sunniest shade -
that lights the daytime up.

West

The piggish want for more and more,
too much cake, too many toys;
never enough, you grab it all -
it spoils your simple joys.
