Dimple

You are a dimple - the little dent that pops into a cheek the moment a smile arrives. You hide in chins and the backs of chubby baby hands, a small soft press in the skin that comes and goes. Nobody puts you there; you are just how some faces are made, a tiny well that fills with happiness. Press a finger to your own cheek and grin - if I appear, that is me, saying hello.
