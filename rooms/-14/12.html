Fact

You are a fact - a thing that is simply, truly so. The sky is blue, a spider has eight legs, water is wet: nobody made these up, and arguing won't change them. You are what is left when the guessing and the wishing are cleared away. You live in books and lessons and the proud 'did you know?' a child blurts out at tea. No hand can hold you - you are a small hard piece of the truth, the same for everyone, whether they like it or not.
