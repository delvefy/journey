Muddle

You are the tangle where nothing is in its place - the sock drawer upended, the sums all jumbled, the sleepy head that cannot think straight. No hand makes you; carelessness and confusion do. Bits everywhere, none of them matching. Sort me out slowly, one thing at a time, and the muddle untangles into order again.

North

A dirty grey smear on the page or your cheek,
where the pencil or jam got rubbed;
you wipe it and only make it worse -
a mark that won't be scrubbed.

East

When you spin fast or lose your specs,
the world goes soft and smeared;
the speeding car is just a streak,
its sharp edges disappeared.

South

The nagging 'maybe-not' inside,
the feeling you're not sure;
you half-believe and half do not -
unsteady, insecure.

West

A great big bother over a little thing,
all flap and flutter and noise;
you make a fuss, you whine and fret -
over lost or broken toys.
