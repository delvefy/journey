Icicle

You are an icicle - a long clear spike of ice hanging point-down from the gutter, grown drip by frozen drip on a cold still night. No hand makes you; dripping water and a hard frost do, building you longer with every freeze. You sparkle in the low sun and drip when it warms, sharp enough to be a sword in a small fist. A frozen tear of the roof, you cling above the door until a thaw or a knock sends you crashing down.
