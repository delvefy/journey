Hunger

You are hunger - the hollow feeling that calls you to the table. You begin as a small grumble and grow, the longer you are ignored, into a roar in the belly that drowns out every other thought. You make the plainest bread taste like a feast. You are the body asking, plainly and often, to be fed. Answer me with a meal and I go quiet; forget me, and I will not let you forget for long.
