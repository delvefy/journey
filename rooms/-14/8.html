Smudge

You are a smudge - a smear of dirt or ink rubbed across a page or a cheek, soft at the edges, hard to wipe away. A smudge of chocolate, a smudge of pencil where the hand dragged through it, a smudge on the window where a nose pressed close. You are not quite a stain and not quite a mark, just a blurry dark patch where something was rubbed and spread. Smear me, and the clean thing is clean no more.
