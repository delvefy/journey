Eel

You are the long slick fish that is almost a snake, sliding through mud and weed in the dark river-bottom. No hand made you; you slip every grip, slimed and quick. You swim a thousand miles to breed in a far sea. Catch me if you can - I will pour through your fingers like cold water.

North

A long stiff hair on the cat's face,
that twitches as she creeps;
it feels the dark, it spans the gap -
a feeler that she keeps.

East

A see-through blob that floats the sea,
with trailing stinging threads;
no bones, no eyes, just drifting jelly -
it bobs where the current spreads.

South

A sea bug, red when cooked, dark when not,
with two great snapping claws;
it scuttles on the ocean floor -
and grabs with its strong jaws.

West

A sea-beast with ten waving arms
that squirts out clouds of ink;
it jets away through the deep dark sea -
quicker than you think.
