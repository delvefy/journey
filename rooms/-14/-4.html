Seahorse

You are the tiny upright fish with a horse's head and a curling tail, drifting among the weeds. No hand made you; the warm sea did. You grip the grass with your tail and sip the water for your food. Strangest of all: it is the father who carries the eggs. Small and slow, you are the sea's own little wonder.

North

A sea bug, red when cooked, dark when not,
with two great snapping claws;
it scuttles on the ocean floor -
and grabs with its strong jaws.

East

A small smooth lizard of the pond,
with a flat and waving tail;
it swims in the cold dark water -
slick and quick and pale.

South

A tiny round red beetle bug
with little black dot spots;
it lands upon your finger-tip -
then flies to garden plots.

West

A sea-thing shut in two hard shells
that clamp together tight;
it buries down in the wet sand -
shut up, and out of sight.
