News

You are the news - fresh word of what has just happened, passed from mouth to ear before it can grow old. No hand can hold you; you are made of facts so new they are still warm, and by tomorrow you are old news and forgotten. You travel fast - a whisper, a shout, a headline - and everyone wants you first. You are how the world tells itself, all at once, what has changed since yesterday.
