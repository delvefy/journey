Hum

You are a hum - a low soft sound made with the mouth shut tight. You are the tune someone half-remembers and breathes through closed lips while they work; you are the steady note of the fridge, the wires, the bees in the lavender. You ask for no words and no real listening, just a warm vibration that fills the quiet. Made by no hand, too soft to catch, you are a sound dozing somewhere between silence and a song.
