Tired

You are tired - the heavy, dragging feeling at the end of a long day, when the eyes droop and the legs are lead and the whole self wants only a bed. No one chooses you; work and play and not enough sleep build you up until a yawn splits your face. You make the cleverest child cross and the bravest grown-up quiet. The body's plea for rest, you are the weight that pulls the eyelids down and turns the softest pillow into the best thing in the world.
