Grumpy

You are grumpy - the cross scowling mood where nothing pleases and everyone is annoying and the whole day got out of bed the wrong side. No one chooses you; too little sleep and too much going wrong make you. You snap at kind questions and huff at small things and fold your arms tight. A biscuit and a nap usually cure you. Prickly and short-fused and secretly a bit sad, you are the cloudiest of moods, the grumble worn on the face all morning long.
