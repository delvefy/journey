Calm

You are calm - quiet and still and unruffled, with not a worry in the world. The sea lies flat as glass, the wind has dropped, and your breathing goes slow and easy. Nothing hurries you, nothing frets you; you could sit and watch the clouds for hours. After a fright or a fuss, you are the slow settling of the heart. Peaceful and gentle and steady, you are the smooth untroubled stillness that comes when all the storms have passed and everything is, at last, perfectly all right.
