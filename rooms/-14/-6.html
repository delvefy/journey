Pigeon

You are the grey bird of the city square, fat and bold, strutting after the dropped chips and the scattered crumbs. No hand made you; you took the town for your own. You coo on the ledge, you bob your head as you walk, you scatter up in a clap of wings - and settle right back down for more.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A tiny round red beetle bug
with little black dot spots;
it lands upon your finger-tip -
then flies to garden plots.

East

A bright-feathered bird that talks like you,
red, green, and blue, and loud;
it sits upon the pirate's arm -
it says your words out proud.

South

A tall pink bird on stilty legs,
that stands on one in the lake;
it bends its neck to sift the mud -
for shrimp and weed to take.

West

A bird that drums upon the tree,
'rat-a-tat-tat' all day;
it bores for bugs beneath the bark -
then knocks the hours away.
