Mind

You are the mind - the thinking, dreaming, remembering part of a person, that lives somewhere behind the eyes and is made of no stuff at all. No hand made you; you grew along with the brain, but you are not the brain - you are what it does. You cannot be touched, only changed: you make things up, you change your mind, you keep secrets and solve riddles. Sharp or scattered, made-up or wandering, you are the busy invisible place where a person most truly lives.
