Fin

You are a fin - the thin flat blade a fish wears on its back and sides and tail to steer and balance through the water. You flick and the fish turns; you ripple and it hovers in place. The shark's tall one cuts the surface and makes the swimmers flee for shore. The dolphin lifts its bent one as it leaps. You are part oar and part rudder, the wing a swimming thing wears instead of feathers. You are how a fish holds its place and finds its way in the wide deep sea.
