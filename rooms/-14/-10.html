Shell

You are a shell - the hard curved house a sea creature leaves behind. You lie on the wet sand, pink and white and pearly, ridged and whorled, and a child picks you up and holds you to an ear to hear the sea still sighing inside. You once kept a soft small life safe from the waves; now you are a treasure for a pocket, washed and tumbled smooth. Grown by no hand, you are the sea's small gift, cast up at the tide line.
