Knuckle

You are a knuckle - the hard round knob of bone where a finger bends, the hill in the back of a folded fist. You crack with a popping sound that makes grown-ups wince. You are skinned in a tumble, white when a hand grips too hard, and rapped on a door to ask to come in. Four of you stand in a row across each hand. You are the hinge of the finger, the working bump that lets a hand make a fist or hold a pen.
