Ache

You are an ache - a dull steady hurt with no edge to it, a soreness that throbs low and will not be pointed at. You settle in a tooth, a head, a tired leg, and stay, neither sharp nor sudden, just there. No hand makes you; the body does, complaining quietly of too much or too little. You cannot be seen or held, only felt, a slow grey throb that comes with the cold or the worry and slowly, slowly fades.
