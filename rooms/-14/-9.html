Mule

You are a mule - half horse, half donkey, and famous for your stubborn will. You carry heavy packs up steep and rocky paths where no cart could go, sure-footed and tireless. 'Stubborn as a mule,' people say, when you plant your hooves and will not budge an inch. You have long ears and a loud bray. Patient, strong, and dogged, you do the hard slow work that other beasts refuse.

North

The biggest deer in cold north woods,
with antlers wide and flat;
I wade the lakes for weed to eat -
a great big nose, at that.

East

I cross the desert, dune by dune,
with humps upon my back;
I store my water, plod for days -
no drink upon the track.
