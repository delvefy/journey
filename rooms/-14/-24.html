emerald

An emerald is a stone of pure green, the green of wet leaves and deep forests, sparkling cold in the light. Dug from the dark rock and cut into glittering faces, it is one of the rarest jewels there is - kings set them in their crowns. Hold one up and the whole world turns green through it. Heavy, hard, and wholly a thing you can grip, it is a little frozen drop of the greenest green, made by the earth itself.

North

I tumble down on winter days,
white and cold and light;
no two of me are quite the same -
a tiny star of white.

East

A great white bear on the frozen ice,
I hunt the seals all day;
my fur is thick, my paws are huge -
in the freezing north I stay.

South

A tiny fluffy baby cat
that chases balls of wool;
it mews and pounces, climbs the drapes -
of mischief always full.

West

A precious gem of deepest blue,
it sparkles in a ring;
as blue as midnight, hard and bright -
a rich and royal thing.
