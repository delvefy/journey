Lobster

You are the armoured crawler of the seabed, blue-black until the pot turns you red, two great claws held out before you. No hand made you; the cold sea did. You scuttle backwards into the rocks and snap at all that comes too near. Older than you look, and slow - the deep dark floor is your kingdom.

North

A long slick fish, almost a snake,
that slips through mud and weed;
slimy and quick, hard to hold -
in dark deep water freed.

East

A clever grey swimmer of the sea,
that leaps and clicks and plays;
it is not a fish but breathes the air -
in friendly leaping ways.

South

A tiny curl of a fish that stands,
with a horse's little head;
it grips the weed with a curly tail -
the dad has young instead.

West

A tiny pink curl of the sea,
with feelers, fine and thin;
smaller than your littlest finger -
a mouthful, tail and fin.
