Freckle

You are a freckle - one tiny brown speck the sun leaves on a cheek or a nose. You come in summer, sprinkled like flecks of pepper, and you cluster across a face that has played outside all day. No hand makes you; the light does, dabbing you on while you weren't looking. You are small and natural and entirely your own, a little dot of warm weather kept on the skin until the winter pales you away.
