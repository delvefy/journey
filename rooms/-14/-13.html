Rib

You are a rib - one of the curved bones that arch around the chest like the staves of a barrel, making a cage to keep the heart and lungs safe. No hand made you; you grew, a dozen on each side, joined to the spine behind. You can be felt beneath the skin, counted with a finger, tickled between. They say the very first woman was made from one. You are the body's own brave cage, bending a little with every breath.
