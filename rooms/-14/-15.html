Hip

You are a hip - the broad bony hinge at the side of the body, where the leg swings from the trunk. You carry the weight when someone stands, and swing when they walk or dance. A mother rests a baby on you; a hand rests there when someone is cross. You are natural, grown not made, a knob of bone wrapped in muscle. Bump a table and it is you that finds the corner first.
