Weasel

You are a weasel - the long, low, slinky little hunter with a thin brown body that pours through any gap. You dart down rabbit holes and slip between the stones, quick as a thought and twice as sly. Pop goes the weasel, the old song says, and pop you do, here and gone. You are natural, grown not made, all fur and whisker and bright black eye. Of all the small wild beasts you are the slipperiest, the one no fence can keep out.
