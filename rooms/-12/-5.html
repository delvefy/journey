Caterpillar

You are a long soft crawler with many little legs, munching your way along leaf after leaf, growing fat. No hand makes you; you hatch from a tiny egg and eat and eat. Then you spin a case around yourself and sleep - and out you come, changed utterly, a butterfly with wings. Striped or green or furry.

North

A little black blob with a wriggly tail
that swims in the springtime pond;
it grows four legs and loses its tail -
a frog it turns beyond.

East

A tiny speck that bites and leaps,
it hides in the dog's warm hair;
it springs a hundred times its height -
a circus all its share.

South

A soft white bird that coos so gently,
the sign of peace, they say;
it carried the leaf to the ark of old -
it flutters white away.

West

Two bright painted wings that flutter
from flower to flower in the sun;
it was a crawler once, but now
it dances - a lovely one.
