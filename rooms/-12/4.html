Hiss

You are the long sharp 'ssss' that warns and threatens - the snake coiled to strike, the cat with its back arched, the kettle letting off steam, the air leaking from a tyre. No hand makes you; sharp breath or escaping air draws you out, thin and steady and dangerous. You say 'keep back'.

North

The dull grey feeling of nothing to do,
when the long wet day won't end;
you sigh, you slump, 'I'm bored,' you moan -
no game, no fun, no friend.

East

The open-mouthed amazement you feel
at the stars, the sea, the snow;
'how can it be so vast, so strange?' -
a marvel, head to toe.

South

A low deep rolling growl of sound -
far thunder, a hungry tum,
a lorry on the cobbled road -
it goes on, rrr, like a drum.

West

The spitting hissing sound of frying -
sausages in the pan,
bacon curling, oil that pops -
'ssss', as hot as it can.
