Cramp

You are a cramp - the sudden tight squeeze that grips a muscle and won't let go, a knot of biting pain. You seize the leg in the swimming pool, the foot in the night, the hand that has written too long. You come without warning and bend the body double until you ease. Rub the muscle, stretch it out, and slowly you loosen your hold. You are the body's painful charley-horse, a muscle clenched too hard and stuck.
