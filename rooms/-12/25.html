itchy

Itchy is the maddening little feeling that begs to be scratched. A woolly jumper, a healing scab, a midge bite on your ankle - and suddenly there is nowhere else in the world but that one spot, prickling and tickling and crying out for your nails. Scratch it and the relief is wonderful for a moment, then it creeps back worse. You cannot see it or hold it; it lives just under the skin. Natural, weightless, impossible to ignore.

until your skin is wet;

East

A light so bright it hurts your eyes
and makes you blink and turn;
the sun on snow, a camera flash -
too brilliant, it can burn.

South

Your mouth is dry, your throat is parched,
you'd love a long cool drink;
a glass of water, gulped right down -
that's how you feel, I think.

West

Not crying, no, but down and flat,
a sad and sulky mood;
the corners of your mouth turn down -
you're sad, in plainer words.
