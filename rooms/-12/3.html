Rumble

You are the low deep rolling growl of sound that you feel as much as hear - far thunder before the storm, the empty tummy at noon, the lorry over the bridge, the avalanche on the mountain. No hand makes you; great moving weight and far-off force set you grumbling along, 'rrrrr', on and on.

North

The long sharp 'ssss' a snake lets out,
or the kettle's steamy breath;
the cat that arches up its back -
a warning sound, like death.

East

The huge deep blast a lion makes,
or a crowd when the goal goes in;
the waterfall, the jet, the storm -
a thunder, loud as sin.

South

Not the burn of fire, just gentle heat -
the sun upon your skin,
the glow beside the hearth at night -
a cosy peace within.

West

The soft quick tapping rain makes
on the roof and windowpane;
or tiny feet across the floor -
pit-pat, light as rain.
