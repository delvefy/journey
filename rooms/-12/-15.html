Mole

You are a mole - a small velvet-furred animal that lives its whole life in the dark, deep under the lawn. No hand made you; the earth did, and gave you great spade-like paws to dig and dig, throwing up little hills of soil that the gardener hates. You are felt only rarely, for you almost never come up. Half-blind, you find your way by smell and touch through a hidden maze of tunnels. You are the quiet digger beneath everything, the secret of the dark soil.
