Secret

You are a secret - a thing known but kept hidden, sealed behind the lips. You are whispered behind a cupped hand, written in code, buried in a diary with a tiny lock. You make the keeper feel special and a little fit to burst. You can be a happy one - a surprise party, a hidden gift - or a heavy one that wants out. No hand can hold you; you live in the quiet between what is known and what is said.
