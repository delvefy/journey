Cream

You are the soft pale yellow-white of the thick top of the milk, of butter and ivory and old lace. No hand made you alone; a drop of yellow warming white makes you. Gentle, rich, and quiet - the colour of pudding and clotted cream and a Labrador's coat. Not quite white, but warmer, softer, kinder.

North

A low soft blur of distant talk,
or the brook over the stone;
a gentle, blended, hushing sound -
not one word on its own.

East

The colour of clouds before the rain,
of stone and a battleship;
not black, not white, but in between -
a dull and misty grip.

South

The colour of mud and bark and bears,
of chocolate and of toast;
of autumn leaves and a conker's shine -
the earthy one we like the most.

West

A light brown shade, the colour of sand,
of a digestive biscuit too;
the sun bakes it onto your arms -
a brownish, sandy hue.
