Eagle

You are the great bird of the high crags, broad wings spread on the wind, eyes sharp enough to spot a mouse from the clouds. No hand made you; you ride the rising air and drop like a stone on your prey. Fierce and far-seeing, you are the king of the sky's edge. Look up - that speck is you.

North

A soft white bird that means us peace,
it coos up in the tree;
it's set free, fluttering, at the church -
as gentle as can be.

East

A great white bird upon the lake,
neck curved into an S;
it glides so still across the water,
in calm and snowy dress.

South

A flat-tailed swimmer of the stream
that gnaws the trees right down;
it builds a dam of sticks and mud -
a builder, wet and brown.

West

A bird that spreads a great wide fan
of feathers, blue and green,
with shining eyes upon its tail -
the proudest ever seen.
