Thirsty

You are thirsty - that dry, parched feeling when your mouth has gone like sandpaper and all you can think of is a cold drink. After a long hot game, after salty crisps, after a run in the sun, you creep up and your tongue sticks and your throat begs for water. A tall cool glass and a few big gulps and you are gone. Dry and longing and easily fixed, you are the body's way of saying its water is running low, and please, please fetch a drink, and quick.
