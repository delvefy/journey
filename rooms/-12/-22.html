Reindeer

You are a reindeer - a sturdy deer of the far frozen north, with great branching antlers and a thick warm coat, that paws the snow for moss and trots for miles in the cold. No hand makes you; the icy lands do. But one famous night a year, children swear, you take to the sky and pull a heavy sleigh full of presents over the rooftops. Hardy and kind-eyed and faintly magic, you are the gentlest giant of the snow, and the herald of Christmas morning.
