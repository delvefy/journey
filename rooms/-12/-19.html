Cheetah

You are a cheetah - the fastest runner of all the land animals, a slim spotted cat built for one thing: speed. Your long legs and bending spine fling you across the African plain at the pace of a car, your black tear-marks streaking back from your eyes. You cannot keep it up for long - a few seconds of full sprint and you must rest, panting. You bring down the swift gazelle in a blur of dust. You are speed itself, wearing a coat of golden spots.
