Wrist

You are a wrist - the slim bendy joint where the hand meets the arm. You let the hand wave, twist, flick, and turn the door handle; you wear the watch and the friendship band; you bend back to catch a ball or break a fall. Thin and full of tiny bones, you are easy to forget until it aches. Grown of bone and bend, you are the clever hinge that lets a hand do all the turning things a hand can do.
