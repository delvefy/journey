Palm

You are a palm - the flat soft middle of the hand. You cup to catch the rain or hold a ladybird; you press flat for a high-five; you carry a coin, a marble, a secret folded inside your fingers. A fortune-teller reads the lines that cross you. Warm and soft and faintly creased, grown of the living hand, you are the gentle inner side that does all the holding, the carrying, and the catching there is to do.
