Husky

You are a husky - a thick-furred, blue-eyed dog of the frozen north, bred to pull a sled for miles across the snow without tiring, then curl up nose-to-tail in a blizzard and sleep warm. No hand makes you; the cold lands and long breeding do. You howl like a wolf and bound like a puppy. A whole team of you flies over the ice as one. Hardy and fluffy and full of bounce, you are the friendliest engine of the snow, all wagging tail and willing muscle.
