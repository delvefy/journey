Hungry

You are hungry - the empty rumbling feeling when the tummy growls for food and you could, you swear, eat a whole horse. No one chooses you; a missed meal and a long morning do. You make tempers short and biscuits irresistible and dinner the best smell in the world. The cure is simple and delicious. Gnawing and growling and never quiet for long, you are the body's loudest, most honest demand: feed me, please, and feed me soon.
