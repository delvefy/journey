poodle

A poodle is a dog dressed up in curls. Its coat is soft and woolly and springs back when you press it, and groomers clip it into funny puffs and pompoms. Don't be fooled by the fancy haircut: a poodle is clever and quick, a swimmer and a bouncer, happy to chase a ball all afternoon. Warm, living, wholly a thing you can hug, it is perhaps the curliest and cleverest dog in the whole barking world.

North

A big white dog all covered in spots,
black dots from nose to tail;
in films I help the firemen's cart -
count my spots, you'll surely fail.

East

Five arms reach out from my round middle,
I cling to the rocks all day;
no head, no tail, I'm shaped like a star,
in the sea I slowly stay.

South

Long ears, soft fur, a twitchy nose,
I hop across the grass;
I live in a hole and nibble greens,
and bolt if dogs should pass.

West

A whale with one long twisted tusk,
like a unicorn of the sea;
I swim in waters cold as ice -
a spear-nosed mystery.
