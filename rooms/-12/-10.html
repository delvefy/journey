Hippo

You are the huge round beast of the African river, grey as wet stone, with a yawning mouth and great blunt teeth. No hand made you; the warm muddy water did. You wallow all day with just your eyes and ears above the surface, and graze the bank at night. Do not be fooled by your fatness - few beasts are crosser.

North

A big brown beast with heavy paws,
that growls and stands up tall;
it loves the honey, sleeps all winter -
the biggest beast of all.

East

A black-and-white chattering bird
that loves a shiny thing;
it grabs the ring, it hides the spoon -
one for sorrow, on the wing.

South

A big round bear of black and white,
that chews bamboo all day;
from China's misty mountain woods -
it munches hours away.

West

A great black ape that beats its chest,
the biggest of them all;
it knuckles through the jungle deep -
huge, and strong, and tall.
