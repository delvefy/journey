Itch

You are an itch - the small maddening tickle on the skin that begs to be scratched. A bug bite, a wool jumper, a healing scab - any can wake you, and the more you scratch the louder you cry out. You cannot be seen or held; you are a feeling, a tiny urge of need on the surface of the body. Even the word makes a hand twitch. You are the skin's small nag, the prickle that will not let a person sit still.
