Surprise

Something leaps out where you expected nothing, and your whole body answers before your mind can. A surprise is a small ambush of the heart - you cannot hold it, cannot plan it, cannot make one for yourself. It arrives unmade, the way weather arrives: a gasp, a jump, a sudden wide grin or a yelp. It is gone in a moment, but for that moment it owns you completely, and the day is never quite the same after.

North

A sudden electric jolt,
or news too sharp to bear;
it stops your heart a beat
and roots you to the chair.

East

The heavy, sinking weight
before a thing you fear;
it grows and grows the more
that something draws you near.

South

The grey and empty feeling
when there's nothing to be done;
the clock will hardly move
and the long day is no fun.

West

A frightening dream
that wakes you in the night;
you sit up in your bed,
your heart loud with fright.
