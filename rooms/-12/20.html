Sting

You are a sting - the sharp sudden hurt that comes out of nowhere. The nettle gives you to a bare leg; the bee leaves you behind with its barb; cold sea-water gives you to a fresh scrape. You are quick and bright, a hot prickle that makes the eyes water and the hand fly to the spot. You fade soon enough, but for a moment you are all anyone can feel. You are pain at its sharpest and smallest, a tiny fierce flash on the skin.
