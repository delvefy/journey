Sore

You are sore - the tender raw hurt of a part that has been overused or knocked or caught a cold, the throat that rasps, the knee that's grazed, the muscles after a long run. No one wants you; strain and scrapes and chills make you. You ache when pressed and twinge when moved and beg to be left alone. Not sharp like a sting, just steady and tender and tired-feeling, you are the body's way of saying go gently here, this part has had enough.
