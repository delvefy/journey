Warmth

You are gentle pleasant heat, not the burn of fire but the soft glow that wraps you cosy - the sun on your back, the fire across the room, the bed still warm from sleep, a hug. No hand makes you; the sun and the fire and the body give you off. You loosen the cold-stiff fingers and make the cat stretch and purr.

North

A low deep rolling growl of sound -
far thunder, a hungry tum,
a lorry on the cobbled road -
it goes on, rrr, like a drum.

East

A great fierce roaring fire of gold
that leaps up wild and high;
far bigger than a single flame -
the bonfire in the sky.

South

The sharp hot prick a nettle gives,
or a bee that's cross with you;
a quick fierce jab upon the skin -
red and smarting too.

West

The snap and pop of a burning log,
or dry leaves underfoot;
'crackle-snap' the bonfire spits -
a sound of burning wood.
