Numb

You are numb - when a hand sat on too long or a foot left to sleep stops feeling altogether, gone dead and woody and far away. No one chooses you; cold or a trapped nerve makes you, and you pinch the skin and feel nothing back. When at last you fade, the prickles rush in like fizz. You are the strange blank of a part switched off, a piece of the body that has, for now, stopped answering.
