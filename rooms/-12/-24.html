Dalmatian

You are a dalmatian - a sleek white dog speckled all over with neat black spots, no two ever quite the same. You are born pure white and the spots come later, one by one. You love to run and run, and once you trotted beside the great horse-drawn carriages; now you ride proud on the fire engine. Friendly and bouncy and unmistakably spotty, you are the dog everyone knows on sight, a hundred-and-one of you starring in the most famous spotty story of all.
