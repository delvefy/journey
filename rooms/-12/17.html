Prank

You are a prank - a playful little trick you spring on someone just to see their face. A whoopee cushion on the chair, a rubber spider in the lunchbox, a bucket balanced on the door. You mean no harm, only a startle and then a laugh shared all round. You cannot be touched, only sprung and remembered. You are mischief with a wink in it, the cheerful sort of trouble that ends with everybody grinning, even the one you caught.
