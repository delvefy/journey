Dove

You watch a white bird settle on the sill, soft-feathered and calm. A dove is a small living warmth you could cup in two hands - all hush and grey and gentle cooing. People send it out as a sign of peace, and it comes home again, faithful to the roost. Made by no hand, it is still the tamest of wild things: a creature you can touch, that does not flinch, that carries quiet wherever it flies.

North

A little green crawler
with many tiny feet;
I munch on leaves all day,
then wake with wings complete.

East

A small brown garden bird,
plain and quick and shy;
I hop among the hedges
and chirp as I flit by.

South

King of all the birds,
I soar on the highest air;
with talons sharp as knives
I seize the mountain hare.

West

A bird of bright colours
from a hot and far-off land;
I copy what you say
and perch upon your hand.
