Shock

You are the jolt that stops the breath - the bad news blurted out, the cold water dunked, the hand on the live wire. No hand makes you; the world springs it, sudden, and you stand frozen with wide eyes. A flash of the unexpected, too quick to soften. For one stretched second, nothing in you moves.

North

The colour of blood and the colour of fire,
of berries and roses too;
the traffic light that tells you STOP -
the warmest of the few.

East

A heavy grey and joyless dark,
the mood of a rainy day;
low spirits and a clouded room -
all light has gone away.

South

What jumps out when you least expect,
the gift you didn't know,
the friends who hid and shouted loud -
your mouth makes a round O.

West

When you spin fast or lose your specs,
the world goes soft and smeared;
the speeding car is just a streak,
its sharp edges disappeared.
