Desert

You are a vast dry land of sand and stone, where the sun beats down and the rain almost never comes. No hand makes you; the wind heaps your dunes and shifts them, slow as years. By day you bake; by night you freeze. The camel crosses you; the lizard suns on your hot rocks. Endless, empty, and shimmering with heat.

North

The sharp hot prick a nettle gives,
or a bee that's cross with you;
a quick fierce jab upon the skin -
it leaves you red and rueful too.

East

A thousand trees that crowd together,
dark green and tall and old;
deer hide in it, owls call in it -
its tales are often told.

South

A long sharp pointed tooth, curved down,
in the jaw of wolf or snake;
it sinks in deep, it grips, it bites -
a deadly point to make.

West

A hot wet forest, thick and green,
where vines and monkeys swing;
parrots screech and tigers prowl -
amid each loud wild thing.
