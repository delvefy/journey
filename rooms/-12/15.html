Riddle

You are a riddle - a question dressed up as a puzzle, that hides its answer behind clever words and waits to see if you are clever too. No hand can hold you; a sharp mind makes you, twisting a plain thing - a key, an egg, the wind - into a knot of clues. You cannot be touched, only solved. You are as old as talking, asked at the city gate and the fireside alike. You are a door made of words, and the answer is the only key.
