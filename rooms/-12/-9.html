Bear

You are the great shaggy beast of the forest, brown or black or white as snow, heavy-pawed and slow until you are not. No hand made you; the wild woods did. You love the honey, you fish the river, you sleep the whole long winter through in a cave. Stand up tall on your hind legs, and you tower over us all.

North

A flat-tailed swimmer of the stream
that gnaws the trees right down;
it builds a dam of sticks and mud -
a builder, wet and brown.

East

The great striped cat of the jungle, gold,
silent on huge soft paws;
it springs from the long grass to hunt -
with terrible jaws and claws.

South

A huge fat beast that wallows in mud,
with a great wide yawning mouth;
it lives in rivers, grey and round -
in hot lands to the south.

West

A desert beast with a humpy back,
that goes for days with no drink;
it carries folk across the sand -
the ship of dunes, you'd think.
