Penguin

You are the bird in a black-and-white suit that cannot fly but swims like a dart - belly down on the ice, then up and waddling, flippers out for balance. No hand made you; the frozen south did. You huddle in your thousands against the cold and dive the dark sea for fish. Stand tall, little waiter of the snow.

North

The proud farm bird with a tall red comb,
that crows the sun awake;
'cock-a-doodle-doo' at dawn -
the loudest call you'll make.

East

A slow sea-swimmer in a green shell,
with leathery flippers wide;
it hauls up the beach to lay its eggs -
then back to the rolling tide.

South

A big fat sea-beast, brown and slow,
with two long tusks of white;
it flops on ice and barks out loud -
with whiskers, quite a sight.

West

The tallest beast, with a neck so long
it eats the topmost leaves;
all patches brown on yellow coat -
on the plain, among the trees.
