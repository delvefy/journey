Clownfish

You are a clownfish - a small bright fish striped orange and white, that makes its home among the stinging tentacles of a sea anemone, which somehow never sting you. No hand makes you; the warm reef does. You bob and dart in and out of your wavy shelter, peeping at the divers. One of you got famously lost, and his dad swam the whole ocean to find him. Bright and bold and snug in your stinging home, you are the cheeriest little fish on the reef.
