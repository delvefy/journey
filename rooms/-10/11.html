Purple

You are the colour of kings and plums and violets, the rich deep dye that once cost a fortune. No hand made you alone; red and blue, mingled, do. Royal and rare and a little bit grand. Bruise the skin, and there I bloom; mix the paints just so, and there I am, the colour of crowns.

North

A muddled, swimming, half-there state,
when your head's gone soft and slow;
after a bump or a boring class -
you stare and barely know.

East

The face that looks back from the glass,
the world flipped in the pond;
it copies all you do, but cannot
be touched, near or beyond.

South

The colour of the wide bright sky,
of the deep and rolling sea;
cool and calm and far away -
as far as far can be.

West

The colour of carrots and of its own fruit,
of pumpkins and a fox;
mix red with yellow, here it comes -
bright as a sunset's locks.
