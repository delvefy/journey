Blank

You are blank - empty, with nothing on it at all. A blank page waiting for a word, a blank wall waiting for a picture, a blank screen, a blank stare. When your mind goes blank you cannot think of a single thing, however hard you try. You are not white or grey or any colour really - you are the absence of marks, the clean nothing before something is made. Fill me, and I am blank no more.
