Daze

You are the swimming, half-there fog of the mind - after the bump on the head, the boring lesson, the too-bright sun, the too-loud news. No hand makes you; a jolt or a dullness does. You stand there blinking, the world gone soft and slow and far. Snap out of me, they say - but I take my own sweet time.

North

An empty space with nothing on it,
the page with not a mark;
your mind when the answer won't come back -
all white, and blank, and stark.

East

The colour of the sun and rings,
of treasure, crowns, and corn;
the shiniest, most precious shade -
a yellow richly born.

South

The colour of plums and violets too,
of kings' robes long ago;
mix red with blue and here it comes -
a royal, deep-dyed glow.

West

A really bad and powerful smell,
of rotten eggs and drains;
it fills the room, you hold your nose -
till not a sniff remains.
