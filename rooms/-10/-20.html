Newt

You are a newt - the small smooth water-lizard that wriggles through the weedy pond, with a wavy crest along your back. You hatch from an egg as a tiny tadpole-like thing, grow your four legs, and learn to swim and crawl. You hunt the wriggling water-bugs and hide under stones by day. In a fairy-tale potion, an 'eye of newt' goes into the witch's brew. You are the pond's shy little dragon, half swimmer and half crawler.
