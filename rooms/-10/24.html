Bellow

You are a bellow - the great deep roar flung up from the chest, the noise an angry bull makes, or a giant, or a grown-up who has run quite out of patience. No squeak about you; lungs full of air and a wide-open mouth do it. You roll out low and loud and make the windows tremble and the room go quiet. Deep and thunderous and impossible to ignore, you are anger and bigness turned to sound, a roar that fills a whole valley.
