Yell

You are a yell - the loud full-throated shout that bursts out when you call across a field, cheer for your team, or warn of danger. You open the mouth wide and let the voice go at full force, far louder than a word. A coach yells from the touchline; a child yells with joy on the swing. You are sound at full volume, the voice flung as far as it will go, a shout that everyone for streets around can hear.
