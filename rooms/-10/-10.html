Rooster

You are the proud cockerel of the farmyard, red comb high, tail a fan of green-black, the loud alarm of the dawn. No hand made you; you crow the sun up and rule the hens. Strut and flap and cock-a-doodle-doo - before the people wake, the day is already yours.

North

A baby bear or lion or fox,
small, furry, and at play;
it tumbles with its brothers rough -
then naps the afternoon away.

East

A baby duck, all yellow fluff,
that paddles after its mum;
it grows up grey or white and proud -
from ugly, swans can come.

South

A black-and-white bird that cannot fly,
it waddles on the ice;
it dives for fish in freezing seas -
in a tuxedo, very nice.

West

A black-and-white chattering bird
that loves a shiny thing;
it steals the ring, it hides the spoon -
one for sorrow, on the wing.
