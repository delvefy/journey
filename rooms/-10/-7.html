Robin

You are a small brown bird with a bright red-orange breast, bold and friendly, hopping close while the gardener digs. No hand makes you; you nest in the hedge and sing your clear sweet song even in the dead of winter. You perch on the spade, on the Christmas card, on the snowy branch. Round, red-breasted, and tame.

North

A small quick reptile, scaled and dry,
that basks upon a wall;
it scurries off on four short legs
and sheds its tail in a fall.

East

A big black bird with glossy wings
that caws from the bare tree;
it steals the seed and mocks the scarecrow -
black as black can be.

South

A baby horse on long thin legs,
that stands the day it's born;
it nuzzles its mother in the field
and gallops by the morn.

West

A big white bird with an S-curved neck
that glides on the still lake;
graceful and proud, it hisses to guard -
the cygnets in its wake.
