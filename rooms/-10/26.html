whoop

A whoop is the wild cry that jumps out of you when something is too good to keep quiet about - 'WHOOP!' - hands in the air, up on your toes. It is pure joy turned into noise, louder than a laugh and shorter than a song. You whoop when you win, when the rollercoaster drops, when school is out for summer. Nobody teaches it to you; it just bursts out on its own, all glad and loud and free.

North

When your team scores the winning goal
the whole crowd jumps and shouts out loud;
a big glad yell to lift someone up -
hip-hip-hooray from all the crowd.

East

Chew your dinner, then send it down,
past your tongue and down your throat;
the big hard gulp that each bite takes -
it slides on down and out of sight.

South

A great loud shout across the yard
to call a friend who's far away;
open your mouth and bellow out -
'come over here!' is what you'll say.

West

Wet things do me in the sun,
dewdrops, snow, and tears that fall;
I shine and sparkle, slick and bright -
a shimmer on the morning, that's all.
