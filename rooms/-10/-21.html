Lizard

You are a lizard - the small scaly reptile that loves the warm sun. You bask flat on a hot rock, then dart away quick as a flicker when a shadow falls. Your skin is dry and patterned, your tail long; lose it to an enemy and you grow another. Your eyes swivel, your tongue tastes the air, and you cling to walls with clever toes. Cold-blooded, you borrow your heat from the day. You are a little dragon scaled down, sunbathing on a stone.
