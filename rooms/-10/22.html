Shout

You are a shout - a voice flung out at the top of its strength, to carry across a field or stop a friend at the kerb. No one builds you; the chest and the open mouth make you in a rush of air. You can be glad or angry, a warning or a welcome, but you are always loud and always meant to reach. You are the word that refuses to be quiet, hurled out where a whisper would be lost.
