Cricket

You are a cricket - a small brown jumping insect that fills the warm summer night with its song, made not with a voice but by rubbing its legs together. No hand made you; the long grass did, and you hide there all day and chirp all evening, chirrup chirrup, until the dawn. You can be heard everywhere and seen almost never, springing away the moment the grass is parted. Lucky, they say, to have one in the house. You are the little fiddler of the summer dark.
