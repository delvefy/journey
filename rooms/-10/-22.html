Snake

You are a snake - a long rope of muscle with no legs at all, made by no hand, sliding cool and quiet through the grass. You taste the air with a flickering tongue and pour yourself over stones and roots. Some of you carry a bite, some only a fright; all of you can vanish into a crack in a blink. You are a living ribbon, a hiss in the leaves, a thing that moves without a single foot.
