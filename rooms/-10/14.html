Wail

You are a wail - a long loud cry of sorrow that rises and falls and will not be hushed. You are the baby who wants its mother, the siren sweeping past, the wind crying round the house at night. You are grief turned into pure sound, climbing and falling, holding its note far longer than a word would dare. No hand can hold you, no door can shut you out - you slip under every gap, the saddest sound there is.
