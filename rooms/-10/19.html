Yelp

You are a yelp - the short sharp cry that jumps out before you can stop it, when a dog treads on a thorn or you stub a toe in the dark. You are not a word, just a quick bright bark of pain or surprise, here and gone. You cannot hold one or keep one; you only let one slip. You are sound made of shock, the body's tiny shout, over almost before it begins.
