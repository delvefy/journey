Cub

A clumsy bundle of paws and fur, all play and no sense of danger - a cub is a wild thing before the wild has hold of it. Bear or lion or fox, it tumbles and bites and chases its own tail, never far from a watchful mother. You would want to hold it, and you must not; the soft warmth in your hands now will be teeth and claws by next winter. For now it is only this: small, alive, and entirely unafraid.

North

A baby horse
on long and shaky legs;
it gallops by its mother
through the meadow as it begs.

East

A baby cow,
soft and brown and small;
it drinks its mother's milk
and grows up big and tall.

South

With a fine red comb
I rule the farmyard pen;
I crow to wake the sun
and strut among the hens.

West

Orange coat with black stripes,
a great cat fierce and grand;
I prowl the steamy jungle,
the strongest in the land.
