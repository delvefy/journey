Growl

You are a growl - the low rolling rumble that comes up from deep in a chest: a dog over its bone, a bear at the mouth of its cave, your own empty tummy at noon. No hand makes you; a throat does, half a sound and half a warning, the word for keep back said without any words. You cannot be touched, only felt in the floor and heard in the dark. You are the oldest way a creature says it is not to be troubled.
