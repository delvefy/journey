Seal

You are a seal - a sleek round-bodied sea creature with big dark eyes and stiff whiskers, at home in the cold grey waves. In the water you twist and dart like a torpedo; on land you flop and shuffle along on your belly, hauling out onto the rocks to bask in the sun and bark. Playful and whiskery and wonderfully clumsy ashore, you are the friendly face that pops up out of the sea to watch the boats go by, then slips back under without a sound.
