Glee

You are bright bubbling delight, too big to keep in - the whoop when the snow falls, the giggle that won't stop, the clapping dance of a child who's just been told the best news. No hand makes you; pure happiness boils up and spills over into laughter and a little jig. You can't sit still for you.

North

A tiny sharp flash of light that winks -
off gold, off glass, off a knife;
a quick bright spark in a treasure-chest,
or a sly look, full of life.

East

Not heavy rain, just fine wet mist
that drifts down soft and slow;
it dampens hair and greys the day -
too light for an umbrella, though.

South

Half rain, half snow, cold and wet,
it stings your cheeks and won't lie still;
it falls in winter, grey and mean -
slush sliding down the hill.

West

The bright warm gladness of something lovely -
a treat, a sweet surprise;
'oh, how lovely!' your whole face says,
pure pleasure in your eyes.
