Scream

You are a scream - the loudest, sharpest cry a person can make, torn out of the throat by terror or thrill or sheer delight. No hand makes you; sudden fear does, or a roller-coaster's plunge, or a spider on the wall. You cannot be touched, only heard - and it empties your lungs and makes everyone jump. A scream of fright, a scream of laughing, a scream for help. You are the body's loudest alarm, the sound that says something, right now, is huge.
