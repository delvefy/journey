Whine

You are a whine - the high thin complaining sound that goes on and on. The tired child does you for the toy in the shop; the dog does you at the closed door; the mosquito does you by your ear in the dark. You are not quite a cry and not quite a word, just a long drawn-out note of wanting that grates on everyone. "Stop that whining," they say, and you only climb higher. You are a small misery turned into a sound that will not quit.
