Firefly

You are a firefly - a small dark beetle that carries a cold green lamp in its tail. When the summer night falls you blink it on and off, drifting low over the grass, flashing a signal only another firefly can read. Children chase you with cupped hands and jars. You are natural, grown not made, a living spark that gives no heat. By day you are nothing special; by night you are a wandering star.
