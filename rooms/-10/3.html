Buzz

You are the low rough drone of a thing in flight or in a hurry - the bee at the flower, the fly at the glass, the phone shivering on the table. No hand makes you; it is the blur of tiny wings or a small motor's tremble that gives you your hum. You warn of the wasp before it is seen; you wake the sleeper. A restless, vibrating, never-quite-still sound, you are the noise of small busy life, a thrum in the warm air that means: something here is moving fast.

North

A great glad bubbling happiness
that fills you to the brim;
it bursts out in a laugh or song -
the opposite of grim.

East

The sound that leaves you when you speak,
or rises when you sing;
a cold can steal it for a week -
it's heard, not seen, this thing.

South

A leaping golden tongue of fire
upon the candle's tip;
it warms, it lights, but can't be held,
and dies if fuel should slip.

West

The huge loud sound a lion makes,
or the sea in a storm,
or a crowd that's cheering wild -
a deep and thundering form.
