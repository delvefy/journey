Toad

You are a toad - the warty brown squatter of the damp garden, fatter and drier and slower than your cousin the frog. You hide all day under a cool stone and come out at dusk to snap up slugs and beetles with a flick of your tongue. Your skin is bumpy and your eyes are gold, and a fairy tale swears a kiss will turn you to a prince. You do not leap so much as lumber. You are the gardener's quiet friend, ugly and useful, lurking in the cool dark with a stomach full of pests.
