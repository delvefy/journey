Eel

You are an eel - a long slippery fish shaped like a snake, that winds through the river mud and the dark sea deeps and cannot be gripped in a bare hand. No hand makes you; the cold water does. You hide in cracks by day and slide out to hunt by night, and some of you can even wriggle overland through wet grass. You travel a thousand miles to be born. Slithery and secret and strong, you are the river's living rope, all muscle and slime and no shoulders at all.
