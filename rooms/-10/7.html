Sleet

You are half rain and half snow, a cold wet mush that falls in winter and stings the face. No hand makes you; the freezing cloud lets you down, too cold to be rain, too warm to be proper snow. You don't settle pretty like snow; you turn to grey slush on the road. Cold, wet, and mean.

North

A bright wild bubbling happiness,
all giggles, claps, and cheers;
you can't sit still, you dance about -
pure joy from ear to ears.

East

The hot-faced feeling of being caught
in something that you should not;
you hang your head, you wish to hide -
it burns you on the spot.

South

A sudden scare that makes you jump,
when something leaps out: 'BOO!';
your heart bangs, your breath catches in -
it shoots right through you.

West

The dim grey half-dark of a cloudy day,
or the low sad heaviness of mind;
no sun, no cheer, just shadowy grey -
a dull and dreary kind.
