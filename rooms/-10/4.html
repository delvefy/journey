Joy

You are the bright leap of the heart, the happiness too big to keep inside, that bursts out in a laugh or a shout or a dance. No hand makes you; you well up at good news, at a friend's return, at the first snow, at a goal scored in the last minute. You make the eyes shine and the feet light. Bigger than a smile, deeper than fun, you are gladness brimming over, the soul's own sunshine, the pure delight of a moment going exactly right.

North

Not soon, not then, but this exact
and present beat of time;
the very instant that you breathe
while reading this line's rhyme.

East

A flicker of time, the briefest bit,
gone quicker than a blink;
it's here, it's past, it can't be held -
less time than it takes to think.

South

The droning hum of a busy bee,
or a fly trapped on the glass;
the phone that trembles on the desk -
a sound that won't quite pass.

West

The open-mouthed amazement felt
at something grand and new;
the stars, a magic trick, a whale -
it fills you through and through.
