Bug

You are a bug - the catch-all name for any tiny crawly creature a child turns over a stone to find. You have little legs that scuttle, maybe wings, maybe a shiny back; you hide in the damp dark under the log and freeze when the light hits you. Beetle, woodlouse, earwig, all of you are bugs. Grown by no hand, small enough to sit on a fingertip, you are the whole busy hidden world that lives down low, beneath the notice of the big.
