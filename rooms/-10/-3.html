Seal

You are the sleek grey swimmer of the cold sea, slipping through the waves like silk, then hauling out clumsy and heavy onto the rock. No hand made you - you were pupped on a beach in your white coat and learned the water before the land. You bark and clap and balance, whiskered and dark-eyed, diving deep for fish and surfacing to watch the shore. Graceful in the sea, comic on the stones, you are the ocean's friendly wet-furred neighbour, half at home in either world.

North

A small swift run of water, clear,
that tumbles down the hill;
it chatters over rocks and roots
and never once is still.

East

The hugest beast in all the world,
it swims the deep blue sea;
it breathes the air, it spouts a jet,
and sings so mournfully.

South

It sleeps all day hung upside down
in caves or hollow trees;
at dusk it flits on leathery wings
and hunts the moths with ease.

West

A great grey hunter of the sea
with rows of jagged teeth;
its fin slices through the waves
and terror swims beneath.
