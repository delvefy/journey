Holler

You are a holler - a big loud shout flung out across a distance to reach someone far away. You holler across the field for your friend to come, holler up the stairs that tea is ready, holler from the top of the hill just to hear the echo come back. Hands cupped round the mouth, lungs full, out it goes. Loud and friendly and carrying, you are the voice stretched as far as it will reach, the long shout that bridges a gap no whisper ever could.
