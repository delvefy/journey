Fright

You are the sudden jolt of being scared - the BOO from behind the door, the bang in the night, the spider dropped down the back. No hand makes you; a shock springs you all at once. Your heart leaps, your breath catches, your hair stands on end - and then it's over, and you laugh. Quick and sharp and gone in a gasp.

North

Half rain, half snow, cold and wet,
it stings your cheeks and won't lie still;
it falls in winter, grey and mean -
slush sliding down the hill.

East

The hot red boil when things go wrong,
fists clenched, the brow drawn tight;
it wants to shout, it wants to stamp -
a temper spoiling for a fight.

South

Not a moment past, not one to come,
but this exact tick of the clock;
the only time that ever is -
gone before you talk.

West

The slow cold fear of something bad
you know is on its way -
the test, the dentist, the dark night -
it sours the whole long day.
