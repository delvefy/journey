Hoof

You are the hard horned foot of the running beast - the horse, the cow, the deer - a single tough toe grown thick as a shoe. No hand made you; the animal grew you to carry its whole weight at a gallop over stone and turf. You strike sparks on the road, you drum the ground until it shakes, and the smith nails iron to your edge to spare your wear. Hard, blunt, and tireless, you are the wild's own boot, worn from the day it is born.

North

A giant heap of rock and snow
that towers to the sky;
the climbers rope up to its peak,
so steep and cold and high.

East

Behind the beast it swings and waves,
a flick to shoo the flies;
the dog wags his when he is glad,
the fox's is his prize.

South

A little run of hurrying water
down the hillside's fold;
smaller than a river yet,
it chatters, clear and cold.

West

The long thick hair upon the neck
of horse or lion grand;
it streams behind them as they run
and gallop cross the land.
