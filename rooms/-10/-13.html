Buffalo

You are a buffalo - a huge shaggy ox of the wide grass plains, with a great humped shoulder and curved horns on a heavy head. You move in vast herds that darken the land and shake the ground when they run. Slow-eyed and patient, you graze the long grass all day, swishing the flies with your tail. Mighty and woolly and gentle until pushed, you are one of the great grazers of the open world.

North

A great fat seal with two long tusks,
it lazes on the ice;
with bristly whiskers on its face,
it bellows once or twice.

East

It hops about on two big feet
and keeps its young in a pouch;
in the hot dry land of Australia,
it bounds with never a slouch.

South

Flat and green, I grow on trees
and drink the summer sun;
in autumn I turn gold and brown -
I fall when summer's done.

West

A big black-and-white furry bear
that munches bamboo all day;
with black patched eyes, so sleepy-looking,
in China far away.
