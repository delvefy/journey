Screech

You are a screech - the sharp high scraping sound that sets the teeth on edge, the car braking hard, the owl across the dark, the chalk dragged wrong on the board. No gentle thing makes you; metal on metal or a throat at full pitch does. You cut straight through every other noise and make everyone wince and cover their ears. Piercing and harsh and impossible to sleep through, you are sound turned into a blade, scraping itself across the quiet.
