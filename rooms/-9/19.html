Sob

You are a sob - the deep, broken cry that shakes the whole chest when the sadness is too big to hold. You come in gulps and hiccups, half a breath swallowed and half let go. A child does you into a shoulder; a grown-up muffles you behind a hand. You cannot be touched, only heard and felt. You are the sound grief makes when it spills right over, the cry that rises from somewhere far deeper down than ordinary tears.

North

A quick sharp pull of air up the nose,
when it runs or you have a cold;
you do it to catch a lovely smell -
a ___, so I am told.

East

The rough harsh shout a startled bird gives,
a parrot or a hen;
loud and ugly, all at once -
a ___, again and again.

South

Tears come running down your cheeks
when you are hurt or sad;
a baby does it loud all night -
a ___, when things go bad.

West

The short sharp bark of a hurt little dog
when its tail gets trodden on;
you let one out at a sudden fright -
a ___, and then it's gone.
