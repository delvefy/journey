Munch

You are a munch - the steady crunchy beat of jaws at work on something crisp, an apple, a carrot, a fistful of crisps. No one teaches you; a good crunchy snack and a hungry mouth do. You go round and round, loud and rhythmic and pleased with yourself, the happiest noise a packet of crisps can make. Not the tiny nibble of a mouse, not the great chomp of a croc, just the honest, contented, ongoing grind of a child getting through a treat.
