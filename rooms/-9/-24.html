Scorpion

You are a scorpion - a small armoured creature of the hot dry places, with two pincing claws out front and a long tail curled up over your back, tipped with a sting. No hand makes you; the desert night does. You scuttle out under the moon to hunt, glowing faintly under a strange light. People shake out their boots in case you are hiding inside. Armoured and pincered and venom-tailed, you are the desert's most feared little hunter, dangerous from both ends.
