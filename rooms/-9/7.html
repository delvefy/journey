Shame

You are the hot, sinking feeling of having done wrong and been seen - the lie found out, the broken cup, the unkind word that cannot be unsaid. No hand makes you; the conscience raises you, burning the face red and bowing the head. You wish the ground would swallow you. Painful and useful both, you teach the heart to do better.

North

Not heavy rain, just fine wet mist
that drifts down soft and slow;
it dampens hair and greys the day -
too light for an umbrella, though.

East

The cold tight grip of being afraid -
of the dark, the dog, the deep;
it makes your knees go weak and shaky
and steals away your sleep.

South

The hot red feeling when things are wrong,
that clenches up your fists;
it makes you shout, it makes you stamp -
the temper no one missed.

West

Half rain, half snow, it stings your face,
cold slush that will not stick;
it falls in winter, grey and wet -
not soft, but sharp and quick.
