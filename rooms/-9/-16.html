Tail

You are a tail - the wagging, swishing, curling part at the back end of an animal. You wag like mad when the dog is glad, you swish the flies off the horse, you curl proud and bushy on the fox and the squirrel, you balance the cat along the fence top. A monkey hangs by you; a peacock fans you wide. Grown by no hand, full of feeling, you are the back-end flag an animal flies to show just how it feels.
