Centipede

You are a centipede - the long flat crawler with more legs than anyone has time to count, all rippling along in waves beneath the stone. You like the dark and the damp, under flowerpots and along the cellar wall, and you move with a quick dry shimmer that makes people jump. A hundred legs, they say, though nobody has ever stopped you to be sure. Fast, low, and many-footed - that is you, gone before the foot comes down.
