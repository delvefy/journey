Haze

You are a thin pale veil in the air that softens and dims the far view - the shimmer over the hot road, the smoky blue of distant hills, the dusty light of a summer afternoon. No hand makes you; heat and dust and far distance raise you. You blur the edges of the world. Not thick like fog, just a soft dim film.

North

When sun off snow is too bright to bear,
or headlamps glare at night;
you screw your eyes, you cannot look -
it blinds you with its light.

East

A sudden flood of total fear,
when all goes wrong at once;
your heart slams, your thoughts fly wild -
you freeze, or run, or bounce.

South

Not heavy rain, just fine wet mist
that drifts down soft and slow;
it dampens hair and greys the day -
too light for an umbrella, though.

West

A tiny sharp flash of light that winks -
off gold, off glass, off a knife;
a quick bright spark in a treasure-chest,
or a sly look, full of life.
