Chuckle

You are a chuckle - the soft low laugh that bubbles up in the chest when something is gently funny: a private joke, a clever line, a memory that catches you. No hand makes you; amusement does, quieter than a giggle and warmer than a grin. You cannot be touched, only heard, a low ha-ha-ha behind a hand. You are the laugh of someone comfortable and content, not bursting out but rolling along - the cosy, grown-up cousin of the giggle.
