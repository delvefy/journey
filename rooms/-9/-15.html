Bone

You are a bone - the hard pale frame inside, hidden under skin and meat. You hold the body up, you let it bend and stand and run; you guard the soft warm parts within your cage. A dog dreams of you, buries you, gnaws you for hours. White as chalk and strong as wood, grown by no hand inside the living thing, you are the quiet scaffold that keeps everything from simply folding to the floor.
