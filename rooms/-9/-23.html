Spider

You are a spider - eight thin legs and a clever body, no hand's making, that spins a wheel of sticky silk between the fence posts and waits in the middle, patient as stone. A fly blunders in and you feel the thread twitch and come running. Some fear you, some welcome you for the flies you catch. You can drop from the ceiling on a single invisible line. A quiet eight-legged hunter, you fill the corners of the shed with your fine and deadly lace.
