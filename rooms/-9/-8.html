Puppy

You are a baby dog, all soft fur, big paws, and floppy ears too big for your head. No hand makes you; the mother dog gives a litter of you, blind at first, then tumbling and biting and chewing everything in reach. You yap, you wag, you wet the floor, you sleep in a heap. Clumsy and warm and full of mischief.

North

A big black bird with glossy wings
that caws from the bare tree;
it steals the seed and mocks the scarecrow -
black as black can be.

East

The big male sheep with great curled horns
that charges, head bent low;
it leads the flock and butts the gate -
hard-headed, white as snow.

South

A baby cow on wobbly legs
that suckles at its mother;
soft-eyed and gentle in the field -
it follows close, no other.

West

A baby horse on long thin legs,
that stands the day it's born;
it nuzzles its mother in the field
and gallops by the morn.
