Calf

You are a baby cow, knock-kneed and wobbly, who stands within an hour of being born and drinks the warm milk from your mother. No hand makes you; the cow gives you in the spring field. Soft-eyed, big-eared, with a coat like brushed velvet, you butt at the udder, you skip when the sun is out, you press close to the herd.

North

A baby dog with floppy ears,
all tumble, chew, and play;
it yaps and wags its little tail
and grows up day by day.

East

A woolly beast that says 'baa-baa'
and grazes on the hill;
the farmer shears its coat for wool -
it follows the flock at will.

South

A fluffy yellow baby bird
that paddles after its mum;
it cheeps and waddles down to the pond -
an ugly one turned handsome.

West

A baby lion, bear, or fox,
all paws and play and fluff;
it tumbles with its brothers and sisters -
soft now, but soon grown tough.
