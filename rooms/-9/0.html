Valley

You are the low green cradle between the hills, the long fold of land where the river runs and the soil lies deep and rich. Sheltered from the worst of the wind, you hold the farms and the villages, the orchards and the winding lane. The mist pools in you of a morning; the sound carries far across you in the still of evening. Where the mountains are harsh and high, you are soft and kind and full of life - the gentle ground where, given the choice, most living things would rather stay.

North

The sense that lives behind the eye,
that drinks the colours in;
the green, the red, the wide blue sky -
without me, all is dim.

East

A sheer high wall where the land falls down,
straight to the sea below;
the gulls all nest on its rocky crown,
where no foot dares to go.

South

At the end of dog or cat I grow,
I wag to show delight;
the fish, the cow, the horse also
all swish me left and right.

West

The highest land of all there is,
with a snowy peak so steep;
the climbers test their strength on this,
and the valleys lie below, deep.
