Drizzle

You are the lightest of rains - a fine wet mist that drifts down soft and slow, too thin to bother with an umbrella, just enough to dampen the hair and grey the afternoon. No hand makes you; the low grey cloud lets you sift down all day long. Not a downpour, never a storm, just the soft persistent damp.

North

A thin pale blur that hangs in the air
on a hot still summer day;
far hills go soft and dim behind it -
it smudges the view away.

East

The gnawing fret of what might go wrong,
that chews one thought all night;
it knots the tummy, steals your sleep -
borrowed trouble, out of sight.

South

The hot-faced feeling of being caught
in something that you should not;
you hang your head, you wish to hide -
it burns you on the spot.

West

A bright wild bubbling happiness,
all giggles, claps, and cheers;
you can't sit still, you dance about -
pure joy from ear to ears.
