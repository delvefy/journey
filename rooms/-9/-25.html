Tarantula

You are a tarantula - the biggest, hairiest spider of all, with eight thick furry legs and a fat round body. You lurk in a burrow or under a log in warm faraway lands, creeping out at night to pounce on beetles and bugs. People shiver at the sight of you, though you would rather hide than bite. Slow and shaggy and far bigger than any spider should be, you are the king of the spiders, the one that fills a whole hand and stars in every scary story.
