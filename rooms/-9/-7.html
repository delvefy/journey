Crow

You are a big glossy-black bird with a harsh loud caw, clever as any beast that flies. No hand makes you; you flock in the bare trees and strut across the ploughed fields, stealing seed and shining things. The scarecrow is set up just for you, and still you mock it. Black from beak to tail, sharp-eyed and bold.

North

A long rope of scales with no legs at all,
it slides upon the ground;
it flicks its forked tongue, it hisses low -
it sheds its skin all round.

East

A big white bird with a long stretched neck
that honks and hisses loud;
it flies in a V across the sky -
a bold and nipping crowd.

South

A baby dog with floppy ears,
all tumble, chew, and play;
it yaps and wags its little tail
and grows up day by day.

West

A little brown bird with a red-orange breast
that hops upon the snow;
it sings in winter, bold and sweet,
on every card you know.
