Blush

You are a blush - the sudden warm pink that floods the cheeks when a person feels shy or caught out or secretly pleased. You climb up the neck and light the face without a word of permission, telling everyone exactly what was meant to stay hidden. No one can hold you back or hurry you away; you simply rise, glow, and slowly fade. You are a feeling that paints itself, for one helpless moment, right across the face.
