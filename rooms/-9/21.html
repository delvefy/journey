Gulp

You are a gulp - the big loud swallow that goes down the throat all at once. You come when you are very thirsty and drink half the glass in one go, and you come when you are scared and your throat bobs and the word will not quite come out. In a cartoon you have your own bubble of letters. Whether it is water or worry going down, you are the throat doing its quick brave work.
