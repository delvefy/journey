Copper

You are copper - the warm reddish-brown metal, and the colour of it too. You are old pennies and shining pipes and the green-gone roof of an ancient dome. You ring softly when struck, you bend without breaking, you carry the spark along a wire into every lamp and plug. Polished, you glow like a small sunset; left out in the rain, you slowly turn a dusty green. A warm, soft, useful shine.
