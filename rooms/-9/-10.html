Duckling

A puff of yellow down on two paddling feet - a duckling is spring made small and soft. It tumbles after its mother in a wobbling line, peeping all the way, and takes to the pond as if it always knew how. You could hold it in one cupped hand and feel its tiny heart race. Made by no hand but the warm dark of the egg, it is all new to the world, and the world is all puddles and reeds and the next splash.

North

A baby cow
on wobbly new legs;
it stays by its mother
and for warm milk it begs.

East

A big strong beast
with horns and an angry snort;
wave a red cloth
and he'll charge for sport.

South

I carry my house
on my back as I roam;
I pull in my head and legs
and hide inside my dome.

West

At the break of dawn
I crow upon the farm;
red comb on my head,
I sound the morning alarm.
