Voice

You are the sound a person makes to speak or sing, carried out of the throat on a column of breath. No hand shapes you; you are made of air set trembling, and yet you carry everything - the lullaby, the order, the joke, the name called across the yard. You can be raised in anger or dropped to a whisper, cracked with weeping or warm with love. Lose me to a cold, they say, and a person falls silent. You are the self made audible, breath turned into meaning.

North

A tiny scrap of time, so brief
it's gone before you blink;
just a moment, people say -
less time than it takes to think.

East

I speak too soft for the room to hear,
close up to a single ear;
a secret breath without a tone,
so none but you come near.

South

A tiny leaping bit of fire
struck off the flint or wire;
just one of me in the dry grass
can start a raging fire.

West

The low and humming sound a bee
will make beside your ear,
or a phone upon the table top -
a small electric blur.
