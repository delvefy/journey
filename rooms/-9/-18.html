Hoof

You are a hoof - the hard horny foot of a horse, a cow, a deer, that clatters and clops on the road. No hand made you; the animal grew you, a single tough toenail grown round into a foot, sturdy enough to gallop on stones. You can be heard before the horse is seen, shod with an iron shoe by the blacksmith, picked clean of stones. Four to a horse, cloven in two on a goat. You are the animal's own sturdy boot, worn day and night and never taken off.
