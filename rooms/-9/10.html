Dazzle

Too much light all at once, and the world goes white - to dazzle is to blind with brightness. Sun off snow, headlights on a dark road, the flash that leaves a ghost swimming in your eyes. It can mean wonder too: a show so bright and grand it leaves you speechless and staring. Either way you cannot hold it or look straight at it. It overwhelms, it floods, and for one stunned moment you can see nothing else at all.

North

Look in still water
or a glass so bright;
I copy your every move
in the mirror's light.

East

The soft dry whisper
of leaves in the breeze,
or paper being crumpled,
or wind through the trees.

South

A shimmer of heat
on a long hot road;
the air goes wavy
where the sun has glowed.

West

The colour of sadness,
of the sky and the sea;
a clear bright shade
on a robin's egg, maybe.
