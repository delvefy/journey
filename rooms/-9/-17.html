Mane

You are a mane - the long flowing hair that grows down the neck of a horse or a lion. No hand made you; the beast grew you, and you stream out behind as it gallops, or ruff up thick and golden round the lion's proud face. You can be brushed, plaited, gripped by a rider, tossed in the wind. You are half the splendour of the animal, the wild banner of the neck, flying out as it runs.
