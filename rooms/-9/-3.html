Whale

You are the largest creature ever to live, a mountain of muscle moving through the cold deep sea. No hand made you - you were born in the open ocean and you breathe the air like us, rising to blow a tall white spout before you sound again. You are no fish, though you swim like one; you sing across a hundred miles of water and eat the smallest things to grow the biggest of all. Vast, gentle, and warm-blooded, you are the sea's great living hill.

North

Water turned to slippery glass
when winter grips the pond;
it cracks and creaks beneath your feet
and melts when spring has dawned.

East

I scuttle sideways on the sand
with claws held up to nip;
a hard round shell upon my back,
I live beside the ship.

South

Like a rabbit but longer-legged and wild,
I box in fields in spring;
I race the wind across the down
and never tame a thing.

West

A sleek grey swimmer of the sea,
I bask upon the rock;
I bark and clap my flippers wet
and dive among the flock.
