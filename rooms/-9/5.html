Today

You are the day you are living in, this very one, neither the gone-by nor the not-yet. No hand can hold you - the sun comes up and you begin, the sun goes down and you slide into yesterday. You are where everything actually happens: the breakfast eaten, the lesson learned, the friend met. Seize me, the old ones say. You are the only day anyone ever truly has, the present made into a full bright span from one waking to the next sleep.

North

The hot and boiling feeling of rage
when things have gone unfair;
it reddens cheeks and clenches fists
and stamps about the chair.

East

About thirty days, one page of the year,
from the first day to the last;
twelve of them fill a whole year up -
by the moon they once were cast.

South

The briefest flick of time there is,
shorter than a song;
here, then gone, already past -
it never lingers long.

West

Not then, not soon, but this exact
and present point of time;
the instant that is happening
as you read this very line.
