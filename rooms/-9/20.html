Sniff

You are a sniff - the quick short draw of air up the nose, to catch a smell or stop a drip. The dog gives you on every lamp-post and blade of grass, reading the world by its scents. You sniff the flowers, the cooking, the new book's pages; you sniff back the tears before you cry. A cold makes you do it again and again. You are the nose at work, a tiny inward gust that pulls the smell of the world right in.
