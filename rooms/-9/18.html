Cry

You are a cry - the sound and the tears of someone weeping, when sadness or pain or even joy spills over and out. No hand makes you; a full heart does, and the eyes well up and the breath comes in gulps. You cannot be touched, only heard and comforted. A baby cries to be fed; a hurt knee draws a cry; sometimes you cry and cannot even say why. You are grief and relief let out together, the way a heart empties itself.
