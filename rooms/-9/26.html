Swallow

You are a swallow - the gulp that sends a mouthful down the throat and away to the tummy. Chew it up, then down it goes with a little bob of the throat. You swallow your food, swallow your medicine in one brave gulp, swallow hard when you're nervous. A whole drink goes down you swallow by swallow. Quiet and ordinary and done a thousand times a day, you are the last step of every mouthful, the little squeeze that carries it safely down inside.
