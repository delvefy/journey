Meerkat

You are a meerkat - a small, slim digger of the dry African plains. You stand bolt upright on your back legs, paws folded, dark eyes scanning the sky for hawks while your family forages below. You live in great burrows full of tunnels and cousins. When the sun comes up you turn your tummy to it to get warm. Always alert, always together - one of you is always on watch.

North

I bound across the outback wide
on two great springy feet;
my baby rides inside my pouch -
in Australia we meet.

East

A great grey beast as big as a truck,
with a horn upon my snout;
I charge and snort across the plain -
you'd better watch out.

South

The dog digs a hole to bury me,
all hard and white and dry;
inside of you there's a framework -
a ___ that won't say why.

West

Flat and green upon the bough,
I drink the summer light;
I turn to gold and drift away
when autumn comes in sight.
