Gasp

You are a gasp - the sharp quick breath sucked in when something startles or amazes. The magic trick works, the diver leaps, the bad news lands, and the whole room pulls you in at once. No hand makes you; the surprised body does, snatching air before the mind catches up. You are wonder and shock with the lungs behind them, the sound of a held breath. You come before the cheer or the scream, the world's first answer to the unexpected.
