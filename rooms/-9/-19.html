Feather

You are a feather - the light soft plume that grows on a bird and lets it fly. You drift down slow and sideways when one comes loose, too gentle to fall straight. People tickle a chin with you, write with you dipped in ink, and stuff whole pillows full of you. You are natural, grown not made, lighter than almost anything there is. You are the proof a bird wears the sky on its back, the softest, slowest-falling little thing the wind likes best to carry.
