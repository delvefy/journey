Moment

You are the smallest sliver of time anyone really feels - shorter than a minute, barely longer than a blink. No hand can hold you; you are here and then behind you, already memory. Wait a moment, we say, or in a moment, meaning almost no time at all. Yet some of you are kept forever: the moment a child is born, the moment a race is won. You are time at its briefest, the now that is always just slipping past.

North

Not gone like yesterday, not far
like the day that's yet to dawn;
the very day you stand in now -
the day you woke upon.

East

The third of the four seasons,
when leaves go red and brown;
the harvest's in, the geese fly south,
and chill creeps into town.

South

The sound you make to speak or sing,
borne out upon your breath;
hoarse with a cold, or raised in song,
heard, though it has no flesh.

West

A great glad gladness in the heart,
a bursting happy glow;
it makes you laugh and skip and sing -
the opposite of woe.
