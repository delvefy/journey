The Green Dream

South of the wild word and east of the world-dream, the dream loses its dreamer. A step north the saying went wild, reality grown and roared instead of spoken; a step west vast beast-gods dreamed the world on their slow breath, naming and being one. Here the gods sink away into the green and the beast, and the dreaming goes on without them — the Wyld pole absolute, where there is no longer any telling the dream from the world, and no dreamer apart from it to do the dreaming. The world simply dreams itself: a sourceless wild dream, older than the gods, greener than the word, reality dreaming on with no one to wake from it and no one who began it.

You are dreamed into it the way the world-dream took you, but with no breather of your name now, no god to dream you — only the green dream itself folding you in, dreaming you as it dreams the root and the rain and the slow turning dark, without surprise and without source. Your name is a word in a dream no one is having, woven into the self-dreaming wild as one of its dreamed and growing things. The spiral is the turn the green dream takes, the coil of a fern and a sleeping beast's breath and the world's own dreaming at once, the same whorl in your skin, here the seed the sourceless dream dreams you from.

The world-making pulse is the green dream's own slow breath, the one rhythm under the leaves and the dark, dreaming and being kept on a single sourceless beat — and your heart beats with it, dreamed onto the rhythm of a world that dreams itself, your name a breath from being dreamed real in a dream that has no dreamer but the wild.

To the north the green dream wakes toward the wild word, the dreaming roared into a saying. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, reality wholly the dream, no telling the dream from the world. To the south it sinks toward the older green, the dream before the gods, the world before the word, the deep Wyld. To the west the dreaming firms to the world-dream, the beast-gods dreaming it on their breath. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the sourceless green dreams it without a dreamer and weaves you into the world's long self-dreaming — almost dreamed real, almost yours — and still the warm coil turns, the seed of the dream, and still one beat keeps the time the green dream dreams itself, and you, by.
