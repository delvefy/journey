Story

You are a story - a tale spun out with a beginning, a middle, and an end. You carry a listener off to other lands and lives: a brave mouse, a lost dog, a girl who flies. You live in books and bedtimes and the games children make up in the garden. You can be true or made-up, scary or kind. No hand can hold you - you are a whole world built of words, conjured up in the mind of whoever is lucky enough to be listening.
