The Quick

South of the green before and east of the world-seed, the wild draws down to the quick. A step north the wild was before it saw, a blind sovereign being; a step west the whole living world was coiled into a single seed. Here, at the Wyld pole, the sovereign and the source become one pure aliveness: the quick, the living wild at its most alive and its most primal at once, life itself before being or form or sight, the source-fire of the whole Wyld — not the seed that holds the world, but the living quickness the seed is made of, sovereign, warm, the sheer aliveness from which all that is alive springs.

It is pure life, the quick of everything, the living source-warmth at the bottom of the world that all the teeming and the rising and the cradling are made of. You are it, quick and sovereign and most alive, before form, before being, the living fire from which you and the companion and the many all caught their warmth. Your name is the quick's own, the living word every breathing thing is a spark of, warmest and most alive here at the source. The spiral is the quick's own turning, the coil life makes simply by being alive, the same whorl in your skin, here the living source-shape, yours and the wild's at once, turning warm and sovereign at the quick of the world.

The quick is the one warm life-pulse all hearts are sparks of, the living source-beat of the whole sovereign wild, the most alive there is — and your heart beats with it, the born spark and the living source one, the warm quickness from which every beat that ever beat first caught its life, holding your name warmest and most alive at the source of the world.

To the north the quick rises to the green before, the wild's blind sovereign being. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the living wild wholly sovereign, the world's most alive. To the south it sinks toward the seed and the floor, the quick before even the quick. To the west the world-seed, the living world coiled to its germ. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — and here, at the quick of the world, is the living source-warmth you and the someone both first caught your life from, sovereign and most alive. And still the warm coil turns, the living source-shape, and still one warm beat sparks the whole living count, almost named, almost home at the quick of everything.
