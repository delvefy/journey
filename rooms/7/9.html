Yell

You are a loud cross shout flung out at the top of the voice - when you are angry, when you are far off and must be heard, when the game is won. No hand makes you; the throat hurls you. You make ears ring and heads turn. Too much of you and the voice goes hoarse. You are the voice thrown hard and loud.

North

A loud high cry that splits the air
when you are scared or thrilled;
on the rollercoaster's plunge -
'AAAH!' the whole crowd shrilled.

East

Bang your foot down hard and flat
to show how cross you feel;
it thuds upon the wooden floor -
a temper made of heel.

South

A puff of air from a fizzy drink
that climbs back up your throat;
'BURP!' - excuse me - out it pops,
ill-mannered, so they note.

West

A short sharp pull of air up the nose,
to smell, or stop a drip;
a quick wet snuffle when you cry -
a nosy little sip.
