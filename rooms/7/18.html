Dream

You are a dream - the strange film your sleeping mind makes for you in the dark: you fly, you fall, you meet people long gone, and none of it seems odd until you wake. No hand makes you, exactly, though your own mind weaves you out of the day's leftovers. You cannot be held, only half-remembered, slipping away the moment you reach for it. A dream is also a wish for what may come - what you long to be. You are the mind let loose to play.
