Question

You are a question - the thing you ask when you want to know something, your voice rising up at the end and a curly mark behind you on the page. Why is the sky blue? What's for tea? Are we nearly there yet? Out you come, all day long, especially from curious children who never run out of you. Eager and open and always hoping for an answer, you are the very start of learning anything at all, the little hook that pulls a brand new fact out into the light.
