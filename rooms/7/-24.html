Cushion

You are a cushion - a soft fat pad of stuffing in a cloth case, plumped on the sofa and the armchair to make the sitting kind. A hand thumps you into shape and a head sinks into you. You get borrowed for forts and snowball fights and falling onto from the stairs. You smell faintly of the room. Squashy and patient and always somehow on the floor, you are comfort you can pick up and throw, the friendliest thing in the living room.
