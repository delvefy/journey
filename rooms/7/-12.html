Wallet

You are a small folding case for money, leather or cloth, kept close in a pocket. Coins, notes, and cards all tucked in your slots; you snap or fold shut to keep them safe. Made by careful hands, you are wealth one can hold. Open you up and the spending begins; close you, and the keeping goes on.

North

You carry your things about in me,
with handles you can hold;
the shopping, the school books, the beach towel -
I sag with what I hold.

East

A metal man that walks and beeps,
with buttons, lights, and gears;
it does just what it's told to do -
a made man, it appears.

South

A little paper stub you buy
to ride or see the show;
you keep it safe inside your hand
and tear it as you go.

West

Tie the laces, tie the string,
pull the loops up tight;
this tangled lump that holds it fast -
undo it if you might.
