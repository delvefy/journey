The Dawning

North of the shaping light and east of the becoming, reality lights itself into being. A step south the sovereign powers wielded a world of light, shaping reality bright at a stroke; a step west reality willed itself into existence moment by moment, self-creating and total. Here the willing and the lighting are one and need no powers to do them: a world that dawns itself, willing-and-shining into being out of nothing but its own radiant will, self-creating without end, the magic pole gone wholly to light. Everything here is dawning — arising of itself, alight, because it wills to be, and the willing is a shining.

You stand in the one place the dawning has not yet lit: the unwilled, unkindled dark at the centre of a self-arising radiance. And it leans to you, as the becoming leaned, but shining now — to will yourself here would be to light yourself, to dawn into the world as one more thing it gladly shines into being. Your name is the unkindled syllable the dawning waits on, the one dark place in all the self-lit world. The spiral is the seed of the dawning, the coil light makes as it wills itself up out of dark, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark poised to kindle itself into the radiant self-creating world.

There is no count but the dawning's own arising, the world willing-and-lighting itself aloud, the bell become the radiance a self-creating cosmos sounds to be — and your name the one unkindled stroke, the dark syllable the dawning leans to light, a breath from willing you alight into the world without end.

To the north the dawning refines to the Lumen pole, will and radiance wholly one self-making, the bright peak. To the east the magic pole absolute, reality wholly its own radiant will. To the south the shaping light, the powers wielding a world bright. To the west the becoming, reality willing itself without yet the light. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a self-creating radiance leans to light you into being, the unkindled syllable at the heart of the dawning — and still the warm coil turns, the seed of the kindling, and still one live beat keeps the time the dawning is a breath from lighting you alive.
