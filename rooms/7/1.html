Code

You are a language built to be unread - words turned to dots and dashes, letters slid along the alphabet, whole messages folded into numbers. To the one without the key you are gibberish; to the one with it, plain as speech. Spies trust you with their secrets, machines think in nothing else, and a child with a decoder ring feels the same old thrill: that a message can be hidden in plain sight, carried past every eye, and opened only by a friend.

North

Eggs and bread and milk and cheese,
I write them in a row;
one beneath the next, in line,
so to the shop you go.

East

A short and friendly little test,
of all the things you know;
the teacher asks you question one,
then two, then on you go.

South

With two great wings of steel I fly,
and carry crowds up high;
I race down the runway, then I lift,
a silver bird across the sky.

West

The witch waves her wand and speaks
strange words to work her will;
a frog becomes a prince at once -
her magic words fulfil.
