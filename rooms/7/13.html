Quack

You are the flat loud call of the duck, 'quack-quack-quack' across the pond, the beg for bread by the water's edge. No human throat makes you; the duck's wide beak does. Cheerful and silly and loud. The toy duck says it in the bath; the real one says it waddling, orange-footed, after the crumbs.

North

The sound the owl makes in the night,
'too-whit, too-whoo' it cries;
a soft round call from the dark old tree -
with big round staring eyes.

East

Jump aside to miss the ball,
quick, before it hits;
you duck and weave and slip away -
the game of quickest wits.

South

The short sharp cry a dog lets out
when its tail is trodden on;
a sudden 'yipe!' of hurt or fright -
then just as quick, it's gone.

West

A cross and puffed-out breath of air
when you don't get your way;
arms folded, blowing out your cheeks -
in a sulky mood you stay.
