Yelp

A short sharp cry of surprise, out before you can stop it - a yelp is pain or shock with no time to think. A dog whose tail is trodden, a child who touches something hot, your own voice when the cold water hits. It is high and quick and entirely honest, gone in an instant. You cannot plan a yelp; it leaps out ahead of you, the body crying out before the mind has caught up at all.

North

The loud flat sound
a duck will make,
waddling round the pond
for a breadcrumb's sake.

East

When a ball flies at you,
reach out and seize it fast;
close your hands around it
and hold the throw at last.

South

A long loud cry
of sorrow or pain;
a siren, a baby,
again and again.

West

To mutter and complain
in a low cross way;
you're not really shouting,
just grumpy all day.
