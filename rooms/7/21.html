Reply

You are a reply - the answer that comes back when someone asks or sends. 'How are you?' they say, and your words return; a letter arrives, and you write one back. You can be quick or slow, polite or sharp, a single word or a whole page. No reply at all is itself a kind of answer, and a rude silence too. You are the second half of every conversation, the volley returned, the proof that a message was heard.
