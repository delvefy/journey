Oven

You are an oven - the hot iron box built into the kitchen, where dough turns into bread and batter turns into cake. A hand made you, a hand sets your dial and slides the tray in; then you fill with heat and a glorious smell, and the door glows orange while you work. You can be touched only on the cool handle - inside you are far too hot. You are the warm heart of the kitchen, the patient maker of every roast and every birthday cake.
