Purse

You are a small bag for keeping coins and notes, that snaps or zips shut tight. A hand sews you of leather or cloth and tucks you in a pocket or a handbag. You jingle when you're full, you sigh when you're empty. Granny fishes her pennies from you at the shop. Small, snug, and full of the day's change.

North

A little cloth pouch sewn in your coat
where hands and treasures hide;
a coin, a sweet, a bit of string
all kept warm inside.

East

A soft warm shoe for indoors only,
no laces and no heel;
you shuffle round the house in them -
how snug and warm they feel.

South

A carrier with handles up,
of paper, cloth, or string;
you fill it full at the shop with food
and lug it home, the whole thing.

West

Thin and soft and good for tying,
to fly a kite up high;
a ball of it sits in the drawer -
you snip a length to tie.
