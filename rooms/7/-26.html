Footstool

You are a footstool - a low, soft, four-legged little stool made just for tired feet. Sink into the armchair after a long day, prop your feet up on me, and a happy sigh comes out. Cats curl on me, children use me to reach the high shelf, and grandads doze with their slippers resting on top. Squat and padded and always underfoot, you are the comfiest footrest there is, the little extra that turns a good sit-down into a truly lazy one.
