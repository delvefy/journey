The Shaping Light

North of the spoken engine and east of the wielding, the wielding takes the light. A step south an engine turned easily beside the saying, word and work one; a step west the sovereign powers wielded reality at will, spell and mechanism and wish fused into a single remaking gesture. Here the wielding goes pole-absolute and begins, as well, to shine: reality wholly the powers' to wield, soft as clay in their grip — and the grip itself become radiant, the Lumen climbing into the magic pole, so that to wield the world is now to light it, will and word and work and a first true brilliance all one shaping act.

The powers shape a world of light, remaking room and ground and air at a luminous stroke, the world bending bright wherever their will falls. And you feel what your own name would do, wielded in this radiance: it would shape a shining world to hold you, reality lit and bent into your shape at the saying of it. The spiral is the grip and the gleam at once, the sigil graven in the turning light, the coil the shaping word winds bright, the same whorl in your skin, here the radiant handle by which a lit world is wielded. The powers lay hold of your name, and the world goes soft and bright, ready to be shaped into you in light.

A name wielded here rings and shines and reshapes in one stroke, the bell and the engine and the will struck together and struck alight, each true name a radiant blow that lights the world it remakes — and yours waits to be the next, the shining stroke that would wield a lit reality into your shape at last.

To the north the shaping light refines toward the Lumen pole, will and word and radiance wholly one making. To the east the magic pole absolute, reality wholly the powers' to wield. To the south the spoken engine, word and work easy and barely lit. To the west the wielding, the powers shaping a world not yet shining. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the powers would wield it and shape a world of light to hold you — the bright world soft in their grip, the saying a stroke from lighting reality into your shape — and still the warm coil turns, the radiant handle, and still one live beat keeps the time the shaping light is a stroke from naming you in.
