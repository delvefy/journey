Dice

You are dice - the little cubes you shake in your fist and tumble across the board. Six flat faces, and on each a pattern of dots, one up to six. You rattle them in a cup, you blow on them for luck, you let them roll and hold your breath: a six to cheer, a one to groan. Snakes and Ladders, Ludo, Monopoly - no board game turns without you. Pure chance, carved into a cube.
