Scream

You are the loudest highest cry the throat can make - the shriek of terror, the squeal of delight on the rollercoaster, the alarm that splits the night. No hand makes you; sheer fear or thrill tears you out, 'AAAH!', and every head turns. You can shatter the quiet in a single breath. Louder than a yell, higher than a shout.

North

A long loud cry of grief or pain,
'wahhh,' it rises and falls;
the baby does it, the siren too -
a mournful sound that calls.

East

A hard hit with a tight closed fist,
swung straight from the shoulder out;
the boxer throws it in the ring -
THWACK, a knockout, no doubt.

South

Too cross for words, too loud to quell,
you puff, you stamp, your face goes red;
you open wide and out it comes -
a great loud ___, it's said.

West

Salt water spills from both your eyes,
your mouth turns down, you sob;
a baby does it day and night
for milk - a teary job.
