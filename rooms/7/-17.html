Lantern

You are a lantern - a light kept safe inside a little case of glass, with a handle to swing it by. A candle or a small flame glows within, throwing a warm pool of light on the path while the wind cannot blow it out. You light the way to the shed, you hang from the camping tent, you bob along a dark lane. Made and soldered by a hand, snug and bright, you are a portable piece of warm light to carry into the dark.
