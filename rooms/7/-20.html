Candle

You are a candle - a little pillar of wax with a thread of wick down its middle. Touch a match to your top and you wake into a small bright flame, melting yourself slowly to give your light away. You sit on the birthday cake to be wished at and blown out; you stand on the table when the power fails. You measure time by your own shrinking. You are the patient little fire that lights a room and spends its life doing it.
