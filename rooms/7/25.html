Answer

You are an answer - the reply that fits a question just right, the solution to the sum, the name on the tip of the tongue that finally arrives. No hand makes you; a question and a bit of thinking do. You can be quick or slow in coming, easy or hard-won, and there is nothing quite like the click of finding the right one. The hand shoots up, the eyes light up. Sought-after and satisfying, you are the other half of every question, the thing that makes the asking worthwhile.
