Letter

You are a letter - a handful of words written down, folded into an envelope, and sent off to someone far away. A child prints one to grandma; a soldier sends one home; a stamp in the corner buys you the long ride. You are also each small mark of the alphabet, the bricks that words are built from. You carry love and news across the miles and wait in the box to be opened. You are speech that travels and keeps.
