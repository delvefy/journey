Fact

You are a fact - a thing that is simply true. Water is wet, the earth is round, two and two make four: these are you, and no amount of wishing makes them otherwise. You can be looked up, checked, and proved. Unlike a story or a guess, you do not bend to please anyone. People argue, but you sit there unmoved, waiting to be found out. You are a small hard piece of the truth, the same for everyone who cares to look.
