alphabet

The alphabet is every letter we have, lined up in one long row from A all the way to Z. Twenty-six little shapes, and from them you can build every word that was ever written or said. You sing it before you can read, chant it to find a name in a list, and lean on it whenever you spell. It has no meaning on its own - it is just the order, the row of bricks waiting to be made into words.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

North

Count the sheep, count the stars,
each one needs me to say how many;
one, two, three, and on and on -
I tell you none, or one, or plenty.

East

Put two and two and make them four,
pile them up to make them more;
the plus sign tells you what to do -
heap the numbers, score on score.

South

I end with a little curly hook,
I'm what you ask when you want to know;
who and what and where and why -
answer me, and off you go.

West

A soft slow song at the end of day
to hush a baby off to sleep;
'rock-a-bye' sung low and sweet,
the gentle tune that sends you deep.
