Speech

You are a speech - a stretch of words spoken aloud to a room of listening ears. You are made and meant: written out, practised, then stood up and delivered, the best man's, the headteacher's, the leader's at the rally. No hand holds you, yet hands shake and hearts thump to give you. You can stir a crowd to cheer or send it to sleep. You are language gathered up and aimed, words made to carry across a hall and lodge in the mind.
