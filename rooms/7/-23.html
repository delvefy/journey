Mattress

You are a mattress - the thick soft slab that lies on the bed and takes the whole weight of the sleeper. Springs or foam inside, quilted cloth without, you sag a little where the body lies and hold the warmth of the night. Made by hands - sprung, stuffed, and stitched - you are jumped on by children and flopped on by everyone. You ask only to be turned now and then. You are the deep soft ground of sleep, the kindest landing in the house.
