Torch

You are a torch - a handful of light to carry into the dark. Click your switch and a clean white beam leaps out, swinging across the camping tent, down under the bed, into the spooky shed. You hunt for lost things in the gloom, you read a book beneath the blankets, you signal across the dark field. Made and screwed together by a hand, smooth and heavy with batteries, you are a piece of daytime that fits in a pocket.
