The Hymn

North of the dawning and east of the fiat, the saying becomes a radiance that sings itself. A step south reality willed itself alight, dawning of its own radiant will; a step west the world was a single willing word, commanding itself into being. Here word and will and light rise together to the magic-Lumen peak and become a hymn: the gods' sovereign saying gone wholly radiant, a cosmos that speaks itself into light continuously, sings itself aloud and alight with no singer outside it, reality nothing but a self-sounding radiant word, sovereign and self-creating, an endless hymn the world is and sings at once.

You stand inside a music that is also a light that is also a saying, every ray a note, every note a word that makes, the whole bright cosmos singing itself into being on every side. Your name is the one phrase the hymn has not yet quite sung — the held note the radiant saying leans toward, the syllable the self-singing light is rising to sound. The spiral is the turn the hymn takes, the coil a self-sounding radiance winds, the same whorl in your skin, here the one mark poised to be sung alight into the singing cosmos, your name about to join the hymn that is the world.

There is no count but the hymn itself, the cosmos singing-and-shining without pause, the bell refined past ringing into a continuous radiant chord the world both is and sounds — and your name the one note not yet sung, your heart beating the rest where it will go, a breath from sounded into the self-singing light of everything.

To the north the hymn refines to the Lumen pole, will and word and radiance wholly one self-singing, the bright peak entire. To the east the magic pole absolute, reality nothing but the gods' radiant saying. To the south the dawning, reality willing itself alight. To the west the fiat, the willing word commanding the world. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the self-singing cosmos leans to sound it, the one held note the hymn is rising to — and still the warm coil turns, poised to be sung, and still one live beat keeps the rest the radiant world is a breath from singing you into.
