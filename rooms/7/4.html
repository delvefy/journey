Knock

You are the sound of knuckles on a door, the polite announcement that someone is there and waiting. Rat-tat-tat, and the house turns to listen; a hush, a footstep, and the bolt is drawn. You can be timid or bold, the friend's quick rap or the law's hard hammering; you can carry hope, when it is the visitor longed for, or dread, when it comes too late at night. Small and sharp and made of waiting, you are how the closed door is asked, gently, to open.

North

A jab of one stiff finger sent,
into your side or back;
'hey, look!' or 'move!' is what is meant,
a small sharp little whack.

East

A sudden loud and crashing sound,
the door slammed, or the gun;
it makes you jump and spin around,
the loudest noise, bar none.

South

A soft warm press of lip to skin,
goodnight, goodbye, hello;
no word at all, yet love within,
the gentlest gift I know.

West

A footprint or a fallen thread,
that points the searcher on;
piece us together, it is said,
and the mystery is gone.
