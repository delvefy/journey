The Green Before

South of the green eye and east of the sovereign wild, the wild is before it sees. A step north a sourceless green regard held the world in being by looking at it; a step west the wild's sheer being was the whole sovereign world. Here being sinks below even the seeing: the green before the eye, the world before sight, the wild's pure existence as it was before it ever looked at itself. Existence and creation are one here, as they were a step west — but blind now, unseen and unseeing, a sovereign being so primal it precedes the regard that would later hold it. The wild simply is, here, before it is even watched.

It is the deepest, oldest being there is: not made, not seen, not dreamed, only the sheer green is of the wild before any eye opened on it. You are it, as you were the sovereign wild a step west, but blind now, a being before sight, before the world had learned to look. Your name is below the green eye's resting-place, a wild-truth older than seeing, known by nothing, not even the regard, because the regard is not yet. The spiral is the wild's pre-sight shape, the coil being makes in the dark before it sees, the same whorl in your skin, here the green's own blind seal, turning unseen and sovereign before the first eye.

The wild's being is the world's pulse here too, but blind, a sheer green beating before there was sight to see it beat — and your heart is that stroke, the one being before the eye, existence keeping time in a green dark that has not yet learned to look, sovereign and unseen and simply, wholly, is.

To the north the green before opens its eye, the wild's being held by sourceless sight, the green eye. To the east the Wyld pole absolute, the wild's sheer being the whole of everything, blind and sovereign. To the south it sinks toward the seed and the floor, the green before even being, the source. To the west the sovereign wild, being and the wild one word. You were going somewhere, under a name, as someone seen. Here you are the wild before sight, blind and sovereign and simply is, your name older than seeing — and still the warm coil turns unseen, the green's own seal, and still one blind beat keeps the time the wild is, before it ever looked.
