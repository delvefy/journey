Ticket

You are the little paper stub that buys you in - the train, the cinema, the fair, the football match. Made and torn and kept in a sweaty hand. Show me, and the gate swings open; lose me, and you're turned away. Keep your ticket safe, they say - it is your one small proof that you may pass.

North

A small folding case for notes and coins,
kept safe inside a pocket;
cards in its slots, it snaps shut tight -
your money's little locket.

East

A shiny brass horn you blow and toot,
with three small keys to press;
it blares out loud across the band -
a golden noisy mess.

South

A stiff little rectangle of paper,
you send one on a birthday;
or fifty-two you deal for games -
a king, a queen, you play.

West

A ring of gold upon the head,
with jewels bright and tall;
the king and queen wear this to rule -
the grandest hat of all.
