The Spoken World

East of the elders, the saying becomes the world. A step west a court of the sovereign dead bent matter by naming it, the old powers ruling from the high board; here there is no board and no ruling, because there is no longer a gap between the name and the thing for a ruler to stand in. This is the magic pole absolute: reality not commanded but agreed, held in being from moment to moment by the old names spoken on every side. Gods walk here as neighbours, the long-dead are simply present, and the world is whatever the unbroken saying says it is.

You move through a place that is wholly word. A god passes and nods, ordinary as a passer in a lane; the dead lean in doorways; a hill is a hill because the saying keeps calling it one, and would be otherwise the instant the saying changed. Your name is in the chorus now — not held to be bestowed, as the elders held it, but spoken, continually, by everything, woven into the agreement that keeps you standing here. The spiral is the figure the saying turns on, drawn in the air by every speaker at once, the shape consensus takes — the same whorl in your skin, here the sign that you too are being said, and so are real.

The tolling of names has become the world's whole speech, every stroke a word that holds something in being, the bell rung not to mark the hour but to keep the hour existing. Under it your heart beats, one small certain saying of its own, agreeing with every stroke that you are here, that you are this, that you are very nearly named.

To the north the saying is fitted to craft and light, the old names given engines, the magitech where word and work are one. To the east the magic pole entire, reality wholly consensus, the world nothing but the names that hold it. To the south the walking gods grow wild and bestial, the saying gone to root and horn, the Wyld. To the west the court of the elders returns, the dead sovereign but apart, naming as command. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the whole spoken world is saying it with you, holding you in being while it does — almost your own to say at last — and still the warm coil turns, the sign that you are being said, and still one beat agrees, and agrees, that you are.
