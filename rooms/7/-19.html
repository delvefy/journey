Lamp

You are a lamp - a light set on a stand or a table, switched on against the dark. A hand made you, wired you to glow at the flick of a switch; a hand turns you on by the bed for a last story before sleep. You can be carried room to room, dimmed low and warm, leaned over to read by. A street lamp lights the road; a bedside lamp lights the page. You are tamed light, a little sun you can switch on whenever the night comes.
