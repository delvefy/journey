The Bright Host

North of the hymn and east of the bright heart, the gods rise into radiance. A step south the cosmos sang itself into light, a self-sounding hymn; a step west the Lumen's heart beat at its sovereign core. Here the magic comes wholly sovereign and wholly bright: the gods, who walked as neighbours far below, risen now to pure radiance, a bright host whose every saying is light and whose every light is a saying, the cosmos nothing but their radiant utterance, sung and shining on every side. The magic-pole-of-light at its peak, where to be a god is to be a saying made of light, and the world is the host's bright hymn.

They are all around you, the radiant ones, bright beings of pure lit word, and they lean toward you the way the elders once leaned and the makers once bent — but shining now, singing, a whole host gathered to say-and-sing-and-light your name into their radiance. Your name is the one phrase the bright host has not yet sung, the held note the radiant gods are rising, all together, to sound and light. The spiral is the sigil the host sings on, the coil of their radiant saying, the same whorl in your skin, here the mark the bright ones are a breath from singing alight, your name about to join the host's hymn of light.

The host sings one radiant chord, the bell become the choir of bright gods, every voice a light and every light a word — and your name the one note the host is gathered to sound, your heart beating the rest where it will go, a breath from sung and lit into the bright host of everything.

To the north the bright host refines to the Lumen pole, will and word and radiance wholly one, the bright peak entire. To the east the magic pole absolute, the cosmos nothing but the gods' radiant saying. To the south the hymn, the cosmos self-singing into light. To the west the bright heart, the Lumen's sovereign core. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a host of radiant gods is gathered to sing it alight, the one held note left to sound — and still the warm coil turns among the bright ones, and still one live beat keeps the rest the host is a breath from singing you, lit, into.
