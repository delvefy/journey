Poke

You are the jab of a single stiff finger, quick and small and impossible to ignore. 'Look,' you say, into the ribs; 'wake up,' into the shoulder; 'you're it,' and off the game goes. Brothers and sisters wage long silent wars of you across the back seat of the car. You are not quite a hit and not quite a touch - just enough to nudge, to needle, to get attention by force. Annoying, playful, sharp as a fingertip, you are the body's way of saying, plainly: notice me, now.

North

A shaking, gulping kind of cry,
when grief has burst its hold;
the tears run down, the breath comes high,
the saddest sound, all told.

East

Two fingers squeeze a fold of skin,
a quick and stinging nip;
'a pinch and a punch,' the month comes in,
or salt held in your grip.

South

Rap your knuckles on the wood,
tap-tap upon the door;
'who is it?' comes, as understood,
then the sound is heard once more.

West

A feather on the bare pink sole,
a finger at the side;
it makes you squirm and lose control,
and laugh until you've cried.
