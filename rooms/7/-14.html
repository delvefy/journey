Card

You are the stiff little rectangle of many uses - the birthday card with the scrawl inside, the playing card dealt for the game, the postcard from the seaside, the one you tap to pay. Made of pasteboard, cut clean. A king, a queen, a joker; a robin in the snow. Pick a card, any card.

North

A little paper stub you buy
to ride or see the show;
you keep it safe inside your hand
and tear it as you go.

East

A wooden box with a long thin neck
and six tight strings to strum;
you pluck or twang to make a tune -
in a band it loves to thrum.

South

A little cube with spots one to six,
you shake and roll for games;
it tumbles, lands, and shows a face -
to move your counter's claims.

West

The grand high chair where a king sits,
all gold and red and tall;
he rules the land from up on it -
the proudest seat of all.
