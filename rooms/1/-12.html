Skirt

You are a garment that hangs from the waist down, with no legs and no top - the part of a dress on its own. A hand cuts you and hems you; you swish and twirl when the wearer spins. Pleated, flared, or straight, short or long to the ankle. You are the half-garment of the lower half.

North

Cloth for the top half of you,
with buttons down the chest;
a collar at the neck, two cuffs -
worn plain, or as your best.

East

A soft light plug for a bottle's neck,
pushed in to seal it tight;
'POP!' it leaves the wine at last -
it bobs and floats, all right.

South

An open shoe for a summer's day,
just straps across the toes;
your bare feet breathe, no socks at all -
to the beach in them one goes.

West

A line of posts with rails between
that runs around the field;
it keeps the cattle from straying off -
the boundary, sealed.
