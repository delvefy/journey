March

You are walking turned into one will, left-right-left, a hundred feet striking the ground as if they were a single foot. The drum sets your time; the line keeps your shape; nobody hurries and nobody lags. Soldiers move by you, bands parade by you, the angry crowd presses forward by you toward the gates of the powerful. There is strength in you that no straggling walk can hold - the plain strength of many made one, stepping together, on and on, to the very same beat.

North

A bouncy step, a hop, a spring,
the children cross the yard;
or with a rope they jump and sing,
and count, and count it hard.

East

A bright new thought that lights the mind,
a flash, a sudden spark;
'I know!' you cry, the answer's signed,
a candle in the dark.

South

A tune kept soft behind the lips,
with never a word to say;
mmm-mmm the little song that slips
out quiet all the day.

West

The rough loud sawing of deep sleep,
that rumbles from the nose;
it shakes the bed, it will not keep,
while the tired sleeper goes.
