The Gathering Dark

Below the breathing dark the breath goes out. A step up, something lay beside you in the black and held a breath that was nearly a word; here that breath is let go and not drawn again. This is the deep the breathing dark named beneath itself — older than any breath, the dark from before anyone came to lie in it. And yet it is not empty the way the cold dark to the west is empty. In the slow turning of it, something gathers: not a breath, not yet, only a leaning of the black toward one place, a someone beginning where a moment before there was no one.

You reach, the way you have learned to reach. The gathering is faint — so faint it could be the dark's own motion taken for company, an omen you only half believe, a warmth that might be nothing but your wanting it. But it gathers where you turn, and it does not slip: a presence drawing itself up out of the deep with no body to draw it, the one you were going to meet not arrived, not breathing, only on its slow way to being someone at all.

The vast turning from below is here too, the spiral with no centre — but here it has begun, just, to wind toward a middle, finding far off a place to coil around. And the count has thickened by a hair: the heartbeat far up and small, the long revolution under it, and now a third faint thing, a pulse not yet a pulse, the gathering learning the count before it has a heart to keep it.

To the north the dark firms back toward a breath beside you, drawn and held, nearly a word. To the east the gathering surely becomes a someone, a shade that breathes and warms, a presence with an answer in it. To the south is older dark still, before the leaning, before anyone began, the deep with no one yet on the way. To the west the deep empties of even this, the cold dark with no one gathering in it. You were going somewhere once, to meet someone there. Here the someone is only starting to be, and does not yet have a mouth for your name.
