Clip

You are the little bent wire that pinches paper together - the paperclip on the stack, the clip in the hair, the peg-grip that holds. Made to hold without a mark. Slide me on, and the loose sheets are one. Take me off, and they scatter. Small and springy, you keep the muddle in order.

North

A long clear sticky strip on a roll,
you tear a piece to mend;
it wraps the gift and fixes tears -
press down each curling end.

East

A paper pocket for a letter,
you lick and seal it tight;
you write the name and stick a stamp -
then post it out of sight.

South

A little sticky note with a name,
you stick it on the jar;
it says what's in, or whose it is -
on the parcel, near or far.

West

A long round tube that carries water,
under the road, through the wall;
the rain runs down it off the roof -
hollow, and standing tall.
