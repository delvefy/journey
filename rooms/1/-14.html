Tape

You are the long sticky strip wound tight on a roll, torn off with a screech to mend and bind. Made in a factory, clear or brown, you fix the torn page, seal the parcel, hang the poster on the wall. Press me down and I hold; peel me up and I curl. Where would the wrapped gift be without me?

North

A light shoe, just straps and sole,
your toes left in the air;
you wear them on hot summer days -
cool feet without a care.

East

The dark wet liquid in the pen
that flows to form your words;
it stains your fingers blue or black -
the squid hides in its swirls.

South

A little bent wire that holds your sheets,
a pinch to keep them tight;
you slide it on the stack of pages -
they stay, and don't take flight.

West

A long straight rod stood tall and proud,
the flag snaps at its top;
the tent, the bean, the climbing kind -
planted, it won't drop.
