Rule

Someone made you up and wrote you down, and now you bind us all: stop at the red light, no elbows on the table, take turns and play fair. A rule has no body - it lives in agreement, in the saying and the keeping of it. Some are wise and some are silly, but a game without you is chaos, and a road without you is a crash. Break one and there is always a price to pay.

North

With my lips closed, no words at all,
I murmur a tune down low;
mmmm-mmmm, soft and steady,
wherever I may go.

East

We chase about the playing field,
'You're it!' the loser cries;
you touch another, run away,
the game where everyone flies.

South

I am a thing that is not true,
told to trick or hide;
my nose grows long, or so they say,
the truth I push aside.

West

A finger to the lips says 'sssh',
and all the room falls still;
not silence quite, but waiting quiet -
be calm now, and be still.
