Legend

A famous old story, half history and half wonder, that has grown in the telling down the years — of Arthur and his sword, of Robin and his bow. A legend is made wholly of words, the tale a people will not let die. It keeps the made north-east, among stories and songs. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

North

A long, long story of northern kings,
of feuds and voyages, blood and snow;
the Norsemen told me by the fire —
name the great old tale. Now go.

East

A short and simple little song,
soon learned and lightly sung;
no solemn thing, just quick and gay —
name the catchy air for any tongue.

South

An old, old tale of gods and beasts,
that tells why thunder splits the sky;
none swear it true, yet none forget —
name the ancient story. Now reply.

West

The dark half of the day I am,
from sunset to the morning's grey;
the stars come out, the world goes still —
name the darkness after day.
