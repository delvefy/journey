Jar

You are a jar - a round glass pot with a screw-on lid, made for keeping things fresh. Jam, honey, pickles, marbles, buttons, the odd lost tooth: you hold whatever is poured or dropped in and keep it safe behind your clear walls. Made by hands - blown or moulded, capped tight - you are see-through, so everyone knows just what you hold. You sit in rows in the cupboard, patient and full. You are the keeper of small sweet things, sealed against the air.
