Leap

You are a great bound through the air, both feet flung off the ground at once - the long jump, the salmon's silver arc, the dancer hanging for a breath above the stage. No object, only a motion a body wills and makes: a gathering, a spring, a moment of flight. You clear the ditch, you take the stairs three at a time, you go before you look. A bold wrought movement, you are the body briefly beating gravity, daring the gap and crossing it.

North

To turn around and round and round
so fast the room goes blurry;
the dancer does it on one toe,
the top does it in a hurry.

East

What you give when asked a question,
the reply that's sought;
the solution to the sum,
the thing a riddle's brought.

South

To hop along from foot to foot,
all light and quick and gay;
or to jump a twirling rope
out in the yard at play.

West

A pause from all your work and toil,
to sit and ease the strain;
not quite asleep, but stopping still
until you're fit again.
