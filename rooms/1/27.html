Nod

You are a nod - a quick dip of the head, down and up, to say "yes" without a word. You nod hello across a room, nod along to a song, nod off to sleep when your head grows heavy and droops. One little tip of the chin and everyone understands. Friendly and silent and easily given, you are agreement made out of nothing but a small movement of the head, the simplest yes there is, passed from one person to another with never a sound.
