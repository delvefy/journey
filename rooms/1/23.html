Trick

You are a trick - a clever, sly little act made to fool the eye or the mind. The magician hides the coin in his sleeve and pulls it from your ear; the fox doubles back to lose the hounds. You can be cruel or you can be play - a prank on a friend, a puzzle that catches them out. You are not quite a lie, for everyone half knows; you are cunning dressed up as wonder, the quick hand that beats the slow eye.
