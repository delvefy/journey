Glass

You are glass - the clear, hard, see-through stuff that lets the light straight in. You are a windowpane, a drinking tumbler, a marble, a mirror's shining back. You are made by hand from melted sand, smooth and cold and brittle, and you smash to bright sharp pieces if a careless hand lets go. You are wrought, not grown, the cleverest trick the maker plays on sand. You are the wall that keeps the weather out yet lets a child watch the rain run down.
