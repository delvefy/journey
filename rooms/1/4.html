Secret

You hold it behind your teeth like a small warm coal. A secret is something made and kept - a fact you know and choose not to share, sealed up where no one else can see. It has weight without substance; the bigger it is, the harder it presses to get out. Whisper it to one trusted ear and it is half gone; tell two, and it is loose in the world. Of all the things people make, this is the one built to stay unspoken.

North

I am a thing that is not true,
made up to trick or fool on cue;
tell me and your nose might grow -
the false report you really know.

East

With dice or cards or ball I'm played,
the rules are set, the teams are made;
I bring you fun, I sort the win -
let's play, and let the match begin.

South

Knock knock - who's there? I start that way,
I'm told to brighten up a day;
a silly tale with funny end,
the laugh I bring to every friend.

West

When night arrives I close your eyes,
and carry you to dreaming skies;
I rest your body, deep and sound,
till morning brings you safe around.
