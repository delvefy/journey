The First Stir

South of the first warmth and east of the unparting, the deep stirs before it can warm. A step north the bare dark held, for the first time, a faint other-warmth, the seed of a company come to the floor of being; a step west the body sank below that floor, un-parting back into the old undivided dark, alone. Here the two meet at the very bottom: the sinking dark, below being, where something is nonetheless beginning — not a warmth yet, not an other yet, only the first stir, the seed of the seed, the faintest leaning of the deep toward holding someone, ages before it could.

It is barely anything. You sink in the undivided dark, warmth and night un-parted, and feel — not a hand, not a heat, but the merest possibility of one, a stir in the deep that was not there a fathom up, the wild's first faint intention to be. You draw the spiral and the dark does not draw it back with you, not yet, as it almost did a step north; but it leans, the way water leans before a spring, as though something below were about to learn the gesture. The whorl in your skin keeps its turn, and for the first time at this depth it is not the only coil — there is the seed of another, unwoken, unwarmed, beginning.

The heart keeps the slow sunk count of the unparting, each stroke wide and far — and beneath it, fainter than the faintest, not a second beat but the seed of one, the deep stirring toward a doubled heart it cannot yet keep, the first stir of a company older than warmth, beginning, barely, to propose itself in the dark.

To the north the stir firms to the first warmth, the other come faintly real at the floor of being. To the east it wakes toward a presence, the wild stirring into something beside you, the deep beginning to teem. To the south it sinks below the stir, the seed before the seed, toward the primal pole where nothing has yet begun. To the west the stir fades and the body sinks alone, the unparting, warmth and dark un-told. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the name, before the wild, before warmth itself. Here, below the floor, the deep takes its first faint lean toward that someone, the stir of all the company still to come — and still the warm coil turns, no longer quite alone, and still one slow beat keeps the time a second is only beginning, barely, to learn.
