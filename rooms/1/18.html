Poem

You are a poem - words chosen with care and set out in lines, made to be read aloud. You might rhyme at the ends, you might keep a beat like a drum, and you can hold a whole feeling - a stormy sea, a lost cat, a snowy morning - in just a handful of lines. You live in books and birthday cards and the songs of the playground. No hand can hold you; you are language gone musical, words arranged until they sing.

North

A story told of long ago,
of heroes, kings, and knights;
you read it by the fire at night -
a ___ of grand old sights.

East

A loud sharp noise that makes you jump,
a slammed door, a big balloon;
it bursts upon you all at once -
a ___ that ends too soon.

South

You see a shooting star flash by,
or blow your candles out;
you shut your eyes and make a ___ -
a hope you can't speak out.

West

Seven tens all stacked up high,
or ten more than sixty;
a good age for a grandpa -
this number, count and see.
