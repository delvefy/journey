Wink

You are a wink - the quick shut-and-open of just one eye, a tiny private signal flicked across a room: I'm joking, we two know, this is our secret. No one taught you; a glint of mischief did. You say a whole sentence without a word. Grown-ups give you to a child to mean it's all in fun. Quick and sly and full of meaning, you are the cheekiest thing one eye can do, a little flash of we-two-know between friends.
