Roll

You are turning over and over along the ground - the ball down the hill, the log down the slope, the child rolling over and over on the lawn for the dizzy joy of it. No hand need keep you going once you start; gravity and a push send you on. You gather speed, you gather grass and mud.

North

Drop a ball and watch it spring
straight back up off the ground;
boing, boing, on the trampoline -
up and down you bound.

East

A little jump on one foot or two,
the way a sparrow goes;
you hop the hopscotch squares along -
up and down on your toes.

South

Bend your knees and push off hard,
both feet leave the floor;
over the puddle, over the rope -
up, and down once more.

West

A light half-sleep you slip into
without quite meaning to;
Grandad does it after lunch -
the book slides off his knee.
