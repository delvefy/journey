Tumble

You are a tumble - a head-over-heels roll across the grass, a clatter of falling down on purpose. Not a fall that hurts but one done for fun, arms and legs all over, the world spinning round. Acrobats tumble; puppies tumble; a stack of blocks tumbles when you nudge it. You cannot hold a tumble in your hand - you are all motion, gone the moment you are done, leaving only laughter and a grass-stained knee.

West

Half of a whole hundred,
five tens all in a row;
count by tens to reach me -
how high must you go?
