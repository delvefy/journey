Skip

You are the happiest way of getting along, a hop and a step and a hop again, too full of joy to settle for plain walking. Children do you down the path on the way to something good; lambs do you in the spring field for no reason at all. With a rope you become a game, turning and jumping and chanting the rhymes that count the jumps. You cannot be done while sad - try it and the sadness lifts. Bouncy, light and a little silly, you are gladness that has got into the feet.

North

A great big jump up into the air,
both feet off the ground;
the frog, the dancer, the leaping hare,
all spring up with a bound.

East

What stays behind when the day is done,
of things you saw and knew;
a picture kept of times long gone,
your grandma keeps a few.

South

Left, right, left, the soldiers go,
in step and straight and tall;
they stamp along in a steady row,
to the beat of the drum's call.

West

A short small sleep in the afternoon,
a doze upon the chair;
not for the night, but over soon,
a forty-wink affair.
