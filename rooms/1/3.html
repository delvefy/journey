Joke

North of the song, the made world learns to laugh. A joke is wrought — built line by careful line, the punch saved for the end — and you can hold none of it: it lives in the breath between the set-up and the laugh, and dies the second time you tell it. A good one buckles a whole room; a bad one earns a groan. Timing is everything. Speak the names, and let the merry way open.

North

Whispered low behind a hand,
I'm something you must keep;
let me slip to just one ear
and out I run and leap.

East

Say 'I will' and mean it true —
that's me, a word you give;
keep me and you're trusted long,
break me while you live.

South

I'm a melody with words,
and meant to be sung aloud;
the choir swells me to the beams,
I gladden all the crowd.

West

Behind shut eyes while you're asleep
I paint a world to roam;
I'm gone the instant that you wake
and can't be carried home.
