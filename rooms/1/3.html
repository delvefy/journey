The Lucent Proof

East of the press and above the lamp, the word and the light come together. The room is bright as a made noon — not one everlamp now but a ceiling of them, ranked and steady, a day with the night taken clean out of it — and that surfeit of light falls on a long rack of fresh-pulled sheets hung to dry. The ink on them is not quite dry, and not quite still. Where the press below only set the letters, here the letters have begun to mean what they spell, and the sheets give off a faint warmth and a fainter glow, as though each printed word kept a little of the light it was read by.

One sheet hangs apart from the rest, and on it a single line is printed clean and whole: a name, the name, the one scattered in lead below and fogged in the dark glass and almost-said inside your skull, here at last set in order and pulled bright. You step close to read it and the ranked lamps pulse, all together, the way the everlamp's hour turned, and the letters swim and re-form a hair out of true — legible for the length of a held breath, then warm and bright and blurred. Held up to the light, the paper shows a watermark: the spiral, pressed into the very pulp, turning slowly under the words.

The wire-note the lamp held and the working thump of the press have braided into one sound — a low bright hum that swells when the letters firm toward sense and sinks when they blur, as if the room were trying to read the sheet aloud and could no more hold the name than you can.

To the south the ranked lamps dwindle to a single everlamp, the word fallen away, only the kept light and its slow cast hour. To the west the glow leaves the letters and the word is merely printed, set true and meaning nothing it was not always going to mean, the honest cold press. To the north the lit word multiplies past paper onto wire and pane, flung faster than any eye, a made day that never dims. To the east the words leave off glowing and begin to do, printed charms that act on the saying, the name on the sheet half-risen to stand and answer to itself. You were going somewhere, under a name. It hangs here printed and warm and almost legible, and the lamps will not stop pulsing long enough to let you keep it.
