Bet

You are a bet - a wager laid on what will happen, your word and your money staked against another's. 'I bet you can't,' someone says, and the dare is on; 'a pound says it rains,' says another, and the gamble is made. You ride on horses, on football, on the toss of a coin. Win and you crow; lose and you pay up. You are a guess with something at stake, a hope dressed up as a dare, settled only when the truth comes out.
