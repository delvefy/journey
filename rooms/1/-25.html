Saucepan

You are a saucepan - a round metal pot with a long straight handle and a lid, set on the hob to boil and simmer and stew. A hand fills you with water and spuds, or milk for cocoa, and stirs you with a wooden spoon. You bubble and clatter your lid and boil over when no one watches. You scorch at the bottom if forgotten. Sturdy and hot and always on the stove, you are where most of dinner happens, the deep little cauldron of the everyday kitchen.
