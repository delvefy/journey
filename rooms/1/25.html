Tease

You are a tease - the playful poking of fun at someone, the pulled face, the called name, the wound-up wind-up that stops just short of unkind. No tool makes you; a grin and a glint in the eye do. You dangle the joke just out of reach, you call them by the silly name, you pretend you won't share the sweet. Half mischief and half affection, you are the fun that lives between friends who like each other enough to be a little bit mean.
