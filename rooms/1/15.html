Flip

You are the quick turn over, top to bottom - the tossed pancake, the flicked coin, the gymnast's heels over head. No slow thing makes you; one snap of force does. Over you go, and land the other way up. Heads or tails? Flip me, and let chance decide.

North

Head over heels you roll downhill,
the acrobat's wild fall;
over and over, round you go -
a clumsy, jumbled sprawl.

East

Round and round upon one spot,
the dancer spins her skirt;
the ballerina up on her toes -
a whirling, dizzy spurt.

South

A ball that drops and springs straight up,
then down and up once more;
the trampoline sends you flying high -
off the rubber floor.

West

Two score, four tens, the thieves in the tale,
the winks of a good long nap;
the days the rain came down on the ark -
how many, on the map?
