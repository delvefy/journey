Funnel

You are a funnel - a wide mouth narrowing to a slender tube. Set your tip in the bottle's neck and pour, and you gather the wide splash into a tidy stream, so not a drop is lost. The cook fills the oil, the gardener fills the can, all through your patient throat. Lift you away and the pouring goes everywhere at once. You are a guide for falling liquid, a cone that teaches a flood to go quietly through one small door.
