Jump

You are the spring of the body up off the ground - knees bent, then push, and both feet leave the earth at once. No hand makes you; the legs fling you up, over the puddle, over the rope, up to snatch the high apple. Gravity always wins and pulls you down again, but for a moment you fly.

North

Turn over and over along the ground,
the way a ball will go;
down the grassy hill you tumble,
round and round, just so.

East

A box of jigsaw bits to fit
into a finished scene;
or any tricky problem set
to tease the brain, I mean.

South

Round and round upon one spot,
a top, a coin, a wheel;
so fast it blurs and makes you dizzy -
a giddy whirling reel.

West

When Mum says 'shush, be ___ now,'
she means: make not a peep;
no shouting and no banging drums -
just still enough to sleep.
