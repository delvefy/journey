Spin

You are turning round and round upon one spot - a top, a coin, a dancer, a wheel set whirling. No hand need hold you once you start; you go until you tire and topple. Fast enough, you blur to a grey smudge and the whole room seems to tilt. You make the giddy, laughing dizziness of children. You are the whirl, the twirl, the world gone round.

North

Bend your knees and push off hard,
both feet leave the ground;
over the puddle, over the rope -
up you go with a bound.

East

It ends in a curly little mark,
it starts with why or how;
you ask it when you want to know -
go on and ask one now.

South

A great big jump, far more than a hop,
that carries you over a stream;
the frog, the deer, the long-legged hare -
one bound, a flying dream.

West

The calm when all the fighting stops,
no war and no more noise;
the still of a quiet sleeping house,
the gift the whole world enjoys.
