Rule

You are a rule - a thing you must do, or must never do, set down so everyone knows where they stand. No running by the pool, bedtime at eight, hands washed before tea. Grown-ups make you and games are full of you, and breaking you brings a frown or worse. You cannot be touched, only learned and kept or broken. You are the invisible fence that lets a crowd of people share a place without bumping, the quiet word that says this is how we do things here.
