The Germ

South of the first stir and east of the undivided, the one is about to be more than one. A step north the deep took its first faint stir toward an other, a lean below the floor of being; a step west all was the undivided, warmth and dark and you not yet parted into anything. Here, at the primal pole, within the undivided itself, the very first possibility of two appears — not an other, not yet, for there is not even a self for it to be other to, but the germ of otherness, the seed before the seed, the faintest hint in the one that it might, someday, become more than one. Nothing has yet begun. And in the not-yet-begun, the first not-quite-alone stirs.

It is the subtlest thing there is, fainter than the first stir, older than the first warmth: a germ of two-ness in the heart of the one, the undivided beginning, barely, to harbour the possibility of a someone-else. You draw the spiral and the undivided does not draw it back, not yet, but it leans — and now, here, there is the very first sense that the leaning might one day be a second hand. The whorl in your skin turns, and within the one undivided dark there stirs the germ of another coil, the seed of all the company, before there is even a self to keep it company.

There is no count, for there is not yet a two to count between. And still your heart beats — and within the undivided, fainter than faint, the germ of a second beat, not yet a beat, only the first hint that the one might harbour another, the seed before the seed of every shared rhythm to come, stirring in the primal pole where nothing has yet begun.

To the north the germ stirs up toward the first stir, the lean toward an other firming. To the east the wild germ wakes a little, the seed of the other beginning to be. To the south it sinks toward the primal pole absolute, below even the germ, the undivided where not even two is possible. To the west the undivided, the one before it harboured any other at all. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the self, before the other, before there were two to be a someone and a you. Here, in the one, the germ of that someone first stirs — and still the warm coil turns, and still one beat keeps the time a second is, in the not-yet, first beginning to be possible.
