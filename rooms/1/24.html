Fib

You are a fib - a little lie, small and silly, the kind told to dodge a telling-off or save a blush. Not the great dark lie that does real harm, just a fishy stretch of the truth: I didn't, it wasn't me, I've cleaned my teeth. You are made of words, and you wobble when poked, and you usually come undone with a giggle. No hand holds you. You are the smallest crack in the truth, the one everybody tells and nobody quite believes.
