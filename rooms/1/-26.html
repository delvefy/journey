Rollingpin

You are a rolling pin - a smooth wooden cylinder with a little handle at each end, dusted white with flour. Hands push you back and forth across a lump of dough until it lies flat and even, ready for pastry or biscuits or a pie. You spin on the table and thump when you are set down. Heavy and hard and patient, you flatten every bump and bubble, and when the baking is done you wash clean and wait in the drawer for next time.
