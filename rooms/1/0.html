Bell

Somewhere above you a bell is struck — once — and the single note hangs in the room long after it should have faded, pressing on your ears like a held breath. Everything here was made by a hand: the bell, the hinges, the iron slots set into the four doors. Made things keep the purpose they were made for. Find the word each riddle was forged to hold.

North

Spoken, I am yours alone,
yet others wear me more than you;
lose your face, your years, your throne —
I am the last of you that's true.

East

I have a face but never frown,
two hands that never wave;
I run all day and never walk,
and count you to your grave.

South

I have a head and I have a tail
but no body in between;
two faces, and not one eye,
I'm flipped to settle the unseen.

West

I am cut with teeth that never chew,
I wear a ring but not a hand;
turn me once, and I undo
the strongest lock in all the land.
