Cup

You are a cup - a little vessel with a handle on the side, made for drinking the warm and the cold: tea, cocoa, juice, water. A hand shaped you from clay or glass; a hand wraps round you, lifts you, and sips. You can be held, filled, clinked in a toast, won as a shiny prize. You sit on a saucer for best, or get a chip on the rim from years of use. You are the friendly daily holder of every comforting drink.
