Bounce

You are the spring-back, the up-again - the ball that will not stay on the floor, the trampoline that throws you skyward, the kangaroo crossing the plain in great glad leaps. No still thing makes you; bounce is all energy returned. Down you go, and up you come, higher or the same. Drop me, and watch me refuse to lie.

North

Turn it right over, top to bottom -
quick, the pancake's tossed;
the gymnast turns heels over head -
over it goes, not lost.

East

The worm does it, all squirm and twist,
the toddler on your lap;
you cannot hold it, side to side -
it slips out of your trap.

South

Over and over, round it goes,
the ball down the grassy hill;
the log, the marble, the wheel set loose -
it tumbles, never still.

West

A little daytime sleep you steal,
five minutes in the chair;
the alarm goes, you press it off -
and drift back, unaware.
