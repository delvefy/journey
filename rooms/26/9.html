Clank

You are a clank - the heavy clinking rattle of iron on iron, of chains dragged across stone, of a great rusty machine grinding into life. There is no ring or music in you, just a dull metallic clatter, again and again. A ghost in a story drags its chains with you; an old robot lurches along with you. Heavy and rough and a little bit eerie, you are the sound of cold metal in motion, clanking out slow and loud in the dark.
