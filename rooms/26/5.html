Blush

You are a blush - the hot pink tide that climbs up the neck and floods the cheeks when you are shy or caught out or paid a sudden compliment. You arrive without permission and give the whole game away: the more you wish you wouldn't, the redder you go. There is no hiding you. Warm and rosy and impossible to fake, you are the face telling the truth about how it feels, glowing like a ripe apple while the rest of you wishes the floor would open up.
