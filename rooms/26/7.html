Escape

You are an escape - the breaking free and getting away from somewhere you are stuck. The prisoner escapes the cell, the hamster escapes its cage, the steam escapes the kettle's spout in a whistle. There is a thrill in you, a great rush of relief as the door springs open and the open air rushes in. Daring and sudden and free at last, you are the slipping of the trap, the dash for the gap, the wonderful moment when whatever held you fast lets go and you are away.
