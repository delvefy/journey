dream

A dream is the film that plays behind your eyes once you are deep asleep. Strange and bright and bending, it lets you fly, or run from things, or talk to people who are not there - and while it lasts it feels as real as morning. Then you wake and it slips away like water through your fingers, leaving only a feeling and a few odd pictures. You cannot hold it or show it; it belongs only to the sleeper.

North

Down in the pond when evening comes
the green frog sings his only note;
a rough low sound, not pretty at all -
the gravelly word stuck in his throat.

East

A big white goose will shout me loud,
a car will blare me down the street;
press the horn or flap and squawk -
one rude short blast, and far from sweet.

South

Heavy chains and iron doors,
a dropped tin pot, a robot's feet;
that hard cold clunk of metal on metal,
a clumsy, banging, ringing beat.

West

When the alarm goes off too soon
you slap it quiet and sleep some more;
a little extra doze in bed,
the lazy nap you can't ignore.
