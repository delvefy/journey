Foil

You are foil - a thin shiny sheet of soft metal, bright as a mirror and easy to tear off the roll. You wrap a sandwich, cover a dish, line a baking tray, and scrunch into a silver ball when you are done. Light bounces off you in dazzling flashes. You crinkle and fold and keep your shape wherever you are bent. Gleaming and crinkly and endlessly handy, you are the kitchen's bright wrapping, keeping food fresh and warm, then crumpling down to a tiny silver nugget in the hand.
