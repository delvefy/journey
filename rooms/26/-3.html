Ink

You are ink - the dark glossy liquid that hides inside every pen, waiting to become words. You flow out in a thin wet line, black or blue or bottle-green, and dry on the page to stay there for years. Spill a drop and you blot and spread and stain a finger blue for days. Octopuses squirt you, printers spray you, fountain pens drink you up. Quiet and quick to dry, you are the very stuff of writing, the colour that turns blank paper into a letter or a story or a name.
