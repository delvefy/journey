Stapler

You are a stapler - the chunky little tool that lives on every desk, full of a long strip of wire staples. Slide your papers into your jaws, press down hard, and clunk: a tidy wire staple bites through and folds flat behind, pinning the pages as one. When you run out you click open hungrily for a fresh strip. Solid and satisfying, you make a small happy thunk each time, and a whole report or a stack of worksheets is held neatly together by your quick metal bite.
