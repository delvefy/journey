croak

Croak is the rough, low, gravelly sound a frog makes, swelling its throat into a balloon and letting it out across the evening pond. On a warm night a whole chorus of them croaks together, loud as anything. People croak too, when a cold has stolen their voice and only a rusty scrape comes out. It is the least musical noise in the marsh, and yet on a summer dusk it is the very sound of the water settling down to sleep.

North

Swing your leg and boot the ball,
strike it hard with the front of your shoe;
a horse does it, a swimmer too -
one good thump, and off it flew.

East

The sharp loud sound a dog will make,
'woof! woof!' at the postman at the gate;
it guards the house and warns of strangers -
a noisy call, both small and great.

South

The film that plays behind your eyes
once you are fast asleep at night;
you fly, you fall, you meet strange folk -
then you wake and it's gone from sight.

West

The donkey's loud and silly call,
'hee-haw! hee-haw!' across the field;
a wheezy honking up-and-down -
the noisiest sound a farm will yield.
