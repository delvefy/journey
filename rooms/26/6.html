Fistbump

You are a fistbump - two closed fists knocked gently together, knuckle to knuckle, a quick cool greeting between friends. "Nice one!" you say without a word, after a goal scored or a joke landed or a job well done. Sometimes the fists spring open afterwards with a little wiggle, an explosion of fingers. Friendly and easy and just a touch cheeky, you are the handshake's relaxed younger cousin, a small happy bump that says we're a team, well played, see you later.
