Notebook

You are a notebook - a stack of blank pages bound between two covers, waiting to be filled. Sums and spellings, doodles and lists, secret plans and half-finished stories all end up inside you. Your pages crinkle when they turn and curl at the corners with use. Some of you have lines, some are plain, some lock with a little clasp. Patient and ready, you carry whatever a busy mind pours out, and you keep it safe between your covers until someone opens you again.
