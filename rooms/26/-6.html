Bunk

You are a bunk - a bed stacked right on top of another bed, with a little ladder up to the high one. Two children sleep in one floor's worth of space, and bedtime becomes a game of who gets the top. Up there you are close to the ceiling, snug and grand; down below you are cosy as a cave. Sturdy and clever and just a little bit daring, you are the most exciting bed there is, a whole bunk-bed adventure for sharing a small room with a brother or a friend.
