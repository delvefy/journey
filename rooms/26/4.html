Pout

You are a pout - the sulky push of a bottom lip stuck out when things have not gone your way. No sweets before tea, bedtime too early, a turn you didn't get: out comes the lip, down come the brows, and the whole face folds into a small storm of crossness. You say without words: it isn't fair. Quiet and stubborn and a little bit funny, you are sulking made visible, the lip that wobbles on the edge of tears, daring anyone to tell you to cheer up.
