Cage

You are a cage - a box of upright bars, set close enough that nothing inside can squeeze through. A budgie hops about in you, a hamster runs its wheel in you, and at the zoo great beasts pace behind you. A little door swings open for feeding and cleaning. Strong and barred and a bit sad, you keep what is inside both safe and shut in, and the kindest thing anyone can do is to open your door and, sometimes, set the creature free.
