Cloth

You are a cloth - a piece of woven fabric, threads crossed over and under a thousand times, that hands cut and stitch into clothes or use to wipe a spill or polish a shoe. You come off a great roll in the shop and fray at the edges if not hemmed. You can be fine or rough, bright or plain. Soft and woven and endlessly useful, you are what the loom makes from thread, the start of every shirt and curtain and crumpled hanky.
