palette

A palette is the flat board a painter holds, thumb poked through the little hole, dotted round the edge with blobs of every colour. In the middle they swirl the blobs together with the brush to mix new shades - a touch of blue into yellow makes green, a little black makes it darker. By the end of the day it is a glorious smeary mess of all the colours at once. It is where the picture is invented, before it ever reaches the canvas.

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

A fat plastic cone you shout right through,
it makes your voice come out gigantic;
coaches and crowds and marchers use me -
squeeze the trigger, get all frantic.

East

A square of tight strong cloth on a frame,
the painter brushes colour on me;
hang me up on the gallery wall -
a window of paint for all to see.

South

A shiny brass horn with three little buttons,
blow hard and out comes a blaring sound;
press the keys to play the tune -
loud and golden, the brassiest around.

West

A wooden stick with soft hairs at one end,
dip me in paint and sweep me about;
I leave bright colour where I go -
red, blue, green, I splash them out.
