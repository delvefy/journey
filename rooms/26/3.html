Cackle

You are a cackle - a harsh shrieking laugh that crackles and breaks, the sort a witch lets out over her bubbling pot. You are not warm or kind like a chuckle; you have a sharp wicked edge, full of glee at someone else's trouble. Hens make you too, clucking and squawking in the yard. Loud and jagged and a little bit creepy, you split the quiet with your cracked high shriek, and anyone who hears you in a dark wood will quicken their step and not look back.
