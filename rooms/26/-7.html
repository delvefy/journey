Blind

You are a blind - a sheet of cloth or slats that hangs at the window and pulls down to shut out the light. Tug the cord and you roll down smooth, or twist the rod and your slats tip closed. In the morning you snap up with a flap and let the sunshine flood in. Neat and flat and tidy against the glass, you are the window's eyelid, drawn down for a nap on a bright afternoon or against the prying dark of night, and whisked up again to greet the day.
