megaphone

A megaphone is a fat plastic cone you hold to your mouth and shout into, and out the wide end your voice comes huge. No wires, no plug - just a funnel that gathers your shout and throws it far across the field, so the whole crowd hears you. Coaches use it, and people leading marches, and anyone who needs to be loud. Hold the handle, squeeze the trigger, and your small voice turns into a giant one.

North

Two soft warm pads joined by a band,
I clamp on your head to cover each ear;
I keep out the cold and the loud loud noise -
fluffy and round, from here to here.

East

Music pours from my round black face,
I'm the box that makes the sound come loud;
plug me in and turn me up -
I'll fill the room and please the crowd.

South

A painter holds me, thumb poked through,
I'm dotted with blobs of every paint;
mix the colours on my board -
bright and bold, or soft and faint.

West

A singer grips me close and sings,
I catch the voice and make it grand;
speak into my round soft top -
I send it loud across the land.
