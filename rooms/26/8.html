Strut

You are a strut - to walk with your chest puffed out and your head held high, showing off to all the world. The rooster struts round the farmyard; the winner struts off the pitch; a peacock struts to fan its tail. Every step says "look at me." Proud and bouncy and just a bit full of yourself, you are walking turned into a show, a swaggering parade of one, certain that every eye is on you and rather hoping they are.
