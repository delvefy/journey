Snort

You are a snort - the sharp snuffly noise blown out through the nose: a pig at its trough, a horse at the gate, or you, when a laugh escapes the wrong way and comes out your nostrils. No hand makes you; surprise does, or amusement, or a stuffy cold. You cannot be touched, only heard - a sudden honk that nobody means to make. Half-funny and half-rude, you are the laugh that got loose through the nose, the most undignified sound a person owns.
