Piglet

You are a baby pig, small and pink, with a snub snout and a curly tail no bigger than a question mark. No hand makes you; the sow gives a squirming litter of you. You squeal, you snuffle, you root in the mud and the straw, you fight your brothers for the best teat. Plump and pink and always hungry.

North

A baby cat, all fluff and mischief,
that chases its own tail;
it mews, it pounces, climbs the curtains -
small, and soft, and frail.

East

A bushy-tailed and nimble thing
that races up the tree;
it buries nuts and digs them up,
then leaps from branch with glee.

South

A stocky beast with a black-and-white head
that digs a deep den called a sett;
it snuffles out worms in the dark all night -
a shy striped digger, rarely met.

West

A big fat bird with a wobbly red throat
that gobbles round the farm;
at Christmas time it's the dinner roast -
'gobble-gobble,' it sounds alarm.
