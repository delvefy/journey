The Unwarmed

South of the old frost and west of the dead calm, the cold goes back before there was ever warmth. A step north a cold older than fire froze the dead dark where it lay; a step east the dead substance came to its dead calm, settled and effaced and still. Here the cold deepens past even the frost into the deep before the first warmth — a cold that is not the loss of heat, as the old cold was, nor heat frozen, as the frost was, but a cold that never had any warmth to lose, original and dead and effaced, the unwarmed deep from before warmth was ever a thing, where even rest is forgotten because there was never anything that moved to rest.

It is the coldest because it is the first cold, the cold the world was before it ever kindled — not chilled, but unwarmed, never once warm. You draw the spiral and the unwarmed deep neither takes it nor freezes it, having no warmth even to refuse; the mark is simply cold, original-cold, in a place that never knew the other thing. And still — impossibly, in a cold from before warmth — the whorl in your skin turns warm, the one heat the unwarmed deep never had and cannot account for, the single warmth in a cold that never warmed, refusing the deep before the first warmth.

There is no count; the unwarmed deep is older than the first beat, from before there was a warmth to beat. And still your heart beats — warm, impossible, the first and only warmth in a cold that never had one, each stroke a heat the unwarmed deep has no place for, a beat from before the first beat refusing the cold before the first warmth.

To the north the unwarmed rises to the old frost, a cold at least once frozen from warmth. To the east the dead calm, the dead substance settled and still. To the south the cold deepens to the primal pole, the unwarmed absolute, older than the first warmth, the deep. To the west the Waste corner entire, dead and unwarmed and effaced through, the pole. You were going somewhere, under a name, carrying a warmth. Here the cold from before warmth would have it that warmth never was — and still the warm coil turns, impossibly warm, and still one beat keeps a heat the unwarmed deep never knew, refusing the cold before the first warmth at the corner of the world.
