Hazelnut

You are a hazelnut - a small round nut in a smooth glossy brown shell, grown on a hedgerow tree and gathered up by squirrels to bury and forget. No hand makes you; the hazel bush does. You crack with a satisfying snap to give up a sweet creamy kernel, ground into chocolate spread and crumbled over cakes. Round and brown and shiny, you are autumn's neat little treasure, the nut a squirrel works all summer to collect.
