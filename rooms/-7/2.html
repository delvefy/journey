Light

You are the first thing the world was given, the swift bright traveller that lets the eye see at all. You pour from the sun, leak from the lamp, leap from the struck match - and the dark simply is not, where you are. You have no weight and cannot be held, yet you paint every colour there is and race faster than anything that runs. Shut the curtain and you are gone in an instant; lift it, and you fill the room before the thought is finished.

North

What the ear catches on the air,
a bang, a song, a word;
no shape, no weight, yet everywhere,
I'm felt the moment heard.

East

Grey and curling, up I rise,
from the fire's red bed;
I sting the nose, I cloud the eyes,
where flames have burned and fed.

South

With the tongue I come to know
the sweet, the sour, the salt;
is the apple ripe or no?
I answer without fault.

West

A single shaft of golden light,
that slants down from the sun;
through cloud or leaf it pierces bright,
a thin straight line of one.
