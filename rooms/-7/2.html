The Dead March

West of the same grey and north of the traceless, the made world reaches its last frontier. A step east the killed future and the never-made past met and stood as the same grey; a step south the last trace of the made dissolved wholly into the forgotten. Here is the farthest the made ever got: the dead march, the Chrome's outermost dead reach, a faint cold grey firming for a moment out of the limitless forgetting before the Waste takes it too. You stand at the very edge of the machine-world's old empire, where its last frontier dissolves into a forgetting with no border, future and past already wholly one dust around you.

It is a borderland of nothing, the dead march — the last grey that still half-remembers it was once a made thing's farthest mile, dissolving even as it remembers. You draw the spiral and it firms for an instant, a cold cast mark at the frontier, and then the forgetting widens and takes it, the way it takes the march itself; only the whorl in your skin keeps a whole warm coil, the one mark that does not dissolve at the dead edge, carried past the last frontier of the made into a grey with no farther to reach.

There is no count, only the dead march's last cold stillness, the machine-empire's farthest tick long since swallowed, the bell that marked its borders melted into grey. Your heart beats at the frontier, the one warm count the dead march never garrisoned and the Waste cannot widen away, keeping on past the last reach of the made into the forgetting.

To the north the dead march firms toward the Chrome's last dead reach, the machine future's outermost grey. To the east the same grey, future and past met as one dust. To the south the traceless, the made dissolved past its last grain. To the west the Waste pole widens, the empire's frontier gone wholly to forgetting, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the made world's last march once held its farthest record of it. Here that record dissolves at the frontier, future and past one dust around it — and still the warm coil turns, whole, and still one beat keeps on past the dead march into the limitless grey.
