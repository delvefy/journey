The Unstirred

South of the unwarmed and west of the quietus, the cold comes to a rest it was never stirred from. A step north a cold older than fire never had a warmth to lose; a step east every death lay down together in one quiet rest. Here both reach the corner-pole and become the unstirred: the deepest nothing, a cold that never warmed come to an absolute rest — not the stilling of motion, for there was never motion, not the cooling of warmth, for there was never warmth, but a rest that was never once disturbed, a still cold from before there was anything to stir it, the sinking and the forgetting and the cold all one undisturbed nothing at the bottom of the corner.

It is the stillest place there is, because it was never troubled into stirring: no warmth ever moved here, no motion ever rose, nothing ever happened to be stilled. You draw the spiral and the unstirred neither takes it nor stills it further, having never stirred to begin with; the gesture rests on a cold that was always at rest. And still — the one disturbance the corner never managed to prevent — the whorl in your skin turns, the single stirring thing in an unstirred cold, the one warmth that troubles the deepest nothing, refusing the absolute rest.

There is no count; the unstirred cold was never stirred into a beat, never warmed into a rhythm, never troubled into time. And still your heart beats — the one stir in the unstirred, the single warm disturbance at the bottom of the corner, each stroke a motion in a cold that never moved, refusing the deepest rest there is.

To the north the unstirred rises to the unwarmed, a cold that never had a warmth. To the east the quietus, every death at one rest. To the south the primal pole, the unstirred absolute, the cold that was never once stirred. To the west the Waste corner entire, sinking and forgetting and cold one undisturbed nothing, the pole. You were going somewhere, with someone, once, a warmth and a motion. Here the unstirred cold would have it that nothing ever warmed or moved or was — and still the warm coil turns, the one stir, and still one beat troubles the deepest rest, refusing the unstirred at the corner of the world.
