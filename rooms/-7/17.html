Laugh

You are a laugh - the happy bursting sound that breaks out when something is too funny to hold in. Ha ha ha, it doubles you over, it makes your sides ache and the tears run down; it leaps from person to person round a room until everyone is helpless. A baby's first one is the best sound in the world. No hand can hold you - you are pure joy turned loud, bursting out whether you mean it to or not.
