Dog

You are the faithful four-legged friend, tail thumping, tongue lolling, ready for a walk before the lead is off the hook. No hand made you, though people shaped your hundred kinds - you came in from the wild long ago to share the fire and guard the gate. You fetch, you herd, you sniff out the lost, you lie warm across the feet at night. Loyal past all sense, glad past all reason, you are the oldest friendship there is, kept alive on a pat and a kind word.

North

The thick warm fleece a sheep grows long,
shorn off in summer heat;
it's washed and spun to knitting yarn
for jumpers, warm and neat.

East

It purrs and prowls and licks its paws
and naps the day away;
it hunts the mouse, it climbs the fence -
a whiskered beast at play.

South

A small grey beast with long soft ears
that carries loads and packs;
it brays hee-haw across the field
and plods along the tracks.

West

A male sheep with great curled horns
that butts with all his might;
he leads the flock across the hill
and charges in a fight.
