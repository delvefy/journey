Moss

You are moss - the soft green velvet that creeps over the damp and shady places. You cushion the old stone wall, you carpet the fallen log, you fur the north side of the tree and the roof of the shed. Press a hand to you and you are cool and springy and slightly wet. No flower, no fruit, just the gentlest green. Grown by no hand, soft as a kitten, you are the quiet green blanket the woods lay down wherever it stays cool and damp.
