Willow

You are a willow - the gentle tree that leans over the water and lets its long thin branches hang down like loose green hair. The wind combs you and you sway; a duck paddles through your trailing tips. You grow where the ground is soft and wet, by the pond and the slow brown river. Children sit in your hollow and call it a den. You are the saddest-looking and softest of trees, weeping your leaves down to brush the stream.
