The Forgotten

West of the Waste, the forgetting forgets itself. A step east was the pure pole of the forgotten, a flat grey plain where magic was dead and nothing answered and nothing recalled that answering had ever been; here even that recollection is gone. The Waste has forgotten that it is a Waste. The grey no longer knows it is grey, no longer holds the faint shape of a thing-once-lost, no longer keeps the memory of having forgotten. There is no absence here, for absence remembers what is missing — only the flat even sourceless light over a ground that has forgotten it is the ground, going on to the end of the world.

You do not call. You find, here, that you have forgotten how — that the old wish to be answered, which crossed even the pole a step east, has thinned out of you in this air, because there is nothing left to remind the wish that it was ever a thing. You draw the spiral in the grey and the place does not even fail to read it; failing would mean remembering that reading was once done. The mark simply is, unread, in a country that has forgotten marks. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its warmth and its turn, the one thing that has not forgotten itself, carried west of the end of remembering.

There is no count, and no memory of counting. No bell; no wind, even — the wind that blew over the open Waste forgot it had anywhere to go and lay down. Only your heart, beating plainly in the forgotten grey, unrecalled and unmissed, the one rhythm in a world that has forgotten rhythm was ever made.

To the north the forgotten grey meets the made world's last dead reach, rust forgotten in the Waste, the Chrome's far edge. To the east the Waste remembers itself a little, the pure pole where at least the forgetting still knows its name. To the south the grey sinks toward the older cold and the dark beneath, the forgetting tilting into the primal night. To the west the pole absolute, grey past grey, where there is not even a Waste to have forgotten, and nothing has ever been. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the forgetting has forgotten there were ever names to forget — yours unmissed, unrefused, simply never — and still the warm coil turns, unforgotten, and still one plain beat goes on into the grey that has forgotten everything, even its own forgetting.
