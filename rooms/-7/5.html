The Subsumed

West of the last certainty and north of the scattering, the dead future is wholly subsumed. A step east a single cold certainty held, the dead machine's last fact; a step south the monument crumbled to grey dust, scattering. Here the Waste takes the last of it: the machine future's outermost grey absorbed wholly into the never-made, no seam left between the dead future's dust and the Waste's old grey, the future forgotten past its own ending and subsumed past its own forgetting. The Chrome ceases, here, to have ever been distinct — not ended, not dissolved, but taken up entire into a grey that goes to the end of the world.

It is the last absorption: the dead future's final grain laid down into the open Waste, indistinguishable, subsumed, the cold machine-grey become simply the grey, with nothing left to mark where one ended and the other began. You draw the spiral and the subsuming grey neither keeps it nor reads it nor recalls that a future was ever here to make it; the mark is taken up with everything else. Only the whorl in your skin keeps a distinct warm turn, the one thing not subsumed, the single untaken mark in a grey that has absorbed an entire future without a trace.

There is no count, only the grey that subsumed the last tick of the made and the last grain of its dust alike, the future taken up past even its ending. Your heart beats into the subsuming grey, the one warm count it cannot absorb, keeping a distinct time where a whole future was taken wholly up and forgotten.

To the north the subsumed firms toward the Chrome's last dead reach, the machine future's outermost grey, not yet wholly taken. To the east the last certainty, the dead future's final cold fact. To the south the scattering, the monument crumbling to dust. To the west the Waste pole entire, the future subsumed wholly into the never-made, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, and a whole future held it once. Here that future is taken up wholly into the grey, your name subsumed past its own ending — and still the warm coil turns, distinct and untaken, and still one beat keeps a separate time where an entire future was absorbed into the Waste without a trace.
