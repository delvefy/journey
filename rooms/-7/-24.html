Walnut

You are a walnut - a hard round wrinkled nut that hangs on a tall tree and falls in autumn, your shell so tough it takes a good squeeze of the nutcracker to crack. Inside, your two halves curl together like a tiny wrinkled brain. No hand makes you; the tree does, slow over a whole summer. You turn up in cakes and stuffing and the bottom of the Christmas bowl. Knobbly and brown and brain-shaped, you are the cleverest-looking nut of all.
