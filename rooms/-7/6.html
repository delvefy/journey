Spring

You are the season that ends the cold, when the frost lets go and the first green spears push up through the dark earth. No hand brings you - the days simply lengthen, the lambs come, the blossom opens on bare branches, and the whole world wakes and warms. You are not a thing to hold but a turning, a promise kept each year: that after every winter the light comes back and everything begins again.

North

Beneath a tree on a blazing day
I'm cool and dim and deep;
the sun is shut away from me -
here from the glare you keep.

East

When day is done the sun sinks low
and sets the sky on fire;
the red and gold along the west
before the dark climbs higher.

South

The hottest season of the year,
when school lets out for play;
the long bright evenings by the sea,
the ice cream on the way.

West

A sudden puff of wind that slams
the door and steals your hat,
then drops as quick as it arrived -
now what do you call that?
