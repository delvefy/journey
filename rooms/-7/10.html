Thought

You are a single quiet flicker in the mind - a notion, a wondering, an 'I think...' that drifts up from nowhere and floats away again. No hand makes you; the busy head spins you out, one after another, all day long. Some of you are bright, some dull, some you keep, most you forget. Silent and weightless.

North

When your eyes go misty and the lesson fades
and your mind drifts off somewhere nice -
you're riding a dragon, you're eating cake -
a waking dream, in a trice.

East

A little bite of cold that creeps
in through an open door;
not freezing yet, but cool enough
to want your coat once more.

South

The quiet belief a friend is true,
that they will not let you fall;
you lean on them, you tell your secrets -
you give them most of all.

West

The soft dry whisper of leaves that stir,
or paper crumpled small;
a mouse moves in the autumn pile -
a crisp light sound, that's all.
