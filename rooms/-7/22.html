Pant

You are a pant - the quick heavy breathing that comes after you have run as hard as you can. Mouth open, chest heaving, you drag the air in and push it out, too fast to speak. The dog does you on a hot day, tongue out, sides going like a bellows. You are the body shouting for more air and grabbing all it can. You fade as the breath comes back and the heart slows. You are the sound of effort, gulping at the sky.
