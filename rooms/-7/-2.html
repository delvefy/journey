Pond

You are a small still mirror set in the grass, a held breath of water with no wish to go anywhere. Rain fills you, the sun trims you back; in your brown depths the tadpole turns to frog, the newt hides, the lily floats its white cup. Cattle drink at your edge and the heron stands in you on one cold leg. No tide troubles you, no current pulls - you keep the sky's reflection still, and let the whole slow patient life of the water gather close.

North

On two small legs I hop and run,
then open wings to fly;
I greet the morning and the sun,
a feathered song on high.

East

A hollow mouth in the rocky hill,
all dark and cold and deep;
the bear crawls in to sleep his fill,
where dripping waters seep.

South

With shining scales and waving fin,
I swim the river clear;
I breathe the water, cool within,
and never come up for air.

West

A little pool left by the rain,
upon the muddy ground;
the children stamp and splash again,
in the small wet round.
