Sniffle

You are a sniffle - the wet little sniff of a stuffy nose, drawn back up again and again because the cold won't let it dry. You go with red noses and watery eyes and a damp hankie crumpled in a fist. No hand makes you; the cold does, and the crying, and the chilly walk home. You are the small sad sound of feeling poorly, the snuffle that comes before the sneeze. You are winter's own little tune, sniffed out under every blanket.
