Chew

You are a chew - the steady up-and-down grinding of teeth on a mouthful, turning a bite of bread or apple soft enough to swallow. No one teaches you; hunger and a back tooth do. Mum says do it thirty times before you swallow. Gum gets chewed and chewed and never goes down at all. Patient and round and rhythmic, you are the slow first work of every meal, the jaw going round and round while the eyes look about for what is next.
