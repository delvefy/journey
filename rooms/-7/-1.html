The Unseen

South of the forgotten and west of the gloaming, the grey goes dark and does not know it. A step north the forgetting had forgotten even itself, a flat grey that no longer knew it was grey; a step east the light was failing, the Waste tilting into a dusk that still half-remembered the day. Here the dusk is over and forgotten with everything else: a dark that does not recall it was ever a dusk, unanswered as the Waste and unseen as the night and unremembered as both — the forgetting tilted wholly into the primal dark, the old cold rising up through it from beneath, lightless, answerless, and unaware that it is either.

You do not call; you have forgotten how, as you did a step north, and now there is not even the grey light to lose the gesture in. You draw the spiral and cannot see it, and the dark cannot either, having forgotten that seeing was a thing — the mark made blind, in a blindness that does not know it is one. Only the whorl in your skin stays sure, felt and not seen, the one coil the unseen dark cannot un-know because it never knew it was there: held by touch alone, the last certain turn in a dark that has forgotten light, and forgotten the forgetting, and forgotten you.

There is no count, no wind, not even the gloaming's falling hush; all of it is forgotten, gone dark and gone still and gone unrecalled. Only your heart beats into the unseen, unheard and unseen and unremembered, the one stroke in a dark too forgotten to take it — keeping a time the night has forgotten could be kept, by feel, in the blind cold.

To the north the unseen lifts to the forgotten, a grey that at least has light to forget by. To the east the dark pales to the gloaming, a dusk still failing out of a day. To the south it sinks toward the older night beneath, the primal dark, the cold come fully up. To the west the pole absolute, the unseen and the forgotten and the cold one dark, where nothing is recalled and nothing shows and nothing answers, to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the dark has forgotten light and dusk and you alike — your name unseen, unheard, unremembered — and still the warm coil turns, felt in the blind cold, and still one beat keeps on into the dark that has forgotten everything, even that it is dark.
