Trust

You are the quiet belief that someone will not let you down - the hand you take in the dark, the secret you can tell, the back you turn without fear. No hand makes you; it is built slowly, kept word by kept word, and broken in a single careless moment. Hard to win, easy to lose, slow to mend.

North

A small quiet something in your head,
a notion passing through;
'I think...' you say, and there it is -
a quick mind-picture, new.

East

Not last night, gone, nor nights to come,
but this one, dark and near;
the stars that rise when this day ends -
the night that's almost here.

South

Still and easy, free of worry,
breathing slow and deep;
a glassy sea without a wave -
a quiet you can keep.

West

A sudden flood of total fear,
when all goes wrong at once;
your heart slams, your thoughts fly wild -
you freeze, or run, or bounce.
