Sound

You are everything the ear can catch - the bang and the birdsong, the slam of the door, the far-off bark, the voice that says your name. You are made of nothing but air set trembling, yet you travel the whole house in an instant and turn a corner the light cannot. The newborn knows you before it can see; the blind read the world by you alone. Stop your ears and the brightest morning goes strangely dead. You are how the world tells us it is still there.

North

The sky goes pink, the dark grows thin,
the cock begins to crow;
the great gold sun comes climbing in,
to set the day aglow.

East

No leaping flame, no blazing light,
just a soft and steady shine;
the ember's red, the worm's pale white,
a gentle warmth of mine.

South

Swift and bright, I fill the room,
the instant curtains part;
I drive away the deepest gloom,
and let the eye take heart.

West

A tiny voice, as soft as breath,
held close beside your ear;
a secret hushed so none but you
will ever, ever hear.
