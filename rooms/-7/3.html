The Unmourned

West of the grey terminus and north of the dead march, the machine future's ending is forgotten. A step east the dead glare stood at its terminus, the made world's last cold idea; a step south its outermost frontier dissolved into the limitless forgetting. Here even the ending is taken: the open Waste's grey silence has swallowed not only the dead machine but the very fact that it ended, so that there is no terminus left to point to, no last mile, no "the made stopped here" — only a grey silence that has forgotten the made ever was, and forgotten that it ever ended, the whole machine future unmourned and unremembered, gone past even its own conclusion.

It is stranger than the terminus and quieter than the march: a place where an entire future died and no silence was held for it, because the silence forgot there had been anything to fall silent from. You draw the spiral and the grey neither ends it nor remembers endings; the gesture is unmourned as it is made. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its turn, the one mark that is not forgotten and not ended, carried into a grey that has lost even the memory of the machine future's last cold breath.

There is no count and no last tick to have been the last; the made hour's ending is forgotten with the rest, the final silence so complete it does not know it follows a sound. Only your heart beats into the unmourned grey, the one warm stroke no one held a silence for, keeping a time the forgotten ending forgot could end.

To the north the unmourned firms toward the Chrome's last dead reach, the machine future's outermost grey. To the east the grey terminus, the dead machine's ending still legible. To the south the dead march, the frontier still dissolving. To the west the open Waste takes it wholly, grey silence to the pole, the made and its ending alike forgotten. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the made world ended once, somewhere behind you. Here even that ending is unmourned and unremembered — your name lost not at the terminus but past it, where endings are forgotten — and still the warm coil turns, unended, and still one beat keeps on into the grey that forgot the machine future even stopped.
