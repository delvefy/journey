Gnaw

You are a gnaw - the patient on-and-on biting at something hard that will not give up easily, the dog at the buried bone, the mouse at the skirting board, the beaver at the tree. No one teaches you; sharp front teeth and stubbornness do. You work away at the same spot for hours, wearing it down sliver by sliver. Slow and steady and toothy, you are eating turned to hard work, the chewing that gets through what one quick bite never could.
