Oak

You are an oak - the great broad king of the trees, slow to grow and strong as iron, spreading wide branches over the field for hundreds of years. No hand made you; one small acorn did, sprouting and reaching skyward century by century. You can be climbed, sat beneath, carved with initials, hugged by three children with arms outstretched. Your hard wood builds ships and beams and barrels. Mighty and patient and old beyond counting, you are the sturdy giant of the wood.
