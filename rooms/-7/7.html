Shade

You are the cool dimness where the sun cannot reach - under the oak, behind the wall, beneath the broad umbrella on a blazing day. No hand makes you; it is only something standing between the ground and the sun, and there you pool, grey and gentle and cool. Cattle gather in you, travellers rest in you, and you swing slowly round as the sun rolls over. Not the dark shape itself, but the cool relief of it.

North

Still and quiet, not a breath of wind,
the sea like glass, all peace;
the storm has passed, the air is hushed -
a settled, soft release.

East

Seven days make up my span,
from Monday through to Sunday;
I bring the school, I bring the rest,
then start again on one day.

South

The season after winter's cold,
when buds and lambs appear;
the days grow long, the blossom blows -
the freshest of the year.

West

The cold tight feeling in your chest
when something makes you scared;
it speeds your heart, it grips your throat,
when danger has you snared.
