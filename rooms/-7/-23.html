Peanut

You are a peanut - a little nut in a crinkly shell, two to a pod, and unlike all the others you grow in the ground, not on a tree. Roasted and salted, ground to butter, or thrown to the monkeys at the zoo, you are everyone's favourite handful. No hand made you; the warm earth did. Crack your papery shell and there are the two pale beans inside. You are the nut that hides underground, small and humble and loved by all.
