Hamster

You are the small soft pet that lives in a cage, fur warm in the hand, cheeks stuffed fat with seeds. No hand made you; you stash and hoard by old instinct. All night you run your squeaky wheel, going nowhere fast. Cup me in your palms, and feel the tiny drum of a heart far quicker than your own.

North

A stout grey digger, striped of face,
that trundles out at night;
it lives in a set beneath the roots -
shy, and black-and-white.

East

What's left when the tree is felled,
a short fat trunk, cut low;
you sit on it and count its rings -
the roots still down below.

South

A clever beast that swings through trees,
with a long tail and quick hands;
it loves bananas, makes you laugh -
in hot jungle lands.

West

A horse in black-and-white striped coat,
that gallops on the plain;
no two are striped the same, they say -
a wild and stripy mane.
