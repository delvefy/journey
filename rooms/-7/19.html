Sneeze

You are a sneeze - the sudden explosive achoo that bursts out when something tickles the inside of your nose: dust, pepper, a feather, or a bright light. No hand makes you; your own nose does, all by itself, and you cannot stop it once it has started. You cannot be touched, only heard and dodged - and bless you, says everyone. You scrunch your face, you breathe in fast, and then ahh-choo, the whole of you shakes. You are the body's quick way of blowing out what does not belong.
