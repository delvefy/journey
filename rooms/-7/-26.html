Pistachio

You are a pistachio - a small pale-green nut tucked inside a smooth shell that has split half open all on its own, leaving a handy gap to prise apart. Salty and crunchy and faintly sweet, you come by the bagful and you cannot stop at one. The empty shells pile up in a little dish. Green and moreish and made for sharing, you are the nut that turns eating into a fiddly happy game, cracking shell after shell to free each tasty green morsel inside.
