The Scattering

West of the cenotaph and north of the unmourned, the dead monument crumbles to grey. A step east the Chrome's last cold idea stood as a frozen monument; a step south even its ending was forgotten, unmourned. Here the corner takes the monument entire: the dead geometry worn past stone to a grey powder, the machine future's outermost reach crumbling grain by grain and scattering into the never-made. This is the scattering — the last form of an age of certainty come apart into dust so fine it can no longer be told from the Waste it disperses into, the dead future's farthest grey worn to nothing at the corner of the world.

It is the monument's afterlife and its end: every exact line of the cenotaph crumbled to a grey grain, the whole dead future a slow drift of powder dispersing toward the pole. You draw the spiral and it is cut, for an instant, into a crumbling grey grain — a maker's mark scattering as you make it, the dead future coming apart under your finger. Only the whorl in your skin keeps a whole coil, the one mark not scattering, the single intact thing where the machine future has worn wholly to grey dust and is dispersing, grain by grain, into the never-made.

There is no count, only the slow scattering, the dead future's last form coming apart grain by grain, the bell ground to a grey powder that does not ring. Your heart beats among the scattering, the one warm whole thing where the machine future disperses, keeping a time the crumbling grey can no longer hold together to count.

To the north the scattering firms toward the Chrome's last dead reach, the machine future's outermost grey, the monument not yet wholly dust. To the east the cenotaph, the dead idea still a monument. To the south the unmourned, the dead future's ending forgotten. To the west the corner takes the scattering entire, the dead future worn wholly to dust, the Waste pole. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold age cut it into its monuments. Here the monuments crumble to grey powder and scatter into the never-made — your name dispersing grain by grain — and still the whole warm coil turns, unscattered, and still one beat keeps on where the dead future comes apart into dust at the corner of everything.
