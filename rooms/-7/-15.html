Sloth

You are a sloth - the slowest creature in the warm green wood. You hang upside-down from a branch by hooked claws, blinking, in no hurry at all. You eat a leaf, then doze; you cross one tree in a whole long day. You move so slowly that green algae grows in your shaggy fur, and moths come to live there. The world rushes; you do not. Slow is simply how you are.

all made of woody scales;
I open wide in sunny weather,
shut up tight in gales.
