Cough

You are a cough - the rough bark of air the chest shoves up the throat when something tickles or a cold has settled in. You come in fits in the night and make the whole bed shake; you ask for a hand over the mouth and a sip of water after. You cannot be held, only let out or held back. A doctor listens for you with a cold round ear. You are the body clearing its own throat, loud and rude and not to be helped.

North

A little jump in the back of your throat,
that comes again, again;
you hold your breath to make it stop -
a ___, and then again.

East

A tiny far-off point of light,
that pricks the night-time sky;
you wish upon the first you see -
a ___, so high, so high.

South

The pepper tickles inside your nose,
you breathe in, hold, then - whee!
out it bursts with a great atishoo -
a ___, and a 'bless me'.

West

The rude loud noise that comes back up
when you gulp your fizzy drink;
you say excuse me, cheeks gone red -
a ___, quicker than a blink.
