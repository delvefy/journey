Smile

You are a smile - the happy upward curve of a mouth. You light a whole face from the inside, you crinkle the eyes, you say 'I'm glad' without a word. You leap from person to person across a room, catching like a yawn but kinder. You greet a friend, soften a stranger, mend a small hurt. No hand can hold you, for you are only a shape the face makes for a moment - and the warmest thing a face can do.
