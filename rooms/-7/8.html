Calm

You are the great stillness, the quiet after the storm has blown itself out - the sea gone flat as glass, the air without a breath, the heart no longer racing. No hand brings you; you settle on the world when the trouble passes, when the wind drops and the waves lie down. Sailors fear too much of you, becalmed and going nowhere; the frightened long for you. Peaceful and level, you are the world holding its breath in quiet, the smooth untroubled face of things at rest.

North

The feeling that a friend won't fail,
that you can lean and rest;
you give it to the ones you love
and those you know are best.

East

The day not come, just up ahead,
always one sleep away;
wake, and it has turned to now -
it's never quite today.

South

Where a wall or tree shuts out the sun
and the ground stays cool and dim;
cattle crowd into me at noon,
out of the heat's hard rim.

West

A nagging fret that won't let go,
a fear of what's to come;
it gnaws inside, it spoils your sleep,
and makes the stomach numb.
