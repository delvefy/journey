Chestnut

You are a chestnut - the glossy brown nut that grows inside a spiky green case high in the tree. In autumn the case splits and you fall, smooth and shining, the deep warm brown that people borrow for a horse's coat or a girl's hair. Roasted on a winter fire you turn sweet and soft; left whole, you gleam in a child's pocket like polished wood. You are a small burnished treasure, dropped from a tree in a coat of thorns.
