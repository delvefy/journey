Hiccup

You are a hiccup - the little 'hic!' that jumps up from the chest, again and again, that no one can quite explain or stop. You come from eating too fast, drinking fizzy pop, or laughing too hard. People try every cure: hold your breath, drink water upside down, get a fright. You never last long, but while you do you are a small bouncing nuisance, popping out at the worst moments. You are the body's silly stutter, a tiny gulp of air that won't behave.
