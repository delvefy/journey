Snail

You are a snail - the slow soft creature that carries its whole house on its back, a coiled shell to hide in when danger comes. You glide along on a single foot, leaving a glistening silver trail behind, and poke out two long horns with eyes on the tips. Salt is your terror; the rain is your delight. You munch the lettuces and madden the gardener. You are the slowest traveller in the garden, never in a hurry, always at home wherever you are.
