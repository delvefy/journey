Badger

You are a stout black-and-white digger of the night, striped down the face, low to the wood floor. No hand made you; you snuffle for roots and worms and trundle home to your set beneath the trees. Shy and strong, you keep to the dark. The forest is yours when the people sleep.

North

A baby pig, pink, small, and round,
with a curly little tail;
it squeals and trots behind its mum
along the muddy trail.

East

A small round beast all over spikes,
it curls up in a ball;
it snuffles through the leaves at night -
a prickly thing, and small.

South

A little furry pet you keep
that spins around its wheel;
it crams its cheeks right up with seeds -
how round its face can feel!

West

The great gold cat with shaggy mane,
the roarer, king of all;
it hunts the plains and naps in sun -
the biggest, proudest call.
