Pineapple

You are a pineapple - a fat golden barrel in a coat of diamond scales, with a stiff green crown of leaves standing proud on top. No hand makes you; the hot far countries do, growing you low and slow under a tropical sun. Inside you hide bright yellow rings, sweet and sharp enough to sting the tongue. Spiky and tough on the outside, gold and dripping within, you are the proudest, most tropical thing ever to sit in a fruit bowl wearing a hat.
