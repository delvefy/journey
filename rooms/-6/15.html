Tornado

You are a tornado - a great spinning funnel of furious wind that drops from a black storm cloud to the ground. You roar like a hundred trains, twisting and tearing, lifting cars and roofs and whole roads into the sky. Where you touch down you leave a path of wreckage; where you pass over you suck the world up into your spinning grey throat. You are wind gone wild, the angriest weather of all.
