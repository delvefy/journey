Smell

You slip in through the nose without asking, and suddenly a whole world arrives: bread baking, rain on hot stone, the sea, a struck match, a grandmother's coat. You have no shape and no weight, only a way of being unmistakable. A good one stops a person in the street; a bad one clears a room. Stranger still, you reach the memory faster than any picture - one breath, and you carry someone twenty years back all at once.

North

I curl up grey from every fire,
and climb into the air;
I sting the eyes and smell of wood,
and drift off everywhere.

East

You cannot see or hold me,
yet I fill up every space;
you breathe me in to stay alive,
in every single place.

South

I am the place of sand and sea,
where the tide rolls up the shore;
with bucket, spade, and ice cream cone,
you play here evermore.

West

I live upon your tongue, not nose,
of sweet, or sour, or sharp;
a lemon makes me pucker up,
and honey makes me glad.
