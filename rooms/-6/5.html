The Last Certainty

West of the two roads and north of the cenotaph, the dead machine comes down to its last certainty. A step east the killed future and the never-made past met as one grey; a step south a dead monument stood frozen, the Chrome's last cold idea in stone. Here the monument wears down to the single thing it was built around — one bare cold certainty, the last true fact the machine future bought when it sold its wonder, persisting alone in the grey. The dead-machine pole absolute: not an engine, not a mind, not even a monument now, only the final certainty itself, exact and meaningless and forgotten past its own ending.

It is the coldest thing there is, a certainty with no one left to be certain of it — the last true fact floating in a grey that has forgotten there was ever anything to be true about. The Chrome shed wonder for this, and here is all it bought: one bare cold sureness, and the Waste closing over even that. You draw the spiral and the last certainty neither proves it nor doubts it, having no warmth to wonder with; the mark is simply, coldly, there, and then forgotten. Only the whorl in your skin keeps an uncertain, living warmth, the one coil that was never a fact, the single unsure and breathing thing at the dead-machine pole.

There is no count, only the last certainty holding cold and exact in the grey, the bell become a single frozen true note no one hears. Your heart beats against it, warm and unsure and alive, the one thing that does not need to be certain to be — keeping a living time beside the dead machine's last cold fact, forgotten past its ending.

To the north the last certainty firms to the dead-machine pole absolute, the Chrome's final cold fact, certainty without a knower. To the east the two roads, future and past met as one grey. To the south the cenotaph, the last cold idea still a monument. To the west it goes wholly to the Waste, the future forgotten past its own ending, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold age was certain of it once, exact in all its records. Here that certainty is alone and forgotten, your name a cold true fact no one is left to know — and still the warm uncertain coil turns, alive, and still one unsure beat keeps on past the dead machine's last certainty into the grey.
