The Cold Eternity

West of the levelling and north of the last certainty, the dead machine reaches its corner entire. A step east all difference died into one level grey; a step south the dead-machine future came down to a single cold certainty, a true fact with no one left to know it. Here is the corner-pole of it all, the cold eternity: the disenchanted future absolute, wonder banished and the engine left to run cold forever, a sterile grey certainty going on to the end of the world with no knower, no warmth, no wonder, no one. This is the far edge of the future that killed its own magic, the cold mirror of the bright pole, the Chrome entire.

It is sterile and it is endless: a dead even grey that has shed mind and warmth and wonder and kept only the cold fact of itself, running on with no one to run for, certain of nothing and everything alike, forever. And here — at the cold corner of the world, against the whole sterile weight of it — the one thing the Chrome could never compute or kill still turns: the whorl in your skin, warm, uncertain, alive, the single living mark in the cold eternity, the one thread back to yourself the dead-machine pole could never make and so could never take.

There is no count, only the cold eternity holding its sterile grey forever, the bell frozen to a fact no one hears. And still your heart beats — warm and unsure and alive, the one living thing in a future that banished living, the oldest refusal of all, beating on at the cold pole of the world where there is only the dead grey, and the warm coil, and the beat that the Chrome, for all its certainty, has never once been able to still.

To the north the cold eternity firms to the dead-machine pole absolute, the Chrome entire, sterile to the end. To the east the levelling, all difference dead to one grey. To the south the last certainty, the dead future's final cold fact. To the west it levels wholly into the Waste, the killed future and the never-made one grey, to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold age was sure of it once. Here that sureness runs on forever with no one to hold it, your name a cold true fact in an empty eternity — and still the warm coil turns, alive, and still one unsure beat keeps on at the cold corner of the world, the one warm thing the dead machine could never make.
