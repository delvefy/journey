The Dead Calm

South of the dead weight and west of the effaced, the dead substance comes finally to rest. A step north the pitch had deepened into a weight that lay over everything; a step east the deep wore past memory itself, the forgotten of the forgotten. Here the weight reaches the floor beneath the floor and stops — no more sinking, no more lessening, no more wearing-away, only a dead calm, the heavy dark settled at last onto the bottom of the corner, and even the effacing effaced, so that not only is everything gone but the going is over and the having-gone is forgotten too. The Waste corner come to rest: still, heavy, final, done.

Nothing moves and nothing will. The dead calm is not the held tension of the pitch nor the slow subtraction of the lessening; it is after all of those, the settled finality where even the patient takings have finished and lain down. You draw the spiral and the dead calm neither holds it nor takes it nor effaces it, having no motion left even to forget with; the gesture simply rests, unmoving, on the unmoving dark. Only the whorl in your skin keeps the one stir there is, turning faintly in the dead calm, the single thing at the bottom of the corner that has not yet come wholly to rest.

There is no count, only the dead calm, where the falling-still of every wind and weight and wearing has finished falling. Your heart beats once into the settled dark, and the dark does not stir, and a long calm later beats again — the one motion in a place where all motion has lain down, a stir refusing the dead calm at the resting-floor of the corner.

To the north the dead calm lifts to the dead weight, the heavy dark still slowly sinking. To the east the effaced, the deep worn past even its own memory. To the south it settles toward the corner-pole, the dead calm gone absolute, the rest beneath the rest. To the west the Waste corner entire, dead and effaced and still through and through, where even rest is forgotten. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the going has come to rest with the dead weight and been effaced with the rest — and still the warm coil turns faintly in the dead calm, the one unrested thing, and still one stir of a beat keeps on at the settled bottom of the corner.
