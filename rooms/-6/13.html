Blizzard

You are the wild white storm of snow, wind-driven and blinding, where the world turns to a roar of cold and you cannot see your own hand. No man makes you; the winter sky lets loose. Stay in, stay warm, stay found. Step out into me, and in three steps the door behind you is gone.

North

A wild strong wind that bends the trees
and rips the tiles away;
it roars all night and shakes the doors -
a fierce and blowing day.

East

The brave heart that does the hard thing,
though the knees may shake;
it faces the dark, it speaks right up -
for the small one's sake.

South

The shiver of delight and fear
that races down your spine,
on the roller-coaster's plunging dip -
scared and glad, both fine.

West

The hard bright light that hurts your eyes -
the sun on snow at noon,
the headlamp shining in your face -
you squint and look down soon.
