Star

You are a star - a tiny far-off point of light that pricks the black night sky. There are thousands of you, scattered like spilled salt, and children join you up into pictures and wish upon the first to show. You burn so far away that your light takes ages to reach an eye below. You cannot be touched, only gazed at and counted and named. You are the cold bright fire of the heavens, twinkling down on everyone who looks up after dark.

North

A round white face up in the night,
it grows then thins away;
it pulls the tides and lights the dark -
a ___, the lamp of night, not day.

East

The grey curling cloud off a fire,
it stings and makes you cough;
it climbs up from the chimney top -
a ___, then drifts off.

South

Sun and rain together make
an arch of seven hues;
it bends across the sky and fades -
a ___, red through blues.

West

A rough bark from a tickly chest
when a cold has got you down;
you cover your mouth and hack away -
a ___, the worst in town.
