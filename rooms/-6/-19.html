Trunk

You are a trunk - the thick strong central pillar of a tree, the great column of wood that holds up all the branches and leaves. No hand made you; the tree grew you, ring by ring, a new circle for every year, wrapped in rough bark. You can be leaned on, climbed, hugged with both arms and still not reached around, carved with a heart. The whole tree stands or falls by you. You are the strong still middle of the wood, holding the crown up to the sky.
