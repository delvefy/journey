Skyline

You are a skyline - the ragged dark outline a city makes against the evening sky, all towers and spires and rooftops cut from black. Seen from far off across the river, you tell a whole city in a single jagged shape: here a great glass tower, there a domed roof, there a pointed steeple. As the sun sets behind you, you turn to a row of dark cut-outs. Bold and famous and instantly known, you are a city's signature drawn on the sky, the shape that says exactly which place this is.
