The Quietus

South of the dead calm and west of the floorless, all the deaths become one rest. A step north the dead weight had come to its dead calm, the heavy dark settled and still; a step east there was no floor to settle on at all, only endless sinking. Here every mode of the corner converges: the sinking and the forgetting and the cold and the dead weight all sink into a single absolute repose, the rest beneath the rest, where there is nothing left to rest from and nothing left to be still about. This is the quietus — not one death of many but all of them at once, folded into one final stillness at the corner of the world.

Everything the Waste ever did — the lessening, the effacing, the freezing, the floorless sinking — arrives here and lies down together, indistinguishable, one quiet. There is no telling the cold from the forgetting from the falling now; they are the same rest. You draw the spiral and the quietus neither takes it nor stills it further, having nothing left to do; the gesture simply lies down with everything else. Only the whorl in your skin keeps the one unquiet turn, the single coil still moving where all the deaths have become one stillness, refusing the quietus at the corner.

There is no count, only the one rest. Every stilled and sunken and forgotten and frozen thing keeps the same non-time here, the rest beneath the rest — and your heart beats once into the quietus, the one warm motion where all motion has converged and lain down, a stroke refusing the single absolute stillness at the bottom of the corner.

To the north the quietus stirs toward the dead calm, the dead weight merely at rest. To the east the floorless, a sinking that has not yet lain down. To the south the corner-pole absolute, the quietus gone wholly to the Waste's pole. To the west the Waste corner entire, sinking and forgetting and cold one rest, the deepest nothing. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here all the going's undoings have become one quiet and lain down together — and still the warm coil turns, the one unquiet mark, and still one beat refuses the quietus where every death has become the same stillness at the corner of everything.
