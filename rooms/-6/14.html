Gale

You are the wild strong wind that howls all night, bending the trees double, ripping the slates from the roof, slamming the gates back and forth. No hand makes you; the angry sky lets loose. The ships run for harbour; the washing flies off the line. Lean into me, and I will hold you up at an angle, then drop you.

North

A spinning funnel of furious wind,
that twists down from the cloud;
it lifts up cars and roofs and cows -
a roaring, whirling shroud.

East

The quiet art of waiting on,
without a whine or fuss;
you wait your turn, you wait the bread to rise -
it asks the most of us.

South

A wild white storm of driving snow,
so thick you cannot see;
the wind howls hard, the cold bites deep -
stay in, where warm you'll be.

West

Tiny bright flecks that catch the light,
stuck on the card with glue;
a thousand little winking specks -
on snow and tinsel too.
