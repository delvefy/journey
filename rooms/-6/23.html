Planet

You are a planet - a great round world turning slowly in the cold black of space, circling its star year after year. You may be a ball of rock or a swirl of gas, ringed or red or blue like home. No hand made you; gravity gathered you from dust and time. You hold your moons close and ride your long orbit, never stopping, never straying. You are a whole world hung in the dark, one of many, lit on one side by a faraway sun.
