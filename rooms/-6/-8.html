Cat

You are the small soft hunter of the hearth, padding in on silent paws, all whisker and green eye. No hand made you - you are half-wild still, a tiger shrunk to the size of a cushion. You purr on the warm lap and you kill the mouse in the dark; you wash yourself for hours and sleep most of the day. You come when it suits you and not when called. Sleek, proud, and warm, you are the wild kept just tame enough to share the fire.

North

The soft thick coat that warms a beast,
on rabbit, fox, or bear;
stroke it and it gleams and lies,
the wild thing's own warm wear.

East

A fluffy yellow baby bird
new-hatched from out its shell;
it peeps and totters after the hen,
not grown up very well.

South

A long-tailed rodent, grey or brown,
that scurries through the drain;
it nibbles in the granary
and fears the cat's domain.

West

Loyal and waggy, I love a walk,
a bone, a ball, a pat;
I bark at the post, I guard the house,
and chase the neighbour's cat.
