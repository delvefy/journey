Rabbit

You are a soft furry animal with long ears and a fluffy white tail, quick as a blink. No hand makes you; you breed and burrow in the field, popping up to nibble clover and carrots, then thumping a warning and vanishing down a hole. Foxes hunt you; children keep you in a hutch. Big-eared, bright-eyed, and always alert.

North

A grey-brown rodent, bigger than a mouse,
with a long bare ratty tail;
it raids the bins, it gnaws the grain -
sharp teeth that never fail.

East

A tall green crop with golden ears
wrapped up in pale green husk;
boiled on the cob or popped in a pan -
it feeds the farm at dusk.

South

A bushy-tailed and nimble thing
that races up the tree;
it buries nuts and digs them up,
then leaps from branch with glee.

West

A baby cat, all fluff and mischief,
that chases its own tail;
it mews, it pounces, climbs the curtains -
small, and soft, and frail.
