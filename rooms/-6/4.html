The Cenotaph

West of the terminus and north of the grey terminus, the dead machine leaves a monument. A step east the dead pole was dissolving into the Waste, future and past worn to one dust; a step south its terminus stood as a cold even glare at the Waste's edge. Here the Chrome's last cold idea takes a form before it goes — a vast dead geometry standing frozen in the grey, perfect and functionless, a cenotaph of the machine future: the final shape an age of certainty made of itself, an empty monument to wonder banished, raised by no one to no one, already dissolving grain by grain into the corner where the future and the past are one.

It is flawless, and it is dead. Every line of the dead monument is true, every angle exact, a frozen perfection with not one living thing in it and no one left to read the perfection — the engine reduced to its last cold idea, and that idea set in grey stone at the edge of the Waste. You find the spiral cut into the cenotaph as a maker's seal, precise and meaningless, dissolving with the rest; only the whorl in your skin keeps a warm and crooked turn, the one coil that was never drawn to be perfect, the single living mark against the dead exactitude of the monument.

There is no count, only the cenotaph's frozen silence, the last cold idea holding its perfect form and saying nothing, the bell set in dead stone and never to ring. Your heart beats against the monument, the one warm and imperfect stroke at the Waste's edge, keeping a time the dead geometry was built to outlast and could not.

To the north the cenotaph firms toward the dead-machine pole, the Chrome's last cold idea absolute. To the east the terminus dissolves wholly into the Waste, future and past one dust. To the south the grey terminus, the dead machine's cold glare. To the west the corner takes the monument entire, the dead future worn to grey, the Waste pole. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold age cut it once into its monuments. Here the last monument stands empty and dissolving, your name a perfect meaningless seal on dead stone — and still the warm crooked coil turns, the one living mark, and still one imperfect beat keeps on past the cenotaph of the future into the grey.
