Glow

You are light without fire, warmth without flame - the soft, steady shine a thing keeps after the blaze has gone. You live in the last red coals of the hearth, in the green spark of a firefly, in the dial of a watch held under the blanket, in cheeks after running. You never dazzle and never leap; you simply hold, patient and low, in the dark. Where you are, something is still warm, still alive, still quietly giving off the last of its light.

North

The clock has reached its highest count,
twelve strokes ring through the gloom;
the darkest middle of the night,
when ghosts are said to loom.

East

The cock crows out, the stars go pale,
a pink light edges the sky;
the night gives way, the world wakes up,
as morning draws nigh.

South

Grey and curling, up I rise,
from every burning thing;
I sting the eyes and choke the throat,
and to the sky I cling.

West

Footsteps, music, a barking dog,
your two ears drink me in;
I travel through the air unseen,
from a whisper to a din.
