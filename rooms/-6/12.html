Thrill

Your stomach drops and your skin goes electric - a thrill is the body saying yes to fear it has chosen. It is the top of the roller-coaster, the dare taken, the first plunge into cold water. No one can hand you one; it rises in you, unbidden and unmade, and burns bright for a heartbeat before it fades. You chase it because for that instant you are entirely, fizzingly alive, and nothing else exists at all.

North

A storm of driving snow,
so thick you cannot see;
the howling wind blows white
and buries every tree.

East

When you see a hurt thing
and your heart begins to ache,
you feel this gentle sorrow
for the poor one's sake.

South

A prickling, fizzing feeling
running up your skin;
when your foot has gone to sleep
it wakes with this within.

West

The half-light in between,
not day and not yet night;
the first faint stars appear
in my failing silver light.
