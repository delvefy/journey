Almond

You are an almond - a smooth pale oval nut in a pitted shell, grown on a tree that blossoms pink in early spring before any leaves come. No hand makes you; the warm orchard does. You taste faintly of marzipan and turn up flaked on cakes, ground into icing, salted in a bowl. You are good for you, the grown-ups say. Smooth and pale and quietly sweet, you are the gentlest of nuts, the one that sweetens a hundred puddings.
