Coconut

You are a coconut - a big brown hairy nut that grows high in the palm tree and drops when it is ripe. Hard as a helmet on the outside, you hide sweet white flesh and a swallow of milk within. No hand made you; the tropics did, the long beaches and the warm winds. Crack me open and there is a feast inside, and three little eyes that watch from your shell. You are a treasure that has to be broken to be enjoyed.
