Stump

You are what is left when the tree comes down - a low fat trunk, sawn flat, rooted still in the ground. No hand made you; the axe and the saw left you behind. Count the rings on your face and read the tree's whole life. Sit on me in the wood and rest; I will not mind, I am going nowhere.

North

A small round beast all spines outside,
it curls up in a ball;
it snuffles through the leaves at night -
a prickly thing, and small.

East

A long orange root pulled from the earth,
with a green tuft on its head;
the rabbit's favourite crunchy snack -
the snowman's nose instead.

South

A hard brown shiny seed of the tree,
in a spiky green case it grows;
you thread it on a string to fight -
a champion, everyone knows.

West

A little furry pet you keep
that spins around its wheel;
it crams its cheeks right up with seeds -
how round its face can feel!
