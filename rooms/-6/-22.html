Bamboo

You are bamboo - the tall green cane that is not quite a tree, hollow inside and ringed all up its length like a flute. You shoot up faster than anything green, a whole hand's height in a day. The panda munches you for breakfast, lunch, and tea. People build whole houses and ladders and fishing rods from you, light and strong. Bend in the wind, knock hollow when tapped: that is you, growing visibly skyward.
