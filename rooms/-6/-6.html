The Abyss

South of the quietus and west of the nadir, the Waste comes to its corner entire. A step north every death lay down together in one rest; a step east the void voided even itself, the nothing emptied of its own emptiness. Here is the corner-pole of it all, the absolute abyss: the deepest nothing there is, where the subtracting and the effacing and the freezing and the forgetting and the sinking have all arrived and ended, magic forgotten past all asking, being undone past all undoing, the primordial deep past and the dead disenchantment one total void. This is the bottom and the far edge of everything, the dark mirror of the bright pole, the Waste entire.

There is nothing here, and nothing left to be the nothing of: no floor, no dark, no cold, no forgetting even, only the absolute, the abyss that every nothing the long descent passed through was a step toward. And here — at the very corner of the world, against the whole weight of the void — the one thing the Waste could never take still turns: the whorl in your skin, the spiral, warm, the single mark in the absolute abyss, kept by nothing but your refusal, across every undoing, to let it go. It is the one thread back to yourself, and it has not broken, even here.

There is no count, no time, no thing the abyss has not voided. And still your heart beats — the one warmth at the corner of everything, the single having-been in the absolute nothing, the oldest refusal of all, beating on at the dark pole of the world where there is nothing else, and nothing else is needed: only this, the warm coil and the warm beat, going on.

To the north the abyss rises to the quietus, the rest that at least had things to lay down. To the east the nadir, the void below the void. To the south the corner sinks past even the abyss, if past can be said, toward the pole's pole. To the west the Waste's absolute end, the void beyond the void, grey and dead and gone to the edge of the world. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here at the dark corner of everything the going is undone past all undoing — and still the warm coil turns, and still one beat keeps on, the one thread that the abyss, for all it has taken, has never been able to take.
