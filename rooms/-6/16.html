Wind

You are the wind - moving air, felt but never seen. You push the trees till they bow and roar, you fill the sails and lift the kite, you steal the hat and chase the leaves in spinning crowds down the street. Soft as a breath or wild as a gale, you come from nowhere you can point to and go where no one follows. No hand can catch you or hold you still - you are the sky on the move, the breath of the whole wide world.
