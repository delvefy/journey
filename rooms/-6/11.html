Tingle

You are the light fizzy prickling on the skin - the pins-and-needles in a foot gone to sleep, the buzz of cold hands warming by the fire, the shiver of excitement before a treat. No hand makes you; the nerves wake up and fizz. A thousand tiny pinpricks, half-pleasant, half-strange.

North

The shivery rush of pure excitement -
the rollercoaster's drop,
the moment before the present's opened -
a buzz you want to keep, not stop.

East

Sharing your sweets, helping a friend,
a gentle hand, a friendly word;
treating others as you'd want -
the nicest thing you've heard.

South

A little bite of cold that creeps
in through an open door;
not freezing yet, but cool enough
to want your coat once more.

West

When your eyes go misty and the lesson fades
and your mind drifts off somewhere nice -
you're riding a dragon, you're eating cake -
a waking dream, in a trice.
