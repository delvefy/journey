Horizon

You are a horizon - the far-off line where the sky bends down to touch the land or the sea, the edge of as far as the eye can reach. No hand draws you; the curve of the round earth does. Ships sink below you and climb up over you; the sun rises across you and sets behind you. However far you walk, you keep your distance. Far and flat and forever out of reach, you are the line between here and the whole wide rest of the world.
