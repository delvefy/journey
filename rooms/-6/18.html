Hail

You are hail - little hard balls of ice that fall rattling from a stormy sky, bouncing off the road and the car roof with a clatter. No hand makes you; the cold high clouds do, freezing the rain and flinging it down. You can be caught and held a moment, but you melt; you are weather more than thing, the sky in a sudden bad temper. You drum on the window, you whiten the grass, and as quick as you came you are gone.
