Tomorrow

You are the day that has not come yet, always one sleep away. No hand can hold you, and you can never quite be reached: each time the sun comes up, you have slipped ahead again and become today. You are where the promises live - I'll do it tomorrow - and the hopes, and the things put off. Children can hardly wait for you when a treat is coming. Forever just ahead, you are the bright unspent day that keeps the future full.

North

The coming dark of this same day,
when the sun has dropped from sight;
I'll see you then, the friends all say -
it means this very night.

East

Sixty seconds tick away
to make one of me whole;
the long hand jumps from notch to notch -
I'm time's small steady toll.

South

Seven days laid in a row,
from Monday round to Sunday;
five for school and two for play,
then back again on Monday.

West

Still and quiet, peaceful, hushed,
no storm and not a breeze;
the settled hush when all is well -
a state of perfect ease.
