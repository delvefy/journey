Moon

You are the moon - the pale silver lamp that hangs in the night sky, waxing fat and round then thinning to a sliver. You give no light of your own; you only catch the sun's and pass it down to the sleeping world. You pull the great seas back and forth, and wolves and dogs sing up at you. A man once walked upon your dusty face. You are the night's quiet companion, the cool round eye that watches over the dark.
