Maple

You are a maple - the broad shady tree whose leaf has five points like a spread hand, and turns to fire-red and gold each autumn. Your seeds twirl down like little helicopters, spinning on the wind. In the cold lands they tap your trunk and boil your sap to syrup for the breakfast pancakes. Your leaf is stitched on a country's flag. You are the tree of bright autumn and sweet sap, the one whose falling leaves children most love to catch.
