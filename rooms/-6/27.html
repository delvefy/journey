haze

Haze is the air gone soft and pale, the day seen through a thin grey veil. On a hot afternoon the far hills blur and the sun turns to a smudge of light; nothing is sharp, nothing is hidden, everything is gently dimmed. You cannot catch it or wipe it away - drive toward it and it stays always just ahead. Natural and weightless, it is distance made visible, the sky breathing warm over the land.

North

Take off your glasses, look about -
the sharp world melts away;
no edge is clear, all soft and smeared,
a fuzzy, swimming grey.

East

Join the stars with lines of thought
and pictures start to form;
a hunter, bear, or scorpion -
a star-shape, named and drawn.

South

Against the evening's glowing red
the city draws its jagged edge;
towers, roofs and chimney-tops -
where heaven meets the buildings' ledge.

West

Sweet or sour, salt or bitter,
your tongue can tell them all;
the flavour of a thing you eat -
one sense you know it well.
