Wood

You are wood - the hard brown stuff a tree is made of, the most useful thing the forest gives. People saw you into planks, carve you into spoons, burn you for warmth, and build whole houses out of you. You smell sweet when freshly cut and grow grey and grainy with age. You are natural, grown slow ring by ring, yet you become a thousand made things in a carpenter's hands. You are the bones of the forest, turned to roofs and chairs and toys.
