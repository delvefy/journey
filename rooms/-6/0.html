Ice

Water gone hard and clear with cold — slick underfoot on the winter pond, hung in spears from the eaves, cracking when you step. Ice is wholly natural and wholly solid, the freeze made into stone. It keeps the cold west, due along the natural axis. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

Thick gloom you cannot see across,
where fog and smoke and shadow merge;
all shapes are swallowed, blurred and dim —
name the blinding dark. Now urge.

East

Soft white flakes drift down from cloud,
I blanket the hill and hush the town;
roll me up to build a man —
name the winter white, falling down.

South

A soft dull stone in layered beds,
pressed mud gone brittle, grey and dun;
it splits and crumbles in the hand —
name the flaking rock. Name one.

West

Half rain, half snow, that stings the face,
it rattles cold on the windowpane;
not soft like snow, not clean like rain —
name the icy, slushy rain.
