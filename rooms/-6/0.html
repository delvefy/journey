The Waste

East of the road's end is the Waste itself, and there is no end to it. The trackless ground a step east still remembered it had once been a road; here even that is gone, and what remains is the pure pole of the forgotten: a flat grey plain to every horizon, lit by the same sourceless even daylight, with nothing on it, nothing in it, nothing under it — the place where magic came to be forgotten, and succeeded, and then forgot even that there had been anything to forget. Nothing answers here. Nothing recalls that answering was ever a thing the world did.

You call — not a word, you have learned better than to spend words on the Waste, only the old wish to be answered — and the grey takes the sound flat and gives back nothing, not even an echo, for an echo is a kind of answer and the Waste has forgotten how. You draw the spiral in the dust and the daylight shows it plainly, perfectly, and that is the cruelty of the pole: not that the mark is taken, but that it stays, clear and meaningless, in a place that will never send a single thing to read it. Only the whorl in your skin keeps any warmth, the one unforgotten mark in all the forgotten grey.

There is no count. The wind that crossed the lesser Waste has nothing to cross here and has given up; there is no bell, no drip, no voice, not even the silence-shaped-like-a-sound the ruins kept. Only your heart, beating plainly in the flat grey light, unanswered and unrecalled, the one thing the Waste cannot forget because it never knew it was there.

To the north the grey meets the made world's farthest dead reach, rust and ore strewn in the Waste, the Chrome's edge. To the east the plain gathers toward the road's end, a track that at least once went. To the south the grey sinks toward the older cold and the dark beneath, the Waste tilting into night. To the west the pole absolute, where even the Waste forgets itself, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name. The Waste forgot that names were ever a thing the world had; it does not refuse yours, it has nowhere left to keep refusal — and you cross the flat grey carrying the one warm whorl it cannot forget, into a forgetting with no horizon and no end.
