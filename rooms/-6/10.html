Chill

You are the small creeping cold that slips in unasked - the draught under the door, the bite of an autumn dusk, the cool that follows the sun behind a cloud. No hand makes you; the falling warmth lets you in. Not the deep freeze of frost, just enough to raise a goosebump and send you for a jumper.

North

A light fizzy prickling on the skin -
pins and needles in your foot,
or the buzz when cold hands warm again -
a thousand tiny pinpricks put.

East

The warm glow when you've done your best,
chin up and shoulders tall;
the medal won, the picture praised -
you stand the proudest of all.

South

Not last night, gone, nor nights ahead,
but this one drawing near;
the stars that come when this day's done -
the night that's almost here.

West

A small quiet thing inside your head,
a notion drifting through;
'I think...' you start, and there it is -
a flash of mind, brand new.
