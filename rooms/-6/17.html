Dew

You are dew - the scatter of tiny water-beads that gathers on the cool grass while the world sleeps. You hang sparkling on every blade and spider-web at dawn, soaking the toes of early walkers, catching the first sun in a thousand tiny lights. No cloud drops you; you simply appear, breathed out by the night air. Made by no hand, gone by mid-morning, you are the quiet wet jewels the morning lays out and the sun gently takes away.
