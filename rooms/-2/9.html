Flash

You are a sudden burst of light, gone before the eye can fix you - the lightning's white crack, the glint off a wave, the camera's quick blink. No hand holds you; you are over in less than a heartbeat, and you leave the dark darker behind. You light the whole room for an instant and are gone, and the thunder comes lumbering after, too slow to catch you. You are brightness with no body, the briefest word that light can say.

North

A soft and steady shine of light
on gold or polished tin;
not a burst but a low bright look
that draws the eye right in.

East

Above the sky where stars are strung,
the dark and endless deep;
no air, no sound, no up nor down,
where moon and planets keep.

South

I eat the wood and breathe out smoke,
I'm hot and gold and bright;
I warm your hands, I cook your bread,
and dance into the night.

West

What the little star will do
up in the night so high;
a tiny on-and-off of light
that winks across the sky.
