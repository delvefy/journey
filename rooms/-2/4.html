The Unread Light

West of the cold wire and north of the unwatched works, the making sheds the last of its iron. South of here a hall of engines still turned, belt and cam and wheel; east of here the record still raced on copper. Here both are gone to light. The works have thinned past wire into a cold standing glow, ranks of it hung in the sourceless white, an engine of pure signal that thinks without a head and keeps without a hand — no iron left to rust, no paper left to yellow, only the record running as light, filing itself round and round for no one who was ever going to read it.

And it is failing. Not breaking — greying. The cold light holds its even rank but dims by the hair, here and there a line of it guttering toward the long silence, the sending itself beginning to forget how to send. The record of everything flung through here greys as it goes, the bright word thinning to a smear of grey and then to less, on its slow way to the Waste that even cold records come to. There is no eye to mind the loss and so the loss is not minded; the made day simply lets a little more of itself go dark each round and keeps, fainter, on.

The coil is here, shed of its copper at last — a ring of cold light turning round on round, paying the record away into the dimming, the spiral guttering as it spins. Your name runs it still, struck in light a thousand times a second; but the light is failing, and each pass comes round greyer, a letter dropped, then a syllable, the name thinning toward the smear that all of it is going to. North the light sheds even this and stands as pure cold number at the pole, nothing in matter, no one to read. East it firms back to the racing wire, the record still sent. South it thickens to wire and then to iron, the unwatched hall minding itself. West the glow greys out the rest of the way, the sending forgotten, the cold made day gone to the long Waste silence. You were going somewhere, under a name. It is here in the cold light, fading a letter at a time, and there is no one left to catch it before it goes.
