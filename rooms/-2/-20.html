Weed

You are a weed - a wild rough plant that grows wherever it pleases, asked for by no one: up through the path cracks, all over the flower bed, choking the neat rows. No hand planted you; you simply sprang up, tough and stubborn, and you come straight back however hard you are pulled. You can be yanked, dug, sprayed, and still you win. One gardener's weed is another's wildflower. You are nature refusing to be tidied, green and unbidden and everywhere.
