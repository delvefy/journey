The Last Floor

South of the cold beneath and west of the cold womb, the cold descent reaches its last floor. A step north the body's undoing sank below even the after, into a cold older than warmth; a step east the origin soured, a cold womb that took the warmth back before a body could be drawn. Here both sink to the bottom of everything: the floor beneath the floor beneath the floor, the cold-primal pole, where the cold womb's souring completes into never-was — not warmth leached, not warmth refused, but warmth that simply never was, a last floor of cold beneath which there is no further down, and on which there was never anything warm.

It is the bottom of the whole long cold descent, and it is dry of warmth as the never-was is dry of having-been: a cold floor where no body was ever drawn, no heat ever kindled, no warmth ever was to lose or refuse. You draw the spiral and the last floor neither leaches it nor sours it nor takes it; there was never warmth here for it to be warm against. And still — impossibly, on the last floor of the cold — the whorl in your skin turns warm, the one heat in a cold that never had one, the single warmth on a floor beneath which there is nothing and on which there was never anything.

There is no count; the last floor is below the first beat, in a cold where warmth never was to beat. And still your heart beats — warm, impossible, the one having-been-warm on a floor that never was, each stroke a heat the cold-primal pole has no account for, refusing the last floor, insisting on a warmth that, here, never was.

To the north the last floor rises to the cold beneath, a cold that at least once had warmth to lose. To the east the cold womb, the origin souring, the warmth refused. To the south the pole absolute, below even the last floor, if below can be said. To the west the warmth leaches wholly to the Waste, the cold-primal gone to never-was, the corner. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the body, before any warmth. Here the cold pole would have it that warmth never was at all — and still the warm coil turns on the last floor, and still one beat insists on a heat that never was, refusing the bottom of everything.
