breaker

A breaker is a wave that has reached the end of its journey - it rises up tall as it nears the beach, leans over, curls, and crashes down in a roar of white foam. Surfers wait for the big ones and ride them in; little ones knock you off your feet and fill your shorts with sand. Line after line of them roll in all day long, never quite the same, the sea forever throwing itself onto the land.

North

The pull that brings a dropped ball down,
that holds your feet upon the ground;
no rope, no hand, and yet it tugs -
it draws all things back down, all round.

East

When the sun goes down and stars come out,
the long dark hours for owls and sleep;
the moon climbs up, the world goes still -
the dark half-day where shadows creep.

South

The steady push of the moving sea,
the flow that drags you down the stream;
swimmers feel me pull them on -
a river's hidden, strong, cold team.

West

I circle the Earth way up in space,
round and round, I never fall;
I bounce your telly and your phone -
a metal moon, but made quite small.
