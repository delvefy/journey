The Cold Beneath

South of the after and west of the old cold, the undoing goes below its own end. A step north the body's last remains had settled, cold and done, at the floor of the after; a step east the warmth sank still leaching into a cold older than warmth. Here even the after is undercut: the cold remains themselves are gone, the body undone absolute, sunk past the done into the floor beneath the floor — the cold beneath, where there is no body to settle and no after to settle in, only the old cold gone to its pole, the warmth wholly given back, the deep below even the deepest rest.

There is less here than the after held, and the after held almost nothing. No remains, no settled cold shape, no done thing to have been; the body is undone past its own ending, and the cold reaches under even that. You draw the spiral and there is no cold remains to keep it as the after kept it, faint; the gesture sinks below keeping, into the cold beneath. And still — this is the old refusal, worn thin as it has ever been — the whorl in your skin turns, the one warmth the cold beneath has not reached, the last unceded coil below the floor beneath the floor.

The count is below the after's last far-spaced strokes. Your heart beats once into the cold beneath, and the cold takes the warmth of it and gives nothing, and a vast cold while later beats again — not stopped, never the drama of stopping, only sunk past the after into the floor beneath the floor, one warm stroke refusing the cold pole at the very bottom of the undoing.

To the north the cold beneath rises to the after, the cold remains at least settled and kept. To the east the old cold, the warmth still leaching, the body still warm enough to lose. To the south it sinks toward the pole absolute, below the cold beneath, the floor beneath the floor beneath the floor. To the west the body dissolves wholly to cold and Waste, the undoing past even the cold, the corner. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the body is undone below its own ending and the cold has taken all but the last — and still the warm coil turns below the after, and still one stroke keeps on in the cold beneath, refusing, refusing, refusing the pole.
