Cloud

You are a cloud - a great soft heap of mist hung high in the sky. You drift white and round on a fine day, or pile up grey and heavy when rain is coming; you hide the sun, you let it through, you cast a sliding shadow on the hills below. Children lie on their backs and find dragons and sheep in you. Made by no hand and far too high to hold, you are the sky's own breath, sailing slowly nowhere.

all grey and cold and wet;

South

round bins and smelly cheese;
you hold your nose and run away -
a ___ upon the breeze.

West

it furs the grass and glass;
it crunches cold beneath your boots -
the ___ that will not last.
