Daffodil

You are a bright yellow flower of early spring, a trumpet in the middle of a star of petals, on a tall green stem. No hand makes you; you sleep all winter as a brown bulb, then push up and trumpet the spring. You nod in the breeze in great gold drifts on the verges and the parks. First of the year.

North

A bright red flower, papery-thin,
with a dark heart full of seed;
it nods in fields of golden wheat -
red among the weed.

East

A round fruit named for its own colour,
sweet and full of juice;
you peel its dimpled skin in bits -
the segments come loose.

South

A little shiny yellow flower
that grows low in the grass;
hold it beneath a friend's chin -
do you like butter? Let's ask.

West

A long curved fruit in a thick yellow skin
that you peel back in three;
soft and sweet and white inside -
the monkey's favourite tea.
