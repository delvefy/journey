Poppy

You are a bright red flower with papery, crumpled petals and a dark seed-heart at your centre. No hand makes you; you spring up wild and scarlet in the fields of golden wheat, by roadsides, in waste ground. Your petals fall after a day. Your seeds shake out by the thousand to sleep till spring. Red as blood.

North

A smooth bright cup of colour on a stem
that opens up in spring;
red and yellow, tall and neat -
from a bulb it rises in.

East

A soft round fruit with fuzzy skin,
gold-pink and sweet to bite;
a big hard stone sits in the middle -
its juice a sticky delight.

South

A bright yellow flower of early spring
with a trumpet in its heart;
it bobs in the breeze on a tall green stem -
from a bulb it gets its start.

West

A little horse, both small and stout,
with short strong legs and a shaggy mane;
just right for a child to learn to ride -
it trots along the lane.
