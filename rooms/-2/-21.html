Dandelion

You are a dandelion - the cheerful yellow flower that springs up in every lawn, no matter how often it is pulled. When your bloom is done you turn to a soft round ball of white fluff, and a child blows you apart to scatter your seeds and make a wish on the wind. You are natural, grown wild and free, a weed that nobody plants and everybody knows. You are the bright sunny face of summer grass, and then the floating wish that follows.
