Bluebell

You are a bluebell - the little flower shaped like a hanging bell, deep blue-violet, drooping shy on a slender green stem. In spring you come in your thousands, until the whole woodland floor swims in a misty blue and the air turns sweet. You nod when the breeze walks through, a wood full of silent bells. Pick you and you wilt in an hour. You are the colour of the spring shade itself, a bell that rings only to the eye.
