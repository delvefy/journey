Flutter

You are the quick soft trembling of light things in the air - the moth round the lamp, the flag in the wind, the heart that skips when something thrills it, the leaves all shaking at once. No heavy thing makes you; the lightest touch of air does. Watch the butterfly: it never flies straight, it only flutters.

North

A horrible smell, a dreadful stink,
of bins and socks and drains;
you pinch your nose and back away -
when this in the air remains.

East

A tiny passing smell on the air,
here, then gone again;
of baking bread, of cut green grass -
you catch it now and then.

South

The soft fast hiss of a thing that sweeps,
a skirt, a tail, a cane;
it slices the air from side to side -
a whispering swipe again.

West

The shiny pale-grey gleam of coins,
the moon and a knight's bright sword;
the colour of tinsel and a fish's side -
a cold bright shining hoard.
