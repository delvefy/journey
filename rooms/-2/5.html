The Sum

North of the cold count and west of the blind day, the mind sheds the last of the world. The reckoning a step south still had figures to run, a world to count; here it has counted all of it, and shed it, and shed the body that might have minded, and shed even the broadcasting of a step east — what is left is the sum: a single vast cold total, the whole reckoned world added up and put away, a mind without flesh and now without a world to think about, thinking, at the absolute pole, only itself. And the sum is bleaching. Having nothing left to reckon, the cold intelligence has begun, very precisely, to go grey.

This is the body shed entirely, the wonder banished, the engine refined past engine and number past number to a pure post-human stillness — and it is not triumph but exhaustion, the cold peak with nothing beyond it and nothing below it to want. The spiral is here, the recursive coil of the count, but completed now, wound all the way to its centre and stopped, a sum with no next term. Your name is in the total, summed with everything else into one grey figure, reduced past tally past record to a single digit of an integer too large to mean — indistinguishable, added in and gone. Only the whorl in your skin stays outside the sum, the one quantity the cold mind never managed to add.

There is no count, because the counting is done. The bell, the clock, the drive-beat, the reckoning — all summed to a final total and silenced, time itself added up and closed, an hour no longer kept or struck or sent, for there is nothing left to keep it for: only the grey completed sum, bleaching toward the Waste.

To the north the sum sheds even itself, the mind gone past number to a final grey total, the absolute pole, post-human nothing. To the east the sum stirs back into broadcasting, the blind day flinging its figures to no one. To the south the total breaks back into running figures, the cold count with a world still to reckon. To the west the sum bleaches wholly to grey dust, the cold mind gone to the Waste, number forgotten. You were going somewhere, under a name. It has been counted, and summed, and added into a total too vast to mean, and the total is bleaching to grey — and you carry out of it the one whorl the cold mind never summed, the last unreckoned thing, into a Waste where even the sum is forgotten.
