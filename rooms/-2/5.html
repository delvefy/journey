Noon

The sun has climbed to the top of the sky and the shadows shrink to almost nothing — the bright middle of the day. Noon is a moment, wholly natural and impossible to hold, gone the instant it arrives. It keeps the high north-west, among the hours and lights of the sky. Speak the name at each wall to pass.

❦

four ways out · speak the name to pass

❦

North

The bright early hours after dawn,
when birds are loud and the day is new;
'good ___,' folk say with the rising sun —
the morning, short. Now name it true.

East

Not deep asleep, not quite awake,
the warm half-nap a soft chair makes;
a droning room, your eyelids fall —
name the light sleep that gently takes.

South

I break in the east as the night grows thin,
first grey light and the cocks that crow;
before the sun has climbed at all —
name the day's first glow. Now show.

West

A soft warm light without a flame,
the embers' red, the firefly's spark;
I shine but do not blaze or burn —
name the gentle light in the dark.
