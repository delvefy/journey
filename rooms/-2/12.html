Shine

You are the bright clean light a thing gives back when it is polished or lit - the gleam off well-buffed shoes, the moon's path on the sea, the sun on a clean window. No hand need make you, though a rub brings you out. You say a thing is clean, new, cared-for. Bright and clear and bouncing back.

North

The rushing sound of something fast -
a rocket, a slide, a gust;
'WHOOSH!' it goes as it flies right by -
a sweep of air and dust.

East

A wide bright shaft of shining light
the lighthouse throws at sea;
the torch, the searchlight, headlamp's glare -
one solid bar to see.

South

The wand's quick flick, the rabbit pulled
from an empty velvet hat;
spells and wonders, frogs to princes -
the impossible, like that.

West

A loud rude jumble of sound,
all clatter, bang, and din;
the building site, the crashing pots -
shut the door, keep it in.
