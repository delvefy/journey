Lavender

You are lavender - a tall spike of tiny purple flowers, growing in fragrant rows where the bees hum all summer long. Your scent is calm itself: folk dry you in little bags to lay among the sheets and tuck under pillows for sleep. No hand made you; the warm dry hillside did. Brush past and you release your smell into the air. You are the gentlest flower of all, the one that means rest, the soft purple haze that calms a busy mind.
