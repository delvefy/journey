Flood

You are a flood - when the river forgets its banks and the rain has nowhere left to go, and the whole low field turns to lake. No hand opens you; the sky and the swollen stream do, and roads become rivers and the doorstep drowns. You carry off the boots and the toys and the fence, brown and slow and everywhere. You are too much of a gentle thing all at once, water grown greedy and wide.
