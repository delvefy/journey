crocus

A crocus is one of the first flowers of the year, a little closed cup of purple or gold or white that pushes up through the cold hard ground while winter is still grumbling. They come in bright patches on the lawn and under the trees, opening wide when the sun is out and shutting tight when it hides. Small and brave and early, the crocus is the first quiet promise that spring is on its way.

North

A bright red flower with a black middle,
it grows in fields where the wheat once stood;
we wear one for the soldiers brave -
a paper bloom for the brave and good.

East

A yellow flower of early spring,
a trumpet on a long green stalk;
first to nod when winter ends -
it lines the path along your walk.

South

A little black bug with buzzing wings,
it lands on your jam and walks on the wall;
shoo it away, it comes straight back -
it buzzes round the lunch and all.

West

A teeny tiny biting fly,
a cloud of us dance in the evening air;
so small you barely see me come -
one itchy bite, then I'm not there.
