Bulb

You are a bulb - a round fat papery root, plump with a sleeping flower folded up inside. A gardener pushes you down into the cold autumn soil and forgets you all winter; then one bright spring morning up shoots a green spear, and out comes a tulip or a daffodil. Grown by no hand, patient in the dark, you are a flower's secret store cupboard - everything it needs to bloom, packed tight and buried, waiting for the warm.
