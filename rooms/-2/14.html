Swish

You are the soft fast hiss of something cutting the air - the swung skirt, the horse's tail, the cane that slices side to side, the curtain drawn in one quick sweep. No hand alone makes you; movement does. Light and quick and gone. Wave me through the air and listen: that whisper is the sound of going fast.

North

The quick soft flap of little wings,
the flag up in the breeze;
the moth around the lantern's glow,
the trembling of the leaves.

East

A faint and trembling little light,
the candle nearly out;
the far dim star, the last red coal -
a weak and wavering doubt.

South

The rushing sound of a thing gone fast,
the rocket, the swung bat;
air pushed aside in a sudden sweep -
a quick loud blast, like that.

West

The wobbling shine on summer roads,
on water in the sun;
a trembling, watery, glinting light -
heat-haze on the run.
