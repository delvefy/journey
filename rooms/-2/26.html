Tide

You are the tide - the slow breathing of the whole sea, creeping up the beach all morning and sliding back all afternoon. Twice a day you come in and twice you go out, pulled by the faraway moon. You cover the rock pools, float the boats, then leave wet sand and stranded shells behind. Patient and certain, you keep your timetable for ever, and the people who live by the sea learn your hours by heart and plan their days around your coming and going.
