Rain

You are rain - the water that falls from the heavy grey clouds, drop after drop after drop. You drum on the window, fill the gutters, swell the puddles for splashing, and make everything smell green and fresh. You can drizzle soft and grey or hammer down in a storm. A child watches you race down the glass in wriggling lines. Made by no hand, you are the sky giving the thirsty earth a long cool drink.
