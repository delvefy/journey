Root

You are a root - the hidden half of every plant, reaching down into the dark soil while the leaves climb to the light. You grip the earth so the storm cannot topple the tree; you drink the rain and the buried water and send it up the stem. No one sees you, knotted and pale below the ground, yet nothing green could stand a day without you. Grown by no hand, you are the plant's deep secret anchor.
