Wave

You are a wave - a long rolling ridge of water that races in from the deep, rears up green, and folds over to break white and hissing on the sand. No hand makes you; the wind and the far storms do, passing their push along the sea. You lift the swimmer, knock down the toddler, and drag the bucket back out. Surfers wait all day for a good one. Restless and rhythmic and never quite the same twice, you are the sea forever rolling itself onto the land and sliding back for more.
