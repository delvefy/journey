Lily

You are a lily - a tall flower of broad waxy trumpets, white or orange or speckled, with a scent so sweet and heavy it fills a whole room. No hand makes you; a fat bulb in the cool earth does. Some of your cousins float flat on ponds, pads and all, for frogs to sit on. You stand for both weddings and quiet farewells. Stately and fragrant and faintly grand, you are the most elegant flower in the vase, lifting your trumpets up to be admired.
