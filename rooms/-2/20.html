Lightning

You are lightning - the jagged white-hot flash that splits the storm-sky in a blink, brighter than day for a heartbeat, then gone. No hand makes you; the storm clouds do, rubbing up such a charge that it leaps to the ground in a crackling fork. You cannot be touched - and woe to whatever you strike. You come first, then thunder follows, and the gap between tells how far off you are. You are the sky's sudden flashbulb, the fastest, fiercest light there is.
