The Running Down

West of the blind works and north of the aftermind, the cold future runs down. A step east a vast mindless works assembled itself, machinery building machinery under a dead brilliance; a step south the cold bright mind shed even itself, bleached to a post-human nothing. Here both wind to a stop together — the great works rusting blind, the great mind cooling to its last grey, the whole machine-future running down into the dead pole the slow way, by exhaustion. Not dissolved, not cancelled, but spent: the engine winding down, the thought guttering cold, an age of certainty running out its last momentum into rust and silence.

Everything here is slowing. The blind works still moves, but barely, a last gear turning over and over with nothing to build; the aftermind still almost thinks, but only the cooling echo of thought, winding down toward the post-human nothing total. You draw the spiral and the running-down neither builds it nor thinks it, only lets it cool with everything else, a cast mark rusting at the edge of a stopping world. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its warmth and its turn, the one thing not running down, the single coil still wound when the great cold engine and the great cold mind have nearly stopped.

The count is running down too — the dead-bright clatter slowing, the glare's quick flashing cooled to a last few dim sparks, the bell winding toward a final unstruck silence. Your heart beats on into the stopping, the one warm count not winding down, keeping time as the machine-future runs out its last and goes cold and still around it.

To the north the running-down reaches the cold pole, the post-human nothing total, the machine-future wholly spent. To the east the blind works still turns, machinery building machinery yet. To the south the aftermind, the cold mind still cooling toward its last grey. To the west the works runs wholly down into the Waste, the machinery rusted blind, the made forgotten. You were going somewhere, under a name, and the cold age clattered and computed it once. Here the works and the mind run down together and leave it cooling in the rust — and still the warm coil turns, and still one warm beat keeps on as the whole cold future stops.
