Snowdrop

You are a snowdrop - a tiny white flower on a thin green stem, your head bowed down like a little hanging bell. You are the bravest of all, pushing up through the cold hard ground while frost still grips the garden and nothing else dares bloom. In drifts beneath bare trees you nod in the winter wind. Small and pale and hopeful, you are the very first sign that the long cold is ending and that spring, somewhere ahead, is on its way.
