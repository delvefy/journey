Grass

You are grass - the soft green carpet of thin blades that covers the field and the lawn and the hill. No hand made you; you grow everywhere by yourself, springing back up however much you are trodden or mown. You can be sat on, rolled in, pulled up by the handful, chewed by a cow. You hide the worms and the daisies and the lost ball. Common as anything and quietly everywhere, you are the green skin of the whole patient earth.
