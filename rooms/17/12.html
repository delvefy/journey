Bob

You are a bob - the quick light up-and-down a thing does on the water without ever going under. A cork does you on the end of a line; a duck does you on the pond; an apple does you in the tub at a party. Your head does you too, a small nod of yes or hello. You are a motion, not a thing, all tip and rise and tip again, a cheerful little dance on top of whatever holds you up.
