Sandal

You are a sandal - the lightest of shoes, no more than a flat sole and a few straps to hold it on, made for hot days and the beach. A hand cut and stitched you; a hand buckles you over a bare foot so the toes peek out and the breeze gets in. You can be held, slapped on the path, kicked off at the door, lost in the sand. Older than the boot by far, you are summer for the feet, the freedom of going almost barefoot.
