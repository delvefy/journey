Song

You are a song - words and music joined, made to be sung out loud. You have a tune to carry you and a chorus that everyone knows, and you live in the mouths of people walking, washing up, marching, falling asleep. You can make a whole room hum, set feet tapping, bring a tear or a grin. No hand can hold you, for you are only sound and meaning braided - here while it is sung, then humming on in the head long after.

and April, and the rest?
