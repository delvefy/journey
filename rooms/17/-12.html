Accordion

You are an accordion - the squeezebox a player hugs and pushes and pulls while the fingers dance on keys and buttons down each side. Squeeze you in, draw you out, and the bellows breathe a wheezy, jolly tune. You are made by hand, all wood and pearl buttons and folded cloth, heavy in the arms but full of music. You are a whole little band a person can carry, the cheerful heart of a dance in the square.
