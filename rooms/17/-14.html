Plate

You are a plate - the round flat dish that holds your dinner, white and smooth and waiting. The roast, the peas, the pudding all sit on you; you are wiped clean with a crust of bread and stacked in the cupboard. Smash one and it shatters into a hundred shards. Spin one on a stick and the circus crowd gasps. You are the everyday stage for every meal, the patient round you eat from three times a day, asking only to be washed when you are done.
