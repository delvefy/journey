Brick

You are a brick - a hard red block of baked clay, the size to sit in a hand. Lay me in a row, dab on the cement, lay another row on top, and up the wall climbs, brick by brick, until a whole house stands. A child stacks toy ones into towers and knocks them down again. Moulded and fired by a hand, plain and heavy and true, you are the patient little block that whole great buildings are built from, one at a time.
