Rip

You are a rip - the quick rough tearing of a thing in two. Grab the paper, pull it hard, and r-r-rip, it parts in a ragged line; the sleeve snags a nail, the seam gives way, the wrapping comes off the present all at once. You are loud and sudden and just a little bit naughty - the undoing of something whole. No hand can hold you, for you are only the act of the tearing: here in a heartbeat, and a thing made two.
