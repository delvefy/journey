Saw

You are a saw - a long flat blade with a row of sharp teeth, made to bite through a plank with a back-and-forth song. A hand grips your handle and works you to and fro, and the sawdust falls in a soft pale heap below. You screech a little as you go. Patient and rough and toothy, you turn one long board into two, doing slowly and surely the one thing no hand could ever do alone: cut clean through solid wood.
