Magnet

You are a magnet - the small piece of metal with an invisible pull. Hold you near a pin and it leaps to you; stick you to the fridge and you cling without a nail. Your two ends are choosy: one snaps tight to another magnet, flip it round and it shoves away. Children chase iron filings across paper with you and gasp at the trick. You hold notes on the fridge door and the compass true to north. You are a quiet, unseen grip in a lump of grey metal.
