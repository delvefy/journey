Nut

You are a nut - not the kind you crack and eat, but the little six-sided ring of metal with a threaded hole, made to screw onto a bolt and lock two things together tight. A spanner grips your flat sides and turns you down hard. You hold the bicycle, the swing, the whole machine together. Lose one and something wobbles loose. Small and hard and hexagonal, you are the humble fastener that, with its bolt, holds half the made world snugly in one piece.
