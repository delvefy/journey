Flop

You are a flop - the way a tired body drops down loose and heavy, all the stiffness gone out of it. After the long hot day you flop on the bed, arms flung wide; the fish flops on the deck; the rag doll flops in the chair. You are not a thing to hold but a way of falling: soft, sudden, and boneless. No grace in you, just relief. You are the body giving up the fight against gravity, landing in a happy heap.
