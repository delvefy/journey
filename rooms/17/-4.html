Coin

You are a coin - a little disc of metal money, cool and heavy in the hand. A head on one side, a tail on the other, an edge that catches the light. You chink into the piggy bank, buy the gum, spin on the table and rattle slowly down, roll away under the sofa to wait for years. Pressed by the thousand in a mint, small and bright and easily lost, you are money you can hold between two fingers.
