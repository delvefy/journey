Droop

You are a droop - the slow tired sag of something with the bounce gone out of it, the eyelids at bedtime, the flag on a windless day, the picked flower an hour later. No one means to make you; weariness and want and gravity do. You bend things gently downward until they hang. Half a sag and half a sigh, you are the shape of being tired or thirsty or sad, the way a happy upright thing slowly gives in and points its tips at the floor.
