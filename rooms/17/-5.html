Knee

You are a knee - the clever hinge halfway down the leg. You bend so a child can crouch, climb, kneel, and spring; you fold to let them sit and straighten to let them stand. You are the first to meet the playground when they trip, the first to wear a plaster, the place a smaller one is bounced upon for 'horsey'. Knobbly and bare below the shorts, you are the joint that turns a stiff straight leg into one that runs and jumps.
