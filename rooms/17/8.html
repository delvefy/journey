Jump

You are a jump - the push off the ground that sends you flying up. Bend the knees, swing the arms, and up you spring, both feet off the floor for one weightless second before you land. You leap the puddle, you jump for joy, you bounce on the bed when no one's looking. No hand can hold you down - you are the body beating gravity for a heartbeat, the happy little flight up and the soft thump back to earth.
