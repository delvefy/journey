Paddle

You are a paddle - the happy splashing-about a child does in the shallows with trousers rolled up and bare feet in the cold sea. You are also the short flat oar that pushes a little boat or a canoe along, dipped and pulled, dipped and pulled. Either way you live where the water is and the wading is good. You make a splash and a shiver and a squeal of delight. You are the gentlest play with water, half a wade and half a stroke, and always a little bit wet.
