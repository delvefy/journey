Roll

You are a roll - the turning of a round thing over and over along the ground. The marble rolls under the chair, the ball rolls down the hill, the barrel rolls along the deck, the dog rolls over in the grass for a tummy rub. Round and round you go, never quite stopping where you mean to. No hand can hold you, for you are only the going - the long happy tumble of anything round set loose on a slope.
