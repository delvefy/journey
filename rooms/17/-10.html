Post

You are a post - a stout upright stake driven into the ground to hold something up: a fence, a sign, a gate, a goal. A hand cut you and a hand sank you deep, and there you stand, firm and patient, in rain and sun. You can be leaned on, nailed to, tied to, painted with a number. The horse is hitched to you; the letter drops into your cousin the postbox. Sturdy and still, you are the thing that does not move, so that other things can hang on.
