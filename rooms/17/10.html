Day

You are a day - the stretch of time from when the sun comes up to when you fall asleep, full of breakfast and school and play and tea. No hand made you; the spinning earth did, turning your patch of the world to face the sun and away again. You cannot be touched, only spent. Seven of you make a week; a good one flies, a dull one drags. Sunday, Monday, birthday, snow-day - you are the single bright bead of time a child lives one at a time.
