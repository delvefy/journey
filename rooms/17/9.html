Year

You are a year - a great wheel of time, three hundred and sixty-five days long, that turns the seasons all the way round and brings you back to your birthday. No hand made you, though clocks and calendars count you; you are how the earth measures one trip around the sun. You cannot be touched, only lived through. Spring, summer, autumn, winter, and round again - you hold all the holidays and growings-up inside you. You are the biggest piece of time a child really feels.
