spring

A spring is a coil of wire that wants to be its own shape. Squash it and it shoves back; stretch it and it snaps in; let it go and it springs away across the floor. It lives inside the clicky pen, the bouncy bed, the wind-up toy, storing a push to give back later. Cold, coiled, and wholly a made thing you can hold, it is metal taught to bounce - a small wound-up bundle of patience, always ready to leap.

North

A long stiff bar that rests on a block,
press down upon one end;
the far end heaves a heavy load -
it makes your strength extend.

East

Two long arms joined up at the top,
I pinch and grab and lift;
I turn the sausages on the grill -
no burnt fingers, that's my gift.

South

Flick me up to make it light,
flick me down for dark;
upon the wall beside the door,
I turn on every spark.

West

Two glass bulbs joined at a narrow waist,
with sand that trickles through;
turn me over, time begins -
when I'm empty, time is due.
