Tune

You are a tune - the line of notes that climbs and dips and makes a melody. You are the part of a song you can hum without the words, the jingle stuck in everyone's head, the few notes that say 'happy birthday' before a word is sung. You live in whistles and music boxes and the kettle's rising note. No hand can catch you, yet once you are loose in a mind you will not leave - you simply go round, and round, and round.
