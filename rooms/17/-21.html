Lever

You are a lever - a long stiff bar that turns on a point, so that a push on one end lifts a great weight on the other. Slide me under a heavy rock, press down hard, and up it heaves. A seesaw is a lever; so is the handle that works a machine. Simple and strong and wonderfully clever, you are the oldest trick for moving heavy things, letting one small push do the work of many hands by trading a long swing for a mighty lift.
