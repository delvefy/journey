Slither

You are a slither - the smooth winding way a snake moves with no legs at all. You press the ground in long sideways waves and pour yourself forward, silent over leaves and stone. An eel does you through water; a wet bar of soap does you out of a hand. There is something secret and quick about you, a movement that makes the skin prickle. You are gliding without feet, a body flowing along the ground like spilled water.

onto the comfy bed;
a fish out of water does it too -
to ___, all limp, instead.

East

You wriggle and twist about in your seat,
unable to sit still;
the worm does it upon the hook -
to ___ against your will.

South

Quick tiny steps to hurry off,
the way a mouse will go;
it scuttles fast across the floor -
to ___ to and fro.

West

You climb up quick on hands and knees
over the rocks and wall;
a mad rush up the climbing frame -
to ___ before you fall.
