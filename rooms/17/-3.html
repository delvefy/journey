Pan

You are a pan - a flat round dish of metal with a long handle to grab. You sit on the hot ring and sizzle: eggs spitting, bacon curling, pancakes browning at your edges. A flick of the wrist and the pancake flips; a scrape of the spoon and breakfast is served. Hammered and pressed by a hand, hot and heavy and a little battered, you are the friend of every hungry morning, turning raw food warm and good.
