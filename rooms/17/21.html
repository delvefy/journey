Squish

You are a squish - the soft wet squash of something squidgy giving way under a press. A ripe tomato squishes in your fist; mud squishes between bare toes; a beanbag squishes when you flop on it. There is a sound to you, a soft sucking squelch, and a feeling, all squidgy and yielding. Soft and gooey and oddly pleasing, you are what happens to anything soft enough when it is pressed, oozing out around the edges and never quite springing back to the shape it was before.
