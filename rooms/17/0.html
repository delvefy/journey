Hammer

You are the hammer - a heavy head of iron on a wooden handle, made to strike. Swing me down and I drive the nail home in three sharp blows; turn me round and my claw pulls the bent ones out. I build the house and hang the picture, I crack the nut and tap the chisel along. All my work is in the blow: lift, swing, strike. Blunt, simple, and strong.

East

A sharp wide blade upon a handle,
you swing it hard to chop;
split the logs for the winter fire -
down it comes, ker-chop!

South

A thin steel spike with a flat little head,
hammered into the wood;
it pins two planks together tight
and holds them like it should.

West

A tall steel tower on a building site,
with a long arm reaching high;
it hoists the heavy beams aloft
and swings them through the sky.
