Spanner

You are a spanner - a flat steel tool with a notch in the end, shaped to grip a nut or a bolt and turn it tight. The plumber and the mechanic reach for you first; you loosen the rusty and fasten the loose. Made by hands, hard and heavy, you come in a set of every size, racked in the toolbox. Thrown in the works, you stop a machine dead. You are leverage in the palm, the small strong tool that turns what fingers never could.
