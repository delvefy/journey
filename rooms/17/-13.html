Fiddle

You are a fiddle - the little wooden violin tucked under a chin, sawed and sung with a horsehair bow. At the barn dance you set the whole floor stamping; in a quiet room you can make a tune so sad it brings a tear. You are all curves and strings and a long thin neck, light as a bird in the hand. Four strings, a bow, and quick clever fingers are all you need. You are the merriest and the most mournful of voices, scraping out jigs and laments both.
