Crumple

You are a crumple - to crush a thing up in your fist until it folds into a hundred little creases. A spoiled drawing, a sweet wrapper, a letter you don't want to read: scrunch them small and they crumple. Paper does it best, with a crackly papery sound. A dropped tower of blocks crumples too, folding in on itself. Quick and crackly and a little bit cross, you take something smooth and flat and wad it into a creased and crinkled ball, ready for the bin.
