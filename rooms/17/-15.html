Needle

You are a needle - the thin sharp sliver of steel with an eye at one end, made for sewing. Thread me, and I dive in and out of the cloth, drawing the stitch behind, mending a hole or hemming a dress. The doctor's kind gives a quick sharp jab; the pine tree wears a million of me; the compass kind swings always to the north. Small and sharp and bright, you are the little spear that stitches the world's torn cloth together.
