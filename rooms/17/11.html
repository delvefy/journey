Tweet

You are a tweet - the bright little burst of sound a small bird makes, a quick high cheep flung out from a twig at dawn. No hand makes you; a tiny beak does, and a hedge full of you greets the morning. You cannot be touched, only heard - a single tweet, then a chorus, the whole garden waking up in song. The littlest birds tweet the loudest. You are a sparrow's whole say, a sound no bigger than the bird that makes it.
