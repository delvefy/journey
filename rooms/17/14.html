Hope

You are hope - the small warm light that keeps burning in the heart even when things look darkest. You are the feeling that tomorrow could be better, that the lost thing might be found, that the sick might mend. You cost nothing and weigh nothing, yet a person with you can bear almost anything, and a person without you can bear almost nothing. The sailor clings to you in the storm; the child holds you at the wishing-well. You are the quiet faith that good is still to come.
