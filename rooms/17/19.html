Wilt

You are a wilt - the sad slow droop of a flower or a leaf that has gone too long without a drink, the stem softening, the head bowing, the bright thing fading to limp. No hand makes you; thirst and a hot day do. A glass of water can sometimes bring you back; too long, and you cannot be saved. Quiet and drooping and a little bit forlorn, you are a plant's way of begging for a drink, the wilt that says water me, please, soon.
