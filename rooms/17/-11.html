Rail

You are a rail - a long smooth bar fixed in place to hold or to guide. A hand made you and bolted you down: the rail you grip beside the stairs, the rail of the fence you lean on, the two shining rails the train runs along for mile after mile. You can be held, slid down banister-style, balanced on. Straight and strong and going on and on, you are the firm line that says hold here, or stay this side, or this way to the train.
