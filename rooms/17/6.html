Spin

You are a spin - the fast round-and-round turning on a single spot. The top hums and blurs, the coin whirls on the table, the dancer turns till her skirt flies out, the child whirls with arms wide until the whole world tilts and they tumble down giggling. No hand can hold you for long, for you are giddy motion itself - round and round and round, faster and faster, until everything goes blurry and wonderful and dizzy.
