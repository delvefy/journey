Moo

You are a moo - the deep, slow, lowing call a cow lets out across the field. It is the very first animal sound a baby learns: the cow says moo. You roll out long and round and a little sad, drifting over the gate at milking time. You are warm and grassy and enormous, the whole barnyard's biggest voice, asking for hay or calling the calf back home.
