Floor

You are the floor - the flat solid bottom of every room, the one part of the house that holds everyone up. Boards, tiles, or carpet, you take every footstep, every dropped toy, every spill. Babies crawl across you, dogs sleep on you, and the toast lands butter-side down upon you. You are the lowest and the most trodden, and yet without you there would be nowhere at all to stand.
