Jiggle

You are a jiggle - the quick loose shaking of something soft and wobbly, the jelly on the plate, the tummy after a laugh, the knee that bounces under a desk. No one quite means you; a tap or a wobble or pure fidgets do. You shiver and bounce and wobble in place, going nowhere. The wobbliest little motion there is, you are what happens to anything soft and loose when you give it the smallest nudge.
