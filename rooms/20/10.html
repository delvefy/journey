Rest

You are rest - the pause from work and worry, when a tired body and mind get to stop and recover. You put your feet up after a long walk, lean back in a chair, let the muscles go soft. Even the busiest must have you, or they wear out and fall ill. The land lies fallow to rest; the music has its silent beats of rest. You are the sweet doing-of-nothing, the breather every weary thing must take.
