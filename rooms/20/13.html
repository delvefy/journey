Score

You are a score - the running count of who is winning, climbing on the board point by point. You are the goal that just went in, the run that just came home, the number everybody glances up to check. No hand holds you, yet hands win you, earn you, and chalk you up. You decide the cheers and the groans. You are the game kept honest in numbers, the answer to the only question that matters when the whistle blows: who's ahead?
