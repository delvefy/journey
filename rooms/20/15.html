Stroll

You are a stroll - the slow easy ambling walk you take when there is no hurry at all, hands in pockets, looking in windows, in no rush to arrive. No one teaches you; a lazy afternoon and a fine day do. You stop to pat the dog, to read the sign, to watch the river go by. The very opposite of a dash, you are walking for the pure pleasure of it, the gentlest, idlest, most contented way two feet can carry a person about.
