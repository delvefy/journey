shears

Shears are giant scissors, built for tough jobs that ordinary scissors would never manage - clipping the hedge into a neat green wall, snipping the thick wool off a sheep, trimming the long grass at the edge of the path. Two long blades, two big handles, and a satisfying heavy CHOMP as they close. You need both hands and a good squeeze. Where scissors give up, the shears bite straight through.

North

A long iron bar with a curved flat end,
shove it under and lever it up;
prise the lid off the heavy crate -
pop! with a heave and a tug.

East

A long-handled tool with a flat blade end,
chop the weeds and break the soil;
drag it through the vegetable patch -
a gardener's friend for the digging toil.

South

A rough flat tool you rub back and forth,
it smooths the edge of metal or nail;
scrape, scrape, scrape till it's nice and neat -
it wears the bumps off without fail.

West

A heavy tool with a sharp curved point,
swing it high and smash the ground;
miners use me to break the rock -
crack! the stone splits all around.
