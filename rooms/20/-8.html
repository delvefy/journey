Door

You are a door - the swinging slab of wood that stands between one room and the next, between the cold street and the warm hall. You have a handle to grip, a hinge to turn on, a lock to keep you shut. You can be slammed in anger or held open in welcome. Knock, and someone answers; close, and the world stays out. You are the made thing every wall needs to stop being only a wall.
