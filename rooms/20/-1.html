Peg

You are a peg - a small wooden or plastic pinch, made to grip and hold. A hand made you, and a hand clips you onto the washing line to keep the socks from blowing away, or hammers your bigger cousin into the ground to hold a tent. You can be held, snapped onto a nose, used to pin a drawing up. Simple as anything, just a spring and two little jaws, you are the cheap clever gadget that grips so you don't have to.
