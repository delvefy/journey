Broom

You are a broom - bristles bound to a long handle, the trusty tool for sweeping a floor. A hand made you and a hand pushes you, swish-swish, herding the dust and crumbs into a tidy heap by the door. You can be held, leaned in the cupboard, flown by a witch on Halloween. No plug, no batteries, just a willing arm and a bit of elbow grease. You are how a floor gets clean the old, simple way, one sweep after another.
