Gallop

You are a gallop - the fastest gait a horse can give, all four hooves leaving the ground at once. You are thunder made of legs, the drum of a charge across an open field. A rider leans low and the wind tears past; the world blurs to green and brown. You are not a thing but a wild headlong motion, manes flying, the ground eaten up in great leaping strides. You are speed with a heartbeat under it.
