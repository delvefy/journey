Train

You are a train - the long line of carriages that thunders down the iron rails behind a roaring engine. Chuff chuff you go, faster and faster, fields and stations blurring past the windows. Children wave from bridges as you pass and count your wagons end to end. You are made by many hands, of steel and smoke and clattering wheels, the biggest toy and the truest workhorse both. You carry whole crowds of people across the land while they sit and watch the world fly by.
