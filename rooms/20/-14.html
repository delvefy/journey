Kettle

You are a kettle - a fat metal pot with a spout and a handle, made to sit on the heat and boil the water for the tea. A hand fills you and flicks you on, and you grumble, then roar, then sing out loud when you are done. You hold a belly of bubbling water and pour it steaming into the pot. The first thing switched on in a chilly morning, you are the warm heart of every kitchen, whistling that comfort is on its way.
