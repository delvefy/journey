Plan

You are a plan - the steps worked out ahead of time for how a thing will be done: where to go, what to bring, who does what. No hand can hold you; a careful mind makes you, often scribbled on paper or a map. You cannot be touched, only followed - and the best ones have a plan B for when they go wrong. A battle plan, a building plan, a plan for the perfect den. You are thinking-it-through before you leap, the map a deed follows.
