Stretch

You are a stretch - the long lovely reach of a body pulling itself out as far as it will go. You stretch up tall to reach the top shelf, stretch out your arms with a yawn on waking, stretch your stiff legs after sitting too long. The cat does you best of all, spine arched and paws spread. Slow and satisfying and full of a little groan, you are the body unfolding itself, reaching and lengthening until every sleepy muscle wakes and tingles and is ready for the day.
