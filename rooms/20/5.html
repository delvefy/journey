Hug

You are a hug - two arms thrown right around someone and pulled in close. You say hello and goodbye, well done and never mind, all without a word. A bear gives the biggest; a granny gives the softest; a friend gives one when you cry. You are made between two people, given and taken at once. You cannot be kept in a box, only shared. The world is always a little warmer just after one of you.
