Chisel

You are a chisel - a flat sharp blade on a stout handle, made for carving. Set your edge against the wood, give the handle a tap with a mallet, and off curls a clean shaving. Carpenters cut neat joints with you; sculptors free statues from blocks of stone. You must be kept sharp and handled with care. Keen and patient and precise, you do the careful cutting that no saw could manage, shaping and smoothing and hollowing out, peeling away the wood a sliver at a time until the shape inside comes free.
