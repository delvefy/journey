Tremble

You are a tremble - the shaking that runs through a body when it is cold or frightened. Knees knock, hands shiver, a lip wobbles before the tears come. A puppy left out in the rain trembles; so does a child at the top of the high diving board. You cannot quite hold still; the shaking comes whether you want it or not. Small and helpless and hard to hide, you are the body giving away its fear or its chill, quivering all over until the fright or the cold has passed.
