Spring

You are a spring - the quick upward bound a body makes when it gathers and lets go. A cat springs at a fluttering string; a frog springs from the bank into the pond; you spring out of bed when the holiday comes. You are also the coiled wire under the chair that throws you back when you sit. You are the opposite of staying still, all gather-and-release. You are the leap of a thing that has had enough of the ground.

South

or fingers cracked in air;
a turtle's jaws shut fast on a fly -
a ___, and the twig's a pair.

West

both feet up off the ground;
