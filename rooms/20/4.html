Mix

You are a mix - the stirring-together of different things until they are one: flour and eggs and milk into a batter, paints into a new colour, friends into a happy muddle. A hand makes you, round and round with a spoon or a whisk. You cannot be held, only made - and once things are mixed they can be hard to part again. A mix-up is a muddle; a good mix is just right. You are many things becoming one, swirled until you cannot tell where each began.
