Cotton

You are cotton - the soft white fluff that grows in a puff on a bush in hot fields, picked and spun into thread and woven into the comfiest cloth there is. Hands card you and spin you and weave you into t-shirts and socks and sheets. You are the puffy ball that dabs a graze and the bud that cleans an ear. Light and breathable and everywhere, you are the plainest, friendliest fabric of all, worn close to the skin the whole world over.
