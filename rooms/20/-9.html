Shorts

You are shorts - the cut-off trousers that end at the knee, the gladdest clothes of summer. When the sun blazes you come out of the drawer, baring the legs to the breeze and the paddling pool. Footballers wear you, swimmers, holidaymakers; you have pockets for conkers and string. You are cooler than trousers and bolder than a swimsuit. You are the legs let free, the plain proof that the warm days have come at last and the heavy winter clothes are packed away.
