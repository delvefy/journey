Prank

You are a prank - the practical joke set up in secret and sprung with a laugh, the whoopee cushion on the chair, the fake spider in the lunchbox, the bucket balanced on the door. People plan you carefully and giggle while they wait. You are harmless mischief, all in fun, meant to make the victim jump and then laugh too. On the first of April you are everywhere. A trap with a grin behind it, you turn an ordinary day on its head.
