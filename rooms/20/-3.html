Bottle

You are a bottle - a vessel of glass or plastic with a narrow neck and a cap, made to hold a liquid and pour it out a little at a time. A hand blew or moulded you; a hand fills you with water, fizzy pop, ketchup, or a message for the sea. You can be held, shaken, uncapped with a pop, lined up empty for recycling. The narrow neck is your trick: tip you up and out comes a steady glug, never a flood. You are the keeper of every drink.
