Crowbar

You are a crowbar - a long heavy bar of iron with a flattened, curved end for prising. Jam the flat tip under a stuck lid, a nailed-down board, a heavy crate, and heave on the long end: with the lever's mighty help, it groans and gives and pops right open. Burglars in stories use you; so do builders and removal men. Hard and strong and full of leverage, you are brute force with a clever bend, prising apart what no bare hands could ever shift.
