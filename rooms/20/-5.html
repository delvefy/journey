Balloon

You are a balloon - a little skin of rubber you blow full of breath until you swell up round and light. Tie you off and you bob on a string; let you go and you whizz away with a rude squeal. You come in red and blue and yellow, and you crowd the ceiling at every party. You are wrought, made by a hand and filled by a puff. One small pin, one careless squeeze, and you are gone with a bang.
