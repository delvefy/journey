Rise

You are a rise - to go up, to climb and lift toward the sky. The sun rises in the morning, the bread rises in the warm oven, the balloon rises away into the blue, smoke rises from the chimney, and you rise from your chair when a grown-up comes in. Upward and lifting and full of hope, you are everything that goes up: the slow swell of dough, the float of a balloon, the steady climb of the morning sun over the hills.
