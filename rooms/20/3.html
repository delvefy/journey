Party

You are a party - a gathering of friends for fun: balloons and cake and games and music, all to mark a birthday or a win or just a happy day. No hand can hold you; many hands throw you, and the more the merrier. You cannot be touched, only joined in - pass-the-parcel, musical chairs, a great heap of presents. A party comes with hats and a wish blown out over candles. You are joy planned on purpose, a whole afternoon set aside for being glad together.
