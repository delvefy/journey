Lamp

You are a lamp - the light on a stand or a table that drives back the dark of evening. You wear a shade to soften your glare and a switch to wake or sleep you. A genie lives in a magic one, granting wishes when it is rubbed. You light the page for the late reader, the desk for the worker, the hall for the visitor. You hold no flame, just a glowing bulb, but you are the friendly light that makes a room feel like home.
