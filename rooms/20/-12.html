Maraca

You are a maraca - the hollow round shaker on a handle, filled with dried seeds or little beads. Give you a shake and you hiss and rattle, ssh-ssh-ssh, keeping the beat for the dancing. You come as a cheerful pair, one in each hand, and your sound is all sunshine and warm nights. A child makes a rough one from a tub of rice. You hold no tune, only a rattle, yet without you the band would lose its happy swing.
