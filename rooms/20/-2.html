Net

You are a net - a clever web of knotted string, all holes and yet able to catch. A hand knotted you, row on row, into a mesh that lets the water through but holds the fish: a fisherman's net, a butterfly net, the net at the back of the goal. You can be held, cast, mended where it tears, slung between two posts. Your whole trick is the gaps - too small to slip through, just big enough to be light. You are a basket made of almost nothing.
