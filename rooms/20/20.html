bulge

A bulge is a rounded lump that pushes out where something is too full to lie flat - the bulge of marbles in your trouser pocket, the bulge of a snake that swallowed something far too big, the bulge in a bag stuffed past its zip. It strains and rounds and threatens to burst. Press it and it gives a little, then pushes back. Wherever there is more inside than the shape can hold, out comes a bulge.

North

Get taller, bigger, day by day,
a seed becomes a towering tree;
a puppy into a great big dog -
measure me on the wall, you'll see.

East

Push the handle up and down,
blow air into a flat bike tyre;
fill the balloon, fill the lilo too -
puff it fat and lift it higher.

South

Bread does it in the cosy warm,
the sun does it at break of dawn;
smoke and balloons all float up high -
go up, up, up, then you're gone.

West

A sail puffs out, a curtain swells,
smoke rolls up in a great fat cloud;
the wind fills me and out I bloom -
I puff and roll, all soft and proud.
