Shout

You are a shout - a loud raised cry of the voice, flung out as far as it will go: to call a friend across the field, to warn of danger, to roar with the crowd. No hand makes you; a chestful of breath and a wide-open mouth do. You cannot be touched, only heard - and a good one carries right over the noise. You shout for joy, shout for help, get shouted at when you've been naughty. You are the voice turned all the way up.
