Cactus

You are a cactus - the fat green plant of the hot dry desert, bristling all over with sharp little spikes. You hoard water deep inside your swollen stem so you can go for months with no rain at all. People keep you on a sunny windowsill and learn quick not to hug you. You are natural, grown not made, tough as old boots and patient as the sand. You are the plant that proves a thing can live where nothing else will, prickly and proud in the blazing sun.
