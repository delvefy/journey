Pansy

You are a pansy - a small bright flower with petals like velvet, marked in the middle as if you had a little face peeping up from the bed. You come in purple and yellow and deepest blue, planted in tubs and borders where you nod through spring and into summer. Children think you are watching them. Low and cheerful and easy to grow, you turn your soft face to the sun and brighten every windowsill and garden path with your patch of bold, friendly colour.
