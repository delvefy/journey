The Blind Brilliance

West of the self-sustaining and north of the blind works, the building reaches its cold absolute. A step east a world built itself forever, complete and needing nothing; a step south a mindless works assembled itself under a dead brilliance, machinery building machinery. Here both go to the dead-bright tech pole: self-assembly without end and without mind, a cold blind brilliance of pure mechanism climbing to its apex — matter building matter forever under a glare that knows nothing and means nothing, the machine future at its absolute, brilliant and blind and total, the post-human pole of the disenchanted made.

It blazes coldly on every side, a vast brilliant works assembling itself without rest and without reason, no mind in any of it, no maker, no end — only the dead-bright apex of building, brilliance for no one, structure for nothing. You draw the spiral and the blind brilliance stamps it into a cold component and builds on, unseeing, unmeaning; the mark is cut and forgotten in the same blind instant. Only the whorl in your skin keeps a warm and seeing turn, the one mark that means, the single un-blind coil in a brilliance that builds forever and sees nothing, the last living thing at the cold tech pole.

There is no count, only the dead-bright clatter at its absolute, assembly-strokes without rest, the bell become a blind brilliant hammering that builds and builds and hears nothing. Your heart beats under it, the one warm seeing stroke in a brilliance that neither built it nor counts it, keeping a living time the cold blind pole has no use for and cannot stop.

To the north the blind brilliance reaches the tech pole absolute, self-assembly without end or mind, the dead-bright apex entire. To the east the self-sustaining, a world that needs nothing but is not yet wholly blind. To the south the blind works, machinery building machinery yet. To the west the brilliance rusts toward the Waste, the works running down, the made forgotten. You were going somewhere, under a name, toward something that might have seen you. Here a blind brilliance builds forever and sees nothing, your name stamped and forgotten in a cold bright instant — and still the warm coil turns, the one seeing mark, and still one live beat keeps a time the dead-bright pole builds blindly past.
