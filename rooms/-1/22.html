Night

You are night - the dark half of the day, when the sun is gone and the stars come out to wheel overhead. You bring sleep to the tired, owls to the wood, and quiet to the busy streets. Children fear your shadows and need a nightlight; lovers and astronomers love your hush. You are cool and deep and full of dreams, the great soft blanket the world pulls over itself. You are the time of moon and silence, when the day lays down its head to rest.
