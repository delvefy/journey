Midnight

You are midnight - the exact middle of the night, the stroke of twelve when one day quietly becomes the next. No hand made you; the turning earth did, and you arrive in the deepest dark, when the house is asleep and the owls are out. You cannot be touched, only counted to on a clock. The witching hour, the ghost's hour, the time a wish is whispered - you are the hinge of the night, the moment everything turns over and begins again.
