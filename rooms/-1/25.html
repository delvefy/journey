Afternoon

You are an afternoon - the long slow stretch of day that follows lunch and leads down toward evening, the drowsiest, dreamiest part of the clock. No hand brings you; the turning day does. The sun leans westward, the lessons drag, the cat sleeps in the warm square on the carpet. You are homework not yet started and tea not yet ready, a wide golden gap of time. Lazy and long and a little bit sleepy, you are the day taking a deep slow breath before the dark.
