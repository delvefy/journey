Evening

You are evening - the soft slow hour when the day winds down. The sun lies low and gold, shadows stretch long across the grass, and one by one the windows light up warm. Children are called in from their games; the birds settle, the air cools. You are not yet night, only the gentle slope down into it, the time of supper and yawns and the first star. You are the day breathing out, the quiet between the busy and the dark.
