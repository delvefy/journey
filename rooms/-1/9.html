space

You are space, the great nothing that holds everything, the dark between the stars where no air goes and no sound carries. You are emptiness wearing a crown of suns. The rocket climbs all morning and only scratches your edge; light itself takes years to cross a single step of you. You weigh nothing, you cannot be touched or kept, yet you are the room the whole creation stands in. Without you there would be no room for a single thing to be.

North

No hand can hold me, no eye see,
I gnaw the belly hollow;
I drive you to the bread and tea,
and a full plate makes me follow.

East

A little sleep in the afternoon,
not long, with shoes still on;
you close your eyes and wake up soon,
the heavy hour gone.

South

The great gold lamp that lights the day,
too bright for eyes to bear;
I warm the fields, I dry the hay,
and climb the morning air.

West

A sudden burst of blinding light,
that's there and then it's gone;
the camera makes it in the night
to catch you smiling on.
