Fog

You are fog - cloud come down to walk the ground. You hang grey and damp over the road and the field, and the world goes soft and lost inside you; the lamp post fades, the far hill vanishes, sound goes muffled and near. You are made by no hand, and no hand can hold you - only walk into you and feel you, cool and wet, close over everything. Wait long enough and the sun burns you away.
