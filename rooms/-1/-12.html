Peach

You are a soft round fruit with velvety fuzzy skin, gold and blushing pink, sweet and dripping with juice. No hand makes you; the orchard tree gives you in high summer, warm from the sun. A big wrinkled stone hides in your heart. Bite you and the juice runs down the chin. Soft as a cheek, sweet as the season.

North

Small and round, in heavy bunches,
green or deep dark blue;
eat it sweet or squash for juice -
to wine it turns, it's true.

East

A line of posts with rails between
around the field it goes;
it keeps the sheep from wandering off -
where one man's land it shows.

South

A round fruit named for its own colour,
sweet and full of juice;
you peel its dimpled skin in bits -
the segments come loose.

West

A bright red flower, papery-thin,
with a dark heart full of seed;
it nods in fields of golden wheat -
red among the weed.
