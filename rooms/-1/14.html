Glimmer

You are the faint and trembling light, the least a flame can give - the candle guttering low, the far star almost lost, the last coal breathing red in the grate. No hand makes you; you are light at the edge of going out. Weak, wavering, easy to miss. Look hard in the dark, and there I am, barely.

North

A tiny passing smell on the air,
here, then gone again;
of baking bread, of cut green grass -
you catch it now and then.

East

A daytime sleep you grab in the chair,
a few stolen minutes' rest;
the alarm rings, you slap it off -
five more, and that's the best.

South

The bright quick flash on water and gems,
a thousand winks of light;
the tinsel, the dew, the diamond ring -
all glinting, sharp and bright.

West

The soft quick sound of a thing that moves,
a skirt, a tail, a cane;
it slices the air from side to side -
that whispering swipe again.
