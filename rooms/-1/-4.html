The Emptying Dark

Below the cooling dark even the cold is finished wanting anything. A step up, a body's last warmth still leached out into the black and a slowing heart still spent its count; here there is no body to cool and no warmth to be taken, only the deep from before the cooling, the dark that has never once been lain in. This is the older dark the cooling promised beneath itself — but a step west of the turning deep, and west is where things empty. To the deep beside it, the dark at least moves. Here it has stopped wanting to.

You reach, out of the old habit of reaching, and the dark does not gather to meet you the way it gathers a step east. It thins. Whatever might have drawn itself up out of it has been let go of before it began — ash that never burned, a someone never gathered, the cold the same the whole way down with nothing in it that could answer. The charms stop here. The dark is only dark, and does not pretend to be more.

The vast slow turning you felt to the east is here too, but unwinding — the spiral gone loose, a coil let out and not drawn back, slowing toward a stillness that has no centre and no edge. And the count is gone: no heartbeat, no long revolution, no drip, no bell. Nothing here keeps time, because nothing here is going anywhere it would need a count to reach.

To the north the grit firms back into rock and the long bone, the cold that at least remembers a body lay in it. To the east the empty dark deepens instead of thins, the turning deep that keeps a count and gathers a someone out of itself. To the south is older dark still, before even this emptying, the deep from before there was a deep to empty. To the west the cold pales the rest of the way into the Waste, ash without a fire, a silence that never had a warmth or a word to lose. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here there is no one to take, and nothing left to do the taking, and no mouth anywhere to hold your name.
