Noon

You are noon - the very middle of the day, twelve o'clock, when the sun stands highest and the shadows shrink to almost nothing. No hand made you; the turning earth did, swinging your place to face the sun straight on. You cannot be touched, only told by the clock or the short black shadow at your feet. Lunchtime, the hottest hour, the cowboy's showdown - you are the bright peak of the day, halfway between one midnight and the next.
