Daisy

You are a daisy - a little flower with white petals round a sunny yellow eye, scattered low across the whole spring lawn. No hand plants you; you spring up everywhere, the cheerfullest weed there is. Children split your stem with a thumbnail and thread you into chains and crowns. You close at dusk and open with the morning. Small and plain and endlessly many, you are the first flower a child ever picks, and the one they love the best.
