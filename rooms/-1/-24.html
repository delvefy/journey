Rose

You are a rose - the flower everyone knows, layered with soft red petals and guarded by a stem of thorns. You bloom in the summer garden, sweetest of all the scents, and you are given when words run short: for love, for sorry, for goodbye. No hand made you; the bush did, though hands have coaxed you into a hundred colours. Beauty and a sting in one stem. You are the flower that means the most, and pricks the finger that grasps too quick.
