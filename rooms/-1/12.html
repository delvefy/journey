Beam

You are a long straight shaft of light, thrown out bright and solid through the dark - the torch's cone, the lighthouse sweep, the headlamp's glare, the sunbeam slanting through a cloud. No hand need make you, though a lamp can. You catch the dust dancing in the air. Point me anywhere and there I shine.

North

The tiny dancing flecks of light
on the sea, on the snow, on a ring;
a thousand winking little stars -
a bright and twinkly thing.

East

When 'shush!' is said and all is still,
no banging and no peep;
the library, the snowy dawn -
hushed as a house asleep.

South

A slow dull hurt that will not stop,
not sharp, but deep and grey;
a sore tooth, a heavy head -
it nags you all the day.

West

Rub a shoe, a spoon, a coin
until it's bright and clean;
the light that bounces off it then -
the brightest ever seen.
