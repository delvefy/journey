Ache

You are the dull steady hurt that settles in and stays - not the sharp sudden sting, but the slow grey throb of a sore tooth, a tired leg, a heavy head. No hand makes you; the body raises you to say something is wrong. You wear a person down by going on and on. You are the long low note of pain, more weary than fierce.

North

A wide bright shaft of travelling light
the searchlight throws at night;
the torch, the lighthouse, headlamp's glare -
one solid bar of bright.

East

No guns, no quarrels, no raised voice,
the war all laid to rest;
two enemies shake hands at last -
of all calm, this is best.

South

The growl deep in an empty tum
that calls aloud for food;
it gnaws, it aches, it will not hush -
you eat to mend its mood.

West

What the wizard works with a wand,
what makes the rabbit appear;
spells and wonders, tricks of awe -
the impossible made clear.
