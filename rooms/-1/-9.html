rock

You are rock, the bones of the world, here long before the first footstep and patient long after the last. No hand made you; frost and river only break you smaller. You hold up the mountain and the cottage wall alike; you turn the plough and stub the toe. Children skim you across the pond, and the sea spends a thousand years learning your shape. Heavy, blunt, and honest - you are the ground's hard answer to the sky.

North

A little stone the stream rubbed round,
so smooth and small and grey;
I rattle in your shoe, or pound
flat where the children play.

East

Square and flat and fired from earth,
I line the bathroom wall;
I shine when wiped for all I'm worth,
set neat in rows, and small.

South

Inside a shell beneath the wave,
I grow both round and white;
a milky bead the oysters gave,
strung up for ladies bright.

West

A flower red upon its stem,
with thorns to guard its grace;
of all the blooms the brightest gem,
that lovers give for praise.
