Seed

You are the seed - the small hard speck that holds a whole plant folded up inside, waiting. Dropped in the dark earth, you lie still through the cold, until the rain and the warmth wake you. Then you crack, and a thread of root goes down, a thread of green goes up, and the waiting is over. Tiny and dry in the hand, yet the oak and the apple and the field of wheat all begin as one of you.
