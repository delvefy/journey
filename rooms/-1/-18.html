Petal

You are a petal - one of the soft bright leaves that ring a flower's heart. You are velvet-red on the rose, sunny-yellow on the daffodil, paper-thin and pink on the cherry tree. A child pulls you off one by one - loves me, loves me not - or scatters you for confetti, or presses you flat in a book. Grown by no hand, soft as a whisper, you are the bright dress a flower wears to call the bees and gladden a passing eye.
