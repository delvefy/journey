nighttime

Nighttime is the dark half of the day, when the sun has gone right down and the sky fills up with stars. The world goes quiet; lamps come on in the windows; owls wake and people sleep. It is the time of dreams and shadows, of the moon climbing slowly across the black, of the soft creaks a house makes when no one is about. It lasts until the first grey light, and then the day comes back.

North

The hottest, brightest time of year,
long light days and the school let out;
ice cream, beaches, buzzing bees -
the sunny season, no doubt.

East

A hundred and seventy, count up by tens,
ten more than a hundred and sixty, you'll find;
one, seven, oh - three digits long -
a big round number, never unkind.

South

The soft dim light when the sun has set
but the sky is not yet fully dark;
shadows blur and the first star peeps -
the in-between, the dimming spark.

West

A great tall wave that curls and falls,
it crashes white upon the sand;
surfers ride me to the beach -
I tumble, foam, and meet the land.
