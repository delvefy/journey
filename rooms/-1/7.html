Comet

You are a wanderer with a tail of light, a dirty snowball older than the world, falling toward the sun on a path that takes a hundred lifetimes to round. As you near the fire you smoke and stream, and for a few nights you hang in the sky like a smudge of silver chalk, frightening kings and delighting children. Then you swing away into the long cold dark, and the patient ones wait for your far-off, faithful return.

North

A ball of fire in the sky,
I rise to bring the day;
I warm the world as I roll by,
then sink and slip away.

East

When sleep is near I open wide,
a great slow stretch of jaw;
I'm catching, so they say, beside -
one starts, then more, then more.

South

A great round world that spins in space,
and circles round the sun;
like Mars or Earth, I keep my place,
and orbit, never done.

West

The fire breathes me, the sun pours me down,
I make you fan your face;
in summer I lie thick on the town,
and melt the ice away.
