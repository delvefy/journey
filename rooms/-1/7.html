The Blind Works

West of the lattice and north of the glare, the building loses its mind. A step east a self-assembling lattice raised matter out of pure logic, climbing toward a tech pole that at least still thought; a step south a cold brilliance thought everything perfectly and cared for none of it. Here the two cold things fuse: a vast works assembling itself blindly, endlessly, with no mind left anywhere in it — the lattice gone cold, the glare gone to mere light, machinery building machinery under a dead brilliance, the works simply running themselves because nothing remains to stop them or want them or mean them.

It builds without thought now, not even the lattice's cold logic, only the dead momentum of a process too far gone to halt — struts laid on struts, courses on courses, an architecture extending itself into the cold glare for no reason and toward no end. Your name is somewhere in the works, stamped into a girder, a serial on a blind assembly line, processed and built-around and never once meant. The spiral is cut into a thousand cold components, the same glyph blind-stamped at every joint, the maker's mark of a works with no maker left — the same whorl in your skin, here the one coil that is warm, the only turn in all the cold machinery that anything ever meant.

There is no count, only the dead-bright clatter of the works, assembly-strokes falling without rhythm or rest, the bell become the blind hammer of a line that runs itself — time spent building nothing for no one, an hour with no one in it to keep it. Your heart beats under the clatter, the one warm stroke the blind works neither built nor counts, keeping a time the cold machinery has no use for.

To the north the blind works reaches its absolute, self-assembly without end or mind, the dead-bright tech pole. To the east the building warms back toward the lattice, a structure that at least thinks itself. To the south it sinks to the glare, cold brilliance flashing thought it does not keep. To the west the works runs down into the Waste, the machinery rusting blind, the made forgotten. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here a mindless works has built it into a girder and clattered on, unmeant, unread — and still the warm coil turns, the one mark in the cold machinery that meant a thing, and still one live beat keeps the time the blind works runs past without keeping.
