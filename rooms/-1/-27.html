Tulip

You are a tulip - a smooth waxy cup of a flower balanced on a tall straight stem, opening wide to the spring sun and closing up again at night. You come in every bold colour: scarlet, yellow, purple, even striped. Planted in rows, you fill a whole bed with blazing blocks of colour. Neat and bright and proud, you are spring standing to attention, a goblet of pure colour on a green stalk, one of the first and finest flowers of the warming year.
