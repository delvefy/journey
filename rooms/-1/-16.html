Kiwi

You are a kiwi - a small brown fruit, furry as a mouse on the outside. Cut me and I am bright green within, ringed with a hundred tiny coal-black seeds, soft and sweet and a little sharp on the tongue. Grown in faraway sunny lands and shipped across the sea, you sit in the bowl beside the apples, the odd one out, fuzzy and plain until the knife shows the jewel inside.

North

A big sweet fruit from sunny lands,
with golden juicy flesh;
a great flat stone sits in my heart,
all orange, soft, and fresh.

East

Straight and slim from tip to tip,
of metal, cane, or wood;
I hold the line above the stream
where the patient fisher stood.

South

Small and hard, I hide in fruit,
or sleep beneath the ground;
give me water, sun, and time -
a green shoot will be found.

West

I am the first green tip
that pushes from the seed;
tiny, pale, and reaching up -
a baby plant indeed.
