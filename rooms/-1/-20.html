Bush

You are a bush - a sturdy little plant of woody stems and crowding leaves, lower than a tree and thicker than a flower. No hand made you; you grew in the hedge, the park, the garden, spreading wide and round. You can be trimmed into shapes, hidden behind in a game, picked of berries, rummaged in for a lost ball. Sparrows squabble in your tangle and a cat dozes beneath. You are the green crouching shape that fills the corners of every garden.
