Twilight

You are twilight - the soft dim glow that lingers after the sun has gone but before the dark comes down. The sky turns grey and gold and purple, the first stars prick through, and the world goes hushed and shadowy. Bats flit, lamps come on, and everything loses its edges in the gentle gloom. Halfway between day and night, you are the in-between hour, neither bright nor black, when the world holds its breath and waits for the dark to settle in.
