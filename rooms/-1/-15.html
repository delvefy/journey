Mango

You are the golden fruit of the hot lands, sweet and soft and dripping, the big stone hidden in the orange flesh. No hand made you; the tropical sun did. Slice me into a hedgehog of cubes, or just bite in and let the juice run to your elbow. Summer in a single sticky fruit.

North

A big round fruit, green-skinned and cool,
pink-red and sweet inside;
full of black pips and dripping juice -
on a hot day, your pride.

East

A long round hollow tube of clay or steel,
that carries water down;
under the road, along the wall -
it drains the rainy town.

South

A small brown fuzzy fruit, egg-sized,
bright green and black-seeded within;
from far New Zealand lands it comes -
sweet-sharp beneath its skin.

West

A big round ball of green leaves
that grows down in the row;
leaf wrapped on leaf, all tight and crisp -
it's chopped for soup, you know.
