The Cold Womb

South of the old cold and west of the undivided, the origin goes cold. A step north the body sank into a cold older than warmth, its heat leaching beat by beat into the dark; a step east lay the undivided, the warm origin from which warmth and being were drawn. Here the two meet at the primal pole and the origin sours: the undivided gone cold, a source that does not bring forth but takes back, the warmth leaching away before it can ever part into a body. This is the cold womb — the origin tipped from making toward un-being, where the one dark refuses to become two, and the body, unmade past even the after, is never drawn out at all.

It is the bleakest of the primal places, not because it ends a body but because it refuses one before it begins — the warm drawing-out reversed, the heat sinking back into a cold undivided that will not part. You draw the spiral and the cold womb neither parts to keep it nor warms to draw it; the gesture sinks back unmade. And still — against the souring, against the cold origin's refusal — the whorl in your skin turns, the one warmth not yet leached, the single coil insisting on being drawn out where the undivided has gone cold and will not give.

There is no count; the cold womb takes the warmth before a beat can part from the dark. And still your heart beats — the one refusal of the souring, the single warmth the cold origin cannot take back, each stroke a being drawn out where the undivided would un-make it, a body insisting on parting from the cold one dark that will not part.

To the north the cold womb rises to the old cold, a body at least once warm enough to lose. To the east the undivided, the warm origin from which being is drawn. To the south it sinks toward the primal pole, the cold origin absolute, un-being beneath the floor. To the west the warmth leaches wholly to the Waste, the cold womb gone to never-was, the corner. You were going somewhere, with someone, once — before the body, before the parting. Here the cold origin would un-make you before you were ever drawn out — and still the warm coil turns, undrawn and unleached, and still one beat insists on being, parting you, against the souring, from the cold one dark.
