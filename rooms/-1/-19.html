Flower

You are a flower - the bright sweet bloom a plant lifts up to be seen, all colour and soft petals around a yellow heart. No hand made you; a seed did, growing a green stem just to hold you to the light. You can be picked, sniffed, pressed flat in a book, given to someone loved. The bees come for your nectar and carry your dust away. You are the plant's gift to the world, lovely for a week and then gone to seed.
