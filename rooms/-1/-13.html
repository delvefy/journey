Orange

You are a round fruit named for your own bright colour, full of sweet juice in segments under a dimpled peel. No hand makes you; the sunny orchard ripens you. A thumb digs into your skin and the peel comes away in a spiral, and the smell of you sprays the air. You break into segments to share.

North

A soft round fruit with fuzzy skin,
gold-pink and sweet to bite;
a big hard stone sits in the middle -
its juice a sticky delight.

East

A thin dry piece of fallen wood
you find beneath the tree;
you throw it for the dog to fetch
or poke the fire, you see.

South

A great big round fruit, green or gold,
so heavy it weighs a ton;
red and juicy and full of black pips -
you slice it in the sun.

West

A bright yellow flower of early spring
with a trumpet in its heart;
it bobs in the breeze on a tall green stem -
from a bulb it gets its start.
