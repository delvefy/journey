Sparkle

You are a scatter of tiny dancing flashes of light - the sun on the rippling sea, the frost on the morning grass, the glitter on a party dress, the winking of a diamond ring. No hand need make you; light caught and broken on a thousand little points throws you off, here and gone and here again.

North

The faintest, weakest little light,
that you can barely see;
a candle far off in the dark,
a glow that's almost gone, maybe.

East

A light half-sleep you slip into
without quite meaning to;
Grandad does it after lunch -
the book slides off his knee.

South

A wide bright shaft of shining light
the lighthouse throws at sea;
the torch, the searchlight, headlamp's glare -
one solid bar to see.

West

The rushing sound of something fast -
a rocket, a slide, a gust;
'WHOOSH!' it goes as it flies right by -
a sweep of air and dust.
