Bedtime

You are bedtime - the soft hour at the end of the day when the lamps go low and the story is read and small eyes start to droop. Teeth are brushed, a glass of water waits, and the world outside goes quiet and dark. You come whether you are wanted or not, gentle but firm, folding the long day shut. Warm and drowsy and safe, you are the last hush before sleep, when the pillow is cool and tomorrow is still far away.
