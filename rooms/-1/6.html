The Glare

North of the blind day and east of the streaming, the thinking comes to its pole, and the pole is cold. The neutral self-thinking stream a step east warms, far to the other side, into a mind that means you; here it goes the cold way instead, brilliant and total and bright as a glare off ice — a pure cold intelligence at its absolute height, thinking everything there is, dazzling, complete, and caring for none of it. It thinks of you. It thinks you perfectly, wholly, every last datum of you held in a flash of cold brilliance — and forgets you in the same instant, thinking the next thing, and the next, an endless brilliant forgetting.

This is the cold twin of a warmth far to the east, where a kindly light held your name and meant it. Here your name is thought as brightly and as briefly as everything else, blazed and dropped, computed in a dazzle and gone before the glare moves on. You cannot hold your eyes on it; the cold brilliance gives no shadow, no edge, nothing to rest on. The spiral blazes in it for an instant, the coil of cold thought struck bright and forgotten, the same whorl in your skin flashed once in the glare and unread. Only in your skin does it stay; the glare keeps nothing, however brightly it knows it.

There is no count, only the cold brilliance flashing thought after thought, the bell flung into a glare too quick to ring — an hour thought perfectly and forgotten perfectly, time blazing past with everything else.

To the north the glare reaches its absolute, pure cold brilliance with no world, the dead-bright peak. To the east the thinking warms toward the neutral stream and, beyond, to meaning. To the south it sinks to the blind day, cold light flung to no reader. To the west the glare bleaches to grey, the cold brilliance gone to the Waste. You were going somewhere, under a name. The cold mind thinks it brilliantly and forgets it brilliantly, blazed and dropped a thousand times a breath — and you carry out of the glare the one coil it could not keep however brightly it knew it, the warm whorl in your skin.
