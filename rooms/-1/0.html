River

Water runs through the room, though you cannot find its source or its end. It crosses the floor in a bright cold seam and slips beneath the far wall, talking to itself the whole way. Nothing here was built; it was carved, slowly, by the going of the water. Follow it. The way out, like the river, only goes forward.

North

No hand, and yet I turn the mill,
no voice, and yet through night I moan;
I drive the cloud across the hill
and chill you to the bone.

East

What has teeth but cannot bite,
and turns, and yet is no wheel,
and frees, with one cold word of iron,
whatever the locks conceal?

South

Below the trunk I creep and spread,
I never see the sun;
I anchor what the storm would take
and feed it, every one.

West

I drink the rivers whole and never fill,
no foot will ever cross to my far side;
ships ride my back, salt is my blood,
and the moon commands my tide.
