Violet

You are a violet - the small shy flower of deep purple, hiding low in the spring grass under a heart-shaped leaf. You smell sweet to anyone who kneels to find you, though you hardly lift your head. Your colour is so much your own that people borrowed your name for it. You ask for no notice and get little, yet there you are at the woodland edge each spring. You are a small purple secret, kept close to the cool ground.
