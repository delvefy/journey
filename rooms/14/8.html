Crouch

You are a crouch - the low, folded shape you make when you bend your knees and sink down small. A cat crouches before it springs; a runner crouches at the start; you crouch to peer at a beetle or to hide behind the sofa. Knees bent, head low, ready to leap or to wait. You are not a thing but a way of holding the body, coiled and close to the ground.
