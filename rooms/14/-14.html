Tuba

You are a tuba - the biggest, deepest horn in the whole brass band, a great coil of gleaming pipe that wraps around the player and ends in a bell as wide as a bucket. A hand made you, and a player blows into you and presses your valves to bring out the lowest oompah notes of all. You can be held - just - polished, dented, lugged about. You are the band's deep foundation, the big friendly giant of the brass, going oom-pah-pah beneath everything.
