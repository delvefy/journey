Hat

You are a hat - the thing you wear upon your head, against the sun or rain or cold. A woolly bobble hat in winter, a straw one in the summer field, a tall black topper for a smart affair, a paper crown from a Christmas cracker. You can doff a hat to say hello and tip it to say goodbye. Whatever the weather, whatever the day, you crown the very top of someone.
