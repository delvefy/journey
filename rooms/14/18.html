Waddle

You are a waddle - the rolling, side-to-side walk of a duck come up from the pond, of a penguin on the ice, of a toddler in a fat nappy. Short legs and a round middle, and so you tip left, then right, then left, never quite falling, never quite hurrying. You are the funniest way of getting somewhere, slow and certain and bottom-heavy, a walk that makes everyone watching smile.
