Cot

You are the little barred bed of the baby, high sides to keep the small one safe, a soft sheet and a hanging mobile above. Made by careful hands. The baby sleeps and stands and shakes the bars and calls. Lower the side, lift the small one out - a cot holds the whole of someone's first long nights.

North

A cold white cupboard, humming low,
that keeps the milk from spoiling;
you open it for cheese and juice -
inside, it's never boiling.

East

A baby's bed that rocks and sways,
on curved feet, to and fro;
'rock-a-bye' you sing to it -
soft, and swinging low.

South

A scrap of cloth sewn over a hole,
on the knee of your old jeans;
a square of new on the worn-out coat -
it mends the rips and seams.

West

A tall shut box with doors that swing,
against the kitchen wall;
it hides the plates, the cups, the tins -
shut tight, it keeps them all.
