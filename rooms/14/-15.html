Harmonica

You are a harmonica - a flat little box of metal and reed that hides in a pocket. You slide along the lips, and a breath in or a breath out wakes a row of small bright notes. Cup your hands and you wail, or wheeze a slow and lonesome tune. You are wrought small enough to carry anywhere, played by nothing but air and a moving mouth. No other instrument is so easy to lose and so easy to love.
