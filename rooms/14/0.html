Tractor

You are the great workhorse of the farm, big-wheeled and grumbling, dragging plough and trailer through the mud. Made of steel and diesel, you do what ten horses once did. Slow but mighty, you climb the field in the rain. The farmer sits high in your cab; behind you, the gulls follow the fresh-turned earth.

North

Fingers on one hand, count them up,
the points upon a star;
one more than four, one less than six -
how many, near and far?

East

A big yellow machine that scoops
great bites of earth and stone;
its long arm digs the giant holes -
on building sites it's known.

South

The glass pear in the lamp
that glows when switched on bright;
it lights the room at a flick of the thumb -
a little globe of light.

West

A great big boat that crosses seas,
with sails or engines strong;
it carries crowds and cargo far -
on the waves it rolls along.
