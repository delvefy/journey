Trolley

You are a trolley - a big wire basket on four little wheels that you push up and down the shop. Tins and bread and bags of apples pile up inside you as you trundle along, and one wheel always squeaks or wants to go its own way. At the checkout you are emptied, and outside you are nudged into a long nesting line of others just like you. Sturdy and patient, you carry the heavy load so nobody's arms have to.
