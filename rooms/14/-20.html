Chest

You are a chest - a big stout wooden box with a heavy hinged lid, made by hands to hold what matters, from a pirate's gold to a child's toys. You shut with a thump and sometimes a lock, and you keep your secrets dark inside. On the map an X marks where you are buried. You are the deepest, most promising kind of box, the one a treasure goes in. Lift my lid, and who knows what waits, gleaming in the gloom?
