Barrel

You are a barrel - the fat wooden cask, round at the belly, bound with hoops of iron. You hold cider and rainwater, salted fish and rolling apples. Push me and I roll on my side with a deep wooden rumble. In the old stories the pirate hides in me, the monkey is over me, and somebody always goes over the waterfall inside one. Fat, sturdy, full of something good - that is you.
