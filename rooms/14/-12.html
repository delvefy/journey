Chime

You are a chime - the soft clear ringing of tuned bells. You are the three notes of the doorbell, the hanging wind-bells that sing when a breeze stirs them, the gentle bong of the clock marking the hour. Softer than a gong, sweeter than a clang, you ring out clean and fade slowly away. No hand can hold the sound itself - you are bells at their most gentle, a little run of clear notes ringing out across the quiet.
