Squawk

You are a squawk - the loud rough scratchy cry a bird flings out when startled or cross. The parrot squawks for a cracker, the hen squawks when the fox sneaks near, the seagull squawks for your chips. You are all edges, no tune at all, a scrape of sound that makes everyone look up. No hand can hold you - you burst out rude and sudden, say your piece in one harsh blast, and are gone.
