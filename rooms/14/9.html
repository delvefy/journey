Fold

You are a fold - the neat bend you make when one part of a thing is laid over another. You fold the warm laundry into squares, you fold a letter to fit the envelope, you fold paper into a crisp little boat or a crane. Bring two corners together, press the crease, and there you are. You can fold and fold a thing until it is small enough to tuck away.
