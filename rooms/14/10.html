Trust

You are trust - the quiet sureness that someone will not let you fall. It is the hand you take crossing the road, the friend you tell your secret to, the back you lean against in the dark and know it will hold. You are slow to build and quick to break, and once you go you are hard to win again. No one can hold you in a hand, yet you are the strongest thread there is between two people.
