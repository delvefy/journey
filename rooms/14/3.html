Jingle

You are the bright thin ring of little bells - the keys on a hook, the coins in a pocket, the sleigh in the snow, the ice-cream van down the street. No deep sound you make; just a tinkling, merry chime. Shake me, and I sing in silver. Wherever I sound, something cheerful is on its way.

North

The quick small cut of the scissors' blades,
'snip-snip' through paper and thread;
the barber takes a little off -
short clips around your head.

East

One more than three, one less than five,
the legs upon a chair;
the wheels on a car, the sides of a square -
count them, fair and square.

South

Three rows of three all counted up,
one less than ten you'll find;
the cat is said to have these lives -
the last lone digit signed.

West

The tiny high sound a mouse lets out,
the unoiled hinge, the shoe;
a thin small cry, 'eek-eek-eek' -
the new toy does it too.
