Whirl

You are a whirl - a fast spinning round and round, quicker than a stroll and dizzier than a turn. A dancer does you on one toe with arms flung wide; the merry-go-round does you with all its painted horses; a dry leaf does you in the corner where the wind eddies. You make the world blur and the head go light. You cannot be held, only made, and only for a moment before the dizziness wins. You are the giddy delight of going round and round and round.
