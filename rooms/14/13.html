Flap

You are a flap - the quick up-and-down beat of a thing too loose to stay still: a bird's wings, a flag in the gale, the tent door in a storm, a fish on the deck. A wind makes you, or a wing does; you are busy motion, all noise and no going anywhere. You cannot be held, only heard - flap, flap, flap. You are how a loose edge argues with the wind, and how a bird climbs the empty air.
