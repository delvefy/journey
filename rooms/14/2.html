Nine

You are the number just shy of ten, three rows of three, the cat's fabled lives, the last single digit before the count rolls over. A made idea, you stand at the edge of the small numbers, almost large. Nine lives, nine pins, cloud nine. Count to me, and the next breath tips you into the tens.

North

The bright ring-ring of little bells,
the keys that chime and clink;
the sleigh of Santa coming near -
a tinkling sound, you think.

East

One more than seven, one less than ten,
two fours stacked up high;
turn it sideways - there's the sign
for endless, by and by.

South

The fingers of one open hand,
the points upon a star;
one more than four, one less than six -
count them, near and far.

West

Ten lots of ten all counted up,
a great round mighty sum;
a century holds this many years -
the biggest, when they come.
