Swerve

You are a swerve - a sudden sharp turn off your line to miss what is in the way. The car swerves round a pothole; the cyclist swerves to dodge a dog; the footballer swerves past a tackle. One quick wrench of the wheel or the hips and you are clear, leaning hard into the curve. Quick and sharp and last-second, you are the body's emergency steer, the swift jink sideways that saves a crash, snapping back on course as soon as the danger is safely past.
