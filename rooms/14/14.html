Toss

You are a toss - a light easy throw, up and over, with a little spin: a coin to decide, a pancake in the pan, a ball to a friend, your hair out of your eyes. A hand makes you, a flick of the wrist sending the thing arcing through the air. You cannot be held, only watched as it flies and comes down. Heads or tails, catch or drop - you are throwing made casual, the careless little flight of something sent up to come down again.
