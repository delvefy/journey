Bulb

You are the little glass pear in the lamp, thin wire inside that glows white-hot when the switch clicks on. Made by clever hands, you turned night into a thing one could end with a thumb. Screw me in, and the dark room blooms. One day I flicker, buzz, and die - then someone fetches a new one, and the light comes back.

North

The big farm machine with great fat wheels
that drags the plough through clay;
the farmer rides it high and slow -
it works the field all day.

East

Flick me up and the light comes on,
flick me down, it's gone;
on the wall by the door I wait -
click, and the dark is done.

South

A little bar that holds the gate,
you lift it up to go through;
it drops back down and clicks it shut -
on door and window too.

West

A light you carry in your hand,
a candle in a case of glass;
it swings and glows along the path -
and lights you as you pass.
