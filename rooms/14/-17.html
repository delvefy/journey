Saucepan

You are a saucepan - the round metal pot with one long handle, the busiest thing on the stove. You boil the pasta, simmer the soup, bubble the porridge in the morning. You wear a lid to keep the heat in and hiss when the milk threatens to climb your sides. Your bottom goes black from the flame and your handle stays cool for the hand. You are the kitchen's hardest worker, the deep little pot where most of dinner gets its start.
