Sled

You are a sled - a little seat on two smooth runners, made for one glorious thing: whizzing down a snowy hill faster and faster with the cold air streaming past. Hands wax your runners and a bundled-up child climbs aboard and shoves off. You bump and slew and spill everyone into the soft white at the bottom, and they haul you straight back up to do it again. Simple and swift and full of squeals, you are the very best of a snow day.
