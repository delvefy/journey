Swish

You are a swish - the soft brushing sweep of sound a thing makes slicing through the air. The long skirt swishes across the floor; the horse's tail swishes at the flies; the wand swishes as it casts its spell. You are half a sound and half a movement, a quick whispering sweep that's gone in an instant. The curtains swish shut; the cane swishes down. You are the gentle hiss of something moving fast and smooth, the whisper of motion itself.
