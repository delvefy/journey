Suitcase

You are a suitcase - a flat stiff box with a handle and a zip or a pair of clasps, packed to bursting before a holiday and lugged down off the wardrobe once a year. Hands fold the clothes in and sit on your lid to shut you. You trundle behind on little wheels, you go round and round the airport belt, you come home full of sand. Sturdy and travel-worn and faintly exciting, you are the box that means somewhere new, the start of every trip away.
