Wardrobe

You are the tall wooden cupboard for clothes, a door that swings on a long mirror, a rail of coats and dresses within. Made by the carpenter, deep and dark. Push to the very back, through the fur coats, and who knows - snow, a lamppost, a whole winter world. Open me, and choose what today will be.

North

A little bar that holds the gate,
you lift it up to pass through;
it drops back down and clicks it shut -
on door and window too.

East

A hot metal box in the kitchen wall
where the cake and the roast go in;
you turn it up and shut the door -
and dinner can begin.

South

A cold white cupboard, humming low,
that keeps the milk from spoiling;
you open it for cheese and juice -
inside, it's never boiling.

West

A long hard seat for two or more,
in the park or by the door;
just a plank on legs, no back at all -
you rest your feet, footsore.
