Rod

You are a rod - a long straight slender bar, stiff from end to end. You are the fishing rod that bends and sings when a fish bites, the metal rod that holds the curtain up, the bright steel rod that props and pins and measures. Cut and shaped by a hand, smooth and hard in the grip, you are strength stretched out in a single line - a thing that does its work by simply, stubbornly, staying straight.
