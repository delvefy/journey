Flick

You are a flick - a quick light snap of a finger or wrist, sending a small thing flying: a crumb off the table, a switch up the wall, a marble across the floor. A hand makes you, fast and sharp, all in a fraction of a second. You cannot be held, only done, here and gone before you blink. A frog flicks out its tongue; you flick the light on, flick a page, flick a pea at your brother. You are the smallest, quickest little jab of motion there is.
