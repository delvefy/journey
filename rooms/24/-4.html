Rubber

You are a rubber - a soft pink block that lives at the top of the pencil and at the bottom of the case, made to undo what the pencil has done. A hand rubs you back and forth over a wrong word and the grey lines vanish, leaving little crumbs of yourself behind. You grow smaller with every mistake you forgive. A kind and crumbly thing, you are the second chance of every drawing, the friend of everyone who got it wrong.
