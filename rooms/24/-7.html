Mirror

You are a mirror - a flat sheet of glass silvered behind, that shows back whatever stands before it, exact but flipped left for right. Hands make you and hang you on the wall, and you give everyone their own face to pull at. You cannot lie, but you cannot tell the truth either, only echo it backwards. Honest and shallow and endlessly patient, you hold a perfect copy of the room as long as the room is there, and an empty wall the moment it leaves.
