Mattress

You are a mattress - the thick padded springy slab that lies on the bed frame and takes the weight of a sleeping body all night. Hands stuff you with springs and foam and stitch you up tight. You sag a little in the middle where someone always sleeps, and you are the very best thing in the house to bounce on when no one is looking. Soft and patient and quietly faithful, you cradle a whole night's dreams and ask only to be lain upon.
