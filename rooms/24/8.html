Stagger

You are a stagger - the wobbly lurching walk of someone who can barely stay upright, dizzy or worn out or heavy-laden, swaying from side to side and grabbing at the wall. No one chooses you; exhaustion or a spin or a knock to the head does. You weave where you meant to go straight and nearly go down with every step. Unsteady and lurching and almost-falling, you are walking gone wrong, the body fighting hard just to keep its feet beneath it.
