Doze

You are a doze - the light easy half-sleep that creeps up in a warm armchair on a slow afternoon, the book sliding down, the eyes falling shut, the head nodding then jerking up again. No one decides to do you; comfort and a full tummy do. You drift in and out, half hearing the room. Grandpa is famous for you after lunch. Warm and woozy and not quite asleep, you are the gentle drowse on the edge of dreaming, the nap you did not mean to take.
