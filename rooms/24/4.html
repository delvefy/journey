Grin

You are a grin - the great wide stretch of a smile gone bold, all teeth from ear to ear, the look of someone pleased with a secret or a plan. No tool makes you; delight and mischief tug your corners up and back. A cat wears one, a winner wears one, the boy with crumbs round his mouth wears one. You are happiness caught red-handed, cheekier than a smile and brighter than a laugh, splitting a whole face in two.
