Penalty

You are a penalty - the one big free shot at goal, taken from the spot with only the keeper to beat, given when someone fouls inside the box. The whole ground goes quiet. The taker breathes, the keeper crouches and guesses, and in one kick it is glory or heartbreak. Tense and dramatic and over in a heartbeat, you are the most nerve-wracking moment in football, a single shot that can win a match or lose it, with everyone holding their breath.
