Hutch

You are a hutch - a little wooden box with a door of wire mesh and a run for hopping, built in the garden to keep a rabbit or a guinea pig safe and dry. Hands hammer you together and line you with sweet straw. The pet thumps inside you and pokes its nose to the wire for a dandelion. You shelter the small and the soft from the rain and the fox. Snug and wooden and full of straw, you are the whole world to one fat happy bunny.
