Hush

You are a hush - the soft pressed sound of a finger to the lips, the 'shhh' that asks a noisy room to go still, and the gentle quiet that falls when it obeys. People make you in libraries and over sleeping babies, half a word and half a wish. You are not quite silence, but silence asked for kindly. The lull before a story, the calm laid over a fretful child, you are the quiet that is given, not just found.
