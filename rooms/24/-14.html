peeler

A peeler is a little blade that shaves the skin off vegetables in long curling ribbons. You hold the potato in one hand and run the slotted blade down it, and away peels a thin brown strip, then another, leaving it pale and clean. Cheap, sharp, and shaped for one small job, it lives in the kitchen drawer with the forks. Wholly a made thing you can hold, it turns a fiddly chore into a quick, satisfying curl.
