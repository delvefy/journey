Jangle

You are a jangle - the loose clattery jingling of metal things knocked together, a fat bunch of keys shaken, a pocketful of coins, a tambourine. No single thing makes you; lots of little metal bits and a shake do. You go on jingle-jangle, busy and bright and a touch annoying. Your nerves can be set jangling too, all on edge. Clattery and metallic and never quite in tune, you are the loose loud music of a key-ring on the move.
