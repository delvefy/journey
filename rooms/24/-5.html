Paper

You are paper - thin pale sheets pressed flat from wood and rag, made by busy mills to take the pencil and the paint and the printed word. A hand can write you, fold you into a plane or a boat, tear you, crumple you, or smooth you flat again. You burn quick and soak quicker. The whole school runs on you, and so do letters and books and birthday cards. Light and white and patient, you hold whatever anyone cares to put on you.
