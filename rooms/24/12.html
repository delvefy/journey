Header

You are a header - the ball met not with the foot but with the forehead, nodded hard towards the goal or away from danger. Up you jump as the cross comes in, twist your neck, and thump: the ball flies off your brow. Tall players love you at corners; defenders use you to clear. Brave and bouncy and a bit of a thud, you are football played in the air, the leaping nod that turns a high ball into a goal, with never a hand or a foot involved.
