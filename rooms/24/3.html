Giggle

You are a giggle - the small high bubbling laugh that escapes behind a hand and will not be shushed. No one quite decides to make you; a tickle or a secret or a silly word sets you off, and out you come in fits and squeaks. The more you must be quiet, the worse you get. You are mischief turned to sound, laughter too small to be a roar and too live to be held in.
