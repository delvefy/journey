clamber

To clamber is to climb in the clumsy, scrambling way - not smooth and graceful but all hands and knees and heaving, hauling yourself up over rocks, up the climbing frame, into the back of the high van. You grab whatever you can reach and pull, scrabbling with your feet for a hold. There is nothing tidy about it. It is the climb you do when there are no proper steps, getting there in the end by sheer scrambling effort.

North

Bang your foot down hard on the ground,
thump! when you're cross or marching in line;
the giant does it - fee-fi-fo -
a heavy foot comes down each time.

East

Reach out fast and snatch it up,
your hand shoots out and holds on tight;
grab the rail before you fall -
a quick firm catch, with all your might.

South

Make-believe you're a pirate king,
or a dragon, a queen, or a cat;
it isn't real, it's all in your head -
you play that you're this and that.

West

Walk slowly through the shallow sea,
the water dragging round your knees;
each step a heavy splashy push -
you paddle through it as you please.
