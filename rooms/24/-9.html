Glove

You are a glove - a hand-shaped cover stitched with a snug little pocket for every finger and the thumb, pulled on against the cold or to keep the hands clean. You come in pairs, and one of you always goes missing. You let the fingers wiggle and grip while staying warm. Goalkeepers and gardeners and surgeons all need you. Handy and warm and forever losing your partner, you are the closest, cosiest thing a cold pair of hands can wear.
