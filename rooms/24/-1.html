Thread

You are a thread - a long fine strand spun from cotton or silk, wound on a reel and pulled out as needed. You slip through the needle's eye and dive in and out of the cloth, joining seam to seam, mending the torn, hemming the loose. Made by hands - spun, twisted, dyed - you are thin as a whisper and stronger than you look. Snip and you stop; knot and you hold. You are the small quiet line that stitches the made world back together.
