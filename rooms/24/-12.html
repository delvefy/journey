You are an organ - a mighty instrument with rows of keys and a forest of pipes soaring up the church wall. Press the keys and air rushes through the pipes, and out rolls a great booming sound that fills the whole building and rattles your chest. Some pipes squeak high as a whistle, some rumble deep as thunder. Grand and thunderous and centuries old, you are the biggest, loudest instrument of all, played with hands and feet at once, shaking the rafters and lifting every voice in song.
