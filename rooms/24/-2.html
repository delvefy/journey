Belt

You are a belt - a strap of leather or cloth worn snug around the waist, buckled to hold the trousers up. You have a row of little holes to choose from, tighter after sport, looser after dinner. Made by hands - cut, stitched, and punched - you keep things up and pull things in. The karate champion wears you in colours, white to black. You are the simple loop that holds the middle together, the buckle that keeps everything where it belongs.
