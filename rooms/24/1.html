Ticket

You are a ticket - a small printed slip that buys your way in: aboard the train, into the film, onto the ride. You are kept safe in a pocket and shown at the gate, then torn or clipped or beeped, and you have done your one job. Made by hands - printed, numbered, dated - you are a tiny paper promise that says yes, this one may pass. You are the difference between waiting outside and going in, a doorway folded small enough to hold.
