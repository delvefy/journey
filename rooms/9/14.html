Tick

You are a tick - the small dry sound a clock makes, over and over, all day and all night. Tick, tick, tick: you are the heartbeat of every watch and wall-clock, chopping time into tiny even pieces. You never rush and never rest. You are also the neat little mark a teacher makes beside a right answer - a quick V to say well done. Small, sharp, and always on time.
