Headstand

You are a headstand - balancing the whole body upside down, the top of the head on the floor and the legs pointing straight up at the ceiling. Hands spread out to steady you, and the blood rushes to your face as the whole room turns over. It takes practice and a soft cushion to learn. Topsy-turvy and wobbly and rather grand once you've got it, you are the world flipped on its head, the trick of standing on the one part of you never meant for standing at all.
