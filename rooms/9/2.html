sum

You are a sum, the answer that waits at the bottom of the adding. Pour numbers into a column, draw the line, and out you come - one figure that holds all the rest. The shopkeeper counts you in coins, the child checks you on small fingers, the whole world balances its books by you. You are not a thing you can hold, only a truth the figures agree on. Get me wrong, and the pennies will never, ever come out right.

North

A little snap the buttons make,
the latch, the pen, the mouse;
a tiny sound, and shut or wake -
it sounds throughout the house.

East

I am made of sixty minutes,
the clock counts out my fall;
a day has twenty-four within it,
and twelve upon the wall.

South

A thing that's true and known and plain,
not rumour, tale, or guess;
that fire is hot, that rain is rain -
the truth, no more, no less.

West

I count the sheep, I mark the door,
I tell you three from ten;
add me, take me, more and more -
then count me up again.
