Apron

You are a cloth tied around the front to keep the clothes beneath you clean - the cook's, the painter's, the carpenter's. A hand cuts you and sews on your strings. You catch the flour, the gravy, the splash of paint. You have a big front pocket for the pegs or the spoon. Tied at the waist or up round the neck.

North

A woolly glove with one warm pocket -
the fingers all together,
the thumb alone in its own nook -
worn in freezing weather.

East

The band that goes around the neck -
on a shirt, it's stiff and white;
a dog wears one to hang its tag -
buckled, not too tight.

South

A little metal clasp with a pin
that fastens a belt or a shoe;
you slot the strap and snap it shut -
it holds the two ends true.

West

A soft small hat with a sticking-out brim
to shade your eyes from sun;
you wear it backwards, wear it round -
on a sporty head it's fun.
