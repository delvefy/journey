Towel

You are a towel - the big soft cloth that rubs a wet body warm and dry after a bath or a swim. You hang on a rail and drink up every drop, then need hanging in the sun yourself. You come in stripes and colours, fluffy when new and thin when old and much-loved. You are made by hand on a loom, woven thick and thirsty. You are the snug end of every bath and beach day, the warm hug of cloth that wraps a shivering child until the chill is gone.
