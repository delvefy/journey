Purr

You are the soft warm motor of a happy cat, the rumble deep in the chest when the world is right - a lap, a stroke, a saucer of cream. No hand makes you; contentment does, buzzing low and steady. You are the sound of a creature at peace. Rest a cat upon your knee, and feel me hum through your bones.

North

The sound the cat makes at the door,
to ask for milk or in;
a soft high call, 'mee-ow, mee-ow' -
it rubs against your shin.

East

A short hard pull, a quick sharp yank,
on the rope or the lead;
the dog strains forward, you pull back -
one strong jerk indeed.

South

The low deep rumble in the throat,
the cross dog's warning sound;
the bear, the empty hungry tum -
a danger, going round.

West

Pull back your arm and let it fly,
the ball goes through the air;
you chuck it far across the field -
for the dog to fetch, with care.
