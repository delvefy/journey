Meow

You are the cat's own word, the soft high call at the door - 'let me in,' 'feed me,' 'here I am.' No human throat makes you; the cat's does, winding round your shins. Plaintive and sweet and endlessly repeated until you give in. One small 'mee-ow,' and the whole house jumps to serve.

North

A short bright ring, a tiny chime,
the lift that's reached your floor;
the microwave that says it's done -
one note, and nothing more.

East

A hard rough push to move it along,
you shove the heavy door;
no gentle nudge, but all your weight -
it scrapes across the floor.

South

The soft warm rumble of a happy cat,
low in its chest it hums;
a lap, a stroke, a saucer of cream -
the gentle motor comes.

West

Snatch it out of the air with your hands,
the ball before it lands;
quick eyes, quick palms, and hold it tight -
it stops, safe, in your hands.
