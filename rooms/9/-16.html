Bowl

You are a bowl - a round deep dish, cupped to hold whatever might spill. You hold the breakfast cereal swimming in milk, the soup, the popcorn, the goldfish swimming round and round. You are warm in two hands on a cold day, and you catch the drips. Thrown from clay or pressed from plastic by a hand, smooth and curved and deep, you are the friendly dish that holds the soft and runny things a flat plate never could.
