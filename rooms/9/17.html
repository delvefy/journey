Joke

You are a joke - a little thing said to make people laugh: a silly question with a sillier answer, a story with a twist at the end you never saw coming. No hand can hold you; a quick wit makes you, and the laugh is how you know you landed. You cannot be touched, only told - and a good one gets passed around the whole playground by lunchtime. Knock knock, why did the chicken - you are the small kind trick that turns a frown the right way up.
