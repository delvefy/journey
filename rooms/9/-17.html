Mirror

You are a mirror - a flat sheet of glass with a silver back, that shows you yourself whenever you look. A hand made you, polishing the glass dead smooth; now you hang on the wall and copy everything in front of you, exact but backwards, your right hand its left. You can be held, hung, breathed on and fogged. You never lie and never keep a thing - the moment you look away, you are blank again. You are how a person meets their own face.
