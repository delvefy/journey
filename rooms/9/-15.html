Bubble

You are a bubble - a thin skin of soapy water stretched round a breath of air. Dip the wand, blow soft and slow, and off you drift, wobbling and shining with every colour at once, rising and bobbing on the lightest air. A child chases you across the garden with both hands out - and the moment a finger touches, pop, you are gone, just a tiny wet ring left behind. Blown by a breath, you are joy that lasts a heartbeat.
