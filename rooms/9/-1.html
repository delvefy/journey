window

You are a square of glass set in the wall, the eye of the house. Through you the morning light comes spilling in, and the rain runs down your face in beads while the warm room stays dry behind. You frame the street, the garden, the slow turn of the seasons; children breathe on you to draw, and lovers watch the snow. Shut, you keep the cold out; open, you let the summer in - the wall that still lets the whole world be seen.

North

I run the road on rubber wheels,
a family sat inside;
turn the key and feel how she feels
to wake, and take a ride.

East

A coloured cloth upon a pole,
that lifts when breezes blow;
I stand for land, for team, for goal,
and wave for all to know.

South

A table where you sit to write,
with drawers to hold your pen;
at school I'm yours from morn to night,
then tidied up again.

West

Look in me and see your face,
I copy all you do;
I hang upon the wall in place
and hand you back to you.
