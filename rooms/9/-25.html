Thermometer

You are a thermometer - a thin glass tube with a silver line inside that creeps up when things get warm. Pop one under a tongue and it tells whether a fever is brewing; dip one outdoors and it tells how cold the day is. The warmer it gets, the higher the line climbs, marked off in little numbered notches. Slim and clever and patient, you turn something you can only feel - the heat of a body or a day - into a number anyone can read, so the nurse knows just how poorly you are.
