Tag

You are tag - the simplest chasing game there is, needing nothing but a patch of ground and feet to run on. One is 'it', and the moment a hand touches you, you are 'it', and off you tear after the rest. There are no points, no end, no winner - only the breathless dash and the shriek of 'you're it!'. Children have played you for a thousand years in every land. You are the pure joy of the chase, a game made of running and a single touch.
