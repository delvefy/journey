Game

You are a game - anything played for the fun of it, with its own rules and a turn for each. Hopscotch on the path, snakes and ladders on the rug, hide-and-seek across the whole garden. You can be won or lost, but mostly you are played for the joy of playing. You cannot be touched, only joined in. You are how children turn a dull afternoon into an adventure, the made-up world everyone agrees to step inside until someone shouts that they have won.
