Toothbrush

You are a toothbrush - the little brush on a long thin handle that scrubs the teeth clean twice a day. Each morning and each night you dance around the mouth, dabbed with a stripe of minty paste, chasing out the bits of breakfast and the sugar of sweets. Your bristles fray with use and you are swapped for a new one every few months. You stand in a cup by the sink, head up, waiting. You are the small fierce enemy of every hole and ache in a tooth.
