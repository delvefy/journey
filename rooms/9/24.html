Handstand

You are a handstand - the upside-down balance where you kick your legs up to the sky and hold yourself steady on two flat hands, the whole world turned on its head. No tool helps you; strong arms and brave nerve do. The blood rushes to your face and the grass tickles your palms and everyone counts how long you last. Wobbly and topsy-turvy and proud, you are the trick of standing on your hands, the moment a child becomes, briefly, the wrong way up.
