Backflip

You are a backflip - a bold leap up and backwards, tucking and turning right over in the air to land back on your feet facing the way you started. Gymnasts spring into you off the beam, divers throw you off the board, show-offs do you on the trampoline. It takes nerve to throw yourself backwards into thin air. Quick and daring and dizzying, you are a whole somersault done the brave way round, over in a flash of upside-down sky, and stuck with a grin if you land it just right.
