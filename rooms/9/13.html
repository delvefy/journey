Ping

You are the small bright single note - the lift arriving, the microwave done, the text that lands in your pocket, the spoon flicked against the glass. No long ringing in you; one clear high 'ping,' and then silence. The little sound that says: something is ready, look up.

North

The small dry sound the clock gives out,
'tick-tock' all day and night;
a steady beat that counts the time -
a tiny knocking, slight.

East

Pull it slow along the floor,
too heavy to lift or throw;
you drag the sack across the room -
a scrape, and on you go.

South

The sound the cat makes at the door,
to ask for milk or in;
a soft high call, 'mee-ow, mee-ow' -
it rubs against your shin.

West

Jump aside to miss the ball,
quick, before it hits;
you duck and weave and slip away -
the game of quickest wits.
