Tissue

You are a tissue - a soft thin square of paper that pops up from the box one after another. You catch the sneeze, dry the tear, wipe the runny nose, and then you are crumpled and thrown away, used just the once. Made by hands - pressed from pulp, soft as a cloud and weak as a breath - you are everywhere a cold is. You are the little white kindness in a pocket, ready for whatever the nose or the eyes let go.
