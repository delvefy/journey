Gong

You are a gong - a great round disc of metal hung in a frame. Swing the padded beater and strike, and out rolls a sound so deep and long it shivers the floor and hums in the chest. You call the house to dinner, you open the show, you mark a grand and solemn moment. Cast and hammered by a hand, broad and golden and grave, you speak just once at a time - but when you speak, the whole room falls silent and listens.
