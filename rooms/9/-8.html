Mitten

You are a woolly glove with no separate fingers - just one warm pocket for the four, and a little house of its own for the thumb. A hand knits you, often in a pair joined by a string through the coat-sleeves so you cannot be lost. You keep small fingers warm in the snow. Snug and soft, the no-finger glove of winter.

North

One piece from the shoulders to the knees,
that a girl can twirl and spin;
not a skirt and not a shirt -
slipped over the head, tucked in.

East

A warm knitted woolly top
pulled on over your head;
no buttons, just a cosy fit -
when the cold days come ahead.

South

A cloth you tie around the front
to keep your clothes from stain;
the cook wears one to bake the bread,
kept free of any paint.

West

The soft cloth cap sewn to a coat,
pulled up when rain comes down;
it hides the face in shadow deep -
or hangs behind, hung down.
