Stretch

You are the long slow reach of the body waking up - arms flung wide, fingers spread, spine pulled out to its full length, a great yawn at the top. No hand makes you; the body does it on its own after sleep or sitting still. The cat does it, the rubber band does it. Pull, and you grow longer; let go, and you spring back.

North

A flat hard hit with an open hand,
it lands with a stinging SMACK;
red on the cheek, red on the leg -
a wallop, front or back.

East

Drag your nails across an itch
to ease the tickling spot;
the cat does it down the chair -
it leaves a mark, like as not.

South

Your cheeks go hot and turn bright red
when you are shy or caught;
you cannot stop the rosy rush -
it shows your secret thought.

West

'Ohhhh,' you let it out, long and low,
too tired, too bored, too sore;
the deep sad moan of 'not again' -
when homework's set once more.
