Cauldron

You are a cauldron - the big black iron pot that hangs above the fire and bubbles with strange brews. A witch stirs you with a long wooden spoon, dropping in frogs and herbs and who-knows-what, while green steam curls from your rim. Bubble, bubble, toil and trouble. You are heavy and round, with three little legs and a great curved handle. Whatever simmers in you, it is surely some kind of magic.
