Helmet

You are the hard hat that guards the head, plastic or steel, strapped beneath the chin. Made by careful hands for danger's sake, you ride the bike, the building site, the battlefield. Knock me, and I take the blow your skull would. Wear me, they say, and walk away from the fall you would not have walked away from.

North

The metal clasp upon a belt,
a frame and a sharp prong;
you thread the strap and pull it tight -
to hold, your whole life long.

East

A ring of beads around the wrist,
on a chain or string of gold;
a pretty band you slip right on -
a circle to behold.

South

You wear it on your face to hide,
at parties or on stage;
a monster, a hero, a cat, a clown -
pull it on, change your age.

West

A roof you carry in the rain,
on a stick with ribs and skin;
you push it up and it spreads out wide -
to keep the wet from getting in.
