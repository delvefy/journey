Giggle

You are the smallest, silliest laugh, a bubbling little fizz that escapes through the nose and behind the hand. No object, only a sound a person makes when something tickles them - in church, in class, in the quiet bit, exactly when you mustn't. You are catching: one giggle sets off another until the whole row is shaking. Light, helpless, and hard to stop, you are joy that has slipped its leash, a small wrought music of pure delight.

North

When you're shy or caught out wrong
your cheeks go hot and red;
it climbs your face without your leave -
you wish you'd hid instead.

East

A great big nervous swallow, loud,
when you're scared or very dry;
down the drink goes all at once -
one gulp, and then you sigh.

South

The bright clear note of a little bell,
one chime and nothing more;
the lift, the timer, the counter top -
a ping, then gone for sure.

West

A quick sharp nip of finger and thumb,
a squeeze of skin held tight;
for luck, to wake, or out of spite -
a small surprising bite.
