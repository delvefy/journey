Buckle

A small clasp of metal with a tongue and a frame - a buckle is how a strap remembers to hold. It bites the belt at your waist, fastens the shoe, snaps the satchel shut with a satisfying click. Pull the strap, slip the pin through a hole, and it grips until you say otherwise. Polished brass or dull steel, it is a tiny machine you wear, doing one humble job all day: keeping the loose ends firmly together.

North

Tied behind the back,
worn by cook and maid;
I take the splash and stain
so their dress won't fade.

East

No sleeves at all,
I hang and flow behind;
fastened at the throat,
I stream out in the wind.

South

A hard hat of plastic
strapped beneath your chin;
wear it on your bike
to guard the head within.

West

A soft and cosy shoe
you wear about the house;
warm upon the carpet,
quiet as a mouse.
