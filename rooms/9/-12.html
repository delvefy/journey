Mask

You are the false face worn over the real - the monster at Halloween, the hero's domino, the doctor's white cover, the carnival's painted grin. Made to hide and to become. Pull me on, and you vanish; out looks someone else. Take me off, and there you are again, blinking, only you.

North

A hard hat strapped beneath the chin,
to guard the head from harm;
you wear it on the bike or site -
it takes the knock with calm.

East

A tiny clock you strap on tight
around your wrist to see;
its little hands go round all day -
to tell the time to thee.

South

A thin straight stick the wizard waves,
with a star upon the end;
a flick, a 'swish,' and magic comes -
to enemy or friend.

West

A metal man that walks and beeps,
with buttons, lights, and gears;
it does just what it's told to do -
a made man, it appears.
