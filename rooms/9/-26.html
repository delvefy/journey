Stethoscope

You are a stethoscope - the doctor's listening tool, with two earpieces, a long rubbery tube, and a cool round disc that presses against your chest. Through it the doctor hears your heart go thud-thud-thud and your breath whoosh in and out, listening for anything amiss. It hangs round the doctor's neck like a badge of the job. Clever and cold-on-the-skin and just a little bit ticklish, you turn the quiet workings hidden inside a body into sounds a doctor can hear.
