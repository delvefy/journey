Blush

You are the sudden red that floods the cheeks - no hand brushes you on. A kind word, a small mistake, a name said out loud, and up you rush, warm and rosy, giving the secret away. You cannot be held back or hidden; the more they try, the brighter you burn. You are the body's own quiet flag of shyness, raised before a single word is said.

North

You wake, you yawn, and reach up high,
your arms spread wide, your back grows long;
a cat will do it, a rubber band -
pull yourself out, slow and strong.

West

gives little jumps that won't let be;
