Toothpaste

You are toothpaste - the cool minty stripe squeezed out onto the brush morning and night. You foam up white and fizzy, scrub the breakfast and the sweets away, and leave the mouth tingling and the teeth squeaky clean. The tube is always rolled up wrong from the middle, and the cap always rolls off down the sink. Small, bright, and minty: you are the last good taste before bed and the first of the day.
