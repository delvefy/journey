Plough

You are a plough - the heavy iron blade the farmer drags across the field. You bite into the earth and turn it over in long brown shining waves, breaking the hard ground open so the seed can go in. Once a horse pulled you; now a tractor does. Behind you the gulls come down to hunt the worms you have unearthed. Patient, heavy, and always going up and down the field in straight tired rows - that is you.
