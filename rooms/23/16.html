pivot

To pivot is to keep one foot planted and swing the rest of you around it, like a door on its hinge. The basketball player pivots to dodge; the dancer pivots on a toe; you pivot to spin and face the other way without taking a step. One point stays still while everything else turns about it. It is the trick of changing where you face all at once, fast, on a single fixed spot.

North

When you're cold or scared your body shakes,
a fast little tremble you can't hold still;
your teeth go chatter, your shoulders quake -
that shudder that comes with a chill.

East

Bang your foot down hard on the ground,
thump! when you're cross or marching in line;
the giant does it - fee-fi-fo -
a heavy foot-thud, loud and fine.

South

Walk slowly through the shallow sea,
the water dragging round your shins;
each step a heavy splashy push -
in up to your knees it begins.

West

An office chair will let you do it,
spin yourself around one spot;
your head can do it, looking back -
a twist in place, around the lot.
