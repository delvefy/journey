Radio

You are a radio - a box that plucks music and voices out of the empty air and sings them into the kitchen, with no one hidden inside. A hand twists your dial through hiss and crackle until a station comes clear. You wake the house in the morning and keep the cook company all day. You played the news in the war and the chart hits ever since. Invisible-fed and always chattering, you are company with no body, a friendly voice pulled out of nowhere.
