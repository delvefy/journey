Pillow

You are a pillow - the soft fat cushion that waits at the head of the bed for a tired head to fall. Stuffed with feathers or foam, you take the shape of whoever leans on you and spring back when they rise. Made by hands - sewn, stuffed, and cased - you are punched into shape each night and drooled on by morning. You hold dreams and bedtime secrets. You are the soft landing at the end of every long day.
