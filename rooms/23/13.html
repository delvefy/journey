Pass

You are a pass - the ball sent from one player to another, the thread that stitches a team together. A short tap sideways, a long ball up the wing, a quick one-two through the defence: all of them you. Without you a player can only run alone; with you the whole team moves as one, the ball zipping from boot to boot. Quick and clever and unselfish, you are sharing made into a skill, the difference between a crowd of players and a team that really plays.
