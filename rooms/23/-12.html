Keyboard

You are a keyboard - a long row of black and white keys, each one a different note, that sings out at a touch and can pretend to be a piano, an organ, a whole band. Fingers run up and down you and a tune appears. You plug in and light up and let one player sound like many. The same word means the rows of letters a computer is typed on. Black-and-white and full of music, you are a whole orchestra laid out flat, waiting under ten fingers.
