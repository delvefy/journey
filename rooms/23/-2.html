Tape

You are tape - the long thin strip wound on a roll, sticky on one side, clear or brown or coloured. You find your end with a fingernail, tear off a length, and hold the world together: the torn page, the wrapped present, the box ready for the post. Press hard and you grip; pull wrong and you crease into a useless twist. You ask for no nails and no glue-pot. You are stickiness made into a ribbon, mending by simply holding on.
