Snore

You are a snore - the deep rumbling rattle that rolls up out of an open mouth in the depths of sleep, loud as a saw, that the sleeper never hears at all. No one means you; a slack jaw and a deep breath do. You buzz and snort and whistle and shake the bed, and someone gets a nudge in the ribs. Grandpa is famous for you. Helpless and loud and faintly comic, you are the noise sleep makes when it forgets to be quiet.
