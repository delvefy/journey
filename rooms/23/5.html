Snuggle

You are a snuggle - to curl up warm and close against someone you love. You burrow under the blanket, tuck your head into a shoulder, and feel the warmth of another person soak right in. Babies do you, kittens do you by the fire, the whole family does you on the sofa for a film. You are safety and warmth and love all at once, made of nothing but being close. You are the cosiest thing two people can do without saying a word.
