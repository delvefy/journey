Stable

You are a stable - a long warm building of wood and brick where the horses live, divided into stalls and floored with sweet straw. Hands built you to keep the big gentle beasts dry and fed, and you smell of hay and leather and warm animal. The horses stamp and snort and lean their great heads over the half-doors. A house made just for hooves, you are where the rider hangs the saddle and the pony dreams the night away.
