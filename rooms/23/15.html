Wade

You are a wade - to walk slowly through water that pushes and drags at every step. Knee-deep in the sea, thigh-deep across a stream, you lift each foot high and push the next one through, the water heavy and cold around your legs. Herons wade after fish; children wade after crabs with a net. Slow and splashy and a little bit clumsy, you are walking made hard work by the water, pushing onward through the wet with every careful, sloshing stride.
