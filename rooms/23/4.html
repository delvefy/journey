Smile

You are a smile - the happy curve of the mouth that needs no words at all. You turn the corners up, crinkle the eyes, and tell the whole room that someone is glad. You are catching: aim one at a stranger and quite often one comes back. You greet, you forgive, you say thank you and welcome, all in a single silent shape. A photo asks for you; a baby learns you early. You are kindness drawn quickly on a face.
