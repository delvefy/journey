Blackboard

You are a blackboard - a big dark slab on the classroom wall, written on with white chalk that squeaks and crumbles and leaves a dusty trail. A hand chalks the date and the sums across you, then sweeps it all away with a felt rubber and a cloud of dust. Generations of children have stared at you and daydreamed. Dark and dusty and patient, you are the front of the classroom, where the lesson appears in chalk and vanishes again with one wipe.
