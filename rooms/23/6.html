Bow

You are a bow - the deep polite bend at the waist that says thank you, or welcome, or I am at your service. The actor gives you at the curtain, the dancer at the end, one hand sweeping low. No hand holds you; a whole body makes you, folding forward and rising again. You are courtesy made out of movement, a wordless little ceremony. Older than handshakes, you are the way the body says respect.
