Baa

You are a baa - the wobbly bleating call of a sheep on the hillside, the one word a whole woolly flock seems to know, passed back and forth across the field all day. No tool makes you; a soft fleecy throat does. The lamb cries you thin and high for its mother, who answers low and gruff. Patient and woolly and a little bit silly, you are the sound of a green hill in spring, drifting down on the wind from the wandering flock.
