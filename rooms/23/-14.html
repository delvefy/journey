Masher

You are a masher - a kitchen tool with a flat holey head on a handle, made for squashing. Boil the potatoes soft, then push down hard, again and again, and the lumps break up into fluffy white mash. Carrots, swede, even bananas give way to you. You need a strong arm and a good firm pan. Sturdy and simple and a bit of a thumper, you turn a pot of hard boiled lumps into smooth soft mounds, the comfiest mouthful on the dinner plate.
