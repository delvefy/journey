Label

You are a label - the little slip of words stuck onto a thing to tell what it is. You name the jam in the jar and the size of the shirt; you list what's inside the tin and the price upon the toy. Peel the sticky one off and it curls and will not lie flat. You carry only a few small words, yet without you the cupboard is a guessing game. You are a thing's name, written down and worn on its sleeve.
