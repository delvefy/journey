Table

You are a table - a flat top held up on legs, the place where the day gathers. Meals are eaten on you, homework spread, jigsaws built, candles lit; elbows lean and games are played. Made by hands - sawn, joined, and polished - you stand steady in the middle of the room and ask only not to be drawn on. Round or square, you bring people together to face one another. You are the level ground of the household, where everything is set down and shared.
