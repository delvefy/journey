Leather

You are leather - tough, strong material made from the tanned hide of an animal, used for shoes and belts and bags and footballs. You start stiff and shiny and grow soft and supple with years of wearing, creaking gently and smelling rich and warm. A good leather thing lasts a lifetime. Hard-wearing and handsome and only better with age, you are the sturdiest cloth there is, the one that scuffs and creases and softens into the comfiest, most loyal old boots a person ever owned.
