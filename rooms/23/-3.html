Wallet

You are a wallet - the little folding case, leather or cloth, that lives in a pocket and holds the money. Notes lie flat in your fold; coins jingle in your zip; cards line up in your slots, with maybe an old photo or a bus ticket forgotten at the back. You grow fat on payday and thin by the weekend. Pat the pocket, feel for you - the small daily panic of not finding you. You are a folded purse for grown-ups, the keeper of the spending.
