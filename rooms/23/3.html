Laugh

You are a laugh - the happy burst of sound that breaks out when something is too funny to hold in. Ha-ha, hee-hee, ho-ho: you shake the shoulders, crinkle the eyes, and steal the breath. You are catching, leaping from one person round a whole room until everyone is helpless. A joke sets you off; so does a tickle, or sometimes nothing at all. You cannot be ordered up or truly faked. You are joy that has found a voice and will not be kept quiet.
