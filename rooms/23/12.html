Shoot

You are a shoot - the moment a player swings a boot and strikes the ball hard at the goal, aiming for the corner where the keeper cannot reach. No machine makes you; a good touch and a brave swing do. You can fizz, you can curl, you can blast it over the bar. The whole crowd holds its breath as you fly. Sudden and thrilling and final, you are the strike that ends the run, the ball flung goalward with everything a foot has got.
