Coat

You are a coat - the warm outer layer with sleeves and big buttons, pulled on last of all before stepping out into the cold and wet. Hands sew you thick and line you snug, and you hang by the door waiting for the weather. You keep the wind out and the warmth in, and your pockets hide gloves and crumpled tissues and a forgotten conker. The faithful shield against winter, you are the hug a house gives you to take out into the cold.
