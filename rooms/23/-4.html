Pencil

You are a pencil - the slim wooden stick with a core of grey graphite running down your middle. You write and draw and shade, and best of all your marks can be rubbed away when wrong. Sharpen one end to a fine point, chew the other when you're thinking, and grow shorter and shorter with every page. A school day is full of you. You are the friendliest of writing tools, the one that lets you change your mind.
