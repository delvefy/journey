Chalk

You are chalk - the soft white stick that squeaks across the blackboard and draws bright on the grey pavement. You crumble as you write, leaving a dusty trail on fingers and sleeves. Children draw hopscotch squares and giant suns with you, then the rain washes them clean away. You come from old sea-creatures pressed into white cliffs long ago. You snap if pressed too hard. You are writing made of dust, here for a moment, gone with the next shower.
