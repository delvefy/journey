Sink

You are a sink - a deep basin set into the kitchen counter or bathroom wall, with taps above and a plughole below, made for washing. A hand plumbed you in; a hand fills you with warm soapy water to do the dishes, or runs the tap to scrub small hands clean. You can be leaned over, splashed in, scrubbed, blocked with bits of food. You hold the water and then, when the plug is pulled, you let it all gurgle away. You are where the day's dirt goes to be rinsed off.
