Race

You are a race - the flat-out dash to be first, when everyone strains for the line and only one breasts the tape. On your mark, get set, and you are off: legs pumping, lungs burning, the crowd a roar. Cars run you, horses run you, two friends run you to the gate and back. You are made by people who agree to compete, a thing of rules and a finish line. You cannot be touched, only won or lost.
