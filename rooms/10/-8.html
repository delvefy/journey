Jumper

You are a warm knitted top, pulled on over the head, no buttons, just a cosy woolly hug for the chest and arms. A hand knits you from wool, perhaps with a reindeer or a snowflake on the front. You scratch a little at the neck. You bobble with age. You are pulled on the moment the cold days come.

North

A sleeveless top worn next the skin,
soft, snug, and warm, and white;
no arms, no buttons, slipped straight on -
worn under shirts, from sight.

East

A long loose cape with no sleeves at all,
it hangs from the shoulders down;
a wizard wears one, swept and dark,
clasped at the throat, no gown.

South

The band that goes around the neck -
on a shirt, it's stiff and white;
a dog wears one to hang its tag -
buckled, not too tight.

West

A woolly glove with one warm pocket -
the fingers all together,
the thumb alone in its own nook -
worn in freezing weather.
