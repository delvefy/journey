Razor

You are a razor - a little flat blade in a handle, made keen on purpose to scrape a chin smooth in the morning steam. A hand drags you carefully over soap and skin, and the dark bristles fall away in the foam. You are quick and clever and not to be played with, kept high on the shelf out of reach. A small bright edge whose one job is to take a face back to bare.
