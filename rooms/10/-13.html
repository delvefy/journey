Glasses

You are glasses - two round panes of glass in a little frame, hooked over the ears and perched on the nose. For eyes that see the world all blurry, you make it sharp and clear again: the letters on the page, the faces in the crowd, the clock across the room. Made by careful hands to bend the light just right, you steam up over hot soup and slip down when you sweat. Put me on, and the fuzzy world clicks into place.

North

A little clock strapped to your wrist,
with a strap and a tiny face;
glance down to check the time of day -
it keeps the steady pace.

East

Two hands sweep round upon my face,
I tick away the day;
I hang upon the kitchen wall
and tell the time away.

South

A hundred funny cardboard shapes
that fit to make a scene;
press the right ones edge to edge -
the trickiest you've seen.

West

A slim and slender magic stick,
a star upon the end;
wave me and say the magic words -
a spell I'll gladly send.
