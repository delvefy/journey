Chase

You are a chase - the breathless run-after where one tears along behind the rest until somebody is caught. The cat does you after the mouse; the dog does you after the ball; the whole playground does you when the game is on and the squeals go up. You are all pounding feet and pumping arms and the laughter that comes from being nearly caught. You cannot be held, only run. You are the oldest game in the world, the joy of the after and the dread of the being-after, all in one mad dash.
