Sling

You are a sling - a wide cloth loop tied round the back of the neck to cradle a poorly arm. Break or sprain it and into me it goes, held snug against the chest so it can rest and mend and nobody knocks it. Friends sign their names along me in felt-tip. Soft and supportive and a badge of bravery, you hold a wounded arm still and safe for all the long weeks it takes to heal, and you turn an ordinary playground into a place of careful, gentle sympathy.
