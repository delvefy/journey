Stomp

You are the heavy stamp of an angry foot, the boot brought down hard - the toddler's tantrum, the giant's tread, the march of cross feet up the stairs. No quiet thing makes you; you are temper turned to thunder on the floor. Bring the foot down, and the room shakes. Stomp off, and everyone hears you go.

North

One quick hard pull and back it comes,
a sharp yank on the string;
the kite, the lead, the loose back tooth -
a sudden snatching thing.

East

A quiet little laugh held in,
low in the throat, not loud;
a soft 'heh-heh' at a private joke -
amused, but not a crowd.

South

A light quick knock with finger or foot,
tip-tap on the desk or door;
a gentle drum to pass the time -
soft beats, and nothing more.

West

The low deep rumble in a throat,
the dog's warning sound;
the bear, the tummy when you're hungry -
a threat that rolls around.
