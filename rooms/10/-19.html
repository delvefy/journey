Toilet

You are a toilet - the white china seat in the bathroom where everybody goes and nobody talks about it. You wait there patient all day, and when the handle goes down you flush with a great swirling rush and fill yourself up again. You are the loo, the lav, the little throne of the house, and the most useful seat anyone ever sat upon. Children learn you with great ceremony. You are the plainest necessary thing, doing a dirty job cleanly, flushing it all away.
