Shampoo

You are shampoo - the slippery soapy liquid that lives in a bottle on the edge of the bath. Squeezed into wet hair and rubbed, you froth up into a mountain of bubbles a child can shape into horns and crowns. You smell of strawberries or green apples, sting if you sneak into the eyes, and rinse away down the plughole with the day's dirt. You leave hair squeaky-clean and soft. You are a bottle of bubbles whose whole job is washing the head.
