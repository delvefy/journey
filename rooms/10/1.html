Lesson

You are a measured portion of learning, an hour with the teacher and the board, a chunk of knowledge handed over on purpose. No object, no thing to touch - you are made by people for people, the careful passing-on of how to read, to add, to behave. A hard day teaches a lesson too, the kind nobody planned. You begin with a bell and end with one; you build, lesson on lesson, into everything a person knows. The patient wrought work of being taught.

North

Sixty minutes make me up,
the clock's big slice of time;
school has one, then bells will ring,
and noon is on its climb.

East

A set of questions, hard or fair,
to check the things you've learned;
pass me and you pass the year,
a good mark to be earned.

South

A long red box on rumbling wheels
that carries crowds to town;
ring the bell to make it stop
and let you safely down.

West

A thing that's true and can be checked,
no fib and no pretend;
the date, the name, the honest sum -
on me you can depend.
