Tap

A small knock, lighter than a knock - a tap is the gentlest way to be heard. One finger on a shoulder, rain on the glass, a pencil against a desk while you think. It asks rather than demands; it says I am here without ever raising its voice. You make it and it is gone, a tiny sound dropped into the quiet, but it turns a head every time. Soft as it is, nothing else gets noticed quite so quickly.

North

To bring your foot down hard,
heavy, loud and proud;
a marching giant's step
that thunders long and loud.

East

To walk on the points of your toes,
as quiet as can be,
past a sleeping baby
so it will not wake and see.

South

The opposite of pull -
I shove a door to swing,
lean my weight against it
to move a heavy thing.

West

The long rising cry
a wolf sends to the moon;
it drifts across the hills
like a lonely tune.
