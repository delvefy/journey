Push

You are the press of the hands to move a thing away - the heavy door, the stuck pram, the swing sent flying, the friend who's in your way. No hand-but-yours makes you; you set your feet and lean and shove. The opposite of pull. Hard work against a heavy thing.

North

A light little knock with a finger or foot -
'tap-tap' upon the door;
you tap your toe in time to a tune -
a soft small touch, no more.

East

Reach out fast and snatch it tight,
your fingers close around;
you grab the rail before you fall -
quick hands that hold what's found.

South

Drag your nails across the itch
to rub the tickle out;
the cat does it down the chair -
it leaves red lines, no doubt.

West

A flat hard hit with an open hand,
it lands with a stinging SMACK;
red on the cheek, red on the leg -
a wallop, front or back.
