Gulp

You are the big quick swallow - a whole glass of water in one go, down it pours with a sound in the throat. No hand makes you; the throat makes you, in thirst or in fright. Scared, you swallow nothing at all and still you go, the throat bobbing up and down. You are the loud throat-swallow, half a drink and half a fear.

North

When winter wind slips down your neck,
your teeth go click-click-click;
you tremble, shake, you cannot stop -
a cold and shaky kick.

East

The noisy way you drink your soup
or suck a long thin noodle in;
'SLLLRP!' goes the spoon against your lips -
a sound your mum says is a sin.

South

The little ting two glasses make
when raised to say 'cheers';
or coins that knock inside a purse -
a small bright sound one hears.

West

A small bright laugh you can't hold in,
all bubbles, squeaks, and fun;
it slips out at the silliest time
and sets off everyone.
