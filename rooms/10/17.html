Zoom

You are a zoom - the way a thing tears past so fast it is gone before you can look: a racing car, a jet, a bee, a bike downhill. No hand makes you, though a fast engine helps; you are speed itself, a blur and a whoosh and a fading roar. You cannot be held, only chased and never caught. A camera zooms in close; a child zooms a toy car along the floor going vroom. You are the world going by too quick to see.
