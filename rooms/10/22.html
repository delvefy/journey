Tickle

You are a tickle - the dance of light fingers under an arm or along a sole, the touch too soft to hurt and too much to bear. A hand makes you on purpose, but the laugh that bursts out is not asked for at all. You twist away, you kick, you beg them to stop and hope they don't. You are a small kind cruelty between people who love each other, half a hug and half a hunt.
