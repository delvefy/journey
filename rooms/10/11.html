Tug

You are the short hard pull, the sudden yank - the rope in tug-of-war heaved your way, the dog straining at its lead, the loose tooth given one last sharp drag. No slow thing makes you; a quick strong haul does. One good tug, and the thing comes free, or comes to you, all at once.

North

A hard rough push to move it along,
you shove the heavy door;
no gentle nudge, but all your weight -
it scrapes across the floor.

East

Round and round with a spoon in the bowl,
to mix the cake or tea;
you stir the soup so it won't catch -
a circling, blending spree.

South

The heavy stamp of an angry foot,
brought down hard on the floor;
the toddler's tantrum, the giant's tread -
it shakes the room, and more.

West

The soft warm rumble of a happy cat,
low in its chest it hums;
a lap, a stroke, a saucer of cream -
the gentle motor comes.
