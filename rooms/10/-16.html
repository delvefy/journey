Tap

You are a tap - the little spout with a handle that lets the water out. Turn me one way and out it gushes, cold or hot; turn me back and it stops with a last few drips. You fill the kettle, the cup, the bath; you wash the muddy hands and the soapy plates. Cast and screwed to the pipe by a hand, shiny and dripping, you are the gatekeeper of the water - the small handle that decides, with a single turn, whether the water flows or holds its breath.
