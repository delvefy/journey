Wiggle

You are a wiggle - the cheerful side-to-side shake of a thing that won't hold still, toes in fresh socks, hips in a silly dance, a loose tooth worked at with a tongue. No one makes you on purpose; happiness or a fidget does. You twist and jiggle and squiggle and shimmy, going nowhere but never still. The friendliest, silliest little motion there is, you are the way a body says it is far too full of life to sit there straight and quiet.
