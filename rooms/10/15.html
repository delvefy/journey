Zip

You are a zip - a streak of pure speed. The car zips past, the bee zips by your ear, the kid on the scooter zips down the path and is gone before you can call out. You are too quick to see properly, just a blur and a whoosh of air. No hand can catch you, for you are only speed itself - the dart, the dash, the there-and-then-not-there of something moving far too fast to follow.
