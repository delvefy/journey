Drag

You are a drag - the slow heavy pull of something too big to lift, hauled scraping along the ground. You drag the full sack across the yard, the sledge through the snow, your feet when you are tired and do not want to go. Slow and effortful, you leave a long scuffed trail behind. You are work against weight, the long groan of moving what would rather stay still.
