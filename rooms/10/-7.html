Vest

You are a soft sleeveless top worn next to the skin, under the shirt, to keep the chest warm. A hand knits or sews you; no arms, no buttons, just pulled straight over the head. White and plain and snug, you are the hidden layer no one sees, doing the quiet work of keeping out the cold.

North

A thin bright strip of silky cloth
to tie a present neat,
or bind a braid of long brushed hair -
a bow both soft and sweet.

East

A long thin strip you knot at the throat,
worn down a smart shirt's front;
striped or spotted, pulled up tight -
a grown-up's collar stunt.

South

A warm knitted woolly top
pulled on over your head;
no buttons, just a cosy fit -
when the cold days come ahead.

West

One piece from the shoulders to the knees,
that a girl can twirl and spin;
not a skirt and not a shirt -
slipped over the head, tucked in.
