Collar

It rings the neck, the finishing touch at the top of a shirt - a collar is a small made thing with a job to do. Folded crisp and white for work, soft and worn for play, buckled in leather round the neck of a dog with a jingling tag. You can turn it up against the wind or button it down for best. Cloth or leather, plain or starched, it sits where it is put and frames whatever wears it.

North

A warm and woolly top
you pull over your head;
on a cold winter day
it keeps you snug instead.

East

A short coat for outdoors,
with a zip or buttons too;
you shrug it on at the door
when the cold wind cuts through.

South

A cloak with no sleeves
from the shoulders it flows;
a hero wears one flying,
it streams behind as he goes.

West

Tied around the waist,
I keep your clothes from stain;
the cook and the painter
both wear me again and again.
