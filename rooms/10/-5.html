Sheet

You are a wide flat cloth, smooth and cool, spread over the mattress before you sleep. A hand tucks you tight at the corners; you slip and rumple in the night. White and plain, you lie between the sleeper and the bed. A sheet of paper is your cousin - flat and thin and waiting. You are the flat clean spread of cloth that dresses the bed.

North

A soft full-of-holes squishy thing
that drinks up all the water;
you squeeze it out, you scrub the bath -
it shrinks, then swells back fatter.

East

A little flat thing on the floor
to wipe your dirty feet;
it lies before the door all day -
'wipe!' it says, 'be neat.'

South

A thin bright strip to tie a bow,
in a girl's hair or round a gift;
silky, shiny, every colour -
a pretty knot to lift.

West

A thick warm patch of woven wool
you lay upon the floor;
the cat curls up and sleeps on it
before the fire's roar.
