Seek

You are seek - the looking half of the game, the hunt for what is hidden. You cover your eyes, count to ten while the others scatter, then call "coming, ready or not!" and go searching. You peer behind curtains, under beds, into cupboards, listening for a stifled giggle. To seek is to want to find, to keep looking even when the trail goes cold. You are curiosity turned into a game, the patient hunt that pairs with every hiding place.
