Shiver

You are the quick shaking the body does in the cold or the dark - teeth chattering, shoulders trembling, a ripple running down the spine. No hand makes you; the cold or a fright shakes you out, all by itself, to warm the body or warn it. A ghost story brings you; an icy wind brings you. Quick and small and all over at once.

North

Drag your nails across the itch
to rub the tickle out;
the dog does it with its back leg -
it leaves red lines, no doubt.

East

When you run hard or the day is hot,
small drops break out on your brow;
salty water on your skin -
you mop it with a towel now.

South

A big loud swallow down the throat,
a whole glass in one go;
or a scared dry swallow of nothing at all -
the throat bobs to and fro.

West

Your cheeks flush hot and turn bright pink
when shy, or caught, or praised;
the rosy rush you cannot stop -
your secret feeling's raised.
