Bandage

You are a bandage - a long strip of soft cloth wound round and round a cut knee or a sprained wrist, pinned or tucked at the end to hold it snug. Hands wrap you firm but not too tight, and you keep the dirt out and the swelling down while the hurt mends beneath. You unwind a mile long when a child plays mummies. Patient and plain and faintly hospital-smelling, you are the wrapping that holds a body together while it gets better.
