Shiver

You are a shiver - the quick all-over shake that runs through you when the cold gets in. Out of the warm pool, into a chilly wind, under a thin blanket: up you come in a rush, teeth chattering, arms wrapped tight, a brrr you can feel from head to toe. Sometimes a scary story brings you instead, a shiver down the spine. Quick and trembly and gone again, you are the body's fast cold flutter, the brrr that begs for a jumper and a hot drink and a fire.
