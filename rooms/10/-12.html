Watch

You are the little clock strapped to the wrist, ticking out the hours on a face the size of a coin. Made by the cleverest, tiniest hands. Glance down, and there is the time, wherever you are. Wind me, or feed me a battery; either way, I keep the minutes marching while you forget all about me.

North

A ring of beads around the wrist,
on a chain or string of gold;
a pretty band you slip right on -
a circle to behold.

East

A tiny ball with a hole right through,
you thread it on a string;
a hundred make a necklace bright -
a small round shining thing.

South

Two round lenses in a frame
you wear upon your nose;
they make the blurry letters sharp -
and where you look, they go.

West

You wear it on your face to hide,
at parties or on stage;
a monster, a hero, a cat, a clown -
pull it on, change your age.
