flinch

Flinch is the jump your body makes before you even decide to. A loud bang, a sudden hand near your eye, the cold sting of a needle - and you snap back, shoulders up, all on their own. You did not choose it; the body chose for you, faster than thinking. It lasts no longer than a blink and then it is gone, leaving only your quick breath behind. It is fear made small and quick and bodily.

North

Bend your knees and sink down low,
fold yourself small near the ground;
a cat will do it before it pounces,
a hider does it to make no sound.

East

A small light jump, sometimes on one leg,
the way a bunny and a sparrow go;
across the puddle, stone to stone,
from rock to rock you bounce just so.

South

When you're cold or scared your body shakes,
a little fast trembling you can't hold still;
your teeth go chatter, your shoulders quake -
that quick small shudder that comes with a chill.

West

Flip yourself up so your feet point high
and the top of your head holds the floor;
the whole world turns and the sky's below -
a trick you balance against the door.
