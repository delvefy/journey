Fuss

You are a fuss - a great deal of bother over not very much. You are the wail when the sock has a wrinkle, the flap when guests are coming, the whole house turned upside-down to find one shoe. You bustle and flap and worry aloud, making a small thing seem enormous. No one can hold you, for you are only commotion - a cloud of bother that blows up loud and quick, then settles, and was about nothing much at all.
