Crutch

You are a crutch - a tall padded stick tucked up under the arm to lean on and swing along when a leg is broken or sore and cannot take the weight. Hands fit you to the right height and a hobbling patient leans hard and hops. You come in pairs and clack along the corridor. Doctors lend you out; friends sign your owner's cast. Sturdy and helpful and tucked tight under the arm, you are the spare leg that gets a hurt one home.
