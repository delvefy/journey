Bolt

You are a bolt - the sudden burst of running flat-out. The gate swings open and the pony bolts across the field; the startled rabbit bolts for its hole; the moment the bell rings, the whole class bolts for the door. You go from still to top speed in a heartbeat, gone before anyone can grab you. No hand can hold you - you are the body's panic-button dash, all four feet flying, sprinting away faster than thought.
