Hide

You are hide - to tuck yourself away where no one can find you, behind the curtain, under the bed, in the dark of the wardrobe. You hold your breath and try not to giggle as the seeker's footsteps come close. The mouse hides from the cat; the treasure hides in the ground; the sun hides behind a cloud. To do you well is to make yourself, for a while, as good as gone. You are the art of not being found.
