Twitch

You are a twitch - a small sudden jerk of a muscle that happens all on its own, an eyelid flickering, a nose wrinkling, a leg kicking out as sleep arrives. No one chooses you; a tired nerve or a startle does. You flicker once and are gone, too quick to catch. A rabbit's nose does you a hundred times a minute. Tiny and sudden and beyond control, you are the body's little hiccup of movement, a flick that nobody meant to make.
