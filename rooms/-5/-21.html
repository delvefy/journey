Holly

You are holly - the dark glossy bush that stays green when all else is bare, prickly to the touch and hung with hard red berries. The birds eat your fruit in the hungry cold; people cut your branches to deck the hall at Christmas. Your leaves are stiff and spiked, daring a careless hand. You shine through the grey of winter when the other trees have given up. You are the brave green of the darkest, coldest days.
