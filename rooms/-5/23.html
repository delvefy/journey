Sun

You are the sun - the great golden ball of fire at the centre of the sky. You rise in the east, climb high at noon, and sink in the west, dragging the day along behind you. You warm the seas, grow the crops, and paint the morning gold. Too bright to look at, you make every child squint and reach for sunglasses. Without you there would be no light, no warmth, no life at all. You are the giant burning star the whole world turns toward.
