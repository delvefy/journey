Blaze

You are a blaze - a great roaring fire, all leaping flames and heat and light, eating up the logs and lighting the whole night orange. No hand need make you, though a match will; once started, you grow and grow, climbing higher with a crackle and a roar. You cannot be touched, only watched from back and warmed by. A bonfire, a forest fire, a hearth heaped high - you are fire at its biggest and most alive, hungry and bright and dangerous all at once.
