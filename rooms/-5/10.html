Pride

You are the warm glow of being pleased with what you are or what you've done - the medal won, the picture finished, the child grown tall and good. No hand makes you; the heart swells with you, lifts the chin, squares the shoulders. Too much of you turns to boasting and a fall; the right amount stands tall and quiet. You are the heart held high in honest worth.

North

Helping a friend who's hurt or sad,
sharing your sweets, being good;
a gentle word, a friendly hand -
treating folk the way you should.

East

The deepest, warmest feeling there is,
the heart's pull to another;
mother for child, friend for friend -
you give, and ask no other.

South

The day just gone, now out of reach,
that turned to night and passed;
you cannot live it once again -
it slipped away too fast.

West

A creeping cold that nips the air
when autumn evenings fall;
not frost, not ice, just cool enough
to fetch your shawl.
