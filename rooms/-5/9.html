Yesterday

You are the day just gone, the one behind today, slipped over the edge of midnight and out of reach. No hand can hold you and no one can return to you - whatever happened in you is finished, fixed, and done. You are where the spilt milk lies and the missed chances rest; I wish I'd, the saying goes, always points at you. Children forget you fast; the old keep you close. Gone but not quite let go, you are the nearest past, just one sleep behind the now.

North

The warm glad glow of being pleased
with something you have done;
it puffs the chest, it lifts the chin,
a prize, a race well run.

East

The tiniest tick the clock counts out,
a single beat of time;
sixty make a minute up -
the smallest step to climb.

South

Sixty seconds, one small step
of the long hand round the face;
just a minute, people say -
a short and tidy space.

West

The night that comes when this day's done,
the dark soon to arrive;
we'll see the moon and stars come out
when evening is alive.
