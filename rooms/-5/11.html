Kindness

You are gentleness made into deeds - the shared sweet, the helping hand, the soft word for someone hurting. No hand makes you; the warm heart gives you freely, asking nothing back. You comfort the crying, you welcome the stranger, you stop to help the fallen. Small acts of you make a hard world soft.

North

The tender sorrow that you feel
for someone hurt or poor;
'oh, the poor thing,' your soft heart says -
you'd help them, to be sure.

East

The heavy downcast teary feeling
when something good is lost;
it slows your step, it bows your head -
a grey and gloomy frost.

South

The warm glow when you've done your best,
chin up and standing tall;
the medal won, the picture praised -
you stand the proudest of all.

West

A light fizzy prickling on the skin -
pins and needles in your foot,
or cold hands buzzing as they warm -
a thousand pinpricks put.
