The Lightless

West of the standstill and south of the edgeless, the dark goes lightless and dead. A step north there was at least a last grey light and a receding horizon; a step east a windless dark that still held the shape of a stopped wind. Here there is neither light nor wind nor edge nor ash — the deep night of the Waste, the void past the last grain of everything, a dark so complete and so dead that it is not the absence of light but the place where light was never so much as rumoured. You cannot see your hand. You cannot see that you have a hand. The dark gives the eye nothing, not even the red swim of true blindness; only the flat dead nothing of a Waste that put the very idea of seeing away long ago.

It is not the living dark of the deep south, that warms and stirs and wakes; this dark is the Waste's, and it has forgotten there was ever anything to remember. You reach for the spiral and find it by feel alone, the whorl in your skin the one thing you can still be sure of — unseen, unprovable, held in a dark that will not witness it because it has forgotten that witnessing is a thing that happens. You could draw the coil in the lightless air a thousand times and the dark would not know: not because it refuses, but because it has lost the very faculty of knowing, gone dead past forgetting into never-having-known.

There is no count and no sound, not even the wind's, not even the pretence of silence-shaped-like-a- sound the shrine once kept; this dark has forgotten that sound exists, and your heartbeat falls into it unheard, and unheardness itself, a beat in a place that has lost the concept of a beat.

To the north the dark lifts toward the edgeless Waste, a last grey light, a wind, a horizon to recede. To the east the void firms to the standstill, a windless dark that still recalls a wind. To the south the lightless dark sinks toward the pole, the deepest dead dark, before even the Waste was wasted. To the west the void widens to the absolute Waste, where nothing was or is or will be, and the dark forgets even itself. You were going somewhere, under a name. The lightless dark has forgotten that names are a thing the world once had; it does not refuse yours, it cannot — and you carry it, and the unseen whorl, on into a dark that has lost the very memory of knowing.
