Universe

You are the universe - everything there is, all at once: every star and planet and galaxy, all the empty black between them, all of space and all of time rolled into one enormous word. No hand made you; you simply are, and you are still getting bigger. No one has found your edge, or knows if you have one. You hold the whole of everything, with room to spare. Vast beyond all imagining, you are the biggest idea a small mind can hold, the everything that everything is part of.
