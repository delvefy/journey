Onion

You are the round white bulb of layers, wrapped in papery skin, pulled from the row in the autumn garden. No hand made you; the dark soil grew you, ring inside ring. Slice me, and I make the cook cry. Cooked soft and golden, I sweeten everything. Peel me down, layer by layer, and at the heart - another layer.

North

A long orange root pulled from the earth,
with a green tuft on its head;
the rabbit's favourite crunchy snack -
the snowman's nose instead.

East

A round red fruit we eat as veg,
soft and full of seeds;
in sauce and soup and salad bowls -
on the vine it feeds.

South

A big loose ball of crinkly leaves,
green and crisp and cool;
you tear it up for salad bowls -
the rabbit's favourite jewel.

West

A hard brown shiny seed of the tree,
in a spiky green case it grows;
you thread it on a string to fight -
a champion, everyone knows.
