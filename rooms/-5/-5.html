The Floorless

South of the effaced and west of the bedrock, the deep loses its floor. A step north even the forgetting had been worn away, the forgotten of the forgotten; a step east lay the bedrock, the absolute bottom on which a world might one day be built. Here that bottom is gone too. The Waste corner takes even the floor — no bedrock now, no foundation, no last surface to settle onto, only a sinking that finds nothing to end against, the absolute void of the corner where the deep past and the dead disenchantment are one floorless nothing, going down without a down to reach.

There is nothing to stand on and nothing to fall toward, which is its own slow horror, gentler than horror: you do not plummet, you only cease to be held, the last floor worn out from under being. You draw the spiral and there is no surface to take it, not even the effaced blank that almost-failed to keep a mark; the gesture has nowhere to land. And yet the whorl in your skin turns still, the one coil that needs no floor, carried into the floorless corner like a thing kept by nothing but your refusal to let it go — the single mark in a place that has lost even the bedrock the bedrock held.

There is no count, and no floor for one to fall on. Your heart beats into the floorless void and the beat does not echo and does not land and is not lost — one warm stroke suspended over nothing, the only thing in all the corner that is, holding without a bottom to hold it, refusing the floorless dark one beat at a time.

To the north the floorless rises to the effaced, a blank that at least almost keeps. To the east the bedrock returns, a floor to be at the bottom of. To the south it sinks deeper toward the corner-pole, the void below the void, where even the floorlessness gives way. To the west the Waste corner absolute, the deepest nothing, where the sinking and the forgetting and the cold are one. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here there is no floor for the going to rest on and no name the floorless can keep — and still the warm coil turns over the nothing, and still one beat hangs unfallen in the floorless dark, the last unsunk thing at the corner of everything.
