Birds nest deep in your leafy tangle, and small creatures scurry safe along your roots. In summer you are thick and green; in winter your bare twigs make a fretwork against the sky. Living and leafy and full of secret life, you are a fence that grows, a boundary you could never climb through, kept tidy by the snip-snip of the gardener's shears.
