Breath

You are breath - the air you draw in and let out, all day and all night, without a thought. It feeds the body with the air it needs and carries your words out into the world. On a frosty morning you can see your own, a little cloud before the lips. Blow out the birthday candles, sigh, pant, or hold it underwater - it is always there. You are the body's quiet tide, in and out, the plain sign that a thing is still alive.
