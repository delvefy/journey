The Terminus

North of the hush and east of the chrome, the dead pole reaches its terminus. The disenchanted future a step east shed its wonder and its world and its mind and stood at last as a final grey; here even that final grey is dissolving, worn off at the edge into the open Waste, the cold post-human end of one long road giving out into the timeless emptiness of the other. This is where the future that killed magic stops being a future at all and becomes simply Waste — the terminus, the dead end, the place the Chrome wears away and the deepest past and the killed future are revealed, at the last, as one dust.

You stand at the dead end of the made world. There is one last cold flicker here, the dead pole's final signal guttering as it dissolves — and you feel it carried the last record of everything the cold age ever was, your name a cold figure in it, dissolving now into the grey with the rest, future and past and made and never-made worn into the one indistinguishable dust. Only the whorl in your skin stays warm and whole, the one thing that is neither the killed future nor the dead past, the single un-dissolved mark at the terminus of both.

The last cold flicker gutters out, and there is no count after it, only the Waste's silence taking the dead pole's final signal — the made hour's very last tick dissolving into a grey that keeps no hours. Your heart beats on past the terminus, the one warm count the dead end could not end.

To the north the terminus reaches the dead pole's absolute last, the cold future's final flicker. To the east the Chrome firms back to its pole, a final grey not yet dissolved. To the south the hush, the cold hum fading. To the west the terminus dissolves wholly into the Waste, future and past one dust, the corner. You were going somewhere, under a name. The dead future held its last cold record of it and is dissolving now into the one grey — and you carry past the terminus the one warm whorl that was never the future's to kill nor the past's to forget.
