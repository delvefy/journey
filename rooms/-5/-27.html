Sapling

You are a sapling - a young tree, slender as a cane and no taller than a child, with a thin smooth trunk that bends right over in the wind. Planted with care and staked up straight, you put out your first few leaves and inch upward year by year. Give you fifty years and you will be a mighty oak. Tender and hopeful and full of growing to do, you are a tree just starting out, the small green promise of the great shady giant you will one day become.
