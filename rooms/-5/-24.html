Thistle

You are a thistle - a tall tough weed armoured all over in spines, with a tuft of purple at the top like a tiny shaving brush. No hand plants you; the rough field and the roadside do, and you spike anyone who grabs you bare-handed. Scotland wears you proudly on its badge. When your purple fades you turn to silver down and blow away on the wind. Prickly and stubborn and oddly handsome, you guard your patch with a thousand little spears.
