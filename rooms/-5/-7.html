The Nethermost

South of the nadir and west of the last dark, the descent runs out of down. A step north the void voided itself, the nadir, the nothing emptied of its emptiness; a step east even the oldest dark dissolved, the last dark gone. Here is the nethermost: not a deeper nothing, for there is no deeper left, but the place where “below” itself gives out, the absolute floor of the void where the long descent has nowhere further to fall. You have come to the bottom of the bottom of everything — the Waste corner-pole entire, where down has no more down to it, and the sinking, after all its floors and voids and darks, simply ends, with nothing beneath it but the fact that there is no beneath.

It is the end of the descent, and stranger than any of its floors: a nothing with no “further,” a bottom that is the last word “below” will ever say. You draw the spiral and the nethermost neither takes it nor voids it nor dissolves it; there is no deeper for it to sink toward, no further nothing to lose it in. And here, at the absolute bottom of everything, the whorl in your skin turns — the one thing that does not need a below, the single mark at the nethermost, kept where the descent itself has ended and there is nowhere left to fall.

There is no count, and no further down for one to fall through; the nethermost is the floor of all floors, below which is only the no-below. And still your heart beats — the one warmth at the absolute bottom of the world, the single having-been where the descent has run out of deeper, refusing the nethermost, beating on where there is nowhere left to sink.

To the north the nethermost rises to the nadir, a void that still had a below. To the east the last dark, the oldest dark dissolving. To the south — if south can be said — the pole's pole, below the no-below. To the west the Waste corner entire, the void exhausted of deeper, the pole. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here the going has run out of down, the descent ended at the nethermost of everything — and still the warm coil turns where there is no below, and still one beat keeps on at the absolute bottom of the world, the one thread the nethermost could not take.
