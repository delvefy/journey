Fist

You are a fist - the hand drawn up into a tight little ball, fingers folded down and the thumb tucked over. You knock on the door, you bump a friend's fist hello, you punch the air when your team wins, you hold a treasure so no one can see it. A baby grips a finger and will not let go. Made of the hand itself, clenched and strong, you are a hand turned hard - all set to knock, to hold, or simply to say 'I mean it'.
