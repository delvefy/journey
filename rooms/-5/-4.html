The Effaced

North of the worn dark and east of the first silence, the deep wears past memory itself. The worn dark a step north was old and dead at once; the source a step east was the silence everything rose from; here, where the two meet at the Waste's edge of the deep past, even those are gone — not forgotten, that would be too much remembering, but effaced: the forgotten of the forgotten, worn so far down that the very fact of having-been is gone, and then the loss of it gone, and then the gone-ness gone, a blank that has lost even the memory of being blank.

There is nothing here to describe, because describing is a kind of keeping and this place keeps nothing, not even its own emptiness. You draw the spiral and it is effaced as you draw it, not blown or sunk or subtracted but worn out of having-been, smoothed past trace, past the trace of trace. Only the whorl in your skin survives, the single un-effaced thing, a mark in a place that has forgotten what marks were, kept by nothing but your refusal to let the effacing reach it.

There is no count, and no memory that counting was. Your heart beats into a blank that does not even fail to hear it, having worn away the faculty of failing — one stroke, un-effaced, the last thing in all the deep that the effacing has not yet reached.

To the north the blank firms to the worn dark, age and deadness still legible. To the east it rises to the first silence, the source still remembered. To the south the effaced wears toward the corner, the absolute void. To the west the Waste corner, where even effacement is effaced. You were going somewhere, under a name. Here the name is effaced past even loss, worn out of ever-having-been — and still the one whorl and the one beat refuse the effacing, the last un-erased things in the forgotten of the forgotten.
