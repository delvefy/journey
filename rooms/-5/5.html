The Two Roads

West of the pall and north of the terminus, the two long roads end at one grey. A step east a pure featureless pall lay shrouding a nothing; a step south the dead pole dissolved at its terminus into the Waste. Here both arrivals are complete, and there is no longer a shroud over the nothing or a future dissolving into the past — only the single grey they both came to, the killed future and the never-enchanted past worn into one dust with no seam between. You stand where the road that shed its wonder to be certain and the road that never had any wonder to shed meet at last, and are the same grey, and cannot be told apart.

It is perfectly even, this grey: no near, no far, no future ahead nor past behind, no machine and no Waste, only a uniform sourceless nothing in every direction at once, the Chrome's over-pole where being dead and being never-born come to the identical end. You cannot say whether you stand at the farthest tip of the future or the deepest floor of the past; the grey does not say, it has worn away the difference. You draw the spiral and it neither dies, there being nothing here to kill it, nor fails to be, there being nothing that never was; it simply is, warm, the one thing the two roads' single end could not flatten — the whorl in your skin, future-proof and past-proof at once.

There is no count, no flicker, no last cold tick; the dead pole's final signal guttered out a step back, the Waste keeps no hours, and here the two silences are one silence. Only your heart beats in the even grey, the one warm count that belongs to neither road, keeping a time the end of the future and the floor of the past have both, identically, forgotten.

To the north the two roads' one grey reaches the over-pole, pure grey nothing absolute. To the east the pall returns, a shroud still laid over the killed future. To the south the terminus, the dead pole still dissolving. To the west it goes wholly to the Waste, the future forgotten past its own ending, grey to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name, on one road or the other. Here both roads end at the same dust and your name is dissolved into both at once — and still the warm coil turns, the mark that travelled neither road, and still one beat keeps on past the place where the future and the past gave out together into the one even grey.
