The Hush

North of the rust plain and east of the last of the made, the hum finishes fading. The cold carrier you have followed this far — the thinning tone of a whole machine-age, traced out over rust and ore — here gives out its last thread and is gone, and what is left is only Waste: grey, level, silent, the made world's final echo quenched into the open ground. This is the threshold where the Chrome ends. Not with a wall or a wreck, but with a silence: the place just past the last sound the made world ever made.

You stand in the hush of it. A breath ago, you feel, there was still a hum — you almost catch the memory of it, the way a room holds the ghost of a bell — and now there is not, and the not is so complete it has the weight of the Waste's deepest dark, only grey and lit instead of black. The last flecks of the made dissolve here: a final scale of plate, a maker's spiral worn to a grey ghost, fading even as you find it. Only the whorl in your skin keeps its turn, the one spiral that does not fade now that the made ones have, the last coil in a country going silent.

The hum was the last count, and the hum is done. No tick, no tone, no carrier, no bell — the whole long lineage of the made hour quenched into the grey, time gone out with the signal. Only your heart, which the made world never kept and so could not silence, beats on into the hush.

To the north the hush lifts toward the Chrome's terminus, the dead pole's last signal at the Waste's edge. To the east the hum returns, the last of the made, a fading field still sounding. To the south the grey firms to the rust plain, the ore-dust of everything made. To the west, past the hush, the open Waste, the made wholly forgotten, grey silence to the pole. You were going somewhere, and the made world carried it, once, in all its signal — counted, filed, hummed across the grey. Here its last thread fades, and your name fades out with it into the hush, the made world's final echo of you going quiet — and still your own heart beats it, unsignalled, on into the silence.
