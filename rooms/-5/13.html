Courage

You are the brave heart that does the hard thing anyway - steps up to speak, faces the dark, stands for the small one against the big. No hand makes you; the will does, even while the knees still shake. You are not the absence of fear but the going-on through it. Find me, they say, and the dragon shrinks.

North

The quiet art of waiting on,
without a whine or fuss;
you wait your turn, you wait the bread to rise -
it asks the most of us.

East

The warm soft ease that calms you down
when you have cried your fill;
a hug, a blanket, gentle words -
they soothe you, and you're still.

South

The tender sorrow that you feel
for someone hurt or poor;
your heart goes soft, you want to help -
you ache for them, that's sure.

West

A wild white storm of driving snow,
so thick you cannot see;
the wind howls hard, the cold bites deep -
stay in, where warm you'll be.
