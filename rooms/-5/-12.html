Bean

You are a little seed grown in a long green pod on a climbing plant - broad bean, runner bean, baked bean in the tin. No hand makes you; you swell in your pod in the sun, then are shelled or sliced for the pot. Jack planted a magic one and it grew to the clouds. Plain, green, and good for you.

North

A field of golden waving stalks
that ripples in the breeze;
its grain is ground to flour for bread -
it sways like golden seas.

East

A bright yellow fruit, all sharp and sour,
that makes your lips pucker tight;
you squeeze it into drinks and pies -
one suck shuts your eyes with fright.

South

A long orange root you pull from the ground,
with feathery leaves on top;
the rabbit loves it, the snowman wears it -
crunch it raw, don't stop.

West

A little beast all covered in spines
that snuffles round at night;
it rolls into a prickly ball
to hide away from fright.
