Patience

You are the quiet strength of waiting - standing in the long queue without a moan, watching the seed for the first green shoot, sitting still while the slow clock turns. No hand makes you; calm will does. The hardest, plainest virtue. Wait, and wait, and do not fret - all things come to the one who has me.

North

When you could be hard but choose to be soft,
and let the beaten go;
the knight spares his fallen foe -
a kindness you bestow.

East

The soft pale red of roses and sweets,
of piglets and a cheek;
a drop of red in a pool of white -
the gentlest shade you seek.

South

The brave heart that does the hard thing,
though the knees may shake;
it faces the dark, it speaks right up -
for the small one's sake.

West

A wild strong wind that bends the trees
and rips the tiles away;
it roars all night and shakes the doors -
a fierce and blowing day.
