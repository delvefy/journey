Pine

You are a pine - the tall straight evergreen that keeps its dark needles all winter while other trees go bare. Your branches hang with hard brown cones, your bark weeps sticky golden sap, and your smell is sharp and clean and cold. You are natural, grown not made, climbing year by year toward the sky. Cut you down at midwinter and hang you with lights, and you become the tree of Christmas in the corner of the room.
