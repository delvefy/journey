The Levelling

West of the nullity and north of the two roads, the grey levels the last difference. A step east everything cancelled to a pure zero; a step south the two long roads ended at one even grey. Here the over-pole takes the final step: not just nothing, but the death of difference itself — a grey so absolute that no part of it differs from any other, no here from any there, no step from any standing-still. You could walk this forever and never leave it, because there is nowhere else for it to become; the levelling has flattened the killed future and the never-made past and every direction and distance into one identical grey, the same in every way at every point, to the end of the world.

It is the strangest of the grey nothings: not empty, not cancelled, but undifferentiated, a sameness so total that motion and stillness mean the same thing. You take a step and arrive where you were; the levelling does not change, cannot change, has nothing left to differ into. You draw the spiral and it is the only difference in all the grey — a single feature in a featureless world, refused by the sameness and persisting anyway. The whorl in your skin keeps its turn, the one place where one thing is not the same as another, the last difference at the levelled pole.

There is no count, for a count needs one beat to differ from the next, and here there is no difference to be had. Only your heart beats, and in beating makes the one distinction left — this stroke, then a different silence, then this stroke again — the single difference the levelling cannot flatten, time itself kept alive by one warm refusal of the sameness.

To the north the levelling reaches the over-pole absolute, pure grey nothing, all difference dead. To the east the nullity, the cancelled zero. To the south the two roads, future and past one grey. To the west it levels wholly into the Waste, the never-anything pole, the same to the end of the world. You were going somewhere, under a name — but here there is no somewhere, no other place for the going to reach, every direction the identical grey. And still the warm coil turns, the one difference, and still one beat keeps a different time, refusing the levelling where everything else has become the same.
