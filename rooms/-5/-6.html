Claw

You are the hooked and horny weapon at the end of the toe, curved for gripping and tearing and holding fast. The cat sheathes you and shows you at will; the hawk locks you round the mouse; the crab waves you on the shore. You climb the bark, you rake the earth, you score a line where you are dragged. Hard as bone and sharp as need, you are how the soft-bodied get a grip on a slippery world - the small tool every hunter wears, and never sets down.

North

By night I hunt on silent wing,
two round eyes wide and bright;
'too-whit, too-whoo' the call I bring,
the wise old bird of night.

East

Up overnight on the damp dark floor,
a small round roof on a stalk;
no leaf, no flower, no seed I store,
I push up soft as chalk.

South

A hard sharp horny mouth I am,
worn proudly by the bird;
I peck the seed, I crack the gram,
no tooth in me is heard.

West

The soft padded foot of cat or bear,
with claws tucked up inside;
it pads the ground without a care,
and swipes from side to side.
