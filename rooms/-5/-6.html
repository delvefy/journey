The Nadir

South of the floorless and west of the unfounded, the void goes below itself. A step north there was no floor to fall toward, only endless sinking; a step east the deep had no foundation, the ground resting on nothing. Here even those give way: the void below the void, the corner-pole, where the floorlessness itself stops holding — for even "there is no floor" needs a there to be said in, and the there is gone. Magic is not forgotten now but forgotten past all asking; the nothing is not empty but emptied of its own emptiness; the descent reaches the nadir, the bottom that has undercut even the having of a bottom.

It is the deepest collapse there is, a nothing folded under nothing until even the folding fails. You do not sink, for sinking needs a down, and the down has given way; you simply are at the nadir, where the void has voided itself. You draw the spiral and there is not even the floorless nowhere to fail to take it; the gesture has no there to happen in. And still — impossibly, at the bottom of every bottom — the whorl in your skin turns, the one coil that holds where even holding has given way, the single mark at the nadir of the corner, refusing the void below the void.

There is no count, and no there for a beat to sound in. Your heart beats into the nadir and there is no void left for it to echo in, no floorlessness for it to fall through, only the bare warm stroke itself, hanging at the bottom of everything where even the bottom has given way — the one thing that is, where even nothing has stopped being able to be.

To the north the nadir rises to the floorless, a sinking that at least still sinks. To the east the unfounded, a deep that at least rests on nothing. To the south the corner-pole absolute, below even the nadir, if below can be said at all. To the west the Waste corner entire, magic forgotten past all asking, the void voided to its pole. You were going somewhere, with someone, once. Here even the void has given way beneath the going, and the name is past all asking — and still the warm coil turns at the nadir, and still one beat hangs, impossibly, where even nothing has given out, refusing the corner of everything.
