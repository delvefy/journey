Nettle

You are a nettle - the green plant of the hedgerow and the ditch whose leaves are covered in tiny stinging hairs. Brush you with a bare leg and you raise an itchy rash of white bumps; the dock leaf growing nearby is the cure. Grasp you firmly, oddly, and you sting less. Soup and tea can be made of you once you are cooked soft. You guard the wild edges where children play. You are a humble green plant that bites back, best left well alone.
