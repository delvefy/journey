Ivy

You are ivy - the dark glossy climber that creeps up walls and old tree-trunks, gripping the stone with a thousand tiny rootlets. Your leaves are shaped like little stars and stay green right through the winter. You can swallow a ruined cottage whole, draping it in deep green. At Christmas you twine through the holly on the mantel. You are the patient green that climbs and clings and never lets go, dressing the old and bare in living leaves.
