Spark

You are a spark - a tiny bright fleck of fire flung up into the dark. You leap from the crackling log, you fly when flint strikes steel, you snap blue from a woolly jumper pulled off in winter. You live for half a heartbeat, glowing fierce, then wink out. Yet from one of you a whole great fire can grow. Made by no hand and gone in a blink, you are the smallest, quickest, most dangerous piece of flame there is.
