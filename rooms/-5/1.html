Air

It is the one thing you cannot do without for more than a minute, and the one thing you never think about at all. Air has no colour, no taste, no edges - you look straight through it to everything else. Yet it fills the room, the sky, your two lungs, and presses on every inch of you all day without your leave. Blow it into a balloon and it pushes back. Set it moving and it becomes the wind. Invisible, weightless, and absolutely everywhere.

North

I am a dark shape on the ground,
I copy you without a sound;
where there is light I'm always near,
but in the dark I disappear.

East

A gentle wind, both light and cool,
I ruffle pond and ripple pool;
I lift the kite and turn the mill,
a soft wee wind that's never still.

South

Held in the hills, I lie so still,
a sheet of glass below the hill;
no salt, no tide, I do not roar,
just lapping softly on my shore.

West

You cannot see or hold me tight,
yet your nose can catch me, day or night;
of roses, bacon, rain, or sea,
just sniff the air and you'll find me.
