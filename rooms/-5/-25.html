Bramble

You are a bramble - a thorny scrambling tangle that climbs the hedge and the fence, hooking everything that passes with its little curved claws, and growing fat sweet blackberries in late summer for the brave-fingered. No hand plants you; you spread on your own, fast and far. You guard your fruit with thorns and stain the picker's hand purple-black. Prickly and generous at once, you are the hedgerow's barbed wire and its harvest both, defended sweetness on a thorny vine.
