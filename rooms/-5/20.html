Smoke

You are smoke - the grey, curling cloud that climbs off anything burning. You rise from a candle just snuffed, from a chimney in the cold, from a great fire roaring in the wood. You sting the eyes and catch in the throat and smell of all you have eaten. You cannot be held, only watched as you twist and thin and vanish into the sky. You are the breath of fire, the proof that something is, or was, alight.
