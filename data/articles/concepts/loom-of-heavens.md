The **Loom of Heavens** is the master frame of the multiverse: a vast tapestry hung in an empty between-place, on which every universe appears as a single stitch of cold fire. [[Threads of Space vs. Karma Threads|Threads of space]] run between the stitches like tunnels drawn through the void. Seen from outside, the whole resembles the inside of an enormous brain — bright cores knotted at the centre by thousands of crossings, and lonely points at the dim edges tied to the rest of creation by only three or four thin threads.

> Bright cores at the heart, lonely points at the rim — and between them, roads no mortal has ever walked.

## Centre and frontier

Central universes are dense and well-connected. Frontier universes hang at the rim with almost no threads at all. Most known stitches are frontier backwaters — which is exactly why almost no one within them has ever suspected the Loom is there. The world of [[Universe: Blaze|Blaze]], tied to creation by only four faint golden threads, is one such rim-stitch.

## The lid above every sky

A dreaded truth surfaces from more than one universe: the sky of a lower world is not open — it is a **lid**, a seal placed from above by higher-realm powers, and the world beneath it is farmed. Ignorance is the keystone of the seal; a world that *knows* what its sky truly is becomes far harder to keep sealed.

The Loom, then, is no neutral tapestry but a hierarchy, its lower stitches kept small and quiet on purpose. As the saying runs across many worlds: *there is always a smaller sky, and a larger one above it.* The mountain beyond the mountain never ends.

## Crossing between stitches

The Loom permits a pattern — a soul — to move between universes by several local mechanisms, which the inhabitants of each world name in their own terms, never suspecting they describe the same act: ascension up the [[The Nine Realms|nine realms]], void-resurrection, divine summoning, judged reincarnation, and engineered gates. These are five names for one door.

## Theories & resonances

The **weaving motif** recurs across three universes that do not know of one another: the Loom itself; the Weavers and their Codex, whose machine has begun detecting *"a current — the same shape repeating across worlds"*; and the found notebooks known as the Weavings. Whether these are three views of one weave remains an open thread the wiki may pull, but must not tie.
