The [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom of Heavens]] is bound by two distinct kinds of thread. Confusing one for the other is a common error among lower cultivators, for only the second becomes visible to them, and only late.

## Threads of space

**Threads of space** are the great roads of the Loom — the tunnels binding universe to universe. Travel between stitches runs along them, and only a being of sufficient power can walk them. A frontier world such as [[Universe: Blaze|Blaze]] hangs from only a handful of these; a central universe is crossed by thousands.

A cultivator gains the capacity to step onto a thread of space at the fifth of [[The Nine Realms|the nine realms]] — Void Stepping — when a small inner world first opens within them.

## Karma threads

**Karma threads** are finer and private: the web running *between souls* rather than between worlds. Debt, love, grudge, the pull of master to inheritor, the alignment of a destiny — each is a cord in this web.

Karma is invisible to mortals. The heaviest cords begin to glow to advanced cultivators at Soul Expansion; to the very highest beings, nearly all of it is legible. Fate can be *read* along a karma thread and — very carefully — nudged. It was by reading his own karma along such a thread that [[Skolar]] recognised a trajectory in a distant child that mirrored his own.

> Space threads bind worlds. Karma threads bind souls. They are not the same web, and the wise do not mistake the one for the other.

## Theories & resonances

The Weavers' Codex reports a *"current — the same shape repeating across worlds."* Whether that current is a karma thread read by a machine that does not know what it holds is an open question. The wiki may theorise; it may not conclude.
