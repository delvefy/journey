**Ghor** is the protagonist and first-person narrator of [[Universe: The Engine|the Engine]] — a man born a Neanderthal around 45,000 BC, dead at fourteen, and entangled ever since with the void entity [[Vael]]. Across a hundred thousand years he has died and woken again life after life, and he has buried more people than any city holds.

> I have had more names than most men have years. Adam is the one that keeps coming back.

## Origin

Ghor was born a Neanderthal — short, broad, cold-adapted — and the body he wakes in is always recognizably him. His first death at fourteen bound him to Vael, and from that binding came [[The Engine|void-resurrection]]: when he dies, his pattern is pulled through the Void and reseeded in a new body on a compatible world, by the Planet Rule that sends him roughly every other life back to Earth. He cannot choose when, where, or into which body.

## Character and powers

He is weary and long-tempered, amused more often than angry, having outlived every attachment many times over. Off-world lives are hostile and usually solitary, and the loneliness — not the dying — is the true cost of what he is. His powers are all Vael's gift, grown slowly: the [[The Engine|Hoard]] that carries his keepsakes undecayed across millennia; the discipline of absolute bodily control and the berserker organ called the Bell; and slow-acquired knacks — perfect recall, silent movement, fast language-learning, minor animal-sway, going unseen. He is emphatically **not a god**; a bullet still hurts him. Only what happens after is different.

## Names

He goes by many names, **Adam** most of all — by his own account "older than the Hebrew" — and also Yam, Adan, Adem, Adamski, Adam Cory, "the Stranger," and more. Deep-history figures cross his lives: Spring Drop, One Eye, and Rat, his first killer; his most recent crew aboard the *Long Stoop* were Mira at the helm, one-eyed Onsho on the guns, and Pinch.

## Theories & resonances

His most-used name, **Adam**, invites a resonance the wiki tracks but does not assert: a recurring first-man archetype seeded across the [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom]] — echoed in summoned champions, in the first-men of other worlds, and in the "second pair of eyes" that [[Skolar]] seeks. Whether these are one pattern or many is left open.

## Open hooks

Why Vael chose him, of all the dead; whether the Weavers' detected "current" is the wake of his crossings; the Hoard's true ceiling; and the vast tally of off-world lives still unrecorded, any of which might yet be told.
