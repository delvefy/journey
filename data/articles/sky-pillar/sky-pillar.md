**Sky Pillar** is a xianxia universe told from a non-human point of view — the slow, patient rise of a sentient tree up the cultivation ladder — and one of the [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom's]] stitches. Its protagonist is not a person who finds power but a plant that wakes into it.

## The world

The setting is **Yunus Planet**, whose "Small Southern Archipelago" alone holds hundreds of continents. Its cultivation ladder runs Qi Condensation → Foundation → Core Formation → **Hegemon** → **Monarch** → **Autarch**. Divine sense — spiritual perception — reaches hundreds of kilometres for a Core Formation cultivator and thousands for a Monarch. Monarchs form inner worlds, pocket universes of their own; Autarchs create galaxies and can eventually roam between worlds.

## Power system

Yunus runs on qi-cultivation, but its ladder maps onto the wider [[The Nine Realms|nine realms]] of the Loom at the top: a Monarch's inner world answers to the small inner world that opens at Void Stepping, and an Autarch's galaxy-making and world-roaming answer to the upper rungs where a cultivator first steps between stitches. The [[Ten Primordial Daos|Dao of Life]] figures heavily in its protagonist's survival.

## Key characters

- **[[Evergreen]]** — a sapling that woke to sentience after absorbing the blood of a dying cultivator, and climbed from City Guardian to enslaver-ruler to Monarch, a thousand kilometres and more in height.
- **Dedalus** — the boy who first spoke to Evergreen; he grows into a Hegemon and dies trying to liberate the city Evergreen came to rule.
- **Davion** — an ancient Monarch who, with the Monarch **Snowy Wood**, comes to investigate the massive energy-drain Evergreen causes.
- **Sage Qilin, Undying Summer, Blind Evening** — Hegemons of the region; one killed, one enslaved, in Evergreen's rise.

## Key events

Awakening from a dying cultivator's blood; growing ear-shaped leaves to learn human speech; the long climb to Core Formation; a near-total destruction by distant immortals and a decades-long regrowth, during which the Dao of Life is learned; the turn to enslaver-ruler by soul-marks; the killing of Dedalus; and the ascent toward Monarch as elder cultivators converge on the drain.

## Theories & resonances

Evergreen's origin — waking from the blood of a dead cultivator — is a case of the [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom's]] scattered-seed thread: dying high-realm beings appear across the weave to leave themselves in lower stitches, as a hunted master does in [[Universe: Blaze|Blaze]] and a dead immortal does within a relic elsewhere. Whether these are attempts to cheat severance or to plant inheritors is a theory only. So too is the resonance between the god-tree and the barrack-parable [[Alden]] tells of a devouring oak that must be made to die.

## Open hooks

What grows in Evergreen's pocket inner-world; who the distant immortals were that nearly killed it; whether an unbodied intelligence can climb the whole ladder at all; and the coming clash with Davion's generation.
