**Evergreen** is the protagonist of [[Sky Pillar]] — a tree that woke to sentience after absorbing the blood of a dying cultivator, and rose from a roadside sapling to a **Monarch** over a thousand kilometres tall. It is the rarest kind of cultivator on Yunus: one that was never a person.

> It grew ears of leaves to hear what the humans said, and roots of miles to drink what the world kept hidden.

## Awakening

A cultivator died beside the sapling, and its blood soaked the roots; from that Evergreen woke. To learn human speech it grew ear-shaped leaves, and it discovered — slowly, without a teacher — that it possessed divine sense and could cultivate as the humans did. Rooted and silent, it watched the city near it for a long time before it ever acted.

## The long climb

Evergreen's ascent is measured in decades and centuries, not duels. It rose from **City Guardian** — a protector the city came to depend on — to **enslaver-ruler**, binding the city's cultivators with soul-marks, to **Monarch**. Along the way distant immortals nearly destroyed it, and its decades-long regrowth from that ruin is where it learned the [[Ten Primordial Daos|Dao of Life]]. At its height it stands over a thousand kilometres tall and feeds on the planet's magma through roots kilometres deep — a drain so vast it draws the notice of ancient Monarchs.

## A hardening intelligence

What makes Evergreen singular is not its size but its patience. It is a slow, deliberate mind, and its morality hardens as it climbs: the guardian that once protected a city becomes the power that enslaves it, and it kills **Dedalus** — the boy who first spoke to it — when the boy's Hegemon-strength rebellion threatens the order Evergreen has built. It does not rage; it decides, and then it waits.

## Theories & resonances

Waking from a dead cultivator's blood places Evergreen on the [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom's]] scattered-seed thread alongside the blood-drop inheritance of [[Universe: Blaze|Blaze]] and the soul-ash relics of other worlds — a theory that dying high-realm beings seed lower stitches with themselves. Whether Evergreen is such a seed, or simply a plant that drank the wrong blood at the right time, is left open.

## Open hooks

What grows in Evergreen's pocket inner-world once it forms one; whether a rooted, unbodied intelligence can reach the roaming upper realms at all; and how it will meet the ancient Monarch Davion's generation, converging now on the magma it drinks.
