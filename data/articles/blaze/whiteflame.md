**Whiteflame** is the master from whom [[Rin]] inherits the [[The Blaze Tempering|Blaze Tempering]] in [[Universe: Blaze|Blaze]]. Hunted and dying, he did not simply die: he scattered his soul into **a hundred blood-drops** cast across the stitches of [[The Loom of Heavens|the Loom]], so that some fragment of him might land where his enemies could not follow and wake again.

> A hundred sparks thrown into the dark, on the chance that one lands where a fire is wanted.

## Role and history

Whiteflame was a body cultivator — a walker of the same hidden road he later leaves to Rin — and a hunted one, pursued by enemies the drafts have not yet named. His dying act was a dispersal: a hundred drops of blood, each carrying a fragment of his soul, flung across the weave. One of them settled in the waterfall cave below Scarlet City, in the pearl that ignited Rin's road. His surviving **wisp** teaches Rin the first layers of the Blaze Tempering and, with them, leaves a revenge debt — the price of the inheritance is that Rin carries Whiteflame's unfinished war.

## Powers and techniques

Whiteflame walked the [[The Blaze Tempering|Blaze Tempering]], the concealed body-cultivation road that consumes qi rather than storing it. His signature act, though, is the scattering itself: a dying master's dispersal of his own pattern into a hundred seeds, a technique aimed less at survival than at continuation — the road preserved even if the man is not.

## Relationships

- **[[Rin]]** — the slum orphan whose road one of the hundred blood-drops seeded; Whiteflame's inheritor, and the carrier of the revenge debt attached to the gift.
- **His enemies** — the hunters who forced the scattering, unnamed in the drafts and left as an open hook.

## Open hooks

Who Whiteflame's enemies were and whether they still hunt his fragments; where the other ninety-nine blood-drops fell, and what woke where they landed; and whether any two fragments could ever meet.

## Theories & resonances

Whiteflame is the clearest instance of [[The Scattered Master|the scattered-master thread]] — a dying high-realm being seeding lower stitches with himself. The pattern recurs: the [[The Hollow Lantern|Hollow Lantern]] of [[The Nine Vaults Continent|the Nine Vaults Continent]] holds a dead immortal's soul-ash, and a dying cultivator's blood wakes [[Evergreen]] in [[Universe: Sky Pillar|Sky Pillar]]. Whether such scattering is a way to cheat the [[The Nine Realms|severance]] that exiles a Primordial God, or to plant an inheritor who will climb where the master could not, the wiki leaves open.
