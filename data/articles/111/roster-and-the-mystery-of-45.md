The **Roster of the Hundred-Eleven** carries, at rank **#45**, a soldier named **Blaze** — the same name as the protagonist-lineage of an entire other stitch of the [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom]]. This is **Anchor B**, one of the multiverse's only two confirmed cross-universe crossings, and unlike its sibling anchor it is a knot the wiki is forbidden to untie. *(The other is [[Skolar]], Anchor A.)*

> The Grimoire holds thousands of names. One of them belongs to a world that has never heard of Veleh.

## What is confirmed

Two facts stand as canon, and no more:

- On the roster of [[Universe: The Hundred-Eleven|the Hundred-Eleven]], soldier **#45 is named Blaze** — the same name as the protagonist-lineage of [[Universe: Blaze]].
- The narrator [[Alden]] holds **#82**. So the roster of the company and the world of Blaze share at least one figure.

That the link is real, and that it runs through the [[The Grimoire|Grimoire's]] roster, is settled. Everything past that is a thread.

## What stays open

The **how** is deliberately unresolved, and the wiki must resist closing it. The seed poses the question in three unanswered forms, and the wiki may deepen each without choosing between them:

1. Does the fire-road of Blaze — the Blaze Tempering — end with its bearer bound into a grimoire company in another world?
2. Is the Hundred-Eleven a company that recruits *across the stitches of the Loom*? Its Grimoire holds thousands of names though only 111 are ever bound — far more than one world's dead could fill.
3. Is "Blaze" a title passed down through bearers, rather than a single person — so that the name, not the man, is what crosses?

## Why it is the harder anchor

Anchor A can be walked as a single soul's biography, from the Charlatan's sealed rebirth to Skolar's severed godhood. Anchor B offers no such spine — only a name on a roster and a warning against filling the gap. It is the multiverse's clearest proof that stitches connect, paired with its firmest refusal to explain how. A thread the reader may pull; never a knot already tied.

## Open hooks

Whether the thousands of names in the Grimoire are drawn from across the weave; whether Blaze #45 remembers a fire-road, a grey planet, or a waterfall; and whether the [[The Five Doors|door]] by which a soul reaches the roster is one of the known five, or a sixth the wiki has not yet named.
