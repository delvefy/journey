**Alden** — born **Sadim** — is the narrator of [[Universe: The Hundred-Eleven|the Hundred-Eleven]], an eighteen-year-old orphan of Tullanon who swore on the [[The Grimoire|Grimoire]] for food and can never unswear it. Ranked **#82** at his oath, he falls to **#39** as the roster bleeds. His journal is the whole of the saga, and it is honest in ways his life cannot be.

> The Grimoire will not let me put down the sword. It has never said a word about the pen.

## Origin and history

Alden grew up in **Tullanon**, a floating clam-town on rotting pillars over swamp water. His dead adoptive father — "Pa" — taught him to read, the single skill that makes his chronicle possible. He took the oath not out of ambition but hunger, and the brand closed the only door out. The company nicknames him **"Paymaster."**

## The chronicler under the brand

What sets Alden apart is not strength but witness. Bound like the rest, compelled to kill by the [[The Grimoire|Grimoire]] that feeds on the company's dead, he records the campaign plainly — the sieges, the bribery, the renumbering of the dead above him — and his account grows number arc by arc as trauma wears the feeling out of him. His writing is where the company's cruelties are kept, including the ones he committed.

## Relationships

- **Mira** — a baker's daughter he loved at the Rift, murdered by one of his own company. Her death is the hinge of his character, the point after which the journal changes.
- **Elric, the Centurion (#1)** — the company's ancient commander, loyal to the contract and not to any man in it; Alden studies him without ever being trusted by him.
- **Malkiron** — the brutal squad-leader who mentored Alden in the Pattern before dying.

## Theories & resonances

Alden dwells on a barrack-myth — the Day-61 story of an oak sapling that grows into a city, takes too much, and chooses to die so something cleaner can grow — which he says "reads like the soul-oath in my own brand." The same image of a devouring tree that must be made to die recurs, unknowing, in the [[Sky Pillar|god-tree of Yunus]]; whether that is a shared parable of the [[The Loom of Heavens|Loom]] or a coincidence of two frontier worlds is a thread the wiki may pull, not tie.

## Open hooks

Whether Alden ever falls far enough up the renumbered roster to face the Centurion's secrets; what becomes of his journal after the long retreat home; and whether a chronicler who merely records the Grimoire, rather than commanding it, matters to whatever the book is truly feeding.
