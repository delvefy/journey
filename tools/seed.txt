================================================================================
THE LOOM OF HEAVENS — MULTIVERSE SEED
A worldbible for a neverending wiki
================================================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This file is the seed for an ever-expanding wiki (a "wikia") of a single
multiverse — the LOOM OF HEAVENS — under which every story in this writing
folder is a separate universe. It gathers, in one place, the worlds, the
characters, the power systems, the events, and (most important for a wiki that
must keep growing) the OPEN HOOKS that can be expanded into new articles
forever.

The seed does not tell the stories. It maps them, so that lore, events, and
characters can be spun out into wiki entries indefinitely without contradicting
what the source drafts already establish.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
0. HOW TO USE THIS SEED (rules for the wiki generator)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CANON DISCIPLINE. This is a LOOSE multiverse with LATENT links. That means:

  1. Each universe is self-contained. Its own physics, its own cast, its own
     history. Treat it as a standalone world first.

  2. The LOOM OF HEAVENS is the shared frame that contains them all — but the
     inhabitants of almost every universe do NOT know the Loom exists, do not
     know other universes exist, and have never met anyone from another stitch.

  3. Cross-universe connections are, by default, LATENT — hooks, resonances,
     and theories, NOT confirmed facts. The wiki may write "Theories" and
     "Resonances" sections that speculate about links. It must NOT state a
     crossover as canon unless this seed explicitly marks it CONFIRMED.

  4. There are exactly TWO CONFIRMED cross-universe anchors (see Section 2).
     Everything else stays a thread the reader may pull, never a knot already
     tied.

TIERS. Universes are marked by how much source material exists:
  - [ESTABLISHED]  — has real drafted chapters. Highest canon weight.
  - [SKETCH]       — has a premise, meta notes, maybe a partial draft.
  - [BLUEPRINT]    — fully outlined but unwritten.
  - [META]         — the frame layer (the Weavings). Handle with care.

When source drafts and this seed disagree, the DRAFTS win for that universe.
When expanding, prefer to fill OPEN HOOKS over inventing new top-level facts.

ARTICLE TYPES the wiki should support: Universe, Character, Location, Power
System / Cultivation Path, Faction / Sect / Organization, Event, Artifact,
Species, Concept (cosmology), and Thread (an open mystery tracked across
articles).

1. THE MASTER COSMOLOGY — THE LOOM OF HEAVENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE WEAVE. Above and between all worlds hangs the Loom of Heavens: a vast
tapestry in an empty between-place, on which every universe is a single stitch
of cold fire. Threads of space run between the stitches like tunnels drawn
through the void; from outside, the whole resembles the inside of an enormous
brain — bright cores knotted at the center by thousands of crossings, lonely
little points at the dim edges tied to the rest of creation by only three or
four thin threads.

Central universes are dense and well-connected. Frontier universes hang at the
rim with almost no threads at all. Most worlds in this seed are frontier
stitches — backwaters — which is exactly why almost no one in them has ever
suspected the Loom is there.

TWO KINDS OF THREAD.
  - THREADS OF SPACE: the great roads of the Loom, the tunnels binding universe
    to universe. Travel between stitches runs along these. Only a being of
    sufficient power can walk them.
  - KARMA THREADS: the finer, private web running BETWEEN souls — debt, love,
    grudge, the pull of master to inheritor, the alignment of a destiny. Karma
    is invisible to mortals; the heaviest cords begin to glow to advanced
    cultivators; to the very highest beings nearly all of it is legible. Fate
    can be READ along a karma thread, and — very carefully — nudged.

THE LADDER (the known ascent, in cultivation-type universes). The road of
cultivation has nine realms:
    1. Spirit Gathering      — first sense and draw of qi
    2. Foundation Forging    — body, meridians, dantian made into a vessel
    3. Core Condensation     — qi wrought into a permanent core
    4. Soul Expansion        — the soul widened; spiritual sense sharpens;
                               heavy karma begins to glow
    5. Void Stepping         — a small inner world opens; travel between
                               universes becomes possible
    6. Heaven Harmonizing    — the inner world expands into an inner galaxy
    7. Divine Seed           — an inner universe takes seed; mastery of at least
                               one Dao
    8. Dao Divinity          — godhood, scaled to the number of Daos mastered
    9. Primordial God        — the peak; all ten primordial Daos fused into the
                               being; stands outside any single universe

Across most universes no one has ever passed the middle realms. Across the
entire Loom, in all the eras of all creation, only a handful have ever reached
the ninth realm at all.

THE PRICE OF THE NINTH REALM: SEVERANCE. The moment a cultivator completes the
ninth realm — the moment all ten primordial Daos fuse into their being — every
thread of space binding them to their home universe snaps at once. They rise
out of their world into the empty between-place above the Loom. Alone. A
Primordial God is exiled by their own ascension.

THE TEN PRIMORDIAL DAOS (the fundamental laws all lesser Daos descend from):
  - Two polarities: the DAO OF ORDER (shape, position, consequence) and the
    DAO OF CHAOS (change, drift, consumption).
  - Four Daos of Order: Time, Space, Life, Death.
  - Four Daos of Chaos: Wind, Earth, Fire, Water.
  - Every smaller Dao — Sword, Healing, Lightning, Deception, and the rest —
    is a child of these.

THE LID ABOVE EVERY SKY (the political truth of the Loom). A recurring, dreaded
truth surfaces from more than one universe: the sky of a lower world is not open
— it is a LID, a seal placed from above by higher-realm powers, and the lower
world beneath it is FARMED. Ignorance is the keystone of the seal; a world that
KNOWS what its sky really is becomes far harder to keep sealed. This is the
cosmology's cruelty: the Loom is not a neutral tapestry but a hierarchy, its
lower stitches kept small and quiet on purpose. "There is always a smaller sky,
and a larger one above it." The mountain beyond the mountain never ends.

HOW SOULS CROSS BETWEEN STITCHES. The Loom permits a pattern (a soul) to move
between universes by several local mechanisms — which the inhabitants of each
world explain in their own terms, never suspecting they are all the same act:
  - ASCENSION: climbing the ladder past Void Stepping (cultivation worlds).
  - VOID-RESURRECTION: a void entity pulling a dead pattern into a new body on
    a compatible world (the Engine universe).
  - DIVINE SUMMONING: a god or an entity calling a champion out of one world
    into another (Champion of the Light; the Tavern).
  - JUDGED REINCARNATION: a soul weighed by a bureaucracy of death and reseeded
    into a new life, sometimes across worlds (the Charlatan).
  - ENGINEERED GATES: technology weaponizing the same seam (the Exodus
    stargates of the Engine universe).
These are five names for one door.

THE WEAVING MOTIF (a resonance to track, not yet a fact). Three universes carry
the same image without knowing the others exist:
  - the LOOM OF HEAVENS itself (a weave of worlds);
  - the WEAVERS and their CODEX, a far-future civilization whose machine has
    begun detecting "a current — the same shape repeating across worlds";
  - the WEAVINGS — a collection of found notebooks and lost memoirs (the meta
    layer).
Whether the Weavers are unknowingly reading the Loom, whether the Codex's
"current" is a karma thread, and whether the Found Notebooks are fragments
recovered from across the weave — all of this is OPEN. The wiki may theorize.
It may not conclude.

2. THE CONFIRMED SPINE (the only crossovers treated as canon)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ANCHOR A — SKOLAR: THE CHARLATAN WHO BECAME A PRIMORDIAL GOD. [CONFIRMED]
The being who floats above the Loom as the Primordial God SKOLAR (see Universe:
Blaze) and the reincarnated con-artist cultivator SKOLAR (see Universe: The
Charlatan) are ONE SOUL across different eras. The Charlatan is the mortal
origin — or an early incarnation — of the entity who eventually climbs all nine
realms to the top of the Loom.

  Why it fits: the Charlatan's soul is unusually strong (measured at mid-Hegemon
  strength) and was SEALED by the Judges of Reincarnation, yet he kept his
  memories anyway — the mark of a pattern too heavy to fully bind. His chosen
  Daos are DECEPTION and SPACETIME. The Primordial God Skolar's signature act is
  the grandest deception in the multiverse: shaping a qi-less double of himself
  and wrapping it in seven veils of TIME, SPACE, and ORDER so that not even
  another Primordial God can see it. The con artist grew up to fool the Heavens
  themselves.

  This is the SPINE of the whole multiverse — the one soul the wiki can follow
  from the bottom of the ladder to the empty place above the Loom. Everything
  between the Charlatan's sealed rebirth and Skolar's lonely eternity is open
  territory the wiki may expand: which universes he climbed through, whom he
  loved and lost, when the severance took him, and how many eras he waited
  before he found the second pair of eyes (see Blaze).

ANCHOR B — THE HUNDRED-ELEVEN ROSTER SHARES A NAME WITH BLAZE. [CONFIRMED]
The mercenary company of Universe: 111 (the Hundred-Eleven) has, on its roster,
soldier #45 named BLAZE — the same name as the protagonist-lineage of Universe:
Blaze. The narrator of 111, ALDEN, is soldier #82. So the roster of the
Hundred-Eleven and the world of Blaze share at least one figure.

  What is CONFIRMED: the name/character link and the shared roster.
  What is OPEN (a Thread): HOW. Does the cultivator's fire-road ("Blaze
  Tempering") end with its bearer bound into a grimoire company in another
  world? Is the Hundred-Eleven a company that recruits ACROSS stitches of the
  Loom (its human-skin Grimoire holds thousands of names though the company is
  only 111 strong)? Is "Blaze" a title passed down rather than a person? The
  wiki should keep this a mystery and resist resolving it prematurely.

Everything NOT in this section is latent. See Section 4 for the resonance
catalogue.

UNIVERSE: BLAZE   [ESTABLISHED]   —   the frontier cultivation world & the Loom
================================================================================
TYPE: Xianxia / cultivation progression. This universe also HOLDS the master
frame (the Loom of Heavens is seen from here, in the prologue).

THE WORLD. A small grey planet at the dim edge of the Loom — thin qi, shallow
seas, tied to the rest of creation by only four faint golden threads of space.
Its sects reach no higher than Spirit Gathering, with rare Foundation Forging
elders at the heads of the great city walls. A backwater, worth no second glance
— except for a destiny about to be born on it.

KEY LOCATIONS:
  - SCARLET CITY — the main city; concentric wards from a red-tiled crown down
    to the merchant districts, craftsman wards, tenements, and the SLUMS on the
    eastern flank beyond a dry, bone-littered moat. Contains a hidden
    cultivators' market.
  - THE EASTERN FOREST, THE RIVER, THE WATERFALL CAVE — where Rin is swept over
    the falls and finds the pearl that ignites his road.
  - VANCIA — a northern farming village; site of the qi-stone warehouse heist.
  - THE GREY MIRE, THE GREAT GREY CLIFF — the poison bog and thousand-meter wall
    of the Sky Sect candidate race.
  - THE SKY SECT COMPOUND & OUTER SLOPE — white-walled sect on the highest hill;
    a true, larger headquarters sits on a different continent.
  - THE CRIMSON FOOTHILLS / CRIMSON MOUNTAIN — highland rumored to hide
    reclusive body-cultivation masters (a name: "Blue Fire").
  - THE DEAD MOUNTAINS — a lifeless range where Skolar's veiled avatar sits.
  - THE LOOM OF HEAVENS & the empty between-place above it — Skolar's vantage.

POWER SYSTEMS:
  - The standard nine-realm qi-gathering LADDER (see Section 1).
  - THE BLAZE TEMPERING — Rin's secret BODY-cultivation road. Forty layers
    across seven Condensations. It does not store qi in the dantian; it CONSUMES
    qi, igniting it via an [Igniting Sigil] at the solar plexus and burning
    impurities out through the skin as slag. Each layer doubles raw strength,
    density, and durability. Its bearer reads to any standard test as an
    ordinary mortal with disconnected meridians — invisible by design.
  - QI MECHANICS: qi drawn from air, herbs, pills, qi stones (marble-sized
    crystals of crystallized qi), and spirit veins; stored in the dantian and
    carried by meridians. Qi deviation and overdraw kill.

KEY CHARACTERS:
  - RIN — the protagonist; the "bearer" of the rare destiny from the prologue.
    A red-haired former slum orphan; born meridian-defective ("a bucket with no
    bottom"), which closes the standard road but makes him perfect for the Blaze
    Tempering. Secret body cultivator, thief, patient and cold under pressure,
    illiterate at first (learning letters). Carries revenge debts. UNAWARE that a
    Primordial God has chosen his trajectory.
  - SKOLAR — the Primordial God; the only being ever to reach the ninth realm
    and read the Loom from above. Gaunt, white-haired, ancient, dry-humored;
    severed from his home universe for eras. Discovers Rin's karma trajectory
    mirrors his own and could carry Rin to the ninth realm — a possible second
    Primordial God, "a second pair of eyes." Cannot go himself (his karmic
    weight would crush the fragile destiny — a mountain set beside a pebble), so
    he pinches a wisp from his own TRUESOUL, shapes a qi-less double, wraps it in
    SEVEN VEILS (3 Time, 2 Space, 2 Order), and sends it to wait on a dead
    mountain for the child's birth, two hundred years off. Its only orders:
    "Watch. Wait. Do not interfere." [SPINE — same soul as the Charlatan.]
  - WHITEFLAME — a hunted body-cultivation master who scattered his dying soul
    into a hundred blood-drops across the Loom; one drop seeds Rin's road. His
    wisp teaches Rin the first layers and leaves a debt of revenge as the price
    of inheritance. [Multiverse hook: a master scattered ACROSS the weave.]
  - VIOLET — scout of Myron's bandit crew; black hair, purple eyes, knife
    thrower; Rin's mentor and love interest; vanishes after the massacre,
    leaving a letter Rin can't yet read pointing to a place called Sisma.
  - MYRON'S CREW — the bandit chief Myron, his wife Wanda, and others; a
    surrogate family, massacred by Barza.
  - BARZA — Foundation Forging Sky Sect enforcer; bladed chains; slaughtered
    Myron's crew as a casual contract; a primary revenge target who does not
    recognize Rin as the survivor.
  - AVALYN — arrogant formation-master's daughter and race rival; her Heaven-
    ranked [Eight Directions Binding Net] fails against Rin.
  - MENG — Rin's blinded childhood friend, whom Rin means to return for.
  - Others: the Sky Sect outer-slope circle (Pim, Sela, Garron, Bren, Old Cobb);
    watchers who sense something wrong with Rin (Yorrin, Kell); the vendetta
    list (Roian the betrayer, the merchant Liu family, Young Master Liu).

KEY EVENTS (Volume 1): the slum heist and blinding of Young Master Liu; Rin's
broken legs and flight; the waterfall and the awakening of the Blaze Tempering;
months with Myron's crew; the Vancia qi-stone heist; the massacre and Violet's
disappearance; the ruin of the Slums (Meng blinded, guardians killed); selling
one qi stone for a fortune; the Sky Sect candidate race (mire, beast, cliff);
arrival on the outer slope with his nature still hidden.

OPEN HOOKS: Skolar's two-century vigil and the moment of Rin's birth; who
Whiteflame's enemies are and where the other 99 blood-drops fell; Violet's past
and Sisma; the true Sky Sect headquarters and the higher continents; Rin's
climb toward Void Stepping (the realm at which HE could step between stitches);
the far-horizon seeds (a "Limitless Company" mercenary guild; an "Eternal
Emperor"; a watership named Murky Waters).

UNIVERSE: 111 — THE HUNDRED-ELEVEN   [ESTABLISHED]   —   the grimoire company
================================================================================
TYPE: Grim military dark-fantasy. A journal-narrated mercenary saga.

THE WORLD. VELEH — a fractured vassal kingdom squeezed between three empires:
MECHIL (northeast), BLORIA (northwest), and USTRUS (southeast), which has ruled
Veleh by proxy for fifty years. Medieval-equivalent, with magic but NO
cultivation. Its politics are modeled on real history — the seed folder keeps a
chronology of Michael the Brave (Mihai Viteazul), 1594-1600, as the structural
template: a three-way squeeze, a claimant prince, tax-farming despots, shifting
alliances, and betrayal.

KEY LOCATIONS: TULLANON (a floating clam-town on rotting pillars over swamp
water, the narrator's birthplace); OCRISTAN (the walled capital); THE RIFT (the
siege fortress of seven kimberstone towers); BEIT HAIFUD (the southern imperial
capital of Ustrus); STROVA (the eastern coast); TRANTAR (the late-book battle
city); rivers Olta and Vorn Danu.

POWER SYSTEM: THE GRIMOIRE — a parasitic, human-skin-bound book. A recruit
swears an oath on it, takes the "111" brand, and is bound body and soul into the
company. The Grimoire grants strength, endurance, magic-resistance, and clean
healing — and COMPELS killing to feed itself; it can lock a bound soldier's
muscles. It BURNS mages who try to swear; women's oaths "don't take." Its pages
hold THOUSANDS of names though the company is only ever 111 strong — a hook
toward reincarnation, long cycles, or recruitment across worlds. Rank is a
number (#1 the Centurion down to #111); as soldiers die, the survivors are
renumbered downward. Trained combat is called "the Pattern."

KEY CHARACTERS:
  - ALDEN (born Sadim), #82 → #39 — the narrator; an 18-year-old orphan of
    Tullanon who joins for food and cannot leave. His journal is honest in ways
    his life can't be; trauma numbs him arc by arc. Nicknamed "Paymaster." His
    dead adoptive father "Pa" taught him to read.
  - ELRIC, the Centurion (#1) — ancient, cold, from a wild land across the
    Eastern Sea; has outlived many companies; loyal to the contract, not to
    people. Hints of a very long life. [Hook: how long, exactly, and how?]
  - BARON MIHAIL — the employer; a candidate for Veleh's throne; rumored
    revenant (waxy-pale, wolf-gold eyes, never ages), quietly backed by Bloria.
  - The Deans (#2-#11): MALKIRON (Alden's brutal-mentor squad leader; dies),
    FARRICK, THORNE ("you've got the right kind of luck, boy"), KELLEN, ZANE,
    and others.
  - MIRA — a baker's daughter Alden loves at the Rift; murdered by one of his
    own; her death is the hinge of his character.
  - BLAZE — soldier #45. [CONFIRMED cross-universe anchor with Universe: Blaze.]
  - Antagonist-adjacent: Voievod OLEGZANDER (tax-farming despot, later
    assassinated); Lord ANDREJ (the Rift's defender).

KEY EVENTS: the oath at Tullanon; the siege and sack of the Rift (and Mira's
murder); the gold-heist embassy to Beit Haifud; the assassination/bribery
campaign that buys Veleh's council; the incited peasant revolt in the west; the
disastrous battle and retreat at Trantar; the long bleed home as the roster
falls from 82 to 39.

OPEN HOOKS: the true nature and origin of the Grimoire (the thousands of names);
Elric's impossibly long service; the mechanism of Anchor B (how "Blaze" comes to
be #45); the boy's Day-61 myth of the oak sapling that grows into a city, takes
too much, and chooses to die so something cleaner can grow — which Alden says
"reads like the soul-oath in my own brand" (a possible loom/cycle parable).

UNIVERSE: THE ENGINE (Neanderthal / EngineNeanderthal)   [ESTABLISHED]
================================================================================
TYPE: Immortal-wanderer science-fantasy spanning 100,000 years and 50 galaxies.
"EngineNeanderthal" is the developed version; "neanderthal" the earlier draft.

THE COSMOLOGY. The setting spans the Local Group and its neighbours — the ~50
nearest galaxies. The MILKY WAY has five planets in play (EARTH is Planet 1);
each other galaxy has three. Named early worlds: PLANAR (toxic-plant world),
BALOR (wet, high-gravity Earth-analog), LYRIA (near-Earth twin that independently
evolved a near-Neanderthal species). The far future adds ANDROMEDA and the
Weavers' station (~55,000 AD).

THE MECHANIC: VOID-RESURRECTION. Death is a doorway. When the body fails, the
void entity VAEL pulls the wanderer's pattern through the Void and reseeds it in
a new body on a biocompatible world. THE PLANET RULE: die on Earth, wake off-
world; die off-world, usually wake on Earth. Roughly every other life is Earth.
He cannot choose when, where, or which body. Off-world lives are hostile and
often solitary — the loneliness is the true cost.

OTHER POWERS:
  - THE HOARD — a subspace inventory keyed to the man, not the planet; contents
    never decay (a cigarette lit in a 1944 foxhole is still lit millennia
    later). Cannot store anything heavier than his current body, anything still
    breathing, or another void-touched thing.
  - IMPROVED-BODY ("the discipline") — absolute autonomic control and clean
    healing, built with Vael over centuries; includes "THE BELL," a grown organ
    behind the sternum that floods him into a berserker state at severe cost.
  - Slow-acquired knacks: perfect recall, silent movement, fast language
    learning, minor animal sway, going unseen. He is NOT a god — a bullet still
    hurts; only what happens after is different.

KEY CHARACTERS:
  - GHOR / "ADAM" — the protagonist and first-person narrator. Born ~45,000 BC,
    a Neanderthal; dies at fourteen and entangles with Vael. Short, broad, cold-
    adapted; the body is always recognizably him. Weary-amused, long-tempered,
    has buried more people than any city. Goes by many names (Adam most of all;
    also Yam, Adan, Adem, Adamski, Adam Cory, "the Stranger," and more).
  - VAEL — a Spectral Void Entity bound to Ghor since his death. STRICT RULE:
    she is never seen or heard directly; everything about her reaches the reader
    only through Ghor's paraphrase. Quirky, funny in inhuman ways, alien in her
    sense of time; the source of all his powers. Not the only one of her kind —
    Ghor has felt OTHERS at distance, and felt her flinch.
  - SEFIRA — a Weaver; the liaison "in the chair across from his," selected over
    four thousand years to interview him.
  - The Weavers — a hyper-advanced Andromeda civilization ~55,000 AD; orphaned
    descendants of the Exodus colonists, cut off when the stargate network
    collapsed. Their CODEX (inherited machinery older than they understand)
    reconstructs lost data and has begun DETECTING "a current — the same shape
    repeating across worlds," and has glimpsed the Hoard at distance.
  - The Long Stoop crew (his most recent life): MIRA (helm), ONSHO (one-eyed
    Carrick, guns), PINCH.
  - Deep-history figures: Spring Drop, One Eye, Rat (his first killer), Wet
    Grass, Wet Tip, Adamski (the Krakow printer whose page the Codex recovered).

KEY EVENTS: the first death (~45,000 BC) and the binding to Vael; millennia of
lives (he invents the wheel; loses and has his mind rebuilt around the year
1,000; seeds a "Dynasty of Starman"); THE EXODUS (~3,500 AD) — Ghor weaponizes
one of his own deaths into a network of stargates, humanity spreads across the
galaxies, the network frays and collapses, and he never rebuilds it; Chapter 1
(~55,000 AD) — he wakes in the Weavers' white cube and negotiates terms with
Sefira.

OPEN HOOKS: what Vael IS and why she chose him; the other void-touched entities
(and why Vael fears them); the Codex's "current" and whether it is reading the
Loom; the Hoard's ceiling; the many unwritten off-world lives (150 named-slot
planets to fill); whether he is ever put on the same off-world planet a third
time, and what that would mean.

UNIVERSE: SKY PILLAR   [ESTABLISHED]   —   the god-tree
================================================================================
TYPE: Xianxia from a non-human point of view — the rise of a sentient cultivator
tree to world-shaking power.

THE WORLD: YUNUS PLANET, whose "Small Southern Archipelago" holds hundreds of
continents. Cultivation ladder: Qi Condensation → Foundation → Core Formation →
Hegemon → Monarch → Autarch. Divine sense (spiritual perception) reaches
hundreds of kilometers for Core Formation, thousands for Monarchs. Monarchs form
inner worlds (pocket universes); Autarchs create galaxies and can eventually
roam between worlds.

KEY CHARACTER:
  - EVERGREEN — a sapling that woke to sentience after absorbing the blood of a
    dying cultivator. It grows ear-shaped leaves to learn human speech, discovers
    it is a cultivator with divine sense, and climbs from City Guardian to
    enslaver-ruler to Monarch, reaching a thousand-plus kilometers tall and
    feeding on the planet's magma through roots kilometers deep. A slow, patient,
    morally hardening intelligence.

OTHER CHARACTERS: DEDALUS (the boy who first spoke to Evergreen; grows into a
Hegemon and dies trying to liberate the enslaved city); Elder Hamas; the Hegemons
Sage Qilin (killed), Undying Summer (enslaved), Blind Evening; the ancient
Monarch DAVION and the Monarch Snowy Wood, who come to investigate the massive
energy drain Evergreen causes.

KEY EVENTS: awakening from blood; learning language; the long climb to Core
Formation; near-total destruction by distant immortals and a decades-long
regrowth (learning the Dao of Life); becoming an enslaver via soul-marks;
killing Dedalus; ascending toward Monarch as elder cultivators converge.

OPEN HOOKS: Evergreen's pocket inner-world and what grows in it; the distant
immortals who nearly killed it; whether an unbodied intelligence can ascend the
whole ladder; the coming clash with Davion's generation.

UNIVERSE: THE CHARLATAN   [SKETCH]   —   the con artist who fooled the Heavens
================================================================================
TYPE: Xianxia trickster progression. THE MORTAL ORIGIN OF SKOLAR. [SPINE]

THE WORLD: a xianxia cultivation world of sects, noble families, and a kingdom
at war; ladder from Qi Condensation up through Foundation, Core Formation,
Nascent Soul, and Hegemon. Above it sits a celestial bureaucracy — the JUDGES /
COURTS OF REINCARNATION — that weighs souls and reseeds them.

THE PROTAGONIST: SKOLAR — an old charlatan guru who, at seventy, had an
enlightenment go wrong on a mountain: his soul expanded until he detonated. His
soul (as strong as a mid-stage Hegemon's) was hauled before a stoic Judge, sealed
with two soul-seals, and reincarnated into a respectable family — but he kept ALL
his memories and Dao knowledge. Reborn a prodigy, he builds his path on stealth,
thievery, illusion, and fabricated cultivation techniques. His signature is the
DAO OF DECEPTION (turning others' belief in falsehoods into cultivational
currency; shifting tribulations onto enemies; layered "truth veils") paired with a
DAO OF SPACETIME (spatial storage items, teleportation tokens; becoming the best
thief and escape artist in the world).

SUPPORTING CAST: mother Akoto; father Ryuru; uncle Sondras (8th-layer, early
teacher); Elder Mogor; teacher of arms Ostrom; the rival prodigy Sharah.

WIKI DIRECTIVE: this is the low end of the SPINE. The wiki may trace, in open
territory, how the Charlatan's Deception-and-Spacetime road eventually becomes
the seven-veiled avatar-craft of the Primordial God Skolar (Blaze). Do not
contradict either endpoint.

OPEN HOOKS: the identity of the Judges and the machinery of reincarnation; how a
Dao of Deception climbs toward Order; the eras between this life and severance;
the first universe he ever left.

UNIVERSE: CHAMPION OF THE LIGHT (Cotl)   [SKETCH]
================================================================================
TYPE: Summoned-hero portal fantasy with a litRPG attribute system.

THE WORLD: a medieval fantasy of small kingdoms under invasion by the TRAXIAN
empire from the north. Power comes from elemental "gates/essences" (Earth, Wind,
Fire, Water) measured in attributes (str/agi/reg); combining gates forms
"confluences" that double an attribute. THE LIGHT is a goddess who summons
champions in times of need; an opposing COVENANT tears the sky and pours in

These are the threads the wiki may pull to grow, always as "Theories" /
"Resonances," never as confirmed crossovers (except the two Spine anchors).

R1. THE WEAVING TRIAD. Loom of Heavens (Blaze) ↔ the Weavers & Codex detecting
    "the same shape repeating across worlds" (Engine) ↔ the Weavings notebooks
    (meta). Theory: three views of one weave. The Codex's "current" may be a
    karma thread; the Found Notebooks may be its recovered output.

R2. THE FIVE DOORS ARE ONE DOOR. Ascension (Blaze, Sky Pillar, Nine Vaults),
    Void-Resurrection (Engine), Divine Summoning (Cotl, Tavern), Judged
    Reincarnation (Charlatan), Engineered Gates (Engine's Exodus). Theory: every
    world explains soul-movement between stitches in its own language.

R3. THE LID ABOVE EVERY SKY. The Nine Vaults' "sky is a seal, worlds are farmed"
    is the same hierarchy that severs Primordial Gods from their worlds in Blaze
    and that keeps frontier stitches small. Theory: the Prefecture, the Sky
    Sect's distant HQ, and the higher realms are all rungs of the Loom's
    enforcement of ignorance.

R4. THE VOID ENTITIES. Vael (Engine) is "not the only one of her kind." Theory:
    void-touched entities are agents (or parasites, or weavers) of the between-
    place — kin to whatever a Primordial God becomes after severance, or to the
    Tavern-entity, or to the Grimoire of the Hundred-Eleven.

R5. THE NAME "ADAM." Ghor's most-used name is Adam, "older than the Hebrew."
    Theory: a recurring first-man archetype seeded across stitches (echo him in
    summoned champions, first-men like "The First" of the Source, or the "second
    pair of eyes" Skolar seeks).

R6. THE CHAMPIONS AND THE CALAMITY. Cotl's Light, the Tavern's ten champions,
    the line.holder hero, and even the Hundred-Eleven are all "forged champions
    against a doom." Theory: something in the Loom keeps drafting mortals to hold
    a line — and keeps wiping or spending them.

R7. THE SCATTERED MASTER / THE ASH IN THE RELIC. Whiteflame scatters his soul
    into a hundred blood-drops (Blaze); the Hollow Lantern holds a dead
    immortal's soul-ash (Nine Vaults); Sky Pillar's Evergreen wakes from a dead
    cultivator's blood. Theory: dying high-realm beings seed lower stitches with
    themselves — a way to cheat severance, or to plant inheritors.

R8. OBSOLESCENCE ACROSS TIME. Ikaros arriving too late (Hubris) ↔ the Engine's
    Exodus network collapsing ↔ the Weavers orphaned from a forgotten origin.
    Theory: the Loom's frontier is littered with civilizations outrun by their
    own reach.

5. WIKI-GENERATION GUIDELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

VOICE & TONE. Write wiki articles in a neutral, in-universe encyclopedic
register (like a fan wikia). Match each universe's flavor in flavor-text pull-
quotes only. Respect the author's hard style rules: no tautological "X was X"
constructions; do not give inanimate things human agency (a bog does not
"teach," a mountain does not "decide") — a person or a named entity owns every
active verb.

EXPANDING WITHOUT BREAKING CANON:
  - Prefer filling an OPEN HOOK over inventing a new top-level fact.
  - Keep each universe internally consistent first; only then reach for a
    Resonance.
  - Never resolve a Thread that this seed leaves open (especially Anchor B's
    "how," Vael's nature, the identity of the higher powers). Deepen mysteries;
    do not close them. A neverending wiki needs its questions to stay alive.
  - When you introduce a new named entity, give it: a universe, a tier, at
    least one relationship to an existing entity, and one open hook.

CROSS-LINKING. Every article should link to: its Universe, its Power System,
and any Threads it touches. Spine articles (Skolar, and the Blaze↔111 link) may
link ACROSS universes. Non-spine articles link across universes ONLY inside a
clearly labeled "Theories / Resonances" section.

    their home universe.
