Ginger Rogers

American actress and dancer (1911–1995)

Ginger Rogers was one of the great stars of Hollywood's golden age, immortal as the dancing partner of Fred Astaire and accomplished in her own right as a comedienne and dramatic actress.

In their celebrated series of 1930s musicals she matched Astaire step for step—doing, as the famous quip had it, everything he did but backwards and in high heels. Their grace together made them the most beloved dancing pair the screen has known.

But she was far more than a partner. A gifted comic actress, she held her own with Katharine Hepburn in the ensemble of Stage Door and traded quips with Cary Grant in screwball comedies, and she proved her dramatic range by winning the Academy Award for Best Actress in the wartime drama Kitty Foyle. For a time she was among the highest-paid stars in Hollywood.

Her life off screen drew attention too, including romances with the aviator-mogul Howard Hughes and the actor James Stewart, her co-star in the comedy Vivacious Lady.
