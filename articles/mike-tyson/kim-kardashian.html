Kim Kardashian

American media personality and entrepreneur (b. 1980)

Kim Kardashian is among the most famous people of the social-media age, a celebrity who turned attention itself into one of the great fortunes of her generation.

She first drew notice as a friend and stylist to Paris Hilton, then became the centre of her family's reality television series, which followed the Kardashians for over a decade and made them a worldwide phenomenon. With an instinct for self-promotion unmatched in her time, she amassed an enormous online following and built it into a business empire—a shapewear brand and cosmetics lines that carried her to billionaire status—defining the very model of the influencer-entrepreneur.

Her marriage to the musician and designer Kanye West joined two of the era's biggest names and filled the tabloids until their divorce. In a surprising turn, she took up the study of law and became an advocate for criminal-justice reform, going to the White House to press President Donald Trump for the release of prisoners she believed wrongly held.

For all her own family's fame, the partner who shared her most public years was Kanye West.
