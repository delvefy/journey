Kobe Bryant

American basketball player (1978–2020)

Kobe Bryant was one of the greatest basketball players of his era, a fierce competitor who spent his entire twenty-year career with the Los Angeles Lakers and became a global icon of the game.

He came to the professional ranks straight from high school in 1996 and soon formed, with the towering Shaquille O'Neal, one of the most dominant partnerships the league had seen, winning three straight championships before their famous falling-out. Bryant then carried the Lakers to two more titles on his own, was crowned the league's most valuable player, and once poured in eighty-one points in a single game.

He built his game in conscious imitation of his idol, Michael Jordan, matching footwork, fadeaway, and above all an unrelenting will that he branded the “Mamba Mentality.” After retiring he even won an Academy Award for a short animated film about the sport he loved.

In January 2020 he was killed in a helicopter crash along with his young daughter Gianna and seven others, a sudden loss mourned around the world.

He had shared the stage of basketball's golden years with the player who would inherit his mantle and his Lakers, LeBron James.
