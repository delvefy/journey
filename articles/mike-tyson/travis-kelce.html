Travis Kelce

American football player (b. 1989)

Travis Kelce is one of the greatest tight ends in the history of American football, a star of the Kansas City Chiefs whose play and personality have made him one of the most recognisable figures in the sport.

After college football in Cincinnati he was drafted by Kansas City, where he became the favourite target of his quarterback and the engine of a championship offense, winning multiple Super Bowls and rewriting the receiving records for his position. Big, fast, and sure-handed, he redefined what a tight end could be.

Off the field he became a celebrity in his own right, hosting a popular podcast with his brother, a star center for a rival team, and trying his hand at acting and hosting. His exuberance made him a favourite far beyond football fans.

In 2023 his relationship with the pop superstar Taylor Swift became a worldwide sensation, drawing a vast new audience to the game and placing him at the very centre of popular culture.

His football greatness, though, rested above all on his partnership with his quarterback, Patrick Mahomes.
