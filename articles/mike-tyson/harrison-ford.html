Harrison Ford

American actor and movie star (born 1942)

Harrison Ford ranks among the most successful and beloved movie stars in the history of the cinema, the rugged everyman hero of some of its most popular films. He was working as a carpenter, taking small acting jobs, when he was cast as the cocky smuggler Han Solo in Star Wars—a role that made him famous overnight and launched one of the great screen careers.

He went on to play the whip-cracking archaeologist Indiana Jones in the swashbuckling series directed by Steven Spielberg, anchoring a second blockbuster franchise that became a touchstone of popular culture. Between and beyond them he showed real range and grit: the weary replicant-hunter of Blade Runner, the hunted innocent of The Fugitive, the upright everyman of Witness, and the resolute hero of Patriot Games and Air Force One.

Famous for his laconic charisma, his carpenter's practicality, and his passion for flying his own aircraft, he remained a star across five decades, returning again and again to the two characters the public loved most.

Both of those characters sprang from the imagination of a single filmmaker, the creator of Star Wars and the co-creator of Indiana Jones, who made Ford a star not once but twice—George Lucas.
