Michael Jordan

American basketball player (b. 1963)

Michael Jordan is regarded by most as the greatest basketball player who ever lived, and one of the most famous athletes in the history of sport.

He first seized the national eye with a title-winning shot as a freshman at North Carolina, then joined the Chicago Bulls and transformed them into a dynasty. Six times he led them to the NBA championship, and six times he was named the most valuable player of the finals; his scoring, his ferocious will, and his flair for the decisive moment became the stuff of legend.

His fame spread far beyond the court. His Air Jordan shoes, launched with Nike and promoted in unforgettable commercials made by Spike Lee, created a global brand and changed sports marketing forever. He won Olympic gold with the fabled 1992 Dream Team and became, for a generation, the very symbol of excellence.

After his father's murder he stunned the world by retiring to try professional baseball, only to return and win three more titles. In later years he became a team owner, and in 2016 President Barack Obama awarded him the Presidential Medal of Freedom.

His example shaped those who came after, none more devotedly than Kobe Bryant, who built his own game in Jordan's image.
