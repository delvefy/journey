Volodymyr Zelensky

President of Ukraine (born 1978)

Volodymyr Zelensky was the comedian-turned-president who became the defiant face of Ukraine's resistance to Russian invasion and a symbol of national courage to the world.

Born in the industrial city of Kryvyi Rih, he won fame as an actor and comic, starring in a television series in which he played an ordinary teacher unexpectedly elected president. Life imitated art when, in 2019, he ran for the office in earnest and won in a landslide. Early in his term he was thrust onto the world stage by an American scandal, when a phone call in which Donald Trump pressed him for political favors triggered Trump's first impeachment.

His defining ordeal came in February 2022, when the armies of Vladimir Putin launched a full-scale invasion expecting to topple him within days. Zelensky refused to flee—reportedly answering an offer of evacuation that he needed ammunition, not a ride—and through nightly video addresses he rallied his people and galvanized the West. Backed by the vast support of Joe Biden's America and its allies, Ukraine held, and Zelensky became one of the most recognized leaders on earth.

The war ground on for years, testing the unity and patience of his foreign supporters. Among the first and most ardent of those champions, who flew repeatedly to Kyiv and became a hero in its streets, was the British prime minister Boris Johnson.
