Russell Westbrook

American basketball player (b. 1988)

Russell Westbrook is one of the most ferociously athletic and relentless players in basketball history, a guard whose explosive energy and all-around production rewrote the record books.

Out of UCLA he joined the Oklahoma City franchise, where alongside Kevin Durant—and for a season the young James Harden—he formed a thrilling core that reached the Finals. When Durant departed, Westbrook carried the team almost single-handedly, and in a single astonishing season he averaged a triple-double across the whole year, a feat not seen in half a century, winning the Most Valuable Player award. He holds the all-time record for triple-doubles.

His later career took him to Houston, where he briefly reunited with Harden, then to Washington, and to the Lakers, where he played beside LeBron James. Off the court he became a noted figure in fashion, as bold in his dress as in his play.

His defining early partnership had been with Kevin Durant; the third of that young Oklahoma City trio was James Harden.
