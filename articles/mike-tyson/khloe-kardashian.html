Khloé Kardashian

American media personality and entrepreneur (b. 1984)

Khloé Kardashian is an American television personality and businesswoman, one of the central figures of a family that turned celebrity itself into a global enterprise.

She rose to fame on the reality series Keeping Up with the Kardashians, which followed her family's lives for fourteen years and made them household names around the world. The daughter of a Los Angeles lawyer—famous in his own day as part of O.J. Simpson's defence—and of the family's tireless manager-mother, she parlayed her television fame into business, most successfully a clothing brand built around body inclusivity.

Her private life played out in public: a marriage to one professional basketball player, a much-covered romance with another, James Harden, and a long, troubled relationship with a third. Through it all she remained a fixture of tabloids and social media, beloved and scrutinised in equal measure.

Yet within her own famous family, the brightest star of all—the one whose celebrity defined the age of social media—was her sister, Kim Kardashian.
