Tom Brady

American football quarterback (1977)

Tom Brady is regarded by most as the greatest quarterback in the history of American football, the winner of more championships than any player who ever lived.

His beginnings gave no hint of it: overlooked until the very late rounds of the draft, the two-hundredth name on no one's list, he seized his chance with the New England Patriots and never let go. With his coach he built the most enduring dynasty the sport had seen, winning six Super Bowls in New England and then, astonishingly, a seventh after moving late in his career to Tampa Bay.

His gifts were not of the body alone but of preparation, poise, and an almost uncanny calm in the decisive minutes of a game. He played at the highest level into his mid-forties, defying every expectation of an athlete's decline, and along the way defeated the rising young star Patrick Mahomes in the Super Bowl.

His fame reached well beyond the gridiron, not least through his long marriage to one of the world's most celebrated supermodels, Gisele Bündchen.
