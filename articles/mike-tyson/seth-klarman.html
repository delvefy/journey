Seth Klarman

American value investor and author (born 1957)

Seth Klarman is among the most respected investors of his generation, the founder of the Boston-based Baupost Group and an heir to the value-investing tradition of Graham and Dodd. Admirers, noting his patience, his contrarian deep-value bets, and his willingness to sit on mountains of cash until bargains appear, have ranked him beside Warren Buffett as one of the great practitioners of the discipline, and dubbed him the “Oracle of Boston.”

His reputation rests partly on a single slim book, Margin of Safety, a treatise on cautious investing that went out of print and became a legend, changing hands among devotees for thousands of dollars a copy. So closely is he identified with the founding texts of his craft that he led the team that produced a new edition of Security Analysis and wrote its preface, carrying the work of Graham and Dodd to a new century of readers.

Beyond finance he became one of the most consequential philanthropists and political donors in the United States, his giving large enough to shape campaigns and causes for years at a time.

Among the candidates he backed most prominently, in his years as a leading donor, was the businessman, governor, and presidential nominee Mitt Romney.
