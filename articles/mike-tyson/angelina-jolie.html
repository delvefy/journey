Angelina Jolie

American actress, filmmaker, and humanitarian (born 1975)

Angelina Jolie became one of the most famous women in the world, equally renowned as a film star and as a campaigner for the world's refugees. The daughter of an acclaimed actor, she won an Academy Award early, for her fierce performance in Girl, Interrupted, and then conquered the box office as the gun-slinging adventurer of Lara Croft: Tomb Raider and a string of action hits.

She proved her dramatic depth in graver films—the grief-stricken reporter's wife of A Mighty Heart and, above all, a mother searching for her abducted son in the period drama Changeling, which brought her another Oscar nomination. In time she moved behind the camera as well, directing serious films about the wars in Bosnia and Cambodia and about a prisoner of the Pacific war.

Beyond the cinema she devoted herself to humanitarian work, serving for many years as a goodwill ambassador and then special envoy for the United Nations refugee agency, visiting war zones and refugee camps across the globe and campaigning against sexual violence in conflict.

Her private life was followed obsessively by the world's press, above all her marriage to the actor Brad Pitt. The veteran star and director who guided her to one of her finest performances, in Changeling, was Clint Eastwood.
