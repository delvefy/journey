King Charles III

King of the United Kingdom (born 1948)

King Charles III is the monarch of the United Kingdom, who acceded to the throne in 2022 after the longest apprenticeship in British royal history.

The eldest child of Queen Elizabeth II, he was heir apparent from the age of three and waited more than seven decades to become king, the longest-serving Prince of Wales the country had ever known. He used those long years to carve out a public role of his own, championing environmental causes decades before they became fashionable, promoting organic farming and traditional architecture, and founding a charity that helped hundreds of thousands of young people.

His private life drew far greater attention. His 1981 marriage to Princess Diana, celebrated as a fairytale, collapsed into a bitter and public estrangement, and his long devotion to Camilla, whom he eventually married, became one of the great royal sagas of the age. Diana's death in 1997 left him to raise their two young sons amid intense scrutiny.

When his mother died in September 2022 he at last became king, crowned the following year as a careful, dutiful sovereign for a changed nation. The elder of his sons, the new heir to the throne and a future king in his turn, is Prince William.
