Taylor Swift

American singer-songwriter (b. 1989)

Taylor Swift is among the best-selling and most influential musicians of the twenty-first century, a songwriter whose confessional craft and shrewd command of her own career have made her a defining cultural figure of her time.

She began as a teenage country prodigy, then made a seamless crossing into pop and later into a hushed, literary folk, reinventing her sound with each album while keeping a vast and devoted audience. Her songwriting—intimate, narrative, and sharply observed—set her apart.

When her early recordings were sold without her consent, she answered by re-recording her catalogue under her own control, a move studied as a landmark in artists' rights. Her record-breaking concert tour became a global event that moved economies and dominated the culture for two years.

Her path crossed early and lastingly with Kanye West, whose snatching of her microphone at an awards show in 2009 began a feud that followed them both for years. As an artist she stands beside Beyoncé among the defining women of modern music.

In recent years her romance with the football star Travis Kelce became a worldwide sensation in its own right.
