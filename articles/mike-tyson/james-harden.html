James Harden

American basketball player (b. 1989)

James Harden, known across the basketball world as “the Beard,” is one of the most prolific scorers the game has ever produced and one of its most influential offensive minds.

He began as a brilliant reserve for the Oklahoma City Thunder behind Kevin Durant and Russell Westbrook, earning recognition as the league's best sixth man. Traded to the Houston Rockets, he blossomed into a superstar, repeatedly leading the league in scoring, winning the Most Valuable Player award, and pioneering a style built on the step-back three-pointer, isolation play, and the art of drawing fouls—an approach that came to define an analytics-driven age of basketball.

His later years took him to Brooklyn, Philadelphia, and the Clippers, always in pursuit of the championship that eluded him.

Beyond the court, his celebrity reached the wider culture, above all through his much-publicised romance with the reality-television star Khloé Kardashian.

The partnerships that launched him, though, were with his old Oklahoma City teammates, Kevin Durant and Russell Westbrook.
