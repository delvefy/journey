Grace Kelly

American actress and Princess of Monaco (1929–1982)

Grace Kelly was a Hollywood star of cool, patrician beauty who, at the height of her fame, left the screen to become a real-life princess.

Born to a prosperous Philadelphia family, she rose with remarkable speed in the early 1950s. She won the Academy Award for The Country Girl, played opposite Gary Cooper in High Noon, and became the favourite leading lady of Alfred Hitchcock, who cast her in Dial M for Murder, Rear Window beside James Stewart, and To Catch a Thief beside Cary Grant. Her serene elegance made her the very type of the Hitchcock heroine.

At the Cannes Film Festival she met Prince Rainier III of Monaco, and in 1956 their marriage was celebrated as the wedding of the century, watched around the world. She gave up her career to become Princess Grace, devoting herself to her family, to charity, and to the arts of the little principality.

Her life ended in tragedy: in 1982 she suffered a stroke at the wheel of her car and died in the crash on a mountain road above Monaco.

Among the partners of her brief, brilliant career was the singer and actor Frank Sinatra, with whom she starred in the musical High Society.
