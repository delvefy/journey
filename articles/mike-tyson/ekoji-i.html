Ekoji I

Founder of the Maratha kingdom of Thanjavur (d. 1687)

Ekoji I, also called Venkoji, was the founder of the Maratha kingdom of Thanjavur and the younger half-brother of the great Shivaji. A son of the warlord Shahaji Bhonsle by a second marriage, he was raised in the service of the Sultanate of Bijapur, under whose banner he won his early fame as a soldier in the Deccan and the south.

In the mid-1670s, commanding a Bijapuri expedition into the Tamil country, Ekoji seized the city of Thanjavur, displacing its previous rulers, and made himself its king. So began the Bhonsle dynasty of Thanjavur, a Maratha house that would rule the Kaveri delta for a century and a half and become a great patron of southern art and music.

His relations with his celebrated brother were uneasy. In 1677 Shivaji swept into the south to claim his share of their father Shahaji's southern estates, and the two met in a tense confrontation over the inheritance. Shivaji took much of the disputed land but left Thanjavur in Ekoji's hands, and the brothers parted without lasting reconciliation.

Ekoji ruled his new kingdom for some years before passing the throne to his son, and his line endured long after the wider Maratha confederacy had risen and fallen in the Deccan.

Though overshadowed by Shivaji, Ekoji founded one of the most durable of Maratha states and planted the family of his father Shahaji deep in the soil of the south, where its kings would reign at Thanjavur into the nineteenth century.
