Gisele Bündchen

Brazilian supermodel (b. 1980)

Gisele Bündchen is one of the most famous and successful fashion models in history, for many years the highest-paid in the world and the defining face of her era on the runway.

Discovered as a teenager in southern Brazil, she rose to the top of fashion in the late 1990s and was widely credited with sweeping away the gaunt, waifish look that had reigned before her, bringing in a healthier, more athletic ideal of beauty. As a Victoria's Secret Angel and the face of countless luxury houses, she became the muse of the great designers and one of the most photographed women alive.

Her private life drew the world's attention: a long romance with the actor Leonardo DiCaprio, and then a marriage to the football champion Tom Brady, with whom she had two children. She has also lent her name to environmental and humanitarian causes as a goodwill ambassador.

Among the titans of fashion who treasured her, none was more celebrated than the designer who reigned over Chanel for decades, Karl Lagerfeld.
