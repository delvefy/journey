Brad Pitt

American actor and film producer (born 1963)

Brad Pitt became, in the 1990s, one of the most famous movie stars in the world, his striking looks at first threatening to overshadow a real and growing talent. After his breakthrough in Thelma & Louise he proved his range across a remarkable variety of films—the serial-killer thriller Se7en, the anarchic Fight Club, the slick Ocean's heist comedies, the time-spanning Curious Case of Benjamin Button, and the wartime revenge fantasy Inglourious Basterds.

He grew into one of Hollywood's most powerful figures off screen as well. Through his production company he backed bold and acclaimed films, winning the Academy Award for Best Picture as a producer of 12 Years a Slave, and he later took the acting Oscar himself for Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, sharing that film with Leonardo DiCaprio.

For two decades his private life drew as much attention as his work. His marriage to one famous actress and then to another—a union the press christened with a single blended name and followed obsessively around the globe—made him a permanent fixture of the world's celebrity culture.

The actress he married in that most scrutinized of unions, herself among the most famous film stars and humanitarian campaigners in the world, was Angelina Jolie.
