Donald Trump

45th and 47th President of the United States (born 1946)

Donald Trump rose from New York real estate and reality television to become one of the most prominent and divisive figures in modern American history. He built and branded a sprawling family business of towers, hotels, and casinos, cultivated a celebrity persona, and hosted a popular television show before turning to politics.

In 2016, running as a populist outsider under the slogan “Make America Great Again,” he defeated Hillary Clinton to win the presidency in one of the great upsets of American political history. His first term brought large tax cuts, a reshaping of the federal courts including three Supreme Court appointments, a hard line on immigration and trade, and an intensely combative style; he was impeached twice by the House of Representatives and acquitted both times by the Senate.

He lost the 2020 election to Joe Biden and disputed the outcome, a conflict that culminated in the storming of the Capitol on January 6, 2021. Then, in 2024, he won the presidency again, becoming only the second man to serve two non-consecutive terms. Few figures have so polarized the country, commanding fervent loyalty and fierce opposition in equal measure.

Among the foreign leaders whose dealings with him drew the most persistent scrutiny throughout his career was the president of Russia, Vladimir Putin.
