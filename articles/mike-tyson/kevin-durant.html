Kevin Durant

American basketball player (b. 1988)

Kevin Durant is one of the most gifted scorers ever to play basketball, a seven-footer with the shooting touch and ball skills of a guard, who has won championships and divided opinion in equal measure.

He came out of the University of Texas to join the franchise that became the Oklahoma City Thunder, where with the explosive Russell Westbrook he formed a thrilling young pair that carried the team to the Finals. He won the league's Most Valuable Player award and built a reputation as an almost unstoppable scorer.

Then, in a move that startled and angered many, he left to join the Golden State Warriors—the very team that had just eliminated his own—and there, beside Stephen Curry, he won two straight championships and was twice named the most valuable player of the Finals. Later he played for Brooklyn and Phoenix, still among the game's elite.

He had grown up idolising Kobe Bryant, and his fiercest battles came against LeBron James on the sport's greatest stage.

His first partnership, and one of the most electric in the league, had been with Russell Westbrook.
