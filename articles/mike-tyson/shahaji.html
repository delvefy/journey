Shahaji

Maratha general of the Deccan, father of Shivaji (1594–1664)

Shahaji Bhonsle was a Maratha general of the Deccan and the father of Shivaji, founder of the Maratha Empire. In an age when the Deccan was contested between the Mughals from the north and the sultanates of Ahmadnagar, Bijapur, and Golconda, Shahaji rose as one of the most able and ambitious soldiers of fortune of his time.

He began his career in the service of the Sultanate of Ahmadnagar under its famous regent Malik Ambar, the African-born statesman who taught the Deccan the arts of mobile warfare against the Mughals. After Malik Ambar's death and the collapse of Ahmadnagar's power, Shahaji for a time held up a puppet sultan of his own, until the advance of the emperor Shah Jahan compelled him to come to terms with the Mughals.

Passing then into the service of Bijapur, he was granted estates around Pune and later a great command in the Karnataka south, where he conquered and governed widely. He left his wife Jijabai and their son Shivaji at Pune, where the boy would be raised in dreams of independent rule.

Shahaji's loyalties shifted with the dangerous politics of the Deccan, and when Shivaji began to seize forts in open defiance, Bijapur imprisoned the father to check the son. He was released, and lived to see his son's rise before dying in a hunting accident in 1664.

Through his two marriages Shahaji fathered both Shivaji, who built the Maratha state in the Deccan, and Ekoji, who founded the Maratha kingdom of Thanjavur in the far south. He was the root from which the whole Bhonsle greatness grew.
