Patrick Mahomes

American football quarterback (b. 1995)

Patrick Mahomes is the finest American football quarterback of his generation and, many believe, a player bound for a place among the greatest who ever lived.

The son of a Major League Baseball pitcher, he was a gifted all-round athlete who chose football, and almost as soon as he became the starter for the Kansas City Chiefs he was named the league's most valuable player. His game is built on improvisation and daring—sidearm throws, no-look passes, and impossible escapes—paired with a cool command of the decisive moment.

With his coach and his great receiving partner Travis Kelce, he turned Kansas City into a dynasty, winning multiple Super Bowls and taking the game's top honour each time. He brought a new style and a new excitement to the most-watched sport in America.

Early in his rise he met, and was beaten by, the veteran master Tom Brady in the Super Bowl—a passing of the torch between the quarterback who had defined an era and the one who would define the next.
