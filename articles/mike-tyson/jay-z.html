Jay-Z

American rapper and business magnate (b. 1969)

Jay-Z is one of the most acclaimed lyricists in the history of hip-hop and the genre's first billionaire, a man who rose from a Brooklyn housing project to the summit of American business and culture.

Unable at first to find a label, he founded his own, Roc-A-Fella Records, and released a debut album now counted a classic. Across a long run of records he established himself as rap's preeminent storyteller, and he used that platform to build an empire that reached far beyond music—a clothing line, nightclubs, a streaming service, and a management company spanning music and sports.

He mentored and launched the career of Kanye West, and discovered and signed other stars in turn. Politically engaged, he became a prominent and visible supporter of Barack Obama, lending his fame to the campaigns and celebrations of the first Black president.

In 2008 he married the singer Beyoncé, and together they became the reigning power couple of popular music, their lives and art intertwined for the public to follow.
