Robin Wright

American actress and director (b. 1966)

Robin Wright is an American actress admired for her range and her quiet authority, a performer who has moved gracefully from fairy-tale heroine to one of television's most formidable political schemers.

She first won wide attention as the radiant Buttercup in the cherished fantasy The Princess Bride, then gave a tender and painful performance as Jenny, the lifelong love of the hero, in Forrest Gump, opposite Tom Hanks. For years she built a career of intelligent, often understated film roles.

Her greatest triumph came late, in the political drama House of Cards, where her cold and calculating Claire Underwood matched and finally surpassed her scheming husband. She won acclaim and awards for the part, directed several episodes herself, and carried the series as its lead in its final chapter. She later took up the sword as an Amazon general in Wonder Woman.

Her private life was long entwined with that of the actor Sean Penn, to whom she was married for many years.

Of all her screen partners, none was at the heart of a more beloved film than her Forrest Gump co-star, Tom Hanks.
