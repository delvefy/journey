Prince Harry

Duke of Sussex (born 1984)

Prince Harry, Duke of Sussex, is the younger son of King Charles III who traded life as a working royal for a new and turbulent existence outside the institution.

Born in 1984, he was only twelve when his mother, Princess Diana, was killed, and he grew up in the glare of her legend. As a young man he cultivated a reputation for mischief before finding purpose in the army, serving two tours in Afghanistan and later founding the Invictus Games for wounded service personnel, an achievement that won wide admiration.

His life changed course when he married the American actress Meghan Markle in 2018. Chafing against the pressures of royal life and the British tabloids, the couple stepped back from their duties in 2020 and moved to California. From there Harry became an outspoken critic of the monarchy and the press, straining his bonds with his father and his brother, the future king, almost to breaking; he laid out his grievances in a record-breaking memoir.

The moment that made their rupture global news was a sensational television interview the couple gave in 2021, in which they aired explosive allegations against the royal household before an audience of tens of millions. The celebrated American broadcaster who drew those confidences out of them was Oprah Winfrey.
