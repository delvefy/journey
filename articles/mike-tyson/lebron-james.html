LeBron James

American basketball player (b. 1984)

LeBron James is one of the greatest basketball players in history and the all-time leading scorer of the NBA, a figure whose dominance and longevity have made him a defining athlete of his age.

Hailed as a prodigy while still in high school in Akron, he entered the league amid unprecedented expectation and more than met it. He won four championships across three franchises—most memorably ending Cleveland's half-century title drought—earning four Most Valuable Player awards and a reputation for all-around mastery and basketball intelligence rarely equalled.

His career unfolded forever in the shadow of comparison with Michael Jordan, against whose legend his own greatness was endlessly measured. On the floor his defining rivalry came against Stephen Curry's Golden State Warriors, whom he met in four straight Finals.

Beyond the game he built a vast business and media empire and became a prominent voice on social questions, founding a public school for at-risk children in his hometown.

He overlapped with and inherited the throne of the player who came just before him as the face of the Lakers and the league, Kobe Bryant.
