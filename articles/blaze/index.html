Blaze

ESTABLISHED

A backwater cultivation world at the dim edge of the Loom — and the vantage from which the Loom is first seen.

  • RinCharacter

    A red-haired slum orphan of Scarlet City, born meridian-defective and for that reason the perfect vessel for the Blaze Tempering — unaware that a Primordial God has chosen his trajectory.

  • SkolarCharacter (Spine)

    The con artist who climbed all nine realms to become a Primordial God — the single soul the wiki can follow from the bottom of the ladder to the empty place above the Loom.

  • The Blaze TemperingPower System

    The secret body-cultivation road of Blaze — forty layers that do not store qi but consume it, igniting it to burn impurities out as slag and doubling raw power with each layer, while leaving its bearer invisible to every standard test.

  • Universe: BlazeUniverse

    A small grey cultivation world at the dim edge of the Loom — and the vantage from which the Loom of Heavens is first seen.

  • WhiteflameCharacter

    A hunted body-cultivation master who, dying, scattered his soul into a hundred blood-drops across the Loom; one drop seeded Rin's road, and his surviving wisp taught the first layers of the Blaze Tempering — leaving a debt of revenge as the price of the inheritance.
