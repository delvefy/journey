Skolar

Character (Spine) ESTABLISHED

The con artist who climbed all nine realms to become a Primordial God — the single soul the wiki can follow from the bottom of the ladder to the empty place above the Loom.

Skolar is the only figure in the wiki whose thread runs from the very bottom of the ladder to the empty place above the Loom. He is the Spine of the multiverse: the mortal con artist of the Charlatan universe and the Primordial God who watches over Blaze are one soul across different eras. (This crossover is confirmed canon.)

The con artist grew up to fool the Heavens themselves.

The two endpoints

At the low end of the Spine stands the Charlatan: an old guru whose botched enlightenment detonated his soul at seventy. The karma-heavy pattern was hauled before a Judge of Reincarnation, sealed twice, and reborn into a respectable family — yet he kept every memory and every scrap of Dao knowledge. His chosen paths are the Dao of Deception and the Dao of Spacetime.

At the high end stands the Primordial God Skolar: gaunt, white-haired, ancient and dry-humoured, severed from his home universe for eras. He is one of only a handful ever to complete the ninth of the nine realms.

The signature deception

Skolar's greatest act is the grandest deception in the multiverse. Having read a karma trajectory in a distant child — Rin of Blaze — that mirrors his own, he judges that his own karmic weight would crush so fragile a destiny, a mountain set beside a pebble. So he does not go himself.

Instead he pinches a wisp from his own truesoul, shapes a qi-less double, and wraps it in seven veils — three of Time, two of Space, two of Order — so that not even another Primordial God can see it. He sends the avatar to wait on the Dead Mountains for the child's birth, two hundred years off. Its only orders: Watch. Wait. Do not interfere.

The price he has already paid

The moment a cultivator completes the ninth realm, every thread of space binding them to their home universe snaps at once. A Primordial God is exiled by their own ascension. Skolar rose, alone, into the between-place — and has waited eras since for a second pair of eyes.

Open hooks

Which universes he climbed through between the Charlatan's sealed rebirth and his severance; whom he loved and lost; how many eras he waited before he found Rin's trajectory; how a Dao of Deception ever climbed toward Order. The wiki may deepen each; it must not close them.
