Vlad’s Journey is a personal encyclopædia — a place where articles accumulate slowly, one at a time, on whatever subject proved worth writing down. It borrows the manners of a traditional encyclopedia: titled entries, sections, tags in place of categories, and a search desk at the front.

How it is organised

Every article is a single, self-contained page shelved in the articles folder. A small ledger of titles, tags and dates is kept in the db folder; the front page consults it to offer search and to list what was added most recently.

Tags do the work that categories do elsewhere. Clicking a tag — here or on the front page — searches the archive for everything filed under it.

House style

Entries open with a lead paragraph bearing an illuminated initial, continue in sections divided by ruled headings, and may carry an infobox in the right margin for facts best given briefly. Quotations are set apart in the old manner:

“Not all those who wander are lost — some are merely taking notes.”

This very page follows the house style and may be read as a specimen of it.
