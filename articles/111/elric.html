Elric

Character ESTABLISHED

The Centurion, #1 of the Hundred-Eleven — ancient, cold, and from a wild land across the Eastern Sea; loyal to the contract rather than to people, he has outlived more companies than any soldier should, by a means the drafts do not explain.

Elric is the Centurion — rank #1 of the Hundred-Eleven, the top of the roster narrated from below by Alden. He commands the grimoire-bound company with the cold economy of a man who has done it many times before, and by every hint the drafts allow, he has: he has outlived company after company, and the how of it is one of the universe's quiet mysteries.

He does not mourn the roster. He renumbers it.

Role and history

Elric hails from a wild land across the Eastern Sea, an outsider to Veleh and its three-empire squeeze. As Centurion he is bound to the Grimoire like every soldier beneath him, but where the others are spent and replaced across a single saga, Elric endures — the constant at the head of a company whose numbers fall and reset around him. His loyalty runs to the contract, not to the men who die fulfilling it; he keeps the Hundred-Eleven a functioning weapon and lets sentiment fall where the roster does.

Powers and techniques

Elric carries the standard gifts of the Grimoire's brand — strength, endurance, resistance to magic, and clean healing — trained through the disciplined combat the company calls the Pattern. What sets him apart is not a technique but a duration: a service far longer than a single life should allow. The mechanism is unstated. The drafts pose the length of his service as a question and decline to answer it.

Relationships

  • Alden — the narrator, #82 falling to #39, who watches the Centurion from far down the roster and records what little he can read of him.
  • The Grimoire — the human-skin book that binds Elric as it binds the rest, and whose thousands of names may bear on how one soldier outlasts so many companies.
  • Baron Mihail — the throne-claimant employer whose contract the company serves, himself a rumoured revenant who never ages.

Open hooks

How long, exactly, Elric has served, and by what means a bound soldier outlives company after company; whether the Centurion's endurance and the Grimoire's thousands of names are the same mystery seen from two ends; and whether the seat of #1 has ever been held by anyone else.

Theories & resonances

Elric's impossible longevity sits beside the Grimoire's roster of thousands on a company only ever 111 strong — the puzzle the wiki calls the mystery of #45. Whether the door by which the roster refills is one of the five doors, and whether a Centurion is simply a name passed down rather than a man who persists, are threads the wiki keeps open. Only the #45 link to Blaze is confirmed; the rest is theory.
