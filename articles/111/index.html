The Hundred-Eleven

ESTABLISHED

A grimoire-bound mercenary company in the fractured kingdom of Veleh.

  • AldenCharacter

    Born Sadim, soldier #82 falling to #39 — the eighteen-year-old orphan of Tullanon whose honest journal narrates the Hundred-Eleven, and who cannot leave the company he joined for food.

  • ElricCharacter

    The Centurion, #1 of the Hundred-Eleven — ancient, cold, and from a wild land across the Eastern Sea; loyal to the contract rather than to people, he has outlived more companies than any soldier should, by a means the drafts do not explain.

  • The GrimoirePower System

    The parasitic, human-skin-bound book that binds a mercenary company body and soul, grants its powers, and compels the killing that feeds it — holding thousands of names though the company is only ever 111 strong.

  • The Hundred-Eleven Roster and the Mystery of #45Thread (Spine)

    One of the multiverse's two confirmed cross-universe anchors — the roster of the Hundred-Eleven carries a soldier named Blaze at #45, sharing a name with the protagonist-lineage of an entire other world. What is confirmed is the link; what stays open is how.

  • Universe: The Hundred-ElevenUniverse

    A grim journal-narrated mercenary saga set in the fractured vassal kingdom of Veleh, where a company of exactly 111 soldiers is bound body and soul to a parasitic human-skin grimoire.
