The Engine

ESTABLISHED

An immortal wanderer reseeded life after life across fifty galaxies and a hundred thousand years.

  • GhorCharacter

    Born a Neanderthal around 45,000 BC, dead at fourteen and bound to a void entity ever since — the immortal wanderer of the Engine, who has gone by Adam more than any other of his hundred names.

  • Universe: The EngineUniverse

    An immortal-wanderer science-fantasy spanning a hundred thousand years and fifty galaxies, in which a man dies and is reseeded life after life across worlds by the void entity bound to him.

  • VaelSpecies

    The Spectral Void Entity bound to Ghor since his first death — the unseen source of all his powers, never shown directly, and by her own hint not the only one of her kind.
