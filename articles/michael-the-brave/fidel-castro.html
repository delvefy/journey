Fidel Castro

Cuban revolutionary and head of state, 1926–2016.

Fidel Alejandro Castro Ruz was born on his Spanish-immigrant father's sugar estate at Birán in eastern Cuba. He took a law degree at the University of Havana, where he was already a militant in the populist Orthodox Party, and rose to political visibility on 26 July 1953 with the failed assault on the Moncada Barracks at Santiago de Cuba. Captured and imprisoned, he turned his defence speech into the manifesto "History Will Absolve Me," was amnestied in 1955, and went to Mexico to organize an expeditionary force.

In December 1956 the yacht Granma landed eighty-two guerrillas, including his brother Raúl and the Argentine doctor Ernesto "Che" Guevara, on the south-eastern Cuban coast. From the Sierra Maestra they conducted a two-year insurgency that, with mounting urban support, drove the dictator Fulgencio Batista into exile on New Year's Day 1959. Castro became Prime Minister, nationalized US-owned property, declared himself a Marxist-Leninist in 1961 after the failed CIA-sponsored Bay of Pigs landing, and turned the island into the Caribbean outpost of the Soviet bloc. In October 1962 Khrushchev's placement of nuclear missiles on Cuban soil, and their forced withdrawal after thirteen days of confrontation with John F. Kennedy, brought the world the closest it ever came to a great-power nuclear war.

For the next half-century he was the longest-serving non-monarchical head of state in the world. He sent Cuban troops to fight in Angola and Ethiopia, sheltered Latin American guerrilla movements, exported tens of thousands of doctors and teachers, survived a US embargo and dozens of CIA assassination plots, and outlasted ten American presidents from Eisenhower to Obama. The Soviet collapse in 1991 forced the austerity of the "Special Period" on Cuba; Pope John Paul II visited the island in 1998; Pope Francis brokered the normalization of relations with the United States in 2014. Castro had handed power formally to Raúl in 2008 and died in Havana in November 2016, ninety years old, the last great Cold War survivor.
