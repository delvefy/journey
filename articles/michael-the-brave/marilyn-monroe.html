Marilyn Monroe

American actress, 1926–1962.

Born Norma Jeane Mortenson in Los Angeles to a film-cutter mother whose mental illness sent her in and out of institutions, Marilyn passed her childhood through foster homes and the Los Angeles Orphans Home Society. At sixteen she married a neighbour to escape another placement; while working at a Burbank munitions plant during the war she was photographed by an army photographer named David Conover, and her career as a pin-up model began. By 1946 she had divorced, dyed her hair platinum, taken the name Marilyn Monroe, and signed her first contract with Twentieth Century-Fox.

Through the early 1950s, in small parts that lengthened with each picture, she made her face the new American ideal of female beauty: the figure on the Niagara poster, the model on the cover of the first issue of Playboy in December 1953, the gold-digger of Gentlemen Prefer Blondes, the next-door temptress of The Seven Year Itch (whose 1954 skirt-blowing-up subway-grate shot remains her single most reproduced image), the husky-voiced "Sugar Kane" of Billy Wilder's Some Like It Hot. She was, by 1956, the most photographed person in the world. She worked very hard at her own image, studied with Lee Strasberg at the Actors Studio, and resented bitterly the dumb-blonde casting that had made her famous.

Her brief marriage in 1954 to the retired baseball star Joe DiMaggio collapsed under his jealousy; her four-year marriage to the playwright Arthur Miller ended after the strained New York and Nevada productions of The Misfits, the screenplay he had written for her. She is widely believed to have had an affair with President John F. Kennedy, for whom she sang a sultry "Happy Birthday, Mr. President" at Madison Square Garden in May 1962; about a possible parallel involvement with Robert Kennedy the evidence is more contested. She was found dead in the bedroom of her Brentwood bungalow in August 1962 of an overdose of barbiturates — ruled probable suicide — at thirty-six. DiMaggio sent roses to her crypt three times a week for the next twenty years.

The road continues to Joe DiMaggio.
