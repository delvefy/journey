John Lennon

English musician and founding member of the Beatles, 1940–1980.

Born in Liverpool during a German air raid and raised by his aunt Mimi after his merchant-sailor father drifted out of his life, Lennon left art college to throw in with the skiffle group he had formed at Quarry Bank High School. In 1957 a fifteen-year-old Paul McCartney heard the group play at a church fête in Woolton; the two became writing partners, were joined by McCartney's schoolfriend George Harrison, and after a hard Hamburg apprenticeship in the Reeperbahn clubs took on the drummer Ringo Starr in 1962.

Under the management of Brian Epstein and the production of George Martin at EMI's Abbey Road studios, the Beatles released a string of records — Please Please Me, A Hard Day's Night, Rubber Soul, Revolver, Sgt. Pepper's Lonely Hearts Club Band, the White Album, Abbey Road — that compressed the development of popular music into eight years and changed it irreversibly. Lennon's contributions ran from the early rockers ("Twist and Shout," "I Saw Her Standing There" alongside McCartney) through introspective psychedelia ("Strawberry Fields Forever," "A Day in the Life") to plain confession ("In My Life," "Julia"). In 1965 he and the band were appointed MBEs by Queen Elizabeth II; in 1969 he returned his medal in protest at British support for the Nigerian war and the slipping of his single "Cold Turkey" down the charts.

From 1968 his life and work fused with the Japanese conceptual artist Yoko Ono. Together they staged the Amsterdam and Montreal "Bed-Ins for Peace," recorded Two Virgins and the Plastic Ono Band singles, and led the New York radical-celebrity life that Nixon's administration tried to deport him for. The Beatles broke up acrimoniously in 1970. After the solo records John Lennon/Plastic Ono Band, Imagine, and the long retirement of his "house-husband" years raising his son Sean, he had just returned to recording with Double Fantasy when, on 8 December 1980 outside the Dakota apartment building on Central Park West, he was shot dead by a deranged fan named Mark David Chapman.

The road continues to Paul McCartney.
