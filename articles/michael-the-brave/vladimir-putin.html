Vladimir Putin

b. 1952, President of the Russian Federation

Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin was born in Leningrad in October 1952, the only surviving son of a factory foreman and a survivor of the 900-day siege. He grew up in a communal apartment on Baskov Lane, took up judo and Sambo, and read law at Leningrad State University, where he was recruited by the KGB. He served in the foreign intelligence directorate, mostly in Dresden, where the Wall came down on his post in 1989 and he and his colleagues burned the files.

Returning to a Soviet Union about to dissolve, he attached himself to his old law professor Anatoly Sobchak, the reforming mayor of St Petersburg, and after Sobchak's defeat in 1996 moved to Moscow into successive posts under President Boris Yeltsin: head of the Federal Security Service in 1998, prime minister in August 1999, and acting president on Yeltsin's resignation on the last day of the millennium. The second Chechen war, opened with the apartment bombings of September 1999, carried him into the presidency on a popular vote in March 2000.

Through two presidential terms and a tandem interlude with Dmitry Medvedev, he reasserted the central state over the regional governors and the oligarchs of the Yeltsin era. The Yukos affair imprisoned Mikhail Khodorkovsky; the murders of Anna Politkovskaya, Boris Nemtsov and Alexei Navalny ringed the dissident press and opposition. Abroad, he fought a brief war with Georgia in 2008, annexed Crimea from Ukraine in 2014, intervened in Syria from 2015, and on 24 February 2022 launched the full-scale invasion of Ukraine against Volodymyr Zelensky's government.

His relations with the United States cooled through the Bush, Obama and Biden presidencies and warmed during the two Trump presidencies; with Xi Jinping he proclaimed a partnership “without limits” in February 2022; with Angela Merkel, who spoke fluent Russian to his fluent German, he kept a working personal channel. The International Criminal Court issued a warrant for his arrest in March 2023 for the deportation of Ukrainian children. He remains in office.
