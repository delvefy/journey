Richard II

King of England, 1367–1400

Richard of Bordeaux was born in the abbey of Saint-André at Bordeaux on 6 January 1367, the second surviving son of Edward of Woodstock, the Black Prince, and Joan of Kent. His elder brother died in 1370, his father in June 1376, and a year later, on 21 June 1377, the death of his grandfather Edward III made him king at ten. The minority was managed by a series of continual councils dominated by his uncle John of Gaunt. The Peasants' Revolt of June 1381 was the first great test of the young king: with the Tower of London beaten in and the chancellor Sudbury murdered on the steps, Richard, fourteen years old, met Wat Tyler at Smithfield, watched him cut down by William Walworth, and told the rebels he himself would be their captain — defusing the rising long enough to disperse it.

His chosen counsellors — Robert de Vere, Michael de la Pole — and his extravagant household alienated the magnates. In 1386 the Wonderful Parliament impeached the chancellor; in 1387 five Lords Appellant under Gloucester, Arundel, Warwick, Bolingbroke, and Mowbray defeated de Vere at Radcot Bridge and forced through the Merciless Parliament of 1388, which condemned the king's favourites. Richard reclaimed government in 1389 and bided his time. The death of his beloved first wife Anne of Bohemia at Sheen in 1394 darkened him; the marriage to the six-year-old Isabella of Valois in 1396 brought a twenty-eight-year truce with France.

In 1397 he struck. Gloucester was arrested and murdered at Calais, Arundel executed, Warwick exiled; the parliament of Shrewsbury voted him taxes for life. Quarrelling charges between Bolingbroke and Mowbray gave him an excuse to banish them both in 1398. On John of Gaunt's death in February 1399 he seized the duchy of Lancaster; while he was campaigning in Ireland, Bolingbroke landed at Ravenspur. Richard surrendered at Flint, was paraded through London, deposed in Parliament on 30 September 1399, and died — almost certainly starved — at Pontefract Castle in February 1400.

The road continues to Edward the Black Prince.
