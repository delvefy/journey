Champion of the Light

SKETCH

A goddess summons champions to hold the line against the Covenant — and wipes their memories to summon them again.

  • Universe: Champion of the LightUniverse

    A medieval kingdom under invasion where a goddess called the Light summons champions from other worlds to hold the line — and, when they fall, summons them again with their memories wiped, turning heroism into a loop.

  • VirahCharacter

    The summoned champion of the Light, also called Roger — monstrously strong, regenerative, and stripped of memory by design; a hero who dies to close a rift and is summoned again, emptied, to fight the same war a second time.
