Cosmology & Concepts

META

The shared frame that contains every stitch — the Loom, its threads, its ladder and its lid.

  • The Five DoorsConcept

    The five local mechanisms by which a soul moves between stitches of the Loom — ascension, void-resurrection, divine summoning, judged reincarnation, and engineered gates — which are five names for one act.

  • The Lid Above Every SkyConcept

    The political truth of the Loom — that a lower world's sky is not open but a seal placed from above, beneath which the world is farmed and kept ignorant.

  • The Loom of HeavensConcept

    The tapestry of worlds in the empty between-place, on which every universe hangs as a single stitch of cold fire.

  • The Nine RealmsConcept

    The nine-realm ladder of cultivation — the known ascent from a mortal's first breath of qi to the severed godhood of a Primordial God.

  • The Ten Primordial DaosConcept

    The ten fundamental laws from which every lesser Dao descends — two polarities, four Daos of Order, four of Chaos — and the fusion of all ten that makes a Primordial God.

  • Threads of Space vs. Karma ThreadsConcept

    The two kinds of thread that bind the Loom — the great roads between worlds, and the finer private web between souls.
