The Five Doors

Concept META

The five local mechanisms by which a soul moves between stitches of the Loom — ascension, void-resurrection, divine summoning, judged reincarnation, and engineered gates — which are five names for one act.

The Five Doors are the mechanisms by which a pattern — a soul — moves between the stitches of the Loom. Each world explains soul-movement in its own terms, never suspecting the others exist, and never suspecting that all five describe the same act. As the frame has it: these are five names for one door.

The five names

  • Ascension — climbing the ladder past Void Stepping, where a small inner world opens and travel between stitches becomes possible. The road of the cultivation worlds: Blaze, Sky Pillar, and others.
  • Void-resurrection — a void entity pulling a dead pattern through the Void into a new body on a compatible world. The door of the Engine, worked by Vael.
  • Divine summoning — a god or entity calling a champion out of one world into another. The door of the summoned-hero worlds and the champion-forging tavern.
  • Judged reincarnation — a soul weighed by a bureaucracy of death and reseeded into a new life, sometimes across worlds. The door of the Charlatan, worked by the Judges of Reincarnation.
  • Engineered gates — technology weaponizing the same seam. The Exodus stargates of the Engine, built by Ghor out of one of his own deaths.

One door, five languages

The doctrine of the Five Doors is that the difference between them is a difference of description, not of act. A cultivator's ascension and a wanderer's death-and-waking are the same crossing seen from two worlds that have never met — one calls it a reward for a lifetime's climb, the other calls it dying. Even the Grimoire's thousands of names, on a company only ever 111 strong, may be a sixth face of the same door, or the fifth wearing new skin.

A ladder, a death, a summons, a judgement, a gate. Ask the door which name is right, and it opens all the same.

Theories & resonances

If the Five Doors are truly one, the implication is heavy: soul-movement across the Loom is a single phenomenon governed from somewhere above the worlds that experience it — a claim that touches the lid above every sky. The wiki may hold the unity of the doors as its strongest connective theory. It remains theory: only the two Spine anchors are confirmed crossings.

Open hooks

Whether any soul has ever passed through two different doors in succession, and noticed; whether a door can be closed from above; and whether the between-place has a door of its own, out of which its exiles might return.
